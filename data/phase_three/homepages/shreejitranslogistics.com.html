why choose us?

Shreeji has always been a unique and innovative player in providing superior solutions to customers. Our constant drive to innovate has ensured that we provide the best of services to our customers. If you are wondering why you should choose us, then here are a few reasons why?

Lower Costs

We own vehicles and we also source directly from fleet owners and this helps us keep costs lower. The trucks are also under AMC contracts with the manufacturers thus ensuring better condition of the vehicle.

Technology Enabled

All the company owned trucks are equipped with GPRS Tracking System. The entire company functions with a network of ERP Enabled systems.

Path Breaking Services

Custom Bonded Trucking services that have been greatly appreciated by the Airline Industry have been in service for more than a decade.

World of Experience

We have been providing Door-to-Door Delivery of consignment in Parcel, Full Load & Part load Services since 1983.

Live Support

We let your track your orders live and you will always know the progress of your consignment in real time.
