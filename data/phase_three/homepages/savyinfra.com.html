01

EPC

The Company has “Engineering, Procurement, and Construction” (EPC) projects with comprehensive contracts where the contractor manages the entire project lifecycle—from design and procurement to construction and commissioning. This turnkey approach allows clients to receive a fully operational facility upon project completion.
The Company follows the key phases of an EPC Project: 1) Planning,2) Engineering 3) Procurement 4) Construction 5) Commissioning and start-up.
For Successful EPC Management, the company considers the following: 1) Clear Contractual Terms: Defining the project's scope, budget, and timeline in the contract is crucial to prevent disputes.2) Cost and Time Efficiency: Integrated planning and execution can lead to cost savings and faster project completion.3) Risk Allocation: Properly distributing risks between the client and contractor ensures fair responsibility.
