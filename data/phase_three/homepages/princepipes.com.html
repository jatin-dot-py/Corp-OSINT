Prince Pipes and Fittings: One the largest PVC pipe manufacturers in India and multi polymer processors

Prince Pipes and Fittings Limited (PPFL) is one of India’s largest PVC pipe manufacturers & multi polymer processors. Incorporated in 1987, and a highly regarded PVC pipes brand, winning the Brand of the Year – Pipes Award at INEX Realty+ Awards 2021.

Prince Pipes has entered the list of Fortune India 500 companies, in December 2021.

The company's Jaipur manufacturing facility was awarded a GOLD medal in the 8th edition of National Awards for Manufacturing Competitiveness (NAMC) 2021, organized by International Research Institute for Manufacturing, in strategic association with Moneycontrol. The jury audited several core attributes of Leadership, Expertise, Quality and Excellence. The program recognizes manufacturing organizations that have excelled in business through implementation of a strong competitive strategy and coherence to its growth plan to become world-class.

We take great pride in leading a significant transformation in India’s exponentially increasing water demands through our operations spanning plumbing systems, borewell, agriculture pipes, water storage tanks, industrial piping and electrical conduit pipes, together with the widest sewage product range and underground drainage solutions. Prince Pipes and Fittings Limited has 7 state-of-the–art manufacturing units at Haridwar (Uttarakhand), Athal (Dadra and Nagar Haveli), Dadra (Dadra and Nagar Haveli), Kolhapur (Maharashtra), Chennai (Tamil Nadu), Jobner (Rajasthan) and Sangareddy (Telangana).

The best PVC pipe company in India

Prince Pipes specialises in manufacturing world class plumbing, sewerage & drainage piping systems for both residential and commercial applications and CPVC piping systems or industrial pipes that find applications in industrial and infrastructure projects. As a leading UPVC pipe manufacturer, and makers of PVC pipes and CPVC pipes in India, Prince Pipes also serves the agriculture sector through products in borewell and agriculture piping categories.SmartFit Plus CPVC Pipes & Fittings by Prince Pipes offer a reliable and high-performance plumbing solution for hot and cold water applications. Designed for durability and safe potable water supply, SmartFit Plus ensures consistent performance across residential and commercial plumbing systems.

Our association with Tooling Holland BV [based in The Netherlands] - a global leader in plastic mould manufacturing association enables us to draw upon Tooling Holland’s technical expertise and deep knowledge, strengthen internal operational efficiencies and optimize production costs.

Storefit – Water Storage Tanks: Paani Ka Bank

In June 2020 we launched Prince Storefit Water Tanks as part of the company’s introductory range of overhead water storage solutions.

Today, health and hygiene have taken centre stage. Our country’s water related challenges are severe and need to be addressed on an urgent basis as over 600 million Indians face high to extreme stress over water, while 75% of Indian households do not have drinking water at their premises. The launch of Storefit water storage tanks was an organic extension of our product segments to leverage our expertise as one of the leading pipe manufacturer in India. This extension also offers the market a high quality, trusted and value-led water storage product in a largely fragmented and unorganised market.
