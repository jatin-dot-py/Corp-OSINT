About Mercury EV-Tech Limited India's One-Stop Electric Vehicle Manufacturer

Mercury EV-Tech Limited is an end-to-end electric vehicle manufacturer based in Vadodara, Gujarat, and a one-stop solution provider for every EV need. Just as our tagline suggests, we are more than an EV maker.

We manufacture the core electric vehicle components in-house — EV batteries, chassis and motor controllers — supported by our own cathodic electrodeposition (CED) coating plant. This vertical integration lets us control quality and cost from the smallest component to the finished vehicle.

Our product range covers electric two-wheelers, electric buses, electric loaders and electric passenger vehicles, built for Indian roads, Indian load conditions and Indian buyers. Every vehicle we build supports Atmanirbhar Bharat and Bharat's sustainable development by replacing conventional fuel with clean electric mobility.

From a single EV component to a complete electric vehicle — when you think EV, think Mercury EV-Tech.

End-to-End In-House Production Ecosystem

Battery, BMS, chassis, CED coating and complete vehicles — every core EV component made in-house.

Battery

Cells matched and packs assembled in-house — from a 3.7 kWh NMC pack for the scooters to 18.2 kWh lithium packs for the loaders. Three-year warranty on every one.

Battery Management System

Built and tested alongside the packs it governs, so the cells and the electronics that protect them leave our line as a single, matched assembly.

Chassis

ERW2-grade steel tube frames, welded and jigged on site, then CED-coated in the same building before they ever reach an assembly line.

CED Coating Plant

A fully automated cathodic electrodeposition line with robotic handling and automated process control, coating every frame we build for corrosion resistance.

E-2W, E-3W, E-Buses and E-Loaders

Fifteen models across two, three and four wheels — passenger scooters and autos through to cargo loaders rated from 350 up to 1,000 kilograms.

In-House Chassis Manufacturing

Robust, high-quality chassis systems designed and fabricated for precision, durability and heavy load.
