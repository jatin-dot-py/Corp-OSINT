A Visionary Conglomerate

Kiri Industries Limited is a diversified industrial group shaped through continuous evolution across sectors that form the foundation of India’s economic and manufacturing landscape. From chemicals to metals and agri-inputs, the Group is emerging into new areas with a clear focus on building manufacturing capability, securing essential supply chains, and supporting India’s long-term self-reliance. Its businesses are grounded in integrity and a pragmatic understanding of what it takes to operate, produce, and deliver at scale over time.

Our role is defined by participation in the real economy, creating capacity, enabling downstream industries, and contributing to the strength and resilience of India’s industrial backbone while remaining engaged with global markets.
