Welcome to VPRP LIMITED

"VISHNU PRAKASH R PUNGLIA LIMITED”. is one of India's fastest-growing Infrastructure Development Companies. Established in 1986, the Company achieved exponential growth with the dedication to customer satisfaction and trustworthiness. In April 2013, the company progressed from a partnership firm to a Joint Stock Company under Chapter IX of the companies act 1956 registered with the Registrar of Companies, Mumbai (Maharashtra) INDIA. Maintaining a legacy of innovation in engineering designs and construction, VPRPL continues to add new milestones every year, building world-class infrastructures and creating one-stop solutions in the infrastructure domain.

Innovation to take products from an idea all the way to consumer.
