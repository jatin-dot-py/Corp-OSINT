Welcome to Vivaa Tradecom

Set up in 1995, Vivaa Tradecom Ltd., Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, has attained organic growth by virtue of it offering quality and range wide denim and non denim fabrics and denim garments, catering to international as well as domestic markets.

Vivaa’s denim and non denim fabrics are supplied in 100% cotton, cotton polyester and cotton lycra blends in a range of widths and weaves. Blends provide for extra stretch effect for much needed comfort consideration of the wearer of the prized finished denim – non denim garment with host of finishes to attract attention.

Vivaa’s product range of denim jeans for men and women bears the hallmark of our distinctive sourcing and lean manufacture via engagement with a string of valued and dependable supply chain partners and in house trained manpower.

Our denim jeans for men and women dress up young and old, hip and hop baby boomers and young and upwardly mobile as well as mature customers who wish to comfortably confront the daily rigours and seek value for money preposition.

Vivaa denim jean brands are available in different styles, fits made from 100% cotton or cotton /lycra denim fabric in varied colours and finishes giving a special status marked with comfort and ruggedness.

So, slip in to a cool Vivaa denim jeans pair and take on the day…
