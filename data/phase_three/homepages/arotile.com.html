About The Company

ARO Granite Industries started operations as a 100% Export Oriented Unit in 1991 for processing Polished / Flamed Granite Tiles & Slabs. With a 100 % export-oriented unit, in Hosur, Aro is the largest exporter of processed granite in India, with a production capacity od 1 million Square-meters/year.

The new factory in Jaipur is close to raw materials in North India. Located in Mahindra World City SEZ just 45 min from Jaipur Airport, the unit has a capacity of 150,000 square-meters/year.

About AGIL

AGIL is India’s leading exporter of natural stones. With factories located in Hosur and Jaipur, AGIL has a total capacity of 1 million square meters per year. AGIL exports to over 45 countries around the world.
