Who We Are

Teesta Agro Industries Ltd

Teesta Agro, founded in 1986, specialises in the production and distribution of Single Super Phosphate (SSP), Sulphuric Acid and NPK across North Bengal, Bihar, and Assam. In 2017, we expanded our operations by inaugurating a state-of-the-art SSP plant to cater to the regions of Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Haryana, Punjab, and Uttar Pradesh.
