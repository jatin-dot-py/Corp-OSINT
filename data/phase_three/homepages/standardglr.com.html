About the Group

Headquartered in Hyderabad, India, Standard Engineering Technology (SET), formally Standard Glass Lining Technology, has SIX subsidiary companies working collaboratively to deliver complete solutions across the pharmaceutical and process sectors. In addition to SET, each subsidiary brings deep technical expertise and a shared commitment to engineering excellence, providing scalable equipment solutions that take projects from research and development and pilot plants through to full-scale production and commercialisation.

Standard Engineering Technology Ltd.

Standard Engineering Technology (SET) are a leading supplier in the design and manufacture of glass lined equipment including; Glass Lined Reactors, Receivers, Heat Exchangers, Storage Tanks, Flanges and Valves.

The business invests heavily in automated processes, systems and the latest technology, to maintain and extend their competitive advantage in the marketplace.

Read More

S2 Engineering Industry Pvt. Ltd.

S2 Engineering Industry and CPK Engineers specialise in the design and manufacture of stainless steel and exotic alloy process equipment, including pressure vessels, reactors, receivers, storage tanks, agitated nutsche filter dryers (ANFDs), rotary cone vacuum dryers, conical dryers, vacuum tray dryers, distillation columns, heat exchangers, filters and process pipework.

Like Standard Engineering Technology (SET), S2 Engineering continually invests in advanced manufacturing technologies and modern fabrication infrastructure, ensuring that all equipment is produced in conformity with the most stringent international quality and compliance standards.

Read More

CPK Engineers Pvt. Ltd.

CPK Engineers specialises in the design, manufacture, supply, installation and commissioning of a wide range of process equipment, including stainless steel reactors, heat exchangers, leaf filters, receivers, filtration and drying systems, storage tanks and distillation columns. The company’s ability to offer end-to-end engineering services ensures seamless project execution and dependable operational performance.

Read More

S2 Stanpumps Pvt. Ltd.

Stanpumps is a division of S2 Engineering Industry, and excels in the design, manufacture, and assembly of high-performance dry-running vacuum pump technologies, that support critical process operations without the need for lubricating oils or sealing fluids.

Stanpumps’ product line includes dry multi-stage lobe and claw vacuum pumps, dry single-stage claw pumps, rotary vane vacuum pumps, roots booster pumps and tailored pumping systems designed for vacuum distillation, solvent vapour extraction and recovery — all engineered to deliver efficient, contamination-free performance for demanding industrial environments.

Read More

Standard Flora Pvt. Ltd.

Standard Flora is a specialist in precision-engineered PTFE (polytetrafluoroethylene) solutions, delivering high-performance corrosion-resistant products for demanding industrial applications.

The company designs, manufactures and supplies a comprehensive range of PTFE-lined pipes, fittings, valves and custom components, all engineered to withstand aggressive chemical environments while enhancing process efficiency and operational safety

Read More

C2C Pvt. Ltd.

C2C is a specialist project management and engineering services company delivering disciplined, end-to-end support for infrastructure and industrial projects. As a subsidiary of Standard Engineering Technology Ltd, C2C combines strong technical expertise with structured project controls to ensure efficient, cost-effective, and high-quality project delivery from concept through execution.

Read More

Standard Scigenics Pvt. Ltd.

Standard Scigenics is a specialized life sciences engineering company delivering advanced solutions for pharmaceutical and biopharmaceutical manufacturing. As a subsidiary of Standard Engineering Technology Ltd, Standard Scigenics combines deep technical expertise with regulatory-focused execution through its Injectable Solutions and Bio Process Systems divisions, supporting clients across sterile manufacturing and bioprocessing from design through implementation.
