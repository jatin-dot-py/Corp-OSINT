A Legacy of Quality and Sustainability

At Unique Organics, we believe in the power of nature to nourish and sustain both humans and animals. Our commitment to quality, sustainability, and innovation drives us to source the finest ingredients from trusted growers and producers, bringing the benefits of nature to communities worldwide.

Our Divisions

INTERNATIONAL DIVISION

Unique Organics Ltd. has its business presence in the markets of Europe, USA, Canada, UK, Vietnam, Myanmar, Sri Lanka and many more. We believe in maintaining high-quality standards, professionalism, ethics and long-term association with our valued clients. To achieve these goals, we are backed by experienced management and a team of professionals.

INDIA DIVISION

Unique Organics Ltd. has been providing quality Compounded Cattle Feed, By-Pass Protein and Area Specific Chelated Mineral Mixture etc. since 2017. Company also gets associated with established and well known milk producing companies across India to supply its feed products on contract manufacturing basis. Company get its feed products certified from BIS and marketing under the brand name ‘ROHINI’ in the Indian market from 2021.

A strong initiative toward Women Empowerment – Rohini Choupal

Under the “Rohini Choupal” initiative, all the shops being operated have been placed under the supervision of local women, so that they can be encouraged toward economic self-reliance and independence.

Through this scheme, not only have new employment opportunities been created, but women from both rural and urban areas are now contributing to the development of their families and society with empowered participation.

In this effort run by “Unique Organics Limited,” women are being given the opportunity to become self-reliant through business training, product knowledge, and sales.

If you also want to join this scheme and create your own identity, then connect with Rohini Choupal today and start a new journey.

“No type of fee or security deposit is taken from you to participate in this scheme. It is completely free of cost.”
