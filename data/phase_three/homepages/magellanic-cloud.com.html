Frequently Asked Questions

What is digital innovation for companies today?

Digital innovation is the process of employing emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), cloud computing, automation, data analytics to improve business operations, customer experiences, and decision-making. In today's competitive landscape, digital innovation helps companies increase efficiency, reduce costs and stay ahead of market changes. Magellanic Cloud enables digital innovation through its expertise in AI, digital transformation, cloud services, intelligent e-surveillance, drone technology, fintech automation and software engineering to help businesses become future-ready.

What is Magellanic Cloud?

Magellanic Cloud is a global technology enterprise specializing in Digital Transformation, AI, Cloud Computing, Intelligent E-Surveillance, Drone Technology, FinTech Automation IT Services and IT Staffing Solutions. Through its portfolio of tech-led subsidiaries, including Motivity Labs, Scandron, iVIS, Finoux, and JNIT, Magellanic Cloud delivers innovative solutions for businesses and governments to accelerate growth. With a presence across multiple industries and geographies, Magellanic Cloud continues to build technology solutions designed for the future.

What intelligent E-Surveillance solutions does Magellanic Cloud provide?

Through its e-surveillance technology subsidiary iVIS and Provigil, Magellanic Cloud offers end-to-end intelligent e-surveillance solutions powered by AI, video analytics, centralized monitoring and ICCC capabilities. These solutions help government agencies, railways, BFSI institutions, enterprises and smart cities enhance security, improve incident response and gain actionable insights from real-time data. 

What is Scandron and how does it contribute to the drone industry?

Scandron, a subsidiary of Magellanic Cloud, is one of India's Top 10 Best Drone companies, specializing in the design and development of advanced unmanned aerial systems (UAS) for defence, surveillance, logistics and industrial applications. Through continuous innovation and indigenous technology development, Scandron is helping shape the future of India's drone ecosystem and autonomous drone capabilities.

What industries does Magellanic Cloud serve?

Magellanic Cloud serves a diverse range of industries including Banking & Financial Services (BFSI), Government, Defence, Transportation, Railways, Retail, Manufacturing, Healthcare, Logistics, and Technology. Through its specialised subsidiaries - Motivity Labs for digital transformation, Scandron for drone technology, iVIS for intelligent e-surveillance, Finoux for fintech automation, and JNIT for IT staffing, the company delivers tailored solutions that address industry-specific challenges and growth opportunities.
