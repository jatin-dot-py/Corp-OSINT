About_

Jewellery has an ability to bring out the best in you and your personality. It is not just a decorative piece, it can be a tale. and when it’s brand new, it can be a start of something original. Gargi is an attempt of crafting such original stories for each one. Because the right jewellery brings you closer to who you really are. With Gargi, we aim to make jewellery which is beautiful and unique, just like you.
Gargi by P. N. Gadgil & Sons has introduced ‘Utsaav by Gargi’, a wedding-focused sub-brand that brings fusion-style designs to its bridal jewellery collection.
At PNGS GARGI FASHION JEWELLERY LIMITED, we understand the place of jewellery in your life. With Gargi, our vision is to adorn every beautiful soul at every occasion. Making our jewellery accepted and accessible. This is our tribute to the young generation, crafting a bold and charming story for the generation of bold and beautiful.
