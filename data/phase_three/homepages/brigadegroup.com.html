Commitment to Sustainability

Amidst rapid environmental shifts, it is of paramount importance to rewrite the real estate narrative and place sustainability at the forefront. Sustainability is firmly woven into the fabric of our business ethos, and we remain resolutely committed to responsible operations.

Our achievements underscore our dedication towards reaching Net Zero status by 2045:

A commendable 84% year-on-year upsurge in wastewater recycling

An unwavering commitment to utilising 100% treated wastewater for landscaping

An impressive 14.8 million square feet of our portfolio attained green certification

A remarkable 1,00,000 trees planted to date

A noteworthy 15% year-on-year reduction in carbon emissions intensity
