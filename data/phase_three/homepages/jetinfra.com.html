Welcome to Jet Infraventure Limited


 

Company was incorporated in the year 2001 as Private Limited Company by experienced professionals and young enthusiastic team. Company has its head office at Mumbai with in-house infrastructure. It has completed several residential and commercial projects in last many years under its own banner and few in associate companies. Company is mainly working in Maharashtra and Gujarat. Our aim is to make series of residential & commercial projects and create pan India presence. Company got converted into Public Limited Company with effect from 19th August 2014 and its name is changed to Jet Infraventure Limited.

We as Company have put consistent efforts and excellence to create value for our esteemed customers by providing unique and qualitative projects. Company is making systematic growth in the field and has a goal to make pan India presence.

Vision

 

If foundations are strong, a pillar will stand tall. This basic rule of strong foundations is always top of mind in every objective we set out to achieve.  Our “Vision” is
