Three Decades of Defining the Extraordinary

Founded in 1994, Party Cruisers Limited was built on a singular belief — that every guest must feel like the most important person in the room.

Today, that philosophy powers an integrated ecosystem of six specialist divisions, delivering world-class weddings, celebrations, and corporate experiences across India and the Middle East.

House of Vivaah — Design · Décor · Production

The décor is the first impression your guests receive and the final memory they carry home. As PCL's flagship design house, House of Vivaah functions as a couture studio for the wedding world. Our team of floral artisans and production specialists treat every brief as an original work of art.

Whether it is a mandap framed by floating doves or a desert odyssey featured in Vogue, we operate at a scale where imagination meets flawless execution. With an exclusive in-house inventory, we ensure that every celebration remains an unrepeatable original.

Accolades: Multiple Platinum & Gold honours for Best Décor, Sangeet, and Mehendi (2019–2023).

Explore House of Vivaah

Vows Vachan — The Wedding Curators

Planning a wedding is deeply emotional — and you shouldn’t have to navigate it alone. Vows Vachan, PCL’s dedicated wedding planning and curation division, partners with you from the first conversation to the final farewell, managing every detail so you can truly be present for your celebration.

From intimate ceremonies in Goa to grand destination weddings in Rajasthan, our experienced curators bring clarity, creativity, and calm to every moment. Our work has been featured in Vogue and trusted by families across India and the Middle East.

Specialties: End-to-End Wedding Planning, Destination Wedding Management, Vendor Curation, Guest Experience

Locations: Pan-India | Dubai | Doha | Bahrain | Oman

Known For: Personalized curation, transparent vendor management, on-the-day coordination

Meet Vows Vachan

Event Factory — Corporate Impression Management

Your brand deserves more than a conference room. Event Factory is PCL's corporate events division, helping India's leading companies create experiences that inspire teams, impress clients, and elevate their brand narrative.

From product launches and leadership summits to annual celebrations and MICE events, Event Factory combines strategic thinking with creative production to deliver corporate events that leave a lasting impression — not just a lasting memory.

Specialties: Product Launches, Corporate Galas, Team Events, Brand Activations, MICE Events

Capabilities: Full AV Production, Stage Design, Guest Management, Live Streaming

Clients: Across industries — BFSI, Technology, FMCG, Real Estate, and more

Explore Event Factory

Party House — Milestone & Social Celebrations

Life's greatest milestones — a 50th birthday, a silver anniversary, a baby shower, a retirement — deserve the same level of attention that a wedding gets. Party House is PCL's social celebrations division, bringing luxury-event sensibility to personal milestones of every kind.

The team at Party House understands that these events are intensely personal. They work closely with hosts to understand the story they want to tell, then build an experience around it — from themed décor and entertainment to bespoke menus and personalized experiences that make every guest feel the love.

Specialties: Birthday Celebrations, Anniversaries, Baby Showers, Retirement Parties, Themed Events

Scale: Intimate gatherings of 20 to grand celebrations of 500+

Signature Touch: Fully themed environments, custom entertainment, bespoke styling

Celebrate with Party House

Live Space — Entertainment & Artist Management

The best events are remembered for how they made you feel — and nothing shapes that feeling quite like live entertainment. Live Space is PCL's entertainment and artist management division, connecting events with the perfect performers, from Bollywood playback singers to international DJs and live bands.

With a deep roster of artists across genres and a team that understands the rhythm of live event production, Live Space handles everything from artist sourcing and contract management to technical riders and on-night coordination — so the show always goes on, flawlessly.

Specialties: Artist Booking, Live Music, DJ & Sound Production, Celebrity Management

Genres: Bollywood, Classical, Sufi, Jazz, EDM, International Acts

Services: Artist Sourcing, Contract & Rider Management, Stage & Technical Production

Discover Live Space

Venue Affairs — Venue Booking & Management

Choosing the right venue is the single most consequential decision in event planning — it sets the stage for everything else. Venue Affairs is PCL's dedicated venue curation division, with an unrivalled network of premium properties across India and the Middle East.

The team at Venue Affairs goes beyond simply shortlisting options. They negotiate on your behalf, manage venue contracts, coordinate with property teams, and ensure that your chosen space is set up exactly as planned — so that from the moment guests arrive, the experience begins.

Specialties: Venue Sourcing, Contract Negotiation, Venue Management, Site Inspections

Network: Luxury hotels, heritage palaces, beach resorts, private estates — across India & Middle East

Best For: Destination weddings, large-scale corporate events, exclusive private celebrations

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Party Cruisers Limited — from planning luxury destination weddings to executing large-scale events across India and the Middle East.

What does Party Cruisers Limited do?
Party Cruisers Limited (PCL) is India's premier luxury event management company, operating since 1994. We specialise in bespoke weddings, luxury destination weddings, corporate events, social celebrations, artist management, and venue booking across India and the Middle East — through six dedicated specialist brand divisions.
Is Party Cruisers Limited a listed company?
Yes. Party Cruisers Limited has been listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) of India since 2021, making it India's first and only publicly listed event management company. This brings a level of transparency, governance, and accountability that is unique in the industry.
What are the 6 brand divisions of Party Cruisers Limited?
PCL operates through six specialist brands: House of Vivaah (wedding design, décor & production), Vows Vachan (end-to-end wedding planning & curation), Event Factory (corporate event management), Party House (social & milestone celebrations), Live Space (entertainment & artist management), and Venue Affairs (venue sourcing & management).
How much does a luxury wedding planner in Mumbai cost?
Luxury wedding planning in Mumbai varies based on scale, services, and the number of events involved. A full-service luxury package at Party Cruisers Limited — covering décor, planning, entertainment, and venue management — is tailored to each client's vision and guest count. We recommend reaching out for a personalised consultation and quote.
Does Party Cruisers Limited plan destination weddings?
Absolutely. Destination weddings are one of our specialties, both domestically — in Goa, Rajasthan, Hyderabad, and beyond — and internationally across the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, and Oman. Our Vows Vachan and Venue Affairs divisions collaborate to make destination weddings seamless from logistics to last-minute details.
In which cities does Party Cruisers Limited operate?
PCL is headquartered in Mumbai with offices in Pune, Nashik, Goa, Hyderabad, Lucknow, and Delhi across India. In the Middle East, we operate from our Dubai headquarters with presence in Doha, Bahrain, and Oman.
Can Party Cruisers Limited manage both weddings and corporate events?
Yes. Unlike most event companies that focus on one segment, PCL's six-division structure allows us to bring specialist expertise to both personal celebrations and professional events. Vows Vachan and House of Vivaah handle weddings while Event Factory specialises exclusively in corporate impression management.
How do I get started with Party Cruisers Limited?
Simply fill in the enquiry form on our Contact page, WhatsApp us at +91 84336 59225, or reach out through any of our social channels. Our team will respond within 24 hours to schedule a consultation and guide you to the right brand division for your event.
