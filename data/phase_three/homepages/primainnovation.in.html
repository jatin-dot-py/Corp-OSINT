About us

Prima Innovations Limited

Prima Innovations Limited is a Trusted Provider of Practical Plastic and Safety Solutions for Modern Industries. We Focus on Delivering Reliable Products That Support Storage, Transport, and Road Safety Needs. Our Current Product Range Includes Insulated Boxes and Crates, Plastic Pallets, and Road Safety Products. Quality, Durability, and Ease of Use are at the Core of Everything We Offer. We are Committed to Dependable Supply and Long-Term Customer Relationships.

Driven by Innovation and Sustainability, Prima Innovations Limited Continuously Invests in Advanced Manufacturing Practices to Deliver Quality Solutions that Meet Global Standards and Evolving Customer Needs.
