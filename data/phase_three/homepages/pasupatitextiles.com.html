Welcome to Pasupati Spinning & Weaving Mills Ltd.

Pasupati Spinning & Weaving Mills Ltd- an ISO 9002 company, which prides itself on the caliber of its skilled people and its expertise in all major technology areas. Benchmarking global standards in Speed, Innovation, Technology, Quality, Consistency and Punctuality, Pasupati offers world class integrated textile solutions.

Who we are

We have Years of
experience in this field

The company started operations producing yarns of international quality by setting up a unit at Dharuhera in Haryana in 1981 with an installed capacity of 12,500 spindles.

Today, we have a combined installed capacity of 80,912 spindles manufacturing 15,450 tonnes of yarn and threads per annum and the expension goes on….

Our Uniqueness

Why Pasupati?

  • Pasupati threads have a greater strength.
  • Superb silicon lubrication for better needle coaling.
  • Flawless thread with correct twist balance & controlled elongation providing better sewability.
  • By using an item as seemingly insignificant as thread, you could not only save valuable time but also rejections, as well as ensure customer satisfaction.
