Our Foundation

“Enterprise must serve a purpose beyond business, contributing to communities and to humanity at large.”

S. K. Poddar

Chairman, Saroj Poddar Group

Saroj Poddar Group has been built on a strong foundation of values, with the belief that enterprise carries a responsibility beyond performance. Across its businesses, the Group focuses on creating long-term value while remaining accountable to the communities it operates in. This extends into its engagement with education, culture, and heritage, reflecting a broader commitment to contribute meaningfully to society and to the future it helps shape.

Life at Saroj Poddar Group

The strength of Saroj Poddar Group lies in its people. Across our businesses, teams come together to learn, collaborate, and contribute to industries that support India's growth.
