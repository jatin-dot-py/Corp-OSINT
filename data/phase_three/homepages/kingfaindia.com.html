Welcome To Kingfa Science & Technology (India) Limited

We Providing Best Industrial Solution for Our Customers Business Growing In Last 34+ Years.
Kingfa Science & Technology (India) Limited is a leading manufacturer and supplier of high quality Reinforced Polypropylene Compounds, Thermoplastics Elastomers, Fibre Reinforced composites and all engineering plastics. Being a global leader in modified plastics used in automotive and consumer products. At the onset of the current COVID 19 pandemic, our group companies globally embarked on a diversification into personal protective essentials (PPEs). We are producing Respiratory Protective Devices – Disposable filtering facepiece which comply with global specifications of EN149:2001 + A1:2009, IS: 9473-2002. Kingfa also imports and supplies hand protection products like Nitrile gloves
