Building a sustainable tomorrow with aluminium, the 'Metal of the Future'

Overview

Vedanta Aluminium Metal Limited is one of the world’s leading producers of aluminium, the ‘Metal of the Future’. We are also India’s largest aluminium producer, with operations in the states of Odisha and Chhattisgarh.

Aluminium’s unique properties find applications across a vast spectrum of critical industries like aerospace, aviation, electrical, transportation, infrastructure, consumer goods, and more, as well as sunrise sectors such as electric vehicles and renewable energy; thus making it essential for modern life and crucial for a low carbon future. It is no wonder, therefore, that aluminium is the second most important metal in the world today and is poised to become the most important commercial metal in the near future. With industry leading technologies, we are proudly paving the way for a sustainable future for our customers, communities and the world at large.

Our Journey

2001

Vedanta acquired the 100 KTPA BALCO plant in Korba, Chhattisgarh.

2004

A new smelter with 245 KTPA capacity commissioned at Korba, Chhattisgarh along with a captive power plant of 540 MW.

2005

Vedanta Group’s first Greenfield project gets launched in Jharsuguda, Odisha.

2008

First hot metal tapped from Jharsuguda’s 0.5 MTPA smelter, 9×135 MW Captive Power Plant commissioned at Jharsuguda, Odisha.

2009

Billets and Wire Rods introduced in product portfolio.

2010

4×600 MW Thermal Power Plant commissioned at Jharsuguda, Odisha.

2014

First Metal from 1.25MTPA Smelter (Special Economic Zone)

2015

A new smelter of 325 KTPA commissioned at Korba, Chhattisgarh with a captive power plant 1200 MW.

2018

Vedanta’s Jharsuguda complex enters the global ‘1 Million Tonne’ production & export club.

2020

Vedanta becomes first domestic producer to produce & supply Primary Foundry Alloy (PFA) to the Indian automotive sector.

2021

Commissioning and ramping-up of Potline-6

2022

Launch of India’s first ever ‘green’ aluminium, branded Restora

2024

At 1.8 MTPA capacity, Jharsuguda plant becomes the largest in the world

2026

Vedanta Aluminium Metal Limited is formed
