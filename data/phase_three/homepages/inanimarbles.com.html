Since 1987

4 Decades of
Expertise

Established in 1987, Inani Marbles and Industries Limited is a multinational company with nearly four decades of expertise in manufacturing and exporting natural and engineered stones. Furthermore, led by Captain S.K. and M.K. Inani, we stand as the largest manufacturer and exporter in North India. As a premier stone MNC, we are also proudly traded on the Bombay Stock Exchange.
