      Steel Sector
      Revolutionizing the Steel Sector
      With over five decades of expertise, we provide high-performance conveyor chains and sprockets for the steel sector, catering to blast furnaces, continuous casting areas, plate mills, wire rod mills, sinter plants, hot strip mills, and bloom & billet casting.
      Know More
      Cement Sector
      Revolutionizing the Cement Sector
      We have been offering chains and sprockets for more than half a century and are well placed to offer majority of the chains and sprockets for Deep Bucket Elevator, Reclaimer for Lime Stone, Plate Feeder, Deep Pan Conveyor and various other applications.
      Know More
      Power & Energy
      Revolutionizing the Power & Energy Sector
      Over the five decades of our excellence in engineering, we have gained number one position for supply of chains & Sprockets for apron feeder, (BRU) elevator, boiler feed, drop forged, fly ash conveyor and ESP unit system. These feeder chains carry heavy/abrasive material.
      Know More
      Mining
      Revolutionizing the Mining Sector
      Mining sector requires chain to be very robust due to higher breaking load. Durability, wear life and toughness are USP of the chain construction. High alloy steel raw materials with special heat-treatment process are used to manufacture these chains.
      Know More
      D I Pipe
      Unmatched Performance for D I Pipe Applications
      As one of our most exclusive products, the D I Pipe Chain and Sprocket Shaft Assembly stand unmatched in quality and reliability, delivering superior performance and longevity across demanding industrial operations.
      Know More
      Chemical / Fertilizer
      Enhancing Material Handling Efficiency
      With decades of engineering excellence, we deliver durable and high-performance chains and sprockets for the chemical and fertilizer industries. Designed to endure extreme temperatures and abrasive conditions, our drag conveyor chains ensure smooth, reliable, and efficient material handling in every operation.
      Know More
      Automotive
      Precision Chains for the Automotive Industry
      As leading manufacturers of drive and conveyor chains, we deliver customized solutions for the automotive sector. Our precision-engineered chains and sprockets are crafted with zero tolerance for error, ensuring superior performance, durability, and reliability in every application.
      Know More
      Pulp & Paper
      Reliable Solutions for Pulp & Paper Mills
      Our conveyor chains and sprockets are engineered to withstand the demanding conditions of pulp and paper mills. Designed for applications such as wood chip, drum infeed, reclaimer, and paper roll conveyors, they ensure high performance, durability, and minimal downtime in critical operations.
      Know More
      Sugar Industries
      Reliable Chains for the Sugar Sector
      With over five decades of expertise, we manufacture a specialized range of cane sugar and beet chains and sprockets. Our feeder table chains—featuring tough, simple designs—are perfectly suited for cane infeed conveyors, ensuring durability and smooth operation in demanding sugar mill environments.
      Know More
      Sugar Industries
      Reliable Chains for the Sugar Sector
      With over five decades of expertise, we manufacture a specialized range of cane sugar and beet chains and sprockets. Our feeder table chains—featuring tough, simple designs—are perfectly suited for cane infeed conveyors, ensuring durability and smooth operation in demanding sugar mill environments.
      Know More
      Food Product
      Custom Chains & Sprockets for the Food Industry
      We design and manufacture specialized chains and sprockets for leading food brands, including KHS Plant, Mohan, Ready Roti, Patanjali, APS, and Ruchi Soya. Our range includes hollow pin and solid pin type chains, as well as oil extractor chains—crafted to meet hygiene, precision, and durability standards.
