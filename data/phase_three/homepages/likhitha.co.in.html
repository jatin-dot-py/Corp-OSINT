Founded in Hyderabad in 1998, Likhitha Infrastructure Limited has grown from a regional contractor into one of India’s most trusted oil & gas infrastructure companies — a track record built mile by mile, weld by weld.

1998
ISO CERTIFIED 9001:2015, 14001:2015, 45001:2018

Likhitha Infrastructure Limited operates across four principal business lines: Cross-Country Pipelines, City Gas Distribution (including CNG Stations), Operation & Maintenance services for existing networks and Storage Solutions / Terminal.
Our clients include GAIL, NRL, BPCL, HPCL, IOAGPL, IHB Limited, IOCL, IGGL, IGL, AGP, etc — organisations that trust us with India’s most critical energy infrastructure.

In 2019, we delivered a landmark achievement: the 10.75" OD × 69 km trans-national pipeline connecting India to Nepal — the first of its kind in South-East Asia.

We have also contributed to the Kandla–Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline (KGPL), one of the world's longest LPG pipeline networks, facilitating the safe and efficient transportation of LPG from Kandla, Gujarat, to Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh.
