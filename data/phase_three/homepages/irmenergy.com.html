FAQs of CNG
What is CNG?

It is natural gas compressed up to 200 bar pressure used as an automobiles fuel mainly comprising of Methane (80% to 90%) which is in gaseous sate. Read More

FAQs of PNG Domestic
Is Piped Natural Gas, as a domestic fuel, better than LPG?

Yes, Piped Natural Gas (PNG) is definitely a better fuel for your kitchen, then LPG. PNG gives you the advantage of an uninterrupted supply, saves you from the effort of booking a refill or changing cylinders, and helps you enjoy more kitchen space, as PNG reaches you through pipelines. Moreover, no pilferage is experienced in case of PNG, as the invoicing is done for the exact quantity of PNG utilized by he customer as per the meter reading. Read More

FAQs of PNG Commercial
Who will appoint a downstream contractor?

Customer is required to appoint a reputed and experienced piping contractor for installation of internal piping from regulator/meter upto burner at his own cost. The piping work should be done strictly as per IREMPL technical specifications and guidelines. Read More

FAQs of PNG Industrial
Is the supply of PNG regular?

The supply is absolutely regular. The pipeline distribution network is an ‘on-line’ supply system that consists of safety valves and regulators that control and monitor the gas supply and pressure, and assist in identifying system faults and ensures uninterrupted supply at requisite pressure.
