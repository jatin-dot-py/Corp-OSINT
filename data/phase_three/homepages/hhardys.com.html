For over 50 years, we have been manufacturing durable and high-quality propeller shafts, double cardan shafts, universal joint kits and drivetrain components

Spread over 40,000 sq. meters of land, our factory is located in Nashik, Maharashtra

We sell our products in more than 15 countries spread over 6 continents as well as all over India

Our customers include several leading global players in the commercial vehicle, tractor, off-highway, construction equipment, aftermarket and industrial sectors

Single-minded focus on quality assurance is at the core of our corporate philosophy and this is visible in all aspects of our operations
