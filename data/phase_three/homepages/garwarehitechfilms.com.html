Garware Hi-Tech Films, a global leader with over 90 years of legacy, epitomizes technology, innovation, and excellence in Suncontrol Window Films, Paint Protection Films and Polyester Films. Our Suncontrol Window Films are revolutionizing spaces by enhancing cooling efficiency across residential, commercial, and automotive sectors, while driving substantial energy savings. At the forefront of automotive protection, our Paint Protection Films have set industry standards with pioneering technology and unmatched resilience. Our Speciality Films (BOPET) represents the pinnacle of innovation, setting benchmarks across diverse applications from shrink films to thermal films to lidding films to liner films. In Architecture business, our films showcase unparalleled excellence, offering superior UV protection and energy efficiency, advancing both comfort and sustainable solutions for future generations.

Businesses

Sun Control Films Improve your car’s engine longevity and driving comfort by blocking sunlight and managing heat dispersion.

Paint Protection Films Preserve your car’s look for years to come with a self-healing, scratch-resistant invisible film.

Architectural Films Optimize indoor energy use with secure window films that preserve your view.

Industrial Films Highly customisable, eco-friendly protective films suitable for packaging labels and shrink films.
