About Us

Our Company is engaged into plywood and lamination industry. Our Company is into trading of decorative laminates, acrylic sheets, printing paper (base), plywood and others. Additionally, to expand the business, our Company has entered into manufacturing of flush door and other customized products from the month of September 2023. Our Company also provides a one stop customized solutions i.e.; right from designing to manufacturing to supplying flush doors and laminated products. Our Company has a network of direct distributors and / or dealers across Maharashtra through whom it promotes market and sells its products. Additionally, our Company also sells its products through one of our franchisee located at Mumbai. We have our own in-house designing team who constantly keep themselves updated on developing the latest design as per the market demand. As on the date of the Draft Prospectus, our Company has a wide variety of portfolio for our products such as laminated sheets, acrylic sheets, etc.

Why Choose Us

Lamosaic India Limited is a leading provider of high-quality laminates designed to meet the diverse needs of our customers in the furniture, interior design, and construction industries. With a legacy of over 20 years in the business, we have established ourselves as a trusted source for premium laminates that enhance the aesthetic appeal and durability of various surfaces.

OUR COMPETITIVE STRENGTHS

Experienced Management & Dedicated Team

We have an experienced management team with our Managing Director having experience of more than 13+ years in Laminate industry. It is through the constant efforts and experience of our management team that we have been able to build a sustainable business model.

Diversified Products Portfolio

We believe our comprehensive range of products enables to capitalize on growth opportunities and demand in the Decorative Laminate industry. We offer all services at one stop - Right from Concept creation to designing to manufacturing to despatch.



In House Designers

Laminate industry is highly exposed to the risk of frequent change in customer preference for designs trends and obsolescence of designs. We insulate ourselves from these changes by keeping pace with changing preference trends and introducing new and innovative designs and products. Currently, we have over 200+ design for offering.

Niche Market Focus

Over a period of time, we have been able to carve out a niche segment for our products. We believe this is our strongest point which enables us to have a consistent business. This allows us for marketing to a specific audience leading to increased customer loyalty and improved margins.

Locational Advantage

We are strategically located and have our presence in Goregoan, Kandivali, and Chembur in Mumbai. We are also present in Pune. Being available at the centre of the city, our customers are able to reach us easily for personal discussion which is of utmost important at the time of customisation.

Quality Assurance

As an ISO certified company, our commitment to quality assurance is paramount. Our rigorous standards and continuous improvement processes ensure that every product and service we offer meets the highest levels of excellence.

OUR BUSINESS STRATEGY

  • Addition of Capacity

    Until recently, our manufacturing was outsourced on contract / job work. With a vision of providing all services under one roof, we recently installed our own machine at our factory in Chembur. Installed capacity that can manufacture doors will be between 6,500 to 7,000 doors on a monthly basis which is roughly valued at Rs 2cr per month.

  • To Continue Strengthening Our Reach to New Customers

    With a vision to expand and with newer capacity, we have planned on increasing our franchisee network. We will focus on expanding geographically outside Maharashtra.

  • To Continue adding more creative designs to our portfolio

    In order to maintain our competitive edge over our competitors, we will continue adding more design, textures, shades, etc to our portfolio.

  • Brand Building

    Currently, we do not have any registered brand. Going forward, we shall focus on brand building which will help us differentiate ourselves from our competitors. This is helps us increasing customer loyalty..
