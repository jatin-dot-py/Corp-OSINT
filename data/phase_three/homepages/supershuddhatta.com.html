Welcome to Super Bread

Bread Manufacturer & Wholesaler in Ahmedabad Since 1978

Super Bread is Ahmedabad's leading bread manufacturer and bulk bread supplier since 1978, serving hotels, restaurants, cafes, and distributors across Gujarat and Rajasthan. Established by Shri Shankarlal T. Ahuja, "Super Bread" has become synonymous with fresh, 100% eggless bread in Ahmedabad - commanding over 50% of the city's bread market.

Our wholesale bread supply network spans 100+ distributors and 5,000+ retail outlets across Ahmedabad and North Gujarat. Whether you need white bread, brown bread, burger buns, pav, or specialty breads in bulk - Super Bread delivers fresh every day from our Naroda factory.

Does Super Bread supply bread in bulk for restaurants and hotels?
Yes. Super Bread offers wholesale bulk supply for hotels, restaurants, cafes, canteens, and institutional buyers across Ahmedabad and Gujarat. Call +91 96876 05270 or use the bulk inquiry form to discuss minimum order quantities and wholesale pricing.
Is Super Bread 100% eggless?
Yes, all Super Bread products - including white bread, brown bread, burger buns, pav, and specialty breads - are 100% eggless and vegetarian. Super Bread is one of the few certified eggless bread manufacturers in Ahmedabad, making it ideal for vegetarian households, Jain consumers, and restaurants serving vegetarian menus.
What types of bread does Super Bread manufacture?
Super Bread manufactures white bread, brown bread, atta bread, multigrain bread, 7-grain bread, milk bread, crustless bread, double roti, burger buns, sweet buns, pav, bhaji pav, vada pav buns, pizza bread, garlic bread, kulcha bread, and creamy fill specialty breads. All products are 100% eggless and freshly baked daily in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
What is the difference between multigrain bread and brown bread?
Brown bread is made from partially refined wheat flour with added bran or colour, while multigrain bread contains multiple grains such as wheat, oats, ragi, and flaxseed - offering higher nutritional diversity. Multigrain bread generally has more fibre and protein than brown bread. Super Bread's 7-Grain Bread is a premium multigrain option available in Ahmedabad.
How long has Super Bread been in business?
Super Bread was established in 1978 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. With over 45 years of operations, it is one of Ahmedabad's oldest and most trusted bread manufacturers, holding approximately 50% market share in the Ahmedabad bread market and supplying through 100+ distributors to 5,000+ retail outlets.
Where can I buy Super Bread products?
Super Bread products are available at BigBasket, Blinkit, D-Mart, JioMart, Reliance Fresh, and Grofers across Ahmedabad. The brand is also distributed through 100+ distributors to approximately 5,000+ retail outlets in Ahmedabad and North Gujarat.
