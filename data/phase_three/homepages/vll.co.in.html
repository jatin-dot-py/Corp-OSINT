Welcome to Virat Leasing Limited

Virat Leasing Limited, a Company registered under the Companies Act, 1956 is engaged in the business of investing and dealing in shares and securities of other Companies. The Registered Office of the Company is situated at 1, Crooked Lane, 3rd Floor, Room No - 324, Kolkata – 700069, West Bengal and having its Corporate Office at “Jajodia Tower”, 3, Bentinck Street, 4th Floor, Room No. D – 8, Kolkata – 700001, West Bengal.

The company was incorporated on the 27th day of July, 1984 with registration certificate issued by the Registrar of Companies, Kolkata and got its Certificate of Commencement of Business on the 7th day of August, 1984.
The company is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange Limited (BSE).
