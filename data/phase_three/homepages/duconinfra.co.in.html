Who We Are

Ducon Infratechnologies Ltd. is an  India-based diversified Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) company operating at the intersection of fossil fuel clean technologies, infrastructure, and digital services. The company is India’s pioneer in Flue Gas Desulphurization (FGD) systems, having installed the country’s first Seawater FGD system (2007) and first Wet Limestone FGD system (2009), and has supplied FGD solutions to over thousands MW of combined power plant capacity across India. Beyond air pollution control, Ducon’s portfolio extends to bulk material handling systems, rural and urban electrification projects under government schemes, wet scrubbers, bag filters, and cyclones, as well as digital technologies, security surveillance, and digital distribution services. The company  helps utilities and industries meet stringent emission norms while  allowing them to maintain reliable and efficient power supply.

Ducon’s equipment is recognized for quality workmanship, rugged construction, long service life and high efficiency. Clients feel confident dealing with Ducon because they know that they are getting proven technologies at the lowest
cost. Ducon is a unique company  with its broad technology base and a diverse product line, enabling it to provide    single source responsibility on a wide variety of  turn-key projects in multiple industries. With strong engineering capabilities and project experience, Ducon acts as a single-source partner from design to commissioning and successful startup. We have created an enviable track record of successfully completed projects all across India.

FGD & Air Pollution Control Expertise

Ducon has developed and implemented technologies for seawater and limestone-based FGD systems as well as dry sorbent injection. Our installations serve large thermal power plants of upto 1000 MW single boilers, as well as industrial boilers, reducing SO₂ and particulate emissions in line with regulatory norms and enabling future integration with carbon capture where required.

Reference projects include large-capacity FGD systems at Indian power plants, along with multiple smaller systems deployed across sectors such as alumina, cement, oil and gas, and process industries.

Material Handling & Infrastructure

Beyond FGD, Ducon executes projects involving belt conveyors, pipe conveyors, pneumatic conveying, silo systems, air slides and related bulk material handling equipment. These solutions are deployed in power stations, ports, mines and industrial plants.

Rural electrification and infrastructure works complement our core offerings, aligning with India’s development programs and last-mile connectivity requirements.
