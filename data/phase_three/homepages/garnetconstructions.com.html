Legacy in Concrete

Garnet Construction Ltd. is a 3-decade-old real estate developer having a proven track record of client satisfaction. Our facets are visible across 3 strategic business units diversified into Residential, Industrial & Commercial business.

We are known as one of the most trustworthy real estate companies in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai & beyond, recognized for our quality and excellence in designing real estate projects.

We strongly believe in executing construction with our own funds, primarily because of our innovative approach to every real estate project. To know more about our company & our work, please visit our website www.garnetconstructions.com and www.avirarealty.com

About us

A journey beyond blueprints

Garnet's story began in 1995, with a simple but bold philosophy set by its founder, Mr. Kishan Kumar Kedia: “Build with your own capital, and build to last.” From developing industrial plots that powered early enterprises to shaping large residential townships and Grade-A office spaces, Garnet Construction’s journey has been defined by one constant – credibility.

While others relied on speculation and borrowed funds, Garnet took the harder route; self-funding every project, ensuring that each acre developed was free of financial uncertainty. Over three decades, this discipline has helped the company expand across 600+ acres, delivering homes, workplaces, and industrial ecosystems that have stood the test of time.

Today, under the stewardship of the second generation, Garnet continues to evolve, embracing modern design, integrated planning, and wellness-driven living, while staying true to its founding principles.
