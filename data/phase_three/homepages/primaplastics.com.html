About Prima

For over three decades, Prima Plastics Limited has been one of India’s leading manufacturers and exporters of high-quality plastic products. From our very first chair in 1993 to a diverse portfolio today, we continue to deliver solutions that combine innovation, durability, and trust.  

Our Global Presence 

With six state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities worldwide - Daman, Kerala, Ongole Cameroon, Guatemala - Prima serves diverse markets with global scale and local care. Our strong network ensures that our products are accessible across geographies. 

Our Commitment 

Certified with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and OHSAS 18001, and recognized as a One Star Export House by the Government of India, Prima is proud to be a trusted partner for both businesses and communities. Beyond manufacturing, we actively support initiatives in education, healthcare, and rural development, staying true to our responsibility towards society.
