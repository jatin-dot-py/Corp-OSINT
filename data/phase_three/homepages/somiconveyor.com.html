Decades of Conveyor Belt Expertise

Decades of Conveyor Belt Expertise

Somi Conveyor Belt Limited is a pioneering name in the Indian conveyor belt industry. Since our establishment in the 2000, we have been dedicated to manufacturing high-quality conveyor belts that power India's industrial growth.

As a publicly listed company, we maintain the highest standards of transparency and corporate governance. Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted partner for leading companies across mining, cement, steel, and power sectors.
