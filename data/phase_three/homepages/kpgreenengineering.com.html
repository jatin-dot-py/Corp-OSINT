W-Beam Crash Barrier H1 (TB42) Validation

KP Green Engineering achieves Tier-1 infrastructure compliance for high-speed road safety systems.

The W-Beam Crash Barrier H1 (TB42) has successfully concluded its rigorous validation at the National Automotive Test Tracks (NATRAX), achieving full compliance with the EN 1317-2 international standard.

Clearing this certification on the very first attempt underscores the advanced design and structural capability of our engineering division. This milestone validates KP Green Engineering’s ability to deliver high-performance safety infrastructure in an industry where multiple testing iterations are standard.

Conducted under the National Automotive Board and the Ministry of Heavy Industries, the validation process involved precise impact angles, speeds, and vehicle loads. Our crash barrier system demonstrated fully satisfactory performance in:

  • Effective vehicle containment
  • Controlled redirection
  • Reliable energy absorption

With validated results across car, bus, and truck impact categories, KP Green Engineering is now fully equipped to support major infrastructure bodies, including NHAI, PWD, and CPWD, for national highway and expressway development.

As India’s road infrastructure expands globally, our focus remains on delivering scalable, high-quality crash barrier solutions that ensure performance, safety, and reliability.

Our Pillars of Excellence

KP Green Engineering is undertaking a significant capacity expansion of 2.94 Lakh Metric Tons per annum at a new location in Matar Village, Bharuch District, strategically situated along the express highway. Spanning approximately ~45 acres, the facility will feature a state-of-the-art galvanizing plant equipped with Asia’s largest galvanizing kettle measuring 56 cubic meters, an upgrade from the current 17 cubic meters—and capable of handling 1,100 tons of zinc.

The plant will also incorporate advanced automatic CNC machines to manufacture engineering components for heavy fabrication works, while continuing the company’s extensive portfolio of 22 product offerings, including solar module mounting structures, wind lattice towers, metal beam crash barriers, pre-engineered buildings, cable trays, transmission towers, substations, and switchyards.

Frequently Asked Questions

There are four main types of transmission towers: suspension, tension, transposition, and terminal. All four of these serve specific roles in supporting transmission line towers for power evacuation and grid stability.
Monopole towers are more space-efficient and ideal for urban or rooftop areas, while lattice towers provide good strength and are widely used by telecom tower companies in India for ground-based installations.
We are among the best solar panel structure manufacturers in Gujarat, offering robust solar module mounting structures with superior galvanization, designed for rooftop, ground, and tracker-based solar systems.
An isolator in a substation, such as a double break isolator or tandem isolator, is a critical component used to safely disconnect equipment for maintenance and prevent unwanted current flow in switchyard structures.
We manufacture both ladder type cable trays and perforated cable trays, making us a trusted cable tray manufacturer in India for industrial and infrastructure wiring systems.
As one of the trusted crash barrier manufacturers, we produce galvanized safety barriers like W-beam and Thrie-beam crash barriers using stringent processes. These crash barriers are also used across highways and industrial zones to ensure vehicular safety.
The long-term corrosion protection provided by hot dip galvanizing dictates the life expectancy of transmission towers, PEB structures and exposed steel components in very aggressive conditions.
