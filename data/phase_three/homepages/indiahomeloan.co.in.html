🤝 OUR PROCESS

Transparent Steps to Make Homeownership a Reality

At IHLL, we follow a structured and customer-friendly loan process. Whether you're self-employed or salaried, our team ensures hassle-free approvals with minimal paperwork and maximum guidance.

Consultation

Our representative connects with you to understand your housing needs and eligibility, offering the right product suited to your income profile.

Apply Now

Submit your loan application with essential documents like ID proof, address proof, property papers, and income details — even informal income is considered.

Get Approved

Our credit team evaluates your profile and property documents. If everything checks out, you receive a sanction letter with your approved loan amount.

Disbursement

After legal and technical verification, your loan is disbursed directly to the builder or seller as per agreed terms — quickly and securely.
