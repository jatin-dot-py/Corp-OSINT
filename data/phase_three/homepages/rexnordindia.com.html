Welcome to REXNORD
REXNORD was Established in 1984 with Foreign Technical Collaboration with M/s. Micron Engineering Company, Korea.
Today REXNORD is self independent to develop inhouse BLDC Motor and Energy Saving Fan & Motor.
90% Production is inhouse to have consistency in quality & price competetiveness.
As on 2017, company is manufacturing 2.5 millions fans and motors and by 2020 company will be manufacturing 5 millions fans and motors.
