Who We Are

GCM Securities Ltd.

GCM Securities Limited was incorporated as a limited company in the year 1995. The Company became a Corporate Member of National Stock Exchange of India Limited under the Capital Market Segment in October 1995, became Self Clearing Member under F & O Segment in 2006 and took membership of BSE Limited in 2010.

The promoter of the company Shri Inder Chand Baid (FCA) has more than 40 years' experience as a practicing Chartered Accountant to his credit as well as in the financial sector.

Shri Manish Baid (A.C.A.) with experience of 14 years in financial sector and Shri Samir Baid (F.C.A.) with experience of 12 years in financial sector are also the Directors of the company.
