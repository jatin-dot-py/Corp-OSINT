About Paramount Cosmetics (I) Ltd

Paramount Cosmetics (I) Ltd has been a trusted name in the Indian traditional cosmetic segment since 1985.

We are grooming India with safe, hygienic and best in quality color cosmetics like bindis, kumkum and kajal under the well admired and popular brand name Shilpa.
