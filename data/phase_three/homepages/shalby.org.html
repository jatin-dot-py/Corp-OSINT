About US

Delivering Excellence in Healthcare Since 1994

From humble beginnings to building an empire, Shalby Ltd. is a story of commitment towards providing the best in healthcare. What began as a small orthopedic centre in 1994 with six beds, one operation theatre, and a small staff, is today a chain of 13 units present in 10 Indian cities across 6 states. The group also encompasses diverse business verticals, all dedicated towards transforming the medical landscape of India.
