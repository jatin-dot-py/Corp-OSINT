Who we are

Established in 1998, Net Avenue Inc. started out as a partnership firm between two aspiring individuals with the launch of one of the first Indian gifting portals, Chennaibazaar.com.

After considerable success offering gifting solutions to Indians living abroad, the company diversified its offerings beyond gifts to include Indian designer clothes and jewellery.
