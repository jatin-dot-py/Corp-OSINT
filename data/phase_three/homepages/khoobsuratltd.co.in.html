Welcome to Khoobsurat Limited

KHOOBRSURAT LIMITED was incorporated on 17th April 1982 in city of Kolkata, West Bengal. The Company's present Authorised Capital and Paid up Capital are ₹1500.00 Lakh and ₹ 1328.44 Lakh respectively.
The Face Value of Equity Shares is ₹ 1/- at present. In December 2015, the Company has sub-divided the Face Value of its Equity Shares from ₹ 10/- to ₹ 1/- per share.

The Company is into the business of Investment in Shares & Securities, listed and unlisted as well as doing financing activities; serving to both individuals and Corporate Entities.

Recent Developments

In December 2012, the Company has allotted Bonus Equity Shares in ratio of two Equity Shares for each one Equity Share held by Members and these shares have been listed on CSE prior to its listing on BSE and MSEI.

The Equity Shares of the Company have been listed on BSE under Direct Listing Norms. Apart from its listing on BSE & CSE, Shares of the Company are also listed on MSEI.
