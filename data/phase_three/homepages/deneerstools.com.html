A Heritage Of Breakthrough Innovation

DE NEERS TOOLS is an ISO 9001:2015 Certified and ISO 14001:2015 accredited Company. Today, we are the fourth-generation serving the Hand Tools Industry since 1951. Generation on generations, we have adopted new and improved modern systems and the best practices to satisfy our customers’ needs to the fullest.

DE NEERS is one of the leading hand tools suppliers in India. Our well-acclaimed Managing Director, Mr. Neeraj Kumar Aggarwal, with his committed and diligent workforce, has a focused and target-oriented approach towards meeting the customer requirements and ensuring the quality of their products. De Neers Tools, being the best hand tool suppliers, endeavour to offer the best quality products at the most competitive prices. We aim to provide hand tools of the utmost quality that are worth our customers’ money. We continuously work on innovation and research to develop products that can bring a kind of revolution in the hand tool industry.

De Neers is known as one of the reputed hand tools suppliers that provides the broadest range of hand tools in India. Our extensive range of products includes Spanners, Wrenches, Pliers, Cutters, Allen keys, Hammers, Socketry, Screw Drivers, Tool Kits, Tool Cabinets, Trolleys, etc. We are also specialized in providing Safety Tools like Non-Sparking Tools, Insulated Steel Tools, Non-Sparking Insulated Tools, Stainless Steel Anti-magnetic Tools, and Titanium Tools, with our range of hand tools.

We are counted among the leading hand tool distributors & hand tool exporters due to our consistent hard work to deliver quality products. We aim to provide professional hand tools of the utmost quality, made from German technology with the essence of “Make in India.” Our R&D department has experienced and dedicated personnel that keeps a regular check on the latest trends and opportunities in the market and focuses on improving the design and structure of the products. Our focus is on producing tools that can stand up to the demands of the professionals who use them every day. De Neers is famous for providing the broadest range of products to its customers in India, making us the best hand tool suppliers.

When it comes to increasing workplace safety in different industrial environments, our tools act as support. These tools find their applications in Aerospace, Marine, Petrochemical, Medical, Defence, and Nuclear industries where anti-magnetic, anti-corrosive tools are mandatory for ensuring users’ safety.

Conventional Steel Tools can generate a spark and cause an explosion or fire. Thus, Non-Sparking Hand Tools, also sometimes referred to as anti-spark, safety tools, or sparkless tools, must be used to ensure safety.

Our DE NEERS team welcomes you all with keen interest for a long-lasting association.
