Company Profile

Lotus Chocolates is among the early contributors to India’s chocolate and cocoa-based products industry.

We offer a thoughtfully developed range designed to meet evolving consumer preferences,adhering to all applicable food safety standards. We cater comprehensively to both individual consumers and leading confectionery brands alike.

Operated from a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility, all our products unwaveringly prioritise and promise excellence. We consistently set benchmarks in taste, purity and craftsmanship, ensuring every product delivers a celebratory experience.

OUR LEGACY

Business Verticals

Institutional Business (B2B)

All institutional Lotus products serve as reliable and long-standing partners for many bakery and food manufacturing businesses across the country. We provide high quality compound and couverture products, cocoa derivatives, decoratives, and specialised ingredients.

Our products are meticulously designed to meet the rigorous demands of large-scale production, designed to support and deliver both taste and consistency.

From bakeries and confectioners to hotels and diverse food manufacturers, our customisable solutions, robust supply chain, and technical expertise establish us as a trusted partner for brands seeking to elevate their offerings with superior cocoa-based confectionery.

Consumer Business (B2C)

At Lotus Chocolate, we believe our products transcend being mere treats, and should create many moments of joy while being enjoyed and shared with loved ones.

Our wide and wonderful range is crafted to delight every palate and occasion.

Whether for a moment of personal indulgence, shared enjoyment with family and friends, or a special celebration, our consumer-facing confectionery delivers the perfect fusion of taste, texture, and memorability.
