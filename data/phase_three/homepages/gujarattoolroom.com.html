About our Company

Building a more competitive business sectors.

Gujarat Toolroom Limited (GTL) is a dynamic, BSE-listed industrial conglomerate dedicated to fueling the global economy through a diverse portfolio of high-growth sectors. From our heritage in precision engineering to our modern-day leadership in Mining, Green Energy, and International Commodity Trading, we bridge the gap between natural resources and global markets. With strategic operations spanning from the mineral-rich belts of Zambia to the premier trading hubs of Dubai, GTL delivers unmatched reliability and technical expertise across every project we undertake.
