About us

LEADING GLOBAL AUTO COMPONENTS AND SYSTEMS MANUFACTURER

Uno Minda Limited is a leading global manufacturer and supplier of innovative automotive components and systems for OEMs. We have made important contribution to the automotive industry supply chain for more than six decades with our innovative products. Incepted in 1958, We are one of the leading manufacturers of automotive switching systems, automotive lighting systems, automotive acoustics systems, automotive seating systems and alloy wheels.
