The Company

Dutron Group was founded in 1962 and has been continuously growing since then. Dutron is pioneer of PVC hose pipes in India. Beginning with a modest plastic pipe manufacturer, Dutron went on to procure world specialist Kanaflex PVC hose pipe manufacturing know-how.
