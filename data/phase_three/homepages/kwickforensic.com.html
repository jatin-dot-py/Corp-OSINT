About Us

Who We Are

Kwick Forensic is focused on modernization of Police Departments involved in crime scene investigations and specialises in evidence identification, collection, digitization using scientific kits and user friendly handheld devices.

Supply of fully equipped Micro Mini Mobile C.S.I units and Mini Mobile C.S.I. vehicles, setting up of “Centre for Excellence” at Police Training Institutes and to adopt best practices in total evidence management. We also offer upgradation of Forensic Science Labs, Fingerprint Bureau, Digital Forensics (Cyber Crime), DNA Labs.

Objective

Standardization, Strengthening and Synergizing Law Enforcement with justice delivery system. To minimize the time period to conclude a court case from existing over 15 years to the shortest possible time.

Our Vision

Our Vision is to equip all A grade Police Station across India, all districts to be ready with basic investigations products and to provide authenticated test reports of evidences. To create smart police stations. To provide job opportunities to young graduates from forensic background who could perform a professional crime scene investigation. To equip the state Forensic Science Laboratory, upgrade Fingerprint Bureau, Cyber/Digital Forensic Labs, DNA labs with qualified lab technicians and enable to submit test reports of all evidences within the stipulated time.

Our Mission

Mission Forensic 2028 :
  • Reduce Pending Evidences at the Government Forensic Science Labs, Reduce Court Cases.
  • Minimize Manual Processes & Focus on Digitization Processes from Crime Scene to Court.
  • Shift from CAPEX to OPEX or Transform to Privatization –Faster Results at Minimum Cost.
  • Enhance Responsibilities at Police Stations on Investigation Officers, Provide Forensic Experts.
  • Actively Educate Judges on Forensic Sciences & Adopt Using AI & ML Assisting Judges to Decision Making leading to Conclude court cases, unbiased, fair and much faster.

The Company

With over Five decades of experience in Manufacturing of Consumer and Industrial Electronics specializing in Transformers, Voltage Stabilizers and various Power Supplies, the founder(s) had started as Kwick Soft Solutions Private Limited in 2005 focusing on advanced software development, animation and simulation and later transitioned into forensic science solutions providing Total Evidence Management and Law Enforcement related Products and Solutions offering World Class Technologies at All India Level.
