Our Profile
Indian Renewable Energy
Development Agency Ltd.
Indian Renewable Energy Development Agency Limited (IREDA) is a ‘Navratna’ Government of India Enterprise under the administrative control of Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE). IREDA is a Public Limited Government Company established as a Non-Banking Financial Institution in 1987 engaged in promoting, developing and extending financial assistance for setting up projects relating to new and renewable sources of energy and energy efficiency/conservation with the motto: “ENERGY FOR EVER”
