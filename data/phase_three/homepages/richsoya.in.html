Our Journey

Incorporated

The company was incorporated on 28th February 2003 in Maharashtra, under the name of Shri Venkatesh Refineries Private Limited (SVRPL).

Factory Setup

Started Production at the factory

Setup Plant

Set-up of the Lecithin Plant

Green Initiative

Installation of the Solar power plant for captive consumption

Plant Expansion

Increase in the installed capacity from 17,310 TPA to 36,000 TPA for refining edible oil

Conversion Of Company

Company changed it’s name Shri Venkatesh Refineries Limited consequent upon conversion from Private Ltd to Public.

Horizontal Expansion

Introduced Sunflower oil under the brand name Rich Sun.

Listed On BSE SME

Listing on BSE SME Exchange on 11 Oct 2021
