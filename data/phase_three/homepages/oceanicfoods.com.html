Over 50 Years of Excellence in Food Processing

A proud legacy of leadership and excellence in dehydrated onion, garlic, and vegetable production - serving global markets with quality and trust since 1972.

Oceanic Foods Limited stands as India’s leading manufacturer and exporter of premium-quality dehydrated spices and vegetables, renowned for its commitment to excellence and consistency. Built on a strong foundation of innovation, integrity, and internationally recognized quality standards, we have grown into a trusted name serving over 50 countries across the globe. Every product we create reflects our passion for purity, sustainability, and precision - from sourcing the finest raw materials to using state-of-the-art dehydration technology that preserves natural flavor, color, and nutrition. Our dedication to continuous improvement and customer satisfaction has made Oceanic Foods the preferred partner of top chefs, global food brands, and culinary professionals who seek reliability, taste, and excellence in every ingredient.

Proprietary 12-Step
Processing Excellence

Advanced dehydration technology ensuring superior quality across all our premium product lines.

Step 01

Premium Raw Material Selection

Certified suppliers provide premium vegetables and spices that undergo rigorous quality assessment protocols.

Step 02

Advanced Pre-Cleaning

Controlled washing systems remove impurities while preserving natural characteristics and nutritional integrity.

Step 03

Precision Sorting & Grading

Meticulous manual sorting in hygienic environments eliminates defects and ensures uniform quality standards.

Step 04

Automated Peeling & Trimming

State-of-the-art mechanical systems remove outer layers while maintaining product integrity and maximizing yield.

Step 05

Multi-Stage Washing

Filtered water systems eliminate contaminants and surface microbes while preserving essential flavor compounds.

Step 06

Precision Cutting & Sizing

Advanced cutting systems create uniform specifications ensuring consistent dehydration and optimal final texture.

Step 07

Controlled Pre-Treatment

Specialized pre-treatment optimizes enzyme activity and preserves natural flavor profiles during dehydration.

Step 08

Primary Dehydration

Controlled temperature dehydration chambers remove moisture while preserving nutritional value and flavor intensity.

Step 09

12-Layer Magnetic Purification

Proprietary magnetic separation system removes metallic impurities followed by meticulous manual inspection.

Step 10

Advanced Metal Detection

Sophisticated detection systems ensure complete absence of foreign materials guaranteeing product safety.

Step 11

Comprehensive Quality Testing

FSSAI-compliant laboratory testing ensures every batch meets international standards for safety and quality.

Step 12

Climate-Controlled Packaging

Products are packaged in controlled environments using premium materials to preserve quality and freshness.
