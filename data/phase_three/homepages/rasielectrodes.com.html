Welcome to Rasi Electrodes

Rasi Electrodes Limited is an ISO 9001:2015 Certified Company based at Chennai, India.

The Company is a specialist in manufacturing high quality WELDING CONSUMABLES catering to the requirements of diverse Heavy Engineering and Light Engineering industries. The Company is one of the preferred suppliers of WELDING CONSUMABLES under the brand name RASI for reputed public sector undertakings and larger private sector engineering conglomerates and other Original Equipment Manufacturers.
