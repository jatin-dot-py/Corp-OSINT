About Company

Captain Technocast Limited (CTL) is a leading manufacturer and supplier of investment casting in India. Established in the year 2010, our company is part of the diversified business group “Captain” and listed on Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). We have a state-of-the art facility with a production capacity of 2400 MT castings per annum.

Our plant is equipped with in-house machine workshop to provide end to end solution to customers for the most complex and critical component requirements.
