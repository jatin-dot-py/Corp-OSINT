About Us

Over Three Decades of Expertise. One Commitment: Quality That Moves Healthcare Forward.

Established in 1991, Lactose India Limited has grown over three decades into a trusted name in the pharmaceutical industry, specializing in pharmaceutical-grade excipients and APIs, including our new range of Speciality Grade Lactose.

With a strong focus on innovation and technical expertise, we develop customised solutions for evolving industry needs and provide globally competitive alternatives to support our customers' formulation and sourcing requirements.

Our dedicated excipient manufacturing site in Vadodara, India, has an installed capacity of 10,000 MT per Annum, with an additional dedicated facility of the same capacity being developed at Surat, India. These facilities are complemented by specialized dedicated manufacturing sites for Spray Dried, Anhydrous, and Inhalation Grade Lactose, designed and operated in accordance with stringent ICH guidelines.

Our state-of-the-art Bulk API facility is dedicated to the manufacturing of Lactulose API. We are progressively expanding our manufacturing capacity from the current 2,500 MT per Annum to 3,500–5,000 MT per Annum, through a phased expansion program.

With over three decades of experience, quality-driven manufacturing and continuous innovation, Lactose India Limited continues to deliver reliable, customised and future-ready solutions for the global pharmaceutical industry.

Lactulose

Lactose India has again pioneered the manufacturing of Disaccharide Lactulose, a versatile bulk API. It is a one of a kind plant in the whole of Asia with a capacity to manufacture 2400 MT / Annum. Lactulose is used in the treatment of Constipation, Hepatitis, Encephalopathy, Intestinal Microflora, Dental Care, Diabetes, Chronic Liver Disease & as an effective Prebiotic.
