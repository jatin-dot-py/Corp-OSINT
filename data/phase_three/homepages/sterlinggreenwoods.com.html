Wel Come To Sterling Greenwoods Lake Resort

Vision

To be a leading leisure entertainment and real estate Company of first choice amongst our customers to address their needs for leisure and entertainment at various locations.

Mission

To satisfy every customer’s need for a better experience through quality offerings and employee satisfaction.

Core Values

Value for Money
To offer the best quality at the most competitive rates.

Customer Satisfaction
To ensure our customers are a satisfied lot and come back to us for all their realty & entertainment needs.

Transparency
To ensure we are honest, fair, forthright and ethical in all that we do.

With the inherent philosophy of creating spaces that bring heartfelt joy, five decades ago, in the year 1965, Sterling Group started realizing people’s dreams by developing realty that truly brought peace of mind to them.

The firm was founded by Mr B. Kumar, a civil engineer, having extensive experience in developing major infrastructure projects for the Central Government. He worked on the design and construction of river valley projects, bridges, industrial buildings, plotted developments etc. He was also the founding president of Ahmedabad’s oldest and most reputed Club – The Rajpath Club.

Established as a partnership firm, the company was converted into a public limited entity in 1992 with a paid up capital of 4.46 cr. The Group is managed by a board comprising eminent & qualified engineers, architects and management experts.
