Lumax Auto Technologies Limited (LATL), a key company under the Lumax Group, began its journey in 1981 with its first manufacturing facility in Maharashtra, producing lighting solutions for two-wheelers. Over the decades, LATL has grown organically and strategically, transforming into one of India’s leading integrated auto component manufacturers.
Today, Lumax Auto Technologies caters to two, three, and four-wheeler segments, offering a wide-ranging portfolio through its subsidiaries and strategic partnerships. The company is recognised for its strong R&D capabilities, cutting-edge design, and advanced manufacturing excellence.
As of the end of FY 2025, LATL reported net revenues of ₹3,637 Cr, backed by a diversified product suite that includes, Two-wheeler lighting, chassis & swing arms, Three-wheeler trailing arms, Gear shifters & transmission systems, Intake systems & integrated plastic modules, Oxygen sensors, seat structures, kinematic components, Telematics products and services, antenna systems, power window switches, Interior systems, green and alternate fuel solutions, and aftermarket offerings.
With a legacy of innovation, Lumax Auto Technologies continues to power the future of mobility, designed, engineered, and made in India.

About the
Company

Lumax Auto Technologies Limited (LATL), a proud member of the Lumax-DK Jain Group, is a trusted name in India’s automotive component manufacturing landscape. Headquartered in Gurugram, Haryana, LATL brings over 40 years of industry experience, serving leading OEMs and the Aftermarket with precision-engineered solutions.

With a strong foundation built on innovation, quality, and customer trust, LATL operates with 30 manufacturing facilities across 7 states, 2 advanced Engineering Centres, 9 global partnerships, and 1 technical agreement, and a team of over 7,000 dedicated professionals. Backed by a pan-India marketing and service presence, LATL continues to lead the way in driving India's mobility future forward.
