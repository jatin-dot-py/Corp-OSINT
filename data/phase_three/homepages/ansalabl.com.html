Our Legacy

Vikas Minar

Vikas Minar is a 23-storey building in Delhi that houses offices of the Delhi Development Authority. Built in 1976, it was designed by Habib Rahman and was at completion the first building in Delhi to surpass the Qutb Minar in height.

Statesman House

Statesman House is a prominent building located in Connaught Place, New Delhi, India. It is one of the iconic landmarks of the city and serves as the headquarters of The Statesman, a well-known English-language newspaper in India. The building's architecture reflects the colonial style that is characteristic of many structures in the Connaught Place area.

Prakash Deep

Prakash Deep Building is one of the many structures within Connaught Place and serves as an office and commercial space for various businesses.

Antriksh Bhavan

Antrish Bhavan is famous for its Revolving Restaurant on the 24th Floor (G + 23rd) at a height of 238 feet, situated at 22, K.G. Marg, New Delhi.

Ambadeep Building

Ambadeep Building is a Standalone Commercial Building at K.G. Marg in Connaught Place, Delhi with a total built-up area of 246000 sq. ft., Built in 1985, Typical floor area 11182 Sq. ft., Parking Ratio 1:2500
