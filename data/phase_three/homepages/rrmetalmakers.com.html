Welcome To RR MetalMakers

RR MetalMakers formerly known as (Shree Surgovind Tradelink Limited) is primarily an import export company. It was founded in the year 1995 and continues to successfully cater to a wide spectrum of clientele across diverse industries. The company's business portfolio mainly comprises of Mild Steel, trading of high quality spices, cereals and other similar specialty commodities from across the globe.

The company has a very strong penetration across the length and breadth of India and abroad. This has further accentuated to help it scale larger boundaries and grow a strong customer and supplier base in over 25 countries across the globe. Encouraged by this exemplary response and success, today, the company has also successfully forayed into exports of infrastructure items and many other commodities in alignment with this industry.
