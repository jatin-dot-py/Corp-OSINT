About
HARYANA

HARYANA, a joint venture with ICAP-SIRA ITALY, having technical alliance with VISMON –SPAIN is an integrated research and manufacturing facility for chemicals for leather, textile, footwear and PVC processing. HARYANA's products are not commodities – these are specialties for a high value addition in their respective customer segment.


An intensive research on product application with emphases on safety and environmental responsibility supported by ISO 9001:2015 & ISO 14001:2015 Quality and Environment System has enabled HARYANA carve its niche in highly competitive markets.
