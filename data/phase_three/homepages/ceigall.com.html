Company Overview

Ceigall India Limited stands as a leading infrastructure construction firm specializing in Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) and Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) projects.

With over 23 years of experience, Ceigall has evolved from a modest beginning into one of the fastest-growing EPC and HAM companies in the nation, renowned for delivering complex and large-scale projects. Our comprehensive project management, coupled with superior quality standards and innovation, has established us as the most reliable partner in shaping India’s infrastructure landscape.

Learn More

Our Expertise

We specialize in delivering intricate projects across a wide range of infrastructure, including elevated roads, highways, expressways, railway overbridges, flyovers, bridges, airports, tunnels, metro, solar, specialized infrastructure, water/irrigation, and utility shifting solutions.

Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) Projects

Ceigall is renowned for its expertise in managing every phase of Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) projects, from design and engineering to construction and completion. Our precision-driven approach guarantees timely delivery and superior quality, consistently meeting client expectations.

Learn More

Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) Projects

We manage the construction, operation, and maintenance of crucial Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) projects across India, supported partially by financial backing and in collaboration with the National Highway Authority of India. With our expertise we aim to elevate India’s infrastructure to the highest quality standards.

Learn More

Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Projects

We excel in Operation and Maintenance (O&M) projects, managing infrastructure assets and incident management with exceptional proficiency. Ceigall has a successful track record in supervising O&M responsibilities across EPC and HAM projects, as well as independent ventures, ensuring operational excellence and long-term asset sustainability.
