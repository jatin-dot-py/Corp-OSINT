Our Expertise

At Solex Energy Limited, we are dedicated to leading the renewable energy sector by harnessing the power of solar to build a greener world. Specializing in sustainable, cost-effective solutions for commercial, and industrial clients, we combine innovation with advanced technologies to drive global zero-carbon development. As a trusted partner in clean energy, we are committed to paving the way for a sustainable future.

Solar Technology Innovation

Driven by innovation, we develop advanced solar technologies like Mono PERC and Bifacial modules to ensure maximum energy efficiency and performance across diverse applications.

Quality Manufacturing

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing processes meet the highest international standards, ensuring reliable, durable, and high-performance solar panels.

EPC Management

We offer end-to-end EPC services, ensuring seamless project execution from concept to delivery with engineering precision and on-time completion.

Customization & Scalability

Our flexible, customer-centric approach delivers tailored solar solutions, scalable for projects of any size, from residential installations to industrial solar farms.

Our Team

Dr. Chetan Shah

Chairman & Managing Director, Solex Energy Limited

Dr. Chetan Shah is a visionary and policy-driven leader in India’s renewable energy sector, serving as the Chairman and Managing Director of Solex Energy Limited. Under his leadership, Solex Energy has emerged as a credible solar module manufacturer and clean energy solutions provider, closely aligned with India’s national priorities on sustainability, energy security, and self-reliance. Dr. Shah has consistently focused on strengthening domestic solar manufacturing, advancing high-efficiency technologies, and scaling clean energy adoption across utility-scale, commercial, and rooftop segments. He places strong emphasis on corporate governance, transparency, and long-term stakeholder value, ensuring that growth is balanced with responsibility. With a clear commitment to innovation and environmental stewardship, Dr. Shah continues to shape Solex Energy’s trajectory while contributing meaningfully to India’s transition toward a resilient, low-carbon energy future.

Powering Progress, Preserving Heritage

PM Shri Narendra Modi’s vision for Tribal India is to empower tribal communities with education, healthcare, and livelihoods while preserving their rich heritage, ensuring their holistic participation in a modern, developed nation.

At Solex Energy, we are turning this vision into action.

By training tribal youth, equipping them with employable skills, and welcoming them into our workforce, we are not just creating jobs—we are creating futures.

This journey brings honor, respect, financial independence, and exposure to the modern world, helping them expand their horizons while staying rooted in their values.

From energy that powers homes to opportunities that empower people—this is the Solex Energy Saga
