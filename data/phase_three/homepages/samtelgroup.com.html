About Samtel

Samtel is a five-decade old Group with a multi-dimensional presence in various domains including Defence & Avionics, Railways and Education.

The Group has a well-established history of being India’s largest integrated manufacturer of a wide range of displays for consumer electronics, television, industrial, medical and professional applications. In the last decade, Samtel has been active in high-technology products for a variety of applications – including Defence and Railways.
