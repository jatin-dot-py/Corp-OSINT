About Samyak International Limited

Samyak International Ltd. (Formerly known as McSure Capital Market Ltd.) a Public Limited Company (BSE: SAMYAKINT), was incorporated in 1994 with its Registered Office in Indore. Company is engaged mainly in Import & trading of a wide range of Petroleum & Bitumen Based Products and the manufacturing of Flexible Packaging for FMCG Companies. Our products are widely recognised in the industry for their quality and efficiency. We have defined quality and performance standards in the fields of Marketing of petroleum products. Our developments have ushered in a new era that has set high standards for achieving customer delight.

Our Businesses

Flexible Packaging

Samyak International Ltd is a manufacturer of Flexible Packaging Material for eminent FMCG companies in Central & North India. With a capacity of manufacturing 200MT/month, SIL operates it’s manufacturing unit in Pithampur, Madhya Pradesh, with state-of-the-art, food-grade infrastructure.
Our Product Range includes Roll Form and Pouch Form Laminates of upto 3 Layers including Standup Zipper Pouches, Quad Seal Pouches, Side & Centre Seal Pouches.

Bitumen & Petroleum Products

We are leading Importer and suppliers of an extensive range of Petroleum Based Products. Our products are processed using crude petroleum that make them highly effective and pure. Further, these are processed in various specifications to suit the requirement of the clients. Bitumen products are served to various infrastructure and paint corporations.
