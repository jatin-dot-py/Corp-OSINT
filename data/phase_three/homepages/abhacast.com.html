About Company

Power and Steel Casting

We are located strategically in mineral-rich central India, particularly in Chhattisgarh, specializing in iron and steel foundry. Our focus is on casting and manufacturing customized products across a wide range of materials including mild steel, spheroidal graphite cast iron, manganese steel, stainless steel, low alloy and high alloy castings (high CR & high Ni), HRCS & WRCS. Our products range in size from 0.5 Kgs to 6 Tonnes per single finished casting.
We are certified by RDSO for supplying specific casting products to Indian Railways, and approved by National Mineral Development Corporation and Integral Coach Factory, Chennai, for certain casting products. Additionally, we hold a PED Certificate from TUV-Nord, ensuring our quality management system meets European standards for manufacturing castings, qualifying us to supply pressure equipment to European nations.
