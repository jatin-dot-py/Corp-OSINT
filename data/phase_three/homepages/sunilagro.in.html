WELCOME TO SUNIL AGRO FOODS LIMITED

About Us

Sunil Agro Foods Limited is a Flagship company belongs to B. Shanthilal Group of Brindavan Roller flour mills & Belgaum Roller flour mills Pvt.Ltd having a turnover of around Rs.700.00 crore and holds good market share in Karnataka. The company is in the manufacture of Wheat products namely Maida (Wheat Flour), Sooji (Semolina).

Products

MAIDA: Used in bread, biscuits, pasta preparations. SOOJI: Used in Hotel and household daily consumption. ATTA: Used in Hotel and Household daily consumption. BRAN: Used in Cattle and Poultry feeds. SPECIALITY: Producing to suit the customer’s requirement. The company has Highly Respected Corporate-Institutional.

OUR MISSION

WE AT SUNIL AGRO ARE DEDICATED TO OUR GROWTH WITH THE SATISFACTION OF OUR CUSTOMERS THROUGH QUALITY

THE COMPANY’S VISION IS TO PRODUCE QUALITY PRODUCTS TO SATISFY THE CUSTOMERS.
