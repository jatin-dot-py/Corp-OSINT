About Us

Alpa Laboratories Limited is a leading pharmaceutical manufacturer based in Pigdamber, near Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. Established in 1967, the company operates a WHO-GMP and ISO-certified manufacturing facility and has built a strong reputation for delivering high-quality pharmaceutical products for over five decades.

The company manufactures a wide range of ethical, generic, and over-the-counter (OTC) medicines under its own brands as well as for several reputed Indian and multinational pharmaceutical companies through contract manufacturing partnerships. Its product portfolio includes tablets, capsules, injections, syrups, ointments, creams, eye drops, and other dosage forms for both human and veterinary healthcare.

With a strong focus on quality, innovation, and regulatory compliance, Alpa Laboratories is committed to providing safe, effective, and affordable healthcare solutions. Its dedication to excellence and customer satisfaction has earned the trust of healthcare professionals, distributors, and business partners across domestic and international markets.

Product Development Process

Analytical Development

Structured transfer of product and process knowledge to manufacturing, supported by complete technical documentation.

Sterile Manufacturing

Our parenteral manufacturing operations are carried out in controlled sterile environments with automated systems for ampoule and vial filling, rubber bunging and cap sealing. Similar controlled processes are employed for dry powder injections. These capabilities support the manufacture of a broad range of sterile pharmaceutical formulations while maintaining stringent process controls.

Advanced Controlled Manufacturing Environment

The facility is WHO-GMP certified and incorporates independent air-supply systems and dedicated Air Handling Units for individual manufacturing departments.
