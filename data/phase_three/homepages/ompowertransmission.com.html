Director's Message

The global energy landscape is undergoing a fundamental transformation, with India at the forefront, driven by rising electricity demand and the rapid shift toward renewable energy. At OPTL, we recognize that robust power infrastructure is critical to enabling this transition, especially as renewable sources located in remote regions require seamless grid integration. With our integrated EPC capabilities spanning design, engineering, procurement and execution, and operation and maintenance, we are well-positioned to deliver end-to-end solutions and capitalize on growing investments in grid modernization and high-capacity networks. Our expanding order book, wider geographical presence, and successful execution of complex EPC projects highlight our resilience and progress.

Kanubhai Patel Managing Director

Our journey since inception has been guided by a vision to emerge as a leading power transmission company through high-quality and sustainable power infrastructure solutions. We have built a strong foundation driven by consistent growth, operational excellence, and strong core values. The Company has delivered steady financial performance and profitability, reflecting robust execution capabilities. As we move forward, our focus remains on long-term value creation, strengthening stakeholder trust, and contributing meaningfully to the nation’s power infrastructure. Aligned with our vision and mission, we are committed to innovation, sustainability, and excellence as we support India’s evolving power needs and future growth.

Kalpeshbhai Patel Chairman & Executive Director

At OPTL, execution excellence drives everything we do. We deliver complex power transmission projects with precision, reliability, and strict adherence to timelines. We manage the entire project lifecycle from design, engineering and route planning to erection and commissioning ensuring seamless delivery even in challenging environments. Our experience spans technically demanding projects, including pile foundations, specialized tower structures, and critical infrastructure with stringent quality standards. Backed by strong project management and a skilled team, we consistently exceed expectations. As the sector evolves, we remain committed to advancing our capabilities, adopting new technologies, and delivering projects that set benchmarks in quality, safety, and performance.

Vasantkumar Patel Whole-time Director
