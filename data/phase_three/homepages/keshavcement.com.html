Welcome to
Shri Keshav Cement and Infra Limited

Cement - Keshav Cement and Jyoti Cement are two reputed brands in North Karnataka and Goa. With modern infrastructure, the plant produces high-quality OPC 43 grade, OPC 53 grade, PPC and PSC cements. Our brands are well-recognized and widely used in Southwestern part of India.

​

The Management has over 100 years cumulative experience and is currently expanding the cement production capacity. The new capacity will go on stream by the year 2024. Keshav Cement has in house R & D team to continually improve the product and services.

​

Keshav Cement is probably the only cement plant in the country to run 100% on green power energy.

​

Power – SKCIL has commissioned 37 MWp of Solar Power at Koppal in Karnataka. The power is evacuated at 110 KV power line to the nearest substation. Part of the power generated is for self-consumption and rest being sold to major industries and commercial malls.

​

The management’s main motto is to increase shareholder wealth by continually improving quality, modernizing and running plants efficiently. The management at Katwa Group is very Eco-conscious and strives for great environment for our future generation.

​

The company is listed on a major stock exchange in India.
