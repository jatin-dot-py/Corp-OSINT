Help Centre

Frequently asked questions

Licence, charging, range, warranty and service — answered.

Do I need a licence or registration for a Tunwal scooter?+

No. Every Tunwal model is a low-speed EV (max 25 km/h, Non-RTO), so none require a driving licence or vehicle registration — anyone in the family can ride.

What is the range on a single charge?+

Range varies by model and battery: entry models deliver 50–60 km, mid-range models 60–70 km, and top-range lithium-ion variants (Alfa Pro, Lithino Pro) achieve 90–100 km on a single charge.

Can I charge it at home on a regular socket?+

Yes. Every Tunwal comes with a charger that plugs into any standard Indian household socket (5A or 15A). A lithium-ion charge takes around 4–5 hours and typically costs ₹8–12 in electricity — far cheaper than filling a petrol tank.

How much does it cost to run?+

Just ₹0.15 per km on electricity — compared with ₹3–4 per km on petrol. A typical commuter riding 30 km a day saves over ₹30,000 a year by switching to Tunwal.

What warranty do I get?+

Every Tunwal comes with a 1-Year Comprehensive Warranty covering manufacturing defects in the vehicle and core electrical components, backed by 400+ authorised dealers and service centres nationwide.

Where can I buy and service my Tunwal?+

Through our 400+ authorised dealers across 19 states. Use the Dealer Locator to find the nearest showroom or service centre — just enter your city or PIN code.
