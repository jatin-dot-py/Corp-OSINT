About Us

Established in the year 1992, Yash Group is an established player involved in education, consultancy, fire and safety, infrastructure and real estate projects. 'Yash Innoventure' is a symbol of quality. Today, we are widely regarded as an entity of considerable repute and goodwill.

Since more than a decade, the group is active in the Education and Real Estate Sector which has contributed first of its kind in Fire Education called College of Safety and Fire Technology. The other areas also upcoming are our Consultancy Services, Infrastructure Services, Financial Services and Real Estate. Yash Innoventure has successfully worked with government and semi-government agencies like Urban Department, Revenue Department, Town Planning, Education Department and lot of Semi-Government Department as well as Industries in Government and also Private Sector.
