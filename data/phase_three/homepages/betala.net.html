Welcome to Betala Global Securities Limited

Betala Global Securities Limited (“BGSL”) was incorporated on May 23, 1995 as a Private Limited Company and subsequently converted into a Public Limited Company on February 13, 1996.

BGSL is engaged in providing advisory services in mergers and acquisitions and undertaking investments in the capital market. The Company focuses on delivering strategic insights and guidance to support informed decision-making.

The Company follows a client-focused approach with an emphasis on long-term value creation, prudent investment practices, and maintaining high standards of ethics and professionalism.
