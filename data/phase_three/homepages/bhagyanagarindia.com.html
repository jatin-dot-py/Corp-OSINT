About Bhagyanagar india

Progress With
Integrity and Purpose

Since its inception in 1985, Bhagyanagar India Ltd. has evolved from a pioneer in copper manufacturing to a diversified industrial powerhouse. Backed by three generations of visionary leadership, the company has consistently delivered value through its world-class copper products, renewable energy ventures, and ethical business practices.

With a strong presence in OEM supply chains and a commitment to innovation, Bhagyanagar continues to shape the future of India’s industrial landscape — responsibly, sustainably, and with unmatched credibility.
