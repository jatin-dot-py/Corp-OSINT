About Sparc

About Us

Sun Pharma Advanced Research Company Limited (SPARC) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company that focuses on developing innovative therapeutics to improve standards of care for patients worldwide. At SPARC, our therapeutic focus is in the area of oncology and immunology two critical fields that pose significant challenges in healthcare. 

Read More

Therapeutic Focus

Oncology

We pioneer precision medicines and targeted delivery to overcome treatment resistance and achieve lasting remission.

Immunology

Auto-immune diseases are a group of complex and diverse conditions in which the immune system mistakenly attacks the body’s healthy cells and tissues.
