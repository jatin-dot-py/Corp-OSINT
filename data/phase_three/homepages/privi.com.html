ABOUT US

Privi Speciality Chemicals Limited is India's leading manufacturer, supplier and exporter of aroma and fragrance chemicals and a globally trusted partner and a preferd supplier of bulk aroma chemicals.
Privi achieved 2.60 million safe man hours as on March 31, 2026 and is the recipient of prestigious awards such as ‘Certificate of Merit’ received from the National Safety Council of India and National Safety Council, Maharashtra Chapter in recognition of appreciable achievement in Occupational Safety & Health in chemical and fertilizer industries group under the manufacturing sector category. Privi also received the "Rotary Water Award" for the exemplary work in water conservation during the Water Festival - 2017 held at Pune.

Our Journey

Privi started manufacturing aroma chemicals in the year 1992 with only two products, which it gradually expanded to a range of over 75+ products today, having a capacity of over 48,000 tons per annum. Privi also develops and produces custom-made aroma chemicals as per specific requirement of the customer. The research specialists at in-house R&D center continuously thrive to develop new products and processes.

Privi Speciality Chemicals Limited Awarded Platinum Medal by EcoVadis for Sustainability Excellence

Navi Mumbai, June 26, 2025 – Privi Speciality Chemicals Limited (“Privi” or “the Company”), a leading global manufacturer and exporter of aroma chemicals, is proud to announce that it has been awarded the prestigious Platinum Medal by EcoVadis. This recognition places Privi among the top 1% of companies worldwide assessed for sustainability performance in FY 2024–25, with an impressive overall score of 84 points and 99th Percentile.
