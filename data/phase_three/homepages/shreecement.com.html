Shree Cement

One of India's top three cement producers

Shree Cement is among India’s top three cement producers, built on a legacy of smart entrepreneurship, innovation, cost consciousness, and operational excellence. With an installed cement capacity of 69 MTPA in India and 73 MTPA including overseas operations, the company continues to grow with purpose and efficiency. Supported by 888.55 MW of installed power generation capacity, Shree Cement remains focused on building a stronger, more sustainable future.
