Our Foundation

Reliable Service Provider
Since 1958

Starting in 1958 with one truck and one office , our founder Shri PD Agarwal Ji went on to create Asia’s largest road transport network.

A.B.C. Ltd. became the first public transport company in India by listing on the Calcutta Stock Exchange. A.B.C. Ltd. was renamed ABC India Ltd to reflect our presence throughout India and listed on BSE in 1973.

Over our journey of constant evolution, our values have remained our steadfast guide and inspiration
