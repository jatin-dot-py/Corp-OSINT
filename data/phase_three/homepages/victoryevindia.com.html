About Us

Victory Electric Vehicles International Limited, based in Bahadurgarh, Haryana, is a leading company dedicated to manufacturing top-quality electric vehicles at the best prices in India. Our core business focuses on providing a wide range of e-rickshaws and electric vehicles across India, including passenger rickshaws, loading rickshaws, school vans, garbage autos, and electric two-wheelers like scooties. With a commitment to innovation and quality, Victory Electric Vehicles delivers reliable, eco-friendly transportation solutions on a large scale.

Founded in 2011, Victory Group embarked on a transformative journey, initially as a partnership and evolving into a private limited company before expanding further as a limited company. Our growth reflects our commitment to excellence and innovation in every step of our journey.

Victory is manufacturing High Power Electric Power Grid Panels, Industrial Electric Panels, Metro Railway Electric Panels & Domestic Electrical Panels.

Some Specification

With a flight time of 15–25 minutes and a maximum speed of 8 meters per second, the drone is ideal for covering fields quickly. It sprays across a width of 4–6 meters, with adjustable flow rates to match different needs. The drone operates at an altitude of 1–5 meters for optimal spraying performance, ensuring uniform coverage thanks to its advanced nozzles and electric diaphragm pump.

The drone runs on a high-capacity lithium-polymer battery, which supports fast charging and provides reliable power. For navigation, it uses GPS and Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) technology, enabling precision flight, even in complex fields. It has multiple flight modes, including manual, semi-automatic, and fully automatic options with waypoint navigation. Additionally, sensors on the front and back help avoid obstacles, ensuring safe operation.
