About Seamec

Leading provider of diving support vessel in Indian Offshore Oilfield Industry

Seamec is a powerhouse providing niche services in offshore Subsea Segment.

From a modest beginning in 1986, when it was incorporated as Peerless Leasing Pvt Ltd,to becoming a part of Technip FRANCE. In 2014 SEAMEC became a subsidiary of HAL Offshore Ltd (HAL) thus a part of MM Group, Seamec has achieved many milestones during the journey, and today it is a powerhouse to reckon with.

Presently Seamec has the largest fleet of Diving Support Vessels in the Middle East and India Region. With the well maintained fleet of DSVs, Seamec is catering to variety of clients-be it an turnkey basis or day rate basis.

SEAMEC and its Subsidiaries are engaged in operation of Bulk Careers world wide.It has ventured into EPC Infrastructure through a Joint venture company.
