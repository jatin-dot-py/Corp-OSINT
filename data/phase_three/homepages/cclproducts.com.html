ABOUT US

CCL Products (India) Limited is a public limited company, established in 1994 with a mission to produce premium-quality coffee that meets the highest global standards. Guided by core values of integrity, commitment, customer focus, and quality excellence, CCL Products has steadily grown into a major player in the coffee industry. Our robust infrastructure and dedication to innovation have helped us build a strong presence in over 110+ countries. Today, we are recognised as the largest exporter of instant coffee and a leading private label manufacturer worldwide.
