Welcome to Aravali Securities & Finance Limited

The Company was incorporated as Public Limited Company in June 11, 1980, in New Delhi under the Name of Aravali Trader And Investments Company Limited, consecutively the name of the Company change to Aravali Leasing Limited, which was further change into Aravali Securities & Finance Limited on May 4, 1994 as per the functions and business of the Company. The Company is promoted by the Poddar Family. The Registered Office of the Company was shifted from New Delhi to Gurgaon in 2009.

The company is currently engaged in the business of providing Financial and Other Advisory Services besides dealing in shares and securities.
