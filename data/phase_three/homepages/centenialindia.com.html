Quality

CENTENIAL products follows Quality Management System - a system of procedures and processes validating all aspects of Manufacturing, Risk management, Storage and product labelling as per regulations and also complies with ISO 13485 : 2016, ISO 9001 : 2015, ISO 45001 : 2018, WHO GMP & CE.

Customization

CENTENIAL offers various suture needle combinations. We customize different suture – needle combination as desired by the surgeon enabling them with a seamless surgery.
