Company Profile

A diversified portfolio with over 12 products and an extensive distribution network spanning over 12 countries & 100 customers.

First incorporated in 1939 with India’s first Soda Ash factory in Dhrangadhra, Gujarat, DCW has steadily evolved over 8 decades to become one of the country’s fastest growing chemical companies.

Through the years, we have diversified our product portfolio, transitioning from commodity to intermediate and specialty chemicals, focused heavily on capital expansion and innovated new products and processes.

Discover Our Products

Synthetic Rutile

Synthetic Rutile is a chemically modified ilmenite sand that has had most of the ferrous, non-titanium components removed and upgraded into Synthetic Rutile which contains ≈ 95wt.%.

Synthetic Iron Oxide Pigment

DCW is one of the largest commercial scale manufacturers of both, Red and Yellow Synthetic Iron Oxide pigments in India.

C-PVC

An important thermoplastic, C-PVC is used across a variety of industries from commercial to residential water piping systems.

Ammonium Bicarbonate

Manufactured using Ammonia Condensate liquid & Carbon Dioxide, its primarily used in Chemical & Pharma manufacturing and the Food industry.

Soda Ash

A non flammable, chemically stable white powder, Soda Ash is used to manufacture Detergents, Petroleum, Cleaning Products, Water Treatment & Paper Manufacturing.

Sodium Bicarbonate

A non flammable, chemically stable white powder, Sodium Bicarbonate is supplied in 50kg HDPE bags.

Caustic Soda

Caustic Soda (Sodium Hydroxide), is a basic product widely used in diverse industrial sectors, either as a raw material or as an auxiliary chemical.

PVC

Used in Agricultural Products, Building Industry - Windows, Profiles, Flooring Tiles  etc.

Hydrochloric Acid

Hydrochloric Acid (HCl) is commonly used in the process of water treatment, leaching, and as an analytical agent.

Liquid Chlorine

Elemental Chlorine is commercially produced from brine by electrolysis, through the chlor-alkali process as a by-product in the production of Caustic Soda.

Trichloroethylene

In metal de-greasing, dry cleaning drying electronic parts, as an extractions solvent for oils fats, waxes, refrigerant and also as a fumigant.

Utox

A pale yellow coloured odourless powder, Utox exhibits chemical resistance to acid with an average particle size of 1 micron.

Ferric Chloride

Produced at our Sahupuram facility its used in Effluent Water, Sewage Treatment, Textiles and Etching industry.

Sodium Hypochlorite

Sodium Hypochlorite is produced by the scrubbing of Chlorine Gas with Caustic Soda Lye.
