Our company was originally incorporated on January 07, 1977, under the name Narendra Investments (Delhi) Limited. Later the Company changed its name to Eiko LifeSciences Limited effective from June 24, 2021. The company is among the listed companies, and its equity shares are listed and admitted to dealings on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE Ltd.)


Eiko LifeSciences Limited [formerly known as Narendra Investments (Delhi) Limited] is a leading manufacturer, supplier and exporter of Speciality Chemicals and Pharma Intermediates in India and Globally.


The company has successfully ventured in the business of manufacturing, processing, formulating, producing, buying, selling, and exporting Speciality and Fine Chemicals. In a short span of time, the company has established itself in the race of becoming India's leading Speciality and Fine Chemicals Manufacturer, Supplier & Exporter.

  • Speciality Chemicals product offerings comprise of organic as well as inorganic chemicals.
  • The company has expanded its range of products and, presently, manufactures leading Speciality Chemicals which cater to application across various industries in India and globally.
  • The products are used in Pharmaceuticals, Agrochemicals, Aroma Chemicals, Electronic Chemicals etc.
  • Eiko LifeSciences acquired a manufacturing unit at MIDC, Badlapur and has also tied up with many job-workers for manufacturing
