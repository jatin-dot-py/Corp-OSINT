About Company

Founded in year 1993, Optiemus Infracom is a diversified, award-winning telecommunications enterprise, and a strong player in the Indian mobility ecosystem. The group has over three decades of rich experience in managing, distributing, manufacturing and retailing mobile brands and other telecommunication products in India. To our credit we sold the first official mobile phone in the country way back in 1995 and since then have been bringing innovative technology products to Indian consumers through its widespread distribution and retail operations.

A Differentiated Work Culture

At Optiemus, we believe that the organization is as strong as it’s people and thus we have built a work culture that thrives on ownership, transparency, approachability and innovation. We believe that excellence is not a destination but a journey of continuous improvement and we keenly promote an open culture, encouraging feedback, and actively transforming it into action.
