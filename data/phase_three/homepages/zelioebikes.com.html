ABOUT ZELIO

Zelio is India's premier electric two-wheeler brand, committed to revolutionizing urban mobility through innovation, sustainability, and superior design. Founded with a vision to make electric vehicles accessible to every Indian, we have rapidly grown to become a market leader with a presence in over 350 cities and towns across the country.

Our range of electric scooters is engineered specifically for Indian roads and driving conditions. From students seeking affordable and efficient commute solutions to delivery professionals requiring robust cargo capabilities, and from senior citizens needing comfortable rides to farmers looking for utility vehicles – Zelio has a model for everyone.

As a publicly listed entity on the NSE and BSE, we maintain the highest standards of corporate governance, financial transparency, and stakeholder trust. Our commitment to sustainability extends beyond our products to our entire manufacturing process, contributing to India's vision of a greener future.

With cutting-edge battery technology, intelligent design, and customer-centric service through our HyperService network, Zelio continues to set new benchmarks in the electric vehicle industry. Join us in embracing the future of mobility.
