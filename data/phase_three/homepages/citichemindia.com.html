Who we are

Our Journey as Citichem India

Established in 1987, Citichem India Limited is one of the largest distributor of industrial chemicals. Our organization has progressed immensely under the able guidance of our mentors “Mr. Arif Merchant” and “Mr. Hashim Merchant”, who hold immense experience Across Multiple Business Verticals. They guided us through every step of our business operation. Owing to their constant support, we have successfully resolved complex issues and attained immense progress Across Multiple Business Verticals.
