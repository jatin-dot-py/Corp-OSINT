About Us

Kings Infra Ventures Limited is fully integrated in the seafood value chain with backward-forward linkages in the industry. Our core activities include aquaculture, seafood processing, international trade and domestic retail of marine products, aquaculture consultancy and infrastructure development.

Read more

Mission

Mission is to improve the sustainability of aquaculture and related food production systems with research & development, advance technologies and innovations.

Vision

To emerge as a global player in the sustainable food production sector by continuously improving our stakes in the entire value chain of safe-to-eat food production systems.

Core Values

To build a socially responsible business organisation that will enhance livelihoods and create a safer, healthier, greener and more prosperous planet.

Aquaculture

Aquaculture

Kings is a pioneer in the seafood and aquaculture business in the country. The Company owns and operates high yielding shrimp farms in Tuticorin, Tamil Nadu developed using proprietary technology and protocols, without the use of antibiotics. Our farms have set a benchmark in the industry by harvesting 80gms Vannamei shrimps in a span of 120 days.

Seafood exports

Seafood Processing & Exports

Exporting of processed seafood is at the core of Kings Infra’s business with its strong presence in Europe, US, Japan, China and other major markets through long term buyer relationships and well established brand presence.

Sustainability

For Kings, sustainability involves a deep-rooted social commitment for rural development, woman empowerment with emphasis on producing safe-to-eat food locally for the enhanced prosperity and well-being of all, while addressing the urgent need for food production systems to be more sustainable, carbon neutral, mitigating climate change and preserving our environment for a safer, healthier and greener planet.
