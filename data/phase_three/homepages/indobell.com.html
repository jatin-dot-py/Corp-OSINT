Thermal Insulation is the method of protecting a power-generating unit from any unwanted heat loss.

Unprecedented heat loss leads to lower efficiency of a power-generating unit and also leads to higher production costs.

There are multiple factors which decide the type of Thermal Insulation most suited to one’s need. Some of them are:
Location of the unit – whether indoor or outdoor.
Power generating capacity
Clearance area of the unit
Primary purpose of insulating the unit, etc.

Depending on what you aim to achieve through insulation of your power-generating unit, some common choices for Thermal Insulation materials are – Mineral wool ; Fibreglass ; Silicone fabric ; Ceramic fabric ; etc.

Choosing the type of Thermal Insulation best suited for your unit can help to reduce maintenance costs as the standard longevity of the correct material can ensure hassle-free maintenance for approximately 9-10 years.

GRANULATED ROCKWOOL

Bulk fibers are processed through specially designed machines. The product then undergoes processes like slicing, slitting, etc. which is followed by surface rotation - resulting in nodulation of fibers. These resultant smaller, rounder fibers - are then passed through vibration machines- in order to eliminate foreign particles.

SPRAY INSULATION

Spray foam is a chemical product created by two materials, mineral wool and binder, which react when mixed with each other. Once the wire mesh is in place over the turbine surface which is to be insulated, the spray foam is then sprayed onto the surface through a gun. Once dry, the entire sprayed surface is covered with hard-setting cement - in order to achieve a uniform surface.

LRB MATTRESS INSULATION

Lightly resin-bonded mattresses (LRB) consist of fine fibers spun from selected rocks that are melted at high temperatures. These are then bonded with a thermosetting resin. The raw mattresses are given a fiber lay pattern and are baked to form end products of predetermined density and thickness.
