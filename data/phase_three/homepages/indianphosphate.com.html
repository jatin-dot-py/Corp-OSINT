    Fertilizers

    We are a leading manufacturer of Single Super Phosphate (SSP) in Rajasthan with current installed capacity of 400 MT/day of SSP powder and 400 MT/day of granulated SSP each at our two manufacturing facilities in Rajasthan.

    Chemicals

    We are one of the leading manufacturer of Linear Alkyl Benzene Sulphonic Acid (LABSA 90%) in Rajasthan with an installed capacity of 350 MT/ day.

    Non Renewable Energy

    We have set up Solar Power plant for captive power requirement and thereby saving in power cost as well as contributing towards saving nature through saving in carbon emission for the power used earlier which was generated from thermal power plants.

    Logistics

    Logistics Services was the first business activity, started by group entity way back in ‘70s and since then it has grown multifold with current fleet of 80+ large capacity road tankers.

    Trading

    Our group entity’s trading arm started in 2007 and since then it has grown exponentially.

    Education

    Our group entity runs CBSE affiliated school from nursery to XII Board.

    Mining & Beneficiation

    As a part of our interest in backward integration, recently we have received LOI for rock phosphate mines for which exploration work is in progress.

    Hospitality

    IPhL group has made foray into hospitality sector & constructing two five star projects in Rajasthan with +250 keys in first phase to tap tourism potential.
