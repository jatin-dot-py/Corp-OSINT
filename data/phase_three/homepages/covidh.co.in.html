🃏 Live Andar Bahar Overview

Andar Bahar is a traditional Indian card game brought to life through professional live dealer studios. The gameplay revolves around a single deck of cards and a simple prediction about which side — Andar or Bahar — will receive a matching card first. The core objective is to guess the correct side before the dealer completes the round, making it accessible to players of all experience levels while still providing genuine excitement.

Andar Bahar distinguishes itself from other live casino games through several unique characteristics that contribute to its widespread popularity among Indian players.

📖 Learn How to Play Andar Bahar

Andar Bahar uses a standard 52-card deck dealt by a professional live dealer. The table layout is divided into two clearly marked sections: Andar (inside) on the left and Bahar (outside) on the right, with the Joker card placed in the center of the table.

1
Start and Joker Card

The dealer deals the first card, known as the Joker card. After this, the dealer begins dealing a single card to each side: first to Bahar, then Andar, then Bahar again, and so forth. This alternating pattern continues until a card with the same value as the Joker, regardless of suit, appears on one of the sides.

2
Betting

The player needs to simply predict on which side (Andar or Bahar) a card with the same value as the Joker card will be drawn first. Players usually have 10 to 12 seconds to decide on their bet. Some versions also allow side bets at this stage, such as predicting the number of cards that will be dealt after the Joker card until a card sharing the same value is drawn for either Andar or Bahar.

3
Outcomes and Statistics

Once the matching card appears on either side, the round ends immediately. Players can review the statistics of previous rounds displayed on screen, which provides insight into recent patterns and trends that may inform future decisions.

Familiarity with these three stages gives you a solid foundation for approaching the game with confidence. The combination of quick rounds and clear rules makes the transition from observation to active play a smooth one.
