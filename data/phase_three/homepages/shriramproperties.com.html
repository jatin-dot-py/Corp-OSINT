Shriram Greenfield - PRAVEEN OJHA

We were looking for a place where our kid can safely play and grow. And finally our search ended at Shriram Greenfields by Shriram Properties; a perfect combination of upgraded lifestyle, affordable pricing & open spaces. Schools, Shopping malls & Hospitals all in close proximity make our life convenient. After visiting the project and seeing its open area, maintenance, facilities and amenities, we decided that this is the lifestyle we were looking for.

Shriram Chirping Grove - TULIKA & HIMANSHU

Shriram Properties' team was professional and helped me every step of my way; I was served like tailor fit! While the sales helped me get choice unit, my relationship manager was very supportive. This becomes very-very important when you are NRI buyer; staying thousands of miles away. During my exploration - me and my spouse were spending hours and hours on internet; looking aspects like re-salability/ rentability, cost, appreciation prospect, connectivity to major IT hubs, brand value. Now I am proud owner of Shriram Chirping Grove villa. Shriram Properties being an 'A' category builder, among other reputed ones in Bangalore like Prestige, Brigade, and Sobha, it's a sensible bet considering its price. I am happy joining Shriram Properties family.

Shriram Raynal Gardens - PAVAN KUMAR

One of the most beautifully maintained layouts with loads of trees, lifestyle facilities and even a temple. Simply Amazed!”

Shriram Grand City - INDRANIL KUNDU & FAMILY
