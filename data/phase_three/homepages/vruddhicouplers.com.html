Our Products

Products We Offer

Rebar Couplers

Parallel Roll Threaded Coupler

Vruddhi Parallel Threaded Couplers are designed for connecting reinforcement bars from Ø 16 mm to 40 mm. The rebar threads are parallel threaded by a roller threading machine, supported by a single machine.

Rebar Couplers

Cold Forged Couplers

Vruddhi Cold Forged Couplers are used for rebar splicing with proven performance under compression, tension, cyclic, and fatigue loads. The rebar ends are enlarged by cold forging and then threaded, with the on-site process carried out by two machines. This couplers undergoes manufacturing and testing processes in strict accordance with go and no-go plug gauges.

Rebar Couplers

Taper Threaded Couplers

It uses self-aligning taper threads to join two reinforcement bars with positive locking. Vruddhi taper threaded couplers provide consistent performance and are easy to install.

Rebar Couplers

Bolted Couplers

Vruddhi bolted couplers are utilized to connect two reinforcing bars end-to-end when one bar is already cast in place and there is inadequate space to use a hydraulic cold press for crimping. They are quite easy to install. Installation doesn’t require any special rebar end preparation.

Rebar Couplers

GroutFit Couplers

Vruddhi GroutFit Couplers are specially designed for connecting GroutFit elements. These half grout system coupler have one threaded end and the other end is a hollow cavity for connecting two rebars present in GroutFit elements on site.

Rebar Couplers

Vruddhi Hydraulic Crimping Couplers

Our Cold Extrusion Hydraulic Press connects two non-threaded rebars using a hydraulic power pack and a handheld jack on-site for crimping.

Machines

BTS Rebar Threading Machine

Vruddhi Engineering Works offer high quality BTS rebar threading machine with superior features.  This machine is used for threading reinforcement bars (TMT bars) with BTS technology. It is a semi-manually operated machine with stop switch functionality to control the threading length of rebars. The machines are low on maintenance and easy to clean. They can be easily transported and mobilized from one project to another.

Machines

Rebar Roller Threading Machine

The machine is used for rebar threading by rollers.  We offer low maintenance machines which are known for durability. As they are compact they fit at congested sites easily.

Machines

Rebar Cold Forging Machine

Cold forged rebar couplers are widely used in High rise buildings, infrastructure  and commercial projects. To use cold forged couplers you need to undertake cold forging of rebar ends followed by threading.
The cold forging machine supplied by us  carries out enlargement of diameter of rebar end.

Bulk Commodities

TMT Rebars (Reinforcement Bars)

We supply TMT bars of leading primary and secondary manufacturers. Available sizes are 8mm to 40mm. Buildings come with many variations and require the right TMT bars. They are chosen by the different chemical composition and mechanical properties of steel. For a very strong foundation, the right specifications of TMT rebars are required.

Protection Caps
