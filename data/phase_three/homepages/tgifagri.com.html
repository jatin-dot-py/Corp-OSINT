Brief Introduction

Welcome to TGIF Agribusiness Limited, your premier destination for high-quality horticulture products. Based in Gujarat, we specialize in open & Control farming and are proud to offer a range of our exceptional produce with flagship crop of pomegranates and their value-added products. Our commitment to innovative technology and the use of superior inputs ensures that freshness of each crop is of the finest quality.

At TGIF Agribusiness Limited, we are dedicated to delivering excellence in every aspect of our business. Through our Direct2home E-commerce Platform, we supply farm fresh produces to the domestic market, catering to customers who value freshness and superior taste. Additionally, we have successfully expanded our reach to international markets, serving discerning customers in Europe, Russia, and the Middle East.

Established in 2014 by a group of visionary entrepreneurs, our company operates in both the European Union and India. We collaborate with brilliant and passionate entrepreneurs, fostering their growth and enabling them to become market leaders in their respective domains. Our relentless focus on innovation in farming and value-added products sets us apart in the industry.

Spanning over one million square meters of land in Rajasthan, our infrastructure is designed to support our open and controlled farming technologies. We have ambitious plans to expand our cultivable area to five million square meters across India, embracing state-of-the-art farming techniques. Moreover, we actively engage in contract farming, investing significant resources to enhance our growing operations.

At TGIF Agribusiness Limited, we take pride in our role as a knowledge hub, sharing best practices, cutting-edge technology, and premium agricultural inputs. to achieve remarkable results and create sustainable agricultural practices.

Join us in our journey towards excellence, as we continue to redefine the horticulture industry through our commitment to quality, innovation, and the growth. TGIF Agribusiness Limited is your trusted source for premium horticulture products and a leader in advancing the boundaries of farming technology

Vision

At TGIF Agribusiness Limited, our vision is to become a leading supplier of first-grade farm produce and allied products, while addressing the challenges of limited natural resources, a growing population, and a changing climate. We aim to achieve this by leveraging high-tech horticulture techniques in India and fostering close cooperation with our customers and suppliers.

Mission

Our mission is to achieve profitable growth in an ethically-sound manner through a focus on innovation and operational excellence in the supply of fresh produce.

To fulfill our mission, we are committed to delivering value in the following ways:
  • Customers: We strive to provide our customers with fresh produce of exceptional quality. By establishing enduring relationships, we aim to become their trusted source for backyard farm products.
  • Staff: We value our employees and provide them with a stimulating work environment that encourages innovation and knowledge sharing. We believe that by investing in our team, we can drive continuous improvement and achieve excellence.
  • Society: As a responsible corporate citizen, we contribute to society by adopting high-tech modern farming techniques. By doing so, we ensure the production of high-quality farm produce that serves the needs of the world while minimizing our environmental impact.

Through our unwavering commitment to our vision and mission, we aim to make a positive impact in the agricultural industry, address sustainability challenges, and deliver value to our stakeholders. TGIF Agribusiness Limited is dedicated to excellence, innovation, and the pursuit of a more sustainable future.
