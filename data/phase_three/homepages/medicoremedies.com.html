Medico Remedies Ltd

Our Company, Medico Remedies Limited, is a pharmaceutical formulation manufacturing company with manufacturing and marketing capabilities in formulation with focus on anti-infective, Beta-Lactums, cephalosporins, antimalarial, antiretroviral, anti-ulcer drugs and antacids, vitamins, haematinics and other supplements. Further in addition to the above, our Company also has manufacturing and marketing capabilities in other drugs such as NSAIDS, antihistaminic, anti-diabetics, cardio vascular drugs, diuretics, anti-epileptics, combination drug kits, syrups and cream & gel for various therapeutic segments.
