About Us

OnEMI Technology Solutions Limited (formerly OnEMI Technology Solutions Private Limited) is a leading digital lending platform revolutionizing access to credit in India. With a mission to empower underserved and new-to-credit customers, OnEMI offers fast, secure, and seamless financing solutions tailored to diverse consumer needs. The company provides a wide range of loan products—from small-ticket personal loans to secured loans like Loan Against Property (LAP)—leveraging deep data analytics, alternative credit scoring, and robust technology infrastructure. Backed by a strong governance framework and powered by its NBFC subsidiary Si Creva Capital Services, OnEMI combines regulatory rigor with cutting-edge innovation to deliver responsible and inclusive credit. As a trusted partner to millions, OnEMI is building a future where financial inclusion is not just a goal, but a reality for every Indian household.

Kissht

Kissht is a trusted financial platform that simplifies access to credit through its innovative offerings. Specializing in easy-to-avail personal and business loans, Kissht ensures a seamless experience with minimal documentation and quick approval processes. With a strong focus on customer convenience, Kissht empowers individuals and businesses across India to achieve their financial goals, making dreams a reality with accessible and reliable financial solutions.

Our mission is to make credit easily accessible to India's youth, empowering them to achieve their dreams and unlock new opportunities.
