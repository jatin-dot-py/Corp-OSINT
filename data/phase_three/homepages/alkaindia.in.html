AUDROC Limited (Formerly known as Alka India Ltd) – Company Overview

AUDROC Limited (AUDROC) presents itself in your fold with a rich experience of more than three decades. Incorporated in 1993, the company endeavors to share its rich heritage with all its stakeholders and bring forward a growth trajectory that surpasses the expectations of all at large.

AUDROC is primarily into the business of FMCG with special impetus on agro-commodities. The product line of the companyhas been developed keeping in mind the requirements of its end-consumers and various other stakeholders.

The product line has been diversified to satiate the demand and supply dynamics in order tosuit the requirements of the market. The management constantly makes efforts to better its performance to keep up its commitment towards various stakeholders who have reposed their faith in the company and its management.
