Fermenta Biotech Limited

(Fermenta/FBL)

Established in 1951, Fermenta is one of the largest manufacturers of Vitamin D3 and a key global player. Headquartered in Thane, India, Fermenta caters to clientele across the globe, with a worldwide distribution network. Its state-of-the-art R&D centre is based at Thane, and world class manufacturing facilities are located in Kullu, Himachal Pradesh and Dahej, Gujarat. Apart from Vitamin D3, Fermenta also offers niche APIs, integrated biotechnology platforms such as enzymes for antibiotic synthesis as well as environmental solutions for waste water management and treatment.

Our International Subsidiaries

Fermenta Biotech Limited (FBL) has incorporated wholly-owned international subsidiaries in USA
(Fermenta Biotech USA LLC) and Germany (Fermenta Biotech GmbH), with the aim of expanding our
global footprint and manufacturing our products in USA and Europe respectively.

Merger of Fermenta Biotech into DIL Limited (renamed Fermenta Biotech)

DIL Limited held a 91.20% equity stake in Fermenta Biotech Limited. DIL and FBL were separate
corporate entities. A merger strengthened economies-of-scale, efficiency and organisational value. A
pooling of skills, talent and experience increased competitiveness, lowered costs, simplified the
shareholding structure and reduced borrowing costs. Besides, Fermenta’s pharmaceutical business
has access to the rental income generated by its real estate business, making it possible to grow the
business with resources from within, making it possible to enhance shareholder value.
The Merger of Fermenta Biotech Limited into DIL Limited was effective from September 26, 2019. DIL Limited was renamed to Fermenta Biotech Limited effective from October 17, 2019.

Vitamin D3 and pharmaceuticals

Fermenta is a leading manufacturer and global supplier of Vitamin D3. It produces a range of
variants – crystalline, oil, resin in oil, cold water dispersible (CWD), and feed grade powder, that can
be used in applications like injectables, soft gel capsules, premixes, as well as food and beverage
fortification.
Fermenta also offers niche APIs such as Phenyramidol and Silicon Dioxide.

Nutritional Premixes

Fermenta offers premix solutions for fortifying food, in order to enhance its nutritional content. Our blends are backed by our scientific expertise as well as global regulatory approvals.products add value to oil without altering its sensory qualities.

Biotechnology

Fermenta delivers proprietary specialised technologies through its enzyme portfolio across the
biopharmaceutical product life-cycle. Fermenta’s enzyme technologies enable new and eco-friendly
manufacturing practices, addressing sectors comprising the pharmaceutical industry, fine chemicals,
bio-plastics and cosmetics, to mention a few.

Environmental Solutions

Fermenta Biotech Limited offers a comprehensive set of solutions designed to sustainably tackle
growing environmental concerns. Apart from a plethora of biotechnology products, we provide a holistic range of services ranging from design, engineering, procurement and construction of water as well as wastewater treatment plants to end-to-end operation and maintenance services.

Real Estate

Fermenta entered the property development business in 2012, following the establishment of a state-of-the-art IT park in one of Thane’s prime locations. The project, Thane One, is spread across ~200,000 square feet and is home to some of the marquee brands in the country including Tata Technologies Limited, Ashok Leyland Limited, Samsung India and Datamark BPO Service Private Limited among others.
