Frequently Asked Questions

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is a CPCB-mandated framework requiring producers, importers, and brand owners of electronics to ensure their products are collected and recycled responsibly at end-of-life. Under the E-Waste (Management) Rules, it's legally mandatory; non-compliance can attract penalties and environmental compensation charges. Ecoreco, as a CPCB-registered recycler, handles EPR registration support, target tracking, and issues valid EPR certificates for every kilogram processed.

Ecoreco, also known as Eco Recycling Ltd, is an e-waste and lithium-ion battery waste management company in India. The company provides integrated solutions for responsible collection, recycling, resource recovery, compliance, and sustainable management of electronic waste.

Ecoreco provides a range of services, including EPR Compliance, WEEE Recycling, Reverse Logistics, Data Destruction, IT Asset Disposition (ITAD), Lamp Recycling and Battery Recycling.

Electronic waste can contain valuable materials as well as components that require proper handling. Responsible recycling helps recover resources, reduce environmental impact, and ensure discarded electronics are processed through appropriate recycling practices.

Ecoreco serves a wide range of stakeholders, including corporates, multinational companies, OEMs, retailers, bulk consumers, government departments, educational institutions, households, and other organisations that need responsible e-waste management solutions.

We provide end-to-end e-waste management solutions, including collection and reverse logistics. The final pickup quote depends on the service scope, the type of material, and the location involved. If you need e-waste disposal for a business, household, or campaign, the pricing is usually tied to the full handling route, not just collection alone.

A standard pickup can include collection, reverse logistics, dismantling, data destruction, and recycling, depending on the solution you choose. Eco also offers IT Asset Disposition, lamp recycling, battery recycling, Eco-Bins, and e-waste drop-off points or collection campaigns. Because each customer's needs are different, the quote reflects the specific services requested.
