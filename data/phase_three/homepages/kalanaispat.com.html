OUR INSPIRING
HISTORY

"Established in 2012, Kalana Ispat Pvt Ltd embarked on its journey under the collaborative leadership of Mr. Varghese Joseph Pottakerry and Aftab S. Khandwawala, initially focusing on the trading of metal scrap. At the outset, our operations involved the procurement of raw materials through imports from various countries, along with sourcing from shipbreaking yards and local merchants. As time progressed, we diligently honed our skills and built a remarkable reputation within the market, gaining the trust of a wide clientele

Our remarkable journey and reputation prompted us to venture into the manufacturing of M.S. Billets. By then, we had earned a solid standing in the industry, a testament to the integrity and quality that defined our operations. Today, Kalana Ispat Pvt Ltd is recognized as a stalwart in the sector, catering to a diverse range of leading manufacturers who appreciate the quality of our M.S. Billets. Our enduring commitment to excellence and our well-established goodwill in the market continue to be the cornerstones of our success and the basis of our enduring relationships with our esteemed customers.

OUR MISSION
& VISION


“To be a leading force in the steel industry, Kalana Ispat is committed to providing high-quality billets through cutting-edge manufacturing processes. Our mission is rooted in a dedication to excellence, sustainability, and customer satisfaction, driving our relentless pursuit of innovation and operational efficiency.

Our vision is to empower industries with superior steel solutions, setting the benchmark for billet manufacturing globally. Through continuous improvement and forward-thinking strategies, we aim to be recognized for our integrity, reliability, and contributions to the growth and sustainability of the steel sector.”
