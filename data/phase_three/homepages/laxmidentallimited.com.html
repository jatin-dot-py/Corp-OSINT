OUR BUSINESSES

ILLUSION DENTAL LAB

Illusion Dental Laboratory, headquartered in Mumbai, is a well-established and recognised dental laboratory in India. It boasts state-of-the-art facilities focused on fabricating custom-made dental prostheses, including Illusion Zirconia, our range of branded premium dental crowns.

LAXMI DENTAL EXPORT

Laxmi Dental Export, with service hubs in the US and Europe, has been a leading provider of high-quality dental products since 2004, exporting products to dental laboratories and clinics around the world.

LAXMI DENTAL LAB USA INC

Laxmi Dental Lab USA Inc. is a well-equipped dental laboratory offering superior-quality dental products and services. Its reach is further supported by its division, Solitaire Smile Dental Lab, a full-service dental lab facility located in New Jersey.

ILLUSION ALIGNERS

With Illusion's legacy of over two decades behind its back and a vision to provide cutting-edge orthodontic solutions to the dental fraternity, Illusion Aligners was launched. It offers FDA 510k-cleared Clear Aligners crafted using advanced technology.

TAGLUS

Taglus is a global leader in the manufacturing of innovative aligner and retainer thermoforming sheets, thermoforming machines, biocompatible 3D printing resins and consumables. Taglus aims to meet the most demanding quality standards in the world of thermoforming.

KIDS-E-DENTAL

Our goal at Kids-e-Dental is to offer cutting-edge, reliable, and aesthetically pleasing dental prostheses for kids. In an effort to improve accessibility and convenience for pediatric dentistry professionals, we are currently active in multiple countries.
