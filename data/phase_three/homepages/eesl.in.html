Welcome to Emergent Industrial Solutions Ltd.

Founded in 1983, Emergent Industrial Solutions Ltd. (formerly Emergent Global Edu. and Services Ltd.) is a leading trading company specializing in metals, coal, and ores. Listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), we cater to industries such as Steel, Power, Ferro-Alloys, and agricultural commodities. With vast experience and a global reach, we deliver structured long-term contracts Pay & Pick’ deliveries and JIT (Just in Time) solutions for bulk raw materials like Coking Coal, Coke, and Manganese Ore. Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted partner in the industry.
