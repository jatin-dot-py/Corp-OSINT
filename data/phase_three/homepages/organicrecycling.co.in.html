Bioenergy Solutions for Organic Waste in India

Organic Recycling Systems Limited (ORSL) is a leading bioenergy company in India, dedicated to revolutionizing sustainable waste recycling, solid waste treatment, and eco-friendly disposal solutions.

Advanced Bioenergy Technology

At Organic Recycling Systems Limited, our advanced bioenergy technology transforms waste into renewable energy, sustainable fuel, and reusable resources—driving a cleaner and greener future for India.

We provide a wide range of bioenergy and renewable energy solutions to the government, corporations, and private players. Our business model is based on helping communities and corporations reduce the environmental impact of waste by providing solutions that can convert waste into sustainable green resources.

Our Solutions Are Divided Into Three Core Segments

Ecologically Responsible Bioenergy Solutions for MSW, Industrial, and Private Projects

Organic Recycling Systems Limited (ORSL) is a leading green energy company in India offering sustainable waste management, renewable energy solutions, and waste-to-energy technology. ORSL specializes in solid waste management, waste segregation, dry waste segregation systems, windrow composting, in-vessel composting, and waste and biomass valorization. Using diverse feedstock such as segregated organic waste, agricultural waste, biomass waste, paddy straw, Napier grass, and Super Napier grass, ORSL develops advanced anaerobic digestion systems, anaerobic digesters, biogas plants, biomethanation plants, bio-CNG (CBG) plants, and biogas production facilities.

With strong engineering services, EPC services, PMC services, and laboratory services, the company supports projects under the SATAT scheme and delivers feasibility reports, CBG plant project reports, and complete plant design solutions. ORSL’s expertise covers biogas production, biomethane production, biogas production stages, biogas production process steps, and factors affecting biogas production. Its recycling technologies, material recovery facilities, waste processing plants, transfer stations, bioenzymes support, and waste recovery systems help industries and municipalities achieve sustainable waste management and organic waste recycling.

As a trusted waste recycling company in India and engineering company in Mumbai, ORSL delivers reliable renewable energy, biomass energy, and green energy solutions while promoting efficient agricultural waste management and industrial waste treatment for a cleaner, sustainable future.
