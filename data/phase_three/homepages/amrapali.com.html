Important Security Notice: Stay Vigilant Against Cyber Fraud

Dear Customers,
There are plenty of things that are happening around the globe with respect to cybercrimes that includes but not limited to Impersonating as company representatives through unauthorized communications, fake websites, and calls, that might put your personal and financial details at risk.

To protect yourself:

1. Verify Authorized Personnel: Only engage with verified representatives via official channels listed on our website. Contact support if unsure.

2. Use Secure Channels:Access our official website at https://amrapali.com and never share your login details. Only deal with the SEBI registered entities only.

3. Beware of Suspicious Communications:Avoid unsolicited requests for personal or financial details. Report them to us.

4. Check for Red Flags:Be cautious of high-pressure tactics or promises of quick returns.

5. Secure Your Devices:Use strong passwords, updated security software, and enable two-factor authentication.

If you suspect any fraud, contact our support team immediately. Your vigilance helps us maintain a secure trading environment.

Stay alert, stay safe!
