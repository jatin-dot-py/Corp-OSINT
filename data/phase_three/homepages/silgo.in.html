About us

Why choose silgo

Choosing Silgo means embracing a legacy of excellence, where each piece of jewelry is crafted with precision and passion. Our dedication to quality is evident in every detail, from the purity of our 925 sterling silver to the meticulous artistry that transforms raw materials into stunning jewelry. Silgo is not just about adornment; it’s about creating pieces that resonate with your individuality and stand the test of time.

We specialize in designing and manufacturing exquisite silver jewelry, including Rings, Earrings, Bracelets, Necklaces, Jhumkis Bangles, and customized pieces tailored to customer demand. Each creation is crafted in 925 sterling silver and hallmarked jewelry certified by BIS, ensuring the highest quality and authenticity. We offer both retailing and wholesaling of our silver jewelry, catering to a wide range of customers.
