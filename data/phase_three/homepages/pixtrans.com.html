Frequently Asked Questions

What is the transmission Belt?

A transmission Belt that transfers power between two rotating shafts.

What are the advantages of a V-belt?

The V belt is capable of operating with a higher velocity ratio and a larger center distance drive. Capable of accommodating a wide range of load requirements.

Top V-Belt brands globally?

PIX Transmissions Limited is amongst the top V belt manufacturers in the world.

What is the method of calculating Belt tension?

PIX Drive design software for determining Belt tension values based on drive parameters.

What is the shelf life of drive Belts?

Approx. 6 years, if stored in a proper Belt storage environment.

What are rubber Belts used for?

Rubber Belts are often used for power transmission, conveyor systems, engines, agricultural machinery, and various industrial applications.

What is a drive pulley?

The driver pulley is attached to a power source and the driven pulley is connected to an object being driven.

What are the types of Belts used in the automobile industry?

Belts used in the automobile industry includes Poly Belts, Timing Belts, and REC Belts.
