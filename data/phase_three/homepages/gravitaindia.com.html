About Gravita

Enterprising Craftsmen, Innovative Solutions - We Make a Difference

Gravita has always been a value-driven organization, guided by principles that shape our growth and long-term vision. The Company is engaged in recycling lead, aluminium, plastics, lithium-ion batteries and waste tyres through environmentally responsible processes, while also delivering turnkey recycling solutions globally.

Our Legacy

Rajat Agrawal, a first-generation entrepreneur, founded Gravita in 1992 after recognising the significant potential worldwide for recycling lead.
