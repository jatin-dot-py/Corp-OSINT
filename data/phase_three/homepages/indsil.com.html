About Indsil

WELCOME TO INDSIL GROUP

The group stands out globally for its product quality and pioneering efforts in process technology for speciality alloys. The captive power generation facilities at Kerala and Andhra make the respective smelters in these locations. This in turn has helped the group position itself as a key low cost, high quality supplier in the global low carbon silico manganese industry.

MISSION STATEMENT: Add economic value by creating an organisation capable of meeting every customer requirement in terms of quality, reliability and competitiveness. Maintain focus on economics value addition through continuous improvement of quality of products, process and service.
