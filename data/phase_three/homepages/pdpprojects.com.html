Integrated Logistic Solutions

PDP Shipping & Project Limited (PSPL) is an International Freight Forwarding company specializing in global transportation solutions. Our team of experts customizes integrated logistics to meet critical challenges, ensuring seamless end-to-end global supply chain management for our valued clients.

We transport cargo worldwide of various shapes, sizes, and weights via sea, air, road, rail, coastal barges, or through multi-modal transportation. Our approach optimally matches different means and modes to suit specific client requirements most cost effectively & economically.

Operating in an automated environment with integrated artificial intelligence applications in logistics, we leverage global networking to offer competitive freight rates for destinations worldwide. Our experienced and skilled team optimizes carrier choices, routes, conveyance modes, and handling systems, resulting in significant cost savings.
