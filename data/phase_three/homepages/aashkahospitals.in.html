About Us

Super Speciality Hospital in Gandhinagar

AASHKA means "Blessing" and Aashka Hospital signifies blessing, a blessing of fine health. True to the meaning of our name, we believe in bestowing the ultimate blessing of life - the blessing of health.

Milestones Unveiled

2026

Soon we will unveil our cutting-edge IVY Center, pushing the boundaries of healthcare even further.

2023

Our innovative neuro-rehabilitation services were launched to support patients on their path to recovery.

2021

We made history with the successful launch of the biggest BSE SME IPO of India, a testament to our growth and vision.

2020

As the first government-recognized COVID-19 hospital in Gandhinagar, we stood at the forefront of the fight against the pandemic.

2018

Our dedication to quality earned us NABH accreditation, recognizing our commitment to providing top-tier healthcare services.

2017

We introduced the region's first state-of-the-art Cath Lab, revolutionizing cardiac care standards.

2016

In 2016, Aashka Hospital opened its doors as the first-of-its-kind facility in Gandhinagar and across northern Gujarat, setting the stage for a new era of healthcare excellence.

International Patient Services

01

Aashka Hospitals provides a full spectrum of support for foreign patients, including medical consultations, visa assistance, and seamless airport transfers. Our dedicated team ensures a smooth experience from arrival to treatment and beyond.

Premier Healthcare in Gandhinagar

02

Located in Gandhinagar, Gujarat’s meticulously planned and green capital, our hospital offers top-tier healthcare services in a peaceful and clean environment. We are conveniently close to GIFT City and accessible from Ahmedabad Airport.

Exceptional Care and Comfort

03
