Powering High Growth Sectors

Many Industries #OneGroup

India's Growth Partner

Founded more than a century ago, Swan Corp Limited is a trusted name in India’s industrial landscape. What began as a textile business has grown into a diversified conglomerate with interests in manufacturing, defence, shipbuilding, energy, real estate, and technology.

We don’t just build businesses — we build belief in what India can become. Rooted in heritage and driven by innovation, our mission aligns with India’s vision of self-reliance. By strategically investing in complex and high-stakes projects, we deliver lasting impact and contribute to India’s growth and collective future.
