Welcome to Piccadily Sugar and Allied Industries Ltd.

Piccadily Sugar & Allied Industries ltd (PSAIL) incorporated with a aim to provide boost to State industry by establishing a eco friendly sugar mill in the year 1994 & distillery in 2004 at Village Himjheri, Jakhal Road, Patran , Distt. Patiala in the State of Punjab. It manufacturers White Crystal Sugar from Sugar cane cultivated in the surrounding rural area & Rectified Spirit, Extra Neutral Alcohal (ENA) from molasses/rice/wheat.
