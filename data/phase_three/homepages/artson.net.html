Welcome to Artson Limited

A Tata Enterprise

Established in 1978, Artson Limited has grown consistently as a trusted Tankage and EPC contractor, manufacturer of process equipment & pressure vessels, structural steel fabricator and experienced shipbuilder, delivering end-to-end solutions across critical industrial sectors. A subsidiary of Tata Projects Limited since 2007, Artson is now proudly recognized as a Tata Enterprise, synonymous with quality, reliability, and innovation.

With a strong national footprint and growing global presence, we specialize in the design and fabrication of high-end process equipment, the construction of tankages and industrial infrastructure, and shipbuilding and marine fabrication – our three key verticals.

Today, in addition to remaining a trusted name in the industry, we are also expanding our presence to manufacturing of skid mounted process equipment, Firefighting systems, industrial piping, bulk material handling systems and other structures related to development of the country’s industrial infrastructure. Just as our diversification continue to grow, so do our reputation for standards of excellence in quality, performance and customer service.

Our experienced & dedicated engineers undertake all engineering activities from concept to commissioning with a very high level of professionalism.
