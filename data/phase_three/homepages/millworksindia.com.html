State-of-the-Art manufacturing solutions for Aerospace, Railways and Semiconductor

Millworks Technologies Limited is a AS9100D certified company located in Bengaluru, India. Established in the year 2021 is engaged in manufacturing precision machined components, sheet metal parts, sub assembly and assemblies for Aerospace, Railways and Semiconductor industry. Company is spread across 40,000 sqft in four manufacturing plants.
