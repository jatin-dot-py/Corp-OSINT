About Vivanza

Improving Lives Through Quality Pharmaceuticals

Vivanza is the bridge between people, Medical Practitioners and the Patients — together we are a healthcare organization dedicated to improving the lives of people globally.

Headquartered in Gujarat, India, Vivanza Biosciences Limited came into existence in April 2016 with the sole aim to further spread its wings in the global market with global quality pharmaceutical products at competitive pricing.
