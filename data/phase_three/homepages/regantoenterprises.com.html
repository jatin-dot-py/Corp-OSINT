About Our Company

Driven by Quality. Powered by Innovation.

REGANTO ENTERPRISES LIMITED (formerly known as Vintron Informatics Limited) stands as a dynamic and multifaceted organization at the forefront of national infrastructure development and integrated technology solutions. With a robust foundation in core civil construction, we have strategically diversified into allied sectors that support and modernize the built environment. Our primary focus is the engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) of critical national infrastructure, including highways, railways, airports, and complex bridge & tunnel projects.

We complement our core expertise with specialized divisions in Strategic Manpower Solutions (for infra & IT) and cutting-edge Information Technology & Network Management services. This unique synergy allows us to offer end-to-end solutions, ensuring that the physical infrastructure we build is intelligent, efficient, and future-ready. Our dedicated air cargo logistics arm further enhances our supply chain capabilities for time-sensitive project components.
