Weaving Traditions in Every Stitch

Enter the world of Siyaram’s, where heritage converges with innovation.
Since 1978, our journey has been woven with care, evolving from modest
beginnings to becoming one of the top clothing brands in India,
known for timeless elegance and craftsmanship.

Our Story.

For over four decades,

into the fabric of the nation.

The Beginning

From a single thread of vision in 1978 to shaping India’s
fabric story, Siyaram’s has carried the soul of Indian
textiles into every era, earning its place among
leading men's clothing brands.

The Rise

Our rise from fabric to fashion, was a seamless
extension of our founding vision, one that
embraced evolution while staying rooted.

Siyaram’s Today

Today, Siyaram’s stands at the intersection of
legacy and leadership. With a global presence,
award-winning recognition, and bold new ventures,
we continue to weave the future.
