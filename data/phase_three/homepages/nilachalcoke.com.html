About Us

We are the major Ferro Alloys Grade LAM Coke / Nut Coke manufacturer based in Odisha. Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Ltd. is the name that comes to mind while finding a reliable supplier of Low Ash Low Phos Metallurgical Coke. We also produce Ultra Low Phos LAM Coke. We are also a major player in supplying Metallurgical Coke Fines to all major iron ore pellets and sintering plants.

Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Limited operates a cutting-edge facility in Chadheidhara, Jajpur, Odisha, with three non-recovery, Bee Hive-type coke oven batteries and a production capacity of 60,000 MTPA. We also have an additional 42,000 MTPA capacity through tolling arrangements, located in Odisha and Andhra Pradesh.

To further enhance our production, we plan to expand our Jajpur plant by 48,000 MT with two new coke plants, increasing our total capacity to 150,000 MTPA.
