Who We Are

     Hamps Bio Ltd. with Pharma Formulation (1st Division) start journey in 2007 & after a couple of a year it become renowned brand name in the industry. In initial phase of establishment Company has start working as traditional Pharma marketing company & start appointing stockiest/distributor in Gujarat region as a part of it company has wide network of authorize stockiest/distributor channel in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh to cover it’s medicine brand. It’s distribution network such a strong to cover huge no of Retailer/Chemist of their area. We are having almost 39 register brand, which represent company brand journey such a strong way. Which help brand asset to cater more region & market easily in future. Our product therapeutic portfolio includes Anti Hypertensive, Cardiac, Anti-Diabetic, Analgesic, Anti Pyretic, PPI, Anti Ulcer, Vitamins, Minerals, Iron, Derma Products, Anti-Bacterial, Anti-biotics, General Antibiotics Beta-Lactams & Non-Beta-Lactams, Cephalosporin products. Our more than 31 MR, ASM, DRM, RSM team cover almost all territory HQ of Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh & establish strong network of company. With professionally train well experience team company cover almost 8000 doctor & 8000 Medical/Chemist every month which cater almost 60000 Unit every month sales for it’s pharma division product.

      Hamps Bio Ltd is consistent in delivering quality innovative Freeze Dried Ingredient (2nd Division) for B2b, B2c and Domestic/Export market. With brand name “FzyEzy” our company is having strong presence in online E-Commerce market like Amazon Global, Flipkart, JioMart Freeze dried fruit, and healthy snack category. Our company serving in Freeze dried Fruit, Freeze dried Vegetables, Freeze dried Herbs, Freeze dried Flower for B2b customer manufacturing & branding in Flavors Tea, Soup Industry and Nutrition bar Industry, Chocolate Industry and etc. We are supplying our freeze dried ingredient not only to domestic client but our export client from USA, UAE, Canada, Europe. We are having presence in Amazon USA, Amazon Canada, Amazon Europe in Grocery Category with FzyEzy brand, which enhance the brand value strong foot work to grow like anything in next level of marketing & growth for company.
