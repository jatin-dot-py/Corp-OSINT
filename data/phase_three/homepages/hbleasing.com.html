About Us

HB Leasing and Finance Company Limited was incorporated on 13th March, 1982, with a commitment to providing financial solutions and investment opportunities. As a registered Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) under the Reserve Bank of India, we specialize in proprietary investments in stocks and securities. Over the years, we have established a strong presence in the financial sector by maintaining a disciplined approach to investment management and adhering to regulatory standards.

Our expertise lies in navigating the dynamic world of financial markets, ensuring value creation for our stakeholders through strategic investments and sound financial practices.
