QUALITY IS NOT AN ACT IT’S A HABIT WHICH IS DEVELOPED BY YEARS OF EXPERIENCE

Samrat Pharmachem Limited was incorporated on 16th June, 1992, at Ankleshwar in Gujarat State of India. It was promoted by Mr. Lalit Mehta & Mr. Rajesh Mehta.

The Company is currently manufacturing Iodine Derivatives. The Company has one of the widest range of Iodine derivatives. The finished products produced by the Company are used in various industries like Pharmaceuticals, Chemicals, Food, Fertilizer, Salt etc.

OUR MISSION

We at Samrat Pharmachem Limited think of ourselves as a different kind of company, doing our part to make the world a better place. This pursuit is realized through an unwavering commitment to leadership in everything we do. We don’t just see to the needs of our customers, we also strive endlessly to surpass them.

OUR VISION

Samrat uses indigenous technologies to improve the quality and productivity and also to reduce its operating time and manufacturing cost. The quality of our products is continuously improved by innovations in production process and testing methods. Samrat continues to explore and implement measures that will help in conservation and saving of time, money and energy.
