Welcome   To Our Company Website
 

ALFRED HERBERT (INDIA) LIMITED was incorporated in 1919 and was in the business as a major supplier of state of the art, machine tools and other Industrial Machinery from abroad. The manufacturing division was established in 1973 at Whitefield, Bangalore and has manufacturing innovative machines for the Rubber and Tyre Industry besides High Precision, Jig Boring Machines.
