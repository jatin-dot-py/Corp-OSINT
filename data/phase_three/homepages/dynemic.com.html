Welcome to Dynemic Products Ltd., a beacon of excellence in the realm of food colors. As a distinguished ISO 9001, ISO 14001, FSSC 22000, and GMP Certified Company, we take immense pride in our role as one of the foremost manufacturers and exporters of food colors worldwide. Our comprehensive portfolio encompasses Food Colors, Lake Colors, Blended Colors, FD&C Colors, and Dye Intermediates.

Our journey commenced in 1990, driven by a team of visionary entrepreneurs, each bringing expertise in their respective domains. We embarked on the path of manufacturing Dye Intermediates, pioneering the production of 1 4 SPCP and other pyrazolone-based intermediates, laying the groundwork for our future endeavors.
