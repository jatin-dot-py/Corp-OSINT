Voltamp Transformers Limited, Vadodara (INDIA) is in the business of design, manufacture, and supplies of transformers since last 61 years, having 70000 installations in the country and overseas.

VOLTAMP’s focus is quality conscious product users in corporate sector and private utility segment and is the largest supplier of energy efficient transformers to PSUs, MNCs and large co-operatives: 95% of listed corporates and MNCs in the country are regular customers of VOLTAMP.
