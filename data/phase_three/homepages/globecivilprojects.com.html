    Welcome to Globe Civil Projects Limited

    Globe Civil Projects Limited is a leading infrastructure development company with a proven track record of delivering high-quality projects across India. With decades of experience in road construction, bridge building, and urban infrastructure development, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner.

    🏗️ Turning Vision into Value

    Globe Civil Projects Limited is one of the leading providers of construction and infrastructure development related services. The Group’s service offering in the fields of development, construction, services and operation covers the entire life cycle of infrastructure projects, real estate and facilities.

    Renowned for engineering excellence and innovative solutions, the company specializes in complex market segments such as Infrastructure, Buildings and other Civil and Structural Engineering.

    ⭐ Safety

    Globe Civil Projects Limited greatest concern is the safety of our workforce. We are dedicated to accomplish every project in the safest possible manner fostering a culture where safety is everyone’s responsibility. Continually instilling the responsibility in all our employees that they have the duty to prevent an unsafe work place. Our corporate safety training forms nearly 65% of all training initiatives, reinforcing our goal of “No One Gets Hurt.” We continuously strive to create and maintain workplaces that are free from hazards, ensuring the well-being of every individual on our sites.
