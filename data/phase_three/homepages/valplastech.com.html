About Company

Valplast Technologies Ltd is having expertise in speciality services for water tightness of underground structures against pressurized and non-pressurized water, as well as injection/grouting works. Main areas of application include large-scale projects in tunnelling and infrastructure, as well as in the hydraulic structures. Valplast Technologies Pvt Ltd has become a prominent name in India in tunnel waterproofing, geomembrane application and Injection/Pre-Injection grouting work. The company operational in India since 2014 and did several jobs in other areas like Coastal Protection, Construction of retaining wall and slope stabilisation using Geocell.
