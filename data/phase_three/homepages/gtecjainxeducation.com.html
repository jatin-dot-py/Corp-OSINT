Frequently Asked Questions

What sets G-TEC JAINx Education apart from other institutions?
G-TEC JAINx Education stands out for its commitment to academic rigor and its distinctive educational approach that integrates practical assignments, in-depth case studies, project-driven learning, and industry-relevant seminars for an all-encompassing education. Additionally, we provide our students with the advantage of earning industry-recognized certifications from esteemed entities, including Tally and Microsoft, further enhancing their professional standing and employability.
How long has G-TEC JAINx Education been in operation?
G-TEC JAINx Education boasts an impressive legacy of more than 27 years in the realm of IT education and training. With a robust network of over 700 centers spread across 19 countries, we have reached the significant milestone of imparting knowledge and skills to over 2.8 million students worldwide.
Who are the promoters behind G-TEC JAINx Education?
G-TEC JAINx Education is enriched by the expertise and visionary leadership of prominent educationists and entrepreneurs. This includes Mr. Sudhakar Sonawane, the founder of Keerti Computers in Mumbai, which has been instrumental in computer education in the region and Dr. Chenraj Roychand, the Chancellor of JAIN (Deemed-to-be University), known for his substantial contributions to educational development. Additionally, We have the entrepreneurial spirit of Mr. Mehroof Manalody, the innovative founder and Chairman of G-TEC Group of Education. Together, they forge a formidable team dedicated to advancing IT education and training globally.
