What we do?

Established in the year 1988, Belrise Industries specializes in component manufacturing for Automotive & White Goods Industries. With a turnover of INR 7484.2 CR, our diversified product portfolio consists of Automotive Sheet Metal & Casting parts, Polymer components, Suspension & Mirror systems specifically for Two-Wheeler, Three-Wheeler and Four-Wheeler Passenger and Commercial vehicles. Also started delivering eMobility components & Subsystem. Our core strength lies in “Delivering Customer Success through Agile and Robust Manufacturing”.
