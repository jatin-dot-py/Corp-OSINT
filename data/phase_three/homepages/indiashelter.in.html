Important Security Advisory: Alert Regarding Fraudulent / Suspicious Activity in the Name of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (“India Shelter”)


Dear Valued Customer,

It has come to our attention that certain unscrupulous individuals and entities are involved in fraudulent or deceptive activities such as demanding cash or fund transfer in an unauthorized manner to an individual’s bank account as processing fees/ EMI or any other charges against customer’s loan account with us, attempting to deceive our existing or prospective esteemed customers. Please be advised that India Shelter does not partake in any such practices.

Such claims do not have any association with India Shelter and any request for cash payments or fund transfers to personal bank accounts in the context of loan-related matters is both fraudulent and illegal. Such transactions are not recorded in India Shelter’s systems and do not fall under the responsibility of India Shelter. We request you to deal with India Shelter directly through its existing branch offices only, whose addresses are available on our website www.indiashelter.in

To ensure your financial well-being, we strongly urge you to exercise caution and diligently verify the authenticity of any communication or document that claims to be from India Shelter before proceeding with any payments or transactions.

If you have any doubts, require assistance, or wish to verify the legitimacy of a communication, please reach out to our branch, contact us via email at customer.care@indiashelter.in , or call our toll-free number at 1800-572-8888.

Your safety and security are our top priorities. Thank you for your attention to this matter.

FAQs

What makes India Shelter one of the best home loan companies in India?

India Shelter is a trusted housing finance company committed to making homeownership more accessible, especially for customers in tier 2 and tier 3 cities. With flexible eligibility criteria, minimal documentation, quick loan processing, and personalized support, we help individuals and families access a home loan with greater ease and confidence.

Where does India Shelter provide home loans in India?

India Shelter operates through 300+ branches across 15 states,offering affordable home loans to first-time buyers and self-employed individuals. You can visit your nearest branch or apply online from anywhere in India.

How do I apply for a home loan in India with India Shelter?

You can apply for a home loan online through our website or by visiting any India Shelter branch. Our team will guide you through the eligibility check,  document submission, and loan application process.

Is India Shelter a good housing finance company for self-employed individuals?

Yes. India Shelter offers home loans to both salaried and self-employed individuals. We understand diverse income profiles and may consider alternative income assessment methods where applicable.

What is the interest rate on a home loan in India from India Shelter?

Home loan interest rates depend on factors such as your credit profile, income, loan amount, and repayment tenure. Contact our team or visit our website for the latest applicable rates.

Can I get an affordable home loan in India if my CIBIL score is low?

Yes. While your credit score is an important factor, India Shelter also considers your income stability, repayment capacity, and overall financial profile when assessing your application for an affordable home loan in India.

How fast is the home loan approval process at India Shelter?

At India Shelter, most eligible home loan applications are processed within a few working days, subject to document verification, property assessment, and eligibility checks. This is what makes us the preferred choice for borrowers looking for a quick home loan approval.

What is the maximum home loan amount I can get from India Shelter?

India Shelter offers home loans of up to ₹1 Crore, subject to eligibility and applicable policies. The final loan amount depends on factors such as your income, repayment capacity, property value, existing financial obligations, and loan tenure.

How do I contact India Shelter's customer support for a home loan in India?

You can call us at 1800-572-8888, email customercare@indiashelter.in, visit our website, or give a missed call on 7065515122. Our team is available to assist you throughout the home loan process.

Are there any hidden charges when applying for a home loan with India Shelter?

No. India Shelter follows a transparent fee structure and clearly communicates all applicable charges during the home loan application process.
