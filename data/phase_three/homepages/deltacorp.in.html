India’s Only Listed Gaming & Hospitality Company

Delta Corp Limited (Delta Corp) has established itself as India's only listed operator in the casino gaming industry. With a strategic vision and a strong reputation for diversification, the company is committed to delivering considerable value to its stakeholders.

About US
Driving Real Growth,
Delivering Lasting Impact

At Delta Corp, we are confident in braving the future with greater determination and courage, and in making ourselves more resilient to economic cycles. Through our strategic business initiatives, we are propelling the growth trajectory, embodying the spirit that defines our presence in the Gaming, Hospitality, and real estate industries.

Founded in 1990, Delta Corp began as a textiles and real estate consultancy before strategically diversifying into leisure and entertainment. Over the years, the company has built a vertically integrated model comprising offshore and land-based casinos and hospitality assets, some designed to serve casino guests, others operating as independent properties.

With a focus on regulatory compliance, operational efficiency, and long-term value creation, Delta Corp continues to expand its presence in India’s evolving entertainment economy.

About Our Business
Strategic Pillars of Our Business

Delta Corp holds three of the six offshore gaming licenses issued in Goa and operates a land-based casinos in Goa. Properties such as Deltin Royale, and Deltin JAQK offer a range of gaming formats, from traditional table games to electronic machines. With over 1,400 gaming positions, the company maintains a leadership position in India’s organized casino industry.

Casino Gaming

Delta Corp holds three of the six offshore gaming licenses issued in Goa and operates a land-based casino in Goa. Properties such as Deltin Royale, Deltin Jaqk and Deltin Suites Casino offer a range of gaming formats, from traditional table games to electronic machines. With over 1,400 gaming positions, the company maintains a leadership position in India’s organized casino industry.

Hospitality

Hospitality assets at Delta Corp are designed to complement the gaming business as well as serve independent guests. The company owns and operates Deltin Suites in Goa and The Deltin in Daman, both the five-star properties equipped with recreational and MICE facilities.

Real Estate

The real estate portfolio at Delta Corp is focused on creating high-value assets with strong long-term potential. Leveraging expertise in redevelopment and quick-turnaround projects, Delta Corp is strengthening its presence in the real estate sector and capitalising on its growth potential. The Company maintains a prudent investment approach, backed by the promoter group’s deep-rooted experience, reinforcing confidence in generating sustainable, long-term value.
