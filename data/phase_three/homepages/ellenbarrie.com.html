About Ellenbarrie

Welcome to Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Limited

Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Ltd (EIGL) is one of India's oldest industrial gases companies, with over 50 years of legacy and the largest 100% Indian-owned firm in terms of manufacturing capacity, revenue, and profitability in Fiscal 2024. Operating one of India's largest oxygen plants (1250 TPD), it held a 2.65% market share by revenue. EIGL produces various industrial gases, including oxygen, nitrogen, argon, and hydrogen, serving industries like shipbuilding, steel, pharmaceuticals, and defense. It is a key supplier to India's space and defense programs and also offers synthetic air for environmental monitoring.
