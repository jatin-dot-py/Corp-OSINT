LAKSHMI PRECISION SCREWS LIMITED

The Company, established in the year 1972 has four plants equipped with high tensile manufacturing facilities. The Company is a pioneer in providing fastening technology globally having Joint Ventures, License Agreements, and Alliances with different fastening companies globally. It is also catering the needs of various sectors such as Wind Energy, Oil & Gas, Locomotives, Automobiles, Agriculture Equipments (Tractors), Machine Building and different Industrial and its sub-con items. The endeavor of the Company is to provide high level customer satisfaction.

The Company in the past had been one of the largest exporters of fasteners in India. Whereas 35% of the total sales is contributed from the products exported to a range of countries such as The United States of America (USA), Australia, Canada, Denmark, Dubai, France, Germany, Hong-Kong, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, Singapore, South Africa, Switzerland, Sweden etc. The Company is a partner of the Global Fastener Alliance (GFA).

Location, land & Building
Three units of the Company are located in Rohtak (Haryana) which has a close proximity being just sixty Kms. from the National Capital, New Delhi and falls in the northern region of India. The Company set up another plant in the year 1993 keeping in view the growing global demand. In the recent past the company had set operational two more units, one in Rohtak and another in Industrial Modal Town, Manesar where leading manufacturers such as Hero Honda, Honda Motors Scooters India, Maruti Suzuki, JCB, Yamaha, Winwind, Tata Motors (Fiat), TATA, Ashok Leyland, Volvo, Suzlon, TVS etc. etc. are also established. The said manufacturers are also the customers of the Company.

Collectively all the four units of this Company are spread over an area of 1, 00,000 square meters and have a total production capacity of 25,000 metric tons (MT) per annum.

The company’s manufacturing units and warehouses are built keeping in view the close proximity of its Ancillaries, Raw Material providers and National Highways.
