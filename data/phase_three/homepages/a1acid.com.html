About Us

A-1 Limited (Formerly known as A-1 Acid & Chemicals) is a more then 4 decades old firm. It has a proven track record as the growth rate itself is an evidence of this achievement.

Since our inception, we have been providing high quality and prompt services to our customers across India. Commenced its operation at a low key level way back in 1975, it has seen an overwhelming growth at an unmatched rate over the years. Company continues to surge ahead with its steady growth in terms of its expanding market network which in translates into increased turnover on every succeeding years..
