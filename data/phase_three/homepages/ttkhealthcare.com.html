The future is the creation of today

Great ideas alone do not create a better future, Acting on great ideas does. And so did Mr. TT Krishnamachari – Independent India’s first Minister of Commerce and Industry. Born out of his vision to create a brand that would pioneer, innovate and lead, TTK Group began crafting and shaping its future from 1928. Led by his founding vision, today, TTK Healthcare is one of India’s most well-known and oldest companies. Since then, we’ve forged ahead and become pioneers in our chosen categories of consumer and healthcare products that define the business category as a whole.

The history behind the legacy

Deeply rooted in the minds of Indians for generations, our rich heritage is a testament of quality and reliability. The actual journey began in the early 1930s. We took our first step as a market-making partner and introduced several foreign products into the Indian market. Building on this experience, we moved into the realm of manufacturing. We created a Division specifically for the manufacturing and distribution and of consumer healthcare products. This was the seed that planted the idea of TTK Healthcare. And TTK healthcare came to being in 1958.

A belief system called TTK

More than just being determined professionals, we at TTK like to call ourselves ‘self-believers’. Our belief in the organization and ourselves is what propels us to reach deep within and better ourselves every day. This self-belief has translated into crafting innovative products that add value to the lives of our customers and translates into absolute ‘value for money’ for our customers. With us, trust, capability, and reliability are more than words - It is the very core of our existence. It allows us to tread a path that is extremely customer-centric. Which is why each of our product truly enriches the lives of our customers

Diversity is our Unity

With a plethora of diverse products and services, our unified approach to diverse products across a broad spectrum of business categories is what sets us apart in the Indian Market. Our identity is based on our innovative and quality approach to each and every single product across our portfolio. Over the decades, this core facet of innovation and quality has successfully led us to venture into varied market sectors such as consumer products, pharmaceuticals, medical devices and food products.

The Home base of some of India’s most reputed brands.

At a time when Indians looked for quality and reliable products outside of India, We, at TTK brought the world to India. We introduced the Indian market to world-famous brands. Products from the stables of the world’s most recognizable brands like Durex, Woodwards, 3M, Scotch-Brite, Dr. Scholl’s, Kiwi shoe polish and Brylcreem were launched in the Indian Markets by us. And we have also launched many of our own brands like Eva, Good Home & Skore under the Consumer Products Division. Ossopan and Epidosin through the Pharmaceuticals Division and Heart Valve from the Medical Devices Division are just a few to name.

We touch lives every day, every hour.

Our emphasis, at TTK Healthcare has always been on building a wide network to reach out and make a discernable difference in the lives of every Indian consumer that we touch. With 2,800 authorized wholesale dealers and over 4,00,000+ outlets spread across the country, we as an organisation reach and touch more and more Indian lives every day. Our Pharmaceuticals Division has a network of more than 3000 distributors across the country.
