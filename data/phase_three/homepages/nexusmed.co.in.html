About Us

Nexus Surgical and Medicare Limited having Company’s CIN L33100MH1992PLC328367, incorporate on 8th January, 1992 under Companies Act, 1956. The Company’s Equity Shares were listed on Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) on 8th January, 2015 having BSE Code 538874.

Nexus Surgical and Medicare Limited is involved is Importing and Marketing Quality Surgical and Pharmaceutical Products at an Economical Price. The Company is a wholesale dealer dealing in various sizes of disposal syringe (with and without needle) and pouches of various size used for storage of sterilized equipment and materials.
