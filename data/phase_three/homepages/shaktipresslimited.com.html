At the onset, we would like to introduce ourselves as the market leader in Printing and Packaging Industry in Central India. SHAKTI PRESS LIMITED (SPL) is a premier provider of Total solutions for high quality packaging and hi-tech printing under one roof. Sixty years up the lane, we have kept pace with the latest technology, rendering unsurpassable quality and services to our clients.

When it comes to product packaging and it’s printing to create first impression there is one name you can trust on absolutely – SHAKTI PRESS LIMITED (SPL).

 

Our Esteemed Clients

SHAKTI PRESS LIMITED has another feather in its cap in being consumer dealers for all major brands of paper and board like ITC, Ballarpur and TNPL. To add to that, they have their ink manufacturing in-house. The Company prepares their own special colors to avoid duplicity of the packaging printed at their press. SPL believes in printing as their line of specialization and using this platform the company intends to provide a wide range of products in the form of packaging, stationery and publishing.

SHAKTI PRESS LIMITED (SPL) is been backed and supported by experienced professionals in the field of Sales / Marketing Operations / Production, Accounts / Finance and General Administration.It has the right infrastructure to design, manufacture and prepare art works for any kind of print jobs whether it is for leaflets, brochures, cartons, labels, tags, stickers, danglers, wrappers, corrugated boxes etc.We have our Paper Stationery Division which manufacturers Note books, Copier Paper, Rule papers, Exercise Books for indigenous and Export Quality.
