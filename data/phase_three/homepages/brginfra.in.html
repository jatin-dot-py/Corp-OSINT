Our Journey

Our Journey began more than three decades ago when a group of visionaries started from a partnership firm named “Balkrishna Ramkaran Goyal” formed via a partnership deed dated August 4, 1986. The partners of the firm mutually settled their rights of accounts, and was thereafter converted from a partnership firm to a private limited company in the name of “ B R Goyal Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. “ Under the Provision of Companies Act, 1956 vide certificate of incorporation dated April 1, 2005 at Indore, M.P. Subsequently, our Company was converted into a Public Limited company certificate of Incorporation dated May 9, 2018 and its name was changed to “B R Goyal Infrastructure Ltd.”
