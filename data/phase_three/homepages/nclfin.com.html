Welcome to NCL Research & Financial Services Limited

The company was incorporated on 04 Feb.1985 as N.C.L. RESEARCH & FINANCIAL SERVICES LTD. The company is in activities of trading of Textiles products apart from investing activities in Shares and Securities.

The companies one of the RBI registred NBFC company and is into the business finance as well as investments in Shares and Securities.

Recent Updates

The company had issued bonus shares in the ratio of 4:1 on the record Date 17 October 2014

We would like to place on record that the Company has not been able to do the Textile Business in last few Quarters because of lack of business opportunities in the Textile industry in the recent past. Further, the Company had been carrying the business of advancing loans and investing in Quoted and unquoted securities. However, at present the Company is concentrating mainly on development of NBFC Activities.
