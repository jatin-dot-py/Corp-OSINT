Welcome to RDB Group

RDB Infrastructure and Power Limited
(Formerly known as RDB Realty & Infrastructure Limited).

Led by Sri Sunder Lal Dugar, is one of the leading real estate companies in Eastern India. RDB Infrastructure and Power Limited (Formerly known as RDB Realty & Infrastructure Limited). has pan India presence with all the necessary infrastructure, manpower, and finance. The Company is a part of RDB Group, with diversified interests in packaging, power & telecom transmission equipment, retail chain and transport & logistics. The real estate arm of the RDB Group was born in 1981, with the dream of providing a home to all.
