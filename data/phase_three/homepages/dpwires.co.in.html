About Us

With the Dream to Become the Best Wire Manufacturers in India, DP Wires Ltd. was Established in the Year 1998.

It was a daring feat to start a business of your own back then as there were a lot of mouth which spoke against this idea, however, our perseverance, grit and determination shaped us into what we are today. We have successfully managed to attract thousands of customers from all over the country and most of them were amazed by the quality of the products we manufacture.

OUR PRODUCTS

Our Extensive Experience and Specialized Knowledge Makes us a Leading Manufacturer, Exporter and Supplier of Premium Quality Specialized Steel Wires.

Our Specialized Steel Wires are used in Automobile Industry, Infrastructure Projects such as Dam, Bridge, Flyover, Metro, Bullet Train. in Railways and also in construction of Nuclear Power Plants, Malls and High-Rise Buildings.
