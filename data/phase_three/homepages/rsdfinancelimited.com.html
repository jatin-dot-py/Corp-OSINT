Our History

RSD Finance Limited is a leading Indian Non-Banking Company (NBFC) operating since 1963. The company is registered with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and listed on the Calcutta Stock Exchange (CSE) and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).


As part of our business activities, RSD Finance Ltd is predominantly focused into Investing Funds into capital market as well as in money market. We focus on Investing Activities, Job Work Activities and Real Estate Business.

 

Overview

Investing Activities:

Our company is primarily focused into investing activities such as Deals or transactions involving sale or purchase of equipment, plants, properties, securities, or other assets generally not held for immediate resale.


Job Work Activities:

Company is also engaged into Zinc Plating Business. In which company takes Job for Metal plating which is an intermediate part to manufacturing process.
