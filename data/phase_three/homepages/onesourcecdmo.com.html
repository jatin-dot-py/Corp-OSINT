The new way to CDMO

Complex products require more than manufacturing capacity. They require the ability to align development, analytical, manufacturing, quality, and regulatory activities throughout the product lifecycle.

OneSource is India’s first specialty pharma pure-play CDMO, delivering integrated services across biologics, complex injectables, drug-device combination products, and soft gelatin capsules. Through specialized facilities, multidisciplinary expertise, and a collaborative operating model, we help partners reduce complexity and accelerate execution.

Capabilities built for complex products

Whether developing a biologic, scaling a sterile injectable, integrating a delivery device, or optimizing a softgel, OneSource provides the expertise and infrastructure required to move programs forward efficiently.

Biologics

Integrated development and manufacturing across microbial and mammalian platforms, supporting recombinant proteins, biosimilars, cell and gene therapies, and RNA-based products.

Drug-Device Combination Products

End-to-end development and manufacturing of patient-centric delivery systems, including autoinjectors, pen injectors, and prefilled syringe platforms.

Sterile Injectables

Formulation development, analytical support, sterile manufacturing, lyophilization, and device integration for technically demanding injectable products.

Soft Gelatin Capsules

Advanced softgel technologies supporting complex formulations, bioavailability enhancement, and commercial-scale manufacturing.
