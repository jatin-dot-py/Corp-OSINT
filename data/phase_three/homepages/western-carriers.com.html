What makes us special?

Why Does Western Carriers (India) Limited Stand Out
Among Others?

3PL

We provide 3PL services by combining our offerings of traditional transportation and provision of services with certain ancillary value-added services. Our value-added offerings at various stages in the logistics value chain include but not limited to cargo and material handling, warehouse management, order management, pre-shipment inspection.

Multimodal

Our multi-modal logistics solutions, which includes road, railway, ocean/river/coastal and air logistics services, minimizes the loss of time and risk of loss, pilferage, and damage to cargo at trans-shipment points, reduces multiple layers of documentation and procedures and helps reduce costs.

Single Window Logistics

We operate on a scalable, asset-light business model which enables us to provide differentiated 3PL and 4PL solutions. We endeavour to address complexities by creating customized, one-stop/single-window, end-to-end and integrated logistics solutions for our customers, which involve a variety of value-added services across the supply chain.

Rail

Our rail logistics services are suited to meet the needs of our customer base which can vary from smaller shipments of mini-rakes comprising up to 20 wagons and ‘jumbo rakes’ that support a capacity of up to 2,500 MT.
