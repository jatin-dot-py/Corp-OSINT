Welcome to Stratmont Industries Ltd

 

Stratmont Industries Limited, is a leading trade finance & distribution company specializing in supply chain financing solutions for the construction and infrastructure sectors. Our expertise lies in facilitating commodity sales transactions by offering tailored financial support to corporate customers. We propose a strategic partnership with your company to enhance sales operations by extending credit facilities to corporate clients facing credit limitations, thereby expanding your market reach and profitability. Headquartered in Mumbai, we establish a collaborative lead generation system, in which a two-way communication channel for sharing and acting upon potential customer leads. We specialize in steel commodities like TMT Bars, Structural steel, Plates, coils, Pipes, Wire Rods, Sponge Iron, and Stainless Steel. Apart from steel commodities, we also handle Coke, Coal, Non-ferrous materials, and Bitumen. With a client base of over 300+ companies, we remain committed to driving growth and enabling new business opportunities in the industry.
