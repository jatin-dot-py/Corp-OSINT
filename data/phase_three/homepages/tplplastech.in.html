TPL Plastech

Who are we and What we do

TPL PLASTECH LIMITED is a professionally managed company and India's second largest manufacturer of drums, especially Bulk Packaging i.e. 210 Ltr to 250 Ltr capacities.

Today, the portfolio consists of a comprehensive range of narrow mouth, wide mouth and open top drums ranging from 25 Ltr to 250 Ltr capacity. The Company has a large client base and caters to more than 250 customers in various industries like chemical & petrochemicals, specialty chemicals, pharmaceutical, FMCG, etc.

TPL's products are manufactured from special grade of High Molecular - High Density Polyethylene. This makes drums lightweight, durable, chemically inert, stable, with a high Environmental Stress Cracking Resistance (ESCR) quotient.
