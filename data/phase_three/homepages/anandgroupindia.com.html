ABOUT ANAND

For over six decades, ANAND has been shaping the future of mobility through engineering excellence, innovation, trusted partnerships and a commitment to sustainable growth. What began in 1961 has evolved into a global mobility solutions group, bringing together world-class technologies and deep manufacturing expertise to serve leading automotive manufacturers across the world.

Beyond automotive, the Group continues to expand its impact through complementary businesses and initiatives. ANEVOLVE, ANAND's future-tech platform, is advancing solutions for clean mobility and emerging technologies. SUJÁN redefines experiential luxury through conservation-led hospitality, while the SNS Foundation drives meaningful change through programmes focused on education, skill development, health, hygiene and community conservation.

CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

Transforming Communities. Creating
Opportunity.

Established in 1976, the SNS Foundation (SNSF) is the CSR arm of the ANAND Group focusing on building capabilities, transforming lives and promoting environmental conservation, with its initiatives aligned with the UN SDGs.

Our Focus Areas

Education & Diversity

Partnering with government schools to strengthen education through improved infrastructure and libraries, while empowering girls through the Medhavi Scholarship Programme

Skill Development

Building employability through vocational training, digital literacy, ITI labs, and polytechnic labs, with a focus on women, youth, and persons with disabilities

Health & Hygiene

Improving rural healthcare through mobile medical services, with a focus on HIV/AIDS, reproductive and child health, industrial health and National Health Mission initiatives

Community & Wildlife Conservation

Supporting livelihoods through Self-Help Groups and micro-credit, while supporting sanitation, livestock healthcare and conserving wildlife in Rajasthan
