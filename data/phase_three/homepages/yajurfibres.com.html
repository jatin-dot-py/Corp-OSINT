      Introducing Quality Fibres.

      Yajur Fibres Limited is part of the multi billion conglomerate, 20,000+ workforce strong – The Kankaria Group. YFL is one of the largest bast fibre processing & cottonising unit in India with a large capacity to produce cottonised fibres Flax, Jute & Hemp fibres and over 60 tons of dry spun flax yarn per month. YFL is situated in West Bengal, India.

      Yajur Fibres Limited is dedicated to achieving excellence. It is governed by the 5S principles and with the best-in-class machinery and environment the unit is heavily complied and certified. YFL has already been certified with ISO9000, ISO14000, ISO45000, OEKOTEX 100 and EUROPEAN FLAX certification.
