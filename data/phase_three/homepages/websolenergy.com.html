About Us

Websol is among the earliest manufacturers of solar photovoltaic cells in India

Websol is driving solar energy forward as one of India’s earliest and most trusted solar cell manufacturers, with a strong legacy in photovoltaic cell and solar module manufacturing. Established in 1994, Websol Energy System Ltd operates an advanced solar manufacturing facility at Falta, West Bengal, that supports India’s growing renewable energy ecosystem and global clean energy demand.

Recognised among leading solar cell manufacturers in India and reputed solar cell and module manufacturers in India,Websol combines technology-driven production, stringent quality standards, and decades of expertise to deliver reliable solar cells and solar PV modules for utility-scale, commercial, and industrial applications.

Infrastructure

Websol Energy System operates integrated solar manufacturing plant in India, supported by advanced automation, quality control systems, and skilled technical expertise. As a top solar cell and module manufacturer in India, our infrastructure enables end-to-end manufacturing of solar cells and solar modules, ensuring consistency, traceability, and compliance with international quality standards.
