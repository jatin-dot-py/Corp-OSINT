About Us

Say Good morning to Freshness

Indong is a magnificent Tea Garden in Western Dooars perched in the foothills of a Himalayan Range. Blessed with a bracing climate and a panoramic view of the mountains and meandering rivers, the garden was founded over a century ago by the British Planters under the famous Gillanders & Arbuthnot & Co. Even today, the garden retains many of the British legacies. In the year August 2014, the Company has been taken over by Sri Hariram Garg & his Associates Company who are the Present Promotors of the Company. Under the Leadership of Sri Hariram Garg.

Tea Estate

Indong is a magnificent Tea Garden in Western Dooars perched in the foothills of a Himalayan Range. On the Western boundary is Mateilli town.

Tea Processing Units

The tea production plant is located on the tea estate, so that the freshly picked tea leaves can be processed immediately - the first step on the way to the superior quality. In order to produce high quality of Tea, Tea Estate have installed New modern machinery & Equipment. Black Tea is produced in Five Steps namely Withering, rolling & Cutting, Fermentation, Drying and Sorting.
