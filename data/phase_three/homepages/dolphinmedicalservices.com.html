A Legacy Built on Trust and Precision

Dolphin Medical Services Ltd. has been a cornerstone of healthcare in Vijayawada since 1993. What began as a focused diagnostic centre has grown into a multi-division medical services organisation serving patients, clinicians, and healthcare partners across Andhra Pradesh.

Today we operate five distinct medical divisions covering diagnostic imaging, specialist eye care, laboratory and pathology services, life sciences and biotech research support, and medical equipment supply and servicing. Each division reflects our founding commitment: to deliver accurate, accessible, and compassionate healthcare.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services, diagnostics, eye care, and other healthcare facilities offered by Dolphin Medical Services Ltd.
What services does Dolphin Medical Services Ltd. provide?

Dolphin Medical Services Ltd. offers comprehensive diagnostic services including Radiology & Imaging, Biochemistry, Pathology, and Microbiology testing.

When was Dolphin Medical Services Ltd. established?

The company has been serving the coastal region of Andhra Pradesh since 1994.

Who are the promoters of the company?

The organization is promoted by renowned doctors including Dr. M. S. S. Koteswara Rao and Dr. G. V. Mohan Prasad, who is also the Managing Director.
