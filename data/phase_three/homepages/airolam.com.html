About US

Why Airolam?

Airolam is a young and growing company with commendable 14+ years of experience in the laminates industry. Established in 2008, today Airolam and its products reach over 18 countries all around the world. On the domestic front it has 12+ company depots, 100+ authorized distributors and 5000+ dealers.

 

Airolam has two state of the art manufacturing facilities dedicated to the production of laminates. With 4 presses, the annual combined capacity of these facilities in 19 million sq. meter in addition, Airolam also boasts of a highly advanced plywood manufacturing facility.
