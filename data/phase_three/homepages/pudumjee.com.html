Our Story

Innovating with Purpose

Founded in 1964 in Pune, India, Pudumjee Paper Products Limited  has over six decades of expertise as one of India’s most trusted paper manufacturing companies, specialising in sustainable and specialty paper solutions. We provide eco-friendly packaging solutions for pharmaceuticals, FMCG, and hygiene industries, offering sustainable alternatives to single-use plastics.

Serving clients across 13+ countries, we prioritize long-term partnerships and customer-focused solutions. With a vision to double capacity by 2030, we continue to lead with innovation and sustainability
