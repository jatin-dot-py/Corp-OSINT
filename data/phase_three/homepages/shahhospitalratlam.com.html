FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Shah Hospital provide treatments for general health conditions?

Yes, Shah Hospital is a complete multi-specialty facility offering treatment for general medicine, maternity, pediatrics, diagnostic imaging, and emergency care.

What is the benefit of choosing robotic surgery over routine surgery?

Robotic surgery uses micro-incisions to offer maximum precision, reduced scarring, minimal blood loss, and significantly faster patient recovery.

Are emergency services available 24/7 at Shah Hospital?

Yes, our Emergency, ICU, and Trauma care departments operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week with specialists on standby.

How do international patients plan a robotic surgery at Shah Hospital?

Simply share your medical records online. Our team provides a pre-treatment plan, virtual doctor consultation, visa invitation letters, and travel assistance.
