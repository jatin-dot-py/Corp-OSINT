Frequently Asked Questions

What is Harshdeep India?

Harshdeep India is a leading manufacturer and supplier of indoor and outdoor pots and planters, headquartered in Bhiwandi, Mumbai. The company offers an extensive range of planters for both home users and professionals, crafted from materials such as recycled plastic, biodegradable blends, and recycled wood-composite materials.

Where is Harshdeep India located?

Harshdeep India operates primarily from Bhiwandi, Thane, Maharashtra, where its main manufacturing and distribution facility is located. The company’s registered address is in the Elkunde village area of Bhiwandi. Beyond its headquarters, Harshdeep has retail stores in cities like Ahmedabad and Pune, supported by a nationwide distribution network.

When was Harshdeep India founded?

Harshdeep was established by Hitesh Chundilal Shah around 1999–2000. While some sources cite 1999 as the founding year, the company was officially incorporated as Harshdeep Hortico Ltd. in 2000 and has since grown into a publicly listed organization.

Who are the customers of Harshdeep India?

Architects, interior designers, landscapers, nurseries, hotels, builders, retail customers, and online shoppers.

Does Harshdeep India manufacture its planters in-house?

Yes, all products are designed and manufactured in-house to maintain superior quality and consistency.

Are Harshdeep planters made from recyclable materials?

The Eco Series planters are made from 100% recyclable, environmentally conscious materials.

Do they offer custom planter designs?

Yes. For large projects, custom sizes, colors, and finishes can be developed.
