A Man Who Could Have Kept Driving

On his way to work one morning in 2025, Jitendra passed a wrecked scooter with an injured couple stranded on the road while traffic rushed by. He kept driving too, assuming someone else would stop.

He admitted this part quietly when he told the story, not out of shame, but because he understood how easily people expect someone else to act first.

Something about the scene stayed with him, though, and a few minutes down the road he turned back. He stopped an autorickshaw and took the couple to a government hospital himself.

Most people would have left it there, but Jitendra stayed. He gave a witness statement, registered an FIR, and held his ground even when the accused pressured and tried to intimidate him into retracting it.

He's a quiet, soft-spoken man who doesn't like fuss, and when he talks about that morning, he doesn't dwell on the part where he turned back. He talks more about the FIR, and about how long it took, and about the fact that he never really considered dropping it, even when it would have been easier to.

Such quiet courage keeps humanity alive.

Leading private bank

Reducing HR operational costs by 20% and accelerating case resolution for 50,000+ employees

ServiceNow HRSD AI-Powered Search BFSI

What

Replaced a fragmented, multi-system HR landscape with a single Employee Center — unifying case management, knowledge search, and profile updates across ServiceNow and Oracle HR.

The outcome: faster resolutions, consistent records, and a self-service experience for 50,000+ employees.

Why

HR services were scattered across systems, forcing employees to juggle multiple applications for leave, payroll, benefits, and profile updates — while duplicate data entry created compliance risk and inconsistent records.

The bank needed one connected platform to close these gaps without disrupting Oracle as the system of record.

How

InfoBeans built a Unified Employee Center on ServiceNow HRSD, consolidating 85+ HR services into a single, standardized catalog with automated case creation and routing. AI-powered Enterprise Knowledge Search connected ServiceNow with external repositories using Zero-Copy Metadata Indexing, so employees could search everything from one place without duplicating content. A dedicated Profile Synchronization workflow, powered by FlowAPI Runner, let employees update personal details once and sync them securely to Oracle in real time.

Together, these capabilities cut HR operational costs by 15-20%, sped up case resolution by 20-25%, and reduced manual profile-update effort by 40-50% — giving employees a consistent, always-on HR experience.

Industrial B2B Distribution

Driving 5x customer growth and 50% faster case routing via unified Salesforce Agentforce

Salesforce Agentforce Data 360 Marketing Cloud

What

Consolidated fragmented service, sales, and marketing operations — spanning CRM, SAP Commerce Cloud, and digital platforms — into one AI-driven Salesforce ecosystem serving both customers and internal teams.

The outcome: automated case classification, AI-generated quotes, and a unified customer view powering personalization at scale.

Why

Manual processes were slowing the business: 3,000-3,500 support emails a day sorted and routed by hand, repetitive quote generation reliant on individual product expertise, and customer data siloed across systems with no single view of the customer.

The organization needed automation to scale its engagement model and convert fragmented data into predictive, personalized action.

How

Agentforce introduced AI-driven case classification, brand identification, and automated routing, alongside AI-assisted quote drafting and response generation. Data 360 unified Customer 360 through identity resolution across CRM, ERP, Commerce, and digital channels, while Marketing Cloud powered real-time segmentation, 25+ automated omnichannel journeys, and predictive models for next-best-action and propensity. Experience Cloud and CPQ delivered a self-service portal with guided selling and automated pricing, backed by integrations with Factwise, e-commerce, and chatbot/CTI systems.

Together, these integrations reduced case routing turnaround time by 40-50%, cut manual quote creation effort by 50-70%, and scaled the Believer Score engagement program 5x — from 2 lakh to 1 million customers.
