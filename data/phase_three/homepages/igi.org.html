Verify Report

One of the World's Largest Independent Diamond Grading and Accreditation Services Providers

With 37 laboratories and 21 schools in 10 countries, IGI employs a global workforce of experts and educators dedicated to industry service and consumer protection.

Verify Your Report

Whether purchasing a natural diamond, lab grown diamond, emerald, sapphire, ruby, or finished jewelry piece, certification helps consumers and professionals make informed decisions.

Independent grading reports provide objective information about quality, authenticity, treatments, and value factors, making certification one of the most important considerations when buying, selling, or insuring gemstones and jewelry.

With 31 laboratories and 18 schools in 10 countries, IGI employs a global workforce of experts and educators dedicated to industry service and consumer protection.

IGI’s supreme position in the gemological world is no coincidence. It is the result of continuous research, support, and synergy with professionals and consumers alike. This dedication is reflected by worldwide trust for IGI certified diamonds and IGI certified gemstones, which uphold the highest industry standards.

 

Why Diamond and Gemstone Certification Matters

>Independent certification provides a standardized way to evaluate diamonds, gemstones, and jewelry. A professional grading report documents characteristics that influence quality and value, helping buyers compare stones with greater confidence.

For diamonds, certification evaluates the internationally recognized 4Cs: cut, clarity, color, and carat weight. These factors play a significant role in determining diamond value and allow consumers to understand exactly what they are purchasing.

For colored gemstones such as emeralds, rubies, and sapphires, certification identifies species, variety, geographic origin when determinable, and any treatments that may affect value. Accurate gemstone certification provides transparency and supports informed purchasing decisions throughout the jewelry marketplace.

FAQ’s

Why is diamond certification important when purchasing a diamond?

Diamond certification provides an independent assessment of a diamond’s characteristics, including its cut, color, clarity, and carat weight. A grading report allows consumers to compare diamonds using standardized criteria and helps support informed purchasing decisions.

What information is included in a diamond grading report?

A diamond grading report typically documents the diamond’s 4Cs, measurements, identifying characteristics, plotting information where applicable, and whether the stone is natural or laboratory-grown. These details provide a consistent description of the diamond’s quality and characteristics.

How does gemstone certification differ from diamond certification?

While diamond grading focuses primarily on the 4Cs, gemstone certification may evaluate species, variety, treatments, and other identifying characteristics. Colored gemstones often require different assessment criteria than diamonds due to their unique properties and market considerations.

Can a grading report determine a diamond’s value?

A grading report does not assign a monetary value to a diamond. Instead, it provides objective grading information that consumers, jewelers, insurers, and appraisers may use when evaluating quality and estimating market value.

How can I verify the authenticity of an IGI grading report?

Consumers can use IGI’s online report verification tools to confirm the authenticity of an IGI grading report and review the information associated with a certified diamond, gemstone, or jewelry item.

What is the difference between IGI and GIA?

IGI and GIA are both respected gemological laboratories, but they are often used in different parts of the diamond and jewelry market. GIA has long-standing recognition in natural diamond grading, while IGI is recognized globally for grading both natural and laboratory-grown diamonds, finished diamond and gemstone jewelry, and serving international retail markets. For many consumers, IGI offers a strong combination of international recognition, practical report formats, and broad accessibility. IGI grading reports identify key quality factors such as cut, color, clarity, carat weight, measurements, and natural or laboratory-grown origin, helping buyers understand exactly what they are purchasing. IGI is also especially prominent in the lab-grown diamond market, where clear origin identification and consistent grading are essential.

Does certification apply to laboratory-grown diamonds?

Yes. Laboratory-grown diamonds can be independently graded and certified. A grading report identifies the diamond’s laboratory-grown origin and documents its quality characteristics using established grading standards.

Why do jewelry retailers and manufacturers use independent grading reports?

Independent grading reports provide a standardized method of communicating quality characteristics throughout the jewelry supply chain. They help retailers, manufacturers, and consumers evaluate diamonds, gemstones, and jewelry using consistent terminology and grading practices.
