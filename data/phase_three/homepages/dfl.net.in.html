Salient Feature of Yarn Dyeing Plant

We started our yarn dyeing plant during the year 2004. We have imported fully automatic, sophisticated yarn dyeing machines in order to avoid batch variations. All processes are controlled by computer. The imported radio frequency yarn dryer drys the yarn with even heating and keep the moisture away. The updated rewinding and meter winding machines having the splicing attachment with negative device will keep the yarn in even length on all cones.
