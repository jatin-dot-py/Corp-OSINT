Our Company

SriGEE DLM

Since 2012, Srigee has evolved from a modest operation into a fully integrated leader in plastic injection molding, mastering custom polymer compounds and precision mold design. By producing these critical components in-house, we set new benchmarks for innovation, quality, and efficiency. Today, Srigee shapes the future of manufacturing with a commitment to excellence and sustainability.

Our Services

Plastic injection molding, a cornerstone of modern manufacturing, enables the creation of complex, high-quality plastic products with precision and efficiency. At Srigee, we lead the industry by integrating expertise in custom polymer compounds and precision mold design, setting new benchmarks in innovation and quality.
