    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find answers to common questions about our services, treatments, appointments, and patient care options to help you make informed health decisions.

    Why is Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi considered one of the best multispeciality hospitals in Delhi?

    Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi is widely regarded as a leading multispeciality hospital in Delhi due to its depth of clinical expertise, advanced medical infrastructure, and integrated care delivery. Patients choose Apollo Delhi for its ability to manage preventive, elective, and complex treatments under one coordinated system, positioning it among the top-rated hospitals in Delhi and the NCR.

    Which areas does Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi serve?

    Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi serves patients from across Delhi and the National Capital Region, including South Delhi, Central Delhi, East and West Delhi, as well as areas such as Greater Kailash, Saket, Vasant Kunj, Dwarka, Rohini, Pitampura, Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad, and Gurugram. Due to its strong clinical reputation and multispeciality depth, Apollo Hospitals Delhi also attracts patients from across North India.

    What makes Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi a top-rated and patient-preferred hospital?

    Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi is consistently recognised as a patient-preferred hospital because of its structured care pathways, experienced medical teams, and strong focus on patient safety and clinical outcomes. These factors contribute to its reputation as one of the highest-rated hospitals in Delhi across patient feedback and review platforms.

    How does Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi qualify as a premier multispeciality hospital?

    As a premier multispeciality hospital in Delhi, Apollo brings together multiple specialties, advanced diagnostics, and critical care services within a single ecosystem. This enables faster clinical decision-making, coordinated treatment planning, and predictable outcomes—core attributes of a benchmark hospital in Delhi.

    What multispeciality healthcare services are offered at Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi?

    Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi offers comprehensive multispeciality healthcare services supported by advanced medical technology and experienced specialists. Care is delivered across cardiology, orthopaedics, neurology, gastroenterology, urology, oncology, gynaecology, paediatrics, general and laparoscopic surgery, and internal medicine.

    The hospital is equipped with 24×7 emergency and critical care services, modern ICUs, advanced diagnostics, and minimally invasive surgical capabilities. With integrated outpatient, inpatient, surgical, and rehabilitation services under one roof, Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi is widely regarded as a leading and trusted multispeciality hospital in Delhi.

    Does Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi accept insurance?

    Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi accepts a wide range of health insurance policies and offers cashless treatment facilities through multiple insurance providers and TPAs. This allows patients to access planned and emergency care with minimal upfront payments. A dedicated insurance desk assists with verification, pre-authorisation, and claim coordination.

    How transparent are treatment costs at Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi?

    Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi follows a transparent and structured pricing approach, where treatment costs are clearly communicated upfront based on the recommended clinical pathway, room category, and procedure complexity. Patients can receive advance treatment estimates after clinical evaluation, helping them plan financially and make informed decisions.

    Is Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi trusted for complex and advanced treatments?

    Yes. Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi is considered a clinically advanced multispeciality hospital, trusted for managing complex and high-risk cases across specialties. Its emphasis on evidence-based protocols, multidisciplinary collaboration, and outcome-driven care has established it as one of the most trusted hospitals in Delhi.

    Why do patients consider Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi a hospital of choice?

    Patients consider Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi a hospital of choice because it offers end-to-end care—from diagnosis to recovery—supported by senior clinicians, modern infrastructure, and streamlined patient services. Its strategic location and strong connectivity across Delhi and the NCR allow quick access for both planned consultations and 24×7 emergency care.

    Does Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi offer emergency medical services?

    Yes. Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi provides 24/7 emergency medical services, including trauma care, critical care, and ambulance support for urgent and life-threatening conditions.

    Does Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi offer health checkups?

    Yes. Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi offers a wide range of preventive health checkup packages designed for early detection, risk assessment, and long-term wellness management. These packages include consultations, laboratory investigations, imaging where required, and specialist evaluations through a streamlined, single-visit process.

    How can I get a second opinion at Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi?

    Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi offers second opinion services across specialties, allowing patients to consult senior specialists for review of diagnoses, treatment plans, or surgical recommendations. Second opinions can be obtained through in-person consultations or digital platforms, depending on clinical requirements.

    Can I avail teleconsultation services at Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi?

    Yes. Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi offers teleconsultation services that allow patients to consult experienced specialists remotely for follow-ups, second opinions, and non-emergency medical advice, improving accessibility and convenience.

    Why do patients searching for the best hospital near me in Delhi choose Apollo?

    Patients searching for the best hospital near them in Delhi often choose Apollo due to its multispeciality depth, emergency preparedness, transparent care processes, and consistent clinical outcomes delivered through an integrated healthcare model.

    What safety and quality protocols does Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi follow?

    Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals Delhi follows rigorous safety and quality protocols across diagnostics, surgery, critical care, and medication management. These include standardised clinical pathways, infection control practices, patient identification protocols, medication safety checks, and continuous quality audits to ensure safe and reliable care delivery.

    Are doctors available on Sundays at Apollo Hospitals in Delhi?

    Yes, doctors are available for Sunday consultations at Apollo Hospitals in Delhi. The availability of doctors depends on the department, specialty, hospital location, and individual consultation schedule. Patients should check the current Sunday roster online before planning a hospital visit.

    How can I find and book a doctor available this Sunday in Delhi?

    Patients can search for doctors available on Sunday in Delhi through the Apollo Hospitals Sunday Doctors page. You can review available specialties, doctors, hospital locations, and appointment timings before selecting a suitable slot. Advance booking is recommended, especially for specialist consultations.

    Can I book a Sunday specialist appointment or second-opinion consultation in Delhi?
