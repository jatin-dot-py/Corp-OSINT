  Welcome to Transglobe Food  
 

TRANSGLOBE FOODS LIMITED was originally incorporated as a Private Limited Company on 11th November 1986 and converted into a Public Limited Company by a special resolution on 25th January 1993. Certificate of Change of Name has been issued by Registrar of Companies (Gujarat) at Ahmedabad on 29th March 1993.

The Company has shifted its Registered Office to Mumbai Maharashtra, with effect from 16th September, 2013.

Commercial production was started in March, 1990. The Company was earlier engaged in the production of processed fruits and vegetable products.

At present the Company is in the manufacturing producing, processing, importing and exporting of vegetarian and non-vegetarian processed foods from grain, cereals, seeds, vegetables, fruits, plants, oilseeds, edible oil, meal, fish and eggs.
