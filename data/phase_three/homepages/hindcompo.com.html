⸻   Hindustan Composites Limited

Manufacturers of Friction Products

Hindustan Composites Limited was Incorporated in 1964. HCL pioneered the development and manufacturing of friction materials in India. Our more than 60-year legacy of quality and innovation through strategic collaborations and in-house R&D has positioned us as the industry benchmark. Six decades of undisputed leadership in friction materials to Auto, Rail, Farm-Tractor, and Industrial applications.

  • Largest supplier of CV Brake Linings to OEM & OES
  • Largest supplier of Rail Brake Blocks to Railways with a business share of more than 40%
Complete Solutions

Comprehensive friction solutions spanning Commercial Vehicles, Passenger Vehicles, Tractors & Railways for Brake & Clutch products.

Advanced Manufacturing

State-of-the-art facilities with cutting-edge R&D centers and comprehensive testing capabilities.

Global Reach

Strategic international partnerships driving expansion in OEM, OES and export markets, particularly CV, Rail and tractor segments.
