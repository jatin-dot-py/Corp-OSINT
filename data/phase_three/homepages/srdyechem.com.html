Shankar Lal Rampal Dye Chem Ltd.
Trader, Exporter, Importer of dye and chemical

Shankar Lal Rampal Dye Chem Limited has made a prominent mark for itself in the Chemical industry, since its incorporation in the year 2005. Based in Bhilwara (Rajasthan), the company is named as a prominent Trader, Exporter, Importer, and Supplier of Sulphur Dyes, Paraffin Wax, Phosphoric Acid, Sodium Sulphide, Hydrogen Peroxide, Citric Acid Monohydrate, Phosphate Chemicals, and Refined Glycerine, in India. Our products are known for their purity and accurate chemical compositions.
