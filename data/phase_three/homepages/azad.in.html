Azad Engineering stands at the forefront of global precision manufacturing, specialising in highly engineered, complex, mission-and life-critical components for the most demanding applications.

Business Segments

We serve three rapidly expanding sectors driven by technological innovation and global shifts.   

Our precision capabilities and longstanding partnerships position us for success.

Aerospace & Defence

We are a trusted partner to leading global aerospace and defence OEMs, delivering mission- and life-critical components such as engine assemblies, APUs, airfoils, actuators, and hydraulic systems.   

Our portfolio supports a wide range of platforms, including the Boeing 737, Airbus A320 and A350 families, and Gulfstream G550, among others.

Oil & Gas

Azad’s Oil & Gas division leverages our precision manufacturing expertise to serve critical infrastructure across the entire energy value chain. From upstream drilling operations to midstream transportation, we manufacture high-precision components that ensure reliability in the industry’s most demanding applications.

Know More

Energy

Energy represents one of Azad’s largest and most established verticals. Since 2008, we’ve evolved from a small machine shop into a world-class manufacturing operation, becoming a critical supplier of high-precision turbine components that power the world’s most demanding energy applications.

Azad's Strategic Growth Pillars

Central to our growth strategy is the 5C framework, which integrates our core strengths and drives our performance.

Our success is driven by a steadfast commitment to innovation, precision, and strategic partnerships.

1

Customers

Top global OEMs across sectors

Azad operates across business segments that have a proven track record of stable demand for the last few decades and also promises a stable growth over the long term.

2

Contracts

Long-term revenue visibility

With a current order book (incl. long-term contracts) of ₹6,000+ crore, securing and managing strategic long-term contracts remains vital to our growth and market positioning.

We meticulously oversee each engagement, ensuring that we deliver exceptional value and uphold our reputation for reliability and excellence.

3

Capability

Mission and life critical components

Azad focuses on business segments that have extremely high barriers of entry as most products are mission and life critical in nature.

These entry barriers are driven by long qualification cycles, extremely stringent audits, state-of-the-art infrastructure, technical expertise among other capabilities.

Azad has crossed all barriers and is the only Indian player qualified to produce these components to meet the global demand.

4

Consistency

Repeatability and zero-defect culture

At the heart of our operations lies a commitment to consistency.
We uphold the highest standards of quality and performance in every project, reinforcing our reliability and building enduring trust with our partners and clients.

Delivered over 4 million components since inception.

5

Capacity

10x expansion, 360 degree eco-system

As we respond to the increasing demands of our clients, we are actively expanding our production and operational capacities by 10x over the next
few years.   This growth enables us to scale our services effectively and adapt swiftly to market needs.

ENGINEERING A BETTER FUTURE

Azad is uniquely positioned as the only qualified partner in India for several mission and life-critical components that power the industries we operate in.

These qualifications are the result of years of rigorous audits, testing, and certifications, and represent one of our strongest competitive moats.

With these foundations now in place, we are ramping up production to meet rising global demand.

Backed by long-term contracts, deep OEM relationships, and a strong order backlog, Azad is well prepared to scale execution while continuing to set new benchmarks in precision, reliability, and operational excellence.

PRECISION THAT POWERS THE IMPOSSIBLE

At the heart of Azad’s value proposition lies the ability to deliver precision at a scale few can match. We specialize in complex geometries, advanced materials, and highly demanding applications where system integrity and efficiency are non-negotiable.   

With a proven portfolio of more than 1,700+ qualified parts and 45+ specialized manufacturing processes, our capabilities continue to support the uncompromising standards of the aerospace, defense, energy and oil & gas sectors.
