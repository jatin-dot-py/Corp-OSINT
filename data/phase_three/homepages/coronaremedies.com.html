About Us

We did not set out to top the growth charts; we aimed to silently serve health better in our segments—growth just followed.

Today, CORONA being the fastest growing company among the top 30 companies, with CAGR of 19% — 2x of IPM’s, is a testament to improving lives. This number is not our goal; it is the evidence of trust laid on us.

The same trust is reciprocated with sheer transparency in our process, quality, and commitment. This acts as a strong connection with our internal and external stakeholders.

Our journey of health and innovation is solely based on our tenets. CORONA laid the foundation with values and ethics that support, uplift, and nurture each of us – building a path to Good Health For All!

Our Stories

Celebration of World Environment Day

At CORONA; We are committed to save energy. With this philosophy; we have Zero carbon emission manufacturing facilities. Further to heal the nature; 4500 CORONATES decided to wear "UN-IRONED CLOTHES" on "WORLD ENVIRONMENT DAY", as Ironing is one of the major cause of electricity consumption. With this initiative; we can save electricity worth 744kWh & 1 Ton Carbon emission. It is equivalent to the electricity required to charge 30,000 mobile phones or to drive electric car for 13,000 kms. We have encouraged employees to continue this activity at least once in month. ​

Rainwater Harvesting and Plantation Initiatives​

We understand the significant positive impact of the Green ecosystem and Rain-Water Harvesting hence has initiated rainwater-harvesting projects by investing in two Percolation Wells at its Bhayla plant.

Health Checkups

A healthy life starts with timely diagnosis, and CORONA works fast on the preventive care. We organized multiple BMD and eye-check programmes for coronates to understand and take accurate actions.​
