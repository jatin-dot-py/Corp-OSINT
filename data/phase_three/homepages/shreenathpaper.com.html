About us

Started in 2011, from a tier-II city of Maharashtra, our company now operates with a customer base across India.
We are determined to become one of the largest supply chain Management Company in Writing & Printing Industry in India by providing paper based solutions to our elite customers along with cost effectiveness and best post sales services.
What lies at the core of our execution is our ability to execute the trades at the right price, at the right quality and at the right time. As a Group, we understand the essence and sensitivity of time in a trade and deliver well within the deadline.
