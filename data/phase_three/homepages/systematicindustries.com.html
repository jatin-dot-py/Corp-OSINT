WHO WE ARE

We are a global leader in wire transformation and coatings, with an annual steel wire production capacity of 1,00,000 MT, powering 4% of India’s steel wire demand. Serving industries like Power & Transmission, Infrastructure, Agriculture, Telecom, and Automobiles, we bring innovation and reliability across sectors.

We are proud to be the first in India to introduce Zinc + Aluminium Alloy coated wires under the brand GALVASYS, delivering superior corrosion resistance and longer life. Our offerings also include high-performance Optical Ground Wires and Durafiber – our RDSO-approved fiber optic cables that support India’s digital transformation.

As one of the fastest-growing companies in the steel wire industry, we continue to shape the future with advanced technology, uncompromising quality, and global standards.

Durafiber redefines connectivity with innovative Optical Fiber Cable solutions like Uni Tube Duct Cable,  Multi Tube Cable (Armoured & Unarmoured), Aerial Cable, and ADSS Cables, empowering industries with speed, clarity, and reliability.
