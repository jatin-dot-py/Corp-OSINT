Our Story

For close to 25 year(s), Tasty Bite® has been making flavorful and all-natural ethnic range of retail products (entrees, sauces and rice) and a range of foodservice products (sauces & frozen formed products). We are a prepared foods (not ‘processed’) company, with the sole purpose of providing consumer delight!

Discover our Journey

Manufacturing

We believe in 'convenience without compromise!' At Tasty Bite®, we manufacture food for kitchens of all sizes. We are industry leaders in manufacturing prepared foods and food intermediates across shelf-stable, frozen and chilled food formats. With rigorous quality checks, we ensure that only the finest products reach our customers.

Right from vegetables, rice, grains and dairy to the most flavorsome herbs and spices, all our ingredients are responsibly sourced!

Sustainability

We believe that good food shouldn't come at the expense of people or the planet. We proudly give back to the community and the environment we operate in. We have been consistently committed to meeting UN sustainable development goals.
With solar panels mounted on our rooftops, a biogas plant at our factory premises and an effluent treatment plant to recycle our wastewater, we aim to reduce as much carbon footprint as we can.

More on our Green and Clean Life

Research & Development

Tasty Bite® Research Centre

At Tasty Bite®, we believe we are what we eat. Our team at Tasty Bite® Research Centre (TBRC) is a combination of seasoned culinary experts and food technologists. Focused on innovation, we adapt current global food trends to create one-of-a-kind products.

Our well-equipped research lab helps us develop scalable products across multiple formats – retort, pasteurized, oven-cooked and frozen formed. All products that come out of TBRC are all-natural and preservative-free.
