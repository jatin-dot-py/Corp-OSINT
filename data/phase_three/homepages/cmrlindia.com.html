Legendary Producer of Best Quality Synthetic Rutile & Ferric Chloride

We are a global mineral processing and value addition company that focuses on the manufacture and export of world class Synthetic Rutile and Ferric Chloride using purified titanium ore and having a clear vision on long term growth and sustainability.

Globally Connected to
Hi-Tech Titanium Value Chain

Nearly 40% of global hi-tech titanium requirement is met by Synthetic Rutile produced by CMRL. Leading Japanese companies, MNC’s based in USA, Europe, China and other geographies constitute the esteemed clientele of CMRL.

Smart TiO2 Pigment
Solutions for Optimised Performance and Cost Efficiency

CMTIC TiO2 is a specialised coloured rutile pigment developed for paints, coatings, plastics, ceramics and construction chemical applications, covering Beige, Green, Brown and Grey colour types, including Light Grey shades used in coil backing coats and powder coating applications.

Embraces Industrial Symbiosis and Follows the Motto of
“From Nature, With Nature, Towards Nature”

We are a Model Eco-friendly company that follows principles of industrial symbiosis and fully adhering to nature friendly operations, for long term sustainability.
