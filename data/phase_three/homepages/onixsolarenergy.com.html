About Onix Solar Energy

Onix Solar Energy Limited is a renewable energy company specializing in module manufacturing and IPP project development.

It operates and 1200 MW solar module facilities, produces high-efficiency modules (530–640 WP), and is developing 500+ MW of solar projects in Maharashtra, supporting the Onix Group's 10 GW renewable goal by 2030.

Why renewable energy

Why Should We Shift to Renewable Energy Today?

Renewable energy is the foundation of a cleaner, smarter, and more sustainable future. By harnessing natural resources like sunlight, we reduce dependence on fossil fuels, lower environmental impact, and create long-term value for businesses and communities while protecting the planet for future generations.

1. Environmentally Responsible

Clean energy significantly reduces carbon emissions and air pollution, helping combat climate change and protect ecosystems.

2. Sustainable & Unlimited Resources

Solar energy is abundant and renewable, ensuring long-term energy security without resource depletion.

3. Cost-Effective Over Time

With lower operating costs and stable pricing, renewable energy offers long-term financial benefits.

4. Driving Growth & Energy Independence

Renewable energy supports economic growth, job creation, and reduces reliance on imported fuels.
