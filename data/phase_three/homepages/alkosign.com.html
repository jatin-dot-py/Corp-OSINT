Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ



What is the covering on the surface of Alkosign boards?

The writing surface of the Alkosign dry erase board is covered with a protective sheet to prevent damage during transit. Do not remove this protective sheet until the board is installed. Remove the protective covering after installation, and clean with Alkosign cleaner or Colin the board before the first use.

Can I use your board as a projection surface?

Whiteboards are ideal for use as projection surfaces. The ceramic matte surface prevents glare from projectors and room lights. 

How do I submit a warranty claim?

To file a claim for factory defects in material or workmanship, contact an Alkosign customer service representative within one year of purchase. To file a claim for ghosting or staining on Alkosign dry erase boards, contact an Alkosign customer service representative any time after purchase. Alkosign customer service can be reached for claim submittal by calling or emailing sales@alkosign.com

How frequently we should clean board?

To prevent stains and ghost marks, clean the board every 8 to 10 days using, with an appropriate cleaner.

Are all whiteboards being magnetic?

No Not all Boards are magnetic, it depends on surface & material. We provide Magnetic & Non-magnetic Boards with wide range of sizes & Material.

Can I get a combination board customized?

Yes, we combine boards felt with white writing board (magnetic & Non-magnetic both) 

Can I use your product in and out environment?

Yes, you can use it. Preferably area should be dry. 

All products are customizable?

Not all, few products have standard size & material.
