About STG

Manufacturing precision reed switches since 1988

Switching Technologies Gunther Ltd. (STG) is an Indian manufacturer of GC-series reed switches, operating from the Madras Export Processing Zone (MEPZ) in Chennai. STG is a subsidiary of the Comus International group, supplying precision electromechanical components to distributors across six continents.

Our reed switches are used in position sensing, liquid level detection, proximity sensing, and industrial automation applications worldwide. As a listed company on the Bombay Stock Exchange, STG maintains full regulatory transparency.
