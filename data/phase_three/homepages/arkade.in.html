Welcome Home to Excellence

For over four decades, Arkade has been driven by a philosophy that places passion at the heart of building quality homes. Under the visionary leadership of Mr. Mangilal Jain and Mr. Amit Jain, Arkade has grown into one of Mumbai’s most respected real estate companies, leaving a legacy of excellence across the city. Their forward-thinking approach has made Arkade synonymous with Trust, Quality, Assurance, Ethics and Transparency. Every residential or commercial property that carries the Arkade name reflects a commitment to the highest standards, consistently surpassing customer expectations.
