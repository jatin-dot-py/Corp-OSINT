(01) The House

A wholesale legacy, carried by hand from weaver to counter across India.

Saraswati Saree Depot Limited is a wholesaler of sarees, kurtis, lehengas and other women's ethnic wear — with management spanning three generations, a workforce of decades' experience, and a deeply loyal customer base.

From the entrepreneur beginning her journey at home to the leading retailers of India's largest cities, we serve every customer with the same care — determined not only to grow our own trade, but to carry forward the oldest and most skilled weavers of India.
