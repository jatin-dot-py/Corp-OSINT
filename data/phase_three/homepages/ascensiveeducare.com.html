accreditation & partnership

  • National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS)

National Skill Development Corporation India (NSDC), established in 2009, is a not- for- profit company set up by the Ministry of Finance, under Section 25 of the Companies Act,1956 corresponding to Section 8 of the Companies Act,2013. The present equity base of NSDC is Rs.10 crore, of which the Government of India through Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship (MSDE ) holds 49%, while the private sector has the balance 51%. AEPL is the NSDC funded Partner since 2015.

Ascensive Educare works closely with various Central & State Govt Departments. DAY- NULM, PMKVY CSCM, PMKVY CSSM, Seekho Aur Kamao, Utkarsha Bangla under PBSSD, RSLDC, West Bengal Swarozgar Corporation, Electronics System Design & Manufacturing under Diety, NSDC STAR, Border Area Development Program, Vocational Training under Aliah University, Vocational Training under WBMDFC, Skill training under NBCFDC.

Ascensive Educare Ltd is affiliated with 18 Sector Skill Councils like Aerospace & Aviation, Agriculture, Apparel Made-Ups and Home furnishing, Automobile, Beauty & Wellness, Banking Finance & Insurance, Construction, Electronics, Food Processing, Green Jobs, Healthcare, Handicrafts, Life Science, Management, Media & Entertainment, Retail, Rubber, Tourism & Hospitality

The National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) in India is a programme equipping technically qualified youth with practical knowledge and skills required in their field of work. The Apprentices are imparted training by the organizations at their place of work. Trained Managers with well-developed training modules ensure that Apprentices learn the job quickly and competently. During the period of apprenticeship, the apprentices are paid a stipend amount, 50% of which is reimbursable to the employer from the Government of India. At the end of the training period, the apprentices are issued a Certificate of Proficiency by Government of India which can be registered at all employment exchanges across India as valid employment experience. The apprentices are placed for training at Central, State and Private organizations which have excellent training facilities. National Apprenticeship Training Scheme is one of the flagship programs of Government of India for Skilling Indian Youth.

"Hunar Se Rojgar Tak" (HSRT) is a flagship scheme of the Ministry of Tourism that provides free skills training in the hospitality and tourism sector to youth above the age of 18.
