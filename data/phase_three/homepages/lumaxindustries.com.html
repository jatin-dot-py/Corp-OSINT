Lumax Industries Limited, the flagship company of the Lumax-DK Jain Group, stands as India’s most trusted and experienced name in automotive lighting. With a legacy of four decades and a strategic partnership with Stanley Electric Co. Ltd., Japan, a global leader in vehicle lighting, Lumax has become a preferred supplier to virtually every major OEM in India, including Maruti Suzuki, Hero Motocorp, Honda, Mahindra & Mahindra, Tata Motors, Toyota, TVS, Royal Enfield, Ashok Leyland, and many others.
Backed by 12 world-class manufacturing facilities strategically located near OEM hubs, two advanced R&D centres in India, and global design centres in Taiwan and the Czech Republic, Lumax delivers cutting-edge lighting systems for two-wheelers, four-wheelers, commercial and off-road vehicles. Its wide product portfolio spans headlamps, tail lamps, fog lamps, interior lighting, and more.
With a workforce of over 7,000 professionals and a revenue of ₹3,400 Crores in FY2025, Lumax continues to lead with innovation, engineering excellence, and a relentless focus on quality. The company is also deeply committed to social impact through the Lumax Charitable Foundation, driving initiatives in education and healthcare for underserved communities.
Listed on both BSE and NSE, Lumax Industries is not just lighting the road ahead; it’s powering the future of mobility.
