About Us

A Company of Excellent Services

McNally Bharat Engineering Company Ltd. (MBE) is one of the leading Engineering Companies in India engaged in providing turnkey solutions in the areas of Power, Steel, Aluminium, Material Handling, Mineral Beneficiation, Pyroprocessing, Pneumatic Handling of powdered materials including fly ash handling and high concentrate disposal, coal washing, Port cranes, Cement, Oil & Gas, civic and industrial water supply etc. Over 350 plants have been constructed on a turnkey basis by MBE till date. In 2011 we celebrated our Golden Anniversary year (1961 - 2011).
