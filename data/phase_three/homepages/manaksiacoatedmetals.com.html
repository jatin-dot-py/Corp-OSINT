About Us

Specialists in Delivering Exceptional Steel Products

Established in 2010 as an independent entity from the Manaksia Group, MCMIL has emerged as a leading manufacturer and exporter of coated metal products. Over the years, the company has significantly expanded its product portfolio and strengthened its presence across both domestic and international markets. Today, MCMIL exports to over 22 countries, including key regions in Europe, Africa, and the Middle East, reinforcing its position as a global industry leader.

  • Solutions

    What We Offer

    The company specialises in Pre-Painted Steel (PPGI/PPGL) and Bare Alu-Zinc Steel (GL), catering to industries like construction, automotive, home appliances, and general engineering.

  • Alu-Zinc Steel

    MCMIL’s Al-Zn steel products offer superior corrosion resistance and durability, making them suitable for use in roofing, cladding, and industrial applications.

  • Pre-Painted Steel

    MCMIL's PPGI & PPGL products feature a protective paint layer, enhancing durability and aesthetic appeal. They are widely used in various sectors, including construction, automotive, and home appliances sectors.
