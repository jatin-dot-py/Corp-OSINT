Textile industry plays a pivotal role in providing one of the most basic needs of people–clothing. It is not only second largest employment provider after agriculture, but is one of the critical drivers of Indian economy. Globally, textile industry has seen an unprecedented growth, paving the way for increasing role of Indian Textile companies in the global textile industry. The apparel industry is embracing blockchain technology to bring in revolutionary changes. The technology connects the supply chain of the textile industry with real-time data flow.
