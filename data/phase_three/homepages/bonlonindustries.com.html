What we do

Two ways we operate.

We operate across the metals value chain — manufacturing, processing, and trading copper, aluminium, zinc, and lead in forms ranging from scrap to cathodes to finished wire rods. Two segments drive the business.

Segment 01
Manufacturing
Four core product lines from two facilities in Bhiwadi and Chopaki, Rajasthan — copper wire rods (8mm–16mm), copper wires, and scrap recycling operations across cable grades including Berry, Candy, Birch, and Cliff.

Why Bonlon

01

Quality first.

From raw material sourcing to finished product dispatch — every lot is verified against test certificates and every process is documented. The quality you're promised is the quality you get.

02

Pricing on your terms.

Fix your price before, during, or after delivery on LME or MCX basis — your call. We manage the hedge so you don't carry market exposure.

Listed, banked, KYC-clean.

A public company on NSE & BSE, backed by reputed Indian banks. Every counterparty is KYC-screened before a contract is issued.
