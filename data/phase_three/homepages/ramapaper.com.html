Rama Paper Mills Limited

To execute the purpose of manufacturing Newsprints Duplex Board and producing Writing and printing paper by recycling waste paper, as Public Limited Company we established Rama Paper Mills Limited in 1985 a massive futuristic Industrial Complex rising from a patch of attractive piece of land here at Kiratpur , Distt. Bijnor in Uttar Pradesh.

In the year 1988, Company acquired installed capacity of 3,300 TPA through its duplex manufacturing unit. Within a span of four years (1992-93) it diversified its plant into writing & printing and newsprint. This way, it created the ability to gradually escalate its overall installed capacity of 3 units upto 44000 TPA. The Company has further undertaken the task of manufacturing Tissue and Poster Paper by installing one more unit to achieve the target by producing the above said products nonetheless 16320 TPA.

Thus enhancing the overall output to an enormous 61000 TPA. The site of the unit is easily accessible by roadways as well as railways. That makes it not only easier to procure of raw material and inputs but also brings finished products’ markets in proximity.

The company is professionally managed by a well qualified , highly knowledged , truly motivated and well experienced personnel . Its workforce comprising of 700 skilled, semi skilled and unskilled people.
