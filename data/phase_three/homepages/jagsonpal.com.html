About Us

Founded in 1978, our mission is to provide essential medicines while maintaining affordability with a larger focus on catering to women-specific healthcare needs. The Company has identified Gynaecology and Orthopaedics as its focus segments and diligently built presence in major therapeutic sub-segments.

Jagsonpal has emerged as an organisation of trust and credibility among the medical fraternity, which is evident from the fact that several brands are prominently ranked among the top 5 brands in respective molecule segments.

With women’s health as its pivot, Jagsonpal strives to offer maximum value for its consumers, fostering enduring relationship and making significant contribution to the industry. It is noteworthy that since inception, the Company ascribed importance to product accessibility as a cornerstone to its success which led to assurance of product availability across the country.
