Artefact secures NH 544H Supervision Consultant Assignment

Artefact secures NH 544H Supervision Consultant Assignment under NHAI Artefact Projects Ltd. has been awarded Supervision Consultant (SC) for Operation & Maintenance of Two Lane with Paved Shoulder from Thoppur-Mettur-Bhavani-Erode Section on NH 544H (Km 0+000 to Km 85+000) in the State of Tamil Nadu for a period of 36 months.

Mobile Bridge Inspection Survey Conducted at Loha–Waranga Bridge

A detailed inspection survey conducted using a Mobile Bridge Inspection Unit to assess structural condition.

Artefact Secures NH-334B Independent Engineer Assignment

Artefact Projects Ltd. has been awarded Supervision Consultancy Services for O&M of NH-30 (Km 0.000–70.200) in Andhra Pradesh by NHAI, marking a key milestone in our 30-year legacy of excellence.

Chairman Santosh Yadav Visits PIU Madurai

Visit of Hon. Chairman NHAI Shri Santosh Yadav on our TOT stretch under PIU Madurai.

Artefact Appointed Independent Engineer for NH-16 Project

Artefact has been appointed as Independent Engineer by NHAI for the 102 km Chilakaluripet–Vijayawada NH-16 section.

FWD Survey Conducted for Pavement Structural Evaluation

A Falling Weight Deflectometer survey was carried out on the Talaja–Mahuva road section to evaluate pavement strength and analyze structural performance.

Artefact Wins Supervision Consultant Role for NH-785

Artefact has been appointed as Supervision Consultant for operation and maintenance of the 37 km Madurai–Chettikulam–Natham section of NH-785 in Tamil Nadu.

Chairman Santosh Yadav Inspects Nagpur Outer Ring Road

Visit of Hon. Chairman NHAI Shri Santosh Yadav on Nagpur Outer Ring Road site inspection with backdrop of Miyawaki Plantation near Toll Plaza.

Network Survey Vehicle Deployed for Bhavnagar–Talaja Road Assessment

A road condition survey conducted using a Network Survey Vehicle to capture high-precision pavement data and support maintenance planning.

Artefact–Pioneer JV Secures Independent Engineer Role

Artefact, in joint venture with Pioneer Infra, has been appointed Independent Engineer for operation and maintenance of the 64 km Vadodara–Kim Expressway.

Road Marking Retroreflectivity Survey Conducted at Dornala

A retroreflectivity survey was carried out to measure visibility performance of road markings, ensuring compliance with safety standards for improved night-time driving safety.

Road Marking Retroreflectivity Survey Conducted at Madurai

The survey assessed reflective performance of road markings to ensure visibility, enhance road safety, and maintain compliance with highway maintenance standards.
