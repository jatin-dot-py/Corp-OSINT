About Us

About Us

Established in 1951, Ador (formerly, Advani-Oerlikon) is one of the leading welding companies in India, manufacturing high-quality welding equipment, consumables, and welding automation solutions. Additionally, Ador has been in the Flares & Process Equipment Division for more than 30+ years.

Ador has played a significant role during the post-independence industrialization in India and takes immense pride in its heritage.

With a strong focus on “Make in India,” Ador produces world-class products across six manufacturing facilities nationwide. Its Research and Development Centre, recognized by the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (Government of India), drives innovation at the core of Ador.

With a presence across 15 + countries in the Middle East, Africa, North America, South America, Australia, and Southeast Asia, Ador maintains a robust sales and distribution network across the globe and provides solutions to a wide spectrum of industries including shipbuilding, oil and gas, railways, infrastructure, power, automotive sectors, heavy fabrication and structural engineering.
