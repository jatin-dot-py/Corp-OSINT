About Lahoti Group

Lahoti Overseas was established in 1990 to export quality Cotton Yarns from India’s leading spinning mills. Starting with OE yarns, today, the company’s range covers a wide variety of Cotton Yarns including carded, combed & compact ring spun yarns of coarse & fine counts, ply yarns and special yarns.

 

Lahoti has now expanded to other fibers and offering yarns like Viscose Spun, Polyester Spun, Polyester DTY, Linen, PVA, Blended Yarns and Slubs. Today Lahoti has more than 50,000 spindles running for them on commission spinning + 35 knitting machines + 128 Looms on Job work.

Renewable Energy

At Lahoti, we come with an understanding of our responsibility towards the environment and hence, incorporate eco-friendly practices in our manufacturing processes. Our Rajasthan plant boasts of a multiple solar power unit that generates 2 MW of power, which increases every year.

Moreover, our plants in Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu are loaded with windmills that yield a total of 6 MW each year, contributing to the well-being of the environment.
