Our Network of Showrooms & Service Centers

At Amba Auto, we take pride in offering a wide network of vehicle showrooms and service centres, ensuring customers get the best in both sales and after-sales support. From Bajaj, KTM, and Chetak vehicles to expert servicing, our dedicated facilities are designed to meet every customer’s needs with convenience and care.

Similarly, Amba LG operates multiple LG Best Shop showrooms across prime locations, showcasing the latest LG home appliances with expert guidance and prompt service. Whether you are purchasing your next ride or upgrading your home with world-class appliances, our network ensures you are always close to quality, trust, and exceptional customer experience.
