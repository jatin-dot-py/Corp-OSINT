About Our Company

MIC Electronics Limited. is a global leader in the design, development & manufacturing of LED Video Displays, high-end Electronic and Telecommunication equipment and development of Telecom software since 1988. An ISO 9001:2015 certified, ISO 14001:2015 certified, ISO 45001:2018 certified,  it has marked presence in the highly dynamic domains of:

  • LED Video, Graphics and Text Displays
  • LED Lighting Solutions
  • Embedded, System and Telecom software
  • Communication and Electronic Products
