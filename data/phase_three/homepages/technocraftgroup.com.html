Products & Services

Closures

TIIL is the second largest global manufacturer of Steel Drum Closures including Tite Seal Flanges & Plugs, Tite Seal Gaskets, Tite Seal Leak Locks Plugs, Octagon base Drum Flanges & Auto Closure Systems.

TIIL ventured into manufacturing of plastic closures for small metal and plastic containers majorly used in the O&G industry.

It has 3-global patent products for manufacturing of GRT Flanges, Octagonal Clinched and Drum Top.

It has 6 manufacturing facilities in India and 1 in China.

Scaffolding, Formwork & Towers

Technocraft successfully manufactures & supplies high-quality steel tubes with excellent dimensional accuracy and surface quality. Technocraft Scaffolding System have been designed keeping in mind the ease of erection, health, and safety regulations and without any loose fittings or hardware. Our Scaffolding segment comprises of Scaffolding, Formwork and Tower business. Our scaffolding system namely Techring Scaffolding Systems, T Stage Scaffolding Systems, Techlok Scaffolding Systems comes with all accessories.

Yarn, Fabrics, Garments

The company produces variety of products ranging from NE 20 to NE 40, Carded and Combed varieties of Cotton Yarn. The yarn is Ringspun Raw-White used for knitting & weaving purpose. The Spinning mill is equipped with world-class Swiss, Japanese, German, Spanish equipments.

It also has a captive power generation plant of 15MW coal plant to cater to its power requirements.

“TECHNOCRAFT” is internationally renowned brand name due to its most consistent and premium quality yarn.

Company

Technocraft was established in 1972 by two brothers Mr. S.K. Saraf and Mr. S.M. Saraf. The brothers who are IIT Graduates & Technologists, had started with the aim of manufacturing high precision and sophisticated Drum Closures Products.

Until 1976 Technocraft focused on the domestic market. India was not recognized as reliable export nation during those days, against all the odds, the company launched a major export drive in 1977. “Technocraft” was recognized as an export house by government of India in 1979.

The company subsequently in 1994 acquired “Maharashtra Steel Tubes Ltd” from SICOM, which manufactured Steel Pipes. The initial production in the early years post acquisition was 500 metric ton per month and in those days Europe was a major importer of steel pipe.
