Who We Are

Founded in 1978 by entrepreneur Late Mr. Pratap Mehta, who brought over 25 years of industry experience, Kilitch seized early opportunities with its deep industry knowledge. At Kilitch Drugs (India) Ltd, we are committed to advancing global health through the manufacturing and supply of high-quality pharmaceutical formulations. With a strong focus on excellence, innovation, and accessibility, we proudly serve healthcare professionals and patients around the world.
