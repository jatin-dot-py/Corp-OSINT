About Us

At Worth Peripherals Limited, we don’t just make boxes. We engineer trust.

As a leading corrugated box manufacturer in India with over 25 years of industry expertise, we serve a wide range of sectors from FMCG and textiles to electronics and retail. Powered by advanced technology and built on long-standing relationships, we deliver consistent quality at scale.

We believe great packaging does more than just protect—it adds value across your supply chain. That’s why every box we produce is crafted with precision, sustainability, and customer success in mind.

Our boxes are made with recycled Kraft paper, aligning with a growth model that’s as responsible as it is reliable.

Why Us

  • Value Engineering: Every product is tailored. Right size, right strength, right price.

  • Extra Strength Built-In: Our boxes offer an additional cushion of durability that lasts till the last mile.

  • GPS-Enabled Logistics: Our fleet ensures real-time tracking and timely deliveries, 365 days a year.

  • High-Impact Printing: Up to 6-color graphics on kraft surfaces, ideal for branding and display.

  • Zero-Stoppage Ready: Case erector & packer compatible, keeping your production line flowing.
