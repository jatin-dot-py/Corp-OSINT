Get To Know Us

MIDWEST ENERGY Pvt Ltd

Is a leading technology company specializing in the design, development, and manufacturing of advanced battery packs and Battery Management Systems (BMS) for both low-voltage (LV) and high-voltage (HV) electric vehicle (EV) applications, as well as energy storage systems (BESS). With a steadfast commitment to driving the global transition to sustainable energy, we deliver cutting-edge energy storage solutions that optimize performance, safety, and longevity.

Our core competency lies in creating intelligent battery packs tailored to diverse EV segments, from compact urban vehicles to heavy-duty commercial transportation.
