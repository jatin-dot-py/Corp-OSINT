Overview

The Company was incorporated on May 08, 2012 under the Companies Act, 1956 as ASIAN WAREHOUSING PRIVATE LIMITED. The Registered Office of the Company is situated at Mumbai. The Hon'ble High Court of Judicature at Bombay vide its order dated 10 April, 2015 had approved the Scheme of Arrangement between RT Exports Limited (“Demerged Company”) and Asian Warehousing Private Limited (“Resulting Company”). The resulting company was later converted into public limited w.e.f. 8th December, 2018 and consequent to such conversion the name of the resulting company changed to Asian Warehousing Limited. The Company got listed on the BSE Ltd. w.e.f. 27th June, 2023.

Many Needs, One Choice. Many Solutions, One Company. As the name suggests, Asian Warehousing Ltd. - Warehouses & Logistics Park, are a result of intense understanding of the need for not only safe & quality warehousing, but also well planned, organised & modern infrastructure efficient facilities. The Company is highly active in the warehouse development & management space with additional facilities in Kandla and with a targeted development of 1 million square feet of warehousing space around the country in the next three years.

Master Layout Plan

The site is master planned on total land area of 18 acres. The location offers convenient access to the National Highway 12 and railway station. In addition, the site has been planned keeping in mind international standards for loading, unloading, turning radius of container trucks, openspace and building standards.

WAREHOUSES & LOGISTICS PARKS

Committed to achieve optimization in over all logistics costs, efficient operations & better services in the clients supply chain operations, Asian Warehousing, ware houses would leverage its world class infrastructure facilities & strategic position to provide reliable, high quality and efficient storage infrastructure to its clients.

Salient Features & Amenities

    Operational Advantages

  • Capacity to load/unload large fleet of trucks simultaneously
  • Easy movement for 40 export containers, trailers & multi-axle trucks
  • Wide internal roads with wide turning radiusto avoid logjams
  • Broad frontage enabling wide entry/exit points & avoidance of vehicle queuing
  • High dock door ratio
  • Well designed loading / unloading bays enabling easy maneuverability, parking & docking for 40 trucks & trailers

Site Location

Rajasthan,the largest State in India,is spread over an area of 3.42 lakh Sq.Km, which is 10% of the area of the country. The State's population is 68.62 million, which is nearly 5.5% of the country's population. 77% of State's population resides in the rural areas.

Rajasthan shares a border with 5 major states, offers easy access to 100 ports in Maharashtra and Gujarat and is well connected to international markets through airports. 46% of the Delhi Mumbai freight corridor runs through Rajasthan.

Rajasthan has the second largest network of National Highways in the country with a total roadl ength exceeding 7,000 km. National Highways provide excellent connectivity to cities like Delhi, Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Mumbai, Jabalpur, Bhopal, Agra, Gwalior, Indore, Amritsar.

The length of railway routes in the State was 5,784.16 km. The network connects the state to all major cities in India and links the state to ports in Mumbai, Kandla and Mundra in Gujarat. An Air-Cargo Complex at Jaipur, and In land Container Depots at Jaipur(2), Jodhpur(3), Bhilwara & Bhiwadi facilitate trade with in and outside India.

The warehouse is located at Kota Devli road, Bundi Rajasthan. Bundi town is located at a distance of 4-5kms from the project site.The project site is adjacent to the railway track on oneside and NH12 on the other side. NH12 connects Bundi to Kota and Tonk. The site is connected through a service road running parallel to the NH-12.

The facility is well poised to gain both in terms of locational benefits as well as world class infrastructure facilities being offered by the Company.
