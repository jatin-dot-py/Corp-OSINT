Decades of Excellence in Steel Processing

about us

BMWIL has been a trusted name in the steel industry for steel processing, driven by the vision of Progress with Purpose. BMWIL has a total of 7 steel processing centers in Jamshedpur and Howrah, West Bengal.

Our unwavering dedication to quality and innovation has been the backbone of our enduring success, providing the nation with steel products that stand the test of time. BMW Industries Limited (BMWIL) delivers top-quality products, including steel coil processing like HRPO (Hot Rolled) coils, CR (Cold Rolled) coils, TMT bars, tubes, galvanized sheets, and pipes, meeting the evolving needs of industries with excellence and reliability.

values

BMWIL is driven by people and guided by purpose. BMWIL supplies top-quality steel products while upholding sustainability, ethical practices, and corporate responsibility from its steel plant in Kolkata, West Bengal and Jamshedpur, striving to exceed industry benchmarks with purposeful growth and impact.

Our Compliance

Our company strictly adheres to industry standards to guarantee quality, safety, and efficiency in all our operations.

We are committed to transparency, equity, and sustainability in all our operations, and foster trust and integrity among stakeholders.

We are committed to using sustainable practices to reduce carbon footprints and empower communities for a greener future.
