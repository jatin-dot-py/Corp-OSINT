About Us

KSR Footwear Limited, based in Kolkata and registered with CIN: L46413WB2023PLC264443, was established on August 22, 2023.

Initially set up as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Khadim India Limited, the company was formed for the specific purpose of facilitating the demerger of Khadim’s distribution business. This process was carried out through a Scheme of Arrangement involving Khadim India Limited and KSR Footwear Limited along with their respective shareholders and creditors.
