Kolte-Patil Developers is one of the leading real estate developers in Pune, with a track record of delivering high-quality projects on time and within budget. Kolte-Patil has a number of ongoing residential projects in Pune, located in prime locations with world-class amenities.


Here are some of the ongoing residential projects by Kolte-Patil Developers:


La Vita

Experience premium living at La Vita by Kolte Patil, a thoughtfully designed residential project in Vashi, Navi Mumbai. Offering modern 2 BHK in Vashi, this luxurious development is crafted to provide an exceptional lifestyle with world-class amenities and seamless connectivity. If you are looking for flats for sale in Navi Mumbai, La Vita presents the perfect opportunity with its contemporary design, lush green spaces, and top-notch facilities. As one of the new projects in Navi Mumbai, it promises a blend of comfort, elegance, and convenience, making it an ideal choice for homebuyers. Discover a home that redefines urban living at Kolte Patil Vashi—where sophistication meets serenity.


Life Republic

Welcome to Kolte Patil's Life Republic Project, where your dream of owning a home in Hinjewadi, Pune becomes a reality. A sprawling 395-acre integrated township with a variety of residential options, from apartments to villas. We offer a range of housing options, including spacious 1 BHK flats in Hinjewadi Pune and elegant 2 BHK flats in Hinjewadi.


Western Avenue

Kolte Patil Developers proudly presents Western Avenue, an exceptional residential project located in the vibrant neighborhood of Wakad, Pune. Western Avenue offers a range of luxurious 3 BHK flats in Wakad, Pune that blend aesthetics, functionality, and convenience.


Three Jewels

Kolte Patil Developers proudly presents Three Jewels, an exquisite residential project located in the serene neighborhood of Katraj, Pune. This thoughtfully designed project offers 2 BHK flats in Katraj, Pune that epitomize modern living, combining aesthetics, functionality, and convenience.


24K Altura

Discover your dream home at Kolte Patil's 24K Altura in Baner, Pune. Offering a selection of luxurious 3 BHK and 4 BHK flats for sale in Baner, Pune, including 3 BHK under-construction flats, this high-rise masterpiece promises an affluent living experience that transcends ordinary standards.


24K Manor

Introducing Kolte Patil's latest offering, 24K Manor, one of the most sought-after new projects in Pimple Nilakh. If you're in search of a luxurious 3 BHK flat for sale in Pimple Nilakh, your search ends here. Don't miss this opportunity to elevate your lifestyle in this prestigious development.


24K Stargaze

Kolte Patil's magnificent project, 24K Stargaze, where you can discover the perfect 3 BHK and 4 BHK flats in Bavdhan, Pune. These exquisite flats for sale in Bavdhan offer an unparalleled living experience in one of Pune's most sought-after locales. Don't miss out on this opportunity to find your dream home in this vibrant neighborhood.


Raaga

Kolte Patil Developers proudly presents Raaga, an exceptional residential project nestled in the heart of Bangalore. With a perfect blend of luxury and comfort, Raaga offers a selection of spacious 3 BHK flats in Bangalore that redefine contemporary living.


Vaayu

Kolte Patil Vaayu is a new residential project in Borivali West, Mumbai, by Kolte-Patil Developers. It is a premium project offering 2 and 3 BHK apartments with stunning views of the Sanjay Gandhi National Park.


The project is located in a prime location, with close proximity to major schools, hospitals, shopping malls, and railway stations. If you are looking for a new home in Borivali West, be sure to check out Kolte Patil Vaayu. It is the perfect place to enjoy a luxurious and convenient lifestyle.


Serenova

Discover New Launch By Kolte Patil at Versova – a boutique residential address where Mumbai’s pace gently slows down. With thoughtfully designed 2 and 3 Bed residences, private balconies, and elegant layouts, this new launch by Kolte Patil brings you light, air, and space in abundance.


Perfectly connected via the upcoming Coastal Road, Western Express Highway, metro lines, schools, malls, and hospitals – all while being just a short walk from the beach – this is where convenience meets calm.
