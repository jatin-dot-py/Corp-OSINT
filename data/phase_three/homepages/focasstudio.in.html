Our Brands

TRIBE

Fashion for the Bold & Fearless

TRIBE is the premium menswear brand by Forcas Studio Limited, designed to capture the dynamic style of Gen Z. From bold graphics to eccentric patterns, TRIBE’s collection is all about making a statement. Every piece reflects the latest trends, offering a fresh blend of contemporary aesthetics and urban streetwear. With a focus on creativity and individuality, TRIBE aims to redefine youth fashion in India. Step into the world of TRIBE — Where style meets authenticity!

View Products

FTX

Fashion for Every Man

FTX is the flagship menswear brand by Forcas Studio Limited, offering trendy, comfortable, and affordable fashion to the mass market. From casual wear to athleisure, FTX provides a diverse range of men’s apparel, including boxers, track pants, t-shirts, jackets, sweatshirts, and more. With a perfect balance of style, quality, and price, FTX has become a go-to choice for millions of young customers seeking value-for-money fashion. FTX — Affordable Style, Made for You!

View Products

CONTENO

Comfort, Quality & Joy for Kids

Conteno is the upcoming kids’ wear brand by Forcas Studio Limited, dedicated to providing stylish, comfortable, and high-quality clothing for children. With a focus on durability, playful designs, and affordability, Conteno aims to redefine kids’ fashion with a range of trendy outfits perfect for every occasion. Designed to meet the needs of modern parents looking for fashionable yet practical clothing for their kids, Conteno promises superior comfort and vibrant styles. Conteno — Because Kids Deserve the Best!
