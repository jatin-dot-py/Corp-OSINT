ABOUT US

Touching the lives of billions daily, delivering value as one of India’s largest
specialty chemical manufacturers.

Rossari Biotech Limited is one of India's largest specialty chemical manufacturers, delivering high-performance solutions that touch billions of lives daily. With over four decades of experience, the company offers 4,300+ products to 3,000+ customers across 65+ countries. Operating seven manufacturing facilities with 332,000+ MTPA capacity, Rossari Biotech serves diverse industries including Textiles, Home Personal Care and Performance Chemicals, and Animal Health and Nutrition. The company leads the market across four core chemistries: surfactants, polymers, silicones, and enzymes. Committed to sustainability through green chemistry and zero-liquid-discharge facilities, Rossari Biotech leverages its strong foundation and experienced leadership to shape the future of specialty chemistry, creating lasting value for stakeholders worldwide.
