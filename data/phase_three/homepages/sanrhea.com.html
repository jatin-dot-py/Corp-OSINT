About Us

Welcome to Sanrhea Technical Textiles Limited

Sanrhea is a stock listed ISO 9001:2015 registered company, based in Gujarat, India, concentrating on the production of RFL Dipped fabrics for the Tyre, Conveyor-Belt, and the Rubber Product Industry.

In the year 1997, Sanrhea Technical Textiles Ltd. ( STTL ) entered the field of Technical Textiles by putting up a manufacturing facility for the production of Nylon and Polyester based heavy industrial fabrics for the Tyre and Rubber industry.

  • Infrastructure
Sanrhea’s manufacturing giant features the finest machinery in the field, such as:
  • Jumbo TFO Machines Parallel Winders
  • Prashant Gamatex Sectional Warping Machines
  • Dornier Rapier Looms
  • Sulzer Projectile Looms (weaving fabrics in widths up to 4.5 metres)
The Stenter-based RFL. Dipping Plant is arguably the most modern and automated machine currently installed in India, assuring a consistent and stable quality. The current total production capacity of the company is 180 tons of fabric per month.
A technically qualified, well-trained and optimally experienced human asset complements this technology spine.
