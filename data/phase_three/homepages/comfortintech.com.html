We are dedicated to excellence across a spectrum of ventures

Originally incorporated as Comfort Finvest Limited in 1994, the company transformed, emerging as Comfort Intech Limited in 2000. Today, Comfort Intech is a diversified organization offering innovative solutions across two business verticals.

In a strategic move indicative of our forward-thinking approach, we expanded our horizons to explore new business avenues, including the distillation, manufacturing, import, export, and distribution of IMFL (Indian Made Foreign Liquor) spirits and similar products. Today, Comfort Intech is home to 2 leading Whisky brands in the State of Telangana - Deccan Blue Whisky and Gold Mark Whisky.

Today, we are dynamic players in the trading arena, specializing in a diverse range of goods including consumer appliances, home devices and kitchen appliances. Our presence extends beyond conventional markets, as we actively engage with businesses in harnessing the power of e-commerce solutions to reach a broader audience.

We are poised to chart our course towards a future of growth, sustainability, and innovation. We are committed to driving progress for the communities we serve, aligning our ambitions with the broader vision of a thriving India.
