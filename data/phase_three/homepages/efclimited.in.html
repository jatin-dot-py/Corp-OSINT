About EFC (I) Limited

EFC (I) Limited, founded by first-generation entrepreneurs, Mr. Umesh Sahay and Mr. Abhishek Narbaria, with a vision to build an integrated platform across the commercial real estate value chain. Over the years, the Company has evolved into three diversified as-well-as complementary business verticals spanning Managed Office Solutions, Design & Build (D&B) Turnkey Solutions, and Furniture Manufacturing.

Through its unique Real Estate-as-a-Service (REaaS) model and integrated operating platform, EFC delivers comprehensive solutions across multiple facets of the commercial real estate ecosystem, ranging from workspace management and turnkey interior execution to the design and manufacturing of furniture solutions.

Each of the Company’s business verticals operates as an independent revenue-generating segment with its own growth drivers and customer base, while simultaneously benefiting from strong operational synergies, shared expertise, and cross-functional collaboration within the Group. This integrated approach enables EFC to create sustainable value, enhance execution capabilities, and strengthen its competitive positioning across the commercial real estate sector.
