Automation

Cutting-edge technologies strive to streamline processes, eliminate waste, and enhance efficiency. This focal effort helps to automate various production and processes, and serves to reduce costs, improve consistency, reliability and drive growth across product portfolio.

Production

As a precision engineering company, Harsha believes manufacturing excellence is essential to ensure high quality products, efficient operations, and customer satisfaction. Achieving this objective requires a combination of factors and practices such a robust quality control, process optimisation, skilled workforce, technology, AI & ML integration, continuous improvement and safety and compliance.

  • Stamping – Metal Forming
  • Precision CNC Machining
  • Injection Moulding

Toolroom

The Tool Room is a hub of Tooling Design & Manufacturing Excellence and facilitates the creation of high precision, cutting edge bearing cage and stamping solutions. A dedicated team of skilled engineers and state-of-the-art tool machining equipment combine to sustain this process with great efficiency

Human Capability

In any manufacturing ecosystem, the ability to understand the context, to weigh alternative solutions, and to develop and creatively apply new techniques to achieve better results, gives credence to the human factor. Team Harsha identifies this as a core competence, and has been leveraging critical knowledgeware to address dynamic shifts in demand which may call for strategic realignment and process reengineering in.

Process Capability

Harsha offers world-class precision finishing to meet next-generation requirements. Our advanced processes include Vibro Finishing, Ball Burnishing, Shot Blasting, Zn / Mn Phosphating, Electroplating, Liquid / Gas Nitriding, Heat Treatment, Projection / Pressure Welding etc ensuring top-quality results for every product.

Manufacturing Excellence

Continuous Improvement is a key premise driving the manufacturing ecosystem. One such initiative is the widespread adoption of Total Productive Maintenance (TPM) across the plants. TPM initiatives drive efficiency improvements in both production and support functions, enabling the ability to uphold high quality standards, minimize costs, and ensure timely delivery to valued customers.
