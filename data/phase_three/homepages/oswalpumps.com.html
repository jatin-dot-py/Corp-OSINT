About Oswal Pumps

Oswal Pumps Limited, commenced its operations in 2003, is now recognised as one of the fastest-growing vertically integrated solar pump manufacturers in India. Its success can be attributed to a highly experienced team of engineers and technicians, who have contributed to the Company's growth in the field of solar pumps, submersible pumps, monoblock pumps, pressure pumps, sewage pumps, electric motors, spanning across domestic, agricultural, and industrial ranges. The company's manufacturing facility spans across an area of 41076 sq. meters which is strategically located in or near major agricultural states such as Haryana, Punjab and Uttar Pradesh and is equipped with modern machineries. Oswal Pumps Limited is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company and its products are marked with ISI and BEE certifications.

Our Products

Explore our complete range of solar, domestic, agricultural and industrial pumps and motors.

Domestic Range

Oswal Domestic Pumps are long lasting and reliable for watering of gardens, sprinklers, fountains, cleaning and high pressure washing purpose in homes and small establishments.

Industrial Range

Oswal offers efficient product range of Electric Motors for various machinery applications and High Pressure Pumps for Boiler & RO Plants.

Agricultural Range

Pumps with optimum lift and discharge characteristics, Oswal's Agricultural Pumps are best suitable to water agriculture fields, ideal for drip and sprinkler irrigation.

Solar Pumps

Our solar pumps are designed based on the solar radiation received. Built according to MNRE guidelines, they are suitable for watering purposes in residences, factories, and agricultural fields.

International Range

Oswal Pumps' range consists of stainless steel fabricated borewell submersible pumps that match global standards in performance and efficiency..
