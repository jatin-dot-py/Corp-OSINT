Caution Notice For Job Seekers
It has come to our attention that certain agencies/individuals may be contacting job seekers offering fake employment in The Great Eastern Shipping Company Ltd. The agencies/individuals may demand large sums of money in order to process the application. The agencies/individuals may even be sending appointment letters on fake company letterhead asking the candidate to report at our office. Please be aware of fraudulent and hoax job offers.

Please Note: The Great Eastern Shipping Company Ltd has not authorised any agency/ individuals to issue offers of employment on its behalf. The Great Eastern Shipping Company Ltd does not charge any of the job seekers any money for giving employment offers or for interviews. Anyone dealing with such an agency/ individual will be doing so at their own risk and The Great Eastern Shipping Company Ltd will not be held responsible for any loss or damage suffered directly or indirectly. Should you come across any such fraudulent incident or have any information regarding the same, please assist us in taking appropriate action by reporting the incident on 022-66613151/128 or email us at hrd@greatship.com

INDIA’S LARGEST SHIPPING & OILFIELD SERVICES PROVIDER

The company has two main businesses:
Shipping and offshore. The shipping business is involved in transportation of crude oil, petroleum products, gas
and dry bulk commodities. The offshore business services to the oil companies in carrying out offshore
exploration and production activities, through its subsidiary Greatship (India) Limited.

The shipping business has been awarded the ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001: 2018
standard certification by DNV.GL
