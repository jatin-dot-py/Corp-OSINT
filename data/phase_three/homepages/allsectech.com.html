Managed Payroll Services

Alldigi Tech combines managed payroll services and modern HRMS solutions to help organizations build efficient, compliant, and employee-centric workplaces.

Modern HRMS Solutions

Transform HR operations through a comprehensive Human Resource Management System (HRMS) designed to automate processes, improve employee experiences, and provide actionable workforce insights.

Digitize every stage of the employee journey—from onboarding and role changes to offboarding—while ensuring consistency, compliance, and a seamless employee experience.

Track attendance in real time, manage multiple shift patterns, automate leave requests and approvals, and ensure accurate payroll integration.

Set goals, conduct continuous performance reviews, gather feedback, and monitor employee development through a structured performance management framework.

Enable employees to access payslips, update personal information, apply for leave, submit claims, and access HR documents anytime, from any device.

Securely store, organize, and manage employee records with centralized document repositories, role-based access controls, and digital workflows.

Gain actionable insights into workforce trends, employee productivity, attendance, attrition, and HR performance through interactive dashboards and customizable reports.

Automate routine HR processes such as approvals, notifications, onboarding checklists, and policy acknowledgements to reduce manual effort and improve operational efficiency.

What does Alldigi Tech do for large enterprises?

Alldigi Tech delivers AI-enabled business process management, customer experience operations, payroll, HR, finance, and compliance services for large global enterprises. The company combines automation, analytics, and domain specialists to help enterprises improve operational efficiency, scalability, and customer engagement.

-AI-led operations for CX, HR, payroll, finance, and collections
-Global delivery support across high-volume enterprise workflows
-Human + AI execution for accuracy, compliance, and faster turnaround
-Enterprise integrations with ERP, CRM, and workforce platforms

How does Alldigi Tech combine AI with human service delivery?

Alldigi Tech combines AI-driven automation with trained domain experts to manage high-volume enterprise operations with speed and accuracy. AI handles repetitive workflows, predictive analysis, and intelligent routing, while human teams manage exceptions, compliance decisions, and customer interactions.

-AI automation for workflow orchestration and data processing
-Human oversight for escalations and complex decision-making
-Predictive analytics to improve operational outcomes
-Continuous learning models that optimize process efficiency

Which industries does Alldigi Tech work with?

Alldigi Tech supports enterprises across banking, financial services, healthcare, insurance, retail, telecom, mortgage, and technology sectors. Its services are designed for industries managing complex compliance, high transaction volumes, and customer-intensive operations.

-BFSI operations including KYC, AML, and collections
-Healthcare support for billing, claims, and RCM workflows
-Insurance operations including claims and policy administration
-HR and payroll solutions for large global workforces

How many countries does Alldigi Tech operate in?

Alldigi Tech operates across multiple global markets with delivery capabilities supporting enterprises in more than 70 countries. Its operational model enables multilingual support, compliance management, and scalable service delivery for international businesses.

-Global workforce operations across diverse geographies
-Multi-country payroll and compliance management
-Omnichannel customer support delivery
-Centralized enterprise operations with regional adaptability
