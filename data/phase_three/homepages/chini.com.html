Sugar

BCML is among the leading sugar manufacturers in India with a crushing capacity of 80,000 tonnes of cane per day across 10 plants. All our 10 sugar plants are in Eastern and Central part of Uttar Pradesh.

Ethanol & Alcohol

Ethanol is a green fuel produced by the Company which is blended with petrol for use in vehicles. All its Ethanol producing units are running on Zero Liquid Discharge technology. Molasses which is a by-product is used for producing Ethanol & Alcohol.

Power

The company produces green power as it uses its waste product (bagasse) as raw material instead of using fossil fuels which is harmful for the environment. The Company has a cogeneration capacity of 278.47 MW.

Bagasse

Bagasse is the fibrous matter that remains after sugarcane stalks are crushed to extract their juice and is used as a biofuel. It is also supplied to paper industries for manufacture of eco-friendly paper and is also used in making sustainable disposable food ware.

Agri-Inputs

The company converts left over ash waste from incineration boilers to Bio-potash granules, which is an essential fertiliser. The company also churns out Pressmud which is a rich source of organic manure. These, along with Bio-agents produced for soil health, promote chemical free farming.

DDGS

Balrampur Chini Mill produces DDGS, a valuable by-product from the ENA production process. DDGS is rich in nutrients and suitable for livestock feed. It contains protein, fiber, oil, and essential amino acids, making it ideal for cattle, poultry, fish, and Swan feed. It can be stored without spoilage.

PLA manufacturing (upcoming venture)

BCML is set to venture into Poly Lactic Acid (PLA) manufacturing with a new fully integrated facility. The new venture will facilitate the establishment of India’s first-ever industry scale bio-polymer plant

Sustainability

Sustainability is an integral part of the Company’s business and our Governance framework reflects our commitment towards sustainability.

We understand the significance of being socially responsible. We engage in sustainable practices with a strong motive of enriching the lives of communities where we operate.

The Company’s ESG framework is aligned with United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Environment

BCML has invested in sustainable initiatives with the objective to moderate carbon footprint and costs, strengthening long-term sustainability. The focal point of the Company’s operating policy is an integration through which the by-product of one process serves as raw material for another. This has enabled the Company to optimise resource utilisation while reducing waste disposal and logistics cost.

Social

Over the years, the Company has worked for bringing social and economic change to the underprivileged sections of the society in an equitable and sustainable manner and for the enrichment of lives of communities by creating sustainable livelihoods, promoting education, healthcare, sanitation and development of rural infrastructure and environment sustainability.

Governance

The Company recognises that good governance is a continuous exercise and is the guiding force for sustainable development. Corporate governance refers to, but is not limited to, a set of laws, regulations and good practices and systems that enable an organisation to perform efficiently and ethically to generate long-term wealth and create value for all its stakeholders.
