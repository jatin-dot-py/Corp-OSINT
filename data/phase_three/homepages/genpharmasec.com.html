Welcome to Genpharmasec ltd

The Company was incorporated as Public Limited Company on 16th November, 1992 under the Companies Act, 1956 in the state of Gujarat as “AdiRasayan Limited”. The Company had also received Certificate for Commencement of Business from ROC, Gujarat on November 20, 1992.

Further the name of the Company was changed from “ADIRASAYAN LIMITED” to “GENERIC PHARMASEC LIMITED” on 27th November, 2018.

The Registered Office of the Company was shifted from the State of Gujarat to the State of Maharashtra in Mumbai on 10th April, 2019.
