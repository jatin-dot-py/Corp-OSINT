Bindal Exports Limited

The Company was originally formed and registered as a partnership firm in the name of “M/s J.B. Exports”, in the year 1997. Then it converted into a private Company under the name of “Bindal Exports Private Limited” in the year 2007 and in the year 2016 it was listed on BSE SME exchange under the name of “Bindal Exports Limited”. The company is engaged in the business of manufacturing, finishing, exporting, buying and selling all kinds of fabrics, readymade garments and apparels made from cotton, rayon, polyester, blended-polyester etc
