    FEATURED
    • Venus Remedies Receives Saudi FDA Approval for Plerixafor

      Venus Remedies received Saudi FDA approval for Plerixafor, marking the company's first global regulatory approval for the speciality oncology product. This achievement strengthens our oncology portfolio, expands our presence in the GCC region, and reflects our continued focus on delivering advanced, high-quality therapies to patients worldwide.

    • Venus Remedies Expands Footprint in Latin America

      Venus Remedies strengthened its global presence by securing its first marketing authorisation in Argentina for Ceftriaxone. This milestone marks the company's entry into a key Latin American market, reinforcing our commitment to expanding access to high-quality healthcare solutions and accelerating our international growth journey.

    • Mr. Saransh Chaudhary featured in IndiaMed Today

      We are delighted to share another proud milestone for Venus Remedies. Mr. Saransh Chaudhary, Executive Director & Chief Executive Officer, Venus Medicine Research Centre (VMRC), has been featured in IndiaMed Today for his insightful perspective on antimicrobial resistance (AMR), one of the leading healthcare challenges.
