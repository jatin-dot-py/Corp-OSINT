MMP INDUSTRIES LTD (MMP) had commenced production of Aluminium Powder in 1983 with a technical collaboration with AVL Metal Powders , Belgium.

It has four manufacturing locations :

  • Unit I – Bhandara, District Bhandara (Aluminium Powders, Conductors & Cables)
  • Unit II – Hingna, District Nagpur (Aluminium Powders)
  • Unit III – Umred, District Nagpur (Aluminium Powders and Foils)
  • Unit IV – Umred, District : Nagpur (Composite / Polymer Insulators)

The shares of the Company are listed on National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) since 13th April 2018 (Scrip Code – MMP). The Company is also registered as medium scale industry under MSME Act, 2006.

Founder's Note

MMP Industries Ltd. (Formerly Maharashtra Metal Powders Ltd.) was conceived in 1981 and started manufacturing Aluminium Powders in 1983 with a capacity of 300 MTPA in technical collaboration with AVL Metal Powders, Belgium (Formerly, Est. Andre Van Lerberghe). Our current capacity for all Aluminium Powder verticals is in excess of 22000 MTPA.In the last 40 years or so, MMP has become a well-known brand not only in the Aluminium Powders field but also Aluminium Conductors / Cables and Aluminium Foils.
MMP has consistently stood for excellence in product quality assurance, personalized technical & sales services and working with all our valued customers to adapt our products in their applications.
In line with our Mission & Vision, MMP’s future growth will be driven with the sole objective of becoming the most preferred manufacturer / seller to all its customers and run its business with complete transparency for benefit of its various stake holders and shareholders.

A family company

The Year 1983 marked the beginning of MMP Industries Ltd. MMP Industries Ltd had commenced production of aluminium powder in 1983 with a technical collaboration with AVL Metal powders, Belgium. What Started as a small-scale unit producing Aluminium Powder in Bhandata, Central India has now expanded into a large-scale state of the art setup across 3 locations in central India with a diversified product portfolio encompassing Powders, Pastes, Conductors and Folis.

Proud of it's heritage
