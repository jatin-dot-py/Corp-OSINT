Our Concept

Lead Reclaim & Rubber Products Ltd.

Established in 2012 and proudly listed on the NSE Emerge, Lead Reclaim and Rubber Products LTD (LRRPL) is a centre of innovation in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. We're renowned for our excellent craftsmanship in transforming scrap rubber into top-quality reclaimed rubber, high-grade crumb rubber powder, and premium rubber granules. We combine sustainability and quality in our scrap rubber recycling processes, shaping a greener future for the rubber industry.

Range Of Products

Whole Tyre Reclaim Rubber

We are providing wide range of whole tyre reclaim rubber and these are quality tested at our quality testing unit. With high quality standard and strict quality control system, these range meet the international standard.

Butyl Tube Reclaim Rubber

Butyl Reclaim Rubber is unique product recycled out of butyl rubber tube scrap. The scrap butyl tubes are pure butyl and no other polymer content is present. Also it is a harder and less porous material than other and has enough elasticity to form an airtight seal.

Natural Tube Reclaim Rubber

Natural Reclaim Rubber recycled out from natural rubber tube scrap with well equipped and modernized machinery. Today the use of natural reclaim rubber is widespread, ranging from household to industrial products.
