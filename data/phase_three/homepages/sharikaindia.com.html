About Us

Objective is to address the analytics, automation, and simulation needs of next-generation power systems by establishing a state-of-the-art Simulation Factory. This will integrate advanced tools such as GridApp-D, power simulators, Hypersim, and SCADA systems equipped with IEDs, RTUs, and PMUs. The platform will serve as a reconfigurable and versatile Digital Twin environment, enabling comprehensive testing, validation, and innovation in modern power system operations
