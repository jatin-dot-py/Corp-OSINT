making Strength Meet Precision since 2004

PS Raj Steels Limited is one of India’s leading manufacturers of stainless steel pipes and tubes, trusted for quality, reliability, and innovation since 2004. Guided by a vision to deliver world-class tubular solutions, we have built a strong foundation of advanced technology, modern infrastructure, and skilled expertise.

With a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility and an installed capacity of 20,000 MTPA, we are committed to meeting diverse industry needs with precision and consistency.

Our vision is simple: to be the most reliable partner for stainless steel solutions by offering products that stand for strength, precision, and long-term value.
