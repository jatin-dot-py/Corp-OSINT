About

Shri Techtex Limited (“Shri Techtex”) is engaged in the business of Polypropylene (PP) Non-Woven Fabric manufacturing.

We manufacture PP non-woven fabric in variety of sizes and density. As on the date, we manufacture PP non-woven fabric upto 4.5 meters size and 15 GSM to 800 GSM.
