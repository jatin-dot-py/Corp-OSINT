IM+ Capitals

IM+ Capitals (IMCL) is a leading Corporate and Transaction Advisory company with specialized focus on Real Estate sector. We cater our advisory services to both corporate and retail. IMCL extends advisory services in Capital Management, RERA Consulting and Real Estate transactions. Our investment arm by the name of IM Investment Capitals Pvt. Ltd., wholly owned subsidiary of IMCL, further adds to the credentials in raising the sector specific funds in real estate.
