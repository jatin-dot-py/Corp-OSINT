About Us
Our Story
Where it all started
Founded on January 30, 1984, as Vajreshwari Cosmetics Private Limited, we evolved over time to become Religare Enterprises Limited, marking a significant shift into financial services in 1994 with the establishment of Religare Securities Limited, our retail equity broking arm.
