What are Entry-level & Blue-collar Jobs?

When you hear this term, what is the first question that pops up on your mind? Why are these terms trending and popular nowadays? Entry-level and blue-collar jobs are basically known as the manual workforce. The manual workforce is a primary key factor in industrial sectors such as logistics, food industry, construction, manufacturing, warehousing, agriculture, transportation, beauty, telecom and retail sector, for both entry-level and experienced employees.

There are a huge number of entry-level and blue-collar job vacancies for 9th pass, 10th pass, 12th pass, diploma, and degree candidates across India. Workers such as plumbers, fitters, mechanics, electricians, beauticians, drivers, deliveries, staff nurses, data entry, cooks/chefs, security, and maids/nannies can check, search, and apply for jobs in their own preferred local languages on Teamlease.com.

Why Teamlease.com for Entry-level & Blue-collar Jobs?

Getting placed in an entry-level or blue-collar job market in India is very difficult. If you are looking for entry-level jobs or vocational sector jobs, then you're in the right place. Teamlease.com is a job portal that helps in finding jobs for workers or employees with ease.

TeamLease is considered one of the best job sites for entry-level and blue-collar jobs in metropolitan cities such as Bangalore, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune, Chennai, Coimbatore, Kolkata, Surat and Visakhapatnam. We designed TeamLease.com as a recruitment platform specifically for skilled laborers and entry-level workers, where they can search and apply easily for hiring companies across India such as OLA, Swiggy, Zomato, Uber, Pizza Hut, Dominos, KFC and more.
