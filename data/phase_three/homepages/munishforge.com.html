Munish Highlights

Since 1942, with an expansion into the Forging and Casting Industry in 1981 and a group workforce of 650, Munish operates forging and casting facilities covering 75,000 square meters, offering a comprehensive One Stop Solution for customized Fabrication, Forging, Casting, Sheet Metal and Assembly, proudly holding the status of a One Star Export House and maintaining an overseas office cum warehouse in the UK.
