About Us

JAKHARIA Group relies on its strengths to bring innovation
to the value of its history and experience.

Combining creativity and technology to produce increasingly innovative solutions which meet and anticipate the needs of the textile market. Thanks to this approach and these values, jakharia fabric has strengthened its leadership in market over the years and expanded its presence on national and international markets. Its system of values is based on highly product and market orientated and is enhanced by the desire to promote a model of sustainable and responsible development.

An objective that the Company pursues, aligning its activities to those ethical principles and values declined into the Jakharia Group, formally adopted by its Board of Directors, since Jakharia Fabric is convinced that the achievement of business aims cannot be deemed without respecting fundamentals values.
