About Starlog

Who We Are

Starlog is a leading provider of integrated crane hire and heavy logistics solutions in India. With decades of industry expertise, a modern fleet, and a commitment to safety, we help businesses lift, move, and manage their heaviest challenges—efficiently and affordably.

Our Core Services

Crane Hire Services

High-capacity mobile and crawler cranes for all industrial needs—available on rent with expert operators.

Heavy Lifting & Rigging

Custom lifting solutions for oversized and overweight equipment across industries like power, ports, oil & gas, and construction.

Port Operations & Terminal Equipment

Specialized support for container terminals including RTGs, reach stackers, and port cranes.

Project Logistics

End-to-end logistics planning and execution for turnkey projects, including route surveys, permissions, and multimodal transport.

Equipment Maintenance & AMC

Full-service maintenance contracts and 24/7 support for your lifting and handling equipment.
