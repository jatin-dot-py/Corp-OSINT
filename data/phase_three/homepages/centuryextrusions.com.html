Century Extrusions Limited

Established in 1988, the company is promoted by Late Shri M. P. Jhunjhunwala, a first generation entrepreneur. The Company is one of India’s largest aluminium extrusion manufacturer. CEL’s production facility is located at Kharagpur (West Bengal) in eastern part of India, close to leading primary aluminium manufacturers in India. With an experience spanning a rich 35 years, CEL enjoys a number of first mover advantages comprising a comprehensive understanding of the aluminum and aluminum extrusions market, reputed brand, low historical asset cost and a strong customer base among others.
