Welcome to LCAL.

Established in 1979, we are a leading chemical manufacturer based in Alwar, Rajasthan. With cutting-edge facilities spanning 84 acres in the Matsya Industrial Area, our commitment to quality is evident in our diverse range of high-quality chemicals.

Producing a wide range of finest quality chemicals

With a focus on specialized production capabilities, our expertise lies in manufacturing caustic soda and a diverse array of chemicals. Renowned for our exceptional product offerings which encompass Caustic Soda Lye, Sodium Hypochlorite, Hydrogen Gas, Liquid Chlorine, HCL, CPW, and more. Our reputation is built on a legacy of excellence and innovation.

Environmental Responsibility

Dedicated to environmental responsibility, we permanently closed a 200 Tons Per Day mercury cell factory, eradicating the risk of hazardous mercury release. Our adoption of cleaner fuels like Hydrogen and LPG has curtailed emissions, contributing to a greener planet. Advancing sustainability, a 12-megawatt solar power plant aims to cover over 10% of energy needs, complemented by state-of-the-art RO plants ensuring efficient wastewater treatment and recycling—reflecting our commitment to responsible resource management and environmental stewardship.
