Welcome to Our World

DUKE OFFSHORE LIMITED is one of the countries premier marine companies and is publicly listed on the BSE. Over the years we have built up a huge resource base of experience and pride ourselves on successfully completing each project on time.

Our company started its life as a diving company which specialised in underwater platform inspection, maintenance and repair (IMR) program for oil companies. In addition diving services are provided for drill rig support, SBM and SPM installation, operations and maintenance.

Over the years the company has grown from strength to strength and now owns a fleet of vessels which assist us in force protection of marine assets such as oil fields, high speed offshore crew transport, dredging crew support and in harbour channel survey.

Our Engineering Division has dedicated teams of engineers and support staff who are specialised in hook up and commissioning of offshore oil field platforms, their maintenance, modification, inspection services as well as the maintenance of our fleet.

We thank you for your interest in our company.
