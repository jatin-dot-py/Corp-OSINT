    ABOUT US

    Driven by the vision to emerge as finest independent Oil & Gas company

    HOEC was incorporated in 1983 under the visionary leadership of Late Shri. H.T. Parekh who foresaw the need for private participation in the Indian Oil & Gas sector. As one of the pioneer private players in the Indian E&P sector, HOEC holds a portfolio consists of 9 Oil & Gas blocks of Discovered Resources and 1 Exploratory block, with a diverse geographical footprint in 4 out of 7 producing basins in India.
