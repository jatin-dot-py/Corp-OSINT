Laboratory Information Management System

At Qualitek Labs, we are committed to delivering a seamless and transparent testing experience. Our laboratory operations are powered by eLABSS LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System), enabling complete digital management of the testing lifecycle—from sample registration to report delivery.

With eLABSS LIMS, every sample is securely registered, tracked, and monitored throughout the testing process. Customers can access real-time sample status, download certified test reports, view invoice history, and receive automated SMS and email notifications, ensuring accuracy, transparency, and faster communication.

Sample Registration & Tracking

Every sample is digitally registered and assigned a unique identification number, allowing complete traceability from sample receipt to final report issuance.

Digital Test Reports

Access and download laboratory reports instantly in PDF format, anytime and from anywhere.

Automated Notifications

Receive timely email updates for sample status changes, report availability, and other important milestones.

Billing & Invoice Management

View and download invoices, payment history, and billing details through a centralized customer dashboard.

What services does Qualitek Labs offer?

Qualitek Labs provides a comprehensive range of services including Testing, Inspection, Homologation, Research and Development, Training, and consultancy, Accreditation & Operation. We cater services to various industries such as Automotive, Electrical & Electronics, Environment Monitoring, Aerospace, defence, Medical Equipment, Minerals, Packaging, and Energy

How do I request a quote?
You can request a quote via our website form, email us at customersupport@qualiteklab.com, or call +91-76207-37704. Our team will respond within 4 business hours.
Are your testing services NABL accredited?

Yes, our services are accredited by NABL (ISO/IEC 17025:2017), BIS, FSSAI, CDSCO, MoEF, CPCB and NABCB — ensuring reports accepted by all national and international regulatory bodies.

How long does it take to receive test results?
Standard TAT is 3–10 business days. Express 24–48 hour testing is available for urgent requirements. TAT is confirmed at the time of sample submission.
Do you provide training for industry professionals?
Yes, Qualitek Labs organises training programmes on testing procedures, regulatory compliance, ISO standards, and skill development tailored for organisations and individuals.
