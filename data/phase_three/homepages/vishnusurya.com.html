Vishnusurya Projects and Infra Limited

A member of the renowned Agni Group, Chennai, Vishnusurya Projects and Infra Limited is a leading Indian construction and infrastructure company specializing in EPC Projects delivered across all key sectors such as transportation, rail, resource, and institutional development. The Company also has a strong presence in mining and implementation of transformational civil and commercial turnkey projects.

Vishnusurya has been independently assessed by QRO & is compliant with the requirements of ISO 9001:2015 for Quality Management Systems. The Company holds a Class - I permit from Tamilnadu Highways Department, Tamilnadu Public Works Department, Tamilnadu Water Supply and Drainage Board and  Greater Chennai Corporation.

Vishnusurya is headquartered in Chennai, TamilNadu, India.
