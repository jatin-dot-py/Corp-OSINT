This is Huhtamaki

Our vision is to be the first choice for sustainable packaging solutions globally. We strive to earn this position every day with our leading innovative products that ensure hygiene and safety for food on-the-shelf and food on-the-go. We believe in protecting food, people and the planet and supporting affordability and accessibility to food and everyday necessities for people around the world.

35 Operating countries

17,400 employees

A Nordic heritage of more than 100 years

~4.0 billion euros net sales
