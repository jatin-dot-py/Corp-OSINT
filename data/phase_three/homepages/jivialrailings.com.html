Premium Aluminium Railing Manufacturer in India

Jivial Industries Limited is a manufacturer of premium aluminium railings and stainless steel glass railing products, established in 2006 at Rajkot, Gujarat, India. Under the direction of Mr. Anand Patel (Director), we produce 50+ product variants using Aluminium 6063 alloy, serving architects, builders, and homeowners across India and export markets.

India’s aluminium extrusion market exceeds ₹15,000 Crore and is growing at 8-10% annually. Over 70% of new residential buildings in India now prefer aluminium glass railings over traditional steel or iron, driven by zero maintenance, 25-year lifespan, and modern aesthetics. Jivial Industries is at the forefront of this shift with the widest product range of any railing manufacturer in Western India.

We are a company focused on creating sustainable value by providing interior and exterior railing products to satisfy the customers around the world. We strive to make the future better for our customers by becoming the world's most efficient and innovative Railing Products manufacturing company.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best material for balcony railings?

+

Aluminium 6063, rust-proof, lightweight, 25+ year lifespan, zero painting maintenance. The industry standard for modern railings.

How much does aluminium glass railing cost in India?

+

Bracket systems: ₹800-1,200/ft. Continuous profiles: ₹1,000-1,800/ft. Concealed systems: ₹1,500-2,500/ft. Glass extra.

Top-mount vs concealed glass railing?

+

Top-mount: visible profile on slab, cheaper, easy retrofit. Concealed: hidden channel, frameless look, plan during construction.

Can aluminium railings be used for staircases?

+

Yes, step brackets, side-mount, and continuous profiles for angles. 15 handrail options including LED-integrated.

What glass thickness for railings?

+

12mm tempered (residential). 15-19mm (commercial). Up to 25mm for heavy-duty profiles.

Does Jivial export internationally?

+

Yes: Jivial Industries serves India and export markets. For export enquiries, email export@jivialrailings.com

Available finishes?

+

Silver anodised, wood-grain powder coating, any RAL colour. SS: PVD Gold, Rose Gold, Black.
