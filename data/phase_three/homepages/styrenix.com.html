OUR PRODUCTS

We focus on making the best products in all sectors

Styrenix PS 1

Absolac® High Heat products are heat modified ABS that meets stringent stability requirements for thermally stressed components and are available for the Indian domestic market. These resins can be coated easily and reliably. Be it for automotive exterior parts, hub-caps, spoilers or transmission covers, Absolac High Heat is the material of choice for numerous heat-resistant applications.

Styrenix PS 2

Styrenix® PS general purpose polystyrene resins are transparent polymers suitable for injection molding or extrusion applications. Uses for these products vary from food service and food packaging to refrigerator components, healthcare and diagnostic labware as well as XPS insulation.

Transperent Spl.

Absolan® is Styrenix Performance Materials Limited’s Styrene Acrylonitrile copolymer (SAN) portfolio and is available for the Indian domestic market. Absolan grades feature a very well-balanced property profile ranging from excellent transparency, good chemical resistance to high stiffness and good dimensional stability. SAN GF is also available for high stiffness and high rigidity applications.

ABOUT US

Styrenix Performance Materials  helps its customers succeed by offering innovative and sustainable best-in-class solutions, designed to give them a competitive edge in their markets. The company provides styrenic applications for many everyday products across a broad range of industries, including automotive, electronics, household, construction, healthcare, packaging and toys/sports/leisure.

Our Vision

Our Vision is delivering excellence, with the ambition to become the preferred global supplier of high performance polymer raw materials to our valued customers.

Our Mission

Styrenix Performance is dedicated to enabling customer success by providing innovative and reliable materials solutions. Our name reflects our core philosophy—combining technical expertise with solution-driven thinking. Through a strong understanding of customer requirements, we deliver cost-effective products that enhance quality of life by improving safety, convenience, and performance.

SPEARHEADING STYRENIC SUCCESS

For over 40 years, Styrenix Performance Materials is the leader and number one producer of Absolac (ABS) and Absolan (SAN) in India. We are committed to work hand-in-hand with our customers to drive success together.
