Key differentiators

Our Vision

Our manufacturing plants have full forging capabilities and extensive heat-treating and stress peening facilities. Our tool and die facility has CNC equipment which allows very quick fabrication of the specialized high tolerance tooling required to meet our customer’s demanding specifications. This versatility allows production of a wide range of products in large quantities from single prototypes and small batch sizes to high volumes.

Infrastructure & Capacity

At APIL our endeavor is to meet the growing demand of our discerning customers. To this end we have established a strong manufacturing facility, dedicated human resource and a complete infrastructure to meet today’s standards.

Quality focus

Auto Pins has a habit of consistently delivering quality – whether it is in the form of suspension parts or drive train components. Our extensive product line boasts of automotive components and accessories for vehicles across the globe. Trucks, tractors and transport automobiles over the world bear witness to our quality standards.
