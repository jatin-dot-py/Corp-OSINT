about sharda ispat

Sharda Ispat Ltd.(SIL) is situated in Nagpur, the Center of India. The Company was incorporated in 1960 with innovative thinking and passion towards delivering quality products to market. At Sharda Ispat Ltd., Kamptee road, we manufacture products like Flats, Rounds, RCS etc. For more than six decades SIL is supplying alloy, carbon & spring steel rolled products to various automobile OEM component manufacturer, as well forging & bright bar industries.The unit is fully automated and certified by TUV SUD for System certification ISO 9001:2008.
