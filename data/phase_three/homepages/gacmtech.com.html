// How an engagement runs

A process with no surprises.

01

Discover

A paid, fixed-scope discovery sprint: workshops, users, constraints. You leave with an architecture, a plan and a price — yours to keep either way.

02

Design

UX, brand and system design run together with the technical architecture — so nothing designed can't be built.

03

Build

Senior engineers, weekly demos of working software, and a scope board you can see. Change is priced honestly, never smuggled.

04

Launch & run

Production launch, monitoring, SLAs and an iteration cadence — or a clean handover to your team, fully documented.
