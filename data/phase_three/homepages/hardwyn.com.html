India’s 1st NSE & BSE Listed Architectural Hardware Company

  • Over 60 years of excellence as a trusted hardware company in India.
  • Offering a complete range of kitchen, door, glass, wardrobe, and sliding hardware solutions.
  • Committed to innovation, sustainability, and precision engineering in every product.

Established in 1965, Hardwyn India Limited has been a pioneer in transforming the architectural hardware landscape of India. Renowned for its superior craftsmanship and reliable performance, the company continues to set industry benchmarks with products that combine design innovation, functionality, and long-lasting durability. As India’s 1st NSE & BSE listed architectural hardware brand, Hardwyn remains dedicated to building modern spaces that reflect quality, trust, and excellence across homes, offices, and commercial projects nationwide.
