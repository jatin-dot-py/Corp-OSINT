ABOUT MONOLITHISCH INDIA

Monolithisch India Limited, part of the Mineral Group, is a leading provider of high-performance refractory solutions. Our portfolio includes premium ramming mass for the metallurgical sector, and other advanced materials. With India’s largest premix ramming mass facility, we deliver precision, scale, and consistent quality. Driven by innovation and technical expertise, we ensure superior durability and efficiency.
