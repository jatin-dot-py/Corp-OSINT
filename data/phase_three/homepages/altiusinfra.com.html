India’s Leading Telecom & Digital Infrastructure Trust

At Altius, we unlock the transformative power of connectivity. As one of the largest telecom infrastructure platforms in the world, Altius is dedicated to developing and managing the highest quality telecom infrastructure.

With data traffic surging, there’s a growing need for agile, cost-efficient, and environmentally friendly solutions to meet coverage demands across rural, semi-urban, and urban areas. Our commitment to Green Power and Sustainability is central to our mission.

our portfolio

Altius is your go-to partner for Digital Infrastructure solutions, meeting the connectivity needs of Telecom Operators, Enterprises, and Connectivity Providers. Our infrastructure supports the entire range of telecom technologies, from 2G to 5G, future innovations, IoT, small cells, and AI applications. As the world's largest tower platform, our success is powered by a strong and talented team.

With a comprehensive range of new-age solutions including Macro Towers like GBT, RTT, Lean and Ultra lean Towers, Cell on Wheels (COWs), Camouflage towers, Advanced In-Building Solutions, Small Cells, and Power & Space solutions for mobile network operators, Altius provides innovative and reliable services to meet the evolving needs of Operators. We have a robust portfolio of companies under our umbrella, that is dedicated to enhancing connectivity and driving technological advancements across India.

  • Building infrastructure for the country with largest installed base of 2,58,000+ towers.
  • Building infrastructure capabilities for our customers with pan-India deployment.
  • Modular technical and commercial architecture that enables Operators to spread out their cost.
  • Best-in-class network availability embedded into Operations practices.
  • Green Power and Sustainability are central to Altius's proposition.
  • Fiberized sites to support the growth of Traffic.

sustainability

We recognize that our business success is intrinsically linked to environmental responsibility, social well-being, and strong governance practices. These are the core tenets of our Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) framework.

Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) at Altius

At Altius, Safety Above All is the foundation of everything we do. We believe every individual deserves a workplace that is safe, healthy, and secure. Our commitment goes beyond regulatory compliance -protecting people and the planet while fostering a sustainable future.

To bring this vision to life, we focus on:
  • Leading by Example: Setting industry benchmarks as a world-class operator, responsible corporate citizen, and employer of choice - while learning from global best practices.
  • Empowering Our People: Every team member has the authority to identify risks and stop unsafe activity. Our principle is simple: we work safe, or we don’t work.
  • Building a Duty of Care: Creating an environment where everyone can perform at their best - confidently and safely.
  • Driving Visible Leadership: Engaging teams and embracing innovation to enhance safety, health, and wellbeing.
  • Ensuring Transparency & Accountability: Committing to open reporting and strict adherence to laws, policies, and standards.

Together, we share one unwavering goal: No one gets hurt.

Safety Above All - because every life matters.
