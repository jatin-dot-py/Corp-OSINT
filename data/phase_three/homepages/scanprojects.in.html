Scan Projects

An Integrated Engineering Business

SCAN PROJECTS LIMITED FORMERLY KNOWN AS AMBALA CEMENTS LIMITED WAS ORIGINALY INCORPOIRATED AT 20-02-1992 UNDER THE COMPANIES ACT 1956. IT WAS SUBSEQUENTLY CONVERTED IN TO A LIMITED COMPANY. THE COMPANY WAS ESTABLISHED AS A MINI CEMENT PLANT AND ENTERED IN MANUFACTURING & SALE OF CEMENT. LATERON THE COMPANY HAS DECIDED TO ENTER INTO THE MANUFACTIRING, ERECTION & COMMISSNING OF CEMENT PLANTS AND ITS ALLIED INDUSTRIAL MACHINERY AND EQUIPMNENTS.

Company Vision
