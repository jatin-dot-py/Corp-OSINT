Hydraulic Fittings, DIN Metric Fittings, JIC Tube Fittings, and more


We provide Hydraulic Fittings, Metric Fittings, DIN Metric Fittings, Tube Fittings, JIC Tube Fittings, O Ring Face Seal Fittings, Conversion Fittings, Nuts, Ferrules, Equal Straight Couplings, Equal Elbow Unions, Equal Tee Unions, Bulkhead Straight Couplings, Bulkhead Union Elbows, Welding Connectors, Parallel Male Stud Couplings, Parallel Female Couplings, Male Stud Elbows, Swivel Connectors, Swivel Elbows, Swivel Nut Elbows, Swivel Branch Tee, Swivel Run Tee, Swivel Nut Branch Tee, Swivel Nut Run Tee, Standpipe Adaptors, Hollow Hex Plugs, Caps, Weld Nipples, Mesh Fittings, Soft Seal Fittings. Our setup is situated in Thane, Pune, Nashik (Maharashtra), Pithampur (Madhya Pradesh), India and Virginia (USA - United States Of America); and we primarily serve customers from countries such as India, USA (United States of America), Germany, and Italy.
