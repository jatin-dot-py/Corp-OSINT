About Shreedhar Spinners

Shreedhar Spinners Limited is a modern cotton yarn manufacturer in India, operating from a Amravati MIDC Textile Park in Maharashtra. As a subsidiary of Shreedhar Cotsyn Private Limited, star export house, our promoters combine decades of textile experience with advanced spinning technology to deliver consistent, export-quality yarns for knitting and weaving applications.

With 28,608 spindles, we are growing to meet the demand of our home textile, apparel, and fabric manufacturers.
