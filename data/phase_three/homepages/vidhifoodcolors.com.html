World-Class Color Solutions

Vidhi Specialty Food Ingredients Ltd is a leading manufacturer of Superior Synthetic Colours suitable for Food, Pharmaceutical, Cosmetic, Personal Care & Home Care industries. Our colours are presently being distributed and consumed in over 80 countries across 6 continents. We have 2 manufacturing facilities located in the state of Maharashtra & 1 manufacturing facility in the state of Gujarat. Today VSFIL is an ISO 9001, ISO 22000, ISO 17025, HACCP, FSSC 22000 (GFSI) & GMP certified company. Our Manufacturing facilities have been audited and found satisfactory by the U.S. FDA. All our Food Grade colours carry HALAL and Kosher certification.
