Welcome to Abhishek Integrations Limited

Engineering Better Solutions. Building a Better Future.

At Abhishek Integrations Limited (AIL), we believe that successful industries are built on quality, reliability and continuous improvement.

We are an Engineering, Manufacturing and Technical Services Company, providing products, project execution and technical support for industrial, commercial, institutional and infrastructure sectors.

Our activities cover manufacturing, electrical and mechanical engineering, project execution, testing & commissioning, operations & maintenance, specialized services for circuit breakers, transformers, switchgear and electrical panels, HVAC systems, fire protection systems, utility infrastructure and technical support services.

By bringing these capabilities together under one organization, we provide customers with a convenient and coordinated approach throughout different stages of their projects — from planning and execution to testing, servicing and long-term maintenance.

As a company listed on the NSE Emerge Platform of the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE), we are committed to responsible business practices, transparency, corporate governance and creating long-term value for our customers, business partners and shareholders.

  • Specialized Circuit Breaker Services

    We provide specialized engineering services for inspection, testing, servicing, refurbishment, maintenance and commissioning support for a wide range of circuit breakers used in industrial and infrastructure applications. Our objective is to help customers improve equipment performance, reduce unexpected downtime and support reliable operations.

  • Specialized Transformer Services

    We provide inspection, testing, diagnostics, servicing and maintenance support for power and distribution transformers to help improve reliability and extend equipment life.

  • Switchgear & Electrical Panel Services

    We provide installation, testing, servicing, maintenance and technical support for switchgear, HT Panels, LT Panels, PCC, MCC, APFC Panels, RMUs and associated electrical distribution equipment.

  • Testing & Commissioning

    We verify the performance of electrical systems through inspection, testing and commissioning before they are placed into operation, helping customers achieve safe and dependable performance.

  • Operations & Maintenance

    We provide preventive, predictive and corrective maintenance services to help customers improve equipment availability, reduce operational interruptions and extend the service life of their assets.

  • Mechanical Engineering

    We provide engineering support for mechanical systems through installation, maintenance and technical services for industrial and infrastructure projects.

  • HVAC & Air Conditioning

    We provide installation, testing, commissioning, servicing and maintenance solutions for HVAC and air conditioning systems that support comfortable and efficient working environments.

  • Fire Protection Systems

    We provide engineering support for fire protection systems through installation, testing, inspection, commissioning and maintenance to help customers maintain safer facilities.
