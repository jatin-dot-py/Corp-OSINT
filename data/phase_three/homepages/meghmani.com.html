Research & Development

Meghmani Organics Limited houses a cutting-edge R&D Centre spanning over 5000 square feet in Sanand, Ahmedabad. The state-of-the-art facility is registered by the prestigious Council of Scientific & Industrial Research (CSIR), New Delhi and has earned accreditation for ‘Good Laboratory Practices (GLP) and Norms on OECD Principles’ from the National GLP Monitoring Authority (NGCMA), Government of India. MOL’s R&D Centre is dedicated to various vital activities, including the development of off-patent molecules, process parameter enhancements, time cycle optimization, and scaling up new technologies from laboratory to production levels. The facility also holds approval from the Department of Science and Industrial Research (DSIR), further affirming its credibility and dedication to scientific excellence.
