Why Choose Us

At Agni Green Power Limited, we are committed to providing innovative solar energy solutions to our clients. Here are some reasons why you should choose us:

Experience

With over 28 years of experience in the solar energy industry, we have established ourselves as a trusted and reliable name in this field.

Turn-key solutions

We provide complete turn-key solutions for solar PV power plant projects, from conceptualization to completion, for ratings ranging from a few watts to megawatt capacity, as per international standards.

In-house manufacturing

We manufacture high-quality, high-performance, and robust power conditioning units and BOS systems in our modernized, well-equipped factory. Most of our products are designed and developed by our own R&D unit and tested and certified by accredited certifying authorities like TUV Rhineland and ERTL, etc.

ISO certified

We are an ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, and ISO 45001:2018 certified company, ensuring that our products and services meet the highest quality standards.

MNRE registered

We are a registered channel partner of the Ministry of New & Renewable Energy (MNRE), Govt. of India, for off-grid solar PV applications, and our MNRE-CRISIL grading is SP1B.

Customer-centric approach

We prioritize our customers and strive to provide them with the best-in-class products and services, building strong relationships with them.
