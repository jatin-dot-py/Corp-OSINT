About Us

Kabra Extrusiontechnik (KET) is a part of renowned Kolsite Group. With over 6 decades of experience, more than 15,700 installations and presence in more than 105 countries in Americas, Middle East, Asia and Africa. KET enjoys leadership position in the extrusion market.

It is our pleasure to introduce ourselves as one of India’s premier manufacturer & exporter of extrusion plants.

Our constant endeavour is to offer better solutions to plastics processors across the globe.

Kabra Extrusiontechnik has set benchmarks in plastics extrusion industry by modern R&D techniques and various processes to cater the market requirements.

Plasticulture involves using plastic in agriculture for better water management, crop protection, and storage. It helps conserve water, improve crop yield, and extend the shelf life of produce. Techniques like drip irrigation, mulching, and greenhouse farming make agriculture more efficient and sustainable.

Contributing to the development of rural & urban infrastructure - Enhancing Safety-Convenience & Durability! Infrastructure is one of the key focus areas of growth. Plastics pipes & profiles play a major role in the fields of Housing, Industrial & Public Infrastructure, Sanitation, Water Reticulation, Irrigation, Desalination & Effluent Treatment, Micro-ducts (Fibre optics) & conduits.

Know More

Flexible packaging is widely used across industries like food, beverages, pharmaceuticals, and personal care. In India, it is popular for unit packs of snacks, dairy products, shampoos, and toothpaste. Its advantages include better product protection, cost-effectiveness, high-barrier films, and advanced printing for attractive packaging.

Know More

Telecom cables can be installed underwater, overhead, or underground, usually in polyethylene ducts (25mm–100mm). Microduct technology, now widely used, enables fiber cables with up to 96 fibers to be installed in ducts as small as 8mm. These microducts can be jetted over long distances, improving efficiency in 3G & 4G data communication.
