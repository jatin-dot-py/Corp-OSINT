About Us

Driven by Quality. Powered by Responsibility

Since 2008, Scoda Tubes Ltd. has been a leading name in stainless steel pipes and tubes manufacturing. With a vision that blends technological advancement with environmental responsibility, we offer high-quality seamless & welded stainless steel solutions for a global market. Our solar-powered facility reflects our commitment to sustainable manufacturing, ensuring excellence while minimizing environmental impact.

READ OUR MISSION

Stainless Steel Seamless Pipes

Our stainless steel seamless pipes are engineered for demanding applications requiring high strength, leak-free performance, and resistance to extreme temperatures and pressure. These pipes are extensively used in onshore and offshore oil and gas operations, particularly in OCTG systems for casing, tubing, and line pipe. They're also suited for petrochemical transport, marine fluid systems, and high-load mechanical structures in power plants.

Stainless Steel Seamless Tube

These tubes offer precise dimensional control and excellent corrosion resistance, making them ideal for applications where consistent fluid or heat transfer is essential. Commonly used in heat exchangers, condensers, LP/HP feedwater heaters, and industrial coolers, these tubes perform exceptionally well in systems exposed to varying thermal loads.

Read More

Stainless Steel Seamless “U” Tubes

Our seamless U-tubes are bent to precise tolerances for use in heat exchangers, boilers, and superheaters where space efficiency and thermal conductivity are crucial. These tubes maintain structural integrity under cyclic thermal stress and are suitable for both horizontal and vertical mounting in critical systems.

Read More

Stainless Steel Instrumentation Tubes

Designed for accuracy and cleanliness, our instrumentation tubes are tailored for signal lines, pressure measurement, and chemical dosing systems. These tubes meet stringent tolerances, making them ideal for nuclear energy, automotive, medical, and aerospace industries where performance must be precise and reliable.

Stainless Steel Welded Pipes

Welded stainless steel pipes are a cost-effective solution for medium-pressure systems where internal cleanliness and corrosion resistance are essential. Their reliability makes them a staple in chemical processing plants, gas pipelines, and mechanical frameworks for industrial operations.

Stainless Steel Welded Tubes

Designed for accuracy and cleanliness, our instrumentation tubes are tailored for signal lines, pressure measurement, and chemical dosing systems. These tubes meet stringent tolerances, making them ideal for nuclear energy, automotive, medical, and aerospace industries where performance must be precise and reliable.

Read More

Stainless Steel Welded 'U' Tubes

Welded U-tubes are designed for compact heat transfer systems where space is limited and efficiency is key. These are commonly installed in air preheaters, feedwater heaters, and recuperators, and are valued for their ease of fabrication and installation in cost-sensitive applications.
