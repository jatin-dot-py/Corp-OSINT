Oil Manufacturing Company

Who Are We?

Gandhar Oil refinery (India) Limited is a known name in the world of Speciality Oils. Our Company is a leading manufacturer of white oils by revenue with a growing focus on the consumer and healthcare end-industries. We have a diversified customer base that comprised 3,558 customers during the fiscal 2023. We produce a broad variety of speciality oils and lubricants such as White oils, waxes, jellies, automotive oils, industrial oils, transformer oils and rubber processing oils. Our products are sold under our flagship brand “Divyol”.

Our Company has three decades of experience in the specialty oils industry. We have increased the scale of our operations over the years, while increasing efficiency and reducing costs. Our Company started with the Taloja Plant in 1994, subsequently set up the Silvassa Plant in 2000 and set up Texol (which has become our Subsidiary with effect from March 30, 2022) with a partner in 2017, to expand into UAE. Our business model affords us the flexibility to grow and manage our operations.

Extensive and Diversified Customer Base

Gandhar is a leading manufacturer of white oils by revenue, engaged in producing Pharmaceutical, Health care, and performance oil (PHPO), Process Insulating Oil (PIO) and Lubricants (automotive oils and industrial oils). These products also meet national and international quality standards and are approved by India FDA, ISO Certifications, Kosher, BIS and Halal.

Your trust in our brand is backed by ISO 9001: 2015, ISO 14001: 2015, ISO 45001: 2018, and GMP certified, along with Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and DSIR certifications.

To add more our company is a government-recognized 4-Star Export House and Registration cum membership of the Federation of Indian Export Organization.

Riding high with us

We catered to an extensive customer base of 3,558 Indian and global companies such as Marico, Dabur, Encube during the Financial Year 2023. We manufacture lubricant oils for Gulf Oil, Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone and other users of industrial machines and equipment. Our customers for the PIO division include Toshiba Transmission and other leading manufacturers of transformers and power distribution and transmission companies. We have long-standing relationships with several of our key customers and have been able to maintain high customer loyalty.

Our growth is the result of increase in our share of business with existing customers, winning new customers, expansion of our product portfolio, expansion of our overseas business and our ability to respond to emerging industry trends towards consumer and healthcare end-industries

That the biggest names in industry choose us, is testimony to our credibility

From a modest start to mega growth

Gandhar began its journey to success in 1992, catching the wave of optimism following liberalisation. Gandhar led by a qualified and experienced management team with over 3 decades of experience in specialty oils sector. With a modest start in Mumbai with a limited product line and thanks to a combination of experienced and qualified management team, it soon became a force to be reckoned with.

Gandhar is a leading manufacturer of white oils by revenue, engaged in producing Pharmaceutical, Health care, and performance oil (PHPO), Process Insulating Oil (PIO) and Lubricants (automotive oils and industrial oils). These products also meet national and international quality standards and are approved by India FDA, ISO Certifications, Kosher, BIS and Halal.
With approx 800 strong workforce, the Group is a Leading manufacturer of White oils by revenue and is one of the fastest-growing corporate entities in India with overseas operations catered to over 100 countries globally.

Navigating to success with experience and expertise

Gandhar’s Board of Directors, supported by a qualified and experienced management team, who have experience in and knowledge of the specialty oils sector, including in the fields of administration, marketing and human resource management.
