OUR BRANDS

HISTORY

1968

• Incorporated as Aristo Plast Pvt. Ltd.

1971

• Became a wholly owned subsidiary of Blow Plast Ltd.

1973

• Manufacturing plant was commissioned at Nasik.

1981

• Name of the company was changed to VIP Industries Limited.

1982

• Manufacturing facility was inaugurated at Jalgaon, operations were later discontinued.

1986

• Got listed on BSE.
• Manufacturing unit was set up at Nagpur, operations were later discontinued.

1995

• New manufacturing facility was set up at Sinnar.

1989

• Launched a new brand by the name "Alfa".

2001

• Entered into a strategic alliance with France-based luggage maker Delsey which was later discontinued.

2005

• Commenced MEA operations.
• Design Studio was setup at VIP Head Office.

2006

• Manufacturing plant was commissioned at Haridwar.

2008

• Aristocrat was merged with VIPIL.

2009

• Ms. Radhika Piramal was appointed the Executive Director of the Company in 2009.
• VIP emerged amongst Top 100 most trusted brands in the Brand Equity survey.

2011

• Crossed Rs. 2500 Crores of market capitalization.

2012

• Launched our Ladies Hand Bags brand - "Caprese".

2013

• Incorporated a wholly owned subsidiary by the name VIP Industries Bangladesh Pvt. Ltd. to set-up a luggage manufacturing plant in Bangladesh.

2014

• Hit Rs. 1000 Crores Target.
• Commercial operation of the 1st Bangladesh plant, "VIP Industries Bangladesh Pvt. Ltd." commenced.

2017

• Launched the new brand identity for "Aristocrat" with cricketers Rohit Sharma and R. Ashwin endorsing the brand.

2018

• Crossed Rs. 8900 Crores of market capitalization - all time highest
• Commercial operation of the 2nd & 3rd plants at Bangladesh "VIP Industries BD Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd." commenced.

2019

• VIP got it's new brand identity - power couple Saif Ali Khan and Kareena Kapoor Khan were signed as brand ambassadors.
• Commercial operation of the 4th, 5th & 6th plants at Bangladesh "VIP Luggage BD Pvt. Ltd." & "VIP Accessories BD Pvt. Ltd." commenced.
• VIP Industries Limited won the "Economic Times India's Best Companies to Work For 2019" as well as the "Economic Times India's Best Workplaces in Manufacturing Award 2019".

2020

• VIP Industries Limited got recognized as a "Bronze" employer in the IWEI"s (India Workplace Equality Index) Top Employers for 2020.

2021

• VIP Industries Limited got recognized as a 'Bronze' employer in the IWEI's (India Workplace Equality Index) Top Employers for 2021, won this in 2 consecutive years.
