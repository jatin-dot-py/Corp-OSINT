Welcome to Foundry Fuel Products Limited

Foundry Fuel Products Limited (FFPL) is engaged in the manufacturing of Low Ash Metallurgical Coke and is also engaged in trading of Coal and sand preparation for foundries. The company manufactures Low Ash Metallurgical Coke at its plant in Dhanbad district of Jharkhand with an installed capacity of 45,000 MTPA with a coal washery. It also trades in middlings and rejects, hard coke dust and slurry residue. The coke finds use in steel, foundries and chemical industries. Manufacturing facilities are located in the Dhanbad district of Jharkhand.
