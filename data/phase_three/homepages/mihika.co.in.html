What Is the Crazy Time Slot Casino Game?11

Crazy Time is a fun live casino game based on the classic money wheel game, Dream Catcher. It was first introduced by Todd Haushalter in 2020 at an event in London. The game comes with a special wheel with 54 sections. Each one shows a bonus game or a number (10, 5, 2, or 1).

There are 9 bonus sections in total: 2 for Pachinko,  4 for Coin Flip, 2 for Cash Hunt, and 1 for the Crazy Time bonus. So, on average, you get a chance to trigger a bonus about every 6 spins.

The rules of the Crazy Time live game are very simple. You just bet on a number or a bonus. If the wheel stops on your choice, you win. Sometimes, a Top Slot adds a multiplier to increase your prize. After each spin, the game resets, and you can bet again.

Crazy Time Bonus Rounds

Besides betting on multipliers, players can also unlock one of four exciting bonus games when engaging in the Crazy Time live show. Each has a chance of being triggered, and they are all easy to understand.

Coin Flip

The Coin Flip bonus begins when the wheel lands on one of the blue spots. If you place a bet on it, you will take part in the round while others will spectate. In this game, the dealer uses a coin with red and blue sides. Each side has a random multiplier, usually between x5 and x100. The host then puts the coin into the Flip-o-Matic machine to flip it and show the winning side.

If there is a Top Slot multiplier active, for example 50x, your win can go all the way up to x5,000. But sometimes, the multipliers aren’t that great. In those cases, the Rescue Flip can save the situation. It gives you one more flip, and you don’t need to place another wager for it. Since Coin Flip comes up pretty often, it might be worth including it in your strategy, but don’t over-rely on it. Also, it can massively boost your payout.

Cash Hunt

This bonus game begins when you put a wager on a green section and the wheel lands on it. If that happens, you enter a shooting-style bonus game with a screen full of 108 hidden multipliers. The symbols here are mixed up, and you will need to pick one to shoot using a cannon. You have a short time to choose; if you don’t make a choice, the game automatically picks one for you.

You can win up to a massive x10,000, depending on which multiplier you hit. If there is a Top Slot multiplier too, it can enhance your win even more. Frequent betting may increase your exposure to this round, but not your odds of winning. You can also check past results to spot any helpful trends.

Pachinko

The next bonus round is Pachinko. To join this round, just place your bet on one of the two Pachinko spots on the wheel. If it lands there, you will be taken to a fun, colourful game studio with an arcade vibe. Once you are in, the dealer drops a puck from one of 16 random spots at the top of a tall pegboard-style setup.

The puck bounces all over the place before landing in a prize slot, where each shows a different multiplier. The top payout here is an impressive x5,000. Some slots can even double or triple your prize. To increase your chances, try using the Top Slot bonus and wager on more segments for a better shot at big wins.
