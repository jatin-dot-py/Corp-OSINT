Accord Transformer and Switchgear Limited

Accord Transformer and Switchgear Ltd. is a leading manufacturer and exporter of high-quality power and distribution transformers, switchgear, and related electrical equipment. Established with a vision to deliver reliable and efficient power solutions, Accord has grown into a trusted name in the power sector, serving utilities, industries, infrastructure projects, and renewable energy sectors across India and overseas.
