Morarka Finance Limited (MFL) is a multi-faceted, high-energy financial company based in Mumbai, dealing in securities. An initiative by the promoters of Dwarikesh group, MFL has seen remarkable growth since it was incorporated way back in April 1985. The company, launched with an initial capital of a few lakhs has grown manifold and enjoys a strong net worth today.

MFL has carved for itself an enviable niche in the fiercely competitive financial sector in India. It is a vibrant organisation adorned with high-impact professionals with expertise in financial, market movements and latest technologies.
