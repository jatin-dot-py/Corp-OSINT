FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

All your financing questions, answered in one place.

Moneyboxx Finance is an RBI-registered NBFC that provides business loans to small and micro entrepreneurs, especially in Tier-2, Tier-3, and rural areas of India.

Moneyboxx Finance offers two primary loan products: Vyapaar Loans (Secured) — commercial loans backed by collateral, designed to provide timely capital infusion for business growth — and Vyapaar Loans (Unsecured) — collateral-free loans that empower rural and semi-urban entrepreneurs to expand their businesses. We also offer Renewable Energy Loans and Sikka App, which combines financial assistance with savings tools and financial literacy resources.

Any micro or small business owner, trader, shopkeeper, or entrepreneur operating in Tier-2, Tier-3, or rural areas of India can apply. We particularly focus on supporting underserved communities, including women entrepreneurs, who make up 60% of our borrowers. Applicants should have a running business and a valid KYC identity.

The basic documents required for a loan application include: a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, or Passport), proof of address, proof of business ownership or registration, recent bank statements, and a passport-size photograph. Additional documents may be required for secured loans. Our team will guide you through the process at your nearest branch or doorstep.
