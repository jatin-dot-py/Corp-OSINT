    Our Product Range

    Discover our extensive range of tools including drill bits, end mills, reamers, and custom tooling solutions. We prioritize quality and precision in every tool we manufacture, ensuring that our products meet the highest industry standards and exceed customer expectations.

    High Speed Steel (HSS) Tools

    Hittco’s range of High Speed Steel (HSS) Tools is engineered for versatility, durability, and reliable performance across a wide spectrum of machining operations. Known for their excellent toughness and resistance to heat, our HSS tools are ideal for high precision cutting, drilling, milling, and reaming applications.

    Built to perform. Designed to last.

    Carbide Tools

    Hittco’s Carbide Tools are designed for high precision machining where performance, strength, and accuracy are non negotiable. Made from the finest solid carbide materials, these tools offer exceptional hardness, wear resistance, and thermal stability which makes them ideal for demanding applications and tough to machine materials like stainless steel, titanium, and hardened alloys.

    Precision that powers performance. Strength that stands out.
