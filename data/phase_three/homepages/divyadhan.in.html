INFRASTRUCTURE

DRIL manufactures high grade Recycled Poylster Staple Fiber at manufacturing facility in Baddi, Himachal Pradesh. The plant recycles millions of PET bottle scrap to covert into superior Recycled fiber which are used across numerous applications.

01. Production capacity of 18,000MT of Recycled Fiber per annum. Opearting state of the art Chinese manufacturing machinery along
02. With inhouse PET flakes manufacturing to ensure highest raw material quality.
03. Complete in-house testing facilities and quality control systems for Flakes as well as Fiber
04. GRS , ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certified Fiber and Chips
