About Us

Mamata Machinery Limited is the only company in the world offering complete end-to-end solutions across the entire flexible packaging value chain—from Blown Film Extrusion Lines and Converting Machinery to Primary and Secondary Packaging Solutions. By seamlessly integrating these three critical stages of manufacturing, Mamata delivers high-performance, fully integrated solutions from a single, trusted source, enabling customers to optimize productivity, quality, and operational efficiency.

At the heart of Mamata's engineering excellence is its deep expertise in polymer science and rheology. This scientific foundation enables the design of high-performance machinery that maximizes process stability, material efficiency, energy optimization, and product quality. Leveraging this knowledge, Mamata develops innovative, reliable, and future-ready technologies that help manufacturers improve productivity, ensure consistent performance, reduce waste, and achieve their sustainability objectives.

For more than three decades, Mamata Machinery has been a global leader in the design and manufacture of advanced machinery for the flexible packaging industry. Our comprehensive portfolio includes Blown Film Extrusion Lines, Pouch Making Machines, Bag Making Systems, Horizontal and Vertical Form-Fill-Seal Machines (HFFS & VFFS), Multilane Packaging Machines, and fully integrated Secondary Packaging Automation Solutions. Serving customers in over 90 countries, Mamata partners with leading manufacturers worldwide to deliver innovative, high-performance solutions that enhance productivity, improve efficiency, and create lasting competitive advantage.
