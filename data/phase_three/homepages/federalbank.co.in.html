Notice to Customers Availing Floating Rate Loans

Federal Bank wishes to inform customers availing floating-rate loan facilities, that any change in the applicable benchmark rate (Repo Rate or other benchmark rate as applicable) may result in a revision of the interest rate applicable to the loan account.

In the event of a reduction in the applicable benchmark rate, the EMI will reduce, with the repayment tenure remaining unchanged.

In the event of an increase in the applicable benchmark rate, the loan tenure will be extended while retaining the existing EMI, subject to the terms and conditions governing the loan.

The revised loan repayment parameters, including EMI and tenure, will be communicated to customers whenever there is a change in the applicable Rate of Interest. Customers who wish to opt for a different EMI or repayment tenure may contact their nearest Federal Bank branch within 15 days from the date of such communication.

Additionally, customers have the following options (subject to applicable charges):

  1. To switch to a fixed interest rate for the  remaining portion of the loan.
  2. To prepay, either in part or in full, at any point during the residual tenor of the loan.

For any clarification or assistance, customers may visit their nearest Federal Bank branch.
