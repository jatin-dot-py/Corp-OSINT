Basant Agro Tech has over 130 years of experience in the agriculture technology industry.
We operate in 12 Indian states, including Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Telangana, Odisha, Bihar, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Maharashtra.
We have a manufacturing plant in 11 locations spanning across 6 cities and 3 states, which are strategically placed near raw materials sources and consumption points.
With our modern infrastructure facility, comprehensive product range, long standing industry experience of 130+ years makes us the leading player in agriculture technology.
Basant Agro Tech ensures product quality through strict adherence to BIS standards and ISO certification. Our products are manufactured in compliance with BIS regulations, guaranteeing they meet essential safety and performance benchmarks.
Empowering the agriculture sector with state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities and a wide range of high-quality products that foster growth and ensure a nurturing future.
