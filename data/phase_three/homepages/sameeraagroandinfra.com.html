About Sameera

The Company was incorporated on 8th March, 2002 as ‘Sameera Homes Private Limited’, under the Companies Act, 1956. The name of our Company ‘Sameera Homes Private Limited’ was changed to “Sameera Infra Projects Private Limited” w.e.f. 11th February, 2020. Further, name of the Company “Sameera Infra Projects Private Limited” was changed to “Sameera Agro and Infra Private Limited” 18th April, 2023. Subsequently, the Company was converted into Public Limited Company and the name of the Company was changed to “Sameera Agro and Infra Limited” w.e.f. 28th April, 2023. The CIN of the Company is U45201TG2002PLC038623.

The Company is a multi-faceted infrastructure development and construction of Residential, Commercial spaces, Apartments, Townships, Multi storied complexes, Gated communities, Landscapes, and other related works with the primary objective of delivering quality and value to our clients.
