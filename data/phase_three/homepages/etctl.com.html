ABOUT US

Emkay Taps and Cutting Tools Ltd. (ETCTL) formerly known as Emkay Taps and Cutting Tools Pvt. Ltd. (“ETCTPL”) was established in 1995. EMKAYTOOLS is our brand name as well as NSE symbol. We are engaged in manufacturing and sale of cutting tools and also power generation through its wind mills in Rajasthan and Karnataka. Our business(s) are in line with our Object clause as set forth in our Memorandum of Association.
Established over 25 years ago, EMKAYTOOLS has emerged as a leader in the thread cutting tools domain by offering superior value to its customers. We have gained our customers’ trust and supports through our total commitment to consistent technological advancement. Ensuring the right product for each customer specific production requirement and timely deliveries has given us a tremendous competitive advantage in this high-precision industry.
