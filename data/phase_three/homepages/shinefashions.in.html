About Us

Market Leader For Interlinings
A perfect fit and comfortable wear are crucial selling points for
apparel today. But high quality clothing has to serve many more
purposes. By means of non woven, woven and non textured
materials, interlinings.

Shine Fashions always provide the best possible support
for any garment.

Shine Fashions offers the most comprehensive range of interlining
solutions, such as waterjet interlinings for womens wear, non
texturised & waistband interlinings for menswear, stable and
washable interlinings for durable shirt applications, canvas & fused
interlinings for outer wear, water-soluble interlinings for decicate
embroidery and prefabricated products for tapes and chest pieces.

The products supplied by Shine Fashions are always subject to
Sustainability and the responsible use of natural resources are
essential aspects of all our activities. We always keep in mind the
Market Scenario and accordingly add new products at regular
intervals to meet the demands of the markets.

Our Journey

2012

Started as a Local Interlining Trading firm

2014

Introduced Import Item of mainly for products
Cotton fusible interlining & Waist band interlining

2016

Further expanding product portfolio of non-texturized interlining and non woven thermobond interlining. Manufacturing textile house for interlining business targeting to capture market share of 25%

2018

Complete import house for interlinings new products introduced – polyester fusible for suits, hair interlining, circular knitted fusible, hot melt adhesive interlining

2019

Shifting Trading business to manufacturing, finishing of fabrics of cotton fusible interlinings and waist band interlinings

2020

First phase of manufacturing unit to commence in Maharashtra for waist band interlinings.
