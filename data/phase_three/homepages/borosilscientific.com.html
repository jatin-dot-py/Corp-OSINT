Industries we serve
With leading-edge solutions tailored to meet the distinct and ever-evolving demands of every sector, we have earned the trust of numerous industry giants
Pharma Industry
With over six decades of expertise in manufacturing glassware, we have become the trusted partner of choice for leading pharmaceutical companies in India and beyond. At Borosil Scientific Limited, we are committed to elevating the standards of the pharmaceutical industry through our world-class solutions. Our precision engineered products meet industry demands, enhancing research, development, and production processes, ultimately driving innovation and improved healthcare outcomes.
Chemical Industry
With an enduring legacy in the field, we have earned the trust of leading chemical companies worldwide. Our precision-crafted glassware and lab equipment enhance safety and precision in processes. Our products meet rigorous industry standards, enabling precise measurements, controlled reactions, and reliable analysis. We take pride in empowering the chemical sector to achieve new heights of efficiency, quality, and innovation.
Research
We have been a steadfast supporter of the research community for over half a century. Our products have become essential tools for scientists and researchers across various disciplines, enabling them to push the boundaries of human knowledge. With precision-engineered glassware and cutting-edge lab equipment, we empower researchers to conduct experiments with accuracy and reliability. Whether in chemistry, biology, physics, or beyond, our solutions play a pivotal role in driving breakthroughs and advancing scientific understanding. We are dedicated to providing the tools that fuel innovation and discovery.
Agriculture
In the realm of agriculture, Borosil Scientific plays a pivotal role in enhancing productivity and sustainability. Leveraging our decades-long proficiency in glassware and lab equipment, we provide indispensable tools to agricultural researchers, institutions, and businesses. Our products cater to the unique demands of the agriculture sector, aiding in soil analysis, plant pathology, and the development of cutting-edge agrochemicals.
Food Feed & Beverages
Borosil Scientific is a trusted name in quality assurance for feed, food, and beverages. With a longstanding industry presence, we're the preferred partner for companies dedicated to delivering safe, nutritious products worldwide. Our advanced lab equipment and glassware ensure precise testing, analysis, and quality control in production. Partnering with Borosil ensures regulatory compliance, minimizes contamination risks, and guarantees consistent product excellence. Together, we foster trust and satisfaction in the global food supply chain.
Environment
In the field of environmental sciences, Borosil Scientific is a trusted ally in the quest for a sustainable future. With a history deeply rooted in this critical domain, we offer specialized glassware and lab equipment tailored to the unique demands of environmental research and monitoring. Our tools enable precise analysis of air, water, and soil, aiding in pollution control, resource conservation, and climate mitigation. We empower scientists and agencies, working together for a cleaner, healthier planet.
