About Us

Alok Industries Limited is one of India’s largest vertically integrated textile companies offering end-to-end solutions through its five core divisions: Home Textiles, Cotton Yarn, Apparel Fabric, Garments, and Polyester Yarn.

For nearly forty years, Alok has been at the forefront of the textile industry, providing high quality textile products with reliable delivery schedules and internationally competitive qualities.
