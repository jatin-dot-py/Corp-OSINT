Zodiac-JRD-MKD Limited was incorporated as a Private Limited Company on Janaury 6, 1987 and got converted into a public limited company on October 22, 1992. The company was part of the Jhaveri family [JRD group]. In December 2023, Mudit Jain and Sanghavi family of the Bluerock Group acquired Zodiac JRD-MKJ Limited through SEBI’s SAST Guidelines and Open Offer process. Zodiac JRD-MKJ Limited is engaged in the business of cut & polished Diamonds, Jewellery, Precious stones and Semi-precious stones.
