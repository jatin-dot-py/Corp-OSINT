Learn About Advait

Advait Energy Transitions Limited is Fastest Growing Into New Energy Tech Solutions And Power Transmission Solution Business And Expanding Across Globe.

Advait Energy Transitions Limited is an Ahmedabad-based company that offers robust products and end-to-end solutions for power transmission, substation, and telecommunication infrastructure. The company diversified into renewable energy in 2023.

Founded in 2009, Advait Energy Transitions Limited specializes in stringing tools, ACS wire manufacturing, OPGW operations, ERS, turnkey telecom projects, live line installations, and green energy. The company also provides Alkaline and PEM electrolyser systems, fuel cell systems, hydrogen refuelling stations (HRS), hydrogen blending systems, and hydrogen storage units.

Additionally, the company offers insulators, conductors, transmission line towers, stringing tools, power transmission, and substation services.

FAQs for Power Transmission Solutions

We’re vertically integrated across manufacturing, supply, and EPC. We manufacture OPGW cable, ACS wire, emergency restoration systems, and stringing tools at our Kadi facility. We source and supply insulators through global partnerships. We deliver EPC services for live line OPGW installation and complete transmission projects. Full-service capability.

Power grid, state transmission corporations, major EPC contractors like Tata Projects and KEC International, independent power transmission developers. Plus customers in 45 countries globally. We’ve delivered 400+ projects and been operating for 15 years. That’s real track record.

Manufacturing means we own the production facility – OPGW, ACS wire, ERS, stringing tools all made in-house. Supply means we procure from trusted global partners and distribute of insulators and optical cables. EPC means we assume full project delivery responsibility of planning, execution, commissioning. Each service line has different value propositions depending on what a customer needs.

Yeah, to over 45 countries. Our Kadi facility produces to IEC, ASTM, ANSI, and GOST standards so we can meet diverse international procurement specs. That export capability comes from our testing infrastructure and consistent quality record. We don’t just manufacture domestically, we’re a global supplier.
