About AFSL

Full Service Capital Market Intermediary in India

Aryaman Financial Services Limited is a SEBI registered Category I Merchant Banker involved in the business of Lead Management and Syndication of Small and Medium Sized (Rs. 10 cr. to Rs. 500 cr.) IPO's, FPO's, Rights Issues, Composite Issues, QIP's, PIPE Deals, VC Funding and other forms of Fund Raising. Further we also offer Stock Trading ,Commodity trading, Portfolio management services, Depository Participant Services, NBFC Lending services
