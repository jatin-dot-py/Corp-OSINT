About Us

At JSL, we are dedicated to building infrastructure that enables the transmission of extra high-voltage electric power across India and global markets. As a leading power infrastructure company in India, we deliver end-to-end solutions across power transmission and substations, covering the full project lifecycle from design and engineering to manufacturing, testing, erection, and commissioning.

                    

Our expertise in power transmission EPC spans complex and large-scale transmission line projects across diverse and challenging terrains. With strong in-house engineering capabilities, advanced tower testing facilities, and proven execution experience, we support resilient power networks that ensure reliable electricity reaches millions of homes, industries, and communities.


From concept to commissioning, we work closely with our customers to deliver efficient, safe, and execution-ready infrastructure. As an infrastructure EPC company with deep sector experience, we align every project with evolving grid requirements and long-term infrastructure demands.

About Jyoti Structures Ltd.

Jyoti Structures Limited is a leading engineering, procurement, construction (EPC) company in India's power transmission sector, with deep-rooted expertise in executing complex high-voltage and extra-high-voltage (EHV) transmission projects. Over the decades, the company has played a key role in building critical transmission infrastructure that supports grid expansion, inter-regional connectivity, and reliable power delivery.


What differentiates Jyoti Structures is its integrated execution model, where engineering, procurement, manufacturing, and on-ground construction operate in close coordination. From detailed route surveys and design to tower testing, manufacturing and final commissioning, each stage is aligned to ensure consistency, technical accuracy, and timely delivery, especially across large and technically demanding transmission corridors.


The company’s in-house capabilities, including tower testing, fabrication, and galvanisation, enable early technical validation and tighter control over quality and performance. This becomes particularly critical in EHV transmission projects, where precision in design and execution directly impacts long-term reliability.


Having executed projects across diverse terrains, long transmission lines, and challenging right-of-way conditions, Jyoti Structures brings strong planning capability and disciplined execution to every project. Its teams are equipped to adapt construction methodologies to site-specific constraints while maintaining uniform standards of safety and quality. The company remains focused on supporting India’s growing power needs by building infrastructure that performs consistently on the ground.

FAQ

What does Jyoti Structures Ltd do?

Jyoti Structures Ltd (JSL) is an Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) company with over four decades of experience in power transmission infrastructure. The company designs, manufactures, tests, erects, and commissions transmission line towers, substations, and distribution network assets for utilities and power developers across India and internationally. Its work spans the full project lifecycle, from route survey and foundation engineering through tower fabrication and galvanising to conductor stringing and final commissioning.


JSL operates manufacturing plants in Nashik and Raipur, a NABL-accredited tower testing station at Ghoti near Nashik, and design and engineering offices in Mumbai, Nashik, and Ahmedabad (Mumbai being the head office).To date, the company has built 33,480 km of transmission line, manufactured 1,500,000 MT of transmission towers, constructed more than 1,800 high-voltage substation bays, and electrified over 37,500 villages across India and beyond.

What services does JSL offer?

JSL's services cover six core areas across the power transmission and distribution spectrum:

• Design and Engineering — structural analysis and design of transmission towers, 2D and 3D detailing, foundation design, profiling, tower spotting, and stringing chart generation, supported by engineering offices in Mumbai, Nashik, and Ahmedabad

• Tower Testing — structural performance testing up to 1,200 kV at the NABL-accredited Ghoti testing station, with more than 515 towers and monopoles tested to date

• Manufacturing — EHV transmission line towers, telecom towers, and substation structures, produced at facilities in Nashik and Raipur with a combined annual capacity of more than 100,000 MT

• Construction and Commissioning — end-to-end site execution covering transmission lines from 66 kV to 800 kV, including Right of Way, foundations, tower erection, conductor stringing, and commissioning

• Substation — turnkey substation delivery from 11 kV to 765 kV, covering design, procurement, construction, and commissioning across more than 1,800 high-voltage bays built

• Rural Electrification and Distribution — overhead and underground distribution network work, covering network survey, substation construction and augmentation, line construction, and cable systems

What kinds of projects does JSL typically handle?

JSL's project portfolio concentrates on transmission assignments where engineering depth and execution experience are the determining factors in outcomes. On the voltage spectrum, this covers HVAC lines from 66 kV to 765 kV and HVDC systems up to 800 kV. Geographically, JSL has delivered projects across desert terrain, creek and waterlogged zones, Himalayan elevations, densely forested regions, and seismically active areas. Scale ranges from localised distribution network work to large corridor packages spanning hundreds of kilometres.

The company has erected its tallest tower at 106 metres and its heaviest at 150 MT and has executed a 1,000-metre river crossing over the Ganges using 142-metre towers. On the substation side, JSL handles both standalone substation projects and combined transmission-plus-substation turnkey packages for government-owned and private sector clients. The rural electrification portfolio adds distribution network delivery for both overhead and underground systems across geographically dispersed and logistically demanding locations.
