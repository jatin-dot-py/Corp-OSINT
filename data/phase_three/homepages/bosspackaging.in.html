ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Boss Packaging Solutions Limited

Automatic Labeling Machine Manufacturers

Backed by industry experience spanning about a decade, we are engaged in manufacturing a completely automatic range of machines used in the filling, capping, and labeling of bottles and containers. Our range of packaging machines includes industrial packaging machines like rotary washing machines, a variety of filling machines, a variety of capping machines, labeling machines, all types of conveyors, varying sizes of turn tables, and a shrink sleeve application machine with our steam tunnel and steam generator. We are also offering a semi-automatic shrink wrapping machine and an electric shrink tunnel. Instead of this, we are also doing of a cream filling line, and customized Atomization. These machines are manufactured using high-quality raw materials, including stainless steel and precision-designed parts and components. Our machines are extensively used by clients owing to features like high packaging rates, low power consumption, sturdy construction, low maintenance requirements, and others.
