Our Solutions

We provide a comprehensive range of solutions by combining product and aftermarket services across the entire lifecycle of the Steam Turbine Generator (STG). Through a committed staff of highly qualified and experienced industry experts, we deliver engineered-to-order products. Our network of global representative offices manned by experienced and qualified engineers allow us to meet the aftermarket requirements of customers for our own brand and other brands.

Products

We design, manufacture and deliver advanced technology industrial steam turbine generators of power output up to 100 MWe that work across a wide range of pressure and flow applications with a choice of impulse and reaction series. With our global expertise, we offer American Petroleum Institute (API) 611 and 612 compliant steam turbines and are also prequalified with globally reputed national oil companies, Original Equipment vendors (OEM) and Engineering, Procurement & Construction contractors (EPC) or Project Management Consultants (PMC) companies.

Parts Sales & Services

We have a highly trained and motivated customer-centric field service team with hands-on experience in handling the complete Steam Turbine Generators (STG) and Balance Of Plant (BOP) across all turbine variants. Our experts in Parts Sales and Services are available 24X7 to support any on-site emergencies. We offer upgradation and automation for the complete range of turbines throughout the lifecycle. Our comprehensive turbine maintenance solutions ensure the smooth functioning of the plant for a longer time period. The strength of our customer service proposition is manifested in our customized service solutions, high repeat orders, customer retention, new customer additions and total customer satisfaction.

Triveni REFURB
(multi-brand refurbishing service)

Triveni REFURB is our multi-brand repair arm offering various refurbishment solutions including Efficiency Enhancement services for existing turbines. With rising costs, changes in process requirements and age, operating turbines efficiently is a necessity for cost-saving and creating a positive carbon footprint. We upgrade the turbine steam flow path to meet our customers’ challenging requirements to help their turbines perform efficiently.
