Frequently Asked Questions

While the Main Gate opens at 10:30 AM, the rides actually begin operation at 10:45 AM. The ticket counters close at 6:45 PM, and the rides stop at 7:30 PM. For the Food Court, service continues until 8:00 PM.

Yes, Nicco Park remains open 364 days a year, including Sundays and public holidays, with the single exception of Holi (Dol Yatra), when the park is closed.

The nearest Metro station is Salt Lake Sector V (Green Line), which is the terminal station. From there, you can take an auto or walk (approx. 20 mins). Alternatively, Karunamoyee Metro Station is about 1.95 km away.
