Company Brief

Laxmipati Engineering Works Limited is engaged in the Business of Ship Building, Ship Repair, Fabrication, Heavy Engineering, Engineering Infrastructure and services and Precision Machining. The company was incorporated in 2012 as a Ship Building company and has in short span of time building itself as a reliable partner Manufacturing, Engineering Services, Repair and Upgrade. The company is a part of the Diversified Laxmipati Group which is engaged in Textiles, Real Estate, Power Sector, & Education.

Laxmipati Engineering & Works Limited has obtained the Industrial License from the Government of India for Manufacturing of War Ships.

Our Certifications:

1. ISO 9001:2015

2. ISO 45001:2018

3. ASME and National Board certifications for manufacturing of Pressure Vessels

4. Indian Boiler Regulation for IBR piping and repair and erection of  Class 2 Boilers. 

Palsana Workshop 

Our first state of the art engineering workshop has been set up strategically at Palsana on NH 48. It covers more than 30000 sqmtrs of space. Currently the workshop employs more than 400 people in various activities. When completely operational it can accommodate close to 1500 workmen.

The workshop is ideally suited for the following manufacturing projects depending on a client’s need:

  • Boilers and Heat Exchangers
  • Pressure Vessels
  • Modular Manufacturing
  • Pier Caps and Shutters for Infra Projects
  • Heavy Engineering and Fabrication
  • Defence Related Project
  • Maintenance, Repair and Upgrade
  • IBR Piping
  • Overlay Welding
  • Machining
