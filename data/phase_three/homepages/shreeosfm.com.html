Commitment to Quality

Quality is the guiding principle behind our actions, propelling us toward success.

Stringent Quality Processes: We have established rigorous quality protocols for both vehicles and drivers at OSFM.

Thorough Vehicle Inspection: Each vehicle joining our fleet undergoes a comprehensive evaluation by our expert Technical Team.

Selective Vehicle Criteria: Vehicles are chosen based on strict criteria, including compliance, proper documentation, functional GPS, and safety features aligned with customer needs.

Modern Fleet: Our fleet consists of vehicles no older than three years and with less than 40,000 kilometers driven.

Driver Screening: Ensuring customer safety, drivers undergo mandatory background checks through an independent agency, reinforced by police verification.

Driver Skill Enhancement: Drivers receive regular briefings on defensive driving and undergo annual classroom training sessions.

Ongoing Fleet Monitoring: Our command centers provide continuous oversight, monitoring fleet speed, punctuality, and service quality.

Value-Added Services: We go beyond by offering value-added services, ensuring optimal performance and customer satisfaction.
