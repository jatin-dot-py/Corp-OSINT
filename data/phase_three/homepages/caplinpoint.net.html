Disclaimer notice on Cyber Investment Scams

It has come to the Company’s notice that certain unscrupulous individuals, groups and/or entities are circulating fabricated letters containing the Company’s name, logo, official letterhead, and digital signature of authorized signatories claiming that shares of the Company are available at a price lower than the prevailing market rates, in an attempt to lure and mislead public and investors into participating in cyber investment scams through WhatsApp, Telegram etc.

The Company hereby notifies that it is illegal to offer such shares at discounted price and the Company never authorises any individuals, groups and/or entities to bid for the Company’s shares at a lower price than the prevailing market price and to offer it for investment of public/ investors.

The public and investors are cautioned against participating in such scams that has nothing to do with the Company. If any public or investor encounters such fake letters or communications promising lower purchase rates than the prevailing market rate of the Company’s shares and promising higher returns therefrom, we request you to disregard it. All official communications of the Company which are relevant to the public/ investors are available at www.caplinpoint.net which the public or investor may refer to in case of any doubts.

The Company reserves the right to pursue appropriate legal action against any individual or entity responsible for such fraudulent actions. The Company accepts no liability for any claims, losses, or damages arising from reliance on such fraudulent and unauthorised representations.
