STRONGBOND ONE SHOT is a pre-catalysed Amino Plastic Resin with much Superior Performance over traditional PVAC resins. It is tailored to meet specific customer needs, such as High Moisture Resistance, Warm Water Resistance, and Low Formaldehyde Emission.

Carb P2 Compliance

CARB P2 Compliance as per ASTM indicates that a product’s formaldehyde emissions are within the allowable limits specified by CARB, which are among the strictest in the world. Calibrated and non-calibrated plywood manufactured with a couple of resin formulations of ARCL meet CARB P2 Compliance and thus, contribute to healthy air quality. They adhere to stringent health and environmental safety standards vis-à-vis formaldehyde emissions, enhancing its appeal for international markets and supporting export initiatives

Know More

Formaldehyde 43%

ARCL is one of the few players having the knowhow of producing 43% Formalin. Using Formaldehyde 43% in liquid resin gives a lot of advantages compared to using the usual 37% formalin.

  •  More solid content in resin, resulting in 16% stronger material
  •  12% more productivity

  •  Better Polymerization

  •  Enhanced Bonding

Milestones

Evolving Through Years

1959

ARCL was born, beginning with a technical collaboration with Borden Chemicals (Now Hexion) UK/USA, for the production of spray-dried UF Resin powder and the entire range of thermosetting resins. The collaboration lasted for 20 years. UF resin powder was made for the first time in Asia.

1960

We got a Formaldehyde Plant from Japan Gas (now Mitsubishi Gas). It was the first such plant in India.

1970

Formaldehyde plant from LEUNA Germany.

1983

The present group Chairman - Mr. S.R. Mundhra took over the Management and revived the sick company.

1993

Metal oxide-based formaldehyde plant, Paraformaldehyde plant and UFC-85 from Alder SPA Italy.

1994

Allied Resins went public with the premium issue and listing at Bombay Stock Exchange, National Stock Exchange and Calcutta Stock Exchange.

1995

PF spray-dried powder technology from Bokem INC, Montreal, Canada through Mr K K Sudan including relocation of ANHYDRO make spray drier.

2003

Bought the resin business of Ciba-Atul (A collaboration between Atul Ltd. and Ciba Geigy from Switzerland) and relocated the Niro Spray drier from Gujarat to West Bengal.

2005

ARCL supplied its advanced Formaldehyde and Para Formaldehyde technology to China as a Joint Venture. Overwhelmed with the success of the technology, they expanded 4 times. With the Chinese Company going public, ARCL encashed their equity.

2006

Started manufacturing pellet feed binder under the guidance of a leading American buyer. Today, our company has a leading market share in this field.

2012

Merger of Allied Resins and Chemicals Ltd. with our group company - Aarem Chemicals Pvt. Ltd. and the name changed to ARCL Organics Ltd

2015

Ventured into the manufacture of Speciality resins and glue for the Cooling Pad industry.

2016

With the success of Aqua StrongBond, we started a full-fledged Feed Additives division with prominent R&D doctorates in the industry thus increasing the range for catering to aqua, cattle and poultry industries.

2017

Introduced Speciality resins and glue for Corrugated Paper Packaging and Starch industries.

2019

ARCL’s strong focus on Research and Development was recognized by the Department of Science and Industrial Research (DSIR), Govt. of India.

2020

Ventured into Agrochemicals for potato disease and slow-release nitrogen with guidance from an American speciality chemicals company.

2021

Commissioned state-of-the-art Novolac Phenolic Resin Plant with powder from Air-classified mills (ACM) and pastills from steel belt postulator.

2022

Innovated manufacturing of HMMM and other speciality resins for the Tyre and Rubber industry.
