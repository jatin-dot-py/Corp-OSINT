Paluck Technologies Ltd, established in 2010, is a fast-growing diversified service enterprise headquartered on the Delhi-Jaipur Highway in the NCR region. The company began its journey in power generation services and has since expanded across multiple critical sectors including Automobile & Engineering Services, Logistics & Equipment Rental, Telecom Engineering, and Renewable Energy.

In the telecom engineering domain, Paluck Technologies Ltd has built a strong nationwide presence by managing and servicing more than 30,000 telecom sites across India. The company delivers comprehensive solutions including site deployment, operations & maintenance, energy management, and network optimization, ensuring seamless connectivity for leading telecom operators.

With over 300 employees, 20,000+ customers, and a fleet of 200+ construction equipment, Paluck is strategically positioned to serve India’s core infrastructure, energy, automotive, and telecom industries. The company's focus is to deliver best-in-class solutions through strong OEM partnerships, geographic advantage, and a multi-segment operational model.
