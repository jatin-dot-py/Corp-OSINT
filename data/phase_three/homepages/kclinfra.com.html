Building India’s Infrastructure Future

Established in 1995, KCL Infra Projects Limited is a BSE-listed infrastructure company focused on engineering, construction, and development solutions.

KCL brings together engineering expertise, innovative solutions, and execution capabilities across civil engineering, infrastructure development, mechanized multi-level car parking systems, and diverse construction projects. Our focus remains on delivering quality-driven solutions that create long-term value.

Driven by experience, innovation, and a commitment to quality, we continue to contribute towards India’s evolving infrastructure landscape.
