About Us

Veerhealthcare

Veerhealth Care Ltd. was originally incorporated on 10th July 1992 as Niyati Leasing Limited with a purpose to start business in Investment & Finance. In 2013, the management of the company decided to diversify the business activities and changed its main object to pharma sector and new name of the company is Veerhealth Care Ltd. In 2020, the company started operations in cosmetic manufacturing.
