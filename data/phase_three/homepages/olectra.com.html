    Pioneers of electric buses and Electric Tippers in India

    Olectra introduced India’s first electric bus and developed India’s first electric tipper, advancing electric mobility across public transport and heavy-duty operations.

    Proven leadership in electric mobility

    India’s largest pure electric commercial vehicle manufacturer, with large scale deployments across public transport networks.

    Integrated manufacturing capabilities with in-house prototyping, testing and production support for electric buses and commercial EVs.
