About Us

Ambar Protein Industries Ltd. was founded in 1992 in Ahmedabad with a vision to deliver high-quality edible oils to customers. Recognising the need for trustworthy and nutritious cooking oils, the company launched its flagship brand, Ankur, which quickly became a household name across the state. From its inception, Ambar Protein Industries has been committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality, ensuring that every drop of oil under the Ankur brand meets the expectations of health-conscious consumers. Over the years, the company has grown and evolved, but its dedication to providing pure, wholesome, and reliable edible oil products to its customers has remained steadfast.

Today, Ambar Protein Industries Ltd. stands as a testament to dedication and excellence, continuing to provide pure, wholesome, and reliable edible oil products to its loyal customers. The company’s steadfast dedication to quality and consumer satisfaction ensures that it remains a trusted name in households, contributing to healthier and happier lives across the region.
