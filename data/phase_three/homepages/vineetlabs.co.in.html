Welcome to Vineet Laboratories

Vineet Laboratories Limited is started functioning in the year 2007 as a fully fledged manufacturing company of Intermediates useful for the manufacture of active pharmaceutical ingredients, based at Malkapur, Yadadri Bhuvanagiri District of Telangana State, India.
(Formerly known as Ortin Laboratories Limited, Unit-II).

Facility

Vineet Laboratories is a multipurpose facility well equipped with stainless steel and glass-lined reactors with total volume of 230KL, of different volumes to suit any batch size. Special provisions are made to carry out reactions at very low temperature (up to -100°C).
