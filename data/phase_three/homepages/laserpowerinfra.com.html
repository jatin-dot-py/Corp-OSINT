Legacy spanning over 35 years

LPIL manufactures and deploys Medium Voltage (MV) and Low Voltage (LV) Power Cables, Aerial Bunched Cables, Conductors, Control Cables, Instrumentation Cables, Aluminium Rods, Aluminium Alloy Rods and Armour Rods, serving both retail and institutional segments under the brand name “LASER”.

To strengthen the Supply Chain, LASER believes in expanding to new verticals through backward integration for Wires, Cables, EPC and Power Infrastructure Projects in rural and urban India.

Boasting of a strong foothold in pan-India EPC projects, our clientele includes reputed Central & State Government Power Distribution houses.

Driven by a strong ambition to be the universal brand of choice for consumers across the spectrum of our business segments and beyond boundaries, we have been continuously establishing new touchpoints to connect with them. Our journey shows a continued and committed effort toward building a brand named “LASER” that lives its customers’ ethos and delivers to their aspirations.

Powering a Smarter Grid:
Laser Power & Infra Partners with TS Conductor

Laser Power & Infra has partnered with TS Conductor, a US-based advanced conductor manufacturer, to introduce its cutting-edge Aluminum Encapsulated Carbon Core (AECC) technology to India. As a licensed stranding partner, we manufacture these high-performance conductors locally to meet India’s power sector needs. TS Conductor’s AECC technology significantly increases grid capacity, reduces line losses and integrates seamlessly with existing infrastructure.
