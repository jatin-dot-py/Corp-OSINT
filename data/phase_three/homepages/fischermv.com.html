Our Business

Fischer Medical Ventures Limited leads a paradigm shift in the healthcare sector by pioneering cutting-edge, cost-effective medical imaging technologies and state-of-the-art diagnostics innovations on a global scale.   

Headquartered in Chennai, India, with a manufacturing facility in the Andhra Pradesh MedTech Zone (AMTZ) in Visakhapatnam, FMV is the first Indian company to indigenously manufacture advanced magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) systems in India, marking a significant milestone in the development of advanced medical imaging solutions within the nation. 

Aside from medical devices, FMV holds a diverse portfolio of diagnostic solutions tailored to a myriad of point-of-care diagnostic solutions, empowering healthcare providers with the tools necessary for timely and precise diagnoses.  Leveraging on our expertise, resources, and strategic partnerships, we are relentless in driving progress, accessibility, and affordability in healthcare delivery, to bring significant impact on global healthcare.

Tan Sri Dato’ Dr. Syed Jalaludin Syed Salim
Academician Emeritus Professor Tan Sri Dato’ Dr. Syed Jalaludin Syed Salim is a highly respected Malaysian educationist, scientist and corporate leader with more than five decades of service in higher education, science policy and industry leadership. He holds a PhD from the University of London and has been awarded seven honorary doctorates from international and Malaysian universities in recognition of his outstanding contribution to national development, scientific advancement and institutional excellence. He began his academic career at the University of Malaya before joining Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM), eventually serving as its Vice Chancellor until 2001. Under his leadership, UPM strengthened its position in veterinary sciences, agriculture, biotechnology and life sciences — areas that continue to underpin Malaysia’s healthcare and food security ecosystem. He is also a founding Senior Fellow of the Academy of Sciences Malaysia and a recipient of both the National Science Laureate and National Academic Laureate awards. Tan Sri Syed Jalaludin remains a key figure in shaping Malaysia’s innovation landscape, currently serving as Chancellor of Taylor’s University and Chairman of several university boards. Beyond academia, he has deep corporate governance experience in healthcare, biotechnology, financial services, capital markets and the halal economy. He serves as Independent Non-Executive Chairman of SL Innovation Capital Berhad and PT Resources Holdings Berhad.

Mr. David Yeow
Mr. David Yeow is an accomplished engineer, business leader and technology commercialisation professional with over three decades of experience spanning manufacturing, quality systems, supply chain management, technology transfer, innovation and deep-technology commercialisation. He holds a Diplôme d'Ingénieur (M.Eng equivalent) in Electronic Engineering from the École Nationale Supérieure d'Électronique et de Radioélectricité de Bordeaux, France, and is a GE-certified Six Sigma Blackbelt. Throughout his career, Mr. Yeow has held senior leadership positions across manufacturing, specialty chemicals, technology and supply chain businesses. He has established and commissioned greenfield manufacturing facilities, led major plant relocation and scale-up projects, and managed regional operations across Asia Pacific. As Director, Asia Pacific Logistics and Supply Chain at Lanxess Pte Ltd, he was responsible for supply chain operations supporting 15 business units across multiple territories, with annual expenditure of approximately US$24 million, delivering significant cost savings and inventory optimisation. Earlier, at GE Plastics, he led sourcing and digitisation initiatives across Asia Pacific and delivered substantial procurement and technology-enabled savings. A significant part of his career has focused on technology transfer, innovation and commercialisation. During his tenure as Director at NTUitive Pte Ltd, the innovation and enterprise arm of Nanyang Technological University, he was responsible for technology assessment, commercialisation, licensing and spin-off formation across diverse engineering and technology disciplines. He also founded and led an engineering accelerator operating a stage-gate model to bridge the gap between laboratory research and manufacturable products, assessing technologies from Technology Readiness Levels 4 to 7. His work included securing S$84 million in grant funding through National Research Foundation innovation cluster proposals and expanding international entrepreneurship programmes through partnerships across major global innovation hubs. Mr. David currently serves as Principal at Hunniwell Lake Ventures, a Silicon Valley-based medical device venture capital firm, where he is responsible for Asia Pacific (excluding China) coverage, including sourcing and technical and commercial assessment of medical device ventures. He is also a Partner at EightMercatus Pte Ltd, advising corporates and founders on technology commercialisation, venture building and go-to-market strategy. He has also served on the boards of companies operating in consulting, education technology, thermal management, water technology, filtration, nanotechnology and plastic recycling, giving him broad exposure to technology-driven businesses and emerging ventures. Mr. David brings to the Board a strong combination of engineering expertise, manufacturing and scale-up experience, operational discipline, supply chain and cost management capabilities, and deep exposure to medical-device technology and commercialisation. His experience complements existing clinical and imaging expertise and provides a valuable perspective on new product development, manufacturing readiness, operational efficiency and technology-led growth.
