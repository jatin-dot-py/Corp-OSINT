About Us

Stallion India Fluorochemicals Ltd. endeavors to be Indian customers first choice in purchase of products supplied by it based on the track record of delivering high quality products on time every time at competitive price.

  • 1992 : Stallion started operations in 1992. (Acquired by the company on a Slump Sale basis pursuant to slump sale agreement in the year 2023)
  • 1998 : Stallion established its first stand alone HFC debulking /bottling facility in India in 1998 at its Panvel facility outside Mumbai.
  • 2002 :Stallion started operations at its North India de-bulking/bottling facility at Manesar –Gurgaon in 2002 to support just in time supplies for its customers in the NCR region.
