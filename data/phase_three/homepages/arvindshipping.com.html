About us

Service Provider to Port Construction Companies.

Arvind Port And Infra Limited, group is headed by Mr. Arvind K. Shah who has the 50 years experienced in the field of the Shipping. We have a fleet of marine vessels like Cargo Barges, Flat top Barges, Crane Mounted Barges, Hopper Barges, Spud Barges & Tugs for Cargo movement, Port construction and Maintenance. We also have ancillary equipment’s like Cranes, Back hoes, Loaders, Fleet of Dumpers/Trailers, Bagging Machine, Cargo Conveyors, all types of Grabs (Cargo grabs, stone grapples), Tagline Winders, Rock Breakers, Drum cutter, etc.
