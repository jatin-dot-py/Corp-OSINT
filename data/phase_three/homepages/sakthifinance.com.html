OUR STORY

Since 1955, our story is one of tradition, legacy, and resilience. Sakthi Finance was established in the year 1955, headquartered in Pollachi, as the Pollachi Credit Society private Limited. Sakthi Finance was started with the intent of catering to the hire purchase requirements of the TELCO dealerships of the Sakthi Group. Over 6 decades, the business has transitioned and grown into one of the leading NBFC’s in South India specializing in financing the purchase of commercial vehicles and construction equipments.

The company has a presence of more than 50 branches in the Southern states of Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Telangana.
Since the company caters to a niche market of customers looking for refinancing options for their commercial vehicles and construction equipment, the aim has always been to be a partner in every customer’s journey towards prosperity by providing services with speed, flexibility and integrity.
