Transit Time Finder

The Transit Time Finder provides you with the delivery date for a selected service.

You are required to select the Blue Dart service required by you, the Origin and Destination City and the pick up date and time below.

To confirm if the service required by you is available, for the origin and destination selected, please check with our Location Finder. For detailed information on our services, refer to our Service Guide.

NOTE 1 : The scheduled delivery date given is subject to the Terms And Conditions Of Carriage, details of which are available in our Service Guide.

NOTE 2 : Some locations in or around the city could be out of delivery area and may require extra time for delivery - Contact Customer Service.

NOTE 3 : Transit Time arrived at are on best estimate basis and it is advisable to Contact Customer Service for your definite shipping needs.

NOTE 4 : Non documents shipments are subject to stricter customs, excise, and/or regulatory checks which may cause delays - please refer further to disclaimer section.


Price Finder

The Price Finder helps you to calculate your shipping charges for your service type needs.

Charges will be based on the weight of the shipment in kgs or fractions thereof and may be assessible either on the gross weight of the shipment or the volume/dimensional weight of the shipment - whichever is higher.

The chargeable weight may be expressed as fractions of a kg. such as 0.25 kgs or 0.5 kgs and may go upto 9999 kgs.
