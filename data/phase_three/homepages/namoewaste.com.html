Who We Are

Namo eWaste Management Ltd. is one of the leading companies of India that recycle electrical and electronic (EEE) waste dedicated to e-waste management. 

Successfully operating since 2014, Namo eWaste Management offers comprehensive, complete, and responsible recycling services. Our integrated and best-in-class technology ensures environmental safety and sustainability while recycling eWaste. As an electronic recycling company, we are dedicated to minimizing environmental impact and promoting a greener future.

With our experience in managing large volumes of high-quality EEE waste, we are able to extract all of the components of an electrical item including metals like mercury, lead, cadmium, PCBs, etc., without leaving a trace behind. We are e waste recyclers India, ISO and r2 certified, complying with strict environmental regulations regarding handling hazardous products and disposing of them safely to keep our environment green.

Our Services

IT and Electronics Assets Disposition (ITAD)

IT Asset Disposition (ITAD) is the practice of securely and responsibly recycling electronic goods that are no longer needed or effective.

Namo is one of the leading ITAD providers in India and provide cloud-based solutions. We have the ability to process both data-bearing and non-data-bearing assets for your business. You'll have 100% data destruction and ensured environmental compliance, at the same time maximising asset recovery value with ethical electronic recycling.

Electrical & Electronic Recycling

EEE recycling involves collecting, sorting, and recycling electronic products in order to reuse any salvageable materials in the electronics reducing the impact of eWaste.

Namo has four certified facilities in India, with an annual capacity to shred up to a 1 lakh+ metric tons of electronics per year. This makes Namo one of the the biggest capacity eWaste processor in the industry. Through our specialized process we ensure responsible recycling of EEE waste with authorized recyclers.

Data Destruction

Concerns regarding the security of confidential information on devices are a top concern when it comes to e-waste management. In order to protect your data from being compromised, we provide 2X data destruction services at your doorstep with corporate e-waste solutions, including Data Degaussing, Hard Disk Shredding, and Software Data Destruction with sustainable IT solutions.

Reverse logistics

To assist producers in complying with EPR guidelines, we offer a customized reverse logistics service providing proper eWaste disposal solutions to eliminate all the hassles of handling, transporting, and disposing of eWaste end products offering best e-waste Services in India. The goal is to make recycling easier for consumers, reduce waste in landfills, and reduce greenhouse gas emissions caused by mining, manufacturing, transportation & incineration.

EPR Services

Manufacturers, producers & importers are usually held responsible for providing eWaste disposal solutions and handling disposal of certain eWaste products after their life has ended under eWaste guidelines 2022. This service enables producers to meet their Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) requirements and also helps them in undertaking the complete procedure of Logistics Management -from collection, transportation, through responsible disposal.
