About Us

The Lakshmi Mills Group is well known in the Indian textile industry. The flagship company, The Lakshmi Mills Company Ltd., has been established more than 100 years ago. The name of Lakshmi Mills has become synonymous with quality cotton and synthetic textiles. The group started a number of other enterprises with the aim of making the group a complete player in the field of textiles. Today, the group is a complete textile house with units engaged in manufacturing various textile machineries with distinction.

The need for sophisticated and high quality weaving machines prompted the management to establish Lakshmi Engineering and Warehousing LImited (Formerly Lakshmi Automatic Loom Works Limited) in the year 1973. The company in collaboration with Ruti Machinery Works Ltd, Switzerland manufactured high-speed automatic ‘C’ type weaving machines and Air jet weaving machines. The company’s second Unit at Singarampalayam in Coimbatore District manufactured Circular Knitting machines in collaboration with Terrot, Germany.

The company continues to manufacture spares and accessories for weaving and knitting machines and parts for machines tools. With the thrust on Shuttleless looms the company is discussing with prospective overseas machinery manufacturers for a tie up for manufacture of shuttleless weaving machines.

Lakshmi Engineering and Warehousing LImited is committed to sound practice of corporate functioning and fulfilling the customer’s needs by offering quality products and after sales service.
