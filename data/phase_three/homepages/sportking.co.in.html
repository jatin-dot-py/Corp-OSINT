Journey of Sportking

1977

Sportking Group started as a

Readymade Garment Unit

1992

Active in the garment sector till now

The group started with backward integration

1993

Forayed into spinning by installing 7,000

Spindles at Sportking India LTD., Ludhiana

1995

Spinning increased to

19,000 Spindles

1997

Started a chain of Retail Outlets Named

Sportking Fashion Planet

2009

Took a -quantum leap by setting up another unit at
Bathinda in Punjab equipped with State-of-the-Art Machinery,

Able Human Resources and Sound Quality and
process control Systems

2018

Another expansion project executed successfully at its Bathinda complex adding 70,000 spindles with state of the art 100% Rieter plant achieving a total spinning capacity 2,72,000 spindles.

  • The company today has over 100 retail outlets across India.
  • Provides direct employment to about 12,000 people.
  • Indirect employment to ancillary industries like Packaging, Light Engineering and it's Machinery, Knitting and Job Work Services.
  • Spinning Capacity is now close to 2,72,000 spindles.
  • Readymade Garment production is over 25,000 pieces per day.

Moving forward with latest technology for manufacturing, strong systems and a good work culture, the group is poised to find its place amongst premium brands in the times to come.
