Frequently Asked Questions

What does Ameya Precision Engineers specialize in?

We specialize in manufacturing high-quality precision engineering components such as shafts, stems, hard-facing overlays, precision trim parts, and assemblies for industrial applications.

Which industries does Ameya Precision Engineers serve?

We serve industries including oil and gas, power generation, water and wastewater management, and defence sectors with customized engineering solutions.

Is Ameya Precision Engineers a family-run business?

Yes, we are a family-run company with decades of experience, combining traditional values with advanced engineering capabilities to deliver reliable, high-quality solutions.

What are hard-facing overlays and why are they used?

Hard-facing overlays are protective coatings applied to components to improve wear resistance, corrosion resistance, and durability in extreme industrial environments.

Does Ameya Precision Engineers export its products globally?

Yes, we export a significant portion of our products worldwide, serving international clients across multiple industries.

What types of shafts and stems are manufactured by Ameya Precision Engineers?

We manufacture high-precision shafts and stems used in valves, pumps, and power transmission systems to ensure efficient and reliable operation.

What makes Ameya Precision Engineers a reliable manufacturing partner?

With decades of experience since 1987 and a global presence, we are a trusted partner for engineering solutions. A family-run business like ours offers consistent quality, long-term partnerships, personalized service, and deep-rooted expertise built over generations.

Do you provide custom engineering solutions?

Yes, we offer customized precision components tailored to client specifications and complex industrial requirements.

What quality standards does Ameya Precision Engineers follow?

We adhere to international manufacturing and quality standards, with an ISO 9001 programme, ensuring high precision, durability, and performance of its products.

Where is Ameya Precision Engineers located?

We are headquartered in Pune, India, with a global presence including operations in the UK and the Netherlands.
