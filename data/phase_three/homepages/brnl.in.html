Who We Are

Bharat Road Network Limited, (BRNL) is a road BOT company in India, focused on development, implementation, operation and maintenance of roads/highways projects. Our Company was incorporated in 2006 as a developer and operator for highways, road and related projects on a BOT basis.

What We Do

Highway Development

Develops new infrastructure projects on a BOT basis, leveraging its expertise in project bidding, financing, devoloping and operating

Project Management

Project Lifecycle, Project Valuation, Financial Modelling

Tolling & Operations Management

Operations and Maintenance of Tolling Operations

Claim & Contracts Management

Concession Management, Claim Management and Project Advisory

Sustainability

Social Commitments​

The way of Sustainability is embedded in our Perception, Strategy, Culture and the way we operate. For us it means, creating a sustainable environment based on the principle of building foundations for a progressive society through contribution to the society and nurturing growth.

Infrastructure growth reflects the forward movement of the society we thrive in, and thus BRNL is committed to seek sustainable growth by integrating Environment, Social and Governance (ESG) principles with its businesses processes.
