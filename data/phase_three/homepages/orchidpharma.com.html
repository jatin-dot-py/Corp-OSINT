Novel Medicines

Breakthrough therapies for resistant infections

Pioneering anti-infective research addressing critical unmet needs. Enmetazobactam represents India's first New Chemical Entity to receive US FDA approval.

ORBLICEF™ (Cefepime–Enmetazobactam)

A novel β-lactam/β-lactamase inhibitor combination designed to combat ESBL-producing Enterobacterales, delivering enhanced microbiological coverage with robust clinical outcomes in severe infections.

ORTARO™ (Ceftaroline Fosamil)

A fifth-generation cephalosporin with potent anti-MRSA activity, offering reliable efficacy in Gram-positive infections along with a favorable safety and tolerability profile.

Cefiderocol

A last-line siderophore cephalosporin for the treatment of serious multidrug-resistant Gram-negative infections, including pathogens resistant to carbapenems.
