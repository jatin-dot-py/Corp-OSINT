CMD's Message

GTV has always been the first preferred supply chain partner of its customers since its inception in 1978 with customer retention rate of 100 percent. "Once a customer, always a customer" has been our ethos and all our manufacturing and quality control policies revolve around the same. Also, we are proud to state 0% attrition rate and GTV still today proudly works with the same core management/employees/workers which joined us in 1978. With "Make in India"  initiative getting implemented at its best, under visionary leadership of our respected Prime Minister, and with specific thrust to capex in cement, power, steel and minerals sectors, GTV is poised for exponential growth in coming years. Considering the growing domestic demand, as well as the fact that many of the international buyers are turning to India for steel fabrications, GTV is gearing up towards increasing its production capacity to take advantage of the lucrative growth opportunities, and is quite confident of being one of the leading contenders for high-value projects.
