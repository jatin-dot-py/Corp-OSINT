Welcome To Bombay Wire Ropes Limited

Excellence in Wire Rope Manufacturing Since 1961

Learn More Investor Relations

Bombay Wire Ropes Limited was incorporated in 1961 and is based in Mumbai, Maharashtra. The Company is a leading manufacturer of wire ropes, lifting slings, steel wire ropes, and stainless steel wire ropes. With over six decades of experience, we have established ourselves as a trusted name in the wire rope industry, serving various sectors including construction, mining, shipping, and industrial applications.

Our Objectives

We are committed to creating sustainable value for our shareholders through strategic growth and operational excellence.

Understanding and fulfilling customer needs is at the heart of everything we do. We deliver quality products that meet the highest industry standards.
