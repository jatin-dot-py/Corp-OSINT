Mangalam Organics Limited is a BSE and NSE listed company primarily manufacturing Pine Chemicals such as Terpene Chemistry: Camphor, Camphene, Isobornyl Acetate, Isoborneol, Dipentene and Sodium Acetate and Synthetic Resins: Terpene Phenolic Resin, Alkyl Phenolic Resin, Rosin Based Resins. The company also has a strong retail presence through its brands Mangalam and CamPure which can be seen on www.houseofmangalam.com
The company has a strong foothold in the pine chemicals Industry through its management that has been in the field for over 50 years. Mangalam Organics Limited is a government recognized export house with customers in Europe, USA, Africa and the Middle East
The manufacturing plant was established in 1996 at Kumbhivali, Mumbai. Over the years the company has successfully increased its production, augmented its sales reach and maintained its superior quality for which it is renowned. Going forward Mangalam Organics Limited will continue to strive and innovate to meet the growing requirements of its valued customers.
Mangalam Organics Limited employs highly qualified and trained personnel to monitor, maintain and grow its research, manufacturing and sales activities.

ABOUT

MANAGEMENT

CHAIRMAN
KAMAL DUJODWALA

Kamal brings with him over 40 years experience in the chemical Industry and has spearheaded commissioning and operations at various facilities across India. A regular participant in the Pine Chemicals Association conferences, Kamal is well acclaimed and has delivered speeches on multiple occasions.

Kamal has been the president of the All India Manufacturers Organization and is presently the Vice President of the Indo-China Chamber of Commerce. He holds a BSc.Science degree and has thereafter received extensive training at National Chemical Laboratory.

MANAGING DIRECTOR
PANNKAJ DUJODWALA

Pannkaj has been involved in the Pine Chemicals Industry for over 30 years and brings with him wide experience to oversee day-to-day activities at MOL.

He has been the Past President of Akhil Bharatvarshia Marwadi Agrawal Jatiya Kosh, a renowned institution running schools and colleges.

He has studied Business Administration from Landsdown College, New Hampshire University in the UK.

Chief Strategy Officer
Akshay Dujodwala

Akshay joined Mangalam Organics in 2013 and has since initiated the B2C retail channels by establishing two brands Mangalam – Camphor Tablets for pious purposes and CamPure – Camphor based products for homecare needs such as air fresheners.

Akshay has pursued  Msc . Business Analytics and Consulting from Warwick Business School, post which he worked as a strategy consultant before joining MOL.
