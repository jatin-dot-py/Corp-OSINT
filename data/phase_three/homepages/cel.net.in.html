    ABOUT COMPANY

    COSPOWER ENGINEERING LTD.

    CosPower Engineering Ltd. is an ISO:9001 company incorporated in the year 2004 having a manufacturing facility at Vasai, a suburb in Mumbai. Our Company is a strong force to reckon with, in the field of Power Factor Improvement and Harmonic Mitigation. We have a Team of Experts who have vast experience in this field and are focussed in designing‘tailor-made’solutions towards Harmonics and Power Factor related issues in an Electrical System. We offer precise solutions taking into consideration both the technical and economical requirements. While Reactive Power Compensation & Power Quality Improvement remain the core competency of our Company, we have diversified into related activities of turnkey substation works, complete range of High & Low Voltage electrical panels
