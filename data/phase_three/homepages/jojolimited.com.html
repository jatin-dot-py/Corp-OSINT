JOJO Limited

Pioneering Gujarati entertainment since inception.

We believe that great stories have no borders. As one of the region’s most ambitious entertainment companies, we are building innovative platforms and creative ecosystems backed by in-house technology that will take the Gujarati culture with its spirit, voice and richness to its audiences.

Our approach seamlessly blends cultural authenticity with digital innovation; enabling authentic cultural experiences across every screen, every city, every corner of the Gujarati diaspora.

”Tradition is our foundation, technology our reach, and the world our stage.”

Director's Message

”At JOJO Limited, we are building something that outlasts a moment. As the world's consumption habits shift, language and geography are no longer limits, they are opportunities. Our mission is to ensure that Gujarati content travels: adapting to new formats, reaching new communities, and resonating across borders without ever losing its cultural soul.

The legacy we are building is not measured in content libraries or market share alone, it is measured in the generations who will grow up seeing their stories told in their language with pride, craft and ambition. This is more than entertainment. It is about shaping relevance for generations to come.”

Dhruvin Shah

Founder & Managing Director, JOJO Limited
