Solar Panel Manufacturer in India

Creating a sustainable future is the need of the hour. In this essential journey towards environmental responsibility, solar energy stands at the forefront. The duty to make eco-conscious decisions to create cleaner, greener energy solutions is ours.

Insolation Energy Limited (INA Solar) stands as a leading Solar Panel Manufacturer in India. With over 9 years of impeccable manufacturing performance, INA Solar operates three advanced facilities in Jaipur with a combined annual capacity of 5.5 GW. We are on a rapid growth path with expansion plans to reach 4.5 GW Solar Cells and 18,000 MTA Aluminium Frame manufacturing.

We deliver cutting-edge TOPCon solar panels, Mono PERC Modules backed by innovation and uncompromising quality. Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities leverage AI and robotics to ensure precision, efficiency, and reliability in every panel we produce.

Our strong industry presence, as a top Solar Panel Company in India, is built on a history of trust and performance:

  • 40,000+ Happy Customers
  • 400+ MW of IPP project deployments
  • 1000+ Channel Partners Across 250+ districts

Every INA Solar product is manufactured using the latest technologies, fully automated machinery, and guided by industry veterans. We rigorously comply with global and domestic standards including ISO, UL, IEC, ALMM, CE, BIS, and more—ensuring world-class quality, durability, and performance.

As a part of our commitment to India’s green future, INA Solar supports the Make in India initiative, aligning with the Hon’ble Prime Minister’s vision of achieving Net-Zero emissions by 2070.

This dedication is reflected in our growing market presence. INA Solar, being a Top Solar Panel Manufacturer, has secured the first position among BSE and NSE Emerge board listed companies in India, and ranks 757th among the top 1000 companies nationwide based on market capitalization worth ₹3,412.07 Cr., as per the SEBI Business Responsibility & Sustainability Report (BRSR) dated 31 March 2024.

Reinforcing our industry leadership, INA Solar has also ranked No. 1 among its global listed peers in the Financial Stability Ranking Report by SINOVOLTAICS, a Dutch-German consultancy firm. This recognition—based on stringent financial discipline across multiple parameters—further cements our credibility. INA Solar stood ahead of 60+ Asian, European, and American manufacturers and was proudly featured in Forbes India Magazine Article ‘Best Under A Billion’, 2024 and has been ranked 286th within the India’s manufacturing category, with a reported valuation of ₹8,100 Cr. by Hurun India Manufacturing 400, 2024.

In a bold and strategic move, Insolation Energy Limited (INA Solar) has partnered with the renowned Lucknow Super Giants as an Associate Partner for T20 Seasons 2026–2027, promoting solar energy on one of the nation’s largest platforms.

Our success is a symbol of the trust of the industry and its customers in our capabilities. With an unwavering commitment to innovation in the energy sector, we prioritize customer satisfaction to the fullest. With our motto being "Together We Shine", we strongly believe in sharing our successes with all our stakeholders and consistently invest into innovation to create high performance products that contribute to a more energy-efficient world.

What Makes Insolation Energy One of the Top 10 Solar Panel Manufacturers in India?

Insolation Energy (INA Solar) stands at the forefront of solar innovation by embracing a clear vision for smart, future-ready manufacturing that aligns with India's Atmanirbhar Bharat mission and the global shift toward sustainable energy.

Our leadership, as a top solar panel manufacturer, is defined by our proactive transition towards Industry 4.0 standards. By integrating automation, digital monitoring, and real-time data analytics into our production processes, we are setting new benchmarks in precision and efficiency. Cutting-edge initiatives like IoT-based production tracking and AI-enabled quality control allow us to detect defects early, minimize waste, and enhance product reliability.

As we scale, our upcoming manufacturing facilities are being equipped with advanced robotics, automated testing systems, and smart inventory management-ensuring faster turnaround times, consistent quality, and timely delivery to meet both domestic and international demands.

As one of the top solar panel manufacturers in India, our innovation extends beyond production. We're implementing smart energy management across our plants to optimize power consumption and boost energy efficiency, reinforcing our commitment to clean and green operations.

Sustainability is embedded into every layer of our process-from reducing waste and conserving water to ensuring environmentally responsible sourcing. This holistic approach reflects our dedication to environmental stewardship and long-term impact.

As a premier solar manufacturer India organization, INA Solar is building a digitally enabled, self-reliant, and globally competitive manufacturing ecosystem. By leveraging advanced technology and sustainable practices, we are not just producing solar panels-we're shaping the future of solar manufacturing in India and beyond.

Upgrading Infrastructure, Adopting New Technologies

At INA Solar, we are building the foundation for a future-ready manufacturing ecosystem by embracing cutting-edge technologies that enhance efficiency, accuracy, and sustainability across our operations. This makes us the best solar manufacturer in India.

IoT-Enabled Monitoring Systems

Real-time production tracking allows us to monitor equipment performance, detect issues instantly, and ensure uninterrupted operations with minimal downtime.

AI-Based Quality Inspection

Advanced machine vision systems powered by AI identify micro-defects in solar cells and modules, enabling zero-defect manufacturing and reducing reliance on manual inspections.

Automated Material Handling

Robotic systems and automated conveyors streamline material movement, improving production speed, consistency, and workplace safety.

Digital Twin & Data Analytics

Digital twin simulations help us optimize manufacturing workflows, while data analytics supports predictive maintenance and reduces unplanned equipment failures.

SCADA & Smart Energy Management

SCADA systems track energy usage across the plant, enabling smarter load management, improved power factor, and reduced energy costs-all aligned with our green energy goals.

ERP System Integration

Our end-to-end ERP platform connects planning, procurement, inventory, and production, enhancing transparency, traceability, and operational control.

These digital advancements are not just upgrades-they're transformative steps that position INA Solar as a global leader in smart solar manufacturing.

Being one of the leading solar panel manufacturers in India, we focus on delivering environmentally friendly, cost-effective solutions that align with global sustainability goals..

By collaborating with leading research institutions, we stay ahead of emerging trends and breakthroughs in solar science. Our future-oriented approach ensures we're not just meeting today's energy needs-but shaping tomorrow's.
