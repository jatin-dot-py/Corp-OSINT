About Us
OUR STORY

Saakshi Medtech and Panels Ltd. was founded in 2001 by Mr Vijay Latkar a technocrat and a veteran General Electric employee who had held a variety of management positions during his tenure with the company.

Out of its 3 locations in Pune's Industrial Zone, SMTPL operates in 3 different business verticals, namely the medical industry, electrical industry and mechanical industry.

SMTPL's strength lies in its multi-disciplinary teams within marketing, engineering, procurement, logistics, quality, and testing. As a result, it can deliver all-round customer solutions to meet the needs of its customers. With core business processes designed to support growth, SMTPL's systems have been vetted through global certifications such as ISO 9001:2015, ISO 13485:2016, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001-2018 and AS 9100D.
