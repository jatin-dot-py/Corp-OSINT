Welcome to Supreme Engineering Ltd

Supreme Engineering Ltd, an AS9100D and ISO 9001:2015 certified Steel Manufacturing Company India, has been a specialized manufacturer in high-technology fields of metallurgy since 1987. Supreme operates two advanced manufacturing plants – a Special Steel Plant and a Wire Plant – and is recognized as India’s leading producer of superalloys and other special steels.

We are equipped with state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities, a dedicated R&D laboratory, and hold critical approvals for applications across Aerospace, Space, Defence, Medical, Thermal Power, Nuclear Power, and Oil & Gas sectors. Our commitment to quality and innovation continues to position us as a trusted partner in the global steel industry.
