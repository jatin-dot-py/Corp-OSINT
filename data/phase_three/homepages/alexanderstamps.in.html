A Philately and Numismatics Company

We curate and trade in exceptional rare stamps, coins, paper currency, medals, postcards, original photographs, autographs, newspapers, and historical collectibles. Our Philately and Numismatics collection is meticulously preserved and authenticated, offering discerning collectors’ timeless pieces of history that reflect heritage, craftsmanship, and enduring value.

Introduction

M/s Alexander Stamps and Coin Limited is owned and managed by its Board of Directors comprising Mr. Anirudh Sethi, Ms. Tanmaya Arora, Ms. Divya Batra, Mr. Nikhil Kapoor, and Mr. Jignesh Soni.

Mr. Anirudh Sethi has been an avid stamp collector since childhood, with specialization in classic philatelic stamps and coins from various countries. He brings over 25 years of rich and diverse experience in the philatelic business, including marketing, acquisition, and trading across all levels. He has also been an active supporter and promoter of the philately hobby.
