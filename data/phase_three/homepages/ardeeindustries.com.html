Welcome to

Ardee Industries Limited

Ardee Industries Limited is a leading Indian company in the secondary metals industry, supplying high-quality lead metal to domestic and international markets. With strong expertise in lead recycling, we serve the battery, metal, and manufacturing sectors with a focus on quality and reliability.

Our facilities have an installed recycling capacity of 156,950 MT per annum of finished goods, including refined lead and various lead alloys. Operations follow stringent quality, safety, and environmental standards.

Ardee Industries is one very few companies in india to have its lead ingots brand approved by the London Metal Exchange (LME), reflecting adherence to global benchmarks. Driven by experienced leadership, we emphasize ethical practices, responsible sourcing, and long-term partnerships.

Lead Manufacturing
Lead Manufacturing

International grade manufacturers of Lead and Lead products

Lead is one of the metals with prolific use because of its ease of extraction, processability, and damage tolerance. Every day the industry comes up with new applications for lead, creating a surge in demand in the evolving modern business landscape.

Lead is primarily employed in the lead acid battery business, as a protective metal coating, in artillery, roofing, plumbing, and electrical industries. We employ environment friendly processes to recycle and refine lead and deploy it to manufacturing various lead products.
