It has come to our notice that certain unscrupulous persons are falsely claiming to be representing Naga Dhunseri Group Limited (our Company) and are posting false information by offering loans including by using a fake website www.nagadhunseri.com (Impugned Website).

Please note that we have only one official website viz www.nagadhunserigroup.com (our Website).

The Impugned Website is unlawfully developed using our name, trademark/logo, artistic work (such as but not limited to ), branding, email ids and the like in a manner that it appears to be our Website or deceptively similar to our Website. These persons are offering loans to third parties who are being misled into believing that the Impugned Website and persons working thereunder are offering services on behalf of our Company's. Once the loans are issued, these persons are indulging in disturbing activities. The Impugned Website is maliciously set up to extort money (on the pretext of repayment of loans) in the name of our Company.

Please note that we are in no way connected to the Impugned Website. We disclaim all liabilities arising out of dealings done by anyone using Impugned Website or any such illegal website.
