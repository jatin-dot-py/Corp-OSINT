One of India’s leading vertically integrated manufacturing company

Maral Overseas Limited

Maral Overseas Ltd, has set new benchmarks as Industry leader as one of India's largest vertically integrated textile companies. It was set up in 1991 and its leadership in textile Industry has emerged by ensuring world class products and best business practices in the global market. Its state-of-the-art infrastructure from the machinery for spinning to garmenting which is operative in a highly automated controlled environment is geared towards achieving range of innovative and diversified quality products. As a reliable supplier of world class products and most excellent industry practices, Maral has earned its reputation in the national as well as international market. Its core strength lies in premium quality systems, quality relationships and modern production technologies.

We have now three ultra modern units based at Maral, Sarovar and Noida, and is able to produce 1700 of Grey Yarn, 300 tons of Dyed Yarn, 700 tons of Dyed Knitted Fabric and 5,00,000 pieces of garments every month.
