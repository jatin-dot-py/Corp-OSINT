About Setco Auto Systems

Setco Auto Systems Pvt. Ltd. (Setco), now a subsidiary of RSB Transmissions, is a world-leading manufacturer of heavy-duty clutches, driveline components, and hydraulic solutions for the automotive and construction equipment industries. Renowned for its engineering expertise, Setco’s products are designed for long-lasting performance, smooth engagement, and operational efficiency across challenging terrains.

Catering to both OEMs and the aftermarket, Setco’s growing product portfolio includes flywheels, clutch release bearing, brake linings, center support bearings, UJ crosses, water pumps, load cushions and torque rod bushes, optimized for MHCVs, LCVs, and other commercial vehicles. With a robust manufacturing base, global reach, and commitment to quality, Setco continues to power mobility with reliable, technologically advanced automotive components.

Setco is certified to IATF 16949, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001 standards.
