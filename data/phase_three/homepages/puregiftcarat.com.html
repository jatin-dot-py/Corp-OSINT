Our Brand

We are engaged in trading and retailership of Diamonds and Jewellery in Surat. Our operations include sourcing of Diamonds and Jewellery from primary and secondary source suppliers in the domestic market and sale to the wholesale as well as retail operations in Mainly Gujarat. We primarily sell our products to a customer base spread across domestic markets that includes various jewellery manufacturers, wholesalers, large department store chains and retail stores.
