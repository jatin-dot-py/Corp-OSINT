ABOUT THE COMPANY

About Jasch

Since 1998, Jasch has been developing advanced online measurement systems for industries around the world. With more than 3,000 systems installed globally, we help manufacturers improve accuracy, quality, and production performance.

Today, Jasch combines strong engineering expertise with global service support, with manufacturing in India and offices across South Korea, Brazil, Vietnam, Taiwan and Bangladesh. Our growth is built on technology, continuous improvement, and the trust of customers who continue to choose Jasch.

OUR CAPABILITIES

What We Offer

Comprehensive measurement and gauging technologies designed for specific industrial applications.

X-Ray Gauges

Ideal for heavy industry production quality monitoring, providing online fast and accurate measurement.

Isotope (Gamma / Beta) Gauges

Used in paper, metal, and plastic factory production lines. A cost-effective solution for quality maintenance.

Microwave Sensor

Specifically for the paper industry to manage moisture. Helps control moisture and maintain product quality.

Infrared and Caliper Sensors

For multi-parameter measurement in the paper industry (surface moisture, thickness, etc.). A versatile solution.
