What we do

Grovy India Limited focuses on residential redevelopment projects in select South Delhi micro-markets, combining local execution capability with structured project and capital management. Our work is organised around three core activities:

1. Residential redevelopment through documented landowner partnerships

We work with individual landowners and families through structured redevelopment agreements, with clearly defined rights, obligations, timelines, and commercial terms.

2. Project execution with defined budgets, timelines, and controls

Each project is executed against an approved budget and construction plan, supported by milestone-based monitoring, independent certifications, and regular cost reviews.

3. Capital deployment alongside partners and investors

Grovy invests its own capital alongside financial partners and investors, ensuring alignment of interest across land acquisition, construction, and project completion.
