Duroply towards Sustainability

Duroply is committed towards procuring the finest raw material that must be from legal and sustainable sources. This includes procuring timber that has reached its maturity, the source of origin is known, and must come from forests that are sustainably managed.

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Duro offers a comprehensive range of plywood and blockboards including Marine Grade (BWP), BWR, MR Grade, Fire Retardant, Flexible Plywood, Commercial Plywood, including specialty solutions designed for furniture, interiors, and construction application.

Fire-retardant plywood is specially treated to slow the spread of flames and improve fire safety. With Duro Advanced Fireshield Technology, Duro plywood offers enhanced protection by delaying fire spread for up to 45 minutes, helping provide greater safety and peace of mind.

Duroply products are available across India. Connect with an authorised Duro dealer near you or contact us on our toll-free number 1800-345-3876 (Mon–Sat) for assistance.

Duro Guarantee is Duroply's assurance of product performance against termites and borer infestation and are backed by a guarantee program that offers protection under specified terms and conditions. In the unlikely event of deterioration due to insect attack, eligible customers may receive compensation of up to 3X the value of the defective product or the value of the furniture, whichever is lower, subject to verification and guarantee eligibility.

IS 710 refers to BWP/Marine Grade waterproof plywood, while IS 303 refers to MR Grade plywood for interior applications.
