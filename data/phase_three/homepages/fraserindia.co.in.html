Welcome to Fraser and Company Limited

FRASER AND COMPANY LIMITED, has been incorporated in the year 1917 under the Companies Act, 2013 and has completed more than 100 years since its Incorporation.

Fraser is listed at Bombay Stock Exchange limited.

We are supplier of all type of construction material to the Builders including Interiors of the Property. (i.e From Foundation Till Interior). We offers a range of products to meet the needs at Construction Sites (Residential and Commercial). Even very small volume orders has earned us the trust and confidence of the industry.

Our vision is to provide small to large every requirement at construction Sites.  We are backed by competent Operational and compliance capabilities.

We have the prime motive to satisfy each and every customer beyond the expected levels of business.

Customer satisfaction always gets the top priority at our Organization.
