About Bai Kakaji

Leading Manufacturers of PET Preforms & HDPE Caps

Established in 2013 in Latur, Maharashtra, Bai-kakaji Polymers began with a single unit and has grown into a large-scale manufacturer of closures and PET preforms using advanced technology. Using SACMI, HUSKY, and ASB machines, we produce flash-free, leak-proof products with uniform thickness. Rigorous quality checks ensure clarity, durability, and minimal rejection across all production lines. We offer a wide range of PET preforms, CSD (38) and HDPE caps, all tested to international standards. Our products are supplied pan-India with reliable quality and service.
