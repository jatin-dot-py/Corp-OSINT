FAQs
What does 1Point1 do?

1Point1 helps companies scale digital transformation (DX), customer experience (CX)operations, automate workflows, and unlock efficiency using a blend of human expertise, AI, analytics, and omnichannel support. We design custom solutions that reduce cost, improve service quality, and accelerate growth for businesses.

What services does 1Point1 Solutions offer?

1Point1 offers a comprehensive suite of outsourcing and digital transformation services, but not limited to:

  • Customer Experience (CX) Management: Providing omnichannel, multilingual customer support across email, chat, voice and digital platforms. 
  • Digital Transformation & Automation: Driving business efficiency with the help of AI, RPA, data analytics, IT optimization and automation. 
  • Finance & Accounting Outsourcing: Managing payroll, budgeting, bookkeeping, forecasting and compliance with end-to-end accuracy 
  • Trust & Safety: Protecting brands with the help of fraud detection, user-generated content, moderation and brand review
  • Supply Chain & Operations Management: Operation optimization with efficient order of handling complaint management and inventory control 
  • Creator Economy Support: Supporting creators with brand partnerships, content production, audience growth and IP management. 
  • Medical Records & Litigation Support: Delivering medical records, billing reviews, summaries and litigation documentation.
What Industries Does 1Point1 Solutions Serve?

1Point1 serves a wide array of industries, including:

  • Telecom & Media 
  • Travel, Tourism & Hospitality 
  • E-commerce & Retail 
  • Gaming & Entertainment 
  • BFSI (Banking, Financial Services, Insurance) 
  • Medical Record Retrieval Support
What is the company culture like at 1Point1?

At 1Point1 Solutions, we foster a dynamic, inclusive, and collaborative work environment built on values of respect, integrity, and teamwork. We believe in empowering employees through continuous learning, offering training programs and career growth opportunities.

‍

Our commitment to diversity and inclusion ensures a workplace that reflects the communities we serve. We also prioritize employee well-being and work-life balance, creating a supportive environment for personal and professional success.

What are the working hours at 1Point1?

At 1Point1 Solutions, working hours can vary based on the location, job role, and client requirements. Many positions, particularly those in customer service and call center operations, follow a shift-based schedule to cater to different time zones and ensure round-the-clock support. This means employees may work during the day, evening, or overnight shifts.
