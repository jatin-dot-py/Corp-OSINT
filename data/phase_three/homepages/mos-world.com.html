Company Overview

MOS Utility Limited, or "MOS," has firmly positioned itself as a dynamic and rapidly expanding B2B E-commerce digital fintech powerhouse. Our core vision, "Digital Bano tarraki karo," encapsulates our commitment to driving India's technological evolution.

Embracing innovation, we facilitate seamless digital transactions and financial solutions for businesses, fostering efficiency and growth. In sync with the government's "Vocal for Local" campaign, we ardently support indigenous entrepreneurship, empowering local businesses to thrive in the digital realm. Our journey symbolizes not only our corporate success but also our contribution to the nation's progress, as we continue to reshape India's digital landscape.

Setup A MOS Seva kendra

MOS Seva Kendra (MSK) is a special initiative by MOS Utility Limited that provides a fantastic business opportunity through franchising. Our motto, "One Pin Code One Agent," means that each area has its own dedicated agent. This idea helps our agents effectively serve their communities. It's all about delivering efficient and widespread service, which aligns with our commitment to excellence.

Our Seva Kendra model focuses on providing financial services to communities. It's a simple yet innovative concept that empowers individuals to become agents in their own neighborhoods. This initiative not only benefits the agents but also ensures that financial services reach every corner of society.
