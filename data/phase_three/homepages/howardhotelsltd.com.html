About Us

About US

Founded on December 11, 1989, under the Companies Act, 1956, and seamlessly transitioning to the Companies Act, 2013, HOWARD HOTELS LIMITED is a distinguished public company operating in the dynamic Hotel & Hospitality Industry. Registered with the Registrar of Companies, Delhi, the company boasts an authorized share capital of Rs. 100,000,000 and a robust paid-up capital of Rs. 91,132,000. With an active status, Howard Hotels Limited is classified as a non-government company, functioning as a company limited by shares. With over 33 years of experience, the company has become synonymous with luxury and unparalleled service.​
