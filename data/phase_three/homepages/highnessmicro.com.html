Optical Bonding

Optical Bonding

Optical Bonding is a process of bonding two surfaces to achieve better optical characteristics. This process reduces light reflection and make displays sunlight readable for outdoor visibility. The bonding can be done between following surfaces-

Glass + Glass
Glass + Film
Polycarbonate + Glass
Polycarbonate + Polycarbonate

It is possible to Optically Bond two surfaces using dry-bonding method using Optically Clear Adhesive (OCA) sheets or wet-bonding using Liquid Optically Clear Adhesive (LOCA).

HighnessMicro has engineering capabilities to undertake Optical-Bonding of Displays. These optically-bonded displays have successfully been deployed in applications such as Railways, Medical & Outdoor  Media.

Night Vision Imaging System (NVIS)

Night Vision Imaging System (NVIS)

NVIS enable visibility in dark or extremely low ambient light conditions.
With specially designed backlights, displays feature dual-mode: Day & Night with an option to toggle between the two.

Displays with NVIS feature is critical for Surveillance & Defence application.
HighnessMicro has designed displays with NVIS compatibility for use in Airborne application for Aerospace as well as ground application for Military.

EMI Shielding

EMI Shielding

Optical Fine-wire Mesh for shielding against Electro-Magnetic Interference (EMI) and Radio Frequency Interference (RFI)

These EMI-Mesh can be applied between surface layers in form of films or they can be optically-bonded along with front glass or touch screens.
Copper-tapped busbars on the edges allow termination for better shielding.

Attenuation starting 30dB, 45dB and 60dB are achievable by applying these EMI-Mesh which allows displays to be used in Defence & Medical application.
