Specialties

Conditions we treat

CATARACT

A cataract is a condition in which the eye’s lens becomes cloudy, causing blurry vision.

Learn more
Dr Ashvin Agarwal
Cataract Specialist

"Treating cataract is crucial because if left untreated, the clouded lens not only blurs vision and causes glare, but may also slow down the recovery of vision permanently; modern techniques let us remove the cataract safely and restore sharp, bright vision."

Top Treatments
  • Robotic / Laser-Assisted Cataract Surgery (LACS)
  • Micro-Incision Cataract Surgery (MICS) 
Pediatric

Squint (or strabismus) is a misalignment of the eyes, where they do not point in the same direction, affecting binocular vision and appearance.

Dr Nikhil Rishikeshi
Consultant Ophthalmologist

"It's important to treat squint not only to restore proper eye alignment and depth perception, but also to prevent amblyopia (lazy eye), reduce double vision, and improve social confidence, especially in children."

Top Treatments
  • Squint and nystagmus surgery
  • Myopia management
  • Paediatric cataract management
Glaucoma

Glaucoma is a group of eye diseases that damage the optic nerve, often associated with raised intraocular pressure, leading to gradual and irreversible vision loss if untreated.

Learn more
Dr Ashvin Agarwal
Cataract Specialist

"Early detection and treatment of glaucoma are vital because once the optic nerve is damaged, vision loss cannot be recovered. But with timely care, we can slow progression, preserve remaining vision, and maintain quality of life."

Top Treatments
  • Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery (MIGS)
  • Trabeculectomy
Refractive

Refractive errors occur when the eye’s shape prevents light from focusing directly on the retina, causing blurred vision at certain distances.

Learn more
Dr Jeewan Singh Titiyal
Regional Head - Clinical Services, New Delhi

"Correcting refractive errors early matters, as they can lead to eye strain, headaches, and impact daily life. With the right treatment, we can significantly reduce dependency on specs or contact lenses and improve the quality of vision."

Top Treatments
  • SMILE LASIK
  • Implantable Collamer Lens (ICL)
  • Contoura LASIK
General Ophthalmology

General ophthalmology focuses on daily eye check-ups, early diagnosis, and treatment of common eye conditions to maintain eye health and clear vision.

Dr Srinivasan S
Head - Clinical Service Credentials

General ophthalmology is an important part of early detection of common eye problems—routine, eye check-ups can help detect them before they become more serious and are necessary for the long-term health of your vision.

Top Treatments
  • Cataract surgery including Robotic surgery and premium intraocular lenses
  • ⁠Lipiflow (thermal pulsation treatment) for dry eyes
Cornea

Cornea services care for conditions that affect the transparent outermost layer of the eye regarding infections, damage and dystrophies in order to achieve clarity and prevent vision.

Dr Soosan Jacob
Director and Chief, Dr Agarwals Refractive & Cornea Foundation

Corneal problems can lead to pain, scarring and blurry vision as the cornea is the clear front window of your eye; timely treatment for cornea disorders may improve your vision or at least maintain it and prevent serious long term complications.

Top Treatments
  • PDEK/ PK
  • CXL/ CAIRS
Medical Retina & Surgical Retina

Medical and surgical retina care involves the treatment of retinal diseases such as diabetic retinopathy, macular diseases and retinal detachment in order to preserve and restore vision.

Dr Natarajan
Head & Consultant, Vitreo-Retinal Surgery Ophthalmology

Retinal disorders such as diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration and retinal detachment can cause severe vision loss; requiring immediate and specialized medical (and surgical) retina care to help preserve or restore your sight.

Top Treatments
  • INTRAVITREAL INJECTIONS
Orbit & Oculoplasty

Orbit and oculoplasty treatments address issues with the eyelids, tear ducts, and eye socket. They help restore function, comfort, and the cosmetic look of the eye.

Dr Priti Udhay
Head - Oculoplasty & Aesthetic Services

Orbit and oculoplasty treatments focus on issues with the eyelids, tear ducts, and eye socket. Timely care improves comfort, restores function, and improves cosmetic appearance.

Top Treatments
  • Entropion / ectropion / ptosis correction
  • Botox injection / blepharoplasty
Uvea

Uvea services are used to manage inflammation in the middle layer of the eye (uveitis), which, if not dealt with swiftly, can result in pain, redness and vision loss.

Dr Priti Udhay
Head - Oculoplasty & Aesthetic Services

Uveitis causes inflammation inside the eye which results in pain, light sensitivity, and vision loss. Early detetction prevents complications and helps protect long-term eye health.

Top Treatments
  • Diagnostic vitrectomy
Neurophthalmology

Neuro-ophthalmology deals with vision problems linked to the brain, optic nerve and the nerves that are involved in controlling eye movements. It can assist in the detection of complicated diseases involving both vision and the nervous system.

Dr Soundari S
Regional Head - Clinical Services, Chennai

Neuro-ophthalmology treats vision problems from the optic nerve and brain, as well as eye movement difficulties; a prompt examination will help in detecting serious neurological diseases and preventing additional loss of visual function.
