What we do

One integrated platform.
Three reinforcing businesses.

Cane comes in, sugar goes out, and the by-products power the plant and seed our bio-energy ambition. The same cane stream supports three revenue lines that share the same infrastructure, supplier base and balance sheet.

Sugar

2,500 TCD integrated mill

A modern crushing operation in the Bundelkhand belt — partnering with thousands of cane growers across Datia and adjoining districts to manufacture plantation white sugar.

Learn more

Power

3 MW captive co-generation

Bagasse-fired co-generation runs the plant year-round during the season and feeds surplus back to the unit — closing the loop on the cane fibre we crush.

Learn more

Bio-Energy

200 KLPD distillery (in build)

An ethanol distillery under the Government of India's Ethanol Blended Petrol programme — extending Dollex into India's bio-energy transition, anchored by molasses from the cane stream.

Learn more

Sustainability

The fibre that fuels the mill is the same fibre that decarbonises our footprint.

Bagasse fires our co-generation plant. Press-mud goes back to farmers as compost. Molasses becomes the feedstock for India’s Ethanol Blended Petrol programme — a programme that has saved the country billions of rupees in foreign exchange and millions of tonnes of CO₂.
