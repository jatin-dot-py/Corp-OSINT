ABOUT US

Premier Roadlines Ltd. – Your Trusted Logistics Partner

Premier Roadlines Limited, backed by four decades of logistics expertise, delivers reliable and efficient transportation solutions across India.
We proudly serve marquee clients across a well-diversified portfolio of industries, including Defence, Transformers, Hydro Projects, Oil & Gas, Energy, Infrastructure, Renewables, and Heavy Engineering, among others.
Our services are executed through trusted third-party operators using trucks, trailers, hydraulic axles, etc., along with our own specialized fleet for handling critical and complex assignments. With a well-established PAN India network and a growing presence in Nepal and Bhutan, we continue to expand our footprint across the subcontinent.
Our wholly owned subsidiary, Premier Worldwide, offers comprehensive end-to-end logistics solutions, covering Ocean Freight, Air Freight, Project Logistics, and Warehousing & Distribution.
