Rex Sealing & Packing Industries Limited

Leading Manufacturer And Exporter Of World Class Sealing And Insulation Products.

Established in 1963, Rex Sealing & Packing Industries has grown into a globally recognized name in sealing, packing, and insulation products. Our commitment to innovation, rigorous quality standards, and customer-centric solutions has made us a partner of choice across Chemical, Petrochemical, Power, Pharmaceutical, Gas, Cement and Process Industries.

Headquartered in India with a comprehensive Pan-India distribution network, we export our world-class products to the USA, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, UAE, and Egypt ensuring our products reaches every corner of the globe.
