Skyline Millars Limited (SML): A Century of Trust in Construction and Real Estate.

Skyline Millars Limited (SML) has been synonymous with excellence in the construction industry. Established with a commitment to high standards of quality, trust, and innovation, SML has been in the manufacturing of construction equipment and EOT cranes. Now the company focuses primarily on real estate, leveraging its legacy of success to build a brighter future.

  • With a rich history of over 100 years, Skyline Millars Limited (SML) has evolved into a prominent entity, a dynamic and forward-thinking company focused on real estate development.
  • Our commitment to quality, innovation, and sustainability sets us apart, making us a trusted name in the industry.
  • As we continue to grow and expand, we remain dedicated to our core values of excellence, trust, and customer satisfaction.
