Freight Forwarding

Whether it is sea or Road, We Ensure that your cargo is moved and delivered safely and in the prescribed time frame. We keep a close watch on the deliverables at all times. Our Freight Forwarding services are wide spread across the Globe.
Our services are tailored to fit your needs.

04

Ware Housing

Cargotrans Maritime Ltd. is part of a very elaborate distribution network. A very intricate part of this network is also warehousing. Our warehousing services are flexible to incorporate the varied destinations of delivery. We hold the cargo in the warehouse based on the delivery time and day. This is done keeping in mind, the security and other variables that need to be monitored and controlled for the stored cargo including temperature and cleanliness issues. Cargotrans Maritime Ltd. also provides Transportation from the port to the warehouses and vice versa. This saves time and involvement of a fourth party in the process. Cargotrans Maritime Ltd. is therefore a complete solution provider.

07

Vessel Charter – And Bulk Movement

Cargotrans Maritime Ltd. offers specialized break bulk cargo handling services. We book vessels for break bulk cargo. Our undisputed technical expertise in handling heavy equipment, ship chartering, consultancy and other broking services give us the edge above our competitors. We are well aware of the specific requirements and unconventional cargo handling that transpire in break bulk vessel handling. You will be nothing less than satisfied with our services.
The total cost of transporting goods from one place to another depends to a maximum extent on cargo handling expense. In break bulk cargo handling, cargo is handled manually on several occasions and then again assembled categorically in the warehouse. Cargotrans Maritime Ltd. is well versed with break bulk cargo handling and handles it manually without errors and such that you incur the minimum expenses to transport your consignments by sea.
