Welcome to ISL Consulting Ltd.

ISL CONSULTING LIMITED is a Public Limited Company based at Ahmedabad. The Registered Office of the Company is situated at 504, Priviera, Nehrunagar Circle, Nr. Bank Of Baroda, Ahmedabad – 380015, Gujarat, India.

The CIN of the Company is: L67120GJ1993PLC086576.

The Company was incorporated on 5th January, 1993 as a Public Limited Company with the Registrar of Companies, Tamil Nadu at Madras as Innovation Security Limited. The Company has changed its name to ISL Consulting Limited w.e.f. 27th March, 2000 and obtained change of name certificate on 4th May, 2000.
