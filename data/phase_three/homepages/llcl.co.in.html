Landmarc Leisure Corporation Limited is a listed company with interests in wellness, education and entertainment sectors.

Landmarc Films, A Division of Landmarc Leisure Corporation Limited, is a multidisciplinary production house setup established in 2011, involved in development, production, marketing & distribution of feature films, documentaries, short films and promote all kinds of cinema, filmmaking and creativity.

The company has forayed into the wellness industry with the launch of Svastii Spa & Salon, a new-age Unisex Wellness destination, a place to de-stress, detoxify, relax and rejuvenate and with the Svastii Wellness Academy, LLCL has taken its first steps into the education sector.
