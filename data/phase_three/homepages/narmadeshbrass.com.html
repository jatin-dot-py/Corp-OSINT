Welcome To Our Company

We are one of the Pioneer & Expertise in this field

We are one of the leading manufacturer of brass extrusion rods & Metal Turned Components. Established in 2019, it has now been one of the leading quality manufacturer of Metal Components & Extruded Products.
  • We are only manufacturer of brass rods of size above 3" size to provide more than 2 Meter length.
