CROP LIFE SCIENCE LIMITED.

CLSL began nearly two decades ago as a small-scale company manufacturing crop protection chemicals. CLSL has evolved into a leading manufacturer of a diverse range of products, including Insecticides, Fungicides, Herbicides, Micro fertilizers, new generation organic Bio-stimulants, Bio-fertilizers, Bio-Pesticide’s plant growth regulators, and soil plant health products, through acquisition, backward and forward integration.
