Recognitions that inspire us to do better...

We are proud to have been recognised for our accomplishments as an integrated seafood company in the aquaculture segment.

  • Fortune 500 India’s Largest Corporations

    Avanti Feeds presented with the award for Fortune 500 India’s Largest Corporations in December 2020. The company has been featured in this prestigious list since 2015.

  • India's Best CEO for 2020

    Dr. Indra Kumar Alluri, Chairman and MD, presented with the award for India’s Best CEO in 2020.

  • India's Most Trusted CEO in 2018

    Dr. Indra Kumar Alluri, Chairman and MD, presented with the award for India's most trusted CEO 2018.

  • Forbes India Leadership Awards Fila - 2017

    Dr. Indra Kumar Alluri, Chairman and MD, presented with the Forbes India Leadership Award 2017: Value Creator. The company won the award for creating a fast-growing company in a niche market, with a rise in stocks of 800% over a period of 3 years.

  • Forbes Asia’s 200 Best Under A Billion

    Dr. Indra Kumar Alluri, Chairman and MD, presented with the award for Asia’s 200 Best Under A Billion in Kuala Lumpur on November 2, 2015.
