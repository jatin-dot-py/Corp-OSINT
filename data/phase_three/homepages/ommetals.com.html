Our Business Verticals

With a diversified portfolio, we deliver expertise across Hydropower, Irrigation, Water Supply and Infrastructure(Real estate) projects, contributing to sustainable growth and development.

Hydropower

Specializing in turnkey hydro-mechanical solutions, we deliver end-to-end services for large and complex hydropower projects. Our expertise covers design, manufacture, supply, installation, and commissioning of critical equipment such as gates, hoists, and cranes, ensuring reliable and efficient operations for major power plants.

Irrigation

We execute comprehensive hydro-mechanical and civil solutions for irrigation infrastructure. Our projects span dams, canals, lift irrigation systems, and water conveyance channels—supporting improved water management and higher agricultural productivity in diverse regions.

Water Supply

As an active participant in the Jal Jeevan Mission, we develop rural water supply systems by constructing pipelines, pumping stations, reservoirs, and treatment plants. Our integrated approach guarantees safe and dependable drinking water, uplifting rural sanitation standards and community well-being.

INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT - Real Estate

We deliver quality-focused residential and commercial projects, combining superior construction, timely delivery, and modern amenities. Our developments foster vibrant urban communities, blending innovative design with sustainable infrastructure and reinforcing our position as value-driven real estate developers.
