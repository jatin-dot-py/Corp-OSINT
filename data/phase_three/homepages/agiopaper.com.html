Paper has been in existence for over 2,000 years and when one normally thinks of the origin of paper the first thing that comes to mind is Egypt and the River Nile. It is the valley of the river Nile where the marsh grass called Cyperous Papyrus thrived. The Egyptian cut the stem of the plant into thin strips and soaked in the water of the Nile to soften the stem which were then arranged in a mat form then pounded to form a thin sheet. The sheet was sun dried and was used to write on as it was light and was easy to carry around. The Egyptians, Greeks and Romans to record events, wrote spiritual texts and created works of art used these sheets. It is from the word papyrus that the word paper is derived.
