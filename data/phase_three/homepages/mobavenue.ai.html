Ishank Joshi

Founder, MD & CEO

Founder, MD & CEO

Under his leadership, the company has expanded across India, ASEAN, LATAM, the UK, the USA, and other regions, delivering advertising and marketing solutions to global clients. With a background in Electronics and Communications Engineering, Ishank began his entrepreneurial journey soon after college in 2010, building ventures in advertising and financial technologies. Notably, he co-founded and scaled Goals101, which was later acquired by M2P Fintech, and then joined Mobavenue as a Co-Founder.

With over 16 years of experience in B2B and technology platform sales, he has driven $100M+ in revenue and built high-performance entrepreneurial teams to scale various businesses. He has been recognized with the Businessworld 30 Under 30 award. In 2026, he was appointed to the MMA India Board, where he contributes alongside industry leaders to shape the future of marketing, media, and AI-driven innovation in India. Ishank is also an active angel investor, having backed 40+ startups across India and Asia, and mentors entrepreneurs on scaling and go-to-market strategies.

Looking ahead, he envisions Mobavenue AI Tech at the forefront of leveraging Generative AI & Machine Learning to help businesses grow digitally. In his leisure time, he enjoys traveling, spending time with his daughter, supporting fellow entrepreneurs, and reading about technology and global marketing trends.

Tejas Rathod

Founder & CTO

Founder & CTO

With a strong foundation in Computer Engineering, Tejas began his entrepreneurial journey at 17 by developing technology automation tools and launching an internet marketing agency before co-founding Mobavenue in 2017. Over the past 10+ years, he has helped build Mobavenue into a profitable, bootstrapped company, earning recognition in Forbes Asia 30 Under 30 (2023) and Businessworld 30 Under 30.

At Mobavenue, Tejas leads global technology and finance operations, driving innovation across the advertising and marketing ecosystem. He also serves as an AI Council Member at MMA India, contributing to industry discussions on the responsible adoption and advancement of artificial intelligence in marketing.

As an active angel investor, he supports emerging technology startups and remains a passionate advocate of programmatic advertising, AI automation, and a culture of experimentation. Frequently featured in leading publications and podcasts, Tejas continues to shape the future of digital advertising and technology-led innovation.

Kunal Kothari

Founder, Chairman & COO

Founder, Chairman & COO

With over 15 years of experience, his contributions to the industry and his role in building Mobavenue as a highly profitable, bootstrapped company have earned him notable recognition in Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia (Class of 2023) and Businessworld 30 Under 30.

With a degree in Information Technology, Kunal began his entrepreneurial journey at 17, building profitable commerce and software ventures that laid the foundation for his business acumen and leadership.

Kunal drives product-led growth and operational capabilities in media operations, delivering measurable business impact across sectors like BFSI, fintech, e-commerce, gaming, and more. He also led the AI Center of Excellence at Mobavenue, driving AI innovation across the company's products, platforms, and business operations. Under his leadership, the company has executed multi-million-dollar campaigns and built strategic media partnerships for leading brands.

As an active angel investor, he supports tech-enabled startups, fostering innovation in the digital ecosystem. Frequently featured in publications like Silicon India, CXO Outlook, and Businessworld, Kunal continues to shape the global conversation around growth marketing and entrepreneurship. Looking ahead, he envisions expanding emerging AI-enabled technologies and product-led growth from "Bharat to the World."
