About Us

Prime Industries Ltd. is a strategic investment and industrial holding company capitalising on big opportunities in critical national sectors. We back enterprises with unique technology know-how and help them grow into small giants within their niches. Focused on Defence, Nuclear, Robotics, and Advanced Manufacturing, we nurture companies that strengthen India’s industrial backbone and drive national self-reliance. At Prime, we go beyond capital — offering strategic direction, operational excellence, and partnerships that accelerate growth. Our mission is to build India’s strategic future through innovation, indigenisation, and industry leadership, in line with the nation’s Atmanirbhar Bharat vision.

Our Vision

Building India’s Strategic Future

We acquire, nurture, and scale promising enterprises in Defence, Nuclear, Aerospace, and Capital Goods, unlocking their full potential to drive national growth and self-reliance. Through strategic investments, deep industry partnerships, and hands-on operational support, Prime Industries Ltd. is shaping an ecosystem of innovation-led companies that power India’s industrial transformation. Our focus is on enabling advanced technologies, fostering indigenous manufacturing, and building sustainable capabilities that strengthen the nation’s strategic foundations for decades to come.

Our Mission

Where Strategic Vision Meets Industrial Strength
