ABOUT US

Chartered Capital and Investment Limited (“CCIL”) is a public limited company listed on BSE Limited and is engaged in, and may from time to time undertake, the business of investments and trading in securities, commodities including precious metals such as gold & silver and other financial instruments.

Through prudent investment strategies and efficient portfolio management, the Company seeks to optimise returns while maintaining a balanced approach to risk management. The Company is committed to creating long-term value for its stakeholders by leveraging market opportunities and ensuring disciplined deployment of its funds.
