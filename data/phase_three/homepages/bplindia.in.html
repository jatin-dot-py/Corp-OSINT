One Group. Global Impact.

Bharat Parenterals Limited stands today as the driving force shaping global impact — delivering quality, affordability, and excellence across continents. Through their own operations as well as distinct business models and market focus offered by its group companies - Innoxel & Varenyam Healthcare, Bharat Parenterals have strategically expanded their presence and cater to evolving global market needs with a shared vision for quality and global reach.
