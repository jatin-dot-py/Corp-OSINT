    CLASSIFIED 4 STAR RESORT IN SILVASSA

    Ras Resort by Treat is spread over ten sprawling acres of lush woodlands on the banks of the beautiful Daman Ganga River. Just 170 km from Mumbai or a 2 ½ hrs picturesque drive on NH-8, Ras Resort also has easy rail access from Mumbai, Surat,etc. with the nearest railhead at Vapi – a quick 18 km drive to our Resort.

    Silvassa enjoys a great cultural legacy being ruled by the Maratha and Kohli Dynasties, and its history as a Portuguese colony. It boasts of a striking rustic charm, lakes, gardens, museums, temples and proximity to the beaches at Daman. Explore the aura of picturesque surroundings and discover tranquillity in places where history is a living experience.

    Ras Resort by Treat is strategically located close to the major hubs of Silvassa, yet it is a haven away from the hustle and bustle of the city. Nature is truly at its best at Ras Resort, which makes it an ideal venue for residential conferences, relaxed vacations and destination weddings.

    HISTORY

    Ruled by the ancient Kohli Dynasty many centuries ago, the area was then annexed to the Maratha Empire. Dadra and Nagar Haveli (which constitutes of 72 villages) was later assigned to the Portuguese in 1779 in a peace treaty till its merger with the Union of India on August 11, 1961.

    Today the town is still unspoilt, despite the growth of industries and is soon being recognised as a n idyllic recluse to the humdrum of an hectic urban life.
