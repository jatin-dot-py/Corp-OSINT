About Kabsons

Kabsons Industries Limited was incorporated in 1993 and is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). The Company is engaged in LPG bottling across multiple locations in India.

Over the years, Kabsons has developed operational expertise in LPG handling, storage, bottling and distribution, catering to industrial and commercial requirements.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be India's most trusted LPG infrastructure company — delivering safe, reliable and compliant bottling solutions across every corner of the country.

Mission

To operate world-class LPG bottling and blending facilities with an unwavering commitment to safety, regulatory compliance and operational excellence — creating long-term value for our customers, partners and shareholders.

Our Legacy

Kabsons Industries Limited operates within a broader legacy of experience in the LPG sector that dates back to 1976, with early involvement in the development of LPG cylinders, appliances and gas equipment.

In 1985, PKL Limited (formerly Primus Kabsons Limited) was established through a technical collaboration with Primus AB, Sweden, introducing advanced Copper Brazed Cylinder technology and LPG appliances to the Indian market. Today, PKL manufactures LPG cylinders, gas equipment and LPG appliances for domestic and international markets from its facilities near Hyderabad.

Building on this foundation of LPG technology and manufacturing expertise, Kabsons Industries Limited works closely with PKL and operates LPG bottling infrastructure and specialised LPG blending facilities serving energy marketers and industrial sectors across India.
