GOKUL AGRO RESOURCES LTD.

Gokul Agro Resources Limited (GARL) incorporated in the year 2014, is one of the leading and fastest growing FMCG company engaged in production, distribution & exports of various Edible, Non-Edible oils & its derivatives & Feed Meals. GARL is an FSSC certified 22000:2024, internationally recognized company well respected and known for serving a comprehensive range of quality products.

Gokul Agro Resources Limited is a paramount company catering to market needs for edible and non-edible oils nationally and internationally. Our extensive marketing and distribution network is spread across the globe, amassing customers from different geographies.

OUR BUSINESS

POTENTIAL FOR EXCELLENCE IN EVERYTHING WE TOUCH.

Gokul Agro Resources Limited is a trustworthy name thriving in the FMCG industry. We operate in diverse business verticals like Refineries, Castor oil Derivatives, Seed Crushing and Oleo chemicals. For Gokul Agro Resources Ltd, this infinity holds the promise of limitless potential in everything we do.

As one of the leading manufacturers of edible, non-edible and allied products, our eyes are firmly set on realizing our potential by empowering our people, leveraging technology and providing top quality products with an aim of enriching the lives of those we touch.

Backed by our proficient teams and a dependable, direct supply of raw materials, our certified facilities are dedicated to producing products that adhere to the most stringent standards of quality, safety, and consistency. Our unwavering focus on excellence drives us to pursue continuous innovation throughout every stage of the process, leveraging rigorous research and development (R&D) practices and aligning with industry-leading benchmarks. This commitment not only ensures the highest levels of product assurance but also fosters long-lasting relationships with our valued clients.

At Gokul Agro, there is something for everyone. We bring a wide range of oils, such as Soyabean Oil, Kachi Ghani Mustard Oil, Sunflower Oil, Cottonseed Oil, Groundnut Oil, Ricebran Oil, Premium Vanaspati and Palmolein Oil to suit your cooking style and requirements. Explore this rich variety of cooking oil, deep frying oil and confectionary products under renowned brands like Vitalife, Richfield, Mahek, Pride, Biscopride, Puffpride and Zaika.
