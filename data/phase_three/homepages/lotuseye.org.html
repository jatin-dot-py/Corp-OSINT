Treatments

Advanced eyecare coupled with decades of expertise. Your eyes are safe with us.

SMILE PRO

SMILE PRO is the most advanced AI Enabled Refractive Surgery. Minimal & Precise, 10-Sec Laser & Multi-vision correction.

Know More   >

ReLEx SMILE

ReLEx SMILE is the most advanced form of LASIK. This flapless, painless, bladeless procedure is fully computer operated and highly precise.

Know More   >

Touchfree Laser

The Touch Free Laser method is very gentle on the eyes. With the help of the SCHWIND AMARIS Laser, your vision will be corrected safely without needing suction, flap, blade, or the use of alcohol.

Know More   >

Lasik Eye Surgery

LASIK is the most popular vision-correction treatment that has helped millions to live a glass-free life.

Know More   >

Epilasik

Lotus introduced Epi-LASIK in southeast Asia in 2004. It is an innovative vision correction procedure that can overcome the limitations of Lasik.

Know More   >

Supracor

Zyoptix Supracor is a German Technology Lasik procedure that primarily corrects presbyopia in the age group of 40+. It also corrects near, intermediate, and distance vision correction.

Know More   >

Robotic Femto Laser Cataract Surgery (LRCS)

LRCS is the most advanced form of cataract surgery that is Laser-assisted and semi-robotic. This procedure is safer and more reliable than traditional cataract surgery.
