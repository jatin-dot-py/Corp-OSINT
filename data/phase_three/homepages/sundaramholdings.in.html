TSF Investments (formerly Sundaram Finance Holdings) - A core
investment company of the TSF Group

TSF Investments’ is primarily involved in strategic investments in the auto component and manufacturing industries, backed by the TSF group’s knowledge and expertise in the sector. With a legacy spanning over a century, TSF group’s interest spans the automotive and financial service sectors. Through TSF Investments’ subsidiary, Sundaram Business Services Limited and its own operations, it also provides outsourced business processing and support services.
