HISTORY

Founded in 1992, AJAX is a global leader in Self-Loading Concrete Mixers, renowned for engineering excellence. Based in Bangalore, we provide 360° concreting solutions, from mixers to pumps. With advanced manufacturing facilities in Doddaballapur and Gowribidanur, we are committed to innovation and customer-centric excellence, defining our legacy.

AJAX  sets up its first factory to assemble SLCMs, Near Bengaluru.

HISTORY

Training

At AJAX, we prioritize workplace safety and efficiency. Our comprehensive training programs cover machine operation and maintenance, including classroom sessions at our Bangalore Training Centre, factory visits, and on-site training by our Service & Training Department. We've partnered with the Infrastructure Equipment Skill Council of India (IESC) to elevate India's workforce skills. Interested customers can schedule on-site training lasting 2-3 days, with facilities provided by the customer. Our commitment is to continually enhance skills for safer and more efficient practices.
