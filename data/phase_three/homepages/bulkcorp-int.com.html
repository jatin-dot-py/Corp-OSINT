Welcome To Bulk Corp International

Bulkcorp International is ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14000: 2015, ISO 45000:2018 and BRC-ISSUE VI, certified company by INTERTEK. We are one of the leading manufacturers of food grade bags with international standards clean room facility managed by professionals highly experienced globally, with range of packaging solutions like FIBC (Jumbo Bags), container liners which are currently used in various industries including fertilizer, cement, sand, food grains, sugar, cattle feed, fish meal, pulses, spices, dates, agro products, minerals, resin, polymers, rubber, etc.

A Trend-setting Legacy

A look at our timeline that has been definitive of our present and our future endeavors.

2016-17

Inception of Bulk Corp International, a BRC certified company focusing primarily on international Food Grade FIBC bags markets.

2018-19

With a rich experience of over 30 years and a vision to constantly stay ahead of the market, Umasree Texplast Group has mobilized major expansion projects and technology assimilation plans.

2022

We are proud to announce the appointment of Mr. Punit Gopalka as the first Indian to hold the chair of President of FIBCA (Flexible Intermediate Bulk Container Association) this year.

2024

Successful launch of Bulkcorp’s IPO (Initial Public Offer) on NSE (National Stock Exchange) Emerge Platform.
