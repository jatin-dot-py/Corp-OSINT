Frequently asked questions

The questions we are asked most often, answered with the figures from our own specifications rather than round numbers.

Which company makes lithium-ion batteries in India?

CLN Energy Limited manufactures lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) battery packs at its own plant in Noida, with more than 2 GWh of annual capacity. The company is listed on the BSE SME platform under scrip code 544347. What separates a manufacturer from an importer here is whose name is on the deployments: BSNL runs 22,864 CLN packs across its telecom network, RailTel carries 180 kWh of CLN backup behind the 100 kW UPS at its Pondicherry command control centre, and Southern Railway uses CLN batteries for signalling backup at Chennai. Packs are built and tested to UN 38.3, IEC 62619, IEC 61000, UL 1973 and UL 9540A, and the plant is certified to ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018. The company traded as JLNPhenix Energy before the rename.

How do you compare lithium-ion battery manufacturers in India?

Ask each one the same six questions, and compare the answers rather than the brochures. Who makes the pack, and is the plant theirs? What cell chemistry, and what is the cycle life at a stated depth of discharge and temperature? Which standards has the product been tested to, with reports for that product rather than a company statement? What is the warranty on the line you are buying? Where is the nearest service engineer, and how long are spares available? And which installations can you go and look at? CLN Energy answers all six on this site: the plant is our own at Noida, the chemistry is lithium iron phosphate, the standards and warranties are published by product line, and the projects pages carry the installations with their configurations.

Is CLN Energy a battery manufacturer or an importer?

A manufacturer. Cells are assembled into packs, modules and complete systems at our own 50,000 sq ft plant in Noida, which has more than 2 GWh of annual capacity, and the battery management systems, enclosures and testing are part of that work. It matters after the sale rather than before it: when a pack needs a warranty decision, a spare or an engineering answer, it comes from the company that built it instead of from an importer waiting on a supplier abroad.

Which lithium battery company in India supplies both EV and energy storage batteries?

CLN Energy builds both from the same plant. The e-mobility side covers e-rickshaw (L3), e-auto (L5), two-wheeler, forklift and golf cart packs with their chargers. The stationary side covers home and inverter batteries, telecom, data centre, commercial and industrial systems, and containerised BESS up to 5 MWh. Buying both from one manufacturer means one warranty process and one service channel rather than two.

What does CLN Energy manufacture?

Two lines. E-mobility covers e-rickshaw (L3), e-auto (L5), two-wheelers (L2), e-loaders, golf carts, material handling equipment and chargers. Stationary covers residential - including all-in-one solar units and grid-tie solar inverters - telecom, data centre, commercial and industrial, and grid and utility. In practice that runs from a 1.28 kWh lead-acid replacement up to a 5.015 MWh containerised system. The e-mobility packs are 51.2V, 60.8V and 64V in 105Ah to 200Ah; the telecom modules are 48V in 50Ah and 100Ah, built as 19-inch 3U rack units with RS485 so a site reports its own state of charge and health. Everything is lithium iron phosphate. Warranty runs from 36 months on e-mobility to 120 months on the 1 MWh BESS, because the duty cycles are not comparable.

Can I replace a lead-acid battery with a lithium-ion one?

Usually yes, and it is the most common reason customers get in touch. One point causes most of the confusion: a LiFePO4 cell is 3.2V, so packs land on multiples of it. Sixteen cells make 51.2V, which is the lithium equivalent of a four-block 48V lead-acid set; nineteen make 60.8V and twenty make 64V. So a search for a 48V battery lands on our 51.2V range, and the numbers differ because the chemistry differs, not because the pack is a different size. On the stationary side the surrounding equipment usually stays as it is - the BSNL programme replaced 22,864 sets in the existing 19-inch cabinets, and the RailTel changeover was completed inside a two-hour window without the centre coming off supply. Send us the existing setup and we will confirm rather than assume.

How long do LiFePO4 batteries last?

Cycle life is stated per model - from 3,000 rated cycles at 80% depth of discharge on the rack and stackable ranges, up to 6,000 on the all-in-one energy storage series. Warranty runs from 36 months on e-mobility packs to 120 months on the 1 MWh BESS. Two things are worth separating. Cycle life is an engineering rating measured at a stated depth of discharge, and the depth matters as much as the number - the same pack cycled shallower lasts longer. Warranty is a commercial term and a shorter one. For anything that earns daily, cycle life is the figure that decides the economics, because it sets how many times you buy a battery over the same period rather than what one costs. Ask us for the rating on the specific pack.

Are lithium batteries safe?

Chemistry decides most of it. CLN builds on lithium iron phosphate, which has a higher thermal runaway threshold than the nickel-based lithium chemistries used where energy density matters more than margin. The trade is physical size: an LFP pack is larger for the same energy, which is a fair exchange in a 19-inch rack or under a vehicle floor and a poor one in a phone. Every pack carries a battery management system handling protection and cell balancing, so the failure mode where a set is quietly ruined by overcharging is designed out. Packs are built and tested to UN 38.3 for transport, IEC 62619 for industrial cell and battery safety, IEC 61000 for electromagnetic compatibility, and UL 1973 and UL 9540A for stationary safety and thermal runaway. That matters most at an unstaffed site, where nobody is present to notice a fault.

Where is the CLN Energy manufacturing plant?

Noida. The plant is also the registered office, at Plot No. 18, Sector 140, Phase II, Gautam Buddha Nagar, Uttar Pradesh 201305. Annual capacity is more than 2 GWh from a 50,000 sq ft site, run by a workforce of more than 800, with service centres and service engineers across India. Sector 140 sits on the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway, which puts the plant within a routine drive of Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Delhi, Gurugram and Faridabad. That matters at two moments, and neither is when the order is placed. The first is when something needs looking at: a pack behaving oddly in Ghaziabad is a short drive for an engineer and a shipping problem for a supplier abroad. The second is warranty, which is worth what the company honouring it is worth. Factory visits are by appointment - it is a working production line, not a showroom.

How do I become a CLN Energy dealer or distributor?

Through the partner application - give your area, your current business and the volume you expect to handle, and the team will follow up. CLN already sells through a distributor network of more than 200 and over 100 business partners. Dealers, distributors and OEM buyers all start there, though they are three different conversations. A dealer is selling the existing range into a territory. A distributor is carrying stock and serving dealers beneath them. An OEM buyer needs packs built to their own vehicle or enclosure, and that is the case where a domestic manufacturer changes what is possible: a specification change is a conversation with the people building the pack rather than a change order sent abroad and waiting on a shipping schedule. Say which of the three you are in the first message and it saves a round trip.

More detail by application: telecom tower batteries, e-rickshaw and e-auto packs, 48V and 51.2V packs, replacing lead-acid, solar batteries and inverters, batteries for a home inverter, and the Noida plant.
