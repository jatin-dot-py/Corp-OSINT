Your Relationship Manager at Swastika first speaks with you to understands your risk profile, likes & dislikes. She then coordinates with our research team to recommend the best stocks to you. You do not need to spend any time to do any research.

Swastika regularly organizes Options trading workshops led by NISM certified analysts. Your Relationship Manager also keeps updating you with the recommended Options trading strategies and opportunities. We specialize in Index (Nifty/Bank Nifty) Options trading.
