Tamboli Industries Limited

The principal holding company of Tamboli Group

Tamboli Industries Limited

is the principal holding company and promoter of Tamboli Castings Limited (TCL). It holds 100 % equity share capital in its wholly owned subsidiary Tamboli Castings Limited.

The company through its associates has business interest in other products and investment in financial instruments.
The Company was listed on Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) on March 15, 2010 and trading under the scrip code 533170.

Management & Governance

The promoters have rich and vast experience in managing industries. Over the years, our philosophy is to ensure systematic and professional management, transparent and accountable policies, adherence to corporate governance norms and setting high benchmarks for business vision and objectives to protect the interest of stakeholders, employees, customers, vendors, regulators and society.

Tamboli Industries Limited was incorporated in 2008 with a view to carry on industrial business trade activities by investments in various ventures and projects by way of capital, loan, equity participation and financial assistance.
