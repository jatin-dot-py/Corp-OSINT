      ABOUT US

      Engineered for Excellence,Driven by Innovation

      Automobile Corporation of Goa Ltd. (ACGL), the first major engineering unit to be set up in Goa, a tiny but picturesque state of India, was jointly promoted in 1980 by Tata Motors Ltd (formerly known as Tata Engineering & Locomotive Co. Ltd.), the largest automobile manufacturer in the country and EDC Ltd (formerly known as Economic Development Corporation of Goa, Daman & Diu Ltd.)

      What our satisfied clients are saying

      I am the manager at Royal Holidays India, located in Panjim. We have offices in both Calangute and Panjim, and we operate a fleet of coaches. Our services cater to foreign tourists, Indian tourists, student groups, and corporate clients. We have been using ACGL coaches for our operations, and we are satisfied with the quality and performance of the vehicles. We look forward to continuing our association with ACGL for future fleet needs.

      Mr. Anil Patil Royal Holidays India

      I am Daryl Nunes, a partner at Nunes Travels. We are coach operators and fleet operators in Goa. We have been working with ACGL and having our bus bodies built by them since 2000. Over time, we have built several coaches with them. We are impressed with the quality of the work they do on our coaches, particularly the strong structures, which have never given us any problems. We use these coaches for our inbound tourists, that is, the foreign tourists who visit Goa. We also supply these coaches to major five-star hotels in Goa, who are our clients, and the feedback about the quality of ACGL coaches is always impressive. In terms of recommendations for ACGL, I would suggest that they focus on modernizing the design of their coaches to keep up with current trends. This will help them achieve even greater success, especially in the tourism industry, which demands modern-looking coaches and modern facilities. Thank you.

      Mr. Darryl Nunes Nunes Travels

      I am the owner of Rocket Travels. I have been in the bus business for 40 years, and for the first time, I had the bus bodywork done at ACGL. I had the bus of Jupiter model built with ACGL, and is now 8 years old. There have been no issues to date. There have been minor issues, but whenever we ask, they come here to repair them. If there's not enough time, we go to them for repairs and bring the vehicle back. Now that 8 years have passed, there are no problems with the vehicles. These are two vehicles for which I had the bodywork done at the same time. If I get a good opportunity, I plan to give two more vehicles to ACGL. I would like to thank them for continuing to do excellent bodywork like this.

      Mr. Sudesh Kalangutkar Rocket Travels

      My name is Lume John Fernandes, and I am the proprietor of Royal Holidays India. We were established in 1991, and we have been operating coaches throughout Karnataka and Goa. Our trips include destinations such as Hampi, Badami, Bijapur, and Udupi. We have mostly sourced our vehicles from ACGL, and their buses are in excellent condition. The bodies they build are well-constructed and ideal for long trips. We have been working with them since the year 2000, and we continue to do so. Whenever we hire vehicles, we always choose SML, especially when the body is built by ACGL. Their buses are comfortable, strong, durable, and their service is excellent. Thank you.

      Mr. Lume J. Fernandes Royal Holidays India

      I am the owner of Raj Tours and Travels in Verna, Goa, and our employee transportation company is also based in Verna. We handle this transportation by having the bus bodies made by ACGL. We have been getting our bus bodies built by ACGL for the last 15 years, and to date, we have not faced any issues. The performance of the buses is also excellent, whether it’s the seating arrangement or the overall build. We receive great service, and the buses are ready even within 45 days, even in emergency situations. There have been no issues with the employees either. They enjoy the ride, and there are no problems with the seats or anything else while traveling. Additionally, if we need any spare parts or anything else, we have a contact person at ACGL. We simply call him, and he arranges everything. For example, last time when one of our bus windows broke, we contacted them, and they arranged for the part and fitting after a short while. We receive excellent service, and we are grateful to ACGL for that.

      Mr. Rajesh Bandodkar Raj Tours and Travels

      Hi, my name is Paul Fernandes, and we own a company called Fernandes Tours and Travel. Our business primarily focuses on handling FITs, corporate arrivals and departures, and local tours in Goa. ACGL is our bus body-building partner for many years, we have trusted them with the construction of numerous buses. The quality of service and the quality of the buses they build are exceptional. The materials they use, including the seats, AC systems, overall structure, and flooring, are excellent quality. ACGL consistently delivers buses that meet our expectations. Additionally, the ACGL staff are thorough professionals from start to finish. They take care of all our requirements, delivering buses on time and providing excellent after-service. If we face any issues, they attend to them promptly and ensure timely resolution. We should be proud of Goa, as it is home to one of the best bus body-building companies, ACGL. They deliver bus bodies worldwide, and in terms of their work and quality, we can confidently say that they are the best. We have been working with them for many years and have firsthand experience of the excellent quality of their work and their outstanding after-service. I highly recommend ACGL as the best bodybuilder in Goa, and we are proud that they are a Goan company delivering bus bodies worldwide. Thank you so much.

      Mr. Paul Fernandes Fernandes Tours and Travel
