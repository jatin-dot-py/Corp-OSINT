About US

We Create Your Perfect Suit

Bannari Amman Spinning Mills Limited, is a vertically integrated textile company engaged in the manufacture of cotton yarn, woven and knitted fabrics, home textiles and wind power generation .The company was incorporated in the year 1989 and issued shares to the public in the year 2007. The company shares its roots with the Bannari Amman group of companies which has diverse interests spanning various industries in South India.
