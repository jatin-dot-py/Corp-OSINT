Journey

A story of helping fight diseases better

See our entire journey
1907

Started manufacturing tinctures and alcohol at Vadodara

1940

Began production of cough syrups, vitamins, tonics and sulphur drugs

1961

Inaugurated Penicilin plant with Lal Bahadur Shastri in attendance

1967

Initiated bulk manufacturing of Vitamin B12

1971

Manufactured Erythromycin for the first time in Indian history

1972

Launched Althrocin, a brand of Erythromycin

1997

Althrocin moves to become top selling brand in India

1999

Kickstarted the production of synthetic, organic API

2000

Received ISO 14000 certification for facilities at Vadodara

2001

Began production of Cephalosporin C

2007

Acquired non-oncology business of M/s Dabur Pharma Ltd.

2009

Addressed chronic therapies through multiple marketing divisions

2011

Demerged from Alembic group to form Alembic Pharmaceutical Ltd, and gets listed

2012

Unveiled the Dematology division in domestic market with 8 products

2013

Started first NDA with a partner and commenced filing in EU, Australia and Brazil

2015

Launched Aripiprazole and established the US Front-end as also transitioning to in-house marketing in the US

2016

Struck a JV with Orbicular – Aleor Dermaceutical Limited to develop dermatology products for the international market

2017

Inaugurated the manufacturing facility for Oncology medicines, including oral solids and liquid injectable vials, in the presence of the Hon’ble Chief Minister of Gujarat, Shri. Vijay Rupani

2017

Acquired Orit Laboratories LLC, USA

2018

Received FDA approvals for Aleor’s dermatology facility

2019

Granted approvals for Oncology oral solid facility

Entered a JV to foray into the China market

2020

Achieved the milestone of US$ 250 million sales in the US frontend
