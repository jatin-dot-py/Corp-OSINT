About Us

Liotech Industries Limited,(formerly known as liotech industries private limited) Located in Shapar Sr. No. 269 P 2, New Sr. No. 464, Plot No 21, Kotdasanagani, Shapar, Rajkot, Gujarat, India, 360024 Gujarat was established on 17th june 2020 as the study of experts appointed by the promoters at various levels. The company was established to supply the Hardware Accessories. This company will carter the product for the design, detailing, fabrication and erection of all the accessories. It is equipped with latest designing and detailing resources to ensure the accuracy of the product in addition to expertise of the employees. Our plant has all the available machines and equipments, which is necessary in manufacturing the structures with the factor of quality assurance. Liotech Industries Private Limited is able to design and fabricate different types of hardware structures. It is currently operated on B2B operation model to providing the needs of our different customers.
