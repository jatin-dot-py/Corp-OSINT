    About us

    Nourishing Lives. Powering Innovation.

    Sarda Proteins, a proud member of the Onix Group, is engaged in the sourcing, processing, trading, and distribution of premium agricultural products, dry fruits, nuts, seeds, pulses, and food ingredients, serving customers across domestic and international markets with a strong commitment to quality, reliability, and sustainability. Alongside our agri-business operations, we are actively involved in the trading and supply of solar cells and related technologies, supporting the growing demand for clean and sustainable energy solutions. Driven by innovation, excellence, and responsible business practices, Sarda Proteins is dedicated to creating long-term value through superior products, trusted partnerships, and sustainable growth.
