The services we offer

The Company’s primary activity entails the repair of different type of seagoing Vessels. The team of technicians consist of competent certified welders and hydraulic fitters who are available 24×7 to attend any type of repair. Each of them have 25 years of ship repair expertise in carrying out:

Propeller hub overhaul

Shaft removal & renewal

Stern thruster overhauls

Major day dockings

Bow thruster

The Company has been engaged in the Manufacture and Marketing of Fishing Nets for over 3 decades and is catering to the needs of large number of fishermen throughout the Indian sub-continent.

The Company has an extremely sound marketing network for its products.

A Legacy of Innovation in Fishing Net Manufacturing Since 1975

Garware Marine Industries Limited was promoted by the Late Padma Bhushan Shri. B. D. Garware, founder of the Garware Group of Companies.

Garware Marine Industries Limited (GMIL) is a Public Limited Company incorporated in 1975 as Modern Nets Ltd. The name was subsequently changed to Garware Marine Industries Ltd. in 1981. The shares of the Company are listed on The Bombay Stock Exchange Limited.
