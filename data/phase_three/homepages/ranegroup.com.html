A Trusted Supplier to Leading OEMs Worldwide

Rane Group is a preferred supplier of safety-critical automotive components to some of the world’s most respected OEMs and Tier 1. The company’s portfolio spans steering and suspension systems, brake components, engine components, occupant safety systems and light metal castings, all engineered to deliver reliability, durability and superior performance.

With over five decades of manufacturing expertise, Rane Group partners across the global mobility ecosystem, supporting OEMs through innovation, quality, and operational excellence. Backed by advanced facilities, strong R&D, and long-term technology collaborations, it delivers an integrated value chain from concept, design, validation and manufacturing.

A Legacy of Innovation and Leadership

1929-1958 Distribution

Founded in 1929 as an auto & spare parts distributor, incorporated as a public company in 1936

1959-1986 Manufacturing

Started manufacturing IC valves, tie rod ends, friction materials, and steering gears

1987-2000 Collaboration

Expanded product portfolio through technical collaborations

2000-2014 Operational Excellence

3 Deming Grand Prizes, 5 Deming Prizes; added HP die casting, EPS & airbags

2015 - Present Expanding Horizons

Merged listed subsidiaries, added steering wheels and established global footprint in Mexico

Engineering a Sustainable Future

Focused on minimising environmental impact and enhancing long-term value, the Group continues to advance initiatives that support cleaner processes, efficient resource management and safer workplaces.
