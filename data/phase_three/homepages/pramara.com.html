Tang Sipper
Mondelēz International wanted to establish Tang as a healthier substitute to aerated drinks for consumers of all ages. The brand wished to educate its customers about the simpler manner of fixing a Tang instant drink mix. Pramara designed a one-litre plastic bottle that doubled as a packaging for four 125 g packs of the fruit-flavoured drink mix. The sipper became synonymous with the hassle-free drink-making process, Tang cemented its position as the healthier alternative and recorded higher sales.

Pringles Bluetooth Speaker

Popular saddle-shaped chips brand Pringles is globally identified for its striking tube-stacked packaging. Kellogg’s approached Pramara to devise a functional promotional product targeting millennials and also adding value to the can. True to the brand personality and promo theme, Pramara ingeniously created a Bluetooth speaker that could be affixed on top of the can.

It was a chip shot enticing people to get their hands on the Pringles can that came mounted with a wireless gadget. This added functionality by Pramara was a massive hit across global markets in South Africa, Philippines, Singapore, Japan, Taiwan, Australia/NZ, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia and Korea.

Handyneb

Life is as much about fun merchandise as it is about practical and life-supporting products. Pramara absolutely agrees to this premise which is why we have in our repertoire products like Handyneb.

It is a nebuliser which converts liquid medicine into vapour, mist or aerosol so that it can be inhaled directly into the air tubes or lungs. This is an invaluable product for little ones who just cannot use any other inhalation device and also for old people who are unable to administer inhalers. Having our nebulizer at home is a psychological support to the patient and a reminder that effective help is at hand.
