THE BEGINNING OF AN ERA

True Story: Our Journey into Digital Textile Printing Machines Supplier in India
Founded in Surat by four friends driven by a shared vision, True Colors pioneered the first sublimation textile printer in India. From small setbacks and floods to breakthrough innovations, our perseverance and commitment to quality established us as a trusted provider of digital textile printing machines in India—now headquartered in Surat.

Listing Ceremony of True IPO

We are thrilled to announce the successful listing of True Colors on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). This momentous occasion marks a significant milestone in our journey, highlighting our commitment to excellence and innovation in the digital textile printing industry. The official bell-ringing ceremony was a proud moment, celebrated with key stakeholders, family, and friends. With this listing, True Colors embarks on an exciting new chapter, strengthening our position in the market and paving the way for continued growth and success.

FABRICS

Our premium fabrics are designed to unlock the true potential of digital printing. Carefully sourced and expertly prepared, they ensure vibrant colors, crisp detailing, and long-lasting durability.
