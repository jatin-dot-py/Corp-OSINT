Our Unique Propositions

Extensive Variety

With more than 500 unique toys in our portfolio, we cater to every child’s interest and developmental stage.

Made in India

We are happy that we manufacture all of our toys in India, where we support local artisans and women workers.

Safety First

We use the best safety standards to ensure that all of our toys are made from non-toxic, kid-safe materials.

Educational and Fun

In addition to providing kids with entertainment, our toys are designed to foster mental growth and improve their creative abilities.

Sustainable and Eco-friendly

Compliance with ecological norms is a vital requirement for our company; we use only eco-friendly materials during toy manufacturing.

Innovative Designs

Our toys combine centuries-old artistry with modern technology to create unique, engaging and exceptional play toys.

Durability and Quality

Our toys stand out from other brands because they are built to last and are composed of materials that are durable enough to withstand any amount of rough play by the children.

Affordability

We provide premium toys that meet international quality standards at competitive costs without compromising quality.

Innovative Play Concepts

Our toys incorporate the latest technology and play research, to help kids develop their imagination and problem-solving abilities.

Distributor and Customer Satisfaction

We make sure to have a staff committed to providing excellent customer service as part of our satisfaction promise.
