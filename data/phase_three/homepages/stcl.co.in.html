Late Shri Surendra Savai was the Founder Chairman of S&T Corporation Ltd. He spearheaded as the Emirate President for Federation of Association of Maharashtra (FAM) which works towards solving grievances of multiple associations with the Government of India. He was also appointed as Management Committee Member of one of the oldest trade body of India - Indian Merchants Chambers of Commerce and Industry (IMC).

Born in 1939, Late Shree Surendra Savai was honoured with various awards and recognitions for his selfless service to the society and trade organizations. He was elected as President of Mahajan Committee for 30 consecutive years. He started his career in the textile business in the early fifties. In 1985, with the establishment of Rajyog Group of Companies, he finally embarked his entrepreneurship journey by foraying into the Indian Real Estate.

Mr. Ajay Savai is the Managing Director of S & T Corporation Ltd, Ssavai - Smart Abodes and Partner in Rajyog Group of Companies. He is one of the leading property developers in Mumbai. Over the last three decades he together with its promoters has developed over 1 million sq. ft. of commercial & residential spaces in Maharashtra & Goa.

Mr. Ajay Savai joined the property development business in 1990, at the age of 25. He has been at the helm of affairs for the company and seen the vision for a world-class property development organization, both in terms of size and excellence and transformed it into reality. Starting his career as the liaison executive in the organization, Ajay managed the company during the most challenging and exciting periods in the history of the real estate industry. His expertise and experience permeate the entire spectrum of the property development scenario in Western Region of India.

With the Introduction of Ssavai - Smart Abodes, he aims at disrupting the construction Industry with leading technology and superior quality home at affordable prices.
