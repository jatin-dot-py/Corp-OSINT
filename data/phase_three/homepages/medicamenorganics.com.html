About Medicamen

MEDICAMEN ORGANICS LIMITED!

Medicamen was established in year 1995 and having more than 30 years of experience in pharmaceutical manufacturing. We are engaged in manufacturing of pharmaceutical dosage form such as Tablets, Capsules, Oral Liquids, and Ointments. We are having two WHO-GMP Certified Plant in Haridwar. We are associated with many domestic and international companies such as Morepen, Anglo French, Elder, Bestochem, Shreya Lifesciences, Wipro, Win Medicare, Jagsonpal, Klitch Drugs, Tablet India Limited, Mission Pharma, SH Pharmaceuticals, Knoll, Healing, May & Baker, Emami and many more for manufacturing of their goods on contract basis and on Loan License basis. Our company is also engaged in exporting to African, CIS and SARRC countries and are registered in 13 countries. Our Plant 1 is meant for Iron tablets, and it is WHO GMP approved. Plant 2 is having facility non-Beta lactam Tablets, Capsules and Oral Liquids and ointments, it is also WHO GMP Approved
