ABOUT US

About Sundaram Multi Pap Limited

Sundaram’s journey in paper stationery began in 1985, with a vision to provide dependable, quality and affordable stationery for students and consumers.

Building on this foundation, Sundaram Multi Pap Limited was incorporated on 13 March 1995.

Today, Sundaram brings together over four decades of experience in paper stationery, an integrated manufacturing facility in Palghar, an established distribution network and a growing presence across India.

Our broad product portfolio serves students, educational institutions, professionals, offices and consumers across a wide range of paper stationery categories.
