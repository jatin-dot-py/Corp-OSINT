Swasti Vinayaka Synthetics Limited

Known for the quality of its suiting and shirting, Swasti Vinayaka Synthetics Limited is counted amongst the best in the business, supplying its product directly to big retail chains, garment exporters, respected clothing brands and corporate houses. With the state-of-the-art technology on its side, a qualified and experienced team and an extensive network of agents as well as dealers, the company is all set to take the industry by storm and initiate a revolution in the fabric and apparel sector. The company is also well established in high-end ladies ethnic and formal wear, with a boutique at The Grand Hyatt Plaza, Santacruz (E), Mumbai under the brand name “Ivy League”.
