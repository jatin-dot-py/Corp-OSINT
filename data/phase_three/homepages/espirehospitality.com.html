    Espire Hospitality Limited

    OUR JOURNEY

    Espire Hospitality’s journey began in 1993, when our esteemed Founder Chairperson, Late Mrs. Sadhana Rai, established Country Inn Hotels & Resorts. With a clear vision and a deep-rooted passion for hospitality, Mrs. Rai set out to create a brand that would redefine family holidays in India. Her dedication and leadership were instrumental in the early success of the brand, which initially operated resorts in some of the most beautiful destinations, including Bhimtal, Jim Corbett, Mussoorie, and Kosi. Under her guidance, we also expanded to manage leased properties in Amritsar and Badaun.
