  • Logistics
    Kaushalya Logistics Limited is operating as Clearing and Forwarding agents for major cement Companies like Dalmia Cement Bharat Limited. All operations on behalf of the customer after dispatch from production facilities are handled by the company. The company works on asset light model, wherein it uses warehouses owned / rented by customer only. Embracing a technology-enabled model, the company prioritizes flexibility, enabling the exploration and customization of alternatives tailored to the specific needs of its customers.
  • E-Commerce
    Kaushalya Logistics Limited is a diamond-category seller on a major online e-commerce platform. The company efficiently manages relationships with key suppliers of electronic and white goods across India, operating in 17 states. KLL has secured agreements with authorized distributors, allowing them to purchase products in bulk at the best prices with significant discounts. Sales invoicing is outsourced, and goods are supplied through platform-managed entities. The e-commerce platform handles collections and compensates KLL after deducting applicable charges and commissions.
  • Commercial Real Estate
    Kaushalya Logistics Limited owns 18 shops in a commercial project in Udaipur, which are rented out to leading retail tenants, providing the company with a steady stream of income. The company plans to expand its presence in this commercial real estate business line.
