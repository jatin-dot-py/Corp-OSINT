A Legacy of Mobility Excellence

Azad India Mobility Limited (AIML) builds upon a rich heritage spanning over 45 years in passenger bus manufacturing, spearheaded by industry veterans, Mr. Bhupinder Singh Chadha and Mr. Charan Singh Chadha.

Mr. Bhupinder Singh Chadha, recognized as the ‘Bus-man of India,’ pioneered the introduction and manufacturing of Volvo buses in India, demonstrating a visionary approach to transportation.

A Legacy of Excellence – 45+ Years of Experience

We Are Here to Make
You Thrive

India’s electric bus market is witnessing unprecedented growth, driven by government initiatives promoting clean energy. The National Electric Bus Program aims to deploy 50,000 electric buses by 2027, while the PM E-Bus Sewa scheme targets 10,000 e-buses in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. These efforts reinforce India’s commitment to reducing carbon emissions and achieving net-zero targets by 2070.

Azad India Mobility Ltd. plays a crucial role in this transition by delivering state-of-the-art electric buses designed for efficiency, performance, and sustainability. With 92% of the public transport sector operated by private players, our solutions are shaping the future of India’s urban mobility landscape.
