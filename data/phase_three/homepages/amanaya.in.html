Welcome To Amanaya Ventures Limited

India’s Trusted Name in Pure Gold & Silver Bullion!

At Amanaya Ventures Limited, we are redefining how India buys, saves, and trades precious metals. Built on a foundation of trust, transparency, and innovation, we operate as a BSE SME-listed company and the proud creators of Aurel – Forever Yours, a bullion brand celebrated for its purity, authenticity, and uncompromising quality.
