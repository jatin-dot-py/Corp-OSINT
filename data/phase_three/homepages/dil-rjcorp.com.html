ABOUT US

Established in 1991, Devyani International Limited (DIL) is one of India’s largest and fastest growing Chain Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) operators in the country

As on December 31, 2024, DIL operates more than 2,030 stores across brands in over 280 cities in India, Thailand, Nigeria and Nepal. DIL is an integral growth engine for RJ Corp, a Conglomerate that today is a powerhouse multinational with thriving businesses in beverages, fast-food restaurants, retail, ice-cream, dairy products, healthcare and education.

DIL is India’s largest franchisee for Yum Brands, operating KFC and Pizza Hut outlets, and the exclusive franchisee for Costa Coffee cafes in the country. In addition, DIL has developed its own brands, including Vaango, a popular South Indian vegetarian food destination, and The Food Street, a food court concept featuring multiple brands under one roof. DIL also has a significant presence in Indian airports, offering a wide range of food and beverage options.

Disclaimer

It has come to our attention that certain unscrupulous individuals and entities are impersonating Devyani International Limited and its senior officials, misusing our company's name, identity, and online presence to perpetrate fraudulent schemes. These fraudsters are targeting innocent individuals by falsely offering:

  • Investment schemes promising high returns.
  • Work-from-home opportunities requiring financial commitments.

We categorically state that Devyani International Limited does not engage in any investment schemes, nor do we solicit funds for investments or offer returns on investments.

We do not offer any paid work-from-home opportunities or demand money from individuals in connection with such opportunities.

Any communication, message, or offer claiming to represent Devyani International Limited in this regard is fraudulent and unauthorized. We strongly urge the public to exercise caution and verify the authenticity of any such claims.

We have lodged a formal police complaint with the Police Station, Gurugram, Haryana, regarding these fraudulent activities. The complaint includes detailed evidence such as WhatsApp messages, fraudulent websites, Telegram groups, and bank transaction proofs. The matter is under investigation, and legal actions are being pursued.

Public Advisory:

  • Do not respond to unsolicited messages or calls claiming to represent Devyani International Limited.
  • Do not transfer money to unknown individuals or accounts.
  • Verify official communication by contacting us directly through our official website or customer care.

Report Fraud:

If you encounter any suspicious communication or fraudulent activity, please report it immediately to your nearest police station and notify us at info@dil-rjcorp.com

We remain committed to safeguarding the public and our reputation. Let us work together to prevent such scams and protect innocent individuals from falling victim to these fraudulent schemes.
