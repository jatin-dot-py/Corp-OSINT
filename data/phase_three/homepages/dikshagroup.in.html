About Us

Our Legacy of Quality and Innovation

Since 1998, Diksha Polymers Pvt Ltd has been a pioneer in the manufacturing of high-quality PET jars and bottles. Under the visionary leadership of Mr. Vivek Mandelia and Mr. Vipin Mandelia, our company has grown to become the largest manufacturer of PET bottles and jars in the Gwalior region. Our commitment to innovation and quality has earned us an unmatched brand reputation across various industry segments.
