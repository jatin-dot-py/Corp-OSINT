E&C

Solutions for Energy

The energy sector is witnessing surging demands owing to the rapid development of our country. However, effective implementation remains the single largest obstacle in the power sector. Our E&C division augments our power sector capabilities with end to end implementation of power generation projects on a turnkey basis. This lays one of the main pillars of elevating our nation to greater heights and conformity in the world of tomorrow.

E&C Division-Engineering the Future

Our E&C division offers a single point solution to the execution of power projects from 'Concept to Commissioning' including project development, project engineering, construction & commissioning for its clients.

Strength of Execution

We have a proven track record of execution and commissioning of projects within budgeted cost and timeframe. Reliance Infrastructure with its affiliates and sister companies in the Reliance Group, own and operate over 6500 MW of generation capacity in the country.
