Udyami Sahyog Parishad

Western Overseas is pleased to announce a strategic MoU with Udyami Sahyog Parishad to foster cooperation, collaboration, and joint initiatives aimed at preparing individuals from India for global career opportunities.

This partnership, led by Mr. Virender Kr. Bhardwaj, Founder and President of Udyami Sahyog Parishad and Mr. Pardeep Balyan, CMD of Western Overseas, is designed to equip aspiring candidates with the vocational skills, international standards, and guidance needed to succeed in overseas job markets.

Through specially designed short-term courses, the collaboration will make candidates job-ready for employers in abroad, ensuring they meet global expectations and standards.

Together, Western Overseas and Udyami Sahyog Parishad are committed to empowering India’s youth — building a skilled, confident, and globally competitive workforce prepared for international success.

Collab with SR Institute

Western Overseas is proud to announce its partnership with SR Institute (SR Education Society) through a strategic MoU to establish a Skill Development Center dedicated to preparing individuals from India for global career opportunities. This collaboration between Mr. Ayush Gupta from SR Education Society and Mr. Pardeep Balyan, CMD of Western Overseas, focuses on equipping aspiring candidates with essential vocational skills, international standards, and the right guidance to succeed abroad. Through specially designed short-term courses, the initiative aims to make candidates job-ready for both local employers and overseas markets. Together, we are committed to empowering India’s youth, building a skilled, confident, and globally competitive workforce.
