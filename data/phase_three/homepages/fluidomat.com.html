03 Variable speed · Energy saving

Slow the fan, save the energy bill.

In centrifugal machines, power demand falls with the cube of speed. By varying oil fill through a sliding scoop tube, a Fluidomat SC coupling gives stepless speed control on a rugged squirrel-cage motor — replacing wasteful throttle control and earning money back through energy savings.

  • No-load motor start — starting current decays in seconds
  • 10–40% power saving on typical fan & pump duties
  • Continuous declutching with the motor still running

10 Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A fluid coupling transmits power between a driving and driven shaft through oil rather than mechanical contact. An impeller accelerates the oil, which drives a turbine runner — giving soft starts, overload protection, vibration damping and (in variable-speed types) stepless speed control, all with no wearing parts in the drive path.

For fans and pumps, power demand falls with the cube of speed. A variable-speed scoop coupling controls flow by reducing speed instead of throttling — saving 10–40% of energy. Fluidomat couplings are recognised as energy-saving devices by the Government of India.

Choose a constant-fill (fixed-speed) coupling when you need soft starting, motor protection and load sharing at constant running speed. Choose a variable-speed scoop coupling when the process benefits from speed control — typically fans, pumps and slurry duties. Our engineers will recommend the right type for your duty.

Fluidomat couplings serve industrial and automotive drives up to 6000 KW — fans, pumps, conveyors, crushers, mills, shredders and IC-engine drives across power, cement, steel, metals, paper, fertilizer, chemical and port industries.
