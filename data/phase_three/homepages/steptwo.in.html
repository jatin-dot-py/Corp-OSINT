Welcome to STEP TWO CORPORATION LIMITED
Step Two is a registered Non- Banking Financial Company, a firm based at Kolkata, actively participating in the securities market. Also, our scope of activities includes financial Consultancy Services market. Also, our scope of activities includes Financial Consultancy Serving in this field for about a decade. We are a Public Limited Company quoted with Bombay Stock Exchange, Calcutta Stock Exchange, Ahemedabad Stock Exchange and Jaipur Stock Exchange. The Company was incorporated as a Private Limited Company on 25th November, 1994 by the name of Step Two Finance Private Limited. The Company, gradually, converted into a Public Limited company with a Fresh Certificate of Incorporation issued on 7th August, 1995. Subsequently, the company came out with its IPO and was listed with the Bombay Stock Exchange.
 
Thus, the Company becomes a Public Limited Company with the change in name as Step Two Corporation Limited and a fresh Certificate of Incorporation consequent to the change in name was issued on 24th December, 1996.
 
Our Board of Directors comprises of qualified professionals and eminent business personalities/ industrialists under whose direct guidance and supervision our Company's activities are being conducted. The wide industry and money market experience and strong research abilities are the cornerstone of our ability to serve you.
