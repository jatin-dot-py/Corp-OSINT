Veejay Lakshmi Engineering Works Limited

Veejay Lakshmi Engineering Works Limited was established in 1974, Coimbatore, India. Veejay Lakshmi is the pioneers in the manufacture of Two for one Twister in India and have sold more than 7000 machines in India and in export market. Veejay Lakshmi also manufactures Precision Assembly Winders. The Two for one Twisters are produced at its factory at Sengalipalayam, Coimbatore 641 022.

Textile Division: Veejay Lakshmi also has a textile division with a spinning mill of capacity of around 17904 spindles. It is equipped with machines to produce knitted fabrics and twisting machines to sell the yarn with value addition. A substantial portion of the yarn produced is exported. The Textile Division is located at Thekkampatti in Mettupalayam, Coimbatore District.

The Company also has wind mills installed at Kethanur (Tirupur District), Sinjuwadi (Coimbatore District) and Irukanthurai and Radhapuram (Tirunelveli District). The total Capacity is 4.05 MW. The Textile unit also has roof top solar panels for a capacity of 1 MW.
