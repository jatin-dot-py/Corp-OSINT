Our Products

Transformer
Special Transformers

Counted among advanced transformer companies, TARIL’s special transformers—like converter duty variants—are tailored for motor drives, featuring multi-pulse windings, snubber circuits, and harmonic mitigation, with options for copper/aluminum windings, sealed designs, and ester or mineral oil insulation.

Transformer
Furnace Transformers

Furnished by top transformer manufacturing companies, TARIL’s furnace transformers deliver high secondary currents, wide output regulation, and integrated arc-stabilizing series reactors. Available in single- or three-phase variants, they feature built-in snubbers, LV cooling options, and tailored oil–water heat exchangers.

Transformer
Power transformer

As one of the leading Indian transformer manufacturers, TARIL delivers high-performance power transformers up to 1200 kV and 1000 MVA, engineered for reliability, efficiency, and long service life across utilities, industries, and infrastructure projects around the globe.

Transformer
Reactors

TARIL’s shunt reactors enhance power system efficiency by absorbing reactive power in high-voltage lines. Offered up to 135 MVAr (765 kV), they come in single or three-phase designs, with options for variable ratings, ester filling, and fast delivery.

Transformer
Rectifier Transformers

As a prominent power transformer manufacturer, TARIL’s rectifier transformers bridge AC grids and DC converters with precision. Featuring in-built snubber circuits, configurable LV terminals, and efficient oil-to-water heat exchangers, they're engineered to handle high harmonic currents in processes like electrolysis and metal smelting.

Transformer
Distribution Transformers

Recognized among top transformer rectifier manufacturers, TARIL offers robust distribution transformers ranging from 250KVA to 10,000KVA and up to 33 kV, designed for efficient energy delivery, low losses, and reliable performance across residential, commercial, and industrial applications.
