Starch Manufacturers

Established in 1941, Sayaji Industries Ltd. or SIL found its humble beginnings in Ahmedabad as a corn wet milling unit with a modest corn crushing capacity of one ton/day, catering our own textile units & others located in the city.

Core of Sayaji

We are constantly in search of more and more ways to analyze corn and add something to its range of applications. 

Paper & Gypsum

Maize Starch is used in paper industry for sizing as well as to increase the paper strength. The purpose of size press application is to improve appearance and erasability ink penetration and form a hard firm surface for writing or Printing, to produce surface fiber picking, and to prepare the sheet for subsequent coating. 

Textiles

Maize Starch is used in textile industry to provide stiffness & to add weight to clothes. Our range of modified starches are extensively used in textile application starting from yarn sizing to surface sizing of clothes. Our textile products have appropriate viscosity, penetration power and adequate coating efficiency. Our starch gives excellent performance and this has been our back bone for the last 75 years.

Consumer Products

One of the most sought after products which are used in one or the other form in our day-to-day life is Sorbitol. This product is extensively used in toothpaste, skin cream foundations, toilet soaps, cigarettes, foods, confectionery, cosmetics, textile, adhesives and more.

Pharmaceutical

We manufacture pharmaceutical grade starches of IP & BP grades for a number of applications like tablets manufacturing, coating and capsules covering amongst others.We were the first company in India accredited with import substitution award to manufacture the life saving drug Dextrose Anhydrous.

Food and Beverages

Our native starch and modified starches have applications in preparation of sweetners, food thickener, stabilizer, sweets, frozen food, tomato ketchup, sauces, mayonnaise which provides texture, viscosity binding ,and other properties to food, dry mixes, canned, chilled and frozen foods etc.

Liquid Glucose, Corn Syrups and Dextrose Monohydrate are used extensively for manufacturing a number of foods products like soft drinks, baked goods jams, jellies, chewing gums, canned fruits, pie and cream filling, confectioneries, ice creams and various other syrups.

Functional Intermediates

We have a variety of native and modified starch products act as functional intermediates for food, textile, paper, foundry industries. Our Sorbitol acts as a functional intermediate for paint manufacturing, drug & pharmaceutical manufacturing, cosmetics & toiletry products manufacturing.

Gum arabic is used widely in the flavoring industry whereas our spray dried powders have applications in seasoning mixes, confectionary, ready to drink soup mixes and nutraceutical powders.
