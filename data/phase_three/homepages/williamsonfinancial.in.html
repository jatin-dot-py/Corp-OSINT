Welcome to WilliamsonFinancial.in

Williamson Financial Services Limited is an Investments Company, an NBFC registered with the Reserve Bank of India under the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

We are a part of the Williamson Magor Group of Companies, and have our origins in the tea business. Consequent to the amalgamation and transfer of tea estates the activities of the company were not germane to its name, ie; Makum Tea Co. (India) Limited.

Hence, it was renamed Williamson Financial Services Limited, with effect from December 20, 1994.
