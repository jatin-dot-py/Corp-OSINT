About Transteel

Founded in 1995, Transteel has evolved into a publicly listed, multi-disciplinary firm specializing in commercial interiors, fit-outs, and office furniture solutions. With a legacy of innovation and precision, Transteel blends AI-powered design with human expertise to create workspace solutions that are efficient, aesthetic, and error-free.

Our in-house team of architects, engineers, and designers collaborate seamlessly across sectors like corporate offices, education, healthcare, and manufacturing.

Know us better

Why Choose Transteel

  • Proven Expertise: Over 20 years of B2B and corporate experience in office furniture and interior solutions.
  • Digital-First Approach: Seamless online browsing, customization, and purchasing since 2019.
  • AI-Powered Precision: Tech-integrated design and construction ensure error-free and on-time delivery.
  • End-to-End Solutions: From design and manufacturing to fit-outs and MEP services—everything under one roof.
  • Diverse Project Capability: Trusted across sectors—corporate offices, education, healthcare, factories, and more.
