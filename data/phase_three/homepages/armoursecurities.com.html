Who We Are

ARMOUR Securities

Located in New Delhi, India, We, “Armour Security India Limited”, are one of the premier consultancy firms of India offering a broad range of Security services. Our consultancy services include Transition and Deputation Process Service, Mechanical Clean Service, House Keeping Service, Fire Fighting Service, Supervision Service and Reviews of the Service. We maintain a comprehensive database of efficient professionals to cater the exact manpower requirements of our clients. Our all security and safety services are framed after making detailed assessment of the client’s locations. We provide comprehensive training to our hired security guards and supervisors on latest safety devices so that these personnel can avert accidents or industrial hazards at our client’s sites. Moreover, we also have hired expert web designers, advertising managers and creative artists to make attractive web promotional contents, animation clips and banners for our customers. To effectively provide all brand promotion, security and housekeeping services, we have created a modern office.
