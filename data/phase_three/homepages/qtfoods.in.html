About Us

Welcome to our bakery, a trusted name American Bakers in the bread and bakery industry since 2018. Our journey began in Noida, where our passion for crafting high-quality, delicious baked goods first took shape. From our humble beginnings, we have always strived to blend tradition with innovation, ensuring that each product we offer is made with the finest ingredients and utmost care.

In 2024, we relocated to a state-of-the-art facility in Dasna, Ghaziabad, to better serve our growing community of loyal customers. This move has enabled us to expand our production capabilities while maintaining the artisanal touch that our customers love.

Our extensive range includes freshly baked bread, pav ,bun , pizza base and specialty items, all crafted to perfection by our skilled team. We are committed to sustainability and source our ingredients responsibly, ensuring that every bite you take supports a better future.
