    ABOUT

    Tempsens - Thermal Engineering Solutions

    Tempsens along with its Subsidiaries and Joint Ventures provides thermal engineering solutions with manufacturing facilities in India, Indonesia, UAE, Poland, Germany and South Korea. Backed by decades of experience, our approach positions us to handle complex and critical industrial applications, fosters high customer retention, and creates strong entry barriers for new competitors.

    Tempsens specialises in thermal engineering solutions, which includes our 3 key verticals, Temperature sensing solutions, Electrical Heating Solutions and Specialised Cables that are tailored to the needs of the customer.
