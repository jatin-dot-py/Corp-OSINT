ABOUT
AURO IMPEX & CHEMICALS LIMITED

AURO IMPEX & CHEMICALS LIMITED (Formerly AURO IMPEX & CHEMICALS PRIVATE LIMITED) is an Auro Group Company incorporated in the year 1994 with a vision to cater to the ever growing requirements of industrial air pollution equipment in industries. Auro, with its state of the art facility and a widespread domestic and international network, focuses on the manufacturing, exporting and supply of Discharge & Collecting Electrodes, and Electrostatic Precipitator (ESP) internal parts and spares. We also provide niche ESP electrical control solutions making use of the latest available technology for the same to lower outlet emissions. The organisation is ISO 9001:2015certified, which enables us to operate with high efficiency, work at low costs, and provide superior quality to our customers.
Our vision is to create a quality customer base, and to cater to their developing needs. Our motto is to deliver quality products and to provide all our clients with outstanding after sales support. We established Auro with the aim of contributing to development in a sustainable manner, and to ensure that we can actively contribute to making the world a cleaner and greener place to live in.

QUALITY ASSURANCE

No Compromise on Quality

Unwavering focus on maintaining the highest quality standards is culture is ingrained in our DNA. We recognise that all our clients are discerning in terms of quality. True implementation of this belief has helped Auro grow as a company and in establishing a loyal client base. This has been made possible through ensuring -
  • Quality of Manufacturing & Testing Infrastructure.
  • Quality of Documentation & Implementation Systems.
  • Regular Training of Technical and non-Technical Personnel.

Quality Assurance

Auro is committed to build on its reputation as a reliable ESP Internals and Spares (Collecting Electrode, Discharge Electrode, ESP Internal Components) manufacturer and supplier. Competitive pricing, forward looking technologies, and our team of trained professionals form the backbone of our company's vision. We ensure compliance with all statutory regulations, and continuously monitor our processes and client feedback to build upon our offerings.
