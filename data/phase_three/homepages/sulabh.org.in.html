Welcome to Sulabh Engineers & Services Limited

Sulabh Engineers and Services Limited was set up in April 1983. It is a Non-Banking Finance Company (NBFC) registered with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). Our present product range includes - loans for Business or Capacity expansion, Working Capital loans, loans for Purchase of Equipment and Machinery, Term Loans against Property and loans for Purchase of Commercial Property.
