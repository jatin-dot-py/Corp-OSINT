Who We are

About
Uma Converter
Limited

Since its inception, UMA Converter has been the leading manufacturer and exporter of quality Flexible Packaging Material offering wide range of customized packaging solutions for various industries such as food, beverages, pharmaceuticals, personal care and many more.
  • Strong business values are responsible for transforming UMA Converter into a leader in providing quality packaging solutions rightly coupled with innovation.
  • We have a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility equipped with highly sophisticated machineries.
  • We are rightly able to cater to customers in India and Overseas to their full satisfaction.

Certifications

Our Quality

We offer our clients nothing less than quality packaging solution. The packaging material used in the production unit is sourced from certified vendors.
Our research professionals and technicians constantly experiment on a variety of material mixtures in order to come up with a solution that retains the quality of the products being packed.
