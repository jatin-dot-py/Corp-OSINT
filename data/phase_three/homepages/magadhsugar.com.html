    Built For The Long-Term

    Magadh Sugar & Energy Limited is a key part of the prestigious K.K. Birla Group of Sugar Companies, benefiting from decades of expertise in the sugar industry. Our units, originally established in 1932, were restructured through strategic mergers and demergers on March 19, 2015. Our core operations focus on producing sugar and its by-products: Ethanol and Power Co-generation using biofuel. Led by our Chairman, Sri Chandra Shekhar Nopany, and supported by a team of skilled executives, we are dedicated to sustained growth and excellence in execution.

    We operate three advanced sugar complexes in Bihar: Narkatiaganj, Sidhwalia, and Hasanpur. Our facilities have a total crushing capacity of 21,500 TCD, a distillery capacity of 155 KLPD, and a power co-generation capacity of 38 MW. Our strong infrastructure reflects our commitment to sustainable performance excellence.

    Corporate Social Responsibility

    Our Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives are driven by the vision of creating an equitable and inclusive society. We are dedicated to supporting sustainable transformation and social integration through active collaboration with the government, NGOs, and other organizations. Our partnerships focus on promoting rural education and community upliftment.

    Education

    We see education as a powerful catalyst for change. Our efforts are aimed at improving the educational infrastructure in rural Bihar, creating better learning opportunities for students.

    Healthcare

    Committed to social welfare, we provide healthcare assistance to marginalized communities, ensuring access to essential medical services.

    Rural Development

    Our contributions to rural development include constructing facilities such as a yatri shed in the Gopalganj district, enhancing the well-being and convenience of the local community.
