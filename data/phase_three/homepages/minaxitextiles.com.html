Minaxi Textiles Limited.

Minaxi Textiles Limited is an India-based company, which is engaged in the textile business. The Company is engaged in Weaving and manufacture of Cotton and Cotton Mixture Fabrics. The Company is also engaged in weaving of synthetic grey cloth of suiting and shirting, and supplying uniform to government and semi-government department under tenders floated by them.

The Company deals in suiting products having specialty in fancy suiting fabrics. Its products include suiting lycra fabric, cotton quilts, broken twill fabric and satin fabric.
