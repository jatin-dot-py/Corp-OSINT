About Khyati

We are Khyati Global Ventures Ltd. (Formerly Khyati Advisory Services Ltd.), a US$ 65 million, ISO Certified, SMERA rated, Government recognized 2-Star Export House successfully operating since 1993, catering to a global clientele.

Besides being a reputed Manufacturer and Exporter of Consumer Goods and Pharmaceuticals, Khyati Group also exports Branded/ Non-Branded Food & Non-Food consumer goods and Agro-products, across 50 countries, spanning The Americas, Europe, Australasia, Middle East and Africa.
