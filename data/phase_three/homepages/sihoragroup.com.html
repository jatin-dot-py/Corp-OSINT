Welcome To

Sihora Industries Limited

Where innovation meets tradition in textile manufacturing. Founded in 2023 by a passionate textile visioner, Sihora Industries began with a simple vision: to revolutionize the textile industry by combining high-quality materials with cutting-edge design. Inspired by a rich heritage of craftsmanship and a desire to meet the evolving needs of businesses, our founder set out to create a brand that would not only provide exceptional products but also foster lasting partnerships.

Welcome To

Sihora Industries Limited

Sihora Industries Limited is a leading manufacturer of narrow woven fabrics and garment accessories, known for innovation, quality, and customized textile solutions.
Established in 2023, the company blends traditional craftsmanship with advanced technology to serve diverse industries across India.

OUR PRODUCTION

We Give Top Production
From Every Angle

Machinery

Our state-of-the-art machinery ensures precision, efficiency, and consistent quality in every product. From lace to zippers and elastics, our fully integrated setup supports large-scale, customized textile manufacturing with speed and reliability.

Quality Control and Testing

We follow strict quality control and testing protocols at every production stage. Each product undergoes rigorous inspection to ensure it meets industry standards for durability, consistency, and performance before reaching our clients.

Packing

Our packing process ensures products are securely wrapped, labeled, and organized to prevent damage. We use high-quality materials to maintain product integrity during storage, handling, and transportation to clients.
