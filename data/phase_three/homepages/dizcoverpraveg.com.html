Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about PRAVEG, including its unique hospitality offerings, types of accommodations, and easy booking methods. What types of hotels and resorts do you offer?
Praveg specializes in building luxurious eco-resorts & boutique hotels located in spectacular natural settings throughout India, such as beautiful beaches, lush islands, and ancient ruins. From tent camping to high-end cottages, all of our properties combine sustainable architecture with contemporary amenities and comforts.
How can I book a hotel or resort online?
It is easy to make a reservation; simply log onto the Praveg website, pick a location and check-in/out dates, select the room you'll be staying in, and then make your payment securely via Credit Card, UPI, or net banking. As soon as your payment is complete, you will receive an email confirming your reservation within minutes. Reservations can be made any time, 7 days per week, with no additional fees.
Are your resorts suitable for family vacations?
Definitely! The majority of our family-focused resorts have spacious accommodations for the entire family, play areas for children, swimming pools, and options for family fun, such as playing at the beach and going on nature walks. Many of our resorts also have adjoining rooms (if needed) and are child-friendly, allowing you to enjoy your vacation.
What amenities can I expect at your resorts?
You can expect to find world-class amenities at our resorts, like infinity pools, full-service spas, multi-cuisine restaurants, yoga studios, adventure activities, and fast internet access, in addition to environmentally sustainable features. Each resort customizes its amenities based on what is available at its location.
Are there beachfront resorts available?
Yes, there are some fabulous beachfront resorts we offer in Lakshadweep, Diu & Daman that have amazing locations. Enjoy ocean views, private beaches, and participate in water sports directly from your resort.
