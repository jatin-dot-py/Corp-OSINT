Times Green Energy India Limited

Times Green Energy India Limited (TIMES) is a company founded by a group of women entrepreneurs.It started off by manufacturing of natural organic fertilizers through agri-waste management systems with technical support from Excel Industires along with manufacturing of Sanitary napkins and other women hygiene products. Within five years of its inception, TIMES has built a strong network. With two manufacturing units located in and around Hyderabad, TIMES has grown into a large corporate with Agriculture, Women Hygiene and E-commerce as main verticles.

The board of directors is made up entirely of experienced and dynamic women, who have been managing TIMES since its inception. Recently, TIMES has entered the women’s hygiene and safety segment by manufacturing and marketing sanitary napkins, baby nappies, and adult nappies and other products through our ecommerce site Bazaartimes.in

Empowering farmers, connecting global markets.

Times Green Energy India Limited sources premium-quality onions directly from farmers across key agricultural regions, fostering long-term partnerships and promoting sustainable farming practices. Through an extensive procurement, storage, and logistics network, the company ensures a consistent supply of fresh produce to wholesale markets, retailers, and institutional buyers across India. Our commitment to fair trade, quality assurance, and operational excellence helps create value for both farmers and customers while strengthening the agricultural value chain.

Moringa Powder manufacturing

Our mission is simple: to deliver the purest organic moringa powder with the lightest environmental footprint. By pairing traditional, chemical-free organic farming with a zero-waste, eco-efficient manufacturing loop, we ensure our superfood is as gentle on the planet as it is powerful for your body.

vermicompost manufacturing

We manufacture 100% organic, premium-grade vermicompost. Made using the best natural processes, our nutrient-rich worm castings are designed to give your plants and crops the ultimate boost. Safe, chemical-free, and perfect for superior growth.

VISION & MISSION

At Times Green Energy India Limited, our vision and mission are rooted in growth, sustainability, and global expansion. Having established a proud and highly successful track record as a trusted trading leader across India, we are now channeling our proven expertise and deep-rooted domestic success into the international arena. Our goal is to emerge as a premier, major player in India’s export industry, seamlessly bringing our world-class green energy solutions and reliable practices to global markets. As we transcend borders, we remain steadfastly committed to the same integrity, quality, and environmental dedication that built our success at home, ensuring a cleaner, greener future for our partners worldwide.
