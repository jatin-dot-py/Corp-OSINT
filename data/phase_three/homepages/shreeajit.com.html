About Us

SHREE AJIT PULP AND PAPER LIMITED (“Shree Ajit”) was incorporated as a Private Limited Company on 23/03/1995 and converted to Public Limited Company on 13/11/1995. The Company came out with the Public Issue of Equity Shares worth Rs. 315/- Lacs in the month of October 1996 and at present is listed on Bombay Stock Exchange and Vadodara Stock Exchange.

We started our journey of manufacturing quality kraft paper in 1997 with production capacity of 16,500 tonne per annum. Due to our consistent efforts, continuous innovation, technological advancements, consistent quality products and huge base of satisfied customers, we have increased production capacity to 1,08,000 tonne per annum.

Our History

1995

Beginning of Shree Ajit

SHREE AJIT PULP AND PAPER LIMITED was incorporated as a Private Limited Company on 23/03/1995 and was later converted to a Public Limited Company on 13/11/1995.

1997

Premium Grade Product

The production of the Company started from 10th August 1997 with an installed capacity of 16,500 TPA. During the first year ended March 1998, the Company achieved production of 7699 MT.

2006

Listed on Bombay Stock Exchange

The Company came out with a Public issue of 315.00 Lakhs in the month of October 1996. The Shares were listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange in 2006 under Indo Next Segment.

2010

Company Capacity TPM

During 2010 our installed capacity increased to 60000TPA. In March 2010, our second machine was installed and was successfully commissioned.

2012

Green Energy Project

Wind Energy is one the alternatives used against fossil fuels which is renewable, widely distributed and clean without any emission of greenhouse gases. In 2010, Shree Ajit has a contribution towards the environment by installing Windmill of 1.50 MW in Gujarat. In 2012, one more Windmill project of 1.25 MW was successfully commissioned in Gujarat.

2013

India's top 500 fastest growing company

Our commitment and core values have led us to noteworthy country wide recognition as - India’s TOP 500 fastest growing companies in 2013 and ranked at 112th Position.

2018

Modernization & Automation of Existing Technology

Up to 2018, the modernization & automation of the existing technology enabled to achieve the capacity of 108000 MT per annum. Serving the industry from more than 2 decades of expertise in paper making.

Today

Popular for Quality & Reliability

Shree Ajit is one of the largest manufacturer of Multi-layer Kraft Paper in India, and its premium products have found popularity for its quality and reliability.
