Our Product Range

Discover our extensive range of high-quality metals, ferro alloys, and mineral products designed to meet the demanding needs of global steelmakers, foundries, and industrial consumers. Each product is sourced with precision and delivered with a commitment to quality, value, and reliability.

Silico Manganese (SiMn) - All Grades and Sizes

A metallic alloy of manganese, silicon, and iron. It serves as a dual-purpose deoxidizer and desulfurizer, improving the hardness and durability of stainless and carbon steels.

Read More

Ferro Manganese (FeMn) - All Grades and Sizes

A high-density alloy used to eliminate sulfur-related brittleness. It is essential for increasing the tensile strength and abrasion resistance of heavy-duty structural steel.

Read More

Ferro Chrome (FeCr) - All Grades and Sizes

Produced from chromite ore, this alloy provides critical corrosion resistance. It creates a protective oxide layer that prevents rust in stainless and specialty steel applications.

Read More

Ferro Silicon (FeSi) - All Grades and Sizes

An iron-silicon alloy used as a powerful deoxidizing agent. In foundries, it acts as an inoculant to improve the mechanical properties and graphite distribution in cast iron.
