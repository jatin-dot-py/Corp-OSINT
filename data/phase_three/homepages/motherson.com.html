Updates for our investors

A diversified global design, engineering, manufacturing, assembly, and logistics specialist

Motherson delivers full system solutions to customers across a wide range of industries, leveraging its expertise in design, engineering, manufacturing, assembly, and logistics.

Recognised as one of the world’s leading automotive suppliers to OEMs, Motherson continues to set benchmarks in quality and innovation. The company has also successfully diversified into new and emerging segments, including technology and industrial solutions, health and medical, aerospace, and logistics.
