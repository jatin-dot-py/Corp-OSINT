What is coding and
marking and Why its important

Coding and marking is the process of printing important information directly on products, components, or packaging during manufacturing. This information can include date codes, batch numbers, serial numbers, barcodes, QR codes, logos, and product details. 

The main purpose of industrial coding and marking is to ensure product identification and traceability throughout the supply chain. It helps manufacturers track products from production to distribution, manage inventory efficiently, and prevent counterfeiting. Coding and marking are also essential for meeting regulatory and compliance requirements, especially in industries where product information, expiry dates, and batch details are mandatory.
