Who We Are

Four Decades of Textile Mastery -
Now in Denim.

Bhilwara Spinners Limited, established in 1980, launched its dedicated denim division - Ahinsa Denim - in 2025. We blend four decades of textile mastery with cutting-edge machinery to produce high-quality, premium-grade denim fabrics for domestic and international apparel brands.

Rigorous Quality Assurance

Every yard of Ahinsa Denim fabric is tested to the highest global standards - ensuring our customers receive nothing but perfection.

  • Tensile strength & tear resistance testing
  • Color fastness - wash, rub and light
  • Dimensional stability & shrinkage control
  • Seam slippage & abrasion resistance
  • OEKO-TEX & GOTS compliant processes
