WELCOME TO

Anya Polytech & Fertilizers.

Conceived at the turn of the decade, Anya Polytech Pvt Ltd was registered on 18th January 2011 under the Company Act, 1956.

Today, Anya is recognized as India’s premier fertilizer & Bag Manufacturing company along with being a provider of innovative environmental solutions. Putting forth a staggering capacity of over 750 lakh bags (75 Million bags) per annum, APFL is one of the most renowned players in this domain.

Back in time, in January of 2013, the commercial production had begun and today, APFL proudly delivers 100% of its existing capacity utilization and churns out a turnover beyond Rs. 100 crores, from bags & fertilizers (Zinc Sulphate Division).

Our Products

We also deal in providing premium quality Cattle feed , which is marketed by Anya Polytech & Fertilizers Ltd. This product aims at providing quality feed to the cattle for their complete well being & improved health. This Cattlefeed is made up of high quality nutritious ingredients which include maize, rice, soyabean cake, mustard oil cake, peanuts, wheat choker, gram, rice, salt ,minerals & vitamin E.
