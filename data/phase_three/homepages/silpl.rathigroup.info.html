Why Choose Us

Service failures per million(SFPM)

When you partner with Sampark India Logistics, you are entrusting your business & all your logistical needs, to a proven & dedicated logistics service provider in INDIA. We maintain very high standards, right from the point of pick-up of the consignments till delivery at the consignee’s end, enabling us to follow our work principal ‘Service Failures per Million’ (SFPM)!.

Technology

Through SMS and email, our technology platform keeps you up-to-date on what's going on. For customer satisfaction, we also provide instant proof of delivery (POD) to our clients through cell phones.

Workforce

In addition to being passionate and loyal to your sector, our team is well-versed in the logistics of your industry. They work relentlessly to ensure that your stuff reaches its destination safely. It's all about the client service . As Sampark India believe in " Customer First Approach".

National Presence

Networking across India to meet the needs of developing industry branches that operate 24 hours a day (24 x 7 and transport cargo quickly and efficiently).

Infrastructure

We have a modern logistics infrastructure that connects customers and cargo, a national presence with a professional team, and a fleet that is owned or attached to deliver freight quickly.
