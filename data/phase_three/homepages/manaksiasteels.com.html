GALVANIZED STEEL

Corrosion Resistant & Versatile

At Manaksia Steels Limited, we offer Galvanized Plain (GP) and Galvanized Corrugated (GC) steel products, each tailored to meet your specific needs. The steel sheet is coated with a uniform zinc-plated layer by controlling the amount of zinc coating. The raw materials in coil forms are Galvanized and properly leveled, granting final products superior formability.

Austrang

Corrosion Resistant & Versatile

At MSL India, AUSTRANG branded color coated steel harmoniously brings quality, durability, and value together. We assure you protection against the elements to ensure that we can surpass your expectations.

Coated steel sheet is a unique building material. It combines the strength of the steel with excellent corrosion protection of zinc/aluminum alloy coatings. The coating acts as a barrier against moisture, which is critical for preventing corrosion. These sheets

also have good UV resistance properties. This AUSTRANG grade of products has a longer lifespan than ordinary grades of color coating materials.

How We Feature in the Big Picture

The World: We take a holistic approach to responsibility. By manufacturing superior-grade coated steel sheets with extended service life, we reduce replacement cycles —one of the most effective ways to improve environmental outcomes. Optimized use of finite resources has also helped us moderate our carbon footprint.

Vendors: We prioritize long-term, sustainable partnerships. Our vendor relationships are based on consistency and on-time delivery, which has resulted in strong and stable collaborations.

Shareholders: Strong governance is the foundation of shareholder value.

Employees: We are a learning-driven organization. Community: We are committed to expanding our CSR activities around our manufacturing locations. Our focus areas include extensive healthcare and educational initiatives.
