Our Story

IST Limited was started as a WRIST WATCH manufacturing unit in Collaboration with M/s. RONDA S.A., SWITZERLAND in 1977. By 1984, IST Group has started manufacturing FINE TIME MECHANISMS and most precision parts for consumer durables by creating Special Production Division (S.P.D.). Thereafter, it successfully attempted AUTOMOTIVE SMALL PRECISION parts for two wheelers. In 1998 saw a major change in our clientele as we started supplying automotive parts for four wheelers to M/s. General Motors for Their North America and Hungary plants.

About Our History

1977

Started Manufacturing Wrist Watches in collaboration with M/S. RONDA S.A Switzerland.

1984

Diversified its product by manufacturing Fine Time Mechanism and most Precision parts of consumer durable by creating Special Product Division.

1997

Started manufacturing Automotive small Precision parts(Various type of jets) for two wheller for ESCORT, UCAL, Kehinfie Created Automotive Export Division for manufacturing and export of automotive parts for four wheelers to M/S. General Motors for their North America and Hungary plant.

2007
