    Our vision & history

    Incorporated in 2000, with a vision to deliver informed healthcare decisions to a billion lives connected by our technology, partnerships and human touch, Medi Assist has grown rapidly over the past years and is now considered a trusted name. Through strategic acquisitions, we've expanded our capabilities to serve our members not only in India but also across 185 countries worldwide.

    2000
    Medi Assist is incorporated.
    2002
    Registration of Medi Assist TPA as a third party administrator with IRDAI.
    2010
    Medimatter Health acquired stake in our company from Reliance Capital Ltd. and Medybiz Pvt. Ltd.
    2011
    Investment by Bessemer Health in our company.
    2013
    Investment by Investcorp Private Equity.
    2016
    Medi Assist TPA acquired DHS TPA.
    2018
    Medi Assist TPA acquired Medicare Insurance TPA Services (India) Pvt. Ltd.
    2022
    Medi Assist acquires Mayfair We Care.
    2023
    Medi Assist TPA acquires Medvantage TPA & Raksha Insurance TPA.
    2024
    Medi Assist gets listed on BSE and NSE.
    2025
    Medi Assist TPA acquires 100% stake in Paramount TPA
