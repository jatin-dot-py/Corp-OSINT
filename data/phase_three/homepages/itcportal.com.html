    ITC FMCG

    ITC is one of India's leading marketer in Fast Moving Consumer Goods Business. ITC’s FMCG products are Present in Packaged Foods, Cigarettes, Personal Care Products, Education & Stationery Products, Agarbattis & Matches. Today, ITC's portfolio of 25 vibrant mother brands reach over 26 crore households.

    Know more
    ITC Agri Business

    ITC is one of India's largest integrated agri business enterprises with significant presence across every node of the agri value chain.

    Know more
    Paperboards and Specialty Papers

    ITC's Paperboards and Specialty Papers Division (ITC PSPD) is amongst the leading names in the business worldwide. ITC PSPD manufactures Packaging and Graphic Boards, accompanied by a diverse range of Specialty Papers & Boards fulfilling a variety of needs. Its innovative solutions to meet a diverse cross-section of packaging and communication needs have helped it carve a unique niche for itself.

    Know More
    ITC Packaging

    ITC's Packaging & Printing Business is the largest value-added converter of paperboard packaging in South Asia. It converts over 1,00,000 tonnes of paper, paperboard and laminates per annum into a variety of value-added packaging solutions for the food & beverage, personal & home care products, cigarette, liquor, quick service restaurants and consumer goods industries.

    Know more
    ITC Infotech

    ITC Infotech is a leading global technology services and solutions provider, led by Business and Technology Consulting. ITC Infotech provides business-friendly solutions to help clients succeed and be future-ready, by seamlessly bringing together digital expertise, strong industry specific alliances and the unique ability to leverage deep domain expertise from ITC Group businesses.
