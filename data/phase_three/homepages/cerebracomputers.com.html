RECYCLING, REFURBISHING AND DIGITAL ADOPTION

We collect/purchase end-of-life and off-lease e-waste products. As we practice 3R principles – Reduce/Reuse/Recycle, after safe data destruction, we repair to reduce new manufacturing. If it cannot be repaired, we refurbish it to as good as new. If all fails, we recycle the e-waste. We have achieved zero landfills, with an aim for a greener & cleaner world. We call this 'Circularity', our driving philosophy.

CIRCULARITY FOR A BETTER WORLD

At the heart of Cerebra is a philosophy called ‘Circularity’. We practice this philosophy by not only remanufacturing, but also by responsibly recycling the products that have reached the end of their life.

Cerebra believes it is a social responsibility besides the vast potential. E-waste harms our environment and our dear & loved ones. Cerebra has set up one of the largest e-waste recycling facilities in India, with the aim of becoming a leading player in making this change possible on a global scale.
