About Us

Orosil Smiths India Ltd. offers it's customers precious yet affordable jewellery.  
For nearly 20 years it has striven to set new standards in design and manufacture of high end couture jewellery in India and abroad. Even as prices of precious metals move higher, Orosil's innovative designs and high class manufacturing facilities bring the elusive gold and silver jewelry within the reach of the common man.
