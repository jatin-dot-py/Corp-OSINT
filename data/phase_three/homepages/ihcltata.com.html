For over 120 years, IHCL has been the cornerstone of South Asia's hospitality sector, setting global benchmarks in luxury and service excellence. IHCL's expansive portfolio spans 660+ hotels across four continents, anchored by the legacy of its flagship property, The Taj Mahal Palace in Mumbai. Today, IHCL is a House of Brands spanning all segments of hospitality, food & beverage and services.
