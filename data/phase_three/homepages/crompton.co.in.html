The Crompton experience

'Amazing Every Day' is Crompton's brand promise - a commitment to transform the ordinary rhythm of daily life  into something extraordinary through thoughtful innovation and unwavering dependability.

'Amazing' represents meaningful moments of delight noticeable, improvement-driven experiences that genuinely elevate everyday living. Not theatrical extravagance, but thoughtful enhancements that make life feel tangibly better, easier, and more enjoyable.
'Every Day' embodies dependability and constant presence - a promise that Crompton delivers value and enrichment in the natural rhythm of daily life. From dawn to dusk, across changing seasons, through different life stages, Crompton touches consumers' lives continuously.

The in Amazing has been subtly modified to echo the Wi-Fi symbol, a visual cue representing Crompton's commitment to connected ecosystems and intelligent, loT-enabled solutions.

The in Every Day carries a refined design element inspired by the Electrical Plug and Power Button symbol - a thoughtful homage to Crompton's 85+ year electrical heritage and its core identity rooted in powering Indian homes.

    About Crompton

    In a world fueled by progress, Crompton pioneers the art of innovating with sustainability at its core. We redefine everyday living with state-of-the-art solutions for the modern lifestyle, merging technology and environmental consciousness. In our vision of the future, every product becomes a testament to that synergises technological advancement and environmental consciousness. Our journey is defined by a relentless pursuit of innovation that elevates everyday living experiences. At Crompton, we envision a future that is not just sustainable but also contributes to a greener, more responsible world. Our commitment to sustainable practices extends to every facet of our being, from product design to manufacturing processes, ensuring that each step we take treads lightly on our planet.

    Why Choose Crompton

    At Crompton, we offer a unique blend of time-honoured expertise and cutting-edge innovation. Our commitment to excellence shines through our range of Lighting and Electrical Consumer Durables, all proudly represented by the trusted "Crompton" brand.  When you choose Crompton, you're selecting more than just products; you're choosing a synergy of quality, reliability, and sustainability that is committed to illuminating homes and lives everywhere. Join us and millions of satisfied customers who have made Crompton a part of their lives, and experience the perfect blend of innovation and sustainability.
