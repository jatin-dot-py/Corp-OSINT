Market Context

The Growing Global Pharmaceutical Opportunity

Five markets. Five entry points. One integrated group.

India Exports

$65Bn

Projected export market by 2030 — India is the pharmacy of the world.

Vilin's Position

GMP-certified manufacturing base in Uttarakhand with 1,000+ approved molecules and active export operations across 13 therapeutic categories.

  • Facility scale: WHO-GMP certified formulation facility with high-volume solid dosage production.
  • Portfolio diversity: Formulations span anti-infectives, cardiovascular, and gastrointestinal categories.

US Generics

$150Bn

Annual US generics market — the world's single largest opportunity for Indian pharma.

Vilin's Position

Operational US subsidiary with a signed manufacturing supply agreement with Westminster Pharmaceuticals Inc. and a marketing deal with Activpower Inc., Virginia.

  • Commercial alliances: Established distribution channels targeting high-demand generic products.
  • Regulatory pipeline: Continuous alignment to support ANDA filings and bulk distribution networks.

Oncology Drugs

12% CAGR

Global oncology market growth — the fastest-growing therapeutic segment worldwide.

Vilin's Position

Dedicated oncology division under Chemgenix, targeting cancer therapeutics and biosimilars — an unlisted, high-growth asset within the listed group.

  • Specialized R&D: Focused on cytotoxic oral solids and injectables with high regulatory barriers.
  • Strategic entity: Structured to capture maximum value in high-growth specialized cancer therapies.

CARICOM Markets

20M+

Population across CARICOM nations — underserved by quality generic manufacturers.

Vilin's Position

Vilin Bio Med Jamaica — first-mover advantage as a Caribbean and Latin American market-entry vehicle in a largely untapped region.

  • Regional hub: Leveraging Jamaica as a primary distribution node for trade expansion.
  • Tenders & contracts: Actively targeting government supply tenders and regional healthcare provider networks.

Biologics & Biosimilars

$500Bn

Projected global biologics market by 2030 — peptides and biosimilars driving the next wave.

Vilin's Position

Dedicated biologics pipeline via Vilin Biotherapeutics R&D, targeting peptides, biosimilars, and next-generation oncology molecules.

  • Advanced R&D: Pipeline focuses on bio-better peptides and complex therapeutic proteins.
  • Patent expiry focus: Positioned to capture market share as major biological patents expire.
