About Us

OBCL Limited (formerly known as Orissa Bengal Carrier Limited), established in 1994 by Ratan Kumar Agrawal, is a trusted Logistics Company in India and a well-recognized name in the Indian logistics and transportation industry. With a clear vision to enhance transport accessibility across even the most remote regions of India, OBCL Limited has built a strong and reliable network connecting industries nationwide.

Over the years, the company has evolved into a respected Transportation Company known for efficient logistics solutions, dependable fleet operations, advanced Fleet Management Services, timely deliveries, and transparent customer support. Serving sectors such as manufacturing, FMCG, cement, steel, chemicals, and retail, OBCL Limited continues to be a preferred logistics partner for businesses across India with its commitment to reliability, operational excellence, and nationwide connectivity.

OBCL Limited stands as a leader in logistics through its focus on efficient manpower management, quality standards, and technology-driven, industry-specific Supply Chain Solutions that ensure reliable, seamless, and efficient operations across diverse industries.

OBCL Limited is committed to delivering end-to-end operational excellence with precision, reliability, and a customer-focused approach. As a trusted provider of Bulk Transportation Services, the company ensures seamless logistics operations across India. With a strong nationwide network of branches and associated offices, OBCL Limited efficiently serves key industrial destinations.

Recognized as a dependable Trailer Transport Company, it offers timely and secure transportation solutions for diverse industries. Its advanced fleet operations and strong logistics infrastructure make OBCL Limited a preferred transport partner nationwide.
