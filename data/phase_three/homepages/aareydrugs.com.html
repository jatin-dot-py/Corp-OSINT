Welcome To
Aarey Drugs & Pharmaceuticals Limited

Aarey Drugs & Pharmaceuticals Limited incorporated in 1990, is engaged in the business of

  • Manufacturing of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APls), intermediates and specialty chemicals.
  • Trading of Industrial Solvents & Chemicals

Our high-quality pharmaceutical products cater to well-established pharmaceuticals formulation manufacturers and pharmaceutical merchant exports & our vide array of industrial chemicals & solvents help us keeping our costs in check as well as act as a supplier to different industries across the globe.

Our Mission
