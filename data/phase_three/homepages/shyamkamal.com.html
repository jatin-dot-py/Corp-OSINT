welcome to shyamkamal investment limited

Shyamkamal Investments Limited is a Reserve Bank of India (RBI) registered Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) listed on BSE Limited. The company is engaged in the business of providing various types of short-term and long-term financing. Additionally, it is involved in investment and trading in securities with the objective of maximizing returns on shareholders' equity. Shyamkamal Investments Limited is categorized as a non-deposit taking NBFC
