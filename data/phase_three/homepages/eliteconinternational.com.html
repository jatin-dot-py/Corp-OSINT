ABOUT US

Elitecon. With You -
Wherever Life Takes You

Listed on BSE, Elitecon International Ltd. - earlier known as Kashiram Jain & Company Limited, is a legacy-driven company with a sharp global vision. From cigarettes to sheesha, and soon beyond - our products are designed to meet evolving consumer preferences across global markets. Elitecon’s Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) expansion marks our next chapter - venturing into high-demand categories like snacks brands, edible oils, and other everyday essentials tailored for modern lifestyles.

SUSTAINABILITY

Building
with Purpose,
Growing Responsibly.

We believe resilient agricultural systems are the foundation of strong rural communities. At Elitecon International, corporate social responsibility is rooted in responsible engagement with farming communities and a commitment to efficient resource use, environmental stewardship, and sustainable livelihoods. Our CSR initiatives focus on addressing real livelihood, water, and resource challenges within existing agricultural systems through transparent and measurable action. As we grow, we remain committed to growing responsibly by strengthening farming communities and protecting natural resources. Explore Elitecon International’s CSR roadmap to see how our vision translates into long-term, on-ground impact.
