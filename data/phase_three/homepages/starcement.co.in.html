    Our Plants

    Our state of the art integrated cement plant is based in a strategic location in the idyllic town of Lumshnong in Meghalaya and three grinding units, two at Sonapur near Guwahati, one at Silchar Assam and one at Mohitnagar near Jalpaiguri Town in West Bengal ensures easy availability of the best cement to our customers.

    • Plant Location
    • Environmental Data
    In addition to the integrated plant at Lumshnong, Meghalaya we also have two cement manufacturing units in Sonapur near Guwahati, Assam; one at Mohitnagar near Jalpaiguri town in West Bengal and the latest one at Kalain near Silchar town. This aggregates the installed capacity to 9.7 MTPA and the company aims to increase this capacity to 25 MTPA by 20230.
