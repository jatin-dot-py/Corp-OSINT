  Welcome To Abirami Financial Services (India) Limited

The Company was incorporated on 19-04-1993 having been promoted by Mr.K.V.Aiyappan, Mrs.Abiraami Arunachalam and Mr.Teckchand H.Vaswani. The Company obtained Certificate of Commencement of Business on 13-05-1993. The Company’s main business are Hire purchase, Leasing and Lending Money against Securities, Movable and immovable properties. The Company’s Shares are listed in the Bombay Stock Exchange Limited. The company is not accepting Public Deposits.The Company has no Subsidiary Companies.

Mr.K.V.Aiyappan is the Chairman of the Company. Presently there are Five Directors in the Board of the Company.
The Overall Management of the Company vests with the Board of Directors comprising of persons with Experience in Banking, Business and Industry. The company has been built on high standards of integrity, fair business practices, efficient, safe and trusted financial policies.
The Executive Director under the overall supervision, control and direction of the Board of Directors looks after the day-to-day operations of the Company.
