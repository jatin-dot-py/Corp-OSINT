    About Us

    Citypulse Multiventures Ltd., a BSE-listed enterprise, has grown from its roots in cinema and entertainment to become a diversified group with interests across cinema exhibition, renewable energy, education, digital media, and urban development.

    • We are committed to driving sustainable growth, community impact, and shareholder value, while staying true to our legacy of creativity and innovation.

    With a vision to innovate and create long-term value, Citypulse is shaping the future through projects like the Matar renewable energy plant, GIFT City ventures, and the strategic acquisition of Matrubharti, alongside its strong presence in multiplexes and education.

    Core Verticals

    Cinemas

    Delivering world-class movie experiences across multiple screens, Citypulse Cinemas is where entertainment meets comfort.

    Renewable Energy

    Powering a sustainable future with clean energy initiatives, starting with our flagship Matar solar project.

    GIFT City

    Building tomorrow’s skyline today, at the heart of India’s global financial hub.

    Education

    Nurturing young minds through quality education from foundation years to higher learning with Lovekush Education Group.

    Digital Media

    Empowering storytellers and readers through Matrubharti, India’s homegrown digital content platform.

    Fuel

    Delivering reliable energy solutions with our fully operational Nayara fuel station at Matar.
