About Kothari Petrochemicals

A part of the renowned HC Kothari Group, we are the largest producer of premium quality Polyisobutylene (PIB) in India. Since our founding in 1990, the company has enjoyed a strong market position in India.
In the financial year 2007-2008, Kothari Petrochemicals commissioned a second plant in Manali that doubled our production capacity to 36,000 MT per annum in order to supply Polyisobutylene (PIB) worldwide. We have offices in Chennai, and Mumbai, and supply to customers in 20 countries around the world.
