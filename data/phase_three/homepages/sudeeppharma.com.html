Our Legacy of Establishment

1989

Started Manufacturing of Mineral Excipients with 1 Facility and 2 Products

2020

Incorporation of Sudeep Pharma in US with Warehouses Located at 4 Strategic Locations

2021

Manufacturer of Value Added Functional Food & Nutritional Speciality Ingredients.

2023

Incorporation of Sudeep Pharma B.V. in Amsterdam, The Netherlands.

2024

Ventured into the Sustainable and Eco-friendly Future Chemicals Segment, with a Focus on Green Precursor Cathode Active Materials (pCAM) for Electric Vehicles (EV) and Energy Storage Systems (ESS).

2025

Expanded our Global Presence with a Strategic and Manufacturing  Footprint in Europe—Bringing us Closer to Key Customers and Strengthening our Supply Capabilities across the region.

Founded in 1989, Sudeep Pharma has built a legacy of delivering high-quality ingredient solutions across the Pharmaceutical, Nutraceutical, and Food industries. With over three decades of excellence, we remain committed to advancing health and nutrition on a global scale.

Our journey continues to evolve—with the strategic acquisition of NSS strengthening our specialty ingredients portfolio, and the launch of Sudeep Advanced Materials (SAM) marking our foray into sustainable future chemicals, including green pCAMs for Electric Vehicles (EVs) and Energy Storage Systems (ESS).

Driven by a forward-looking, people-centric approach, we are dedicated to creating long-term value and making a meaningful impact on life—every step of the way, every single day.

Leading Global Manufacturer of Pharmaceutical Excipients and Mineral Actives

Sudeep Pharma is recognized as one of the leading global manufacturers of mineral-based excipients and actives, serving the Pharmaceutical, Food, and Nutrition industries. Renowned for our expertise in calcium salts, we specialize in Calcium Phosphate and Calcium Carbonate, offering a wide range of customized grades to meet diverse customer needs.

Our extensive portfolio includes high-quality mineral-based excipients and actives such as Calcium, Iron, Magnesium, Zinc, Potassium, and Sodium, making us a trusted partner for customers worldwide. With a strong focus on innovation, customization, and quality, Sudeep Pharma continues leading as a top manufacturer in Indian and global markets.
