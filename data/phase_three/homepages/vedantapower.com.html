About Vedanta Power

Transforming Lives with
Reliable and Responsible Power

With 4,780 MW of installed capacity in four strategic assets Punjab, Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Odisha, Vedanta Power stands among India’s leading private-sector power producers. Anchored in operational excellence and disciplined execution, we deliver dependable power that fuels everyday life, powers homes and industries, strengthens grid resilience, and advances the nation’s growth.
