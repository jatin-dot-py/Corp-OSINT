Ludlow Jute Manufacturing Company

100 years of excellence in production

Ludlow Jute is proud to be one of India’s premier jute manufacturers and is trusted for its superior quality jute products. Sprawling along the picturesque banks of the river Hooghly in Chengail, near Kolkata, the Ludlow campus prides itself on its 1.2 million square feet of jute factory premises, living, health and education facilities for 1400 families, community spaces and acres for future expansion. Established in 1921 by the Ludlow Corp. of Ludlow, Massachusetts, United States, Ludlow Jute Mills andis owned and managed by Kankaria Group, Ludlow Jute has been a trailblazer in the jute industry for decades. We, at Ludlow Jute, are driven by a strong sense of social and environmental responsibility.

Why Jute

JUTE, THE GOLDEN FIRE

A natural fiber with a silky lustre and high tensile strength, jute has grown increasingly popular as the world becomes more environmentally responsible. In addition to its natural, earthy appeal, jute stands out as an environment-friendly raw material with a multitude of applications. Right from the first steps of cultivation, jute gives back more than it takes. A hectare of jute consumes 15 tonnes of carbon dioxide and releases 11 tonnes of oxygen in 120 days of growth. Buying jute wholesale from a low-cost manufacturer like Ludlow Jute is an ideal choice for an environmentally, quality and cost conscious customer .

Ludlow ICARE Pilot Project

In association with the National Jute Board, the Ludlow ICARE Project focuses on improving the lives of jute farmers by providing them with technology and educational resources that help them increase their yield. In the pilot phase, we have adopted the Chapra village in West Bengal where we interact with as many as 270 farmers. The improvements in cultivation and yield are ultimately for the dissemination to all jute farmers. We estimate that there are 2 million people in the region whose livelihoods depend on jute farming.

Ludlow Healthcare

We are committed to improving access to healthcare for the community in Chengail and adjoining areas. Under the Ludlow Healthcare programme, we cover medical expenses for families with an income of less than Rs. 300,000per annum. We conduct free health camps for the community and people of economically weaker sections of society, and focus on pulmonary, renal, cardiovascular diseases as well as conditions such as thalassemia and hemophilia.

View More

Ludlow Scholarship

We believe that education is the foundation for a better India. Since 2016, Ludlow has been providing financial support and career guidance to deserving students from economically weaker backgrounds. This full scholarship covers tuition fees, hostel fees, examination fees, books, as well as a stipend. Students are also given career guidance and exposure to industry professionals. We give preference to meritorious students domiciled in Chengail and adjoining areas, as well as children of Ludlow employees who are pursuing studies in the fields of textile technology, engineering, and medicine. We also sponsor the education of specially-abled students.

View More

Ludlow Academy

The Ludlow Academy is a primary school within the premises of the Ludlow Jute Mill, with a total capacity of 250 students and 16 teachers. Our students include the children of our locally-based employees as well as other children from the area.

View More

Ludlow Training Centre

To improve the employability of the semi-literate youth living in the vicinity of the mill, we have established the Ludlow Training Centre, where industry veterans equip the students with employment-enhancing vocational skills. Upon completion of their training, students receive certificates that help them in their pursuit of skilled jobs in the jute industry.

Industry - Academia Partnerships

We work closely with educational institutes around the country to encourage students textile design and textile technology students to undertake projects and internships related to jute. We also organise visits to the jute mill and student interaction events.
