Our Story

Nourishing Lives. Nurturing the Nation

Play Video

AWL Agri Business has been at the forefront of India’s food industry, evolving through decades of quality & trust. Our journey began in 2000 with the establishment of our first refinery in Mundra, Gujarat, marking the foundation of our fully integrated business model. The launch of our flagship brand ‘Fortune’ soon positioned us as India’s leading edible oil brand. Over the years, we expanded into packaged foods, reinforcing our presence in staples. Key milestones, such as the acquisition of Kohinoor rice in 2022, Tops brand of GD Foods and adding the sugar brand Madhur in AWL portfolio, strengthened our integrated approach to food processing and value addition in our flourishing FMCG portfolio.
