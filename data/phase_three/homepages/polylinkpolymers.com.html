About Us

About Us

Polylink Polymers India Limited is manufacturer and exporter of XLPE compounds, Filler Masterbatch, Black, White, Color & Additive Master Batches for the Plastic Processing Industry.

Polylink was incorporated in 1993 and plant commissioned in 1995 with headquarter in Ahmedabad, India. The plant was set up with technical collaboration of AEI UK (Pioneer of Sioplus technology). We are equipped with our machineries and the lab equipment as per their guidelines. Our manufacturing and R&D facilities are located in Dholka - Bagodara Highway Plant with an annual manufacturing capacity of more than 36,000 MT per annum.

A company with strong international orientation with presence in Turkey, Georgia, Bangladesh, Thailand, Nigeria etc. exports to more than 20 countries around the world. It has been listed in the Bombay Stock Exchange.

Polylink also installed a 300 KW of Solar plant, producing electricity that secures for the company the most competitive source of energy.
