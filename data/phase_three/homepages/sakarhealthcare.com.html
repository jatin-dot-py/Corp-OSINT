Aiming towards world-class healthcare….

Sakar Healthcare is committed towards the improvement of the quality of human life by providing world-class healthcare that enables us to live longer. The intention to serve mankind has always been the driving force of the founders of Sakar Healthcare Ltd, Mr. Sanjay Shah and Mrs. Rita Shah.

The aim of Sakar is to offer a complete range of pharmaceutical products and become a single point for total healthcare. Determined with direction since 2004, Sakar has achived a reputable position in the pharmaceutical industry today with the Liquid and Lyophilised injection facility (SVP) is EU GMP approved and all the manufacturing units are certified by ISO 9001:2015 (BVQI), WHO-GMP, cGMP & National Drug Authority, Uganda, Kenya, Yemen, Ethiopia, Congo, Ghana, Zimbabwe(MCAZ),Cambodia, Vietnam, Malawi, Namibia, Nigeria, Cote d’ivoire, Philippines, Peru.

To align ourselves with mission of delivering world-class healthcare that remains at the heart of our vision we intend to:

  • Commercialise Lyophilised products across regions
  • Mapping growth curve for selective registered products
  • Procure Marketing Authorizations to expand globally
  • Establish Strategic Joint ventures to explore depth of markets
  • Expand through Merger & Acquisitions
  • Target specific product portfolio through market segmentation
  • Operate multi-nationally

Vision :
To become a global healthcare organization based on three pillars- people, partnership and performance; making lives healthy happy and more meaningful by providing world class healthcare solutions.
