Our Brands

We manufacture cutting-edge solar products under two trusted brands.

UTL Solar is a renowned brand in the solar industry that provides a comprehensive range of products, including solar panels, solar inverters, lithium-ion and tubular batteries and power systems for residential, commercial, and industrial use. Over the last five years, UTL Solar has delivered a cumulative capacity of 0.9 GW of solar panels, 2.0 GW of solar inverters, and 2.56 GWh of solar batteries for rooftop solar operations across India. The company is committed to promoting renewable energy and sustainability by offering high-quality, efficient, and cost-effective solar solutions.

Visit Website

Fujiyama Solar is a growing Indian brand specializing in the manufacturing and supply of solar energy solutions. With a comprehensive product portfolio in the rooftop solar segment, Fujiyama Solar offers a wide range of products, including solar PCUs, solar off-grid, on-grid, and hybrid inverters, solar panels, PWM chargers, online and offline UPS systems, solar management units, and solar charge controllers. These products are designed to provide exceptional value-for-money to our customers.
