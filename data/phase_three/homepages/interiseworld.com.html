Our InvIT Story

We are
the harbingers
of growth

Interise Trust, formerly Indinfravit Trust, is among India’s first Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvIT) to be listed on a private placement basis on NSE and BSE in May 2018. Interise is committed to redefining global standards in the operations and maintenance of Indian highways by offering an unparalleled experience for road users. 500,000 vehicles traverse daily across our 15 road projects, which aggregate 6,400 lane km and are spread across 8 Indian States.

Through our unwavering focus on safety, innovation, quality and sustainability, we inspire the next level of growth for the communities we serve. From the globalisation of cities to the advancements in trade, India’s growth story continues to unfold. At the heart of this transformation lies a robust infrastructure, marking an era of continuous evolution.

Embracing this transformation, Interise stands resolute; connecting investors, businesses, communities and individuals. By transcending boundaries and reshaping the realm of possibility, we are the bridge to the bright.

India Growth Story Impact

We are propelling a robust surge in economic growth

Bold decisions made in the right direction are often the road to the monumental transformation of a nation over time

61%

India's National Highway network has expanded by over 61% since 2014, strengthening connectivity and reinforcing India's position as the world's second-largest road network.

146,500+ km

India's National Highway network now spans over 146,500 km, connecting key economic corridors, enabling faster, more efficient movement of people and goods across the country.

₹82,900 Cr

National Highway toll collections reached a record ₹82,900 crore in FY26, reflecting rising traffic volumes, improved efficiencies, and the long-term strength of India's road infrastructure assets.

94%

Average toll plaza waiting times have reduced by over 90%, driven by FASTag-enabled digital tolling systems that have made road travel faster, more efficient, and more convenient.

Source: Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, Press Information Bureau
