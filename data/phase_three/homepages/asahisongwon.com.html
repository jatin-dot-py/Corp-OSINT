Adding Color to Industries Worldwide

High-performance pigment solutions driven by quality, innovation, and global expertise since 1990.

Asahi Songwon Colors Limited is a globally recognized manufacturer and exporter of high-quality color pigments, with specialized expertise in Copper Phthalocyanine (CPC) pigments and their derivatives. Established in 1990 and headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, the company has built a strong reputation for delivering reliable, performance-driven pigment solutions to industries across the world. With a focus on quality, innovation, and sustainability, we serve a wide range of sectors including coatings, paints, textiles, plastics, rubber, pharmaceuticals, printing inks, and other industrial applications. Backed by advanced manufacturing capabilities and decades of industry experience, we continue to bring vibrant color solutions that meet evolving global standards and customer expectations.

Exporters of Superior Quality Pigments

Delivering globally trusted pigment solutions with consistency, innovation, and manufacturing excellence.

Asahi Songwon Colors Limited has established itself as a leading manufacturer and exporter of high-quality pigments, serving global industries for more than three decades. Headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, the company is recognized among the world’s leading manufacturers of phthalocyanine pigments and one of the largest producers of CPC Blue Crude globally. With advanced manufacturing capabilities, strong backward integration, and a commitment to international quality standards, our pigments are widely used across paints, coatings, plastics, textiles, printing inks, rubber, pharmaceuticals, and paper industries. Through continuous innovation, strategic global partnerships, and a customer-focused approach, we continue to deliver reliable pigment solutions to multinational companies across international markets.

CPC Blue Crude
The Foundation of Phthalocyanine Blue Chemistry.
CPC Blue Crude is a key intermediate used in the manufacture of high-performance phthalocyanine pigments. Produced under stringent quality standards, it provides the consistency, purity, and reliability required for a wide range of colorant applications.
Pigment Blue 15:0
Consistent Performance For Industrial Color Solutions.
Pigment Blue 15:0 offers excellent color strength, good dispersion characteristics, and reliable processing performance. Its versatility makes it suitable for use across inks, coatings, plastics, and other industrial applications requiring durable blue coloration.
 
Pigment Blue 15:1
Balanced Color Performance With Dependable Quality.
Pigment Blue 15:1 combines strong tinting strength, good stability, and consistent color development. Designed for demanding manufacturing environments, it delivers reliable performance across a broad spectrum of applications.
Pigment Blue 15:2
High-Performance Blue Pigment For Diverse Applications.
Pigment Blue 15:2 is recognized for its excellent color strength, durability, and chemical stability. It provides consistent results in applications where long-term color performance and process reliability are essential.
Pigment Blue 15:3
The Industry Standard For Vibrant Blue Coloration.
Pigment Blue 15:3 delivers outstanding brightness, excellent fastness properties, and superior processing characteristics. Its proven performance makes it one of the most widely used blue pigments across global industries.
Pigment Blue 15:4
Premium Red-Shade Blue For Enhanced Color Performance.
Pigment Blue 15:4 offers exceptional color intensity, transparency, and durability. Engineered for demanding applications, it provides excellent consistency and long-lasting visual impact across a wide range of end uses.
