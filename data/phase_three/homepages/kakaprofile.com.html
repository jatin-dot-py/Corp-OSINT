    Sustainability &
    Environment

    The Two types of polymers most frequently used worldwide are Polyvinyl Chloride or uPVC, Wood Plastic Composite, or WPC. uPVC and WPC are utilised widely in a variety of common applications because of their adaptability. Compared to conventional thermoplastics, both of these materials rely on much less oil. For a very economical use of raw resources, it is also very strong and energy-efficient across a range of applications.
