About Us

Varyaa Creations Ltd., is a trailblazing name in the world of jewellery manufacturing and trading. With a legacy spanning back to 2005, we have been dedicated to delivering exceptional craftsmanship and exquisite designs that captivate the hearts of our discerning clientele. Renowned for our specialisation in the manufacturing and trading of fine jewellery in Jadau, Diamonds and Gemstones, Varyaa Creations has established an unrivalled reputation for manufacturing excellence.

More About Us ⟶

Dazzling Diversity

At Varyaa Creations, we believe that every piece of jewellery tells a story. We take immense pride in using more than 120 types of natural precious and semi-precious stones, carefully handpicked to create designs that are not only unique but also highly desirable and stylish for the modern woman of today. We understand the importance of self-expression through jewellery, and our designs effortlessly blend timeless elegance with contemporary flair.

Baubleberry

BaubleBerry, an enchanting online jewellery shopping brand by Varyaa Creations Limited, is a one-stop destination for jewellery lovers of all ages. We believe that jewellery is more than just adornment; it’s an expression of one’s unique style, emotions, and memories. BaubleBerry offers a curated collection of exquisite jewellery pieces, carefully designed to captivate hearts and inspire self-expression. Whether you are shopping for yourself or searching for the perfect gift, BaubleBerry promises to be your ultimate jewellery shopping paradise.
