FAQ

Quick answers to questions you may have

What services do you offer?

We provide comprehensive logistics solutions including contract warehousing, third-party logistics (3PL), distribution, transportation, and last-mile delivery.

What industries do you specialize in?

We serve diverse industries such as FMCG, Auto Components, Food & Beverage, and Sanitary Ware.

What is 3PL, and how can it benefit my business?

Third-party logistics (3PL) involves outsourcing your logistics and supply chain operations to us, reducing costs, improving efficiency, and ensuring better inventory management.

Do you offer nationwide logistics services?

Yes, we have a pan-India presence, ensuring coverage across key regions and states for seamless logistics solutions.

How do you ensure the safety of goods during transit?

We follow strict safety protocols, use trained personnel, and provide real-time tracking to ensure secure and damage-free deliveries.

What is last-mile delivery, and why is it important?

Last-mile delivery refers to the final step of the delivery process, ensuring goods reach their final destination. It is crucial for timely customer satisfaction.

Can you handle bulk orders and large shipments?

Absolutely! Our infrastructure and fleet capacity allow us to manage bulk orders and large shipments with ease.
