about patels airtempEngineering Excellence Beyond Compare

PAT commenced its ever progressing journey with expertise of Design and manufacturing of process plant equipment in Year 1973 as a private limited company with a range of Air Conditioning, Refrigeration as well as Heat & Mass transfer equipments. The growth saga was further strengthened in 1993, when company has started new manufacturing unit at Rakanpur, Gujarat spread over 33,400 Sq. Meter of Land. It became a Public Limited entity (Shares' Listing on BSE) and thus was born, Patels Airtemp (India) Limited.

In the year 2019 PAT headed towards addition of another unit for dedicated manufacturing of Air Cooled Heat Exchanger / Air Fin cooler in total land area of 44,500 Sq. Mtr. New unit will be completely operational by Mid-2020. This unit will be able to manufacture 450 Nos. tube bundles and dispatch 150 Nos. modular constructed Air Fin Cooler, annually.
