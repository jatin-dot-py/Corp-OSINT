Welcome to Subhash Silk Mills Limited

 
Subhash Silk Mills Limited was founded as a partnership firm sometime in 1949 by Mr. VedPrakash Mehra and his brothers. In November, 1970 the firm was taken over by Subhash Silk Mills Ltd. Currently in its 63rd year, Subhash Silk Mills Ltd. is one of the pioneers of textiles in the art-silk industry in India.
