Delivery and Billing Models – Flexibility for Your Needs

At Dienstentech, we offer versatile delivery and billing models to suit your project requirements:

Managed Services Model

We assume responsibility for projects in the engineering and IT sectors, collaborating closely with our clients to ensure smooth project execution.

Time and Material (T&M)

With this model, resources are deployed at either Dienstentech or the customer’s site, and invoicing is dependent on the quantity of resources deployed and the amount of time spent on projects.

Fixed Price Model

We take full responsibility for projects from planning to completion, scheduling the necessary resources, and completing the project on schedule and within the allotted budget.

Products as a Service

We also provide you with affordable, plug-and-play solutions with our off-the-shelf products, such as Recruitment Management Systems, Payroll and invoice Management Systems, and Leave and timesheet Management Systems.
