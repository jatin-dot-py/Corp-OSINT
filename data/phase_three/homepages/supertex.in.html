ABOUT US

Supertex Industries Ltd was founded in 1986 for processing of synthetic yarns. It is engaged in texturizing, twisting, draw-warping and sizing of Polyester Filament Yarn (PFY).

PRODUCTS

Custom Yarn Creator, you'll be able to control all the variables -- color, fiber, thickness, and amount.

The chief products of the Company’s texturising plant are Polyester texturised and Nylon crimp yarn. It is one of the few companies processing nylon filament yarn. The prime product is FDY beams that are of high precision quality and has application in sizing and draw warping machines. It is the pioneer in manufacturing the product and has no competition in the draw warping segment. Supertex Industries Limited also manufactures sized beams for weaving. It has developed products like bi-shrinkage beams, Lycra beams, nylon beams, etc., which warrant uncommon expertise.
