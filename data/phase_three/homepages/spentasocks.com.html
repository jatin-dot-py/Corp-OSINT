    About Us

    Founded in 1994, Spenta International Limited (Spenta) is a Public Limited company based in Palghar, Maharashtra, India. It specialises in designing, manufacturing and exporting cotton socks for men, women and children of all ages.

    Over the years, Spenta has earned itself the reputation of being India’s most renowned supplier and reliable manufacturer for quality customised socks across India, UK, Europe, USA and South Korea. The socks are designed with an aim to cater to a diverse range of consumers, preferences and sense of style.

    Human Resources Policy

    The main aim for Spenta’s Human Resource Policy is to keep our staff’s morale high, strengthen their devotion to the enterprise and to create a peaceful familial environment for its staff to express opinions, keeping the transparency alive. We acknowledge that an employee is not just a valuable brand asset, but also an active contributor, so as to collectively build and grow for a better future.

    Spenta values the hands that skillfully operate the machineries, so we encourage our work force to undergo numerous training programs and courses for constantly upgrading their skills. This includes quality control inspection, start-to-end value chain system, and the upkeep of machinery.

    Our Infrastructure

    Our plant is located at a distance of 100kms from Mumbai in Palghar (West), Maharashtra, and is easily accessible by road and rail. The Plant and Machinery include 121 LONATI Sock Knitting Machines, which produce over 7 million pair of socks per year, accounting for about 80% of production sold to MNCs.
