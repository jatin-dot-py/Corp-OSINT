Why Retread

Economical and Reliable
  • Purchasing tyres ranks as a fleet’s second noteworthy expense after fuel
  • Retread tyres offer a cost-effective alternative, costing 30% of a new tyre
  • Retreads deliver reliability comparable to new tyres
  • A bus or truck tyre can be retreaded two, or more times depending on quality and condition, leading to substantial cost savings.
Environmental-Friendly
  • Oil Conservation: Retreading requires ONLY 7 gallons of oil, whereas a new tyre requires about 22 gallons
  • A tyre naturally takes 70-80 years to decompose, but the Retread Process allows them to be reused instead of accumulating in landfills

Established by the late Mr. M. E. Meeran, a visionary and adept business strategist, Eastern Treads Limited is situated in Ernakulam District, Kerala. Mr. Meeran, recognized throughout the state for his keen ability to identify business prospects, particularly in the condiments segment, solidified his undisputed leadership in chosen areas. Presently, under the guidance of Mr. Navas Meeran, the company's promoter chairman, Eastern Treads Limited continues to flourish. Drawing from the visionary teachings, Mr. Navas Meeran has led the Meeran Group's expansion into diverse categories, transforming it into a major business conglomerate.

The Company Eastern treads Limited is engaged in the business of manufacturing and marketing tyre - retreading materials like pre - cured rubber, bonding gum, vulcanizing solution and other accessories. The Company, a Public Limited Company, Listed in Bombay Stock Exchange under Trade Code: EASTRED, was originally incorporated as a private limited company, under the Companies Act, 1956 under the name and style as ‘Eastern Precured Treads Limited’ vide Certificate of Incorporation dated 02nd day of July 1993 issued by the Registrar of Companies, Kerala. Later the Company went public on 08th January 1996 and listed its Equity shares in the Bombay Stock Exchange.

The Corporate Identification Number of the Company is L25119KL1993PLC007213. The ISIN of the Company is INE500D01015.
