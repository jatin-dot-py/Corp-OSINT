Range of Products We are Catering

With a commitment to providing comprehensive solutions, the company caters to esteemed clients worldwide.

LSAW

Man Industries manufactures Longitudinal Submerged Arc Welded (LSAW) Line Pipes of various sizes and in conformance with various specifications to meet the growing Global demand.  

EXPLORE NOW

HSAW

The company, keeping up with the pace of industrial movement, is committed to make some major investments for expansion of its HSAW Line Pipe manufacturing facilities.

EXPLORE NOW

Coating

To protect the line pipes from corrosion and to enhance its operational life, Man Industries offers various options of coating systems to the choice of the customer as per the end use of the pipe.  

EXPLORE NOW

ERW

Man Industries manufactures HFW (ERW) High Frequency welded pies of various sizes in conformance with various specifications to meet the growing global demand of pipe line.  

EXPLORE NOW

HIB

Man Offshore and Drilling Ltd. (MODL) manufactures Hot Induction Bend (HIB) process is much heating process as it is a bending process of various sizes in conformance with various specifications to meet the growing global demand of pipe line.
