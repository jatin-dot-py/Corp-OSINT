MILESTONES

Power Transmission business demerged into a separate company - Triveni Power Transmission Limited (TPTL)

Company
The Company announced the upgrade of its long-term rating to ICRA AA+ (Stable) from ICRA AA (Stable) and reaffirmed short-term rating of ICRA A1+ for the Company's bank facilities of ₹3110.37 crore (enhanced from ₹2510.37 crore). This upgrade holds significant value and acknowledges the Company's strong financial foundation.


Acquisition of stake in Sir Shadi Lal Enterprises Limited (SSEL): During the year, the Company acquired a 61.77% stake in a listed integrated-sugar company namely Sir Shadi Lal Enterprises Limited, resulting in SSEL becoming a subsidiary of the Company. With SSEL's proximity to two of the Company's largest sugar units, this transaction was aimed at creating operational/logistical synergies and improved efficiencies to benefit both the companies and key stakeholders including the farmer community.


Alcohol Business
The Company commenced a new multi-feed distillery at Rani Nangal, Uttar Pradesh with a production capacity of 200 KLPD (Kilo liter Per day) which placed the Company amongst the leading ethanol manufacturers in the country.


IMFL Business: The Company commenced marketing and selling its own brands in the premium segment of Indian Made Foreign Liquor (IMFL) as a forward integration of the distillery operations in July 2024. The Company launched two brands in the state of Uttar Pradesh, viz. THE CRAFTERS STAMP (https://www.thecraftersstamp.com) in Super Premium Category and MATSYA (https://www.matsyawhisky.com/) in Premium Category.

During the year, the Company announced a buyback resulting in a record distribution of ₹800 crore to the shareholders. Buyback was completed in March 2023.


Triveni Sports Private Limited (TSPL) through its franchise, Triveni Continental Kings (TCK) embarked on an extraordinary journey by becoming the maiden champions of the Global Chess League. This is a sports joint venture of Triveni Engineering & Industries Limited (TEIL) and Triveni Turbine Limited (TTL), announced its presence in sports with the Global Chess League.


Power Transmission Business: First High Power (27.5 MW) Compressor Gearbox order from Overseas.


Water Business: Maldives - Triveni completed and handed over four out of six islands to Govt. of Maldives project and operations commenced for these plants. Triveni's scope for Water & Sewerage facilities inter-alia including RO treatment system, distribution pipeline network, sewerage pipelines, disposal line terminating into sea and household connections for package-6 in Maldives. These four islands have been commissioned and handed over to operation after successful testing & commissioning of entire facilities.

Alcohol Business: The Company commenced a new multi-feed distillery, with a production capacity of 160 KLPD (Kilo liter Per day) at Milak Narayanpur, Uttar Pradesh, later expanded to 200 KLPD. Expanded existing distilleries at Sabitgarh & Muzaffarnagar (in Uttar Pradesh) to operate at 200 KLPD each. The Company also commissioned a new 60 KLPD grain-based distillery at the existing distillery complex at Muzaffarnagar. With this, the total distillation capacity of the Company has been increased to 660 KLPD.

Triveni Turbines:

On September 21, 2022, Triveni Engineering and Industries Limited has divested its entire 21.85% stake in Triveni Turbine Limited worth Rs. 1,609 crore.

Triveni Brands: The Company relaunched Shagun with brand new packaging and created plans to launch additional brand such as Triveni Sugar. The Company expanded its Private Label business by adding more customers and became the No. 1 contract manufacturing partner for sugar in North India.


Power Transmission Business: The Company signed a 10-year business agreement with GEAE Technology USA to locally manufacture the LM2500 gas turbine base and enclosure .

Alcohol Business: Hand Sanitizers manufacturing facility was set up at Muzaffarnagar distillery.

Power Transmission Business: PTB notched a milestone with the installation of a total of 34,000 MW capacity globally.

Power Transmission Business: Another milestone was achieved with a total of 10000 new gearboxes supplied globally to around 600 customers. Completed retrofitting, replacement, overhauling of 1000+ gearboxes for 80+ global brands.

IMIL Business: Alcoholic beverages vertical, part of the distillery operating segment, started producing Indian Made Indian Liquor at its bottling facility in the premises of its existing distillery in Muzaffarnagar, Uttar Pradesh, to facilitate forward integration of distillery operations.

Power Transmission Business: ISO-45001:2018 (OHSAS) certification was awarded to the Power Transmission Business. The 500th gearbox was dispatched for the thermal project for booster pump drive. Highest power indigenous 63.5MW STG Gearbox for installation in West Africa.

Alcohol Business: 160KLPD distillery was commissioned at Sabitgarh.

Power Transmission Business: 40,000 MW Gears capacity installed globally

Water Business: Secured order for 18 MLD UF-RO based Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) for Textile cluster of Balotra Industrial area (Rajasthan) from their Industry association.

Secured largest order for 205 MLD Sewage Treatment Plant at Kondli site including extensive Biogas power generation system in Delhi city from Delhi Jal Board (DJB).

Secured first PPP job for Mathura city (UP) from UP Jal Nigam under one-city-one-operator program under Namami Gange schemes of Ministry of Jal Shakti through National Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG).

- Addition of SCADA control and monitoring for in-house heat treatment facility

Water Business: secured order for one of largest Sewage Pumping Stations of 210 MLD in Koramangala area of Bengaluru from Bangalore Water Supply & Sewerage Board (BWSSB).

Power Transmission Business: Gear unit won the CII EXIM Bank Award for the third time for Business Excellence 2016 - Strong commitment to Excellence.

2nd assembly bay and highly mechanized large size paint booth were added at Mysore Gears facility.

Water Business: Won a project for design, construction, O&M of primary treatment works at Dehra (Ghaziabad) & 210 MLD water treatment plant at Palla (Greater Noida).

It also won allied works under 85 Cusec Ganga Jal Project for Greater Noida Industrial Development Authority.

Secured order for long term Operation & Maintenance (O&M) for Municipal Utilities covering Water and Sewerage including upgradation of existing Assets for city of Bathinda from Punjab Water Supply & Sewerage Board (PWSSB).

In a New Product Development - 850 KW Planetary Gearbox was supplied for sugar mill drive application.

Turbine Business: Triveni's steam turbine business was demerged into a separate company (Triveni Turbine Limited (TTL).

Power Transmission Business: First locally manufactured drop-in replacement of Frame-6 gear box at for India's leading petrochemical company

Water Business: Secured first order on turnkey basis for 30 MLD Sewage Treatment Plant with Nutrient Removal from Hyderabad Municipal Development Authority (HMDA).

Sugar Business: The sixth sugar mill was commissioned at Rani Nangal in western Uttar Pradesh with a crushing capacity of 5,500 TCD, and the 7th was commissioned at Milak Narayanpur in western Uttar Pradesh with a crushing capacity of 6,000 TCD. A distillery was commissioned in Muzaffarnagar, with a capacity of 160 kiloliters per day (KLPD).

Water Business: Awarded as the largest seawater RO (including PT) based boiler feed water treatment plant for UPCL's 2X600 MW TPP in Udupi, Karnataka (LANCO Group).

Sugar Business: Commissioned its fourth sugar mill at Sabitgarh in western Uttar Pradesh with a crushing capacity of 7,000 TCD in January.

In December, the fifth sugar mill was commissioned at Chandanpur in western Uttar Pradesh with a crushing capacity of 6,000 TCD.

Power Transmission Business: The first integrally geared 37935 rpm compressor was developed for an indigenously designed/manufactured gearbox for a public sector engineering conglomerate.

Power Transmission Business: Renewal of High speed agreement and with enhanced local manufacturing up to 25MW added geographies by Gears business
