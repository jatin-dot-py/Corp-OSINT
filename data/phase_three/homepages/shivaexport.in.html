Welcome Shiva Granito Exports

An initiative by IIT Mumbai and IIT Kharagpur Alumni

Shiva Granito Export LTD (Formerly known as Shiva Exports co.) is a premier exporter of all kind of Marble and Granites to all major parts of the world. It played a role of dominant player in the market for more than a decade . The group decided to extend its operation by entering in the manufacturing of the Engineered Quartz Stone Slabs, Resin & Quartz Powder. The Manufacturing Facility is been established with backward integration and is ONLY Indian Company with such Unique advantages.
