Our Rich History

Service and innovation, since 1960

NHC Foods Limited has emerged as a global leader in domestic trading and the export of agricultural commodities — with an unparalleled proficiency in the Indian and international trade of whole and ground spices, oil seeds, grains, rice, sugar, coffee and pulses.

We have earned a distinguished reputation for excellence, dependability and superior quality within the industry — and today we also offer branded consumer packs under our own labels, Indibite and Saaz.
