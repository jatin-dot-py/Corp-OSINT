Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Ahlada's steel doors, windows, and furniture — from product selection and installation to fire ratings, maintenance, and warranty.

Ahlada manufactures a comprehensive range of steel doors including commercial doors, residential doors, fire-rated doors, security doors, and specialized cleanroom doors. Our product portfolio covers single leaf, double leaf, sliding, and hinged doors in various sizes and specifications.

Yes, many of our steel doors are fire-rated and certified according to relevant standards. We provide doors with fire ratings ranging from 30 minutes to 4 hours, depending on the application requirements. All our fire-rated doors come with proper certification documents.

Yes, we provide comprehensive installation services for all our door products. Our experienced installation team ensures proper fitting, alignment, and functionality. We also offer post-installation support and maintenance services to ensure long-term performance.

We use high-quality steel sheets, precision hardware, and premium finishes in our door manufacturing. Our doors are constructed with galvanized steel frames, stainless steel hinges, and durable locking mechanisms to ensure longevity and security.

Standard door orders typically have a lead time of 2-3 weeks from order confirmation. Custom designs may require 4-6 weeks depending on specifications. Rush orders can be accommodated with additional charges.

Yes, we provide delivery services across India. We have a robust logistics network that ensures safe and timely delivery of our products to various locations. Delivery charges may vary based on location and order size.

We accept various payment methods including bank transfers, cheques, and online payments. Our standard payment terms are 30% advance, 60% on dispatch, and 10% on delivery. Custom payment terms can be discussed for large orders.

Yes, absolutely. At Ahlada, we understand that every project has unique requirements. That's why we offer complete customization — from size and design to color, finish, and hardware. Whether it's for a hospital, school, industrial unit, or residential project, our team will work closely with you to deliver doors that match your exact specifications and standards.

We offer extensive customization options including custom dimensions, color matching, special finishes, hardware selection, glass inserts, vision panels, and specialized locking mechanisms. Our design team can work with your specific requirements.

For custom doors, we typically require a minimum order quantity of 10 pieces. However, this can be flexible based on the complexity of customization and project requirements. Please contact our sales team for specific details.

We provide a comprehensive warranty on all our doors. Standard doors come with a 10-year warranty on manufacturing defects, while fire-rated doors have a 1-year warranty. Hardware components have separate warranty terms as per manufacturer specifications.

Yes, we provide comprehensive after-sales support including maintenance services, spare parts supply, and technical assistance. Our support team is available to help with any issues or queries you may have post-installation.

To claim warranty, please contact our customer service team with your order details and issue description. We will guide you through the warranty claim process and arrange for inspection or replacement as applicable under warranty terms.
