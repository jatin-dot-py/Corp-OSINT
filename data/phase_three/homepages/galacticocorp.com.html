Intro

Galactico Corp

Galactico Corporate Services Limited is a SEBI Registered Category I Merchant Banker, with hands-on experience in the field of Corporate Valuations and Listing and has been promoted mainly with an objective to meet the drastic need of professional advice to SMEs and Startups looking to access growth capital and expand their business horizons. We cater to a diverse clientele of domestic and institutional clients. We have a strong management team with deep experience in the area of issue management, focused on wealth creation in the long run.
