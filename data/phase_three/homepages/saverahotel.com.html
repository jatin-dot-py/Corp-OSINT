WELCOME TO THE SAVERA HOTEL

THE PERFECT PLACE FOR COMFORT STAY

Welcome to Savera Hotel, the iconic hospitality landmark of Chennai city. A fusion of modernity and tradition, Savera Hotel has evolved with Chennai history and stands strong today with 234 rooms, 6 food & beverage outlets, meeting venues and a host of leisure activities.

Savera Hotel by Savera Industries Ltd., is a listed company. The history of the Hotel dates back to 1968 with a distinction of being one of the first star hotels in South India.

FINE DINING CHOICES

Savera Hotel's gastronomic endeavours have always delighted the gourmet. The Master Chefs churn the authentic recipes from the regions with chosen herbs and spices from the natives. Considering the assortment of guests at the hotel and the preferences of the local populace, Savera Hotel has the following food outlets.

The Piano, Malgudi, The Brew Room, Curry Town, Baker's Basket and the lounge bars - Bay146 & Bamboo Bar
