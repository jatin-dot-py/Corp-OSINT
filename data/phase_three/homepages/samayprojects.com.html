What We Offer

Mechanical EPC Services

Samay Project Services Limited (SPSL) (Samay, incorporated in 2001), is in the business of SITC of Fire detection & Protection System, Piping & Appurtenances, Storage Tanks, Cross country waters and Hydrocarbon Pipelines,  System Automation and EPC of BioCNG plant for the past two decades. The company has executed over 120 projects and has about 10 – 15 ongoing projects at any time.
