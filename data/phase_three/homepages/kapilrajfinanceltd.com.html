About Us

Welcome To Kapilraj Finance Ltd

Kapil Raj Finance Limited was incorporated as a public limited company under the provisions of the Companies Act, 1956 on December 13, 1985 & obtained the Certificate for Commencement of Business on June 24, 1986 from the Registrar of Companies, Delhi & Haryana. The Company’s name has not been changed since incorporation.

The Company has been in the business for the last 29 years and has seen many ups and down in the economy of India. The Company follows a systematic process for planning and implementation of its strategies. The Company is exposed to specific risks that are particular to its business and the environment within which it operates, including interest rate volatility, economic cycle, credit risk and market risk. The measurement, monitoring management of risk remains key focus areas for the company.
