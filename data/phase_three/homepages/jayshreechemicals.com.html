Welcome To JCL

Jayshree Chemicals Ltd. (JCL) is a widely held public limited company incorporated on 17th April 1962 in the state of West Bengal. The company was involved in Chlor Alkali manufacturing business for about 50 years. This business was hived off due to commercial consideration. All the major activities of the company include chemical trading and other miscellaneous activities.
