Multi-layer Quality Checks That Inspire Trust

At Premco Global, quality is embedded across every stage of manufacturing. Through rigorous inspections, advanced in-house testing and internationally accredited third-party verification, we ensure precision, consistency and compliance throughout the entire supply chain, from raw material sourcing to final shipment. Our end-to-end quality assurance framework guarantees reliable products that meet global standards and client expectations.

Raw Material Inspection

All incoming raw materials and components are thoroughly inspected to verify quality, consistency, and compliance before entering production.

Pre-Production (PP) Sampling

PP samples are rigorously tested to ensure design accuracy, technical specifications, and performance standards align with client requirements before bulk manufacturing begins

Bulk Production & Shipment Testing

Continuous quality monitoring is conducted during manufacturing, followed by final bulk audits and compliance checks before shipment dispatch. Through a robust cycle of inspection, testing and verification, Premco Global consistently delivers products that meet international standards and reinforce client trust.

Our Story

50 Years of Craft.
Built for Global Scale.

What started in 1966 as a focused operation in elastics manufacturing has grown into a publicly listed global group, trusted by some of the world's most recognisable fashion and industrial brands.

🏭 Machinery

Our production lines are powered by the finest elastics machinery sourced from Germany, Switzerland, Italy, and Taiwan — each chosen for precision, reliability, and low-defect output that global compliance demands.

  • Jacquard weaving machines — Germany & Switzerland

  • High-speed knitting lines — Italian & Taiwanese

  • INNO SOFT finishing — 54% less energy, near water-free

  • FLEXIRIG technology — alternating rigid-elastic innovation

  • 300 million meters annual group capacity

  • 3 compliant factories · India & Vietnam

📦 Packing & Dispatch

We know that how your product arrives matters as much as how it performs. Premco offers multiple packing formats tailored to your production line requirements — reducing handling time and protecting quality through to end-use.

  • Roll packing — standard format for bulk

  • Festooning — for automated cut & sew

  • Layer packing — garment-ready

  • Bobbin packing — precision small quantities

  • Custom cut lengths — to your spec

  • Export-ready with full documentation
