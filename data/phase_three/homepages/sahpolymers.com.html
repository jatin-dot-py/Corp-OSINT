Leading PP Woven Bags Manufacturer

Aeroflex Neu Limited (formerly Sah Polymers Limited) is a leading PP Woven Bags Manufacturer in India (Udaipur, Rajasthan). Since the past 25 years we have been redefining packaging with our state of the art Infrastructure, which helps us meet the needs of the modern world. We manufacture and export PP Woven Bags and HDPE Box Bags, Flexible Intermediate Bulk Containers (FIBCs), and BOPP Bags. With our hard work and perseverance we are now known as the top exporters of PP woven bags, fabric and box bags. Due to the team’s persistent efforts we now have a strong hold in the Indian market & export out products across Europe, Africa, Middle East, Australia, South–East Asia, Caribbean Island, Latin & Central America, and the USA. This has helped us gain a position as leading exporters of PP Woven Box Bags worldwide.

Know More

Leading the Requirement of the Packaging Industry since 25+ Years

Our manufacturing plant located in Udaipur, Rajasthan (India) is an 80,000 sq. ft. state-of-the-art facility ensuring products go into inspection at every stage of manufacturing to deliver world-class quality. We also provide customization facilities that give our customers the freedom to make the best use of our PP Woven Bags. Our Research & Development teams use innovative approaches to facilitate the manufacturing of our products to ensure they have a high strength-to-density ratio for durability and reliability. 

With years of continuous progression, we have successfully extended our product range which now includes Unlaminated bags, Laminated Bags, Valve Bags, Gusseted Bags, Perforated Bags, Liner Inserted Bags, Multi Stripe/Multi Colour Bags & Fabric Rolls. Our range of products Caters to the Packaging Requirements of the world’s leading Industries like: Agro Pesticides Industries, Basic Drug Industries, Cattle Feed Industries, Cement Industries, Chemical Industries, Food Products Industries, Metal Industries, Mineral Industries, Paper Mill Industries, Textile Industries, Tyre Industries, Fertilizer Industries, Salt Industries & Many More.
