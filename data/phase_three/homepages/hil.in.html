What makes us different?

We offer it all, from pipes, construction chemicals, putty, roofs, walls to floors, delivering comprehensive solutions.
With over seven decades of expertise, we are a global leader in home and building solutions, driven by technology, innovation and excellence. Our legacy of building iconic projects, extensive retail presence and deep community engagement make us a trusted industry leader.

InnovationSustainabilityPeople
Innovation

Every breakthrough we pursue is inspired by the needs, aspirations and experiences of our customers. We continuously innovate, pioneering tech driven solutions that address real world challenges and set new industry benchmarks.

From Parador’s eco-friendly Modular ONE flooring to TruFiT leakproof pipe technology, our innovations are built for the evolving needs. Our Ultra Cool Roofs combat heat for better comfort and energy efficiency, while Aerocon AAC blocks and panels enable faster, stronger and sustainable construction. Our advanced Organic Based Stabilizer (OBS) for pipes and innovative electrofusion with integrated service saddle ensure greater durability, reliability and efficiency.

We don’t just evolve to progress, we pioneer it.

Our sustainability efforts focus on reducing our footprint across our products, energy usage, production and materials. We uphold strict environmental and safety standards, ensuring responsible manufacturing and ethical sourcing. By designing long-lasting, high-quality products, we promote a more sustainable future.

We drive circularity by converting industrial waste like fly ash, pond ash, slag and recycled pulp into raw materials while pioneering greener solutions like OBS for pipes, innovations like Aerocon AAC Blocks and OPVC.

Pipes enhance sustainability and for flooring, we source 87% of our engineered wood from FSC and PEFC certified forests.

Our zero discharge plants safeguard the environment. We set industry benchmarks for lowest energy consumption per unit of production and 80% of our energy comes from renewables.

Industry leading certifications like Blauer Engel, FSC, PEFC, EMAS III, GRIHA and GreenPro recognize our efforts, helping us to fulfill our purpose of improving lives for generations to come.

We’re shaping a new, sustainable world.

At BirlaNu, we are driven by a strong value system and a legacy of excellence from the CKA Birla Group. We foster a vibrant, inclusive and technology led workplace that empowers our people.

Recognized as a Great Place to Work across Asia and India, we not only offer career mobility across locations but also flexible working options and a focus on health and wellness for our diverse and global workforce.

With transparent communication, progressive policies and a commitment to continuous learning, we provide a platform for individuals to shape their future and create lasting impact. Through talent development programs like LEGO, LEAP and LEAD, we invest in our people’s growth.
