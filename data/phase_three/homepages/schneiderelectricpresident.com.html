Schneider Electric President Systems

Over the last 30 years, President Systems is India’s leading designer, manufacturer and supplier of standard and customized enclosure systems produced to address essential needs of rack-mount IT critical equipment in a variety of IT environments. President offers a wide range of enclosures for server and networking applications for IT/Networking and ITES, Telecom, General and Industrial Electronics sectors’ infrastructure, systems management and operations. The enclosures are engineered to meet the latest IT standards for better management of space, airflow, and power supply integration.

Products

President's Enclosures are designed for server and network housing and caters to mid-ranged IT requirements, built with high-quality materials, cost-effective, and help secure the IT environments. They are engineered for easy assembly and rapid equipment integration at the site while being extremely robust and flexible. These enclosures are an ideal choice for mid-range use as a server enclosure or network cabling purposes. They are Vendor-neutral EIA-310/DIN 41494 19-inch rack mounting compatibility. They are optimized for high-capacity cable management systems.

Cyberack

Cyberack is a rack enclosure engineered for easy assembly and rapid equipment integration at the site while being extremely robust and flexible. The design is based on the use of aluminum profiles for the four vertical pillars, which are connected to two welded steel end-frames. It is an ideal choice for mid-range use as a server enclosure or for network cabling purposes.

Uniserv DLX

Uniserv DLX combines a highly attractive appearance with robust construction makes these cabinets less susceptible to damage during transportation. The frame is made of heavy-grade aluminum profiles designed to accept front and rear doors which close within the frame itself.
 

Read More

Smart Rack

Smart Rack is an innovative enclosure solution designed for small-and-medium segment customers and offices. Keeping in view the need for economical packaging, it can accommodate any standard 19” equipment. Made completely of high-quality steel, with appealing aesthetics, Smart Rack can be supplied in completely knocked-down condition (CKD) and can be easily assembled at the site.

Flexibox

Flexibox cabinets are thoughtfully designed, easy-to-manage solutions for LAN/WAN applications. They are manufactured with many innovative features, assembly of the FlexiBox at the site can be done by any non-technical person in a few minutes, with the use of commonly available tools.
