          Force Motors

          Pioneering Shared Passenger Mobility Solutions
          Over 6 Decades

          Founded in 1958 by Shri N. K. Firodia, we set out with a vision to make transportation accessible, reliable and efficient for the masses.
          Today, Force Motors is a fully integrated automobile company specializing in the design, development and manufacturing of vehicles, aggregates and components catering to the diverse customer needs globally.

          Led by Dr. Abhay Firodia, Force Motors endevours to build best-in-class shared mobility solutions that keep pace with the changing demands of the customers across India and the world

          Our Legacy

          1958

          Production of Tempo 3-wheeler: ‘Hanseat’ started at Goregaon, Mumbai.

          1988

          Force Motors introduced its rough road vehicle -The Tempo Trax.

          2006

          New state-of-the-art Heavy Commercial Vehicle manufacturing facility was inaugurated at Pithampur.

          2013

          Force Motors introduced the iconic Gurkha, the extreme off-roader.

          2019

          Force Motor’s Monobus was introduced.

          2022

          Force Motors introduced URBANIA – a premium shared mobility solution.

          2024

          Force Motors launched the all-new 3-door and 5-door Gurkha.
