About Us

Overview

Thacker and Company Ltd was established in 1878 as a financing company to cater the needs of private & public Limited Companies, proprietary & partnership firms in terms of granting loans & advances. It has over the tide of time expanded into a five-fold scope of operations, which are known as the Thacker 5 . As one of the oldest listed companies on the Bombay Stock Exchange Thacker grew from a trading company to one that has interests in Investments, Finance, Trading, Information Technology and FMCG – Thus the Thacker 5.

Thacker and Company now owned by the Jatia Group is holding company of Fujisan Technologies Ltd launched in 2004. Thacker and Company strives to stay ever forward.

About

Thacker and Co. Ltd.

Thacker and Company Ltd was established in 1878 as a financing company to cater the needs of private & public Limited Companies, proprietary & partnership firms in terms of granting loans & advances. It has over the tide of time expanded into a five-fold scope of operations, which are known as the Thacker 5 .

As one of the oldest listed companies on the Bombay Stock Exchange Thacker grew from a trading company to one that has interests in Investments, Finance, Trading, Information Technology and FMCG – Thus the Thacker 5.

Thacker and Company now owned by the Jatia Group is holding company of Fujisan Technologies Ltd launched in 2004. Thacker and Company strives to stay ever forward.
