About the company

A Legacy of Engineering Excellence

Since 1969, MTAR Technologies has been a trusted name in high-precision engineering, with unmatched expertise in both conventional and CNC machining. With 600+ advanced CNC machines achieving micron-level tolerances (5–10 microns), we are among the few globally with such capabilities.  Our world-class in-house solutions encompass machining, fabrication, heat and surface treatments, vacuum brazing, welding, painting, assembly and testing. Our expertise extends to manufacturing Electro-Mechanical, Electro-Pneumatic, and Electro-Hydraulic systems for critical applications in Aerospace and Energy sectors.

CSR

Empowering people, enriching communities

MTAR is committed to integrating corporate social responsibility into every aspect of its operations. Upholding our integrity is fundamental as we continue to expand our business potential. At MTAR, nurturing talent is a priority and we are dedicated to fostering growth through continuous training and knowledge transfer, contributing to both the personal and professional development of our employees. In addition, we actively engage in CSR activities that benefit the community, including organizing medical camps and encouraging students to pursue careers in science and technology. These initiatives reflect our dedication to making a positive impact on society.
