About Us

India's Pre-eminent manufacturer of automotive Components

Established in 1956 by Mr. JP Minda.

Founded in 1956 by Mr. JP Minda, the JP Minda Group has built itself as the premier choice for mobility in India over the last sixty years, dedicated to offering tailor-made solutions for our clients.

We have expanded our offerings to include a wide range of services, including our original equipment manufacturer (OEM) capabilities, automated paint shop systems, car accessories, and many more.

Our Commitment

In situations where you prefer to place a report in confidence, the information you provide will be sent to us on a totally confidential basis and can be done anonymously if desired. You have our guarantee that your comments will be heard
