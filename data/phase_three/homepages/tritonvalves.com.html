TOUCHING LIVES. MOVING INDIA.

Triton Valves was established in 1975 by our visionary founder M.V.Gokarn. Triton began manufacturing valves for the tyre and inner tube industry in 1978 and quickly emerged a market leader in India. As the automobile industry grew in India, Triton grew into India’s largest manufacturer of tyre valves. With the advent of tubeless tyres Triton began manufacturing tubeless tyre valves and evolved from a Tier 2 to a Tier 1 supplier to the automobile industry. With its strong focus on product quality and R & D, Triton has now evolved into a precision manufacturer and critical supplier of valves and components to a diverse range of customers and industries from air conditioning and hydraulics to aerospace, mining, defence and industrial HVAC & R.

We are headquartered in Bengaluru India and our manufacturing facilities and R & D Center are located in Mysuru. We also operate warehouses in Bengaluru, Chennai and Delhi in order to serve our customers with Just-In-Time deliveries. All our facilities conform to IATF 16949, ISO 14001 and BS OHSAS 18001 standards.

INNOVATION

At Triton, innovation is at the centre of everything we do.

At Triton we believe that innovation is more than just making small improvements in the course of business. We measure our innovations by the transformation ushered in by our efforts to solve a problem or to address the needs of our customers. We foster and encourage our team members to challenge themselves to push the limits of their imagination. We also let them know that it’s okay to fail.  In the process of innovating, we have often been able to resolve technical and physical contradictions. In a few cases we have even been able to develop completely new technologies.

ONLINE RETAIL CHANNEL

Making OEM specification tyre valves available to even the smallest tyre and puncture repair shops.

The aftermarket for tyre valves in India has traditionally been served with low quality products which do not meet industry standards, thereby risking the lives and safety of vehicle users.

With the objective of raising awareness about the significance of good quality tyre valves for road safety, Triton has launched its own e-commerce B2B platform through which we are able to fill the vacuum in the tyre service industry and make the best quality OEM specification tyre valves available to even the smallest puncture repair or tyre shop anywhere in India.
