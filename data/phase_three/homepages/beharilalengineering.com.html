Global Market

Our biggest market is India. Yet, Behari Lal Engineering Limited (BLEL) is positioned to deliver Special Steel's Quality Products all over the world.

...

A renowned name in the steel industry, Behari Lal Engineering Limited is among the top manufacturers and suppliers of high-quality steel and steel products. These steel products are being regularly used in various industries including mining, infrastructure development, cement plants, sugar factories, wind energy projects, rubber mills, water dam projects, paper mills, automotive, sponge iron plants, hot and cold rolling mills, and forging hammers.

The array of steel products produced by Behari Lal Engineering Limited includes Metal Rolls in various grades. Some of the specialties of the company are Alloy Cast Steel Rolls, Alloy Steel Base Adamite Rolls, S. G. Iron Pearlitic Rolls, S. G. Iron Bainitic/Acicular Rolls, and Indefinite Chill Cast Iron Rolls. Being a major HSS roll, Adamite rolls, Alloy Steel Rolls, and Adamite Rolls manufacturer in India, we make sure that our quality speaks better of us. We also deal in Alloy Steel Rounds, SAE 8620 alloy steel, FHASAE 8622 round bar, SAE 52100 alloy steel, SAE 52100 annealed round, SAE 4140 annealed round, and SAE 4140 quench and temper round. We employ round heat treatment, round annealing treatment, and round steel quenching to produce the best steel rounds in India.

Flats from Behari Lal Engineering Limited are also being extensively used in multiple industry applications. Furthermore, the company also has a division for producing engineering castings in various grades per unit weight ranging from 500kgs to 15MT for core industrial sectors. We also own a bar quenching and tempering facility wherein heat treat bars are being regularly manufactured. These include heat-treated bars, F91 grade alloy steel, P91 alloy steel bars, IF2 carbon steel round bars, SS410, SS420 stainless steel bars, SS430 stainless steel bars, EN16 alloy steel round bar SCM 435, SCM 415 alloy steel, EN353 alloy steel bright bar, EN355 round bars, EN31 alloy steel, EN31 annealed round,EN41 alloy steel, EN1A steel.

Behari Lal Engineering Limited is also a DB6 tool steel manufacturer that is of import quality. We have DB6 die steel, DB6 die round bars, and DB6 forged quench and temper steel. We also deal in forged quench and temper steel. Rounds, dies and blocks are being manufactured precisely using state-of-the-art technology. Furthermore, we are amongst the top H13 steel, H11 steel, SAE 4130 steel, SAE 4140 steel,EN24 steel, F11 steel, F12 steel, andF22 steel manufacturers in India.

All this is possible only because of the quality standards maintained at Behari Lal Engineering Limited. We use top-grade raw materials and state-of-the-art manufacturing technology to meet industry-specific standards. The company's manufacturing plant comprises a Fully Integrated Digital Steel Melting Shop with Vacuum Degassing (VD), Foundry, Heat Treatment, and Machine Shop. Furthermore, we have a dedicated team of engineers and technicians who ensure that each of the finished products is tested thoroughly for quality. Also, the company has a physical testing laboratory, a chemical analysis laboratory, a sand laboratory, and a research and development branch to ensure that only the best goes out of the company’s premises.
