JTEKT HISTORY

JTEKT INDIA LIMITED JTEKT BEARINGS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED JTEKT MICROMATIC MACHINERY INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED
  • 1984 Establishment of Sona Steering Systems Limited
  • 1985 Signed a license agreement with Koyo Seiko (Now JTEKT Corporation), Japan
  • 1992 Company name changed to Sona Koyo Steering Systems Limited
  • 1998 Established manufacturing facility at Chennai
  • 2007 Established manufacturing facility at Dharuhera, Rewari (DHR 1)
  • 2010 Established manufacturing facility at Dharuhera, Rewari (DHR 2)
  • 2011 Established manufacturing facility at Dharuhera, Rewari (DHR 3)
  • 2018 Company name changed to JTEKT India Limited
  • 2019 Merger of JTEKT Sona Automotive Limited with JTEKT India Limited
  • 2022 Start Production of Constant Velocity Joints (CVJ)
  • 2024 Merger of JTEKT Fujikiko Automotive India Limited with JTEKT India Limited
  • 2009 Established Koyo Bearings India Private Limited
  • 2012 Deliveries of Indian-made bearings to customers begin
  • 2013 Start Production of hub unit bearings
  • 2023 Company name changed to JTEKT Bearings India Private Limited
  • 2008 Established Toyoda Micromatic Machinery India Private Limited
  • 2009 Start Business Office at Bangalore
  • 2023 Company name changed to JTEKT Micromatic Machinery India Private Limited

JTEKT BASIC PRINCIPLE

JTEKT has formulated the "JTEKT BASIC PRINCIPLE" with the idea that we will continue to be a company that solves social issues through our business activities and contributes "for the earth, for the world, and for our customers."

Each employees have passion, actively "communication", and "participation and ownership" to practice "continuous improvement".

JTEKT sticks to "safety first, quality second" and "No.1. & Only One", and will continue to contribute to the earth, the social, and our customers" for an ever-expanding future, and create happiness beyond that.
