Who We Are?
We are one of the leading Infrastructure companies of India, focusing on serving central and state road authorities, for construction, operation and maintenance of road infrastructure in India.
An enterprise founded by the Mhaiskar family, MEP Infrastructure Developers Limited is a Mumbai headquartered organisation with presence across 15 States in the country. Established in 2002, as a Toll Management Company, MEP today is an end-to-end road infrastructure company, covering construction, operation and maintenance of road and road assets in India.

What we do?
We are one of the few Indian Infrastructure companies who have the domain expertise to take on projects in road construction, maintenance and toll collection, both independently and collectively.
Our esteemed clientele includes national and state authorities like National Highways Authority and India (NHAI), Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MORTH), Road Infrastructure Development Company of Rajasthan Limited (RIDCOR), Maharashtra State Road Development Corporation (MSRDC), Maharashtra State Road Development Corporation (RSRDC), HRBC (Hooghly River Bridge Commissioners), Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD) to name a few.
One of the leading players in the Hybrid Annuity space, Operation, maintenance and transfer as well as toll coleltion of road projects in the country.
