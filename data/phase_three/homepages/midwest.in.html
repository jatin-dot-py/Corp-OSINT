Introduction

Shaping Tomorrow's Materials, Today.

With over four decades of experience across the dimensional stone value chain with mine-to-distribution capabilities, Midwest Ltd. continues to evolve, diversifying into high-value materials like quartz and heavy mineral sands. These initiatives aim to power industries such as building materials, renewable energy, semi-conductors, titanium-based industries and more.

  • A legacy of over four decades of experience in the dimensional natural stone industry, covering exploration, mining, processing, sales, distribution, marketing and export of natural stones.

  • Mines over 1.38 million cubic meters of granite (an average of fiscal 22, 23, 24 and 25) per annum including saleable and waste granite.

  • India's largest producer and exporter of Black Galaxy Granite—a premium variety of Granite, exporting 64% of the overall Black Galaxy Granite export volume in FY 2025.

  • Highest Royality is levied on Black Galaxy Granite, establishing its position as a premium material. Ability to fetch superior market pricing due to substantial market share of this high demand granite.

  • Strong distribution network with a customer base spread across 17 countries and five continents. Midwest serves as an organized player in an otherwise unorganized and fragmented market

  • Employing sustainable mining practices with solar-powered machinery and induction of electric tipper trucks, reducing maintenance, downtime, and emissions.

  • 23.3 million tonnes of large quartz resources, with planned production expansion of 2.42 lacs MTPA.

  • 4 exploration licenses obtained in Sri Lanka to extract and produce 37,000 metric tonne per annum of minerals such as monazite, Ilmenite, zircon, garnet, sillimanite and rutile.

  • One of India’s Largest producers of Absolute Black Granite, accounting for 15.7% of the overall Black Granite production in India during FY 2025.

  • Operational efficiencies through reduced diesel use, optimized spare parts, and captive solar power; piloting electric dump trucks to lower logistics costs.

  • A Strong track record of consistent growth and profitability, with strong sales visibility, typically backed by customer advances and LC’s.

  • With Phase I, Company will be one of the few players worldwide, which incorporate a mine to market integrated business model for production of Quartz grit and powder*

  • A 1200+ strong team of geologists, engineers, R&D, and finance professionals ensuring excellence across the value chain.

  • Leading contributor of Black Galaxy Granite royalties, between 2020 and 2025, accounting for 26.67% of the total royalty paid for Black Galaxy Granite during FY 2025.

  • Significant establishment costs and long gestation periods favor established players, guided by experienced promoters and a professional management team.

  • Focus on R&D and technology with initiatives like an eco-friendly diamond tools plant and a co-developed electric dressing station with an Italian company.

Source: CRISIL Report

Quick About

Transforming Raw Materials into Strategic Assets.

As a vertically integrated, high-tech processor, we transform raw materials like granite, quartz, and precision-crafted diamond wire tools into advanced, strategic assets for industries worldwide. From engineered stone to solar glass, high-performance titanium, and cutting-edge tools, our innovative processes add value at every step—delivering premium materials that fuel growth in sectors such as construction, aerospace, semiconductors, and renewable energy.

What We Deliver

Mastering the Materials Ecosystem, from Source to Solution.

Our resources, advanced technologies, and focus on sustainable practices support industries globally, with an emphasis on long-term growth and reliability.

Strategic Market Position

With over 23% of the global export market for Black Galaxy Granite, Midwest has the ability to fetch better market pricing through its substantial market share. Entry barriers are high with the company having old mining rights and entrenched operations.

Extracting Excellence

We extract the finest raw materials with precision, ensuring that our products meet the highest standards in global markets. For us, quality isn’t just guaranteed—it’s crafted into every part of the process.

End-to-End Expertise

From extraction to processing to delivery, we manage the entire value chain, focusing on consistency, quality, and sustainability. We support every step, maintaining reliability for global industries.

Operational Efficiencies

We are committed to reducing costs through electrification of machinery, solar integration, and the deployment of electric dump trucks. This focus on resource optimization not only lowers consumption but also enhances sustainability across operations.

Exclusive Resources

We mine quarries of Black Galaxy Granite, a highly coveted natural stone available only in Chimakurthy village of the Ongole district in Andhra Pradesh, India. Known for its premium quality, it is used globally in high-end applications and quotes the highest royalty among all dimensional stones.

Robust Distribution Network

Midwest has mine-to-distribution capabilities spanning the dimensional granite value chain. It operates within a market that is predominantly unorganized and fragmented, providing solutions across the value chain.

Technology-led Precision

We have implemented an ERP system integrated with real-time sensor monitoring on machinery, enabling data-driven decision-making to enhance automation and minimize manual intervention.

Research & Development

We have a strong R&D focus, which supports backward integration with environmentally friendly diamond tools for both captive use and industry consumption. This emphasis on innovation allows us to stay at the forefront of technology while minimizing our environmental impact.

Expansion & Acquisition

With a growing reserve base of 21.5 million CBM and continuously adding mines to the pipe line, we balance the reserve depletion and addition ratio in mines. Our acquisition of Black Galaxy Granite deposits helps retain our market leadership position.
