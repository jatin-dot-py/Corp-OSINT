    Leading Intermediate Bulk Container Manufacturers in India

     

    We specialise in the manufacturing of various packaging products, including intermediate bulk containers (IBCs) ,MS Barrels, and Plastic Barrels. As a leading intermediate bulk container manufacturer, we provide durable and cost-effective bulk liquid containers ideal for storing and transporting a wide range of liquids, chemicals, and other industrial materials. 

    • Our IBC containers are designed with precision and adhere to international quality standards, ensuring the utmost safety and efficiency.
    • In addition to industrial bulk containers, we also manufacture MS barrels and plastic barrels. Our MS barrels offer robust construction and excellent durability, making them suitable for heavy-duty industrial applications. 
    • Our plastic barrels are lightweight yet strong and resilient, making them a reliable choice for various industries. Whether you need MS barrels, plastic barrels, or intermediate containers, we have the expertise and capacity to fulfil your specific packaging requirements.

    Namaste!

    I am glad to inform you that this year Pyramid Technoplast Ltd is celebrating its 23rd anniversary. I would like to thank my colleagues, co-founders, management team, technicians, and customers.     Our customer-first approach and teamwork helped us a lot to complete our 23-year journey. Under the leadership of our management, we have successfully launched innovative intermediate bulk containers in various forms that are ideal for bulk packaging in every industry, business, and product range within a short period of time.
