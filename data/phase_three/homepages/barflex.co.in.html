About Company

Preferred Supplier for Flexible Packaging

Incorporated in June 2005 , the Company manufactures flexible packaging material for a variety of industries including dairy, edible oil, beverages, processed foods, paints & adhesives, chemicals, cereals, snack foods etc. The Company is headquartered in Delhi NCR and has a manufacturing unit based in Himachal Pradesh with in-house designing, manufacturing, and assembly to finishing and packaging.
