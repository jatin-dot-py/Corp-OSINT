Sector We Served

Residential Solutions

SAR Televenture delivers complete digital and security solutions for modern living. With our Tower infra, FTTH Infra & Smart systems support, housing societies and residential complexes enjoy seamless internet, CCTV surveillance, intercom, and automation features. We design, deploy, and manage everything for a connected and safe lifestyle.

Commercial Solutions

For businesses, malls, offices, and IT parks, SAR Televenture provides custom-built connectivity and smart infrastructure. Our services include fiber networks, tower infra, and integrated security systems such as access control, boom barriers, and CCTV monitoring. These solutions ensure high-speed connectivity, reliable operations, and future-ready smart environments.
