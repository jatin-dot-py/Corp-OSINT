20 Years Of

Rich Legacy

Welcome to Sai Silks (Kalamandir) Limited (SSKL), a premier retail textile group founded in 2005 by Mr. Chalavadi Naga Kanaka Durga Prasad and Mrs. Chalavadi Jhansi Rani. Today, SSKL proudly stands as one of South India’s most trusted destinations for ethnic clothing and exquisite sarees.

Our story began in Hyderabad with our very first brand, “Kalamandir,” operating out of a modest 3,213-square-foot store. Driven by a deep passion for quality weavers and rich traditions, that single boutique sparked a remarkable journey. Today, our presence has blossomed across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu, growing into a flourishing network of 82 retail locations.

We are proud to weave elegance, tradition, and modern fashion into the lives of our customers through our expanding family of brands, which includes “Kalamandir,” “Mandir,” “Kancheepuram Varamahalakshmi Silks,” “KLM Fashion Mall,” “Valli Silks,” “Desi Sitara,” and “Rasamayi Jewellery.”

Mastering Retail

We are proud to say that we have transformed what was formerly a disorganised industry and an unpleasant shopping experience into an organised structure. With its many brands purposely designed for all segments of society, SSKL offers a one-of-a-kind shopping experience at an affordable price.
