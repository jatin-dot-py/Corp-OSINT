VIAZ TYRES

Viaz Tyres has been at the forefront of crafting superior quality Butyl tubes for a wide range of vehicles, from reliable Bicycle tyres to high performance inner tubes for 2 & 3 wheeler motorcycles, passenger cars, light commercial vehicles, OTR, and industrial machinery vehicles.

We take pride in our commitment to excellence in Manufacturing, offering premium tyre tube solutions with a strong focus on reliability and innovation. Our dedication to providing affordable tyre tube options and becoming a trusted supplier and exporter has earned us top ratings in Libya, India, Oman, USA, Dubai, Turkey, Romania, Sudan, Egypt, Brazil and Colombia . With modern, high performance vehicle tubes, we serve as the go to source for bulk tyre tube needs, addressing various commercial requirements.

Additionally, we are proud to announce our global presence by launching our subsidiary company, Autobots Trading FZC, in Dubai, U.A.E. further enhancing our reach and capabilities in foreign markets.
