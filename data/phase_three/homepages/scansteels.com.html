About Scan Steels Ltd

Scan Steels Limited is a self-sufficient secondary steel manufacturer, mainly producing TMT rods under the brand name of “SHRISHTII TMT” which is used in various construction activities across different sectors. It was incorporated on 11.12.1990 as a private limited company and was subsequently converted to a public limited company on 18.03.1996 and its commercial operations started on 01.04.1997. The company was a pioneer in starting DRI units of smaller capacity in the state of Odisha in the year 1999 and has grown to be the largest TMT manufacturing company in Odisha through secondary route of steel making. It is now a listed company in BSE, having a market capitalization of approximately 200 crores as per current share price.

2008

During 1998-2008, Scan Steels Limited expanded its production base and started manufacturing DRI and ventured into power generation, utilizing the waste heat generated from the DRI KILNs to establish itself as a secondary steel manufacturing integrated steel plant.

2010

Scan Steels Limited further expanded its production base through the acquisition of DRI KILNs in the state of Karnataka at Bellary.

2017

Scan Steels Limited expanded its finished products portfolio and started manufacturing Structures (angles, flats, square bars, etc.), Galvanized Structures, Binding Wires and PVC coated roofing sheets under the brand name of “SHRISHTII ROOFING”.
