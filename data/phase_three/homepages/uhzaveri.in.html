    Our Story

    A Premier Jewellery Store in Nikol Ahmedabad

    U.H.Zaveri Limited was founded in 1999 and has been a valued Jewellery Store in Ahmedabad ever since. Since we opened our doors, we’ve gained a reputation for being friendly, approachable and affordable, which keeps our customers coming back to mark their special occasions with us.

    ​

    We carry custom items created by our own bench jeweler as well as seasonal collections from some of the most well-known jewelry brands. No matter what type of jewelry you’re looking for, you’ll be able to find it at U.H.Zaveri Limited.
