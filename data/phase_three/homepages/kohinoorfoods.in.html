    Frequently Asked Questions

    What products does Kohinoor Foods offer?
    Kohinoor Foods offers a broad portfolio of authentic Indian food products, including premium Basmati rice, everyday rice, organic foods, ready-to-eat meals, ready-to-cook products, frozen breads, frozen snacks, frozen curries, rice treats, paneer, chakki atta and more. The company also works with retailers and distributors to develop customised private label products across multiple food categories.
    Does Kohinoor Foods supply internationally?
    Yes. Kohinoor Foods supplies its products to customers in more than 60 countries across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Asia-Pacific and other international markets. With an established global supply network and long-standing retail partnerships, the company serves importers, distributors, retailers and food businesses around the world.
    Does Kohinoor Foods provide private label manufacturing?
    Yes. Kohinoor Foods offers comprehensive private label manufacturing for businesses looking to launch or expand their own food brands. From product selection and customised packaging to quality control, manufacturing and international logistics, the company provides end-to-end support across rice and a wide range of Indian food products.
    What quality standards does Kohinoor Foods follow?
    Quality is built into every stage of Kohinoor Foods' manufacturing process, from sourcing and processing to packaging and distribution. The company operates dedicated quality control laboratories and follows internationally recognised food safety standards, with manufacturing facilities certified to HACCP and ISO standards, along with compliance for FSSAI, US FDA, Kosher and other recognised quality requirements. Every batch is produced with a strong focus on consistency, food safety and traceability.
