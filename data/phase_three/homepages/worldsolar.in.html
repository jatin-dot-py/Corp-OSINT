SOLARWORLD POWERING INDIA'S SUSTAINABLE FUTURE

At SOLARWORLD, we offer a comprehensive suite of renewable energy solutions designed to meet the diverse needs of our clients. With over 12 years of expertise and a project portfolio exceeding 1 GW, we are recognized for delivering the highest standards of quality, performance, and reliability.

Our services include end-to-end Solar EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction), Solar Park Development, and the deployment of Rooftop and Ground-Mounted Solar Projects, providing customized energy solutions for government organizations, industries, commercial enterprises, and institutions. As a trusted partner to industry leaders like NTPC, SJVN, RVPNL, GUVNL, Haldiram’s, Coca-Cola Bottlers, and KRBL, we have consistently demonstrated our ability to deliver complex energy projects with precision and excellence.

As one of India’s top solar module manufacturers, our state-of-the-art facilities in Roorkee, Uttarakhand, feature an annual production capacity of 1.8 GW, offering a wide portfolio of high-efficiency monocrystalline bifacial and N-Type TOPCon solar modules.

We also specialize in advanced Grid-Class Energy Storage Systems, available in 20-ft and 40-ft containerized formats, designed to boost energy resilience and ensure grid stability.

As an Independent Power Producer (IPP), SOLARWORLD is currently developing a 650 MWh battery storage project and over 900 MW of solar power projects—contributing directly to India’s clean energy transition and self-reliance.
