International Financial Services Centre (IFSC)
The International Financial Services Centre (IFSC) at GIFT City is transforming India into a global financial powerhouse. Strategically located between Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar, this state-of- the-art financial hub rivals’ international centers like Singapore and Dubai. It enables seamless international banking, insurance, and capital market transactions in multiple currencies, attracting leading global financial institutions.
Governed by the International Financial Services Centers Authority (IFSCA), the IFSC offers a streamlined regulatory framework, ensuring ease of doing business. It provides powerful incentives, including tax exemptions on capital gains and dividends, along with a reduced Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT), making it a highly profitable destination for international investors.
Equipped with cutting-edge infrastructure, secure data centers, and world-class trading platforms like India INX and NSE IFSC, the IFSC delivers unmatched efficiency and security. Its strategic location, with excellent connectivity to international airports, further enhances its appeal to global financial giants. The IFSC at GIFT City is not just a financial hub; it is a catalyst for economic growth, driving foreign investment, creating employment, and integrating India into the global financial ecosystem. With its bold vision and strategic advantages, it is set to redefine India’s position on the world financial map.

Tax and Regulatory Advantages
Tax and Regulatory Advantages are powerful incentives that make GIFT City a preferred destination for global businesses and investors. As India’s first International Financial Services Centre (IFSC), GIFT City offers a favorable regulatory environment and attractive tax benefits, enhancing profitability and ease of doing business.
Businesses operating within the IFSC enjoy tax exemptions on capital gains, dividends, and interest income, along with a reduced Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT) rate. There is also a 100% tax holiday for 10 consecutive years out of the first 15 years, providing significant cost savings. Additionally, exemptions from Securities Transaction Tax (STT), Commodity Transaction Tax (CTT), and GST on certain financial services make GIFT City highly competitive compared to other global financial hubs.
The regulatory framework, governed by the International Financial Services Centers Authority (IFSCA), ensures streamlined compliance and operational flexibility, encouraging international banks, insurance companies, and financial institutions to set up operations. These strategic tax and regulatory benefits make GIFT City an ideal environment for global business expansion and investment.

GIFT City: Catalyzing Gujarat’s Economic Transformation
GIFT City (Gujarat International Finance Tec-City) is India’s pioneering smart city and a financial powerhouse located in Gujarat. Launched in 2007 as a strategic joint venture between the Government of Gujarat and Infrastructure Leasing and Financial Services (IL&FS), GIFT City was envisioned as a world-class financial hub designed to attract global financial services companies to India.
×
Unmatched Advantages of Investing in GIFT City
GIFT City stands out as a premier investment destination, offering unparalleled benefits: 
Prime Location: Close to major business hubs like Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Surat, and Mumbai, GIFT City is a strategic gateway for domestic and international trade. 
Business-Friendly Ecosystem: The city provides world-class infrastructure tailored for financial and tech companies, along with modern office spaces, premium amenities, and robust communication networks. 
Attractive Tax Incentives: GIFT City offers a 10-year corporate tax holiday, customs and excise duty exemptions, and a streamlined single-window clearance system, significantly easing the business setup process.
Abundant Skilled Talent: Proximity to premier educational institutions ensures access to a highly skilled workforce, driving productivity and innovation.
Progressive Regulatory Framework: GIFT City operates under a unique regulatory regime, offering flexibility, simplified approvals, and multi-jurisdictional operations for financial products and services.
