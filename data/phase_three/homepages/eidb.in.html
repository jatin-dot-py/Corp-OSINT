Our Products

Tailor your packaging to your specific needs with our wide range of customizable options.


Close Top Drums

Configured with triple-seamed ends, these drums can be used for filling liquid & semi-liquid products. Our Close Top Drums are made of oil-free grade steel and are in high demand among steel barrel manufacturers in India.

Open Top Drums

These drums have completely removable tops for more access with triple seamed bottom mainly used to store solids and semi-solids. A preferred choice among chemical drum manufacturers.

Gooseneck Drums

These drums are specially designed to fit 80 drums in a 20 ft container, streamlining export logistics for drums and barrels manufacturers.

Composite Drums

These drums consist of MS drum lined with high-density polyethylene having thickness ranging from 1.00–1.5 mm. Ideal for clients needing food grade steel drum solutions.

Galvanised Drums

These drums are used for storage of extremely hazardous chemicals, strong solvents, fragrances, etc. A top-grade product from leading oil drum manufacturers in India.
