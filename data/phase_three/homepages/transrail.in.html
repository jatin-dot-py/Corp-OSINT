About us

Transrail is a leading turnkey engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) company with primary focus on power transmission and distribution business with 4 decades of experience in Construction and Manufacturing. With its headquarters in India, it is a global enterprise with a footprint in more than 60 Countries and 6 Continents.

Our Business Portfolio

Transmission Lines

Our Transmission Lines expertise includes design, engineering, manufacturing (of towers, monopoles and conductors), procurement, construction and commisioning of Transmission lines up to 1200kV on a turn key basis. We have a footprint in more than 60 countries.

Know More

Substations

We have executed various AIS and GIS substations on turnkey EPC basis in India and overseas. Our capabilites include construction of susbstations upto 765 kV through our expert internal teams.

Know More

Solar EPC

As a specialised globally operating EPC Company we also provide holistic solutions for Solar EPC including Solar farms, BESS, Pooling substations and grid connectivity.

Know More

Railways

In the railways business, we provide comprehensive solutions including overhead electrification, S&T systems, earthworks, track laying and other composite works.

Know More

Civil

We undertake complex civil infrastructure projects on a turnkey EPC basis, including bridges, tunnels, elevated roads and cooling towers with the help of our specilist teams.

Know More

Poles & Lighting

We have a vast product range for Pole & Lighting infrastructure including highmasts, Streetlights, Stadium lights, traffic lights, T&D monopoles, Railway portals etc. We are a manufacturers as well as SITC (supply, installation, testing and commissioning) service provider.

Our Strength

EPC

We provide turnkey EPC services across all our business verticals which includes Power Transmission, Renewables, Civil Construction, Railways and Poles & Lighting. It includes design, engineering, manufacturing (of towers, monopoles and conductors), procurement, construction and commisioning of various large scale projects.

Design and Engineering

We take pride in an Engineering focused organization with over 18,000 man month of experience in design and engineering. Design Engineering

We have an experienced team of designers and engineers who are specialists in each segment of our business. We also have access to industry leading software for design and engineering including software such as PLS Tower, PLS Poles, I tower, Bocad, Staad Pro, PLS Star, PLS CAAD, PLS Lit, DiLux, AGI 32, Autocad 3D etc.

Manufacturing

Our facilities are equipped with advanced machinery, including CNC machines from Italy and galvanising set-up from UK, ensuring precision and efficiency in production.

We have CE-certification and NABL accredation, reinforcing our focus on quality assurance and compliance.

Towers

We have multiple factories for manufacturing full range of transmission towers from 66 kV to 1200 kV, including Single, Double, and Multi Circuit configurations for all types of Conductor configurations.

Monopoles

At our Poles factory in Silvassa we specialize in designing, fabricating, and galvanizing all types of monopoles and external lighting products. With an in-house design team and advanced software, we create customized configurations.

Conductors

At our Conductor plant in Silvassa, we not only manufacture conventional conductors but also High Temperature Low Sag (HTLS) and other High performance conductors. We offer wide range of conductors, including AAAC, ACAR, AAC, ACSR, ACSR/AW, and HTLS conductors including HVCRC, Gap conductors, Al59 etc, designed for various voltage levels and environmental conditions. Our solutions ensure efficient energy transmission with minimal losses.

Tower Testing

Our tower testing facility can test 2 towers simultaneously and are equipped with advanced SCADA software technology. It can test all types of towers including – Self supporting, guyed types, monopoles etc. Our Tower Test bed can handle towers up to 85 meters in height, with base widths of up to 30 meters. We have tested more than 500 towers for clients across the globe.
