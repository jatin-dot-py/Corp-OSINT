MAHAMAYA LIFESCIENCES

Mahamaya was established in 2002. With the vast experience of the promoter in the agrochemical sector Mahamaya’s focus and investment were on registration of vital molecules that were important for Indian Agriculture. At Mahamaya, we strive to become a better model in the crop protection industry. It’s our responsibility to make the agricultural world more productive making pesticide usage more conducive and farmer friendly. We have grown with our farmers; it becomes our moral duty to thank them and promote quality agro products among them. As a responsible crop protection producer our pledge is to help the world reach the UN sustainable goals about zero hunger and double agricultural productivity and provide quality of life to the farmers.
