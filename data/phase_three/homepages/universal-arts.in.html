Universal Arts Limited

Welcome to Universal Arts Limited
Universal Arts was incorporated in 1995 and is engaged in the business of media such as TV serials, feature films production, trading in film rights, television software, etc. The company was formerly known as Goldmines Media Limited. Universal Arts Limited is based in Mumbai, India. The company offers movies to Doordarshan for onetime single telecast. The overseas rights are sold country-wise to various buyers. The registered office of the company is located at Mumbai.
