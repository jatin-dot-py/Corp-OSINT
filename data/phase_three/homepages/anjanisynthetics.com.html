Passion for Textiles, Commitment to Quality

Welcome to Anjani Synthetics Ltd.

Welcome to Anjani Synthetics Limited, your trusted partner in textile processing. We are an infrastructure-focused textile process house dedicated to high-quality textiles. Our extensive product portfolio encompasses a spectrum of materials, from natural fibres like Cotton and Viscose to synthetic and blended fabrics.
We nurture a culture of innovation and continuous improvement to ensure that every fabric we process meets the highest quality standards. Our journey of growth and success is a testament to the collective efforts of a dynamic and industrious duo – our skilful management and dedicated production team. Together, we drive the company forward.
About Us

Company Background

Founded in 1984 with a clear mission – to craft world-class textiles for the domestic market – ASL has built a reputation synonymous with quality and excellence.
Since our inception, we have remained dedicated to the pursuit of progress. We have constantly elevated our workforce, upgraded our infrastructure, and streamlined our operations to create an efficient and dynamic workflow.
Our growth journey is a testament to our enduring relationships within the industry and with our valued customers. These connections have been the bedrock of our success, driving us to new heights of achievement.
