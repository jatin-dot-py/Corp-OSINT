Neurosciences Imaging & Surgical Technology

Hannah Joseph Hospital is one of the first centres in Asia to adopt DTI Fibertracking 3.0, a revolutionary navigation tool for brain tumour surgeries. Other cutting-edge technologies include

High-Resolution Digital MRI and CT Imaging

Advanced diagnostic tools for precise neurological assessment and treatment planning

Hybrid Neuro Cathlab with 3D Image Booster

Multiple imaging modalities in Cathlab, enabling real-time, high-resolution imaging during procedures

Neuronavigation & Neurophysiological Monitoring

Enhancing surgical precision and patient safety through real-time imaging and functional monitoring

Endoscopic & Microscopic Brain and Spine Surgeries

Minimally invasive techniques that promote faster recovery and reduce surgical risks

Intraoperative Tumour Fluorescence & Video Angiography

Cutting edge techniques for tumour resection and applications in cerebrovascular surgery
