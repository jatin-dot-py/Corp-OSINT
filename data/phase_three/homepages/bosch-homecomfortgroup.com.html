Bosch Home Comfort Group

We are taking a leading role in the smart provision of heating, cooling and ventilation for homes and industry alike, our comprehensive portfolio provides innovative solutions for climate comfort in buildings.

With deep expertise and a passion for innovation, we leverage the power of our brands, products and regions to create the best climate comfort solutions for life.

One team - one mission

Since our strategic landmark HVAC acquisition last August, an exciting journey has begun: We are now the united Bosch Home Comfort Group! We are incredibly proud to have joined forces and grown into a formidable team of over 24,000 dedicated individuals spanning the globe.

This video captures the spirit and vision that drives us, showcasing how we're uniting strengths, brands, and people to unlock greater potential.

Together, we are building on decades of expertise and a strong portfolio of well-respected products and powerful brands. Our collective mission is to deliver unparalleled solutions and shape the future of climate comfort worldwide.

Our global brands

Bosch, Buderus, Hitachi and YORK® are our global brands – and, like our strong regional brands, they are all built on the same foundation: high-quality products and comprehensive service expertise. Together, they provide energy solutions tailored to local conditions in over 100 countries worldwide.
