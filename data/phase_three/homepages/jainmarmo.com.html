Welcome !!

Jain Marmo Industries was founded in 1981 by JAIN GROUP and is one of the largest producers and processors of Green Serpentine Marble Blocks and Slabs in the world. The company is headquartered at Udaipur in Rajasthan, the western State of India, which is the hub of stone industry. The brain behind the group’s success is Mr. Shrichand Jain, Promoter, whose vision has driven the company to where it stands today.
