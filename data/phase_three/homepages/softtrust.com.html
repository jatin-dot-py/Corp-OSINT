Our Solutions Ecosystem

We deliver innovative, secure, and scalable software solutions designed to drive digital transformation and operational excellence.

Core Banking Software

A comprehensive platform to manage end-to-end banking operations, customer accounts, and transactions efficiently.

Core NBFC Software

Streamlines loan origination with AI-driven eligibility assessment, automated underwriting, digital document verification, and configurable approval workflows.

GST Compliance Software

Enables accurate ITC reconciliation, efficient handling of high transaction volumes, and automated GST return preparation.

GST Suvidha Provider

GSPIndia offers secure, scalable GSP APIs for seamless GST compliance automation across ERP, accounting, fintech, & enterprise platforms.

ONDC Solutions

Enable banks and NBFCs to join an open, interoperable lending network that seamlessly connects lenders and borrowers across India.

SAP Business One

TFL offers SAP Business One services, including implementation, customization, and support, to improve SME operations and efficiency.

Loan Origination Software (LOS)

Streamline the entire lending lifecycle with automated workflows, faster approvals, and improved credit processing.

Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

Robust AML software with real-time transaction monitoring, fraud detection, and regulatory compliance for financial institutions.

Loans & Security Recovery

Streamline loan collections, NPA recovery, and security enforcement with automated recovery workflows and tracking.

Audit Management & Compliance Calendar

Comprehensive audit and compliance solutions to ensure regulatory adherence, risk management, and secure financial operations.

RBI Statutory Reporting Software

Automate statutory reporting and compliance submissions for RBI, NABARD, and other regulatory authorities.

Member Share Management System

Manage member accounts, share capital, dividends, and transactions efficiently for cooperative institutions.

BANL

Ensure real-time verification of beneficiary accounts before each RTGS and NEFT transaction for accurate and secure transfers.

E-Mandate

Enable secure, automated, and paperless mandate-based collections for EMIs, subscriptions, and recurring financial transactions.

IMPS

Enable 24x7 instant fund transfers with a centralized IMPS switch that integrates with your existing CBS.

UPI

Enable secure, scalable, and NPCI-compliant UPI services with Trust Fintech's UPI Issuer Switch ASP Solution.

E-KYC Setu

Enable secure, NPCI-aligned digital KYC workflows via a centralized switch-based service with CBS API integration.

Mobile Banking Application

Secure, feature-rich mobile banking app for seamless transactions, account access, and customer engagement.

Business Solutions

Develop customized enterprise applications that streamline operations, automate workflows, enhance decision-making, and improve customer engagement.

E-Commerce Solutions

Build secure e-commerce platforms with integrated payment systems, inventory management, and seamless digital customer experiences.

Mobility Apps

Develop secure mobile apps for Android, iOS, and cross-platform environments, enabling anytime, anywhere access to services.

E-Governance Solutions

Deliver digital governance solutions that enhance service delivery, transparency, compliance, and operational efficiency.

Offshore Development Centre / Centre of Excellence

Provide dedicated offshore development teams and Centers of Excellence for product engineering, testing, maintenance, and innovation initiatives.

AI Teller Agent

AI-powered virtual assistant for automated banking interactions, customer support, and real-time service assistance.

SmartFill

Automatically extracts data from documents and fills forms instantly to reduce manual effort and improve accuracy.

Document Intelligence

Automatically reads, classifies, and extracts structured data from documents for faster processing and decision-making.

Knowledge Bot

Delivers instant answers from RBI circulars, banking regulations, and internal documents using AI-powered search.

Database Bot

Query, analyze, and manage databases using natural language without requiring SQL expertise.

Audio Transcription

Convert audio and speech into accurate, searchable text with AI-powered transcription.
