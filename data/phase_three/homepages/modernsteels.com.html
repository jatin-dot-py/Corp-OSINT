About us

Modern Steel Ltd. is a Public Limited Company incorporated on 19 November 1973 having Corporate Identification Number (CIN) L27109PB1973PLC003358 and registered office situated at G T Road, Mandi Gobindgarh, Punjab. The Company was incorporated with an object to primarily deal in Iron & Steel products. The authorized share capital of the Company is Rs. 250,000,000/- and the paid up capital is Rs. 137,623,590/-.
