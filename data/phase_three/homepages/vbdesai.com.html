WELCOME TO V.B. Desai Financial Services Limited

V. B. Desai Financial Services Ltd. is a publicly listed company offering value added financial services. Incorporated in 1985 and listed on BSE since 1986. VBDesai has grown into a reliable and established name in the domestic Investment Banking and Corporate Advisory space.

VBDesai provides Investment Banking and Financial Advisory services and caters to exclusive clientele. It is registered with Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) as a Category I Merchant Banker and licensed to carry out Merchant Banking activities.

VBDesai has the unique distinction of combining tradition and values with innovation and adaptability required in today’s ever changing and constantly evolving Capital Markets. VBDesai possesses an in-depth understanding of the financial sector to provide customized solutions and has had a recognizable presence in the Equity, Debt and Money Markets and has many path breaking deals to its credit. It strives to build a relationship based on transparency and serving the interest of clients. VBDesai offers a bouquet of financial and advisory services under its banner and firmly in thoroughly researched customized advisory services based on keen understanding of the client business and financial markets.
