Frequently Asked Question
What is DishTV?
DishTV is India's leading Direct-to-Home (DTH) service provider. With DishTV, you get crystal-clear picture, high-definition audio, and access to hundreds of TV channels — from entertainment and sports to news and kids’ programs — directly on your TV.
Why choose DishTV?
  • Affordable Packages: Customize your own pack and pay only for the channels you watch.
  • Easy Installation: Get Dish TV set-up quickly at your home with a simple phone call or online booking.
  • Recharge Anytime: Top-up or pay your bills online, through our app or website.
  • Hassle-free Customer Support: Our team is available 24/7 to help you resolve any issues promptly. · Add-Ons and Services: Enjoy additional services like DishTV Smart+, multi-TV connection, and video-on-demand.
How DishTV works?
DishTV uses a small satellite dish installed at your home to receive signals directly from the satellite in space. The signals are routed through your set-top box and displayed on your TV in real time.
Who should set DishTV?

New TV Viewers: If you’re setting up your first TV connection at home.

  • Relocating Families: If you’re shifting and want to transfer your connection easily.
  • Existing Customers: If you want to add a secondary connection in another room or a vacation home.
