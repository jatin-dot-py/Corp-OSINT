SK Minerals & Additives Limited

Leading the Way in Flame Retardants, Feed Additives, and Industrial Minerals for Sustainable Progress

Founded in Punjab, India, SK Minerals & Additives Limited has grown from a trusted trader of urea and feed chemicals into India’s only manufacturer of halogen-free flame retardants. With a strong R&D foundation, multiple patents filed, and a commitment to sustainability, we serve industries across electronics, automotive, food, agriculture, and construction.

Founded in Punjab, India, SK Minerals & Additives Limited has grown from a trusted trader of urea and feed chemicals into India’s only manufacturer of halogen-free flame retardants. With a strong R&D foundation, multiple patents filed, and a commitment to sustainability, we serve industries across electronics, automotive, food, agriculture, and construction.

Innovation at the Core of Our Identity

At SK Minerals & Additives Limited, research is not an afterthought – it’s what defines us. With four patents filed, five research papers published, and a DSIR-recognized laboratory, our work sets benchmarks for the industry.

In 2024–25, we were honoured with the Best MSME Innovative Award, a recognition of our role in building India’s first indigenous halogen-free flame-retardant solution.
