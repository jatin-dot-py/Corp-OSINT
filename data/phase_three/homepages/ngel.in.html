NTPC Green Energy Limited

NGEL is an umbrella company for the green business initiatives of NTPC and undertakes projects through organic and inorganic routes and aims to be the flag-bearer of NTPC’s green energy journey to achieve the ambitious target of 60 GW by FY 32...

Vision

To Be World’s leading Green Energy Solutions Company
Driving India’s Energy Transition

Mission

To Provide reliable, affordable and Sustainable Green
Energy Solutions to Achieve India’s Energy Transition
Objectives By Leveraging Innovation and Technology

Green energy: Powering Progress, Preserving the Planet

Green Portfolios

Solar Power

We prioritize a comprehensive and systematic approach for the solar project design and implementation.

Floating Solar Power

Floating solar power generation offers a sustainable and space-efficient solution by installation of solar photovoltaic.

Hybrid Power

The synergistic approach of hybrid energy generation addresses the limitations of adoption of individual renewable.

Wind Power

We're actively involved in the development of onshore and offshore wind power projects across diverse regions.

Energy Storage

Energy storage plays a pivotal role in the transition to a more sustainable and resilient energy system.

Green Hydrogen

Green hydrogen has significant potential to play a crucial role for decarbonizing sectors that are difficult to electrify.
