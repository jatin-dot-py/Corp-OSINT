Welcome to
Rajshree Polypack

Rajshree PolyPack Ltd. is one of the leading manufacturer of Plastic rigid sheets and Food Packaging products ranging from Yoghurt cups to Daily containers, barrier products – MAP, Retort application, Soffule cups & Fruit Punnets, etc. We have a wide range of sizes and designs to meet the discerning customers’ needs. We can also make customized designs and shapes as per the client’s demand. We cater to diverse industries like Dairy, Beverages, FMCG, Ethnic Sweets, Ready to Eat, Ready to Serve, Food Services, Bakery, Retail, Pharmaceuticals & Electronics etc.

We are Taking Small Steps to Make Earth Better Planet

One of the serious issues faced by our modern world is environmental pollution, and it is one the most significant challenges that the world is facing in our day. Environmental pollution has existed for centuries.

  • Protect and enhance the environment for future generations.
  • Ecosystems are imperative to maintaining a healthy planet.
  • Help protect the environment and sustain its natural resources.
  • Products manufactured at RPPL are completely recyclable and less plastics than more commonly used. Thus, reducing the use of polymers by up to 30%.
  • The new line of paper-wrapped cups is made with thinner plastic resulting in lesser use of polymers and ensuring 100% recyclability.
  • Introduction of PLA (polylactic acid), made from biodegradable raw materials helps reduce carbon footprint as it is compostable.
