OUR DIVISIONS

NIBE SPACE (NSPL)

Delivering advanced satellite technology and Earth observation solutions for real-time intelligence and enhanced national security.

LAND SYSTEMS

Our Land Systems portfolio features SuryaAstra, Garudastra, the Vayuastra Series, Counter-UAS, 30mm Proximity Fuze, Silver Bullet, Pinaka, and Satellite-Based Intelligence technologies for advanced defence operations.

AIR SYSTEMS

Advanced air combat solutions featuring GATR, Counter-UAS, SkyStriker, PULS, and Shared Rocket Core technologies for precision strike, force protection, and mission success.

NAVAL SYSTEMS

Engineered for modern maritime warfare, our Naval Systems integrate autonomous platforms, mine countermeasure technologies, advanced rocket launch systems, and high-performance naval ammunition to ensure superior mission effectiveness, fleet protection, and operational dominance at sea.

DEFENCE ELECTRONICS (NDAL)

Specializing in advanced embedded systems, military software, and smart weapon integration.

BIO ENERGY

Bharat Bio Gas Private Limited delivers sustainable CBG infrastructure, transforming agricultural waste into clean fuel and organic fertilizers while supporting India's energy transition.

OUR CAPABILITIES

01. Defence Manufacturing

Advanced laser/plasma cutting and CNC bending systems with monthly capabilities up to 5000 tons and 1200 tons respectively, enabling precise fabrication advanced structural integrity for high-performance defence components.

Large-scale fabrication facilities spanning 550,000 square feet, enabling mass production with rigorous quality control.

Fully/semi-automated welding stations with KUKA & IGM robots for high-precision, repeatable welding.

High-capacity Zayer machines (12M and 16M) for machining large defence components with unparalleled accuracy.

Engineered for large-scale metal fabrication, this CNC Hydraulic Press Brake performs precise bending of long sheet metal sections up to 10 meters. Its advanced CNC control ensures consistent quality and high productivity across every operation.

02. Enigneering and Design

Industry-standard software for Developing details 2D and 3D models that guide accurate production processes.

Nesting software for material optimazation, reducing waste and enhancing production efficiency.

Visual tools for collaborative design, enabling seamless integration from design to fabrication.

03. Research and Development

Designing high-performance Electronics for use in complex defence applications under extreme conditions.

Advanced power systems to support reliable and efficient energy solutions for defence equipments.

Developing responsive, technologically advanced weapon systems for modern defence needs.

Focused R&D into electronic Warfare solutions, enhancing defence resilience against cyber and physical threats.
