About Us

Why MCFL ?

Mangal Credit & Fincorp Limited is a listed non-banking financial services company offering gold loans, personal loans and SME loans.
The Company has a strong presence in the states of Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Rajasthan and has more than ₹ 230 crores of assets under management.
