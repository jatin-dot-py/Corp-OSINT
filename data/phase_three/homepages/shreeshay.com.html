About us

Real Estate Construction and Engineering Focused Solutions Company

Shreeshay Engineers Limited (SEL) is a real estate construction and engineering focused solutions company. our company has been realigning its business policies and recently we have increased our focus towards becoming the "EPC". "EP" stands for "Engineering", "Procurement" and "Construction". We provide supply of materials, designing and contracting capabilities as well as construction project management services.

Ideology

 We believe in building homes and not just flats… Our projects touch hearts of our clients with better amenities like centralized location with ready eco-system, spacious and well-designed engineering with landscaped garden, high speed elevators, etc. We give dreams a real touch… Our well experienced team understands the preferences & trends of the society and the same is reflected in our projects.
