About Us

High Quality Precision Engineering

Akar Auto Industries Ltd, an ISO/TS 16949:2009 certified company is the forward integrated enterprises of R.L. Group of Industries. Situated in Waluj near Aurangabad, Maharashtra, which is one of the major automotive clusters in India with good transport connectivity. Akar Auto Industries ltd was established in 1989. Since, Start of production in 1991, Akar Auto Industries Ltd manufacturer's high quality precision engineered forging components, Hand Tools and Leaf Springs to major Auto & Non-Auto OEM's.
