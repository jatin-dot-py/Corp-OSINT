Years of Engineering
Excellence
1950 - 1970

The foundation years. Powering ahead with sheer Enterprise and a pioneering spirit. First to Manufacture Control Cables in India.

1970 - 1990

The Expansion Years. First to introduce Pressure Die cast cable ends. Introduction of Friction Free Cables

1990 - 2000

The Global Footprint. General Motors the first Global client Setting up of a Hub in England for European Operations, first Engineering Center for Control Cables

2000 - 2010

Manufacturing Excellence. Gear Shifts, Indigenously manufactured Dash Mounted Park Brake Assembly, with Cables. First to manufacture Push Pull cables in India

2010 - 2020

A Truly Global Player. Operations in USA & Europe commenced. Acquisition of Magal UK. First International Production Facility Added global brands like Ford, JLR to to portfolio Foray into Off Highway Cables

2020 - 2023

The next Orbit. Rebranded interface with new identity & brand positioning in Dec 21. Got the Great Place to Work recognition from prestigious GTPW Institute. March 22 A spate of awards and recognition for Employee Engagement & Quality standards
