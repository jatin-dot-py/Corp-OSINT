Corporate Responsibility

 

In line with our vision for sustainable growth and societal development, we have constituted a Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Committee as part of our Board. This committee oversees our CSR policy, which follows the requirements of Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, and the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules, 2014.
We contributed to CSR activities to support social well-being. Our efforts focused on areas like healthcare, education, and social welfare.
