WE ARE BUILDING A SUSTAINABLE FUTURE

Alpex Solar Ltd., a leading SME-listed solar panel manufacturer since 1993, is headquartered in Greater Noida, India. Our state-of-the-art, vertically integrated facility boasts a fully automatic production line, overseen by a team of 250 dedicated professionals. We are at the forefront of the renewable energy revolution, offering a comprehensive range of high-quality solar solutions designed to meet the diverse needs of our customers. This includes cutting-edge bifacial modules that capture sunlight from both sides, resulting in increased energy production. We also offer high-efficiency mono PERC and half-cut modules, incorporating the latest advancements in monocrystalline and polycrystalline cell technology to deliver exceptional performance, durability, and value. Additionally, Alpex Solar manufactures its own high-grade aluminum frames, ensuring complete quality control and optimized compatibility for our solar panels.
