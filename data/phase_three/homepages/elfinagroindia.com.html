Who We Are

Elfin Agro India Limited was setup in the year 2009 in Bhilwara (Rajasthan) Elfin Agro India Limited was primarily engaged in the business of manufacturing of Chakki Atta (High fibre whole wheat flour), R Atta (Refined whole wheat flour), Tandoori Atta (Specialized flour), Sooji (Semolina flour), Maida (Refined Flour) and yellow mustard oil. Our company has Flour processing unit at F-250 and F-251 RIICO Growth Centre, Bhilwara, (“RIICO Land”) started operating in the financial year 2013 for the grading, sorting and packaging of raw wheat (“processed wheat”).
