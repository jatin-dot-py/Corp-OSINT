Our Products

Resonance boasts of a range product categories

Pyridine

Pyridine is a versatile chemical used in the manufacture of agrochemicals, pharmaceuticals & bulk drugs, dyes, rubber chemicals, etc.

Intermediates

Alpha Picolinic Acid and Dipicolinic acid are the two drug intermediates we work on that are used by pharma companies as raw materials.

Cyanopyridines

The company had revolutionized manufacturing process of Niacinamide (Vitamin B3). Cyanopyridine has also improved the efficiency of the Isoniazid manufacturing process.

Lutidines & Collidines

Lutidines are used in the manufacture of new generation anti-ulcer drugs such as Omeprazol, Lansaprazole, Rebaprazole

Picoline

Different Picolines are used in Vinyl Latex Rubber, manufacture of Vitamin B3 and manufacture of the anti-tuberculosis drug Isoniazid.

APIs

The company is presently producing Nutritional products such as Zinc Picolinate, Chromium Picolinate, Niacin and more.

Solvents

A solvent is a substance that is used to dissolve another substance to create a solution. Solvents are often used in chemical reactions to help dissolve other substances.
