Jivanjor is one of the Largest Adhesive Brand in India, offers premium adhesive range made using superior technology.

Vamicol is a Multi-purpose Wood Adhesive for long lasting bonds.

Jubilant is one of the three major global suppliers of Poly Vinyl Acetate (PVAc).

Polystic Super is designed to join wood to wood, decorative laminates and laminate to particle boards.

Hero is multi-purpose wood adhesives, suitable for joining wood to wood, decorative laminates and laminate to particle boards.

Ultra Italia is universal wood coating made in Italy by Verinlegno and imported by Jubilant Agri and Consumer Products. Italian wood finishes are best in class finishes delivering exquisite craftsmanship and sheer elegance.

The Charmwood Wood Finish product line offers a comprehensive solution for decorating

We are the world’s leading manufacturer of Vinyl Pyridine Latex (VP Latex), established as the largest manufacturer in India and second largest in the world. We export VP Latex to all major tyre cord manufacturers around the globe. We also specialize in manufacturing Styrene Butadiene Rubber (SBR)Latex and Nitrile Butadiene Rubber (NBR) Latex.

Jubilant is one of the major global supplier of Ester Gum. Ester Gum is one of the major raw materials for making gum base

RAMBAN, a leading Manufacturer of high-quality SSP fertilizers.
