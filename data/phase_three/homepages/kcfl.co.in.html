Reaping Riches Of The Soil

Khaitan Chemicals & Fertilizers Ltd. is essentially engaged in the manufacturing of Single Super Phosphate (Fertilizer), Sulphuric Acid (Chemical) and Soya Edible Oil. It has the largest Single Super Phosphate (SSP) production capacity of 11,13,500 MT alongwith Sulphuric Acid (SA) production capacity of 2,70,600 MT across the country.

The Company also has a 420000 TPA Soybean crushing capacity along with 30000 TPA Edible Oil Refinery located in Ratlam (M.P.) and uses technology from the world leader n.v. Extraction DeSmet s.a. of Belgium.

Bringing Earth’s Resources To Life

Mr. Shailesh Khaitan promoted Khaitan Chemicals & Fertilizers Ltd. (KCFL) in 1982. The Company is engaged in the manufacturing of Single Super Phosphate (SSP), Sulphuric Acid (SA) Oleum 65%, Oleum 23%, Liquid SO3, Labsa, Soya Oil and De-oiled Cake. Today, it is the largest manufacturer of Single Super Phosphate (SSP) in India and plans to retain its position in the coming years as well. The Company has an installed capacity of 1113500 TPA of Single Super Phosphate (SSP), 270600 TPA of Sulphuric Acid (SA), 4325 KW of captive power generation and 1250 KW Wind Mill in Maharashtra.
