ABOUT US

Jay Bee Laminations Limited was established in 1988 as a manufacturer of CRGO Silicon steel cores for the emerging power & distribution transformer industry of India.

Jay Bee Laminations Limited (JBL) is one of the leading names in the power sector producing high precision transformer cores in the range of 5 kVA to 500 MVA (400 kV class). With 35+ years of legacy in delivering quality and reliability, we have supplied best-in-class transformer components to 250+ manufacturers, earning trust across power industry from India and the world. We regularly supply our components to reputed customers such as CG Power, BHEL, Shilchar Technologies with end users such as PGCIL, Adani Power, Tata Power and many more.
