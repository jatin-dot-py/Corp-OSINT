About 3B Films

3B Films Limited was incorporated on September 3rd, 2014 with an aim to establish state of the art CPP & CPE Film manufacturing unit in Vadodara, Gujarat.

The company has set up a state-of-the-art manufacturing unit of Cast Polypropylene (CPP) and Cast Polyethylene (CPE) Films used in food packaging industry and completed installation of imported Plant & Machinery from Italy & Germany on 30th March 2017.

3B Films has already started exports to Dubai, Nepal, Sri Lanka, African Countries and exploring other part of the world in near future.

With all these customers, 3B Films Limited aims to maximize utilization of existing capacity and hence feels necessity for the expansion to cater new market on account of organic growth of all FMCG companies and new entries in FMCG business.
