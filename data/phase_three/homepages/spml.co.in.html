Years Of Nation Building

For over 45 years, SPML Infra has been building India's most important infrastructure, from water and power systems to urban development and clean energy. We take on large projects that make a real difference in people's lives and support India's goal of becoming a fully developed nation. We believe in building smart and sustainable infrastructure that lasts, and everything we do is aimed at creating a better future that contribute towards the national vision of Viksit Bharat.
