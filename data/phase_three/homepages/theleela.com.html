        What sets a stay at The Leela Palaces, Hotels & Resorts apart from other luxury hotels?

        A stay with us is an immersion into true Indian luxury, where every space celebrates India's rich heritage through opulent architecture, intricate art, and gracious service rooted in Atithi Devo Bhava, 'Guest is God'. Gracious personalised service, regal architecture, lakeside and city views, and locally inspired cuisine create immersive stays. Every visit offers a refined sense of calm, enriched with cultural depth and timeless sophistication.

        Are The Leela hotels a good option for families travelling together?

        Leela Hotels warmly welcome families with tailored amenities, interconnecting rooms, kids menus, supervised activities, and thoughtful services for parents. Spacious accommodation and flexible dining make family stays comfortable. Staff anticipate needs such as high chairs and babysitting options, allowing families to relax, explore, and create lasting memories together in comfort.

        What makes The Leela a special choice for a romantic getaway or honeymoon?

        The Leela is renowned for crafting pure, unforgettable romance. We specialise in intimate moments, private candlelit dinners on palace terraces, sunset cruises on tranquil lakes, and serene spa rituals for two. Every detail is designed to create the perfect, magical backdrop for couples and honeymooners to celebrate their love.

        Does The Leela provide butler service for guests?

        Yes, The Leela provides a truly world-class butler service. Your personal Palace Butler, trained in an intensive 18-month course, is always at your service, anticipating your needs and taking care of everything from unpacking to carefully planned activities, and even customized celebrations, to ensure that your stay with us is a seamless and indulgently memorable one.

        What special experiences does The Leela create for couples and honeymooners seeking a romantic getaway?

        Selected Leela properties offer flexible long-stay options with customised packages for extended visits. Guests can expect spacious rooms or suites, laundry services, discounted dining, and adapted housekeeping routines. Corporate travellers and leisurely residents benefit from long-stay rates and attentive service during prolonged stays. Availability and terms vary by property and season.

        Are vegetarian, vegan, and Jain dining preferences catered to at The Leela?

        Yes. The Leela’s restaurants cater to vegetarian, vegan, and Jain diets. Our chefs adapt traditional recipes and design plant-based menus while observing strict Jain dietary practices when requested. To ensure the best experience, please tell us your requirements before you arrive so our culinary team can source the right ingredients and prepare accordingly.

        Does The Leela arrange airport transfers for guest convenience?

        Your grand experience begins even before you arrive. We provide luxury airport transfer services, including pickup, drop-off, and chauffeur services. Options range from sedan cars to luxury vehicles with luggage help. Transfers may be complimentary with certain packages or chargeable otherwise. Guests should share flight details when booking so that timely transport can be arranged and confirmed.

        What kinds of wellness and spa experiences can guests enjoy?

        The Leela features comprehensive spa and wellness centres at many properties, offering massages, facials, fitness facilities, and relaxation lounges. The Leela’s trained therapists provide special treatments that combine the best of international and local practices. Guests can enjoy serene areas for relaxation, as well as swimming pools and fitness centers to promote overall wellness during their stay.

        Do any properties offer dedicated Ayurvedic wellness retreats or programs?

        Some of the Leela properties have Ayurvedic wellness programs that are based on traditional Indian practices. You can opt for multi-day programs that include treatments, herbal diet advice, and yoga, all of which are aimed at helping you detox and rejuvenate.

        Can The Leela host weddings and large corporate events? What support is provided?

        The Leela frequently hosts weddings and corporate events with elegant venues, bespoke catering, and experienced event teams. From intimate ceremonies to grand celebrations, planners assist with décor, menus, entertainment, and logistics. Corporate clients benefit from meeting rooms, audiovisual support, and customised packages, ensuring professional, seamless execution of special occasions and conferences.

        Are The Leela hotels equipped to manage large group and event bookings?

        Yes, Leela Hotels facilitates large group bookings for weddings, conferences, and other leisure groups. A team of event coordinators is available to handle block room bookings, catering, transportation, and event schedules to ensure smooth organization. Prior notice will enable us to confirm your booking and make arrangements according to your requirements.

        What are the best ways to contact the reservations team to plan my stay?

        You can easily contact The Leela’s international reservations team for help. The main contact method is through email at reservations@theleela.com. You can also dial the toll-free number allocated for your region, such as 1800 1031 444 in India, 855 670 3444 in the USA, or +91 124 4425 444 for all other countries. You can also send your query directly through the contact form available on the official website for an immediate response.

        In which key destinations across India can I find a Leela property?

        The Leela's properties are found in India's most desirable destinations, each offering a unique experience in its own setting. You can spot our signature palaces in the royal cities of Udaipur, Jaipur, and New Delhi, as well as in Bengaluru and Chennai. Our city hotels are the heart of the metropolis in Mumbai, Hyderabad, Gurugram, and Gandhinagar, while our tranquil resorts offer a haven by the sea in Kovalam and Ashtamudi, Kerala.

        What is the DISCOVERY loyalty program, and what are the main benefits for guests?

        The Leela DISCOVERY is a free loyalty program that rewards you instantly and serves as your passport to a world of privileges. Earn DISCOVERY Dollars (D$), which are equivalent to 1 USD, on hotel spend, and redeem them for hotel stays, meals, or spa treatments. Enjoy premium levels (Gold, Platinum, Titanium) with benefits like room upgrades and late check-out times. Your loyalty status is recognized across 950+ hotels worldwide in the Global Hotel Alliance.

        What notable awards and recognitions has The Leela received?

        The Leela hotels have received prestigious global awards. Our group has been ranked the #3 World's Best Hotel Brand in the latest Travel + Leisure USA magazine. Our hotels are known for Michelin Keys for exceptional cuisine, inclusion in Newsweek's World's Most Extraordinary Spas, and top rankings in Travel + Leisure and Condé Nast Traveller surveys, ensuring that your stay with us is the best in the world.

        What payment options are accepted at The Leela properties?

        We accept all forms of payment from our guests, including credit/debit card payments, wire transfers to hotels, and popular forms of electronic payment such as PayPal, Venmo, etc. However; Each hotel may only accept certain types of payment. Therefore, before booking your reservation or before departing any location, please call and consult with the hotel for accurate policy information.

        What makes The Leela one of the world’s best luxury hotel brands?

        The Leela has been recognised as the No. 2 Hotel Brand in the World in the Travel + Leisure World’s Best Awards 2026, backed by an industry-leading Net Promoter Score of 86. This global excellence is rooted in the philosophy of True Indian Luxury and the enduring ethos of Atithi Devo Bhava (Guest is God). Across its portfolio of palaces and resorts, the brand seamlessly blends timeless grandeur with heartfelt hospitality, curating experiences that are distinctly Indian in soul yet global in finesse, from handcrafted heritage architecture to bespoke dining and wellness journeys. 

        How can I experience The Leela’s award-winning hospitality?

        You can experience this living legacy by booking curated stays, dining at signature restaurants, or joining their global loyalty program. True immersion begins the moment you step into any lakeside palace, capital residence, or backwater retreat, where a 40-year narrative of gracious Indian hospitality comes alive. Here, luxury is defined by intuitive service that anticipates your every need. It comes alive through the ancient wisdom of the Aujasya Wellness programme and the artistry of regional cuisine. It is luxury experienced not as a display, but as a genuine devotion to place, people, and the quiet poetry of belonging. 

        How has The Leela shaped luxury hospitality in India over the last four decades?

        For forty years, The Leela has pioneered Indian luxury by elevating the nation's heritage, craftsmanship, and philosophy into a globally revered standard, consistently earning top accolades, including the World’s Best Hotel Brand in 2020 and 2021 by Travel + Leisure. Building on that legacy, the brand has now secured the No. 2 spot globally in the Travel + Leisure World’s Best Awards 2026. Rather than importing Western templates, the brand has raised the bar for service, design, and experiential depth. This legacy unfolds uniquely across its properties, with each serving as a living expression of true Indian luxury:

        • The Leela Palace Udaipur: A lakeside marble masterpiece inspired by Mewar’s royal architecture, where private boat arrivals and jharokha-framed suites evoke the grace of a bygone era, yet with contemporary wellness and gastronomy.
        • The Leela Palace New Delhi: A diplomatic address of sovereign elegance, blending Lutyens’ Delhi grandeur with Indian art deco, hosting state banquets and intimate darbar-style private dining in equal measure.
        • The Leela Palace Chennai: A Chettinad-inspired sanctuary where hand-carved woodwork, temple tank motifs, and thalaikoothal (traditional cooling therapy) at Aujasya Wellness reflect luxury in Tamil heritage.
        • The Leela at Kerala (Kovalam & Ashtamudi): Two backwater and coastal retreats where kathakali evenings, Ayurvedic panchakarma rituals, and sunset kettuvallam (houseboat) journeys celebrate Kerala’s soulful rhythm.
        • The Leela Coorg Forest Sanctuary: A biodiversity-rich escape where luxury is reimagined as conservation - private forest walks with naturalists, starlit Chettinad feasts under canopy, and wellness pavilions woven into the Western Ghats.
        • The Leela Palace Bengaluru: A neo-classical ode to Karnataka’s dynastic past, featuring life-size bronze sculptures, silk-draped corridors, and bisi bele bath elevated to fine dining.
