Who We Are?

Amba Enterprises Limited, with a manufacturing capacity of about 3500 MT per year, is one of India's largest manufacturers of transformer core lamination. We are dedicated to being the most crucial and dependable component in our clients' whole value chain.
We are a multi-product, multi-location, multi-advantage group with a revenue of around INR 220 crore that serves recognised enterprises across India.
We specialize in Silicon Steel Slit Coils, Cut to Size Transformer Laminations, Torroidal Cores, Motor Stamping, and Die Casting Rotor.
AEL products are used in a variety of downstream industries, including UPS, Energy, Distribution Transformer, Electrical, Automobile, Engineering, and Appliances.

What We Offer?

Amba Enterprises Limited provides a wide range of products like Silicon Steel Slit Coils, Cut to Size Transformer Laminations, Torroidal Cores, Motor Stamping, and Die Casting Rotors.
AEL offers a wide range of stampings / laminations suitable for various applications. They are available in different sizes up to 1250mm OD. The range of rotors is available from 40mm dia to 500 mm dia and up to 600mm height.

Quality Certifications

The most important feature of our business is dependable quality. It all starts with the basic materials. To ensure high precision, all dies are made in our own tool room. Stamping is done with high-accuracy, high-capacity automated presses. Notching is done with sophisticated Schuller notching equipment. Furthermore, each batch is subjected to rigorous quality checks for raw material composition and dimensional correctness.
