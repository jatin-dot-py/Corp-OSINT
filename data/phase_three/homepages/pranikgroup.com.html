Leading Logistics service Provider in India

Pranik Logistics – Leading Logistics service Provider in India. We provide complete logistic solutions to our clients i.e. from the point of origin to the point of consumption in order to meet the needs of the customer.  Pranik Logistics is a limited company, Head Office in Kolkata spreading its wings across Eastern India. We manage both physical as well as intangibles items. Our services are an integration of material handling, inventory, transportation, warehousing and often security. Our motivation is the minimum utilization of resources while achieving the maximum. We provide professional and efficient transportation and warehousing services that are directed toward Cost Optimization, Reducing Time Levels and Enhancing Service Levels. We have trained professionals in logistics and transport, which minimizes the risks involved.
