ABOUT AVI ANSH TEXTILE LTD

Laid out in 2012 in Punjab, AVI ANSH TEXTILE LTD. has made a mark for itself as a manufacturer and exporter of 100% cotton yarn. We use superior technology to put our products through a series of quality control checks to ensure stringent quality.


We target at creating a different range of products for the worldwide textiles market. The Company has spinning factories with an all-out limit of 26,000 spindles. Together, they produce around 4,500MT of cotton yarn per annum of counts going from 20s to 40s. Each yarn delivered sticks to the highest expectations of the best quality.
