Welcome to Mukta Agriculture Limited
(Formerly known as Pearl Agriculture Limited)

“Mukta Agriculture Limited (Formerly known as Pearl Agriculture Limited), incorporated on 29 th day of August 2011 under the Companies Act, 1956 is engaged in the business of Agriculture and allied activities. With the development of Agricultural industry and liberal policies of the Government the Company has expanded its activities and intensified its area of operations in the field of Agriculture.

Mukta Agriculture Limited was listed on BSE in the year 2013 and is promoted by Mr. Krishan Khadaria.

Pursuant to the Scheme of Demerger sanctioned by Hon’ble Bombay High Court on 9 th October, 2012, the Agriculture Division of Nouveau Global Ventures Limited was demerged into Mukta Agriculture Limited (Formerly known as Pearl Agriculture Limited). In the year 2016, the Company changed its name from “Pearl Agriculture Limited” to “Mukta Agriculture Limited.”
