Transforming Infrastructure, Powering Business Success and Growth

WSI is a globally focused infrastructure and electrical project handling company with a strong track record of having completed several large-scale projects successfully. We have to our credit of handling successful projects for several state governments in power and electrical sectors.

Backed by a team of highly qualified professionals and robust operational mechanism in place, we are rightly poised to seize the opportunities in both national and global infrastructure markets and expand our footprints in the global arena.
