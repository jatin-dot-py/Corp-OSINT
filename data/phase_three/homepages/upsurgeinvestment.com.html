The way we deal

Plainly, and on terms we have kept for a generation.

No processing fees

We do not charge to look at a proposal or to arrange a facility. The rate is the whole of it.

No prepayment penalty

Settle early whenever it suits you. We have never charged a borrower for repaying us.

Little paperwork

We ask for what we genuinely need to understand the asset, and nothing assembled merely to look thorough.

A prompt answer

A considered yes or no in days, and funds at the pace the matter honestly deserves.

Built to last,
not to stretch.

We run a deliberately conservative balance sheet. We borrow within prudent limits, lend only against security we understand, and size every facility to a repayment we can see. The discipline lives in the underwriting, not in how far we are willing to reach.

Being well-capitalised and unhurried is what lets us move. The homework is done and the temperament is steady, so we can commit when others hesitate, and stay patient with a good borrower when a bank cannot. We have no quota to fill and no quarter to flatter.
