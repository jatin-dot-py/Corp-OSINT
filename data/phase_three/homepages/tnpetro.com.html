About Tamilnadu Petroproducts

Tamilnadu Petroproducts Limited (TPL), was born in the year 1984 with the objective of setting up a world class Linear Alkyl Benzene (LAB) manufacturing facility. TPL grew in strength by thinking differently, laying down a fundamental platform for financial strength and responding to customer requirements with innovative products and services. The company’s strong emphasis on continuous upgradation of technology to match the state of art has been the prime reason for its success in realizing its full potential and its confident march into the competitive future.

Infrastructure at TPL

Tamilnadu Petroproducts is India's leading petrochemical facility, manufacturing an array of products that find application across a range of high-growth industries.

Navigating the COVID-19 crisis with safety and compassion

TPL had faced the wrath of natural disasters like cyclone Vardha in 2016. It had an emergency preparedness plan that was modified to face the COVID-19 crisis.
