Oricon Enterprises is the flagship company of Parijat Enterprises. The core activity of Oricon Enterprises are :

Manufacturing and marketing of packaging products under the Oriental Containers Division. Namely : crown corks, ROPP caps, Aluminium collapsible tubes, Plastic closures for beverages and water bottles, PET preforms, Printed metal sheets. Market leaders in each of these product groups. Visit website : www.orientalcontainers.com for further details

Manufacturing and marketing of petrochemical products at the Khopoli facility near Mumbai. Product includes Pentanes & Heptanes

Real estate – Development of groups real estate business.

Oricon Enterprises is an international player and supplying products in over 40 countries. Cutrrent turnover of Oricon Enterprises Ltd is INRs 40.33 . Oricon Enterprises Ltd is listed in the Bombay Stock Exchange and in the National Stock Exchange
