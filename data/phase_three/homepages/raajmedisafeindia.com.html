About us
About

We Are Solutions For Your Brand Packaging

Raaj Medisafe India Ltd, a proud member of the Packaging People Group and listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange, has been a trusted name in the packaging and hygiene industry since 2014. With over three decades of experience, we specialize in delivering cutting-edge packaging solutions tailored to diverse industries.

Our expertise includes:

  • High-quality HDPE bottles and PP caps.
  • Advanced child-resistant packaging for pharmaceuticals.
  • Comprehensive hygiene products like baby diapers and sanitary pads.
  • Eco-friendly food-grade poly bags and sheets.

At our state-of-the-art 5,84,000 sq. ft. facility in Pithampur, Madhya Pradesh, we combine technology, innovation, and sustainability to meet our customers' unique requirements. Every product we manufacture adheres to the highest quality standards, including ISO 9001:2015 and USFDA certifications, ensuring superior performance and satisfaction.

Let’s Bring Your Packaging Vision to Life!

At Raaj Medisafe India Ltd, we specialize in offering a wide array of packaging solutions designed to meet the unique needs of diverse industries. From pharmaceuticals to FMCG and personal hygiene, we deliver innovative and reliable packaging products that ensure safety, quality, and efficiency.

Our Key Offerings:
  • Pharmaceutical Packaging: Child-resistant packaging, HDPE/LDPE containers, and caps tailored for healthcare needs.
  • Food Packaging Solutions: Food-grade poly bags and sheets adhering to the highest hygiene standards.
  • Hygiene Products: Baby diapers, sanitary pads, and maternity care products designed for comfort and protection.
  • Sustainable Packaging: Eco-friendly and recyclable materials that align with global sustainability goals.
