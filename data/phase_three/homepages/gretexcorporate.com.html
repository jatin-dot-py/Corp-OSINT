Investor Awareness: Beware of Scam and Fraud Attempts Using the Gretex Corporate Services Limited's Name

We have come across reports of individuals and unauthorised groups on WhatsApp, Telegram, email, and social media falsely claiming association with Gretex Corporate Services Limited and offering investment opportunities, guaranteed returns, job offers, or account-related assistance.

Gretex Corporate Services Limited is not associated with any such unauthorised individuals, groups, or platforms. Please exercise caution and independently verify the authenticity of any communication before engaging in any transaction or sharing personal or financial information.

Please click here to learn more about common fraud methods and how to identify genuine communications from Gretex Corporate Services Limited.

Please note:

  • Gretex does not approach investors through unsolicited calls, messages, or unofficial social media profiles for investments.
  • Gretex does not promise or assure guaranteed returns on any investment whatsoever.
  • Investors should verify all communication only through official and authorised Gretex channels before proceeding.
  • If you receive any such unauthorized or illegal communication, please inform us, or report it with local authorities or the Cybercrime Reporting Portal via https://cybercrime.gov.in
