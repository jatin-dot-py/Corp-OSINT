Manufacturing FAQ:
Precision & Support

What industries does Rishi Laser primarily serve?

We are a strategic partner for the Earthmoving, Railway, Power, Automotive, and Telecom sectors, providing precision components for global OEMs.

How many manufacturing plants do you have in India?

We operate 7 modern factories strategically located in Pune (2 units), Vadodara, Bengaluru (2 units), Chennai, and Haryana (NCR).

What is your total steel processing capacity?

Our infrastructure is built to process over 46,000 Metric Tons (MT) of sheet steel annually, supporting massive industrial scale.

Can you handle both small prototypes and large orders?

Yes, From 0.7mm to 100mm in thin sheet metal, heavy plates, and stainless steel processing thicknesses precision parts from a few grams up to assemblies exceeding 1.5 tons in a single facility.

What makes Rishi Laser different from local fabrication shops?

We offer “Under-One-Roof” solutions, combining 30 years of expertise with advanced tech like 12kW fiber lasers and 14+ welding robots.

Do you work with global companies?

 Yes, we are a trusted vendor for international leaders such as Caterpillar, JCB, Volvo, and Alstom.

How do I start a project with you?

Simply click Contact Us to share your drawings / requirements, and our engineering team will provide a technical consultation within 24 hours.
