GROUP OVERVIEW

BKT Group identity

BKT is a global industrial group with a strong manufacturing foundation and a diversified business portfolio.

Our activities span tire solutions for complex mobility needs, from agricultural and industrial applications to everyday transportation, as well as carbon black production serving a wide range of industries.

Across geographies and markets, we operate through integrated processes, consistent quality standards and a governance model designed to sustain growth over time.

Explore who we are

BKT SOLUTIONS

Tire solutions across applications

BKT designs tire solutions for people and industries operating across a wide range of applications, from heavy duty environments to everyday mobility. Each product range is engineered to deliver reliability, performance and consistency in real operating conditions.

CARBON BLACK

Your success, our priority.

Carbon black is a strategic industrial material used across a wide range of applications beyond tire manufacturing. BKT produces carbon black through advanced, vertically integrated processes focused on quality, efficiency, and long-term performance, supporting multiple industrial sectors worldwide.

SUSTAINABILITY

Sustainability as an industrial system

At BKT, sustainability is embedded into the way the Group designs products, manages operations, and governs its value chain. A disciplined and measurable approach supports long term performance, operational resilience, and responsible growth across markets.
