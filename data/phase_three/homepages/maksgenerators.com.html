Our Products

Cummins MAKS Generators is instrumental in distribution of Cummins Generators which are known for their reliable nature. Cummins Generator sets are the epitome of rugged dependability and reliable mechanical and electrical performance.

Perkins MAKS Generators is a supplier of highly reliable and durable Perkins Generators (upto 2000 kVA). Across the ranges, Perkins Generators are designed to meet all emission standards and performance requirements.
