Uni Abex Alloy Products Limited

Established in 1972, Uni Abex is a globally recognized manufacturer and exporter of centrifugal and static castings in high-performance stainless-steel alloys engineered to withstand heat, corrosion and wear.

For over five decades, we have supported critical industrial operations from steam methane reforming and cracking furnaces to DRI units and global separation systems delivering precision-engineered components built for reliability and endurance. Backed by advanced manufacturing infrastructure and rigorous quality systems, we provide customized casting solutions that meet demanding international standards.

As a listed company on the Bombay Stock Exchange and a certified Two Star Export House, Uni Abex combines legacy, innovation and accountability continuously evolving our casting technologies to help industries operate safer, longer and more efficiently.
