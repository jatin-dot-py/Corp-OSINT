Milestones

1979

In 1979, Mirza Tanners Private Ltd was founded by Mr. Irshad Mirza and Mr. Rashid Ahmed Mirza as a small tannery unit with a big vision for the leather industry.

1990

In 1990, Mirza inaugurated its first shoe manufacturing factory, taking the first step towards establishing a global footprint with premium quality leather.

1994

In 1994, Mirza Tanners Private Limited was retitled Mirza Tanners Limited, and it became a deemed public company with a public issue of shares and a listing on the stock market.

1995

In 1995, Mirza won two leather export awards from the Council of Leather Exports shortly after starting to export their pristine quality made-in-India products.

1997

In 1997, Mr. Irshad Mirza became the first exporter from North India to win the Doyen of Leather Industry Award for his significant contribution to the leather industry.
