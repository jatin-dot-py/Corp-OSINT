    Polyester films

    Polyester films, made from polyethene terephthalate (PET), are widely used for their strength, transparency, and resistance to moisture and chemicals. Commonly found in packaging, insulation, and electronics, offering durability and flexibility in various industrial applications.

    Explore

    Recycling solutions

    rPET helps reduce plastic waste by reprocessing PET into new materials. Through our patented rPET technology, we do mechanical recycling of PET flakes to convert them into high quality rPET chips with quality as good as that of virgin chips. This sustainable approach supports the circular economy by giving PET a second life.

    Explore

    Speciality solutions

    Specialty solutions are designed to meet the increasing demands of industries requiring high-performance materials for a range of applications. These versatile materials are essential for bonding, encapsulation, coating, additives, and compounding, serving key sectors such as textiles, packaging, carpets, food and beverages, aerospace, automotive, and electronics.

    ABOUT US

    Customer Satisfaction through Innovation, Development, and Partnership

    Backed by 40+ years of industry expertise, Ester manufactures Polyester Films and Specialty Polymers. Polyester Films find applications primarily in flexible packaging, while Specialty Polymers cater to niche areas including rigid packaging, textiles, and more. We take pride in offering complete solutions to our customers, even for their most demanding needs. We also partner with our customers to derive a sustainable competitive advantage through customized development projects.

    Innovation and Sustainability are at the core of Ester’s endeavors and fundamental to its strategy and vision. Besides manufacturing world-class products and solutions, we also pay close attention to our activities towards protecting the environment, which is reflected not only in what we produce but also in how we produce. What sets Ester apart is not just the extent or distinction of its offering, it is the commitment demonstrated by its employees towards customers and their needs.

    While Ester is headquartered in the National Capital Region of India (Gurgaon), the company operates state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities at Khatima and Sitarganj (rPET) in Uttarakhand, along with a modern BOPET film manufacturing facility at Chandanvelly, Ranga Reddy district, Telangana (Hyderabad region).

    These facilities enable Ester to deliver high-quality Polyester Films, rPET and Specialty Polymer solutions to customers across diverse industries and markets.

    We have a global presence, supplying a large customer base in 50+ countries across Europe, the Americas, Africa, the Indian subcontinent, the Far East, Asia Pacific, and the Middle East. Through our manufacturing excellence, innovation-led approach, and customer-centric philosophy, we continue to create sustainable value for all stakeholders.
