ALMONDZ GLOBAL

Almondz global at a glance

Almondz Global Securities Limited, a listed company, is a financial services provider since 1994 which offers depository participant services, wealth management advisory and equity broking services. The company is a member of: National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE); Bombay Stock Exchange Limited, (BSE); and Central Depository Services (India) Ltd., (CDSL)   (for depository services).

We have experienced & professional Board supported by a team of over 350+ professionals & Support Staff.

Our Vision

To provide a comprehensive range of financial services for businesses and individual investors, to create value for these clients and for our employees and shareholders, and to grow as a stringently compliant organisation.
