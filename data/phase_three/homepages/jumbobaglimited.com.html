Product Overview

Our Jumbo bags are made from food-grade materials and comply with food contact regulations. We offer different types of liners, including with and without tabbing, to protect the contents from moisture, light, or other environmental factors.

Type A Jumbo Bags

Type A Jumbo Bags

Regular Jumbo Bags used for transporting non-combustible substances Jumbo Bag Ltd offers a Type A FIBC, produced using fabric/plastic sheets that do not have any protection against static electricity build-up.

Type B Jumbo Bags

Type B Jumbo Bags

Anti-Static Jumbo Bags permit the secure transport of combustible items. Type B FIBC are fabricated to prevent the incidence of sparks and brush discharges during filling and discharging of materials.

Type C Jumbo Bags

Type C Jumbo Bags

The Jumbo Bags of Type C are fabricated by weaving conductive tapes in both the warp and weft directions to ensure uniform conductivity properties throughout the FIBC.

Type D Jumbo Bags

Type D Jumbo Bags

Jumbo Bag Ltd is among the select few companies worldwide with the capability to manufacture all types of FIBCs based on the IEC 61340 standard, including Type D.

Un Bags – Jumbo Safe

Un Bags – Jumbo Safe

Our UN bags, known as Jumbo-Safe, are the perfect choice for packing and transporting hazardous materials by sea, road, and air.

Container Liners

Container Liners

Jumbo Bag offers Jumbo Bulk containers that are suitable for transporting free-flowing materials across different countries. These containers are suitable for both 20 and 40 foot containers.

Liner Bags

Liner Bags

Jumbo Bag Ltd, also offers a variety of liners, including with and without tabbing, to protect the contents from moisture, light, or other environmental factors.

Baffle Bag / Baffle Lined Bag

Baffle Bag / Baffle Lined Bag

Our baffle bags are ideal for filling low-density fine powder materials, and to prevent any seepage through sewing holes, we offer to sift proofing.
