About

Welcome to Accuracy Shipping Limited (ASL)

Your One-Stop Solution for Comprehensive Logistics Services

With a legacy of over 24 years, Accuracy Shipping Limited (formerly known as Balaji Shipping Agency) has become a trusted name in logistics, providing a seamless range of services tailored to meet your shipping and supply chain needs. From custom clearance to freight forwarding, transportation to warehousing, we've got every link of the logistics chain covered with precision, dedication, and excellence.

Our Service

Our Core Services

At ASL, we offer end-to-end logistics solutions designed to make your business operations smoother and more efficient.

Custom Clearance

Efficient and hassle-free clearance services to ensure smooth handling of all import and export formalities.

Freight Forwarding

• Sea Cargo: Streamlined sea freight services for your global trade needs.

• Air Cargo: Fast and reliable air freight services for time-sensitive shipments.

Transportation

• Road & Rail Transport: Flexible and reliable solutions for local and cross-country logistics.

• Project Cargo: Specialized handling for oversized and heavy-lift cargo.

Warehousing

• Normal Warehousing: Safe and secure storage for all types of goods.

• Bonded Warehousing: Simplified storage for customs-bonded cargo.

• Cold Storage: Temperature-controlled facilities for perishable items.

Chartering

• Vessel Chartering: Tailored vessel chartering solutions for unique requirements.

• Train Chartering: Dedicated train chartering services for bulk shipments.

Our Milestone

2000

Incorporated Balaji Shipping Agency with a focus on delivering exceptional Customs Clearance & Transportation services.

2004

Achieved success with 14 employees, laying the foundation for future growth in Shipping & Logistics Industry.

2008

Later known as Accuracy Shipping Pvt Ltd with expanded freight forwarding services to Gulf, Middle East, Far East, and USA trades, marking a significant milestone.

2011

Introduced Freight Forwarding and Transportation services, establishing branch offices in Mumbai, Chennai, and other states across India.

2013

Obtained WCAworld Gold Medallion Financial Protection Program and All World Shipping Agency licenses.

2016

Evolved into a total logistical solutions provider, offering customs clearance, freight forwarding, shipping, transportation, warehousing and more.

2017

Matured as a 3PL logistics company, expanding into new segments and winning the WCA Best Partner Award 2017.

2018

Transformed into Accuracy Shipping Limited and successfully launched an SME IPO.

2019

Received the Fastest Growing Company Award and Global Logistics Excellence Award.

2020

Shifted to the mainboard in December and consolidated all business under one roof, becoming the 1st NSE-listed company of Kachchh.

2021

Added new business verticals, including an Essar Petrol Pump.

2022

Awarded Leading Shipping Company of Kutch, introduced Ashok Leyland Dealership showrooms at location Gandhidham, Bhuj, Mundra & Morbi. Established new tie-ups with domestic and international companies.

2024

Expanded our presence with an additional Ashok Leyland Dealership showroom at Nakhatrana, Kutch – Gujarat.

Present Year: 2025

Achieved significant milestones, including:
- Successfully completing long-term rate contracts with main carriers.
- Establishing Train Chartering movements with prominent carriers such as Hapag and CMA.
