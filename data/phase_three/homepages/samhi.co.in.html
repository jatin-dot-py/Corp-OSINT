Across India

SAMHI Hotels is one of India’s leading hotel owners and asset management company, operating under an institutional ownership model. With one of the country’s most diversified hotel portfolios, we maintain strategic, long-standing partnerships with global brands such as Marriott, IHG, and Hyatt.

Our presence spans 13 major cities, including Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Pune, with 31 operational hotels and over 4,899 keys. This extensive network underscores our commitment to scale, strategic locations, and enduring global alliances.
