A Journey Built on Science and Purpose

Since 1990, Haleos Labs (erst-while SMS Lifesciences) has grown from a single-product facility into a multi-location organisation supported by its own research centre. Over time, the company has broadened its capabilities and strengthened its presence across domestic and international markets.

As a publicly listed entity with a growing customer base, Haleos Labs (erst-while SMS Lifesciences) continues to build on its technical experience, regulatory discipline, and established industry relationships.
