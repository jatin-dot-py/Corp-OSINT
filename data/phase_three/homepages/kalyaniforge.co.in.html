Kalyani Forge Ltd
Kalyani Forge Limited is a preferred partner to OEM and Tier-1 brands in the automotive and industrial sectors. It is growing as a world-class supplier of high-precision forgings, machined components, and sub-assemblies. It has a deep expertise in high-precision metal forming from over 40 years.

What We Manufacture
Kalyani Forge Ltd. is a leader in manufacturing close tolerance precision forging since its inception in 1979. Fuelled by burning ambition and driven by its core values we specialize in niche precision forgings, fully machined and sub-assembled products for global customers.
