Welcome to Artemis Hospital

Super Speciality Hospital & Trauma Centre in Gurgaon

Established in 2007, Artemis Hospitals was founded to provide the highest standards of medical care to patients from Delhi-NCR, other states in India, and abroad. We provide access to a multitude of in-patient and out-patient services across 40+ specialties with a team of over 400 doctors and 14 Centres of Excellence, including cardiology, oncology, gastroenterology, internal medicine, nephrology, and orthopaedics. Read More

Upholding the highest standards of quality care and patient safety in tertiary healthcare services, Artemis Hospitals was the first hospital in Gurgaon to receive accreditation from the Joint Communication International (JCI) and National Accreditation Board for Hospitals and Healthcare Providers (NABH). As one of the best hospitals for heart, kidney, and cancer care in Gurgaon, we provide state-of-the-art medical equipment and technologies to ensure precise treatments for patients. Read Less

Know More About Artemis

At Artemis Hospitals, we are committed to bringing the latest and innovative medical treatments to India. We continually integrate state-of-the-art, safe, and certified technologies from across the globe. Whether it is radiation therapy, nuclear medicine, cardiology, imaging, or ICU, we have integrated advanced technologies for the benefit of our patients. We are the first hospital in Gurgaon and North India to own the groundbreaking M6 CyberKnife system and also deploy da Vinci Robotic Surgical system.

M6 CyberKnife
M6 CyberKnife offers a painless and non-invasive radiation treatment.
Neurointerventional Lab
Specialized lab for treating brain, spine, and neck conditions with diagnostic imaging
Cath Lab (Endovascular)
Room for endovascular therapy and catheterization procedures
CT Scan
A non-invasive imaging that uses x-rays to create images of all body parts
Gamma Camera
A camera that detects gamma radiation to create images of the body
IGRT
Enhances radiation accuracy, minimizing damage to healthy tissue
O Arm
2D/3D imaging system for spine, orthopedic, and trauma surgery
PET CT
Nuclear imaging for cancer detection and treatment evaluation
Robot Da Vinci
Robotic-assisted minimally invasive surgery with precision
X-Ray
High-energy radiation for diagnostic and therapeutic use
MRI MAGNETOM LUMINA
3T MRI system offering reproducible scans and high patient satisfaction
3D Biplane
Uses 2 x-rays and rotating cameras to create 3D images of blood vessels and soft tissue.
