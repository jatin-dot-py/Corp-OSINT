Building for a New India

A Real Estate company evolving with India’s next growth cycle.

PVV Infra Limited is a listed company with experience across commercial and residential real estate, renewable energy initiatives and Wayside Amenities. Since its incorporation in 1995, the company has developed capabilities in construction, housing, project coordination, material support and allied development services.

Today, PVV Infra is aligning its next chapter with sectors that support India’s evolving growth needs: clean energy, practical real estate development and highway-focused Wayside Amenities. The company’s direction is built around disciplined execution, long-term value creation and assets that serve communities, travellers, partners and stakeholders.

In Progress Projects

PVV Infra Awarded WSA Project on NH‑44 at Dabra, Madhya Pradesh

PVV Infra Limited is pleased to announce that the Company has received a Letter of Award (LOA) from National Highways Logistics Management Limited (NHLML), a 100% owned SPV of the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI), for the development, operation and maintenance of a Wayside Amenity (WSA) on NH‑44, Gwalior–Jhansi Corridor at Dabra, Madhya Pradesh, on a DBOT (Design, Build, Operate and Transfer) basis.

This WSA is envisaged as a modern, integrated facility designed to serve passengers, commercial traffic and long‑distance travelers on one of North India’s important highway corridors.

Project Details

Corridor & Location: NH‑44 / Gwalior–Jhansi Road / Km 112+000 / Dabra, Madhya Pradesh. LOA Reference Number: NHLML-WSA0BIDS(INVI)/8/2026/E-3473/408

Strategic Importance

The Dabra WSA project strengthens PVV Infra’s presence on the high‑traffic NH‑44 corridor, enabling the Company to offer safe, clean and well‑managed wayside infrastructure to highway users while creating a stable, long‑term revenue stream under the concession framework.

PVV Infra remains committed to enhancing India’s national highway experience through high‑quality WSA projects and efficient operations

PVV Infra Secures Premier WSA Project near Ujjain on NH‑752D at Dhanodiya, Madhya Pradesh

PVV Infra Limited is delighted to announce the receipt of a Letter of Award (LOA) from National Highways Logistics Management Limited (NHLML), a 100% owned SPV of the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI), for the development, operation and maintenance of a Wayside Amenity (WSA) on NH‑752D, Ujjain–Garoth Corridor at Dhanodiya, Madhya Pradesh, on a DBOT (Design, Build, Operate and Transfer) basis.

This project is a marquee opportunity for PVV Infra, combining infrastructure development with significant religious and tourism‑led demand near one of India’s most revered pilgrimage destinations.

Project Details

Corridor & Location: NH‑752D / Ujjain–Garoth / Km 46+400 / LHS / Dhanodiya, Madhya Pradesh. LOA Reference Number: NHLML-WSA0WEST(MISC)/1/E-3410/409.

Location Significance

The Dhanodiya WSA site is located in proximity to Ujjain, one of the four sacred cities of India and home to the revered Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga. Ujjain is scheduled to host the Simhastha Purna Kumbh Mela in 2028, one of the largest religious congregations in the world, which is expected to attract substantial pilgrim and tourist traffic to the region.

This WSA will be positioned to serve this rising flow of visitors with organized parking, rest, food and utility facilities, reinforcing PVV Infra’s role in delivering high‑impact, service‑oriented highway infrastructure

Upcoming Projects

A 1 GW N-Type TOPCon solar cells manufacturing facility AP

PVV Infra Ltd signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Andhra Pradesh Economic Development Board (APEDB) on November 15, 2025, for a 1 GW N-Type TOPCon solar cells manufacturing facility in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. 

The project involves an investment of approximately ₹650 crore ($73.4 million). The plant will occupy a 30-acre industrial site in a developing solar cell manufacturing cluster near the airport project. It leverages PVV Infra’s expertise in investment, technology, planning, design, and equipment sourcing for setup and operations in the market.

PVVI partnered with ITCS (Taiwan)

PVV Infra Limited announced a strategic technology collaboration with ITC Services Company Limited (Taiwan) in February 2026, enhancing its Clean Energy Manufacturing Project in Visakhapatnam from ₹650 crore to approximately ₹1,250 crore.

The Partnership provides and covers consultancy, customization, and technology transfer for high-efficiency solar cell manufacturing (TOPCon technology), Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), micro-inverters, and smart power electronics. The partnership and associated its Implementation plan follows a 14-18 month phased timeline, including joint development, trial production, training, and process stabilization.
