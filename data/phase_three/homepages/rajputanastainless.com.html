Who We Are ?

RAJPUTANA STAINLESS - IN STEEL WE TRUST

Rajputana Stainless Limited stands as a premier special steel manufacturer in the country, boasting over three decades of unwavering commitment to excellence in the steel industry. Our enduring mission and vision are exempliﬁed by the outstanding quality of our products.

Nestled on a sprawling facility along Halol-Kalol Road, Panchmahals, Gujarat, we specialize in providing an extensive range of stainless steel offerings. Our portfolio encompasses a diverse array of products, including Billets, Forging Ingots, Round Bars (both black and bright), Square Bars, Hex Bars, Wire Rods,Flat Bars and RCS.

Our pledge to deliver nothing less than top-tier quality is reinforced by meticulous packaging and punctual product deliveries. Our dedication to excellence has earned us a multitude of awards and certiﬁcations, attesting to our commitment to superior standards.
