Our Certificates

At Happy Steels  Limited (Happy Steels), we take pride in the quality and safety of our products. We are committed to delivering the highest standards of excellence to our customers and have earned several certifications to demonstrate our commitment to quality.

Our certificates include ISO 9001:2015 certification for our quality management system, IATF-16949:2016  certification for our automotive-specific quality management system and ISO 14001:2015 certification for our environmental management system.

We are also certified by the International Automotive Task Force (IATF), which recognizes our commitment to continuous improvement and customer satisfaction in the automotive industry.

In addition, we are proud to be a certified member of the Automotive Parts Manufacturers’ Association (APMA), which represents the interests of Canada’s automotive suppliers.

At Happy Steels, we are dedicated to providing our customers with the best possible products and services. Our certifications are just one way that we demonstrate our commitment to quality and safety in everything we do.
