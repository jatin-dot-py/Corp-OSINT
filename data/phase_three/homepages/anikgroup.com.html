EXPANSION PLANS

FERRO-ALLOYS

Ferro alloys refer to various alloys of iron with a high proportion of one or more other element, manganese or silicon. Ferro Alloys is one of the intermediate products used as additives and de-oxidising /de-sulfurizing agents in steel making.
