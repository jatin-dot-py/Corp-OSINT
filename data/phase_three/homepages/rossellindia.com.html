About Us

Rossell India Limited

Founded in 1994 as Rossell India Limited, our journey began with a singular focus on producing exceptional teas. Over the years, we diversified into Aviation and Defence manufacturing and services. To reflect this evolution, the company rebranded as Rossell India Limited on April 19, 2011, representing its broader business ventures.

In 2024, Rossell India Limited embarked on a new chapter by returning to its roots, focusing exclusively on tea production. With the Aerospace & Defence business transitioning to an independent entity Rossell Techsys Limited, Rossell India Limited is now dedicated to nurturing its legacy in the tea industry. This commitment to growth and excellence continues with the acquisition of Dhoedaam Tea Estate in January 2025. Situated in Assam’s prime tea-growing belt, Dhoedaam is now the largest of our estates, producing 1.8 million kilograms of tea annually, further strengthening our position as a producer of high-quality teas.

ROSSELL INDIA: A LEGACY OF QUALITY

Renowned for our dedication to quality, Rossell India consistently produces 7.50 million kilograms of premium tea annually, delighting tea connoisseurs worldwide.

The backbone of our success lies in the collaboration and commitment of individuals who drive our vision forward. With 7800 permanent and 9500 seasonal employees across our seven estates, our team is led by seasoned professionals who bring expertise and passion to every step of the process.

From the tea bush to the packed product, we follow the highest agricultural and manufacturing standards. Every effort is made to ensure that our valued customers enjoy nothing but the finest teas.
