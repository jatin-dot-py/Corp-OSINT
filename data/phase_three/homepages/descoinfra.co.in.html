Underground Installations

Underground Installations

In projects where uninterrupted land stretches exist, Desco Infratech Limited specializes in underground installations of cables, fiber optics, and pipelines to mitigate external damage risks. Each installation undergoes a systematic process comprising six key stages: design, installation, testing, troubleshooting, documentation, and restoration. This methodical approach ensures that projects are executed with precision and efficiency from inception to completion. Our scope of services encompasses a diverse range of sectors including Gas Distribution Networks, Renewable Energy, Water Management, and Power Infrastructure.

Aboveground Installations

Aboveground Installations

The process of installing pipelines, cables, or other infrastructure components on the surface rather than burying them underground. This method involves mounting these components on support structures such as poles, towers, or frames, ensuring they are securely elevated and accessible. Above ground installations are commonly used in various industries, including power transmission, telecommunications, and oil and gas, where easy access for maintenance and monitoring is essential. This approach reduces excavation costs, simplifies inspections, and allows for quicker repairs, though it may require considerations for environmental exposure and physical security.
