Our Product Range

Electric Two Wheelers

Tiger

Your Everyday EV Companion

Tiger is Supertech EV’s entry-level electric scooter range, crafted for effortless daily commutes. With a license-free speed of 25 km/h, it’s perfect for students, first-time EV riders, and gig workers. Lightweight, energy-efficient, and easy to maintain, TIGER offers a comfortable ride backed by reliable battery options and strong after-sales support—making it the smart choice for short-distance mobility in Indian cities and towns.

Zapster

Built for thrill, tuned for performance

Zapster represents Supertech’s high-speed electric scooter range, designed for urban professionals and style-focused riders. With top-tier BLDC motors, lithium-ion battery packs, and sporty aesthetics, ZAPSTER delivers fast, smooth, and safe rides up to 120 km per charge. It’s the ideal blend of performance, tech, and design for today’s fast-moving generation looking to upgrade their daily commute.

Three Wheelers

Pilot

Built to Serve, Designed to Last

The PILOT series is Supertech EV’s dependable electric rickshaw line for both passenger and cargo mobility. Built on a rugged chassis with high-torque motors and durable suspension, PILOT models are designed for India’s urban and rural roads. Whether it’s ferrying passengers or hauling goods, it offers low operating costs, high uptime, and trusted service support, making it a favorite among independent drivers and micro-entrepreneurs.

Maxx

Drive More, Carry More, Earn More

MAXX is Supertech EV’s premium L5-category electric auto range, engineered for commercial transport and logistics. Designed to handle high payloads with ease, MAXX comes equipped with powerful motors, strong suspension, and a long-range lithium battery. Whether you’re moving passengers or goods, MAXX delivers dependable performance, lower running costs, and high ROI—making it ideal for fleet operators and business owners.

What is an electric vehicle (EV)?
An electric vehicle uses a battery and electric motor instead of a petrol or diesel engine, offering eco-friendly, zero-emission transportation with lower running costs.
How do Supertech Electric Vehicles work?
Our EVs use rechargeable batteries to power BLDC motors for smooth acceleration and quiet, efficient mobility.
How long does it take to charge a Supertech EV?
Lead-acid batteries: 6–8 hours | Lithium-ion batteries: 3–5 hours. Charging time depends on battery type and charger.
How far can I travel on a full charge?
• 2-Wheelers: 60–140 km
• E-Rickshaws: 80–100 km
• L5 Autos: 100–120 km
Actual range depends on load and terrain.
Where can I charge my Supertech EV?
Charge at home via standard sockets or public EV charging stations with approved chargers.
How long does a Supertech EV battery last?
Lead-Acid: 12–18 months | Lithium-Ion: 3–5 years (depending on care and use).
