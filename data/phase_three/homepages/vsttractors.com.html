Why Choose Us

VST stands for reliable and innovative farm machines, driven by a deep commitment to customers and a passion for empowering farmers.

With 50+ years of unwavering commitment to agriculture, VST stands as a leader in farming solutions. Our heritage is steeped in innovation, driven by a relentless pursuit of excellence. From power tillers to 4WD tractors, VST offers a comprehensive range of products tailored to meet the diverse needs of farmers across 40+ countries.

Durability is at the core of our ethos – when you choose VST, you invest in equipment crafted to last a lifetime. Built from top-quality materials and engineered for resilience, our machines ensure reliability and minimal maintenance, delivering consistent performance in every field.

We are more than manufacturers; we are partners in agricultural progress. Our cutting-edge technologies empower farmers to enhance productivity, reduce costs, and sustainably manage resources. With a dedication to farm mechanization while focusing on customer satisfaction, VST remains your trusted ally in farm mechanization – where tradition meets innovation, and quality meets enduring value.
