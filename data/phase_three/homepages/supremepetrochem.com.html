Supreme Petrochem: Leading Polymer Manufacturer

Supreme Petrochem Ltd (SPL) was promoted jointly by The Supreme Industries Ltd, and the established, highly diversified Rajan Raheja Group. The former is among India’s largest plastics processors and an acknowledged leader in the industry, with 28 advanced plants that manufacture the country’s most comprehensive plastic products portfolio. The latter is a well-established, highly diversified group with interests in cement, ceramic tiles, automotive batteries, construction, retail superstores, and more.

SPL owns and operates India’s first & only international scale Styrenics complex at Amdoshi (Nagothane), 120 km from Mumbai. The complex hosts a Polystyrene (PS) plant using technology from Huntsman Chemical Corporation, USA, an Expandable Polystyrene (EPS) plant using latest technology, a Compounding unit producing a variety of high quality polymer compounds, and an Extruded Polystyrene (XPS) insulation board plant.
