About Prizor

BIS-ER-certified, Indian-manufactured CCTV and surveillance systems — the compliant choice for businesses and homes that need certified surveillance.

Prizor is a CCTV camera and surveillance systems manufacturer headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, and listed on the NSE Emerge platform (NSE: PRIZOR). We design and manufacture BIS-ER-certified, STQC-tested CCTV cameras, NVRs, DVRs, 4G cameras, Smart TVs, and interactive touch panels, 100% made in India. We are one of the country’s leading suppliers of interactive educational touch panels, backed by an in-house R&D team and a nationwide support network. Our aim is to support the Viksit Bharat 2047 mission through reliable and trustworthy surveillance ecosystems completely Made-in-India.

BIS-ER & ISO Certified Quality

Every Prizor product is certified under ISO 9001:2015 for quality management and tested against BIS IS 13252, the surveillance camera safety standard now mandatory in India. We are also STQC-tested and listed on GeM as an OEM, meeting the compliance bar that government and enterprise buyers require before procurement.

End-to-End CCTV & Surveillance Support

Prizor customers get a toll-free helpline, a dealer and service network across 22+ states spread across North, South, West and East India, and a dedicated in-house R&D team. From installation to after-sales service, we care about the full lifecycle, not just the sale.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the CCTV full form, and how does a CCTV camera work?
    CCTV stands for Closed-Circuit Television. A CCTV camera captures video and sends it to a DVR, NVR, monitor, or video management system for live monitoring and recording. Modern CCTV systems can also support features such as night vision, motion detection, remote viewing, and AI-based analytics. Prizor offers CCTV cameras, DVRs, NVRs, and other surveillance products for residential, commercial, and professional security applications.
    Which is the best CCTV camera for home security?
    The best CCTV camera for home depends on where it will be installed, the required resolution, night-vision performance, storage, connectivity, and monitoring requirements. Features such as clear night vision, motion detection, remote viewing, and reliable recording can be useful for home security. Prizor offers different CCTV camera models, including cameras with ColorView™ night vision and other surveillance features, depending on the selected model.
    What should I look for when comparing the best CCTV camera brands in India?
    When comparing the best CCTV camera brands in India, consider image quality, product range, night vision, AI capabilities, certifications, warranty, compatibility, installation support, and after-sales service. Prizor offers CCTV cameras, DVRs, NVRs, PoE switches, and other surveillance products. Prizor surveillance products are BIS certified and STQC tested.
    Can I use a CCTV camera for home with mobile connectivity?
    Yes. A CCTV camera for home with mobile connectivity can allow remote access to your surveillance system through a compatible smartphone application. Prizor provides Android and iOS mobile applications supporting functions such as live view, playback, motion detection, push notifications, device initialization, and remote configuration for compatible Prizor cameras and recording systems. Feature availability can depend on the selected camera, recorder, and system configuration.
    Which is better for home security: wired or wireless CCTV cameras?
    The right choice between wired and wireless CCTV cameras depends on your property and surveillance requirements. Wired systems are commonly suitable for permanent installations requiring stable connectivity and continuous recording. Wireless or cellular options can be useful where network cabling is difficult. Prizor also offers 4G-enabled surveillance products, and its support information states that compatible 4G cameras can use SIM-based cellular connectivity without a wired network.
    What is the CCTV camera price for home and a full CCTV camera set?
    The CCTV camera price for home depends on the camera model, resolution, number of cameras, DVR or NVR configuration, storage capacity, night-vision features, cabling, and installation requirements. Therefore, the CCTV camera price for a full set varies according to the size and specifications of the surveillance system. Prizor provides project-specific recommendations and quotations based on security requirements.
    How do I install a CCTV camera system in a small home?
    Start by identifying important areas such as entrances, parking spaces, balconies, staircases, and other access points. Then choose suitable cameras, determine recording and storage requirements, and position the cameras to minimize blind spots. Prizor states that its authorized dealers provide professional installation services, while remote commissioning support and installation guides are also available.
    What mistakes should I avoid when installing CCTV cameras at home?
    Common CCTV installation mistakes include placing cameras at ineffective heights or angles, leaving blind spots, overlooking night-vision requirements, selecting insufficient recording storage, using unsuitable cameras for outdoor environments, and failing to secure passwords or remote access. The camera, recorder, storage, networking, and installation location should be planned together to create a reliable home CCTV system.
    Where can I find a CCTV camera shop or dealer near me?
    If you're searching for a “CCTV camera shop near me,” “CCTV camera near me,” or an authorized CCTV dealer, Prizor provides a dealer network for purchasing its surveillance products and getting local assistance. Prizor current website states that its authorized dealer/service network covers 500+ cities in India, and customers can use its Where to Buy section or contact Prizor to find an appropriate dealer.
