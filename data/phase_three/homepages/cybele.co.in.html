Welcome to

Cybele Industries Limited

Founded on May 17, 1993 based in Chennai, India, Cybele Industries Limited has been a driving force in the wires and cables industry. With a commitment to innovation and quality, Cybele has grown into a trusted name, offering a diverse range of electrical products that power progress, ensuring technological advancement and manufacturing excellence.
With a highly advanced manufacturing setup, Cybele continues to shape the future of the wires and cables industry, leading the way in technological innovation, quality enhancement, and strategic expansion.
