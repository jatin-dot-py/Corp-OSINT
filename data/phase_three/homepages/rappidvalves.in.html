Gate Valves

Gate valves are linear-motion devices primarily used for on/off control. They feature a wedge or gate that slides down to block the flow, offering precise sealing. Suitable for high-pressure systems, these valves are designed per global standards like DIN and ASME, with various operation options.

Globe Valves

Globe valves regulate flow efficiently with a movable disc and stationary ring seat. They excel in throttling and are commonly used in pipelines to control flow with low leakage. Available in manual and automated configurations, they adhere to stringent industrial standards.

Explore our range of Globe Valves.

more
Check Valves

Check valves prevent backflow and maintain unidirectional flow in pipelines. Types include swing, lift, and wafer designs, with options for horizontal or vertical installation. They are low-maintenance and ensure system efficiency.

Explore our range of Check Valves.

more
Ball Valves

Ball valves offer quarter-turn operation for reliable on/off control and throttling. Their floating ball design ensures a secure seal. These versatile valves are used across industries, featuring robust materials like stainless steel and exotic alloys

Explore our range of Ball Valves.

more
Butterfly Valve

Butterfly valves, lightweight and cost-effective, are quarter-turn devices with a rotating disc to control flow. They are available in concentric, double-offset, and triple-offset designs, suitable for various pressure conditions.

Explore our range of Butterfly Valve.

more
Marine Valves

Marine valves are specialized for shipboard and offshore applications, ensuring durability in saline environments. They include gate, globe, and butterfly designs, meeting marine industry standards for safety and performance.
