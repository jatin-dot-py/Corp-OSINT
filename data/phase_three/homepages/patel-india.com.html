About Us

Started as a ‘One man’ ‘One truck’ company Est. 1959; Inc. 1962. PATEL is a trusted name in the world of logistics. One of the pioneers of logistics in India. An ISO 9001:2008 company listed with premier stock exchanges like the National Stock Exchange and Bombay Stock Exchange. Today, PATEL has a well deserved reputation for strong business values, quality services & unflagging corporate excellence.
