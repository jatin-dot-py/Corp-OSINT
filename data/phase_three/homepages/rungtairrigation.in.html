About Us

Smart solutions for smart irrigation unlike any other brand only here

Rungta Irrigation is engaged mainly in manufacturing, designing, assembling and marketing pipe-based sprinkler irrigation systems. It was incorporated on Apr. 17, 1986 as a private limited company. Earlier known as Jindal lrrigation, it became a public limited company with the present name on May 09, 1994 through shareholder resolution.

Future Direction
Today Rungta Irrigation Limited (RIL) has become synonymous with agricultural growth of the country. This growth of the company has been across sectors of general Agriculture, Horticulture, Tea, Coffee, Rubber and Cardamom plantations. The company has been exporting it’s products to Nepal, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka and other countries.. With it’s Research & Development wing continuously engaged in new innovations the company is capable of supplying the best products being used by the Agriculture sector of the country for it’s irrigation needs.
Our Mission
It is our mission to produce products and services that meet the quality, performance and price aspirations of our customers and while doing so, maintain the highest standards of ethics and social responsibilities.
Our Vision
We , at Rungta Group work as a team to ensure excellence in everything we do by striving for synergy between technology, systems and human resources to deliver the products and services that exceed customer expectations and delight them.
Our Values
For over thirty years since 1986, M/S Rungta Irrigation Limited (RIL) has continued to have a value-based culture, as a source of pride for each of us as we continue to serve our customers with high standards of ethics and integrity. We remain committed to our values and ensuring best interest of all our stakeholders.
