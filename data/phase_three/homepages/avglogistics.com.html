About AVG Logistics

Expert Solutions for a Diverse, Sustainable Future

At AVG Logistics Limited, we lead in sustainable and innovative logistics. Since 2010, we've grown into one of India’s most trusted multi-modal logistics providers, driven by agility, innovation, and excellence. We offer end-to-end solutions in transportation, warehousing, distribution, supply chain, and 3PL services.

We are committed to cutting carbon emissions (SCOPE I & II) by adopting advanced technologies. Our fleet includes electric and LNG-powered vehicles, and we operate exclusively on electrified rail routes, saving thousands of tonnes of CO₂ annually. Customer centricity drives us. By investing in technology and operational excellence, we deliver smarter, faster, and more reliable logistics—while building a greener future.

Frequently Asked Question ?

What is AVG Logistics Limited?

AVG Logistics Limited is a leading public-listed logistics company offering comprehensive solutions in surface transportation, warehousing, and green logistics. With a focus on efficiency and sustainability, AVG provides reliable, eco-friendly services, optimizing supply chains and reducing environmental impact, while ensuring timely deliveries across a vast network.

What services does AVG Logistics offer?

AVG Logistics specializes in Full Truck Load (FTL) and Part Truck Load (PTL) services, rail and multimodal transportation, and dedicated warehousing & distribution. We offer cold chain logistics for temperature-sensitive goods, integrated supply chain solutions, ISO tanks for bulk liquid transport, and packers and movers for reliable, efficient relocation services.

Which industries does AVG serve?

AVG Logistics caters to a wide range of sectors, including FMCG & Food Products, Pharmaceuticals, Automotive, E-commerce, Industrial Goods, Consumer Durables, and more. Additionally, AVG serves industries like QSR, Telecom, Commodity, and FMCD, providing tailored logistics solutions to meet diverse sector needs.

Does AVG provide cold chain logistics?

Yes, AVG provides temperature-controlled transportation and warehousing solutions, maintaining precise temperatures ranging from -25°C to +25°C. Our cold chain division guarantees the secure transport of perishable goods, including food, dairy, and pharmaceuticals, ensuring product integrity is preserved from origin to destination.

Does AVG Offer Franchise?

No, AVG does not offer franchises. We operate under a Company-Owned, Company-Operated (COCO) model, ensuring complete control over our operations and maintaining high service standards. This approach allows us to directly manage and oversee all aspects of our logistics solutions to deliver consistent, quality service.

How does AVG Help in rail logistics?

AVG supports rail logistics by operating 6 railway routes, offering both Partial Truck Load (PTL) and Full Truck Load (FTL) services. Our well-connected and reliable rail network enables timely, cost-effective, and eco-friendly transportation across key locations.
