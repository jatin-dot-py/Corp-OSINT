Who We Are

Kiaasa as a fashion brand tells the story of a new-age Indian woman who is free-spirited, independent and aware. Our constant endeavour is to provide a fashionable, stylish, unbeatable collection of ethnic and fusion wear to carefully curate into line with fashion trends in India. Kiaasa as a brand believes in making special memories even more precious so that every woman celebrates her dress with full zing. With interiors that match the brand’s personality and with our courteous staff who make your shopping experience memorable, shopping at KIAASA will indeed give every lady a wonderful shopping experience. So when you visit any of the 100 stores spread in 16 Indian states, you will #loveKiaasa for a memorable fashion shopping experience and will certainly #wearKiaasa.

Promoters

Meet the Visionaries Behind Kiaasa: Kiaasa, a leading women’s ethnic fashion retailer, is spearheaded by two dynamic entrepreneurs, Mr. Om Prakash and Mr. Amit Chauhan. With a shared passion for innovation and excellence, they acquired Kiaasa in early 2021, amidst the pandemic.

The Duo Partners- Proven Track Record:
Before taking the reins at Kiaasa, Mr. Prakash and Mr. Chauhan had already established themselves as successful entrepreneurs in the textile industry. As owners of Rugs In Style, a renowned manufacturer and exporter of handmade carpets, they had built a reputation for quality, craftsmanship, and customer satisfaction.

A New Chapter for Kiaasa: Under the leadership of Mr. Prakash and Mr. Chauhan, Kiaasa is poised for rapid growth and expansion. With a focus on offering a diverse range of ethnic wear, including kurtas, suits, lehengas, and accessories, the brand aims to become the go-to destination for women’s clothing in India.
