OUR BUSINESSES
Spanning industries
01
Textile and Apparel

From innovation in fibre to sustainability in fashion, Arvind is powering high-fashion brands across the world, all the while delivering unmatched quality and excellence across the garment value chain.

02
Retail

Our retail stores deliver a premium shopping experience for men, combining Arvind’s finest offerings under one roof. featuring a full range of shirting and suiting fabrics, and super-premium products.

03
Advanced Materials

Arvind Advanced Materials is our endeavour of material science which use textiles as a backbone focusing on new fibres and new technology catering to energy, infrastructure, healthcare, and industrial applications.

04
Environmental Solutions

Arvind Envisol provides end-to-end environmental solutions, offering advanced water, wastewater, and sewage treatment technologies that help industries enhance sustainability, reduce environmental impact, and promote efficiency.

05
Communication, Mobility, and Digital Solutions

Arvind delivers mission‑critical communications and mobility platforms, providing secure, scalable solutions for enterprises and public institutions backed by decades of expertise.
