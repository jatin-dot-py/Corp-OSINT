Welcome to AJOONI BIOTECH LTD

AJOONI is pioneer, innovator and leader in the production of animal feed. It's priorities are the quality, safety, innovations in production process, as well as excellent services provides to customers.

With its own feed range, AJOONI is a rising key player in Animal Feed and aquaculture industry. Choosing AJOONI means relying on a responsive, caring partner having rich experience and well aware of the complex dynamics of Livestock market.

AJOONI's objective is the optimization of productivity, satisfaction of all nutritional needs of animals and achievement of the best balance in their diet. AJOONI tends to be a good partner to all farmers. Our experts shares their expertise and many years of experience, propose a program of diet and gives the best recommendation for optimal growth and progress of the livestock.
