ABOUT US

Sutlej Textiles and Industries Ltd. (STIL), promoted by the late Dr. Krishna Kumar Birla, has evolved into a one-stop shop for all types of yarn and premium range of exclusives, innovative textiles crafted for home fabrics and and Green Fibre with marquee clients in India and a global clientele base spread across 65 countries.
