CNC Machines Manufacturer In India

Lokesh ranks amongst the top five machine tool manufacturers in the country.

Approximately 20% of machines are supplied to auto OEMs, 60% of supplies to auto ancillaries & 20% to general industries and exports.

SPECIAL PURPOSE MACHINES

Lokesh - Special Purpose Machines are supplied mainly to the Automobile industry, Power Generation and General Engineering Industries. These are custom built to the client's requirements. The application of these machines are in the areas of Metal cutting and Machining.

All machines are manufactured as per the process capability requirement of the components.

Lokesh is a leader in Cam & Crank borers, Fine borers & Finish Milling machines.

View Products

AUTO COMPONENTS

The auto component division of Lokesh is india's leading supplier of machined engine Cylinder Blocks, Cylinder Heads & Fractured Connecting Rods.

The line at Lokesh Production facility at Pune is a testimony to the implementation of breakthrough concepts in Automation, Quality Control & Traceability equipped with Poka Yoke and other modern manufacturing concepts.
