TOUCHING MILESTONES SINCE 2004

Iris Clothings Limited is a fast-growing readymade garment company. We are engaged in designing, manufacturing, branding and selling garments for kids wear under the brand name DOREME in India. We deliver a broad range of unique apparels for infants, toddlers and junior boys and girls. Our quality and ability to continuously add new product lines and innovate with a new design makes us a preferred brand in the segment.
