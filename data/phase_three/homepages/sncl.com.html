    COMPANY OVERVIEW

    Sadhana Nitro Chem Ltd. (SNCL) is an ISO 9001:2015 company committed to producing high quality intermediate speciality chemicals.

    Since its inception in 1973, SNCL has built a strong reputation of manufacturing quality products along with excellence in service to clients worldwide. Production of intermediates under strict quality standards has been the hallmark of SNCL's success in attuning and propelling it to customer requirements.

    Presently, SNCL manufactures downstream derivatives of Nitrobenzene and other intermediates for various applications in aerospace, pharma and agro, optical brightening agents, plastic additives, special fibres, epoxy resin hardeners, dyes and performance chemicals.

    SNCL provides immediate and assured service to it's European clientele through its 100% subsidiary Anuchem b.v.b.a. in Antwerp, Belgium.

    SNCL is a government recognized 2-Star Golden Export House.

    Our Products

    1

    Nitrobenzene

    SNCL manufactures Nitrobenzene which is a water insoluble pale yellow coloured liquid produced by nitration of benzene . Nitrobenzene is used to produce intermediates for various applications in aerospace, pharma-agro, cosmetics, dyes and speciality chemicals.

    2

    Meta Amino Phenol

    Derived from Nitrobenzene, Meta Amino Phenol is a specialty chemical intermediate. It finds applications in industries such as pharmaceuticals, dyes, and agrochemicals, serving as a building block for the synthesis of various chemicals and pharmaceutical compounds.

    3

    Aniline 2,5 Disulphonic Acid

    SNCL specializes in the manufacture of Aniline 2,5 Disulphonic Acid, which is a downstream derivative of Nitrobenzene. This versatile chemical compound is widely used in the production of dyes, pigments, and colorants. It enhances color fastness and stability in various applications.
