Perfectpac Limited

We are one of the leading manufacturers of Corrugated boxes since the past 50 years,  From the requirement of B2B clients or core individuals, we cater all sort of demand and we highly promises to fulfill all our orders on time.

Perfectpac Limited is one of the leading manufacturers of Corrugated packaging, established in 1973 with just a single corugation plant ,one of the pioneers in the industry. Diversified into the manufacturer of Expanded Polystrene products in 1996. And, later on they were all successful in setting up an automatic Corrugated plant in Greater Noida in year 2006.

Perfectpac Limited corugation packaging unit initially carried capacity of 60 TPD which was further enhanced upto 100 TPD in stages. It is ISO 9001-14001 certification.

We at Perfectpac Limited have set a benchmark by leveraging advanced machinery to produce tons of cartons daily, efficiently meeting the diverse needs of our clients.

    Emphasizing on sustainability

    Packaging Powered By Sustainability

    Eco-Friendly Materials

    Our operations use battery-operated electric forklifts, ensuring a quieter, emission-free warehouse environment.

    Waste-Conscious Manufacturing

    Our production processes are designed to minimize waste, ensuring optimal use of raw materials and energy.

    In-House Recycling

    We recycle leftover board and paper within our facility, promoting a closed-loop system and reducing landfill contribution.

    Green Partnerships

    We align with vendors and clients who share our commitment to sustainability, working together towards a greener future.

    Powered by the Sun

    We’ve installed solar panels across our facility to harness clean, renewable energy—reducing our carbon footprint significantly.

    Cleaner Transportation

    All our delivery and transport vehicles are CNG-fitted, helping us cut down on harmful emissions during logistics.

    Responsible Waste Disposal

    We ensure that all paper waste is sent directly to paper mills for recycling, promoting a circular economy.

    Smart Waste Management

    A high-efficiency Baling machine is installed in our plant to compress scrap material, making recycling more streamlined and efficient.

    Automated Waste Recovery

    Our automated waste recovery system compacts waste in real-time—minimizing manual handling and optimizing sustainability.
