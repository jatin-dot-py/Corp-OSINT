JHS Legacy
Oral Care Pioneer

We set-up our business with humble beginnings & started as a small-scale enterprise in August 1997, Sunehari Svendgaard Laboratories Limited moved at a brisk pace and was incorporated as JHS Svendgaard Laboratories Limited on October 8, 2004.

By April 2005 the company took over businesses from Sunehari Svendgaard Laboratories Limited, Sunehari Oral Care and Jai Hanuman Exports. Starting with manufacturing of only Toothbrushes the company widened its scope to Toothpastes, Mouthwash and Denture Tablets and today is an oral care product manufacturer and exporter.

Apart from working on its own brands the company also offers Contract Manufacturing Partnership to brands in the domestic and the international market. 

JHS Future
Complete FMCG

With a new aim & fresh ideas we have embarked on a journey to wider our horizons & divulge into complete FMCG sector/personal care. We have modified our perception, capabilities, scope & resources in order to be a capable & competent FMCG Company.

We intend to continue leveraging the Indian consumption story through organic and inorganic expansion, reaching new heights steadily.

Manufacturing Facilties

Manufacturing facilities Situated in the picturesque area of Himachal Pradesh, Kala Amb, our manufacturing facilities are at the forefront of the Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) sector. We produce efficient personal care products, including toothbrushes, toothpaste, mouthwash, and talcum powder, using innovative technology and adhering to the Sky-high industry standards. Our advanced production lines are designed to handle the fast-paced demands of the FMCG market while maintaining extraordinary quality and consistency. From meticulous quality control to sustainable practices, we ensure that every product not only meets but transcend consumer assumptions. Our commitment to innovation and excellence drives us to deliver personal care solutions that are both productive and reliable.

JHS’ well-established research and development department strives for constant innovation and high-quality oral products. This assures that the company is well prepared for the future challenges of this vast and growing field. The company also has complete in-house process facilities from injection molding to bristling and packaging to ensure strict quality control. The presence of a fully equipped Q.C. laboratory with latest gadgets combined with state of the art Vertical & Injection Molding machines from Demag and Cincinnati, Tufting & Trimming machines from Anton Zahoransky Gmbh, Germany and Blister Packing Machines ensures international quality of products.
