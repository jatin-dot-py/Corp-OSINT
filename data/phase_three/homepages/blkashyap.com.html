About BLK

Today, B. L. Kashyap and Sons Ltd. (BLK) is one of India’s most respected Construction, Infrastructure and Civil Engineering Companies with a PAN India presence. Our service portfolio extends across IT Parks, Commercial Spaces, Malls, Hotels, Residential Complexes, Factories, Institutional buildings and Manufacturing Facilities, Healthcare and Transportation.
