Establishing Sustainable Businesses

Change and adaptability have been the hallmarks of Accel group ever since it began its journey in the year 1991, as a multi-vendor service provider for computer systems and peripherals. As the industry grew beyond all expectations, dynamic business models emerged. Sustainability became the mantra for the company, both as an entity and as a part of the global supply chain. For Accel’s founding team led by Mr. N.R.Panicker, one principle remained steadfast. Delivering value through service. This unwavering focus on service enabled the emergence of a diversified portfolio that helped the Accel establish itself as a leading IT services company, providing comprehensive solutions in various domains across different geographies.

Accel's service portfolio includes IT infrastructure management services, warranty management services, managed print services, cybersecurity, software, cloud services and system integration services, with strong presence in the domestic and international markets. Accel also has ventured into other businesses that includes media and realty.

The businesses have been grouped under different strategic divisions addressing the diversified business portfolio.


We offer comprehensive solutions and services that meet the ever-growing demand for digital transformation. Our offerings include IT infrastructure solutions, IT managed services, warranty management services, managed print services, cybersecurity, software and cloud services with our presence in domestic and international markets.


Accel Cybersecurity, a division of Accel IT Services, is a leading provider of cutting-edge cybersecurity solutions. We are dedicated to safeguarding businesses, organizations, and individuals against the growing threat of cyberattacks. Trust us to secure your most valuable assets with innovative and effective cybersecurity strategies.


Striving for excellence in visual effects and digital intermediate services, Accel Media Ventures has emerged as a leading player in the Indian VFX and post production industry by leveraging our strengths and expertise in the IT and computer graphics domain.


Accel Academy is a business unit of Accel Limited. Accel Limited has been the pioneer of IT infrastructure training in India from the time it set up Accel school of computer technology in 1991. Accel through its three decades of sustained efforts, achieved all-round growth with diversifications into IT hardware, networking, system integration, media services, software and digital transformation technologies.


Accel Realty focuses on enabling IT and technology companies with plug-and-play infrastructure. Situated in a 4-acre campus in a serene environment at KINFRA IT SEZ, kazhakuttom, trivandrum close to technopark, the ready-to-use offices with contemporary designs, seamless accessibility, and gorgeous infrastructure are a boon to IT companies to start their full-fledged operations efficiently without much downtime.
