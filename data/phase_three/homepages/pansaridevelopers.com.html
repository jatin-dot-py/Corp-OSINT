Welcome to Pansari

We are an integrated construction and real estate development company focused primarily on constructing and developing residential and commercial projects in and around Kolkata, West Bengal.

About

We are an integrated construction and real estate development company, incorporated in 1996 as Pansari Developers Private Limited, with a vision of building premium houses at affordable prices. Since our residential portfolio covers projects catering to customers across all income groups, we have eventually established a successful track record in the real estate industry in Kolkata. With the expertise of Mr Mahesh Kumar Agarwal for over 20 years, we have successfully delivered sustainable, durable, and innovative projects.

Our Mission

To build an ecosystem of quality spaces that delight our stakeholders in every interaction. We uphold the pillars of trust, reliability and timeliness while keeping the customer at the heart of everything we do.



Our Vision

To become the pioneers of customer-centric real estate in India

Our Strengths

Sustainable Design

Pansari Group strives to build living spaces that are liveable, comfortable and healthy. By preventing environmental degradation caused by the infrastructure and by minimizing maintenance.

Premium Quality

With a commitment to transform the Indian real estate industry, Pansari Group is committed to delivering projects with unprecedented quality.

On-Time Delivery

Pansari Group’s prime focus is to accelerate their project delivery and ensure they deliver on time as promised.
