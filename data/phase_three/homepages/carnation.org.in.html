About Us

The Company was formed for the purpose of manufacture of grey iron castings and to primarily cater to the export markets. The Company began as a merchant exporter and subsequently in 1987 turned into manufacturer and exporter beginning with just one foundry and later on established another foundry after seeing the growing demand for its products.

MAIN OBJECTS OF THE COMPANY

The main objects of the company as stated in the Memorandum of Association are as under:

  1. To acquire by purchase and take over as a going concern the business of the firm now carried on at Calcutta and elsewhere under the name & style of Carnation Enterprise and all or any of the assets and liabilities of the proprietorship firm in connection therewith.
  2. To carry on the business as manufacturers, growers, traders, buyers, sellers, importers, exporters, distributors, stockists, agents, brokers consultants, and dealers or otherwise deal in all kinds and forms of ferrous and non ferrous metals, ferro alloys, iron & steel including mild, high carbon spring, high speed, tool alloy, stainless and special steels, iron, metals and alloys, ingots, billets, bars, joists, rods, squares, structurals, tubes, poles, pipes, sheets, castings wires, rails, rolling material, rollers, semi manufacturers and other materials made wholly or partly of iron, steel, alloys and metal iron masters, iron workers, steel makers, electric and brass founders and metal makers.
