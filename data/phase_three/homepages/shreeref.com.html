WHAT WE DO

Shree Refrigerations Limited is India’s leading defense-focused manufacturer of advanced refrigeration and HVAC systems, proudly serving the Indian Navy and strategic defense sectors for over four decades. With more than 40 years of technical expertise, we specialize in designing, engineering, and delivering mission-critical solutions built for performance, precision, and resilience.

Our Marine HVAC&R systems are custom-engineered to perform reliably under the extreme maritime conditions faced by naval vessels. In addition, we manufacture Radar Cooling Systems, Data Center Cooling Units, and Heat Exchangers, all designed to support sensitive and high-performance defense applications where temperature control is non-negotiable.

Trusted by the Indian Navy and the only Indian company to have naval registrations across all three critical segments — Chillers and Refrigeration Plants, Turnkey HVAC & R Solutions, and Electrical Control Panels.

Our portfolio also includes Electrical Control Panels built for defense platforms and Heavy Fabrication Services supporting large-scale defense manufacturing needs- from critical enclosures to structural components.

Shree Refrigerations Limited is committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and customized solutions that meet the highest standards of quality and durability. Our long-standing collaboration with India’s defense ecosystem underscores our dedication to national capability-building and our role as a trusted strategic partner in the mission of Atmanirbhar Bharat.
