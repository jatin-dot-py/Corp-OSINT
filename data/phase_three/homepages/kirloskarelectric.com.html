    About Kirloskar Electric

    Kirloskar Electric Company produces more than 70 products under eight product groups. At Kirloskar Electric, we bank on The Power of Now. The company is built on the values of our founders, Laxmanrao .K. Kirloskar and Ravi .L. Kirloskar. Kirloskar Electric has already secured a place in world markets as a lean, innovative, and aggressive entity in value engineering.

    We cater to a number of industries in India including power, sugar, steel, cement, agriculture, oil & gas and refineries, and nuclear.

We are the leading manufacturer
of Electrical Equipments in India

Established in 1946, Kirloskar Electric Company pioneered the indigenous electrical manufacturing industry in India. The company offers a diverse portfolio across eight primary product groups, catering to core sectors of the economy such as power generation, transmission and distribution, transportation, renewable energy, and various industrial segments including sugar, steel, and cement.
