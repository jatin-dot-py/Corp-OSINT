About us

Founded in 1994, Page has spent over three decades bringing the world’s most trusted brands to millions. As the exclusive licensee for Jockey and Speedo, we build brands people trust and live with.

Over the years, we have worked to grow both brands with the same commitment and consistency - building them not just as products, but as part of how people live.

We continue to manufacture at scale, market with intent, and build with care.

Jockey has spent 150 years understanding a simple human need — comfort. Since 1995, Page has brought that understanding to India, building Jockey into the country's most recognised innerwear and athleisure brand - city by city, consumer by consumer.

Speedo is the world's most recognised performance swimwear brand — built on decades of innovation and worn on the world's biggest stages. Since 2011, Page has been its home in India, maintaining the same standard that has made Speedo iconic everywhere else.

Our story so far.

Every year, a new chapter. Every milestone, earned.

Discover who we are

  • Page Industries is incorporated, then known as Page Apparel Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd.

  • The 'Burn Your Briefs' campaign and India's first Innerwear Fashion Show announce something new: innerwear as a lifestyle choice.

  • The first Jockey Exclusive Brand Store opens on Commercial Street, Bangalore.

  • Jockey for Her launched — bringing the same quality and conviction to women's innerwear.

  • Turnover crosses INR 50 Crores, the retail network reaches 10,000 outlets, and production hits 1 million pieces a month — the foundation is firmly in place.

  • Jockey Socks launched — the first step toward a complete wardrobe.

  • Jockey Bras launched — deepening the commitment to women's innerwear.

  • Turnover crosses INR 100 Crores and production exceeds 2 million pieces a month — the scale is undeniable.

  • Page Industries lists on the NSE and BSE — a new chapter of transparency and growth begins.

  • Jockey reaches 1,000 towns across India.

  • Three milestones in one year: the Jockey licence renewed to 2030, Speedo launched in India, and Jockey's reach extended to the UAE. 2011 changed the shape of the business.

  • Page Industries moves to a new corporate home in Bangalore — a new base for a business that is set to touch new heights.

  • Jockey.in launches. Revenue crosses Rs. 1,000 Crores — a milestone that changes the conversation.

  • The first globally standardised Jockey Brand Store opens in Bengaluru — setting a new benchmark for how the brand shows up in retail.

  • Jockey crosses borders — the first Exclusive Brand Store opens in Dubai, while back home, Jockey Towels extends the brand into a new category.

  • Jockey reaches 50,000 outlets across India, opens its first Exclusive Brand Store in Sri Lanka, and welcomes its youngest consumers — Jockey Juniors launched.

  • The Jockey license is renewed to 2040 — a vote of confidence in what Page Industries has built. The retail network crosses 500 Exclusive Brand Stores, and Jockey Move launches.

  • A milestone worth marking — 25 years of Jockey in India. To celebrate, Jockey Woman, Athleisure, and Juniors each step forward as independent identities in their own right.

  • The retail network crosses 1,000 Exclusive Brand Stores and 10,00,00 outlets across India — numbers that speak of a brand present in every corner of the country.

  • Jockey.in and Speedo.in are reimagined and relaunched. Quick Commerce goes live — bringing our brands to consumers wherever they are, whenever they need it.

  • E-commerce revenue crosses Rs. 500 Crores. And the journey gets bigger — Jockey expands into Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, and Kuwait, opening a new chapter in Page's international story.
