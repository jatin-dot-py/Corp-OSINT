About Us

About Taylormade

Harnessing the sun, powering a brighter future

  • History

  • Mission

  • Vision

Founded in 2010, TRL commenced its journey as a pioneering force in Solar Thermal Applications, swiftly ascending to market dominance through a commitment to innovation and excellence. A significant milestone was reached in 2017 when TRL debuted on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), solidifying its presence and laying the groundwork for further expansion.

Recognizing the growing urgency of wastewater treatment, particularly in managing hazardous waste, TRL diversified its portfolio to address this pressing global challenge. Collaborating closely with esteemed educational institutions, we've cultivated a culture of research and development, propelling continuous innovation in our products and services.

Central to our success are the numerous patented technologies under our umbrella, developed by our team of experts. These cutting-edge innovations ensure unparalleled efficiency, reliability, and sustainability in our solutions.

Committed to environmental stewardship and excellence, TRL remains at the forefront of renewable energy and wastewater treatment sectors. With a vision for a more sustainable future, we continue to push boundaries, delivering impactful solutions that empower communities and industries worldwide.

Our mission at TRL is to pioneer innovative solutions in renewable energy and wastewater treatment, setting new standards for sustainability and excellence. Guided by our commitment to environmental stewardship, we leverage our expertise in solar thermal applications and wastewater treatment to address pressing global challenges.

Through relentless research and development, we strive to push the boundaries of what's possible. Our patented technologies ensure that we deliver unparalleled efficiency, reliability, and sustainability in all our solutions.

We are dedicated to empowering communities and industries to thrive in a more sustainable world. By harnessing the power of renewable energy and implementing advanced wastewater treatment techniques, we aim to create lasting positive impacts for both the planet and future generations.

At TRL, we believe that through innovation, collaboration, and a steadfast commitment to excellence, we can pave the way towards a brighter, cleaner, and more sustainable future for all.

At TRL, our vision is to emerge as the premier global leader in water and wastewater treatment. Through innovative solutions, advanced technologies, and unwavering dedication, we aim to set new benchmarks for excellence in the industry.

Driven by a commitment to sustainability and environmental stewardship, we envision a future where clean water is universally accessible, and wastewater is efficiently managed. By pioneering advancements in treatment processes, we aspire to safeguard our planet's most precious resources.

Beyond market dominance, our vision encompasses a profound impact on communities and industries worldwide. Through collaboration and innovation, we strive to create a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable world for generations to come.

Together with our partners and stakeholders, we are shaping a future where TRL stands at the forefront of water and wastewater treatment, driving positive change on a global scale."
