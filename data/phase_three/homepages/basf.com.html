BASF Israel

Who we are:

BASF Israel Ltd., formerly Wilhelm Rosenstein Ltd. (WRL), became part of the BASF family in 2020 as part of the integration into the European sales organization BTC Europe, a wholly owned subsidiary of BASF.

With an in-depth knowledge of the Israeli market and extensive technical know-how, BASF Israel offers high-quality chemical solutions to a broad range of industries such as coatings, adhesives, plastics, care chemicals and many others. Over 30 employees work for BASF Israel, committed to offer customers the highest service levels and support.
