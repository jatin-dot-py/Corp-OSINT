ABOUT INFLUX HEALTHTECH

Started as a Micro plant in 2003 with intentions and will to grow, Influx Healthtech has now transpired into one of the leading manufacturing in the healthcare sector. Our manufacturing plants are certified with GMP, HACCP, ISO 22000 and Halal with well-equipped Machineries, Quality Control department and Competent Manpower. Within this span, we have traveled many milestones and introduced various innovative products to customize and cater to the market. We are committed and our continuous aim to keep on producing an innovative range of products with a good concept and quality and build a larger network of customers.

We have a diversified client base throughput PAN INDIA and have a warm presence in 4 continents. Our Major business involves Contract manufacturing of Nutraceuticals, Cosmetics, Ayurvedic / Herbal, Veterinary Feed Supplements and Homecare segment. We can produce large and economic batch sizes with competitive rates for various categories of products viz Soft Gelatin, Tablets, Capsules, Liquid Orals, Oral Powders, Liquid fill capsules, Effervescent tablets, Nutra Pallets, Protein Bars, Cookies, Jellies/Gummies, External preparations like Creams, Gels, Ointments, External Oils, Soaps, etc.
