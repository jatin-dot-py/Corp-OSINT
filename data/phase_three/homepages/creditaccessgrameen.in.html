ANNOUNCEMENT

Dear All,
Please note that Madura Micro Finance Limited (“MMFL”), the material subsidiary of CreditAccess Grameen Limited, Bengaluru (“CA Grameen”), with effect from February 15, 2023 (“Effective Date”), has been amalgamated with CA Grameen by way of an Order passed by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Bengaluru Bench vide its Order C.P.(CAA) No.34/BB/2022 dated February 07, 2023. For more information, please visit the website of CA Grameen at www.creditaccessgrameen.in under the ‘Investors’ Section, titled ‘Amalgamation’.
