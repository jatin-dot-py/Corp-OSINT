Profile
Jigar Cables Ltd stands as one of India’s premier manufacturers of the renowned "SIGMA" brand electric wires and cables. With over two decades of expertise in the industry, our company has established itself as a trailblazer in the electrical field, leveraging advanced technology and the dedication of our highly skilled workforce.
Quality
At Jigar Cables Ltd, quality is not just a commitment—it is the foundation of our manufacturing philosophy. Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is equipped with the latest machinery and ultra-modern laboratories, designed to perform comprehensive testing at every stage of production. With sophisticated type-testing equipment, we ensure that our cables meet the highest standards of performance and safety.
