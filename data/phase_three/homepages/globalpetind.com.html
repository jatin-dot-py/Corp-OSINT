Reshaping The Future of PET Bottles

Global Pet Industries Ltd is a prominent manufacturer and exporter of Two Stage PET Stretch Blow Moulding Machines, specializing in high-quality machinery. Their product line-up includes the Electra Series All Electric Fully Automatic PET Stretch Blow Moulding Machine, the Eco Series Automatic PET Stretch Blow Moulding Machines, and Fully Automatic PET Stretch Blow Moulding Machines. With over 25 years of expertise, the company has earned a stellar reputation as one of India’s most trusted brands, based in Mumbai. Their products serve clients in need of emergency power solutions and electrical device protection.

Explore Our Legacy

With a legacy spanning over three decades, Global Pet Industries has established itself as a leading innovator in the field of PET blow molding technology. Renowned for its precision engineering and commitment to excellence, the company has consistently delivered high-quality, reliable solutions that cater to the evolving needs of the packaging industry.

Global Pet Industries’ dedication to continuous improvement and sustainable growth has cemented its position as a trusted partner for clients worldwide. The company’s forward-thinking approach ensures it remains at the cutting edge of PET molding solutions, setting industry standards for performance, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.
