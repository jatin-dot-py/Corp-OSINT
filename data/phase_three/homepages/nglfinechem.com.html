NGL Fine-Chem: Innovating the Future of Animal APIs

Established in 1981 and headquartered in India, NGL Fine-Chem Ltd is a leading pharmaceutical company with over 40 years of expertise in manufacturing APIs and specialty chemicals for both animal and human health. While animal health remains our core focus, we continue to expand our presence in human health, strengthening our role in the global pharma landscape.
