Who Are We?

About Dreamax Group

Dreamax Group (Balgopal Commercial Ltd.) is a real estate development company rooted in responsibility and refined by experience.

Operating across residential, commercial, and redevelopment projects, Dreamax Group brings structure to complexity and clarity to every stage of development.

Each project is approached as a long-term commitment. To the city. To communities. To families who place their faith in us.

Our strength lies in complete control of the development journey. From approvals to execution. From planning to possession. Quiet confidence defines the Dreamax Group way.
