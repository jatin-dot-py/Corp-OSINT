Overview

DCM Shriram International Ltd. (DCMSIL) is an independent entity, carrying forward the distinguished legacy of the century-old DCM Group, and stands at the forefront of India’s modern industrial transformation. With strengths spanning Industrial Fiber, Defence Armoured Vehicles, Unmanned Aerial Systems (Drones), Shipping Containers, Industrial Research, and Engineering Services, we deliver engineering excellence across diverse sectors. Powered by state-of-the-art infrastructure, deep technical expertise, and a commitment to sustainable innovation, we build products and solutions that strengthen industries, support national security, and elevate India’s global competitiveness. The company stands at the forefront of India’s high-technology manufacturing ecosystem.

DCM Shriram International Limited, carries forward the group’s rich legacy with a future-focused outlook of being “Stronger Together”.
