Gini & Jony: A National Fashion Icon

Gini & Jony isn’t just a brand; it’s a household name with a legacy spanning over four decades. We believe that premium fashion should be accessible to every family, which is why we’ve built one of India’s most expansive retail networks.

  • Exclusive Outlets: We operate 50+ Exclusive Brand Outlets (EBOs), bringing the "Freedom of Spirit" directly to your neighborhood.
  • Multi-Brand Presence: Find us in 300 + India’s leading multi brand stores, including Pantaloons, ensuring a premium shopping experience wherever you go.
  • Cities - 100 +, Ecoom partner - Myntra, Amazon, Flipkart, Ajio, Nykaa

  • Fabric Division & The Manufacturing Edge

Vertical Integration & Industrial Capacity

At Suditi Industries, we don’t just design fashion; we own the process from thread to finish. Our state-of-the-art facility is a hub of precision engineering, housing a fully integrated setup that ensures consistent quality across every garment.

  • Knitting Excellence: With a robust annual production capacity of 2,000 tons, our knitting department crafts a diverse range of high-quality fabrics including Single Jersey, Pique, Rib, Terry, and specialized Jacquards.
  • Precision Dyeing: Our advanced processing unit handles a daily dyeing capacity of 12 tons, utilizing eco-friendly processes to achieve vibrant, long-lasting colors.
  • Total Control: By managing the entire supply chain—knitting, dyeing, printing, finishing, and garmenting—Suditi guarantees that every piece under the Gini & Jony label meets rigorous international standards.
