About Us

Machhar Industries Limited was established in the year 2008 following the de-merger of Anil Chemicals & Industries Limited. We are a prominent public limited company, proudly promoted by the esteemed Machhar Group, a leading industrial house located in Aurangabad, Maharashtra. Our company operates with two distinct divisions: the chemical division and the automotive division.

The chemical division is involved in the conversion of Ammonium Nitrate, a key raw material for explosive industry. The automotive division is involved in manufacturing and distribution of AdBlue® (also known as Diesel Exhaust Fluid - DEF).

We strive to deliver high-quality products that meet stringent industry standards, ensuring customer satisfaction and compliance with environmental regulations.
