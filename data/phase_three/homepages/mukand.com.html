A History of Firsts

We have a long history of firsts and achievements that help us stand apart in a sea of competition, from being the first indigenous Stainless-Steel Producer in India, to being the first to use the VOD Production Process, we are the only Company in the country capable of producing Nuclear-Grade Stainless Steel for the Kalpakkam Nuclear Power Station. Our track record of success is unparalleled in the Indian Steel Industry. While these are just a few examples of our commitment to innovation and excellence, we continue to set the standards for quality and performance that the rest of the industry strives to follow to date, we are Mukand!

Building the Future
of Commerce & Industry

We live in an age of globalised commerce, where the boundaries of technology, innovation, and ingenuity blur to spearhead unprecedented progress. For over eighty years, since our inception in 1937, we have been at the forefront of this advancement that has fuelled our growth into the multi-division, multi-product conglomerate we are today.

At Mukand, our fundamentals are rooted in an unwavering belief in delivering & delighting our customers and exceeding their expectations wherever possible. In an industry as traditional as steelmaking, a customer-obsessed approach may seem out of place, but it has helped us build an unassailable reputation in the market. One that transcends industries and stands for unquestionable quality and delivery.

We believe that we have a responsibility to change the world in every business we cater to, from stronger, more environmentally friendly steel to heavy machinery that outperforms, what we do – can potentially disrupt entire industries for the better.

Led by passionate employees and partners who align with our vision, and by innovating together, we enable ambition to succeed. And when it does, we would have helped shape the future!

Our Business Divisions

We are committed to shaping the industries we serve through our three main business divisions, by defining the future of steel and the products we create with them.

Created by combining carbon steel with other metal and non-metal elements, our alloy
steel products offer varying degrees of strength, corrosion resistance, and
durability
for various applications. In industries such as automobile manufacturing,
construction,
mining, home appliances, and even offshore applications, no form of steel is as
fundamental or versatile to factory operations.
