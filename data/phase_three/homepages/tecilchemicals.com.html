On 28th December, 1994, the name of the Company was changed to “TECIL CHEMICALS AND HYDRO POWER LIMITED” pursuant to Section 21 of the Companies Act, 1956, and a Fresh Certificate of Incorporation was obtained. About Us

TECIL CHEMICALS AND HYDRO POWER LIMITED

  • The Company was incorporated and listed with the name “THE TRAVANCORE ELECTRO-CHEMICAL INDUSTRIES LIMITED” on 04th April, 1945 in the State of Kerala under the Companies Act, 1956 as Public Limited Company. On 28th December, 1994, the name of the Company was changed to “TECIL CHEMICALS AND HYDRO POWER LIMITED” pursuant to Section 21 of the Companies Act, 1956, and a Fresh Certificate of Incorporation was obtained. Tecil Chemicals And Hydro Power Limited was engaged in the manufacture of Electro-Chemical, Electro Thermal furnace products and by-products thereof.
  • The Corporate Identification Number (CIN) of the Company is L24299KL1945PLC001206. The Company was listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange, Madras Stock Exchange, Delhi Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange.
