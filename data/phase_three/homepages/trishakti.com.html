about Trishakti

Incorporated in 1985, listed in Stock Exchanges of India, Trishakti Group takes pride in Powering Progress, Lifting the Future.

Trishakti Industries Ltd. is a leading infrastructure solutions provider in India, with a specialization in the rental and leasing of heavy earth-moving equipment. Founded with a commitment to excellence, we have built a strong reputation as a reliable partner to some of the nation’s largest industrial and construction companies. As a publicly listed entity on the Bombay Stock Exchange, Trishakti Industries Ltd. has consistently demonstrated a focus on quality, innovation, and client satisfaction.

mission

To deliver innovative and sustainable solutions that empower industries and communities to thrive.

vision

We envision a future where our expertise enhances the quality of life, supports economic growth, and contributes to a more sustainable and resilient society.

technology
