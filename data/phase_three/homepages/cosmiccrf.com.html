Manufacturing Precision Steel Solutions for Railways and Infrastructure

Cosmic CRF Limited is engaged in the manufacturing of cold rolled formed steel sections and engineered products for railways, infrastructure, and industrial applications. Operating from its integrated manufacturing facility in West Bengal, the company supplies precision CRF solutions designed to meet stringent quality, safety, and regulatory standards. Our focus remains on consistent execution, certified processes, and long-term reliability for critical infrastructure projects.

  • Certified Quality Systems
  • Engineering & Process Capability
  • Skilled Industrial Workforce
  • Railway-Approved Manufacturing
  • Infrastructure-Focused Solutions
  • Domestic Manufacturing Base

Product & Capability Segments

CRF Sections

Precision cold rolled formed steel sections manufactured for railway, infrastructure, and industrial applications.

Railway Components

Engineered CRF components supplied to railway OEMs and wagon manufacturers in compliance with approved standards.

Infrastructure & Fabricated Products

Fabricated steel profiles and structures designed for EPC, infrastructure, and heavy engineering projects.

Engineering & Custom Profiles

Application-specific CRF profiles developed to meet customer-defined technical requirements.

A focused manufacturing journey built on compliance, execution, and disciplined growth.

2021
Incorporation
Incorporation of Cosmic CRF Limited

Cosmic CRF Limited was incorporated as a focused manufacturing entity to undertake cold rolled forming (CRF) operations for railways, infrastructure, and industrial applications.

2022
Commencement
Commencement of Manufacturing Operations

The company operationalized its manufacturing facility at Singur, West Bengal, initiating commercial production of cold rolled formed steel sections and engineered components.

2023
IPO
Public Listing and Market Expansion

Cosmic CRF Limited was listed on the BSE SME platform, strengthening governance, transparency, and access to growth capital while expanding its railway and infrastructure order book.

2024
Execution
Execution of Major Railway and Infrastructure Orders

The company executed multiple supply contracts for CRF sections, wagon components, and structural sheet piles across railway and EPC projects, reinforcing its presence in infrastructure manufacturing.

2025
Optimization
Operational Consolidation and Capacity Optimization

Focus on strengthening manufacturing efficiency, quality systems, and execution discipline to support sustained growth across railway and structural steel segments.

Applications Across Critical Infrastructure

Railway Wagons & Components

CRF sections and fabricated profiles used in structural and load-bearing railway applications.

Infrastructure & EPC Projects

Steel profiles and fabricated components for bridges, highways, and industrial structures.

Industrial & Engineering Applications

Cold rolled formed sections designed for machinery, enclosures, and custom engineering needs.

Automotive & Fabrication

Precision steel profiles supporting automotive and downstream fabrication use cases.
