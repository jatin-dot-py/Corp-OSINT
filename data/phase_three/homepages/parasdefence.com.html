ABOUT US

Welcome to Paras Defence & Space Technologies Ltd.

We proudly introduce ourselves as India’s most progressive Tier 2 Defence Engineering Company offering the widest range of defence products & solutions. We are a Premier Make in India Company with exclusive capabilities from Concept to Commissioning in the area of Defence Electronics, Defence & Space Optics and Defence Heavy Engineering. We are the only company in India to manufacture Infrared Optics in large quantities. Our company has been significantly contributing to most of the prestigious Defence & Space Programs of our country.

Our sustained growth of over 40 years which we have dedicated only to Defence Engineering has enabled us to offer the widest range of products & solutions for defence applications, under one roof and 100% made in India.
