WHO WE ARE

Kalpataru Projects International Limited

Kalpataru Projects International Limited (KPIL) is one of the largest Engineering and Construction companies listed in India, with a diversified portfolio of projects worldwide, in Power Transmission & Distribution (T&D), Buildings & Factories, Water Supply & Irrigation, Railways, Oil & Gas Pipelines, Urban Mobility (Flyovers & Metro Rail), Highways and Airports in over 75+ countries.

Our Business Streams

Power T&D

One of the most trusted T&D players in both domestic and international markets, with in-house capabilities for design, engineering, manufacturing, testing, installation, and commissioning to execute large-size T&D turnkey projects.

Buildings & Factories

Equipped with PAN India reach, expertise, and experienced manpower to undertake all types of building and housing projects, KPIL provides world-class infrastructure support to India's growing Healthcare, Institutional, Residential, Townships, Commercial, Industrial, Hospitality, IT office spaces, Data centers, Manufacturing Plants, and Services sectors.

KNOW MORE

Water

With over 15 million beneficiaries and 7.5 lakh house service connections worldwide so far, KPIL provides world-class EPC solutions for a wide range of water-related projects for both domestic and international markets, including drinking water supply and distribution systems, wastewater collection treatment, and irrigation, among others.

KNOW MORE

Railways

As a leading EPC contractor in the railways infrastructure space, KPIL caters to turnkey solutions for conventional rail lines and metro rail. The company undertakes comprehensive railway projects encompassing overhead electrification, traction substations, track laying, earthwork, bridges, buildings, stations, workshops, signaling & telecommunications, and 3rd Rail DC systems.

Infrastructure

Among the leading companies offering EPC, design and build services for multi-disciplinary heavy civil and urban infrastructure projects driven by technology, a latest fleet of equipment, and a team of professionals at the helm.

Oil & Gas

KPIL is a leading player in the Oil & Gas pipeline business in India, covering cross-country Oil & Gas pipelines, processing facilities, refineries, and fertilizer plants. The company has delivered projects covering design & engineering, civil, electrical, mechanical, procurement, construction management, testing, and commissioning.

KNOW MORE

Manufacturing Plants

One of the largest transmission tower manufacturing companies with in-house galvanizing and painting facilities spread across two plants in India. Expertise to manufacture structures for overhead railway electrification as per the required standards of Indian Railways.

KNOW MORE

Biomass Plants

Two Biomass power plants in the state of Rajasthan with a combined capacity of 15.8 MW (7.8 MW and 8.0 MW) using Direct Combustion Boiler technology. KPIL was one of the top companies to get registered with UNFCCC in 2005.
