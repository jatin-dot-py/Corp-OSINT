About Company

Quality RO Industries Ltd.

We are a trusted partner in the water treatment industry, specializing in manufacturing high-quality softener equipment and components such as filter parts, cartridges, taps, and membrane housings using premium polypropylene raw materials. We also offer contractual manufacturing solutions tailored to client requirements, ensuring durability and performance.

In addition, we act as reliable traders of reverse osmosis plants and machinery sourced from reputable firms. Our services extend to contract-based logistics, supporting local infrastructure companies with efficient and timely operations. By combining manufacturing, trading, and logistics expertise, we deliver dependable, end-to-end solutions that add value to our clients’ businesses.
