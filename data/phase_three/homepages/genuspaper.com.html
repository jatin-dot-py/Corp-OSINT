About Us

Genus Paper and Boards Ltd is a division of the eminent Kailash Group of Companies. With a production capacity that continues to grow , our facilities are located at Moradabad and Muzzaffarnagar in Uttar Pradesh, India.
Equipped with five state of the art paper machines, our production units make premium quality packaging paper from recovered as well as virgin fiber through sustainable sources.

WELCOME TO GENUS PAPER & BOARDS LIMITED
