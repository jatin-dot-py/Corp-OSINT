At GVE, we are a trusted name in the Power sector, delivering large-scale solutions as a leading Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) contractor. Turnkey EPC Projects

End-to-end implementation of Extra High Voltage (EHV) substations, transmission lines, and underground power distribution systems.

Operations & Maintenance (O&M)

Utility Services

Specialized services for power plants and utilities including cable fault detection and rectification, transformer and switchgear replacement, and advanced retrofitting solutions.

GV Electricals is committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and timely solutions in the power sector that help clients optimise resources and reduce losses. With disciplined execution and a strong growth trajectory, we aim to create sustainable long-term value for our investors.

At GV Electricals Ltd, we take pride in being a trusted partner in India’s evolving power and infrastructure landscape. Our commitment to delivering reliable, efficient, and timely electrical solutions has enabled us to build long-standing relationships with leading utilities, industrial clients, and infrastructure developers. By focusing on operational excellence, quality execution, and safety, we consistently help our clients optimise resources, reduce technical losses, and enhance system reliability.

Over the years, our disciplined approach to project execution, strong technical capabilities, and experienced workforce have driven steady growth across transmission, distribution, and turnkey EPC projects. We continue to invest in modern equipment, skilled manpower, and innovative practices to meet the increasing complexity and scale of power sector requirements. Sustainability, safety, and compliance remain integral to our operations as we align our growth with responsible and environmentally conscious practices.

Looking ahead, we remain confident in the opportunities presented by India’s expanding power infrastructure, and grid modernisation initiatives. With a robust order pipeline, prudent financial management, and a clear long-term strategy, GV Electricals Ltd is well positioned to deliver consistent performance. Our objective is to create enduring value for our investors while contributing meaningfully to the nation’s power ecosystem.
