Decades of Excellence

Decades of Excellence

With a legacy spanning over 75 years, VTM combines heritage with modern manufacturing excellence. Established in 1946 by Karumuttu Thiagaraja Chettiar, the company has evolved into a forward-looking textile manufacturer, driven by precision, consistency, and a relentless focus on quality across every stage of production, principles that continue to define our fabrics today.

pillars of strength, quality and excellence

Inspiration Behind Our Logo

Built on Principles
That Never Compromise

As part of the illustrious Thiagarajar Group, our operations are guided by the enduring values of our founder and the principles of the Tamil home: warmth, integrity, and profound quality.

Uncompromising Quality

Quality has been central to our manufacturing philosophy for decades. It is built into every stage of production through advanced machinery, disciplined process control, and rigorous quality checks. This ensures fabrics that consistently meet global standards for performance, uniformity, and reliability, across every batch and every order.

Responsible & Sustainable Manufacturing

Sustainability is integral to how we operate, not an afterthought. More than 68% of our energy needs are fulfilled through renewable sources such as wind and solar. This is complemented by responsible resource utilisation, reduced environmental impact, and ethical practices across all our manufacturing facilities.

Compliance-Driven Excellence

Our manufacturing processes are governed by internationally recognised standards that ensure product integrity, safety, and transparency. With contamination- controlled environments and adherence to certifications including GOTS, Supima, and OEKO-TEX, we maintain uncompromising compliance across every stage of our supply chain.

Customer-First Commitment

We build partnerships, not transactions. Through clear communication, consistent quality, and dependable delivery timelines, we work closely with our customers as a trusted long-term manufacturing partner, supporting their growth, scale, and success.

Expertise in Premium Home Textiles

Expertise in Premium Home Textiles

VTM manufactures premium home textiles using cotton, linen, bamboo, TENCEL™, and Modal fibres, engineered for comfort, durability, and consistent performance at scale. Our home textiles division offers a comprehensive portfolio that includes bed linen, top-of-the-bed collections, utility bedding, table textiles, bath textiles, window textiles, loungewear, spa textiles, and hospitality textiles. Each product category is developed with rigorous attention to fibre behaviour, fabric construction, and finishing processes, ensuring uniform quality and reliability across large-volume production. We partner with B2B customers including wholesalers and global retailers who value long-term collaboration, manufacturing dependability, and the ability to scale with confidence, without compromising on quality, compliance, or delivery commitments.
