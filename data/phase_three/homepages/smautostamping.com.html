Welcome to SM Auto Stamping Limited

SM Auto Stamping Limited (SM), an IATF 16949:2016 certified group has been one of the leading manufacturers of precision sheet metal stamping and deep drawn components (welded assemblies & press tools) required for automobile & engineering sector since 1995. At SM we focus on finding out customer's needs and providing them with perfect solutions.

We specialize in design of complex sheet metal pressed components, design and manufacturing of high quality stamping dies, welding fixtures, high tonnage stamped components and welded assemblies for vehicles (LCV, SUV) and tractors. We have in-house tool design, development and manufacturing facility. We supply components to IATF 16949:2016 certified clients.

We have three production centers with current production capacity 6 million components per year in Nashik, Maharashtra which is around 160 km from Mumbai and 180 km from Pune.

    Quick facts

  • Established in 1995 and located approx. 175 kms from Mumbai and approx.185 kms from Pune
  • Manufacturers of precision sheet metal pressed components and welded assemblies for automotive and electrical (Class 'A', Class 'B', Class 'C' surface parts)
  • Workforce of 350 direct and indirect employees in 4 locations in Nashik and Sinnar, Maharashtra
