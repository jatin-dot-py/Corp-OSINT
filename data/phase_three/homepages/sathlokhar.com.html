Sathlokhar EPC construction companies specialize in the development of low cost housing, flats, commercial structures, and villas. Additionally, we provide complete remodeling and interior architectural services. Our goal is to serve our clients by offering flawless services and high quality buildings with all conveniences and care.

One of the most popular construction contracts is an EPC contract, particularly for large scale and complicated building projects in sectors like oil & gas, energy, and infrastructure. They have become the preferred construction contract for significant global development projects in project financing. An EPC contract serves as a tool for risk management and control. Construction risk is intentionally shifted to the contractor under EPC contracts. They hold the contractor responsible for all project operations, from the design to the building phases.

The EPC contractor or EPC business handles three major aspects of a project during this agreement:

  • Engineering and Design: This department is responsible for carrying out the facility's comprehensive engineering design.
  • Procurement: The process of procurement is where the EPC company buys all the supplies and machinery required to carry out the design.
  • Construct: This is when the contractors build the infrastructure and deliver it to the project manager or sponsors fit for use.
