NRB Industrial Bearings – Top Bearing Manufacturers in India

NRB Industrial Bearings Limited, headquartered in Fort, Mumbai, is a prominent manufacturer in India, specialising in a comprehensive range of ball and roller bearings designed primarily for the industrial sector in both domestic and international markets. Established in October 2012, NIBL has solidified its presence as a leading provider in the field.

NIBL's industrial bearings find application across a diverse spectrum of industries, including but not limited to machine tools, textiles, industrial gearboxes, cranes and conveyors, cement production, mining and minerals, fluid machinery (such as pumps, fans, and blowers), harvesters, metals, process industries (including fertiliser, sugar, paper, and pulp), bottling, canning, packaging, medical equipment (such as X-ray and imaging machines), industrial electrical components (including electric motors and circuit breakers), and industrial automation.
