Welcome To Divyashakti

Exotic granite in rare colour schemes and patterns compliment the architecture of space, blurring the lines between art and design. Divyashakti Ltd. has been at the forefront of bringing natural stones to life with technology and craft. Be it an edge-finished kitchen countertop or a glossy island kitchen or inviting floors and vanity tops in hotels and restaurants or stone clad facades and high-gloss spaces of high-rises, Divyashakti has become a trusted name to make environments look special – creating impressions that last a lifetime.

Sustainability

For us, building a better world is as important as creating robust businesses. We are committed to religiously follow the universal principles in the areas of human rights, labour, and environment.

  • Most of the production is supported by green solar energy.
  • All water used in the facilities is recycled and reused.
  • The company also provides free housing and education to all its employees.
