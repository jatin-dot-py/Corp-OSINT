Explicit Finance Ltd.(formerly known as Shubhyog Finlease Ltd.) is a registered Non-Banking Finance Company (NBFC). The company was incorporated in the year 1994 and had successfully come out with its maiden public offering in the year 1995. Explicit Finance Ltd. is mainly engaged into Investments, Trading in Securities and Finance activities. At present company's net worth is around Rs. 88.00 millions as on March 31, 2016. The Company's shares are listed on Bombay Stock Exchange. The company is managed by finance professionals, each having more than a decade of experience in their relevant field. The company is operating from Mumbai, Financial Capital of India.
