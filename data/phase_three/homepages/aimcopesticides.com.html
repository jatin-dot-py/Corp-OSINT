Aimco Pesticides Limited is an India-based, integrated agrochemical company specializing in the manufacturing Technical grade Pesticides, diverse range of formulations and agrochemical products. With a strong foothold in both domestic and international markets, the company is committed to enhancing farm yields and ensuring sustainable agricultural practices.

Agrochemical Innovation Pioneers

With over four decades of expertise, the company has led the introduction and commercialization of agrochemicals in India.

Key Agrochemical Manufacturer

First in India for Chlorpyrifos and Triclopyr, and second for Ethiprole and Imidacloprid after Bayer India.
