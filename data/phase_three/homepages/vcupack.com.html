MEDIA UNIT

The VCU Media Device is the world's most advanced portable low bandwidth 2G/3G/4G LTE/ HSPA/WIMAX/WIFI enabled digital live news gathering product. Designed with the same attention to detail as top-tier interior design Dubai projects, this device allows broadcasters to uplink their live as well as recorded high-definition video signals in a reliable, cost-effective manner. The VCU Media platform provides continuous capture and transmission of HD live broadcast-quality video with a portable mobile solution. It empowers broadcasters to cover live breaking news from locations that are costly or difficult to reach with traditional methods.
