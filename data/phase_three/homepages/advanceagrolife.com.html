ABOUT

ADVANCE AGROLIFE

Advance Agrolife was established more than two decades ago in the year 2002 by the Visionary late Mr. Hanuman Sahai Ji with the intent to be recognized as one of the leaders amongst global Agro-input companies which are accelerating the transfer of improved agriculture technology to the farming community to assist the farmers globally in order to maximize their productivity and profitability.

The company is a manufacturer and distributor of broad spectrum of technical and formulated grade of agrochemicals such as insecticides, fungicides, herbicides and plant growth regulators which are marketed by the company to its B2B clients under the brands that are in-licensed by the company, owned by company and through other generic brands.

Advance Agro Life

Established in 2002 by the visionary late Mr. Hanuman Sahai Ji, Advance Agrolife aims to be a global leader among agro-input companies, accelerating the transfer of advanced agricultural technology to enhance farmers' productivity and profitability worldwide.

The company manufactures and distributes a broad spectrum of agrochemicals, including insecticides, fungicides, herbicides, and plant growth regulators, marketed under in-licensed brands, proprietary brands, and generic labels to B2B clients.

Our mission is to promote sustainable agricultural practices and protect the environment by providing economical and effective agrochemicals accessible to farmers globally.

ISO 9001:2015 – Quality Management System for manufacturing and supply of pesticides, herbicides, fungicides, and micronutrients.
ISO 14001:2015 – Environmental Management System certification for manufacturing and supply of pesticides, herbicides, fungicides, and micronutrients.

Advance Agro Life is built on trust—trust from our global customers and talented workforce. Since our inception, we have adhered to the highest ethical standards to deliver sustainable value to all stakeholders.
