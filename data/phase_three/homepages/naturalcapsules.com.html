Capsule Manufacturers in India – Natural Capsules Limited (NCL)

Natural Capsules Limited (NCL) is a leading capsule manufacturer in India, supplying high-quality empty capsule shells to pharmaceutical, herbal and nutraceutical industries worldwide.

Established in 1993 in Bangalore, India and expanded to Pondicherry in 2003, NCL operates modern WHO-GMP certified manufacturing facilities equipped with advanced capsule production technology and strict quality control systems.

As one of the trusted capsule manufacturers from India, we specialize in manufacturing:

 • Hard Gelatin Capsule Shells

 • Vegetarian HPMC Capsules (Vegicaps)

 • Empty Gelatin Capsules

 • Empty Cellulose Capsule Shells

 • Fast Release Capsules

 • Delayed Release & Enteric Capsules

 • Organic Capsule Shells

 • Printed Capsule Shells

We are also recognized among reliable capsule exporters from India, serving regulated and global markets.

Our Focus – Quality, Safety & Innovation

At NCL, quality is at the core of every capsule we manufacture.

As a WHO-GMP certified capsule manufacturer in India, we follow strict international standards to ensure safety, hygiene, and performance.

Our Quality Practices Include:

 • WHO-GMP certified manufacturing facilities

 • ISO 9001 quality management systems

 • Automated and controlled capsule production

 • Cleanroom manufacturing environments

 • Advanced microbiological and quality testing

 • Skilled technical and R&D teams

 • Strict hygiene and contamination control

These processes make us a reliable partner for pharmaceutical capsule suppliers and nutraceutical capsule manufacturers.

Natural Capsules Limited operates advanced capsule production units in India with:

 • High-capacity capsule manufacturing lines

 • Multiple capsule size options (00 to 4)

 • Custom color combinations and printing options

 • Specialized capsule variants (SLS-free, Halal-certified, BSE/TSE-free)

 • Export-ready compliance systems

With decades of expertise, NCL continues to innovate in gelatin and cellulose capsule manufacturing.
