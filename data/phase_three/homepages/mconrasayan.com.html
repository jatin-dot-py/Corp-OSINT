FAQ's

What types of construction chemicals do you manufacture?

+

As a reputed and widely trusted construction chemicals company in India, we manufacture a vast range of products, including waterproofing chemicals, concrete admixtures, ready-to-use tile adhesives, and underfloor finishing and undersurface treatment chemicals.

Do you provide technical support for your products?

+

Yes, we do provide technical support on how to use and store our products. If you have any questions or concerns about our products, get in touch with our team today.

Are your tile adhesives compatible with all types of tiles?

+

Yes, our tile adhesives like MCON NSA grey and white gold offer consistent quality, excellent adhesion, and slip resistance, making them ideal for all types of tiles.

Do you offer customised solutions for specific construction challenges?

+

If you are looking for a construction chemical supplier that offers products tailored to your needs, MCON Rasayan is the right place for you. We offer construction solutions that are tailored to your unique business needs.
