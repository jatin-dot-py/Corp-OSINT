About Us

Three Decade of
Precision & Elegance.

Founded in 1986, Restile Ceramics Ltd. stands among India’s original creators of fullbody vitrified tiles. Our legacy is built on specialisation and consistency staying committed to a product category long before it became an industry standard. For decades, architects and professionals have relied on Restile for surfaces that retain their character through years of use, offering high density, low porosity and enduring beauty.
