These fraudsters are promoting fake “AI-based Investment” schemes by Prime Securities to mislead and defraud investors.

Please be aware:

  • Prime Securities DOES NOT solicit investments through any Mobile Apps, WhatsApp messages, Telegram groups, or phone calls.
  • We have no association with the individual mentioned above or any “AI for better returns” schemes promoted via these channels.
  • Do not share your personal data, bank details, or transfer funds to these unauthorized entities.

 We urge you to observe utmost caution, conduct due diligence, and verify the registration status of any entity claiming to be a registered intermediary of Prime Securities
