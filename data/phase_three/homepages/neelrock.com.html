NEELKANTH ROCKMINERALS LIMITED [NRL] is the largest manufacturer of Granite Stones from INDIA. NRL is integrated Granite quarrying & processing unit. Since our inception, NRL has been a forerunner in the Natural Stone Industry.

For more than a decade the NRL name remained synonymous with a steadfast commitment to quality and service. It is this commitment, combined with adaptability in the marketplace that NRL Granite credits for its longevity, growth and prosperity.

NRL has always stood out for its progressive achievements in stone markets all over the Globe, its benchmark being constant activity towards efficiency, Quality & punctuality on the delivery of materials, continuous updating of its processing technology and its constant search for new products.

The process house is situated at JODHPUR [Rajasthan], 100 K. M. distances from Captive Granite quarries. NRL is a Public Limited Company listed in Bombay Stock Exchange(BSE), India. We are Life Member of "ALL INDIA GRANITE & STONE ASSOCIATION".

NRL is managed by well-known "VIJAY LAXMI GROUP", a pioneer in the India Textile Market and was AWARDED BY THE PRESIDENT OF INDIA for its excellence.

Our product range includes Polished, Flamed, and Cut to Size and Random Granite Slabs of Gang saw and vertical sizes of different thickness levels. Our substantial inventory of Granite Blocks and in-house production capacities allows us to execute quantum orders at any given time. Team work, professional & innovative approach enable us to satisfy our valued customers all over the world.

The time less beauty, appreciating value and affordability of NRL Granite products make them premier choice for construction and landscaping.
