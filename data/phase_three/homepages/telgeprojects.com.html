Who we are|||who we are

Telge Projects is an integrated BIM company deeply connected with the AEC industry. We believe in working smarter by leveraging data-driven insights and cutting-edge technology. Our expertise lies in identifying where and how BIM can create the most impact—what we call “Connected Construction.” Headquartered in India, our diverse team of civil engineers combines creativity, technical skill, and innovation to redefine construction practices. Through global experience, rigorous R&D, and a passion for progress, we continue to shape the future of the built environment.
