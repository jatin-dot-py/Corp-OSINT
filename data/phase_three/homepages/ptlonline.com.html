Prime urban is a BSE Listed Company. (Formerly Known as Prime Textile India Limited ) incorporated in the year 1936. The Company has varied portfolio, with its main focus in Real Estate Development and Export of Yarn

Prime Urban has been helping people realize their Aspiration for lavish living, by building luxury high-end residential communities , with facilities that even the affluent could earlier only dream about Prime urban also build commercial spaces like malls, shopping and office complex, independent villa

The Company has successfully completed its two Reality Projects of Residential sector in the city of Tirpur, Tamilnadu, Further two projects are under progress under SPVs with residential cum commercial complex projects at Coimbtore and the land Development project at Kotagiri
