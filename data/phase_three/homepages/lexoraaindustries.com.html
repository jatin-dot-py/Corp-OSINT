About Us

Lexoraa Industries Limited, originally established as Servotech Engineering Industries Ltd. (SEIL) and later known as Servoteach Industries Limited, has been a cornerstone in the industry since its inception on October 7, 1994. With the receipt of our Certificate of Commencement of Business on November 21, 1994, we embarked on a journey of innovation, quality, and excellence.

Since 1994

Servotech Engineering Industries Ltd. (SEIL) was incorporated on 7th October, and received certificate of Commencement of Business on 21st November 1994.

Past 2017

Ministry of Corporate Affairs w.e.f. 04/11/2017 issued a fresh Certificate of Incorporation providing their consent to change of name of the Company from Servotech Engineering Industries Limited to Servoteach Industries Limited.

Present 2024

Ministry of Corporate Affairs w.e.f. 12/06/2024 issued a fresh Certificate of Incorporation providing their consent to change of name of the Company from Servoteach Industries Limited to Lexoraa Industries Limited.
