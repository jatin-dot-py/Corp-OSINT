About Us

SWELECT Energy Systems Ltd. (formerly known as Numeric Power Systems Ltd.) is a leading renewable energy player and a trusted partner in clean energy. With 41+ years of expertise in Clean power/energy and 1 GW+ of solar experience, we deliver Solar, Wind, ESS, EPC, O&M and IPP solutions for Corporates, Industries and home segments.

Guided by our promise - Responsible, Innovative, Sustainable Energy solutions - SWELECT powers progress today, building a greener, stronger, tomorrow. Powering the World responsibly is just not a tagline; it's our mission.

State-of-the-Art Solar PV Module Manufacturing at SHPV

SWELECT HHV Solar Photovoltaics Pvt. Ltd. (SHPV), a fully owned subsidiary of SWELECT ENERGY SYSTEMS LIMITED, operates a cutting-edge 1 GW+ manufacturing facility in Coimbatore. This automated facility ensures top-tier production quality and efficiency. SHPV modules meet global standards such as IEC, UL, MCS, CEC, and BIS, and are listed in the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM) by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE). This facility produces both Mono PERC and TOPCON modules from 365 Wp to 630 Wp for both mono facial and bifacial applications.

Read more

MMS and Electrical BoS Manufacturing facility

SWELECT offers a complete range of Electrical and Mechanical Balance of Systems (BOS), including Module Mounting Structures (MMS), AJBs, ACDBs, and DCDBs, from its state-of-the-art facility in Salem. The plant's roll forming machines have an annual production capacity of 750 MW+ of MMS, ensuring a lifespan of over 20 years. Additionally, this facility also manufacturers Power Products including Servo Stabilizers, Isolation Transformers, Electrical Switch boards and Distribution boards.

Solar Water Pumps
