Since 1987

For nearly four decades, Carysil has been redefining the modern kitchen.

What began in 1987 as Asia’s first quartz sink manufacturer is now a complete kitchen brand. Sinks, faucets and built-in appliances, engineered in partnership with Germany and trusted in homes across 50+ countries. Every piece is held to the same brief: quiet where it should be quiet, powerful where it matters, and finished to a standard the rest of the kitchen has to live up to.

Why Carysil

Four decades in, three things haven’t changed.

German Engineering

Built on a culture of innovation

It started in 1987 with a German technology partnership and a new idea: a sink cast from quartz composite rather than pressed from steel. That material science still sets the benchmark for surface strength, heat resistance and colour depth, and the same discipline now runs through every appliance we build.

Craftsmanship

Made by hand, measured by machine

Every sink is cast, cured and finished in our own plants, then checked against tolerances measured in fractions of a millimetre. It is why a Carysil surface still looks right ten years in.

Sustainability

Responsible by design

The most sustainable kitchen is the one you never have to replace. We engineer for decades of use, and manufacture with the same care.

  • Sustainable manufacturing processes
  • Water-saving faucet technologies
  • Eco-friendly composite materials
  • Long-lasting engineering, less replacement

Trusted Worldwide

A Legacy Built One Kitchen at a Time.

Since 1987, Carysil has been creating the details that shape modern kitchens. From pioneering Asia’s first quartz sink to bringing surfaces and appliances together as one complete system, its approach to design now lives in kitchens around the world.
