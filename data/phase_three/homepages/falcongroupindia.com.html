All Project Solutions Under One Roof

Falcon Group’s companies (FGC) is a Mumbai based that specializes in MEP ( Mechanical, Electrical, Fire fighting & Plumbing ), Fire Alarm System, Networking System, Security & Surveillance System , CCTV & Access Control System, Intercom system, Solar Rooftop PV module Installation , Solar Water Heater , Gate Automation System ,Civil & Construction Work , Building painting, Sliding Window, Grill , Railing & Fabrication Work, operating since the year of 2010.

From the very beginning we adopted a unique approach towards our clients, catering to their specific needs and delivering upon their expectations. Behind the name of FGC stands an experienced and professional team that works together to deliver and maintain the highest standards of MEP & security services. Our highly qualified and technically competent managers, engineers and technicians, allow us to capture a sizable share of the market.
