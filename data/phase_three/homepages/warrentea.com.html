Certain endeavours - like fragnance, like music, and tea - take time. When James Warren decided to create a tea estate in 1850, inspiration set its own timetable. Warren, and thereafter, his family, wanted teas that evoked beautiful emotions in people, all over the world.

Today the 160 year Warren Legacy presents a history so rich in experience and achievement that it has changed the very course of Indian tea producers' art. Generations of dedicated and gifted tea people, working with the most popular of nature's herves, have transformed Warren from a mere tea company to a house of rare quality. Warren Tea presents to you a "Warranty" a Mark of Quality, which like Warren itself, is as unique as India and the world.
