WELCOME TO

ELECON ENGINEERING - India's largest Industrial Gearbox & Material Handling Equipment Manufacturer

Welcome to Elecon Engineering, a company with a legacy that dates back to 1951, founded by the visionary Late Shri Ishwarbhai B. Patel. What started as a small venture manufacturing ELEvator & CONveyor systems for the mining sector has grown into a trusted name in material handling solutions India.

Over the seven decades, Elecon has expanded its reach far beyond its origins. Today, it is recognized as one of Asia’s leading industrial Power Transmission Solution providers and manufacturers, proudly serving customers not only in India but across globe Southeast Asia, Middle East, North America, UK, Europe, Nordic and Africa region.

Elecon offers a wide range of power transmission solutions designed to meet the needs of various industries. These include helical and bevel helical gearboxes, worm gearboxes, couplings, planetary gearboxes, high-speed gearboxes, and Custom built gearbox solutions. With a strong focus on modular design and advanced engineering, Elecon ensures reliability, efficiency and performance in every product.

In addition to power transmission, Elecon is known for its innovative material handling equipment. Its product portfolio includes stacker reclaimers, conveyors, crushers, feeders and wagon tipplers —each built to handle demanding industrial applications. The company also brings expertise in industrial castings, further strengthening its capabilities.

Driven by a commitment to quality, continuous innovation and customer satisfaction, Elecon continues to raise the bar in engineering excellence. It’s a place where experience meets forward-thinking solutions, delivering value across core sectors worldwide.
