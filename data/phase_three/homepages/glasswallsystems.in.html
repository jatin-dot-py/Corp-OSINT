About Us

“We create future architecture marvels by building them today.”
Glass Wall Systems (India) Limited leading facade fabricator. We provide turnkey architectural facade solutions where we translate the vision of our clients into reality. Our Design, Manufacturing and Project Management facilities are amongst the best in the country.

We are headquartered in Mumbai, with a strong presence in Bangalore, Delhi, NCR, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad and Pune making us a true Pan India company. We have also expanded our operations and expertise to USA, Australia, Qatar, Sri Lanka, where we offer a full range of glazing systems and custom-designed architectural metal works.
