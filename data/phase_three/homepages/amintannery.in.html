Welcome to Amin Tannery

Amidst the chaotic and turbulent time of the partition of the Indian subcontinent into two independent nations, India and Pakistan, It was 9th April, 1946 which marked the dawn of a new era in the history of Indian leather industry, it was the day when a person, meagre in resources but high in ambition established a small tannery in the north city of Kanpur, With his visionary Skills and farsightedness ,this austere person understood the winds of change and opted for a new venture, i.e. from family business of Trading to Manufacturing.
