The Company

Built on better seed.

Mangalam Seeds Limited was incorporated in 2011 by Shri M.J. Patel, a farmer's son who spent decades learning what farmers actually need. Today, the breeding programme is guided by Dr. I.D. Patel — an agricultural scientist who has developed 46 improved and hybrid varieties across grain, oil, pulse, spice, fibre and vegetable crops.

We are a SEBI-listed, ISO 9001 certified company, with research farms in Unjha, Gandhinagar and Palanpur, and a dealer network spanning Gujarat, Rajasthan and Andhra Pradesh.

The Science

46 varieties. One lifetime of breeding.

Dr. I.D. Patel, our technical advisor, has dedicated 42 years to plant breeding and genetics — transferring non-shattering genes into psyllium, blight resistance into fennel, six-locule genes into sesamum. 41 research papers. Six national awards, including the Sardar Patel Award.
