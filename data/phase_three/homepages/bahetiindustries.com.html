Welcome to Baheti Recycling Industries

Baheti Recycling Industries Limited (BRIL) was incorporated in the year 1994 to expand and consolidate the group’s business activities.

Exponential expansion was undertaken with a new production facility at Dehgam District of Gujarat for recycling Aluminium and producing Ferro alloys to become an incomparable industry player.

Baheti (BRIL) is fully geared to meet the emerging global requirements of varied industries with premium quality alloys manufactured at one single location using innovative techniques.
