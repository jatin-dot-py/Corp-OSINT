Prime Cable Industries Limited

Three Decades of Innovation. Certified Excellence. Scaled Production.

Prime Cable Industries Limited has spent over 30 years anchoring the wire and cable industry. As an ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and OHSAS/ISO 45001:2018 certified organization, we operate a massive 170,000 sq. ft. manufacturing footprint spanning Delhi and Rajasthan. Our engineered products are proudly brought to market under our trusted premium brands: ‘PRIMECAB’ and ‘RENUFO’
