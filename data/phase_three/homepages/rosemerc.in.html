    ABOUT ROSE MERC LTD

    Rose Merc Limited is a dynamic group with a profound understanding of the Indian consumer across various sectors. With years of experience, a robust growth generation capability, and a team of highly qualified and experienced board members, Rose Merc has successfully constructed a diversified business portfolio spanning a wide range of B2C sectors.

     

    Our diverse portfolio includes event management services, Spiritual Radio Broadcast, Sports Management, Customer B2C Product and Financial Consulting Services, all operating under one roof. Rose Merc aims to create a universe of profitable business ventures, with each sector contributing to its overall success.

     

    As a group, Rose Merc Limited takes pride in its profitable business ventures across different domains, constantly striving to expand and innovate to provide top-notch services to its customers and partners. We are dedicated to delivering excellence and creating a bright future for our diverse portfolio of businesses.
