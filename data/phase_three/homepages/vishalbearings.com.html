Manufacturer & Exporter of Bearing Rollers Rollers From The Size of 5MM To 150MM Diameter TAPPER ROLLERS CYLINDRICAL ROLLERS SPHERICAL ROLLERS

We are manufacturer of bearing rollers based at Rajkot (Gujarat), India. We manufacture taper rollers, cylindrical rollers, spherical rollers and supply it to reputed bearing manufacturer and OEM across India. We are also exporting our bearing rollers and our quality is accepted by overseas buyers.

Manufacturing of taper, cylindrical and spherical bearing rollers was started by Shri Gordhanbhai Changela in the year 1991. At VBL we have latest / sufficient infrastructure, resources and experience to handle all type of customer requirement with assurance of quality bearing rollers.

Entire process of manufacturing bearing rollers is done in-house which ensures quality product and timely delivery. We are certified as ISO 9001:2008 & ISO/TS 16949:2002.
