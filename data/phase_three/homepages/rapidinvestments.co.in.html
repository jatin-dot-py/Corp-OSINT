About Us

Rapid Investments Limited was incorporated on 02.06.1978 as a Limited Company under the Companies Act, 1956. The Company is also registered as a Non-Banking Finance Company (Non-Deposit taking- Asset Financing Company) under Reserve Bank of India vide registration No.13.00783 on 25 May 1998. It is a BSE Listed Publicly Traded Company. Read More

Loan Portfolio and Terms

Rapid Investments specializes in providing loans for home construction, extension, and MSME finance against the borrower’s primary residence. Loan amounts range from 2 to 10 lakhs, with flexible tenures of 3 to 7 years, all capped at 40% of the collateral’s market value. The company offers funding with an average interest rate of 24%.

Robust Operations and Talent

Rapid Investments boasts a strong operational foundation, prioritizing iron-clad legal, technical, and financial processes. It prides itself on hiring industry-leading talent, ensuring the highest standards of service and reliability.

Local Expertise and Market Focus

Rapid Investments excels in local market expertise, particularly in rural and semi-urban areas of Rajasthan. The company’s well-trained staff is adept at understanding and adapting to the local market dynamics. It maintains close connections with customers through personalized interactions with marketing and customer support teams.

Socially Inclusive Approach and Compliance

The company focuses on serving customers engaged in agriculture, small businesses, and transportation, fostering social inclusion. It extends loans based on future earning capacity, especially for those at the lower end of the income pyramid. Rapid Investments places a primary emphasis on self-employed customers. It diligently adheres to KYC norms and follows prudential norms as mandated by the RBI. Ethical business conduct is a top priority, with strict adherence to a comprehensive code of conduct.
