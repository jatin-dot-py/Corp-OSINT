About Flair World

"FLAIR" - introduced in 1976, commenced its success story 4 decades back with a range of metal pens under the guidance of Mr Khubilal Jugraj Rathod.

Over the years, FLAIR has expanded its business into multiple territories with the efforts of Mr Vimalchand Jugraj Rathod, the Managing Director, who was previously the chairman of PLEXCONCIL and currently the chairman of the Pen and Stationery Association of India".

Maintaining FLAIR's pillar-stones - 'Commitment to Quality & Innovation'. Our products were sold by us and our distributors in 97 countries as of March 31, 2023".

Corporate Social Responsibility

Flair Writing Industries Limited CSR objectives are based on the embedded tenets of trust, fairness and care.

The CSR initiatives undertaken by the Company so far have been in the areas of education and basic healthcare.

Work With Flair World

Flair World is a people oriented organization. We believe that our employees are the most important element in the success of our company. We are an equal opportunity employer and offer a merit and achievement led career growth path.
