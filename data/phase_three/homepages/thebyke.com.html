Our Story

Our Belief: An Essence of India

At the heart of The Byke Group of Hotels and Resorts lies a belief we call An Essence of India. It is more than a philosophy - it is our way of celebrating the timeless traditions, flavors, and warmth that define this country. As the first chain of veg hotels in India, we honor India's cultural richness by offering guests a stay rooted in authenticity and care. Guided by the principle of Atithi Devo Bhavah (the guest is God), we ensure every traveler at our hotel in India feels welcomed, valued, and at home. Our top hotels in India are designed to provide comfort and quality at budget-friendly prices, making refined hospitality accessible to all.
