About Us

Welcome to Aastha Spintex Limited
Your Trusted Partner in Textile Excellence!

Aastha Spintex Pvt. Ltd. is set to herald a new revolution in cotton spin yarn with exceptional quality and production capabilities. The sprawling unit located in Saurashtra has been inaugurated by our Hon'ble Chief Minister Mrs. Anandi Ben Patel in a grand ceremony held on 5th April 2015 at the factory premises.

At Aastha Spintex, we weave dreams into reality. Established with a vision to redefine the textile industry, we embarked on a journey of innovation, sustainability, and uncompromising quality. Since our inception, we have been committed to pushing boundaries and setting new standards in the world of textiles.

Our mission is to be a global leader in textile manufacturing, recognized for our commitment to excellence, sustainability, and customer satisfaction. We aim to deliver premium products that not only meet but exceed the expectations of our clients, while contributing positively to the environment and communities we serve.

Spinning the Future: Powering Progress with Solar and Wind Energy

The driver behind the sustainability revolution in Aastha Spintax is the utilization of solar and wind energy. we have embraced cutting-edge technologies, such as solar panels and wind turbines, to harness the abundant and renewable power of the sun and wind. By strategically integrating these sources into their operations, we are achieving a 100% clean energy profile. Solar and wind energy are not only sustainable but also cost-effective. we are experiencing substantial savings in operational costs, as they are no longer subject to the fluctuating prices associated with traditional energy sources.

Women Empowerment: Unleashing the Strength of Women

Welcome to Aastha Spintex, where innovation meets inclusivity. Step inside a workplace revolution that champions diversity and empowerment. With a commitment to progress, we proudly boast a 70% female staff, redefining the narrative in the textile industry. This is more than just a workplace; it's a community of women breaking barriers and spinning success stories.

Vertical Brilliance: Redefining Success with a Comprehensive Vertical Setup

At Aastha Spintex, our vertical unit set-up represents a holistic approach to textile manufacturing. From raw material procurement to the finished product, every stage is seamlessly integrated under one roof. This not only streamlines our operations but also enhances our ability to respond dynamically to market demands.

Our vertical unit set-up isn't just about efficiency; it's a cornerstone of our sustainability initiatives. By controlling the entire production chain, we can implement eco-friendly practices, minimize waste, and reduce our carbon footprint. We constantly invest in cutting-edge technologies and sustainable solutions to ensure a brighter, greener future for the textile industry.
