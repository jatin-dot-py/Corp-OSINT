About the Group

WPIL Limited is a global leader in flow solutions, with diversified operations spanning the full spectrum of the pumping industry. Backed by over 70 years of expertise and a strong roster of marquee clients, we continue to build on solid fundamentals that drive sustained global growth.

As we reinforce our leadership in India through specialized manufacturing, turnkey solutions, and O&M services, we are also expanding our international footprint through strategic acquisitions and joint ventures — strengthening our position as a trusted partner worldwide.

Products

Since its inception in 1952, WPIL has upheld a strong legacy of engineering excellence, delivering specialized pumps across diverse applications. Originating as Johnston Pumps India, the company has built unmatched competency, with industry leading technologies, and implemented it across the entire product portfolio.
Decades of expertise, strengthened by our global subsidiaries, reaffirm WPIL’s leadership in reliable, high-performance pumping solutions.
READ MORE

Turnkey Projects

To address India’s growing demand for reliable turnkey contractors in the water sector, WPIL established its Project Division in 2000. Focused on municipal, irrigation, and industrial projects, the division delivers comprehensive end-to-end solutions backed by deep technical expertise. Over the years, it has built strong competencies and established a leading market presence.
Today, through our subsidiary companies, we also extend turnkey water and wastewater solutions to clients across Europe and Africa.
READ MORE

International

At WPIL, we follow a proven buy-and-build strategy to strengthen our presence in core markets worldwide. Through extensive diligence, we have successfully acquired nine companies across Italy, South Africa, and Australia, expanding our portfolio with new flow control products and marquee clients.
These strategic acquisitions, combined with the strength of our advanced Indian manufacturing base, have firmly positioned WPIL as a leading global player in the pump and flow solutions industry.
