About our Company

Welcome to
Gulf Lloyds (INDIA) Limited

Gulf Lloyds is a world leader in Inspection, Verification, Auditing, Testing, Training and Certification services. Created in 2011, as an Independent Conformity assessment body with its main objective to safeguard life, Property and Environment through Quality Assurance and total Quality Management.
We partner with you to offer independent quality services that will help you reduce risk, streamline your processes and operate in a more sustainable manner.
Gulf Lloyds (GL) has been providing their value-added Third-Party Inspection and Certification Services to the Government, Public and Private Sector Organizations involved in Onshore, Offshore & Marine activities. It is a professional organization backed by devoted, highly qualified and experienced personnel.
