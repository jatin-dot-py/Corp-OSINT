Welcome to Lesha Industries

The company primarily is into business of trading and manufacturing of various steels products and in the business of Information Technology & Allied Services. The company also operates in the capital market in shares & stocks.

Steel Division:
The company is currently doing business of trading of various steel products. The company’s steel plant located at GIDC Kerala Estate, Near Anmedabad is under implementation. The main products that the company shall manufacture shall be M.S. Ignots, M.S. Billets, S.S. Billets and Rolled products like rounds, bars, sections etc.

Information Technology Division:
The company was established with aim to provide cutting edge technological services & solutions in heterogeneous networking segment. The company has expertise in designing, setting-up, managing and enabling infrastructure for e-governance with most cost-effective, most reliable network. The company is a provider of internet & allied services in Western India, based in the State of Gujarat. The company is managed by a team of professionals having more than 12 years of IT experience & having expertise in offering solutions for Internet Access, Web Solutions, Networking & Communication, Data Entry operations and other value added services, distribution of Computer Hardware & Peripherals, Database Management on various platform by latest technologies.

Over and above the main businesses mentioned above, the company also operates in trading of various toy items and also operates in shares and stocks in the capital market.
