Explore Our Diverse Business Segments

Marketing and Distribution

  • Marketing and distribution of steel products

Manufacturing of pre-engineered buildings (PEB)

  • Manufacturing of pre-engineered steel sheds/units that are suited for deve loping warehousing facilities and factory shed

Manufacturing of steel girders for railways

  • Setting up fabrication unit and manufacturing of steel girders approved by the Research Designs & Standards Organisation (RDSO) for composite and other steel plate girder

In addition to the business segments mentioned, the company also manufactures and trade in polyvinyl chloride (PVC) pipes and coloured sheets on a small scale.
