Terms & Conditions

  • Age should be 15 years and above in order to play the game.
  • All team must have at least 4 members and not more than 8 members.
  • Registration will be from 10:45 am to 12:00 pm near  Main Retail Store – Souvenir Shop
  • Teams must stay together during the Treasure Hunt and are encouraged to work together to solve the clues from each location.
  • The teams will be responsible to decipher the clues and safely acquire the clues from each of the locations.
  • Each clue once solved will lead you to a location where you will be able to collect your next clue.
  • Whether or not all clues are solved, teams must return to Robertos Restaurant Area by 05:00 pm to qualify for exciting prizes.
  • Disqualification will result from any of the following:-
    • Damaging any property.
    • Interfering with other teams or their members in any way.
    • Copying from other teams.
    • Stealing from other teams.
  • If there’s a tie, whoever came first as per the time log will be chosen.
  • 3 Winners will be qualified to win exciting prizes.
  • In order to get your next clue from the location, every group have to show the previous clue in original. (No photo or copy will be allowed).
  • In order to claim the prize, all clues must submit in original. No duplicate copy will be provided if any clue lost or damaged.
  • All group members will be responsible for their own personal belongings while playing the game.
  • Kindly take care of own health and safety while playing the game.
  • Management reserves the right to amend the rules due to any unforeseen circumstances rising during the game.
