Our introduction

Taste the Freshness,Feel the Difference

Stanbik Agro Limited started in the year of 2021 with a mission of bringing fresh fruits and vegetables easily from farm to table as well as supporting sustainable farming practices. People consider us today as the most reliable fruits and vegetables supplier in India for our long-term experience and expertise, and core values of freshness and quality. At Stanbik, we are a team of professionals dedicated to cherishing a healthy food culture for everyone. Our director Mr. Ashok Bhai Prajapati MD and CFO with our entire team, are making continuous efforts to make our supply chain management of fruits and vegetables more efficient and seamless.

About Info

Stanbik Agro Limited carries on the business of manufacturing, wholesaling and supplying of agricultural commodities. Our Company was founded with a clear mission: to bring the fresh fruits and vegetables directly from the farm to the table while promoting sustainable farming practices. Our Company has earned a reputation as the reliable supplier of these products. Our extensive experience, deep expertise, and unwavering commitment to our core values of freshness and quality has led to consistency & customer satisfaction.
