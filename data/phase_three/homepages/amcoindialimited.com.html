:: WHO WE ARE

About Us

Amco India is a trusted name in foil manufacturing, delivering high-quality aluminum foil solutions that meet the evolving needs of diverse industries. With a strong focus on precision, consistency, and innovation, Amco India manufactures foils that offer superior barrier protection, excellent formability, and reliable performance.
Our advanced manufacturing facilities are equipped with modern technology to ensure strict quality control at every stage of production. Amco India’s foils are widely used in packaging, pharmaceuticals, food & beverage, insulation, and industrial applications, providing protection against moisture, light, oxygen, and contaminants.
Driven by a commitment to sustainability and customer satisfaction, Amco India emphasizes efficient processes, material optimization, and compliance with international standards. By combining technical expertise with customer-centric service, Amco India delivers foil products that enhance product safety, shelf life, and brand value.
