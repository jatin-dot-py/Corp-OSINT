About Studio LSD

Innovative storytelling rooted in creativity and excellence!

Studio LSD is a vibrant Mumbai-based multimedia production house specializing in original storytelling for television and OTT platforms. With a pan-India presence, we collaborate with talented individuals to create captivating and genre-defining content.

Our journey began with a mission to push the boundaries of storytelling, partnering with visionary creatives to develop shows that showcase our craft. With years of industry expertise and a commitment to excellence, we've established ourselves as a go-to production house for premium content creation.

Mission

Create high-quality, engaging content for television and OTT platforms, captivating audiences with compelling storytelling and innovation.

Approach

Collaborate closely with clients to understand audience needs, delivering exceptional work on time and within budget.

Experience

With over 7 years, we partner with top broadcasters like Sony, Zee TV, and Colors TV to deliver standout programming.

Why Us

Choose Studio LSD for creativity, reliability, and outstanding multimedia production.

Studio LSD delivers innovative, original content that resonates with diverse audiences. Our expert team ensures every project is crafted with creativity and precision.

We build long-term partnerships with top broadcasters and OTT platforms, guaranteeing quality and reliability. Our bold decisions set us apart in the multimedia industry.

Founders

Prateek Sharma

Managing Director, Founder

The founder of Studio LSD, Prateek Sharma, brings a wealth of experience from a distinguished career in the television industry. With a notable tenure at Star India, he helmed several popular shows. He also served as the showrunner for the highly acclaimed series "Beyhadh" on Sony TV, producing the sequel for . Prateek's expertise and visionary leadership have been instrumental in establishing Studio LSD as a leading creator of premium, captivating content across both television and OTT platforms.

Parth Shah

Whole-time Director, Co-Founder

The Co-Founder at Studio LSD, Parth Shah brings over more then a decade of industry experience with a strong foundation in finance, production and music business. Formerly serving as a Business Head at a prominent post-production studio, Parth possesses deep understanding of the financial dynamics of the industry. His strategic insights have been instrumental in driving company's growth and success. Parth's effective management continue to garner widespread respect in the industry, aligning with our commitment to diversify and delivering high-quality, engaging content across platforms.

Rekha Vaid

Business Head

Renowned for her contributions as a seasoned producer and creative force behind several successful shows and films. With an experience of more than 25 years in media and entertainment, Rekha has been instrumental in shaping compelling narratives and fostering ground breaking content for various consumers. Her visionary leadership has not only elevated the standards of storytelling but also propelled her to prominence as a key influencer in programming.

Chakir Hussain

CEO, The LSD Music

A passionate music entrepreneur, curator, and sound enthusiast, Chakir brings years of experience in the music industry — from engineering chart-topping tracks to building artist-first platforms. At The LSD Music, he leads the label’s artistic direction, forging new sounds and discovering the voices of tomorrow.
