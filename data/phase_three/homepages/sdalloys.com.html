ABOUT OUR UNIT

Our astounding production unit (Yard) is well equipped with cutting edge technology and modern machinery for high quality products.

SagarDeep Alloys Ltd. is the umbrella brand of Sagar Metals, established in 1972 and promoted by Mr. A. S. Mehta. With over 35 years of experience in the Indian market, SagarDeep Alloys Ltd. is the most trusted and reputed suppliers of alloys consumables, Copper pipes and tubes, Copper flats, Copper coils, Copper rods, Copper anodes, Copper nuggets and Copper plates, Stainless Steel sheets / coils, rods, pipes and tubes, Seamless pipes, Sheets, as well as Copper & Brass scrap in India and all over the world.
