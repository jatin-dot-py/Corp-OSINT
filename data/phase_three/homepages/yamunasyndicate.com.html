ABOUT

The Yamuna Syndicate Limited

The Company is engaged in trading & marketing of a wide variety of products. The Company commenced its business in the year 1955. The Registered Office of the Company is at Radaur Road, opp. Saraswati Sugar Mill, Yamunanagar-135 001 (Haryana).

The Company has long experience in trading of a wide range of products, components, and consumables related to the auto sector; agro-chemicals; as well as industrial and consumer electricals.

The Company started trading of tractors with dealership from prestigious companies in the year 1957, with branches in Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, Haryana, and UP.

The Company’s Retail Outlet, licensed by M/s. Hindustan Petroleum Ltd has been in existence in Yamunanagar since 1961.

Since 2001, The Yamuna Syndicate Ltd is a major Franchisee of M/s. Amara Raja Energy & Mobility Ltd. engaged in distribution of the reputed AMARON range of automotive batteries.

In the past, the Company had also been involved in dealership business of Yamaha Motorcycles, passenger cars of Hindustan Motors, commercial vehicles of Ashok Leyland; as well as spares parts of Maruti Suzuki.

The Yamuna Syndicate is backed by a strong Associate Company M/s. Isgec Heavy Engineering Ltd, which is a diversified heavy engineering company engaged in manufacturing and project businesses with an extensive global presence. Isgec manufactures Process Plant Equipment, Presses, Iron & Steel Castings, and Boiler Pressure Parts. Isgec also undertakes turnkey projects for setting up Boilers, Power Plants, Sugar Plants, Distilleries, Factories, Industrial Water Treatment Facilities, and Bulk Material Handling Facilities. Isgec has also developed strengths in Construction.

The Yamuna Syndicate Ltd is a professionally managed trading Company with a team of qualified, trained, and experienced staff. The Company enjoys high reputation in the market based on its commitment to Safety, Total Quality, and Customer Focus.
