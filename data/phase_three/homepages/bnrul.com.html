About us

BNR Udyog Limited (formerly Rathi Stock & Share Brokers Limited), founded by Late Shri B.N. Rathi (1922-2013) was incorporated on 29th November, 1994 to carry on the business of Stockbroking, Under writing, Merchant Banking and other Financial Activities. Later, the company has diversified into IT & ITES, e-Governance & Construction activities as their core business activities.

We are multi-disciplinary service provider located in Hyderabad, Telangana. Our collective skills cover everything from  Archival, Document Management & Delivery,  Scanning & Digitization, software development, Data management. We are registered with “Software Technology Park of India” Hyderabad and have an outstanding reputation coupled with 20 years of experience in the field of IT & ITES. We are ISO 9001-2008 & ISO 27001:2013, ISO/IEC 20000-1:2011certified.

Founder Chairman

Late Shri. B. N. Rathi

Late Shri B.N. Rathi founded BNR Group of Companies namely BNR Udyog Limited, B.N. Rathi Securities Limited and BNR Capital Services Private Limited.

 Our respected Chairman was an exceptional human being and an outstanding leader. His life was a remarkable blend of wisdom, humility, and service. He was associated with numerous social organizations and served on the boards of many listed companies, leaving a legacy of integrity and foresight wherever he went.

Deeply respected by all who knew him, Shri Rathi was a man of immense patience and calm demeanor. He spoke less and listened more—a rare and admirable quality in today’s world. His ability to truly hear others, combined with his thoughtful silence, made his words carry immense weight when he chose to speak.

His vision was expansive, often stretching beyond the imaginable. He had a unique ability to think ahead of his time, and his ideas often opened new possibilities and perspectives for those around him. A cool-headed visionary, Shri B. N. Rathi inspired many by example rather than rhetoric.

His contributions—both in the corporate world and in social service—will continue to inspire generations to come.

Board of Directors

Shri. Janampalli Vikramdev Rao

Chairman

Shri. Janampalli Vikramdev Rao is a chairman of the Company holds a MBBS Degree. He is a prominent business leader and philanthropist based in Hyderabad. He serves and holds key roles in several other organizations, including The Hyderabad Race Club and Hyderabad Golf Club.

He is also the President of Sivananda Rehabilitation Home, a leading NGO dedicated to the care and rehabilitation of individuals affected by leprosy and tuberculosis. With a legacy rooted in public service, Shri Vikramdev Rao combines corporate leadership with a deep commitment to social welfare.
