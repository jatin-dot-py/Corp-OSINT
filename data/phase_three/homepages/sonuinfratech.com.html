About Sonu Infratech

Founded in 2001 as “M/s. Sonu Enterprises,” Sonu Infratech has grown from a registered partnership firm into a trusted public limited company. Backed by a strong foundation laid by its founding partners and guided by experienced leadership, the company has evolved through dynamic changes and steady growth.

Today, Sonu Infratech Limited stands as a recognized name in the construction sector, committed to creating lasting infrastructure across key economic segments with integrity, innovation, and excellence.
