OUR PROCESS

How We Create The Best Of the Industry?

Raw Material Selection

We begin by sourcing the finest 100% cotton, ensuring only premium quality fibres are used for spinning.

Blending and Carding

The cotton fibres are carefully blended and carded to separate and align them, creating a uniform sliver for further processing.

Drawing

Multiple slivers are drawn and stretched to achieve the desired thickness and consistency, ensuring a smooth and even yarn.

Roving

The drawn sliver is then converted into roving, a finer and slightly twisted strand, preparing it for spinning.

Spinning

The roving is spun into yarn using state-of-the-art ring spinning technology, giving the yarn its final twist, strength, and durability.

Quality Assurance

Throughout the process, we conduct rigorous quality checks to ensure that our yarn meets the highest industry standards for strength, consistency, and performance.

Winding and Packing

Once the yarn is produced, it is wound onto cones, bobbins, or spools, and carefully packaged to ensure safe and efficient delivery to our clients.
