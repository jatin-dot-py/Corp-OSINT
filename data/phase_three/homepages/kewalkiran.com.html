Brands

Kewal Kiran Clothing Limited (KKCL) has been a leading manufacturer and retailer of branded apparel since 1981. Our integrated play across the value chain – from designing to manufacturing, branding and retailing – ensures quality that blends style and comfort seamlessly. We boast a diverse portfolio of in-house fashion brands such as such as Killer, Integriti, Lawman, Easies, Junior Killer and Punya captivating apparel and accessory lovers nationwide. Headquartered in Mumbai, Kewal Kiran Clothing Limited is amongst India's premier lifestyle companies with consumers across Asia and the middle east. Our ISO 9000 certified manufacturing units are the key to our growth.
