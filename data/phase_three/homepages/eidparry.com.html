E.I.D. – Parry (India) Limited is a listed company engaged in the business of Sweeteners and Nutraceuticals. Headquartered in ‘Dare House’ a heritage building, in Chennai, India, the Company is a part of the INR 778 Billion Murugappa Group, one of India’s leading business conglomerates.
