Events and Exhibitions


ARCHIDPANEL hosted their dealer partners in a remarkable meet from April 6th-8th, 2024, celebrating the launch of their cutting-edge MDF (Medium Density Fireboard) and EDHMR (Extreme Density High Moisture Resistant) manufacturing facility under the Archidpanel brand. Set against the luxurious backdrop of the Golden Tusk Resort in Jim Corbett National Park, the event highlighted innovation, strengthened relationships, and showcased the brand’s vision.

The gathering began with a lavish Royal High Tea, followed by a mesmerizing live band performance, creating the perfect prelude to the grand unveiling. Mr. Rajiv Daga, Managing Director, introduced the company’s latest initiatives, and the ceremonial ribbon-cutting revealed the innovative MDF and EDHMR range, poised to boost Archidpanel’s turnover by ₹200 Crores. The scenic venue and the grandeur of the launch underscored Archidpanel’s industry leadership.


Matecia – the much-celebrated building material extravaganza was held at Pragati Maidan from August 18th to 20th. Our innovative showcase, featuring Medium-Density Fiberboard (MDF), Extreme Density High Moisture Resistance (EDHMR), Prelaminated MDF, High-Pressure Laminates (HPL), and superior-quality Plywood, was aimed at reshaping the industry standards and setting the construction world abuzz. Architects and designers from all over were invited to envision stunning finishes with unparalleled ease and experience the long-lasting beauty that Archidply's products bring to residential, commercial, and hospitality projects.

While Matecia has concluded, Archidply's quest for innovation and excellence never stops. Stay tuned for more exciting developments, and join us on this journey to shape the future of construction. Your vision, our materials – together, we build better.

Archidply hosted its dealer partners in a grand evening of celebration, recognition, and new beginnings at Tivoli Resort, Chattarpur, Delhi, on July 7th, 2025. With over 100 prominent dealers in attendance, the event highlighted the company’s commitment to innovation, collaboration, and continued growth.

The evening commenced with a traditional lamp lighting ceremony led by Managing Director Shri Rajiv Daga, Vice President Mr. Rakesh Gulati, and esteemed senior dealers, setting an inspiring tone for the celebrations. A major highlight was the launch of two new 1mm laminate folders — Innoveta, designed for the retail segment, and Endura, crafted for project requirements.

Honoring excellence, Archidply felicitated 18 top-performing dealers across Gold, Diamond, and Platinum categories for their outstanding contributions during FY 2024–25. A proud moment was the recognition of the Delhi Sales Team for achieving a historic ₹100 Crore turnover milestone. The night concluded with electrifying music, live performances, and celebrations, marking a perfect blend of recognition and entertainment.

Archidply Industries Ltd marked its Golden Jubilee with a grand three-day celebration held from April 8–10, 2026, at the Hilton Goa Resort — a fitting tribute to five decades of trust, craftsmanship, and industry leadership since 1976.

The event brought together leadership, dealer partners, and the extended Archidply family for an experience that seamlessly blended reflection with celebration. From a tribute film honoring the Chairman's five-decade contribution, to an awards ceremony recognizing the pillars of the company's growth — every moment was a nod to the people who built this legacy. Beach Olympics at Baga Beach, a sunset evening at Purple Martini, and a spectacular musical night by Goa's iconic Black & White band made this a celebration as memorable as the milestone itself.

Fifty years. One unwavering promise. And a future as strong as the foundation we've built.
