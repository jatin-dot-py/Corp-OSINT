INDIA’S SUSTAINABLE PAPER, PACKAGING BOARD, TISSUE PAPER & CEMENT MANUFACTURER

Tamil Nadu Newsprint and Papers Limited (TNPL) manufactures sustainable paper, packaging board, tissue paper and cement using renewable resources, advanced technology and circular economy practices, delivering high-quality, eco-friendly solutions to customers in India and global markets.

Paper

India’s first bagasse-based paper mill, producing 4,40,000 MT and exporting to 60+ countries annually. With low water usage processes, TNPL sets the benchmark for next-generation.

Read More

Packaging Board

Serving a fast-growing nation of 1.3 billion doesn’t have to strain the environment. TNPL’s Packaging Board supports brands while conserving resources through efficient solid waste management.

Read More

Cement

In a country generating 196 million tonnes of fly ash every year, TNPL turns waste management into a thriving business. With a ‘waste to wealth’ approach, we are redefining how the paper industry views waste.

Read More

Tissue Paper

Produced using advanced manufacturing processes, TNPL Tissue Papers deliver dependable hygiene solutions while upholding our commitment to quality and responsible production.
