Duncan Engineering Ltd | Formerly known as Schrader Duncan


Who we are

Erstwhile Schrader Duncan Ltd, Duncan Engineering Limited is one of India’s pioneers in Industrial Pneumatics, Valves, Actuators, Valve Automation Systems, and Accessories. A part of the AG Ventures (https://agventures.co.in), and with a legacy spanning over six decades, we have built a strong reputation as a trusted partner to Indian industry—serving OEM and aftermarket segments nationwide through an extensive network of sales offices and channel partners.

We offer a comprehensive portfolio ranging from standard catalogue products to bespoke engineered solutions tailored for demanding applications. Our state-of-the-art, environment-friendly manufacturing facility and dust-free, temperature-controlled assembly plant at Ranjangaon, Pune, position us at the forefront of industrial innovation, precision, and quality.
