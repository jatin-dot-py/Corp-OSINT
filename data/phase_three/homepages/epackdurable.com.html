Company Overview

ORIGINAL DESIGN

MANUFACTURERS

A leading name in the living appliances space, EPACK Durable specializes in manufacturing a wide range of products—including room air conditioners, large domestic appliances such as air coolers and washing machines, small appliances like induction stoves, mixer grinders, water dispensers, air fryers, and various components.

Driven by a customer-centric approach, continuous innovation, and operational excellence, EPACK Durable has been on a transformative journey—evolving into a trusted manufacturing partner for leading brands over the past two decades.

Why EPACK

INTEGRATED SOLUTIONS

PROVIDER

Our endeavour is to cater to all aspects of Room Air Conditioners and Small Domestic Appliances value chain. EPACK Durable works jointly with the customer team and customises the products, according to different client requirements. Our manufacturing and design capabilities allow us to develop, design and manufacture models of RACs of varying designs and technical specifications. Further, the current RAC product offerings enable us to offer more customisation to RAC brands in terms of completely built-up units or IDUs and ODUs separately.
