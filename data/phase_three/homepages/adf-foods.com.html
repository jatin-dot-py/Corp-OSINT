ADF Foods is a leading global food manufacturing company and distributor with a presence in over 55 countries. Our product offerings include delicious sauces, pickles, chutneys, pastes, ready-to-cook, ready-to-eat products and frozen foods for consumers around the world. Taste, ease and quality are at the heart of what we do at ADF Foods. Our corporate interests include a core manufacturing and export business, an agency distribution network and domestic business.
