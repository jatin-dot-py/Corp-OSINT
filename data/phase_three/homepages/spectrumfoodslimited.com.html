Salt
We boast a 200,000 MT/YR salt production capacity in Phalodi, Rajasthan. Positioned advantageously in North India, our state-of-the-art facilities minimize transport costs. We excel in Industrial grade salt, offering seamless packaging and automated loading, serving diverse sectors like chemicals, power, and textiles. With three decades of expertise, we optimize salt usage, positioning it as a catalyst for your company’s triumph.
Spices
We, at Spectrum Foods Ltd., have established an advanced Spice Manufacturing facility in Phalodi, Rajasthan. Our diverse spice range includes whole, ground, blended, and flakes such as turmeric, red chili, cumin, fennel, coriander, fenugreek, and more. We offer these spices in various sizes of pouches and boxes to suit your needs
Renewable
Harnessing sustainable energy is our priority at SFL. Our 400 kWh captive solar power plant fulfills our manufacturing needs, with any surplus sourced from the grid. Our refinery, guided by Swiss Salt Technologists, showcases our commitment to innovative chemical engineering, ensuring minimal utilities and unmatched purity.
