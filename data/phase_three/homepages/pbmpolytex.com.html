About Us

“We make your need for quality yarn our only priority at the Patodia Group of Industries.”

When customer focus is not just a value statement but a way of life, the mission and vision of a Company just naturally fall into place – to provide you, the customer, with high quality cotton yarns and textiles at reasonable prices and at service levels which rival the world’s best.

The Patodia Group of Industries founded by the late visionary B L Patodia in 1938, as a trading concern in cotton and art silk yarns, which went on to become the largest distributor of yarns in India. Today, the Patodia Group focuses on producing top of the line 100% combed cotton yarn and has 4 spinning companies under its umbrella.
