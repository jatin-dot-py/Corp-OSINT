* ABOUT US *

AMD Industries Limited

AMD Industries Limited is a leading manufacturer of high-quality packaging solutions across a wide range of industries, including food & beverages, home care, personal care, liquor & spirits, paints & adhesives, pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals, dairy, automotive, and agrochemicals.

With unmatched capabilities, we are India’s only complete one-stop packaging provider for the carbonated soft drink and beer industries.

Founded in 1958 as a trading company, AMD has grown into one of India’s foremost beverage-packaging manufacturers. Our products are trusted by leading global and national brands, including:

  • Coca-Cola
  • PepsiCo
  • Anheuser-Busch InBev (AB InBev)
  • Carlsberg A/S
  • Heineken Holding N.V.
  • Dabur
  • HUL
  • Hamdard
  • RCPL(Campa) and many more

We also serve numerous prominent Indian beverage, pharmaceutical, and healthcare companies, delivering packaging built on quality and reliability.

At AMD, we are driven by innovation, precision, and excellence, providing advanced packaging solutions that support and enhance some of the world’s most iconic products.
