What we do

Pioneers in specialty packaging

We create specialty packaging for the world’s leading FMCG companies. Our global expertise spans five large categories – Oral Care, Beauty & Cosmetics, Pharmaceuticals & Health, Food & Nutrition and Home Care. Our portfolio of products comprises laminates, laminated tubes, extruded tubes, caps & closures and dispensing systems/applicators.
