JIWANRAM SHEODUTTRAI INDUSTRIES LIMITED

JIWANRAM SHEODUTTRAI INDUSTRIES LIMITED(JSIL) (ERSTWHILE JIWANRAM SHEODUTTRAI INDUSTRIES PRIVATE LIMITED), founded in 1997, is a globally renowned manufacturer and exporter of Industrial Safety Gloves and garments, Work Wear and Casual Wear.We are an ISO 9001:2015 accredited company and have been recognized on several platforms for our capability to create value, innovate solutions and deliver quality.

With a distribution network that is omnipresent on a National as well as a Global scale, JSIPL aims to provide its customers with products that exemplify quality and precision.

Via our corporate social responsibility efforts, we actively promote growth and ensure the social development and empowerment of our workforce.

Social Responsibility

Being conscious of and dedicatedly concerned with its corporate social responsibilities, JSIPL has been ever supporting social cause services. For this, our group companies collaborate in a substantial way with a leader social service NGO and contribute a large outlay on social service projects of permanent and time needs.

Activities is collaboration with Social Services Organization, our chief collaborator is Lions Club of North Calcutta which has been a brand citadel for last 52 years in the areas of humanistic services, especially in the tri-fold of Environmental Preservation, Education and Health where JSIPL has been sponsoring a wholesome financial fund support.
