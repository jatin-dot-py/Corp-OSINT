Tenneco India

We supply critical, highly engineered and technology intensive automotive solutions tailored for Indian OEMs and export markets, with leading market shares across automotive industry sub-segments. Our market leadership is underpinned by long-standing relationships with a broad base of customers including leading Indian and global OEMs. We believe our leadership position enables us to solidify our relationships with existing customers and attract new customers across vehicle categories.

About Us

Proud to be part of the Tenneco Group

Tenneco Group, headquartered in the US, is one of the leading suppliers of various engineered automotive components with a cumulative revenue of $16.78 billion for the year ended December 31, 2024. Tenneco Group operates across 5 segments - Clean Air, Powertrain, Ignition, Performance Solutions and DRiV (the aftermarket business) through which it drives advancements in global mobility by delivering technology solutions for light vehicles, commercial trucks, off-highway segment, industrial, and the aftermarket.
