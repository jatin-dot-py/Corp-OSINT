Welcome to Tarapur Transformers Ltd.

We are a ‘Power Engineering Solutions Company’ based out of Mumbai, India with multiple manufacturing locations in India, manufacturing allied equipments for the Power Engineering Sector.


Tarapur Transformers Ltd., an ISO 9001:2000 certified company, was established in 1988 and has since grown to be one of the prominent players in the Indian Power Industry for Repairs, Refurbishing and  Up-gradation of Power Transformers and manufacturing of Power & Distribution Transformers, Instrument Transformers.  It has state-of-the-art manufacturing and testing facilities at Boisar, Wada (near Mumbai) and Vadodara (Gujarat) which is considered as the hub of transformer industry. Tarapur’s list of satisfied customers is evidence to its commitment to quality and excellent customer service.
