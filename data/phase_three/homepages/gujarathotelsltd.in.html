About Vadodara City

Vadodara, a rapidly developing industrial city, where the past is still alive in its traditional handicrafts, its culture and legends. A city of impressive contrasts, the ethos is reflected in Welcome Hotel Vadodara, the leading hotel in Gujarat, a fascinating harmony of traditional architecture and modern comforts.
