Best Hard Shell Capsule Manufacturer In India – Sunil Healthcare

Established over four decades ago, Sunil Healthcare has become the second-largest manufacturer of Empty Hard Gelatin Capsules in India. Being one of the leading pharmaceutical brands, we enjoy immense credibility in various global markets with our best practices for environmental friendliness and quality. We had introduced the concept of ‘Double Lock Capsules’ which is now becoming a standard in the industry. It was the first of its locking system that has been later adopted by other manufacturers of capsules. With our continuous efforts and improvements in policy & adherence to top-notch quality monitoring measures, we have become one of the best gelatin capsule manufacturers.
