About Us

Enhancing Global Nutrition Profile with Nutritional Solutions

Hexagon Nutrition is a pioneer in the Indian nutraceutical industry founded in the year 1993.

Our advanced research-based nutraceutical agency is focused on three verticals of holistic nutrition including Micronutrient Premix Products, Clinical Nutrition and Therapeutic Foods. We are leading supplier of nutritional products in 70+ countries.

Why Choose us?

Reinforced By Experience And Expertise In The Nutraceutical Industry
