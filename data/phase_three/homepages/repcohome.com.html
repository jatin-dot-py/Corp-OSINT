Our Products

Home Loans

Repco Special Loans
Housing loans are provided for the purpose of construction or purchase of residential property. For construction of residential house/flat the loan quantum is provided upto 85% and 80% for purchase.

Dream Home Loan

This loan is recommended for the purpose of repairs, renovation and extension/improvement of a residential property. For the building purpose the loan quantum is provided upto 50%.

Home Makeover Loan

Loans are recommended for the outright purchase of plots for construction of houses thereon. The loan tenure is fixed for maximum 10 years and loan quantum for the cost of plot is provided upto 75%.

Plot Loan

This loan is offered to Non-Resident Indians for construction or purchase of houses in India. The loan tenure is fixed for maximum 10 years and loan quantum for construction of residential house/flat is provided upto 85% and 80% for purchase.

NRI Housing Loan

Loans are provided for the mortgage of immovable properties whereas its purpose is depended on the desire of the borrower. The loan tenure is fixed for maximum 10 years and the loan quantum for the value of immovable properties is provided upto 70%.

Prosperity Loan

This loan is recommended for the purchase and/or construction of non-residential and commercial properties such as Marriage halls, Shopping complex, Office buildings etc. The loan quantum is provided upto 70% for purchase and

New Horizon Loan

This loan is recommended for construction/purchase of integrated house buildings comprising some commercial spaces like shops/office combined with residential buildings with substantial floor space allotted for residential premises.

Commercial Real Estate (CRE) Loan

This loan is provided for construction (including extensions and additions to existing property) on the land owned by the borrower’s parents .The loan quantum is provided upto 85% for construction and 80% for purchase.
