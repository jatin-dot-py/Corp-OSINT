InterContinental

The InterContinental Marine Drive is a 59 bedroom luxury boutique hotel located strategically in the heart of Mumbai’s Art Deco district, on the city’s most spectacular sea-front promenade, also known as the Queen’s necklace.

A veritable jewel in the crown of the Queen’s necklace, the InterContinental stands out with its large rooms and suites, best described as oversized. Each one of these well-appointed four fixture rooms offer a panoramic view of the Arabian Sea and the ever dynamic city skyline.

Equipped with the latest technologies, cutting edge design and a host of personalised services including butlers for every room, the InterContinental has consistently been rated amongst the top hotels in the city.

The Dome

Mumbai's First Ever Rooftop Bar

Perched atop the boutique luxury InterContinental Marine Drive, the Dome is an al fresco sky bar that offers a panoramic view of the Mumbai skyline and the city’s waterfront promenade.

With the addition of our new Bar 360, customers can enjoy a fully stocked private bar, with a view of the Bay while still being indoors! Just one level up is the hotel’s Infinity Pool Deck.

As the city transitions from light to darkness, you can enjoy beautiful sundowners, followed by post work relaxation to pre-dinner drinks and even a fun night out with your friends at Dome.

Kebab Korner

50 – year – old Indian Restaurant

The Kebab Korner has been indulging customers since 1960. Even in its newest avatar, we have retained some of our more classical elements.

At Kebab Korner there are no written recipes or measure of ingredients. In fact, the ingredients remain a bit of a mystery. A smile is all that the Masters are willing to share.

At Kebab Korner there is no cooking in bulk. Meat is fresh and marinated for at least 12 hours. Masalas come from the lanes of the Jama Masjid, as our tradition dictates.

There is a separate kitchen for vegetarians. Only the finest, most authentic ingredients are accepted.
