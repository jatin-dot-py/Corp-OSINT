About Us

Welcome to Shri Krishna Devcon Limited (SKDL)

SKDL is a public Limited Company listed at Bombay Stock Exchange. The company has been in the business of finance since its inception. The company was acquired by the group in 2007 and has made Real Estate Development its primary focus.

The promoter’s initiative in real estate in the last 5 years have resulted in creation of many prime properties. This success in the Real Estate market is now being consolidated under the umbrella of SKDL.
