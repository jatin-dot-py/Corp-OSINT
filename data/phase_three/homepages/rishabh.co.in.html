Welcome to Rishabh!

What We Do

Rishabh Group is a global leader in energy efficiency solutions, specializing in electrical automation, metering and instrumentation devices. We are renowned for our analog panel meters and are among the top global manufacturers of low-voltage current transformers. Our subsidiary, Lumel Alucast, provide high-precision aluminium high-pressure die-casting (HPDC) solutions for industries such as automotive and flow meter production. With a network of over 350 authorized dealers and operations in more than 100 countries, we maintain a strong global presence. We are supported by five state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities in India, Poland, and China, with seven accredited R&D centres to drive continuous development in products, processes, and applications.

New

RISH ED21X1

RISH ED21X1 measures important electrical parameters in 1Ph 2 Wire Networks. The meter is available in 100A Max.current measurement range on direct connection. The instrument has 2 SO outputs and 1DI for tariff counter. It displays parameters on bright LCD, Impulse LED for energy monitoring.

New

RISH ED11X1

Rish ED11X1 supports a direct connection max. 45A, saving money & reducing the hassle of connecting additional CTs. With remote monitoring & setting, there's a SO o/p along with a MODBUS port available.

New

RISH DC2111 MOD - Single Channel DC Energy Meter

RISH DC2111 is an advanced single-channel DC energy meter designed for accurate bidirectional energy measurement in applications such as EV charging, telecom base stations, solar PV, and industrial DC systems. It supports current measurement up to 2000A via external shunt, ensuring Class 1 accuracy as per IEC 62053-41:2021. The meter offers RS485 MODBUS RTU communication, pulse output, and a bright backlit LCD, all housed in a compact 2-module DIN rail enclosure.

New

Advanced - RISH LM1360+

RISH LM1360+ is an intelligent multifunction energy meter designed for industrial and commercial power monitoring applications. It measures key electrical parameters, energy consumption, power quality, and harmonics while offering built-in data logging and optional Modbus, Ethernet, and BACnet communication for seamless integration with energy management systems.

New

Smart Energy Meter - RISH LM1360+

Rish LM1360+ RJ12 CT Multifunction Energy Meter provides accurate monitoring of electrical parameters in single- and three-phase systems. Featuring plug-and-play RJ12 CT connectivity, built-in data logging, harmonic analysis up to the 31st order, and optional Modbus, Ethernet, or BACnet communication, it is ideal for energy management and sub-metering applications.

About Us

Rishabh Instruments, founded in 1982 by Narendra Joharimal Goliya, is a leading manufacturer in the electrical industry. He is a Promoter & Executive Chairman, holds a Bachelor's in Electrical Engineering from IIT Bombay and a Master's from Stanford University. Building on its 40+ year presence in India, the company has expanded globally through seven foreign subsidiaries—three in Poland and one each in the UK, US, China, and Cyprus.
