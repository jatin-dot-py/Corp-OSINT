About us

ARC Insulation and Insulators Ltd. (Formerly known as ARC Insulation & Insulators Private Limited) is a leading manufacturer of high-performance glass fiber reinforced polymer (GFRP) composite products. Founded in 2003, our company has been at the forefront of bringing innovative GFRP solutions to the construction, infrastructure, chemical processing, and other industrial sectors.

What sets us apart is our commitment to continuous research and development in advanced composite materials. Our team of engineers and material scientists works tirelessly to push the boundaries of GFRP applications, creating products that outperform traditional materials like steel, wood, and aluminum in strength, durability, and longevity.

Why Choose Us

Innovation At ARC Insulations, innovation is hard-coded into our DNA. Our team of engineers and material scientists continuously push the boundaries of GFRP technology through dedicated research and development. We don’t just manufacture GFRP products, we pioneer new composite formulations and applications that outperform traditional materials.

1

Advanced Manufacturing

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities utilize cutting-edge techniques like pultrusion to produce consistent, high-quality GFRP components. Stringent quality control and testing ensure our products meet or exceed industry standards across dimensions, strength, and durability parameters.

2

Extensive Product Range

ARC offers one of the most comprehensive GFRP product portfolios including rebar, structural profiles, pipes, tanks, cable trays, gratings, ladders and handrails. Whatever your project requirements, we have the right GFRP solution for unmatched strength and longevity.

3

Technical Expertise

Our team has cumulative decades of experience in advanced composite design, engineering and manufacturing. We collaborate closely with customers to understand their challenges and requirements, providing end-to-end technical guidance from concept to installation.

4

Cost-Effectiveness

While superior performing, GFRP products offer lower lifetime costs compared to steel, aluminium and concrete alternatives. With their corrosion resistance and low maintenance needs, ARC’s GFRP solutions deliver great value over an extended service life.

5

Sustainable Solutions

GFRP’s lower carbon footprint makes it an environmentally sustainable choice over steel and concrete for infrastructure projects. Our products promote green construction while delivering the strength and longevity needed for resilient assets.

GFRP rebar stands for Glass Fiber Reinforced Polymer rebar, a type of reinforcing bar used in concrete structures made from glass fibers embedded in a polymer resin matrix.
What is GFRP rebar?
GFRP rebar is produced using an extrusion process. Straight rebars are fully cured during extrusion, while bent rebars remain partially cured to allow shaping before final curing.
How is GFRP rebar manufactured?
GFRP rebar is twice as strong as steel in tensile strength, up to seven times lighter, and rustproof, offering enhanced durability and efficiency over traditional steel rebar.
How does GFRP rebar compare to steel rebar?

What are the key benefits of using GFRP rebar?
ARC GFRP rebar is well-suited for various applications including masonry walls, concrete roads, drainage systems, foundations, friction slabs, crush barriers, bends in diaphragm walls, piling, precast elements, retaining walls, swimming pools, sea walls, and underground tanks.
