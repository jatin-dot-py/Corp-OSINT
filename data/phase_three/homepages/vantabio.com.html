Welcome to Vanta Bioscience Limited

The Right Dose of Intelligent Toxicology

Vanta Bioscience is a full service GLP certified preclinical contract research organization located in Chennai (Tamil Nadu), India. Vanta Bioscience is established as a center of excellence for GLP toxicology and safety assessment offering services in India and across the globe.

Research Facility

Spread across 56,000 sq. ft, Vanta Bioscience’s state-of-the-art research facility houses core toxicology, analytical, clinical chemistry, hematology and histopathology suites. Some notable features of the facility include:
  • Facility designed and built from the ground–up in accordance with AAALAC & GLP specifications
  • Class 100,000 facility with efficient HVAC control at 15 air changes per hour
  • Individual AHUs for each animal room to avoid cross contamination
  • Equipped with “Individually Ventilated Cage” HEPA-filtered racking system
  • Dedicated service floor to ensure integrity of the core procedural areas
  • Three level pressure gradient system with supply and return corridors
