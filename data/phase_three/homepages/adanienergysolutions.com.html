Dear Visitor,

At Adani, we value the trust and aspirations of all job seekers who approach us in pursuit of their career enhancement. It is, therefore, our responsibility to alert you about fraudulent activities that may target individuals during their job search.

We have been made aware of instances of unscrupulous individuals demanding cash payments or other forms of compensation in exchange for job offers. Instances of candidates been asked to deposit money in a particular bank account, as part of the recruitment process, has also come to light.

These activities are not only unethical but also illegal. It is crucial to remember that legitimate employers, including the Adani Group, do not charge fees of any kind during the hiring process.

If you have been approached with any such offer, we urge you to report it immediately to local authorities and also inform us by citing details of the person through the Contact Us tab on this site. Please note that the Adani Group will not be liable for the consequences of anyone falling prey to such false solicitations.
