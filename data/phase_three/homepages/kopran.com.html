Kopran Limited has established an indomitable presence in the realm of manufacturing an extensive spectrum of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and Formulations.

Our products have traversed borders and have found their way into more than 60 countries, including African nations, South Africa, the United States, Latin America, Southeast Asia, the European Union, China, Australia, CIS region and the United Kingdom. This global acceptance reflects our firm dedication to deliver excellence and ensure top-notch quality.
