Meet Aequs

Aequs, with its Ecosystems of Efficiency, helps deliver robust engineering capabilities, creating innovative products and solutions for OEM customers. Driven by precision, it provides zero error manufacturing solutions for the Aerospace and Consumer industries.

At the heart of our integrated approach is our commitment to collaboration, solidarity and partnership… symbolized by the two parallel lines in our logo.

We are Aequals at heart. Aequals in delivery. And Aequals in our partnerships.

Find out more

Enabling flight.
Across the globe.

We are India’s only precision component manufacturer operating within a single special economic zone, to offer fully vertically integrated manufacturing capabilities in the aerospace component sector.

With a wide range of offerings and a vertically integrated manufacturing ecosystem, we work with the biggest and best Aerospace OEMS from across the globe.

Find out more

Driving smiles.
Driving delight.

We are a platform for custom manufacturing of consumer products, based on specific client requirements, developing innovative manufacturing processes that cater to the ever-changing demands of consumers across plastics, metal and electronics.

With a client list that spans the biggest and best OEMs, our offerings cover every aspect of a consumer’s life, from household appliances to toys and electronics.
