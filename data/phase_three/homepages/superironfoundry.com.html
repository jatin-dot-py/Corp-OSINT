ABOUT SUPER IRON FOUNDRY

Super Iron Foundry was founded by a visionary, Mr. Narendra Jain in 1977. It is one of the oldest manufacturers of Grey and Ductile Iron Castings in India. This ISO 9001:2015, 14001:2015, 45001:2018 and various International Certifications Company also holds the distinction of being the first company in eastern India having the capacity to produce wide range of casting for varied application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our infrastructure and industrial castings.

Super Iron Foundry is a leading manufacturer of grey cast iron and ductile iron castings for infrastructure and industrial applications. Our product portfolio includes grey iron and ductile iron manhole covers, frames and covers, channel gratings, gully gratings, trench covers, tree grates, surface boxes, catch basins, inspection chambers, agricultural castings, and custom sanitary castings. We also manufacture galvanized steel gratings, fabricated steel components, and project-specific cast products for municipal infrastructure, roads and highways, drainage systems, utilities, and global export markets.

Yes. Our manhole covers, channel gratings, gully gratings, and trench covers are manufactured in compliance with international quality complying with the load-bearing requirements, ensuring safety, durability, and long service life. Most of our products are also enlisted with the BSI Kitemark, demonstrating independent third-party certification and compliance with stringent British and international standards.

Yes. Super Iron Foundry is a global exporter of grey cast iron and ductile iron products, supplying infrastructure and industrial castings to over 38 countries worldwide. Our export portfolio serves municipal authorities, infrastructure developers, EPC contractors, utilities, and industrial clients across multiple international markets.

Super Iron Foundry has a strong competitive advantage backed with around five decades of industry experience, with exports dating back from the late 1980’s. Our globally recognized brand, combined with state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities, enables us to deliver consistent quality, competitive pricing, and reliable supply to customers across international markets.

Yes. We integrate digitalization and Industry 4.0 technologies, including advanced simulation software, digital twin technology, data analytics, and smart process monitoring. These solutions improve process efficiency, predictive maintenance, production planning, and product quality, allowing us to deliver high-quality grey and ductile iron castings consistently.

With Carbon Emission reduction being a focus area worldwide- Super Iron Foundry ( SIF ) is doing its bit by operating as a green and sustainable foundry, implementing clean production methods, energy optimization, emission reduction, and recycling initiatives. We are also on the verge of finalizing Solar Panels as renewable energy source. Our eco-friendly practices attract environmentally conscious customers and ensure responsible manufacturing of municipal and industrial castings.

Super Iron Foundry specializes in customized grey and ductile iron castings for infrastructure, industrial, and agricultural applications. Flexible production systems allow us to deliver on-demand manufacturing, country-specific compliance, and tailor-made solutions, all while maintaining shorter lead times and consistent quality.

Our modern manufacturing infrastructure and advanced process controls enable the company to manage bulk, repeat, and long-term export orders efficiently. We ensure consistent product quality, on-time delivery, and dependable supply for international clients and this is possible with our State of the art Automatic Molding lines, Robotic Painting and Fettling (grinding) arms, dedicated team of professionals and Management expertise, know-how and goodwill in the business since a few decades.
