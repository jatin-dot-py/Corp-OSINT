LUXURY AND ULTRA LUXURY

Dive into our expansive portfolio.

Lotus Developers has established a foothold in the ultra-luxury and luxury real estate market of western suburbs of Mumbai. We and our promoter have developed over 3.99 million square feet of luxury and ultra luxury residential and commercial spaces, with a record 20+ years of experience of our promoter in the real estate industry. We currently have more than 3.8 million square feet of space under development for our ongoing and upcoming projects. We intend to continue to promote and expand our brand.

Lotus Developers presents a diverse portfolio of ultra-luxury and luxury residential and commercial properties. We seek to enhance the value of our projects by creating a better living environment through the provision of comprehensive community facilities and by engaging experts in various specialized fields. Our core competency lies in professionally managing the real estate value chain as our in-house teams oversee every project stage, from conceptualization to completion. Our projects go through meticulous market research and analysis to identify suitable locations.


Mumbai’s position as the commercial capital of India, together with the demographics of high-income customer base and an expanding segment of young professionals provide a substantive market for our projects - ultra luxury and luxury residential properties in the western suburbs of Mumbai. Our commitment to customer satisfaction and safety has garnered us several satisfied clients over the years.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

QUALITY
We create real estate spaces for those who value impeccable design, superior craftsmanship and the finest appointments. Since our establishment, we have aimed at building a brand centred around customer satisfaction, by creating environments that enhance our customers’ lifestyles.
LUXURY
True luxury is a matter of comfort. The Lotus standard is widely recognized as a unique value proposition among our key presence area of Western suburbs of Mumbai. Allowing buyers to choose from a sophisticated selection of finishes, designs, and layouts to tailor homes to their individual tastes. We have observed an increase in the value of our projects which may be correlated to the customers’ perception and appreciation for our projects.
EFFICIENCY
Efficiency comes from our commitment to working with talented professionals. We also leverage the expertise of external specialists to match our wide range of operations, such as architects, design architects, liasioning consultants, interior designers, technical consultants, engineers, construction contractors and building services consultants. Our project execution capabilities are present across the real estate development value chain and developed extensive in-house capabilities right from identification of land, conceptualization of the project, to execution of the project involving planning, obtaining regulatory approvals, designing, supervising construction and culminating in delivery of the project.

THE LOTUS DIFFERENCE

Our philosophy of perfection elevates and distinguishes every Lotus development. This passion originates from our founder Mr. Anand Pandit's years of experience in the luxury segment of the real estate industry. Every aspect of our project revolves around customer satisfaction, with an emphasis on quality and luxury construction, strong project execution, contemporary architecture and timely delivery, which in turn have enabled us to establish a reputable brand. When designing and developing a project, we leverage our experience and strategic research on various aspects of a project, such as layout planning, floor space index utilization, unit size, amenities and interior design, etc., in order to maximize customer appeal. The Lotus attention to detail is apparent from our ability to customise amenities as is reflected our projects Ananya and Ayana which come with rooftop amenities such as swimming pool, cabanas and sit outs space for our audience, while Signature to include amenities such as private theatre, banquet, private lounge, etc. in addition to standard amenities of an office building.
