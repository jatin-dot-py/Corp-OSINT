Procedure

Step-by-Step Process
Process

Step 1

IML Labels are manufactured using a unique procedure and then placed by robotic hand in the machine.

Step 2

Product is injected into the mould with the IML label to bond them together as one.

Step 3

The formed product is ejected from the mould as a single finished unit.

Step 4

Inspection for label bonding and print quality is performed.

Step 5

Products are packed and sealed for distribution.

Step 6

Final shipment is sent to retailers and customers.
