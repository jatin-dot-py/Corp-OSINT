About Pecos Pub

Founded in 1989 ‘Pecos’ was one of Bangalore’s first Pubs. It’s distinctive style of ‘Classic Rock Music’ and ‘Retro Ambiance’ set it apart….30 years later it is now one of Bangalore’s iconic restaurants and has earned almost a cult status and a loyal fan following in the city. We endeavour to preserve this legacy and take it to many more cities across the country.
