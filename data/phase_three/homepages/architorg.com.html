Who we are

Welcome to Archit Organosys

ArchitOrganosys Ltd., established in 1989, is a manufacturing and trading company along with various sister concerns in Chemical segment.

Today ArchitOrganosys Ltd. is on the forefront in the Indian Chemical Industry, producing a variety of Organic and Inorganic Chemicals andSpeciality Derivatives.

Quality Policy

The Quality Assurance Department, managed by qualified personnel, constantly examines quality considerations – performing systematic sampling and testing at every stage from raw materials, through each procedure of intermediate and finished formulations.

Environments & Classification

At Archit, we care for the Environment. We have dedicated green belts surrounding the plant area and inhouse ETP and sewage treatment plant making it an entirely Zero discharge plant. We also have ultra modern scrubbers controlling zero gas emissions to control air pollution.

We as a company are aware of our responsibility towards our society and we maintain the highly required standards, for packaging, labeling, calibration and various other factors related to hazardous products.
