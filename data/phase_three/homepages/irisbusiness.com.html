About IRIS

At IRIS RegTech Solutions, we are passionate about building transparency and driving growth for economies, enterprises, and MSMEs around the world. Our integrated product suite supports the complete regulatory lifecycle, helping organizations and supervisory authorities accelerate digital transformation across reporting, compliance, and supervision.

We achieve this through deep domain expertise combined with purpose-built AI, trusted technology and open data standards for financial and non-financial reporting, enabling organizations to make better decisions, and regulators to build smarter regulatory ecosystems. 

The Journey So Far

Two decades and counting, jurisdiction by jurisdiction. 

2000

Founded

Conducted the company's first XBRL seminar, defining the course of its journey.

2006–07

First product launches

Launched IRIS iFile, the company's first product, for BSE-NSE.

2010–11

Recognition & IRIS iDeal

Received NASSCOM Social Innovation Honours for using technology to drive social impact in electronic voting; launched IRIS iDeal for banks.

2013–14

IRIS CARBON® launches

Developed and launched IRIS Carbon, successfully transitioning from a services-led business to a product-led SaaS model.

2017

IRIS GST & BSE-SME listing

Launched IRIS GST for TaxTech and listed on BSE-SME.

2021

Main Board listing

Listed on the BSE & NSE Main Board.

2022

Best TaxTech, SAAS Award

Awarded Best TaxTech by TIOL (Silver).

Most Agile SaaS Product, SaaS Awards 2022

2024

Award-winning year

Recognized as an IRP; awarded Best FinTech by FE and Best TaxTech by TIOL (Jury); won Best RegTech Award; crossed ₹100 Crore revenue.

2025

AI launches & rebrand

Launched AI-driven XBRL tagging platform IRIS Instant and the IRIS MSME app; divested IRIS GST to Sovos UK; rebranded to IRIS RegTech Solutions Limited.

2026

IRIS RegAI launches

Launched IRIS RegAI, an AI-powered regulatory compliance platform for regulators, stock exchanges, and listed companies.
