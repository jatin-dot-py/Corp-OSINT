About SPEB Adhesives

SPEB Adhesives Limited (Formerly Known as Speb Adhesives Pvt Ltd) is a pioneer in manufacturing synthetic rubber-based adhesives, trusted across India and globally since 1972. With over 40 years of experience and a legacy of innovation, SPEB-7 has become a symbol of strength, reliability, and unmatched bonding power.
Operating from a state-of-the-art facility with a capacity of 3600+ tons per year, we serve a wide range of sectors with cutting-edge adhesive solutions tailored specifically for craftsmen and artisans across India & UAE.
