Why Retreading?

Retreading is Environment Friendly

  • Retreading means lesser carbon footprints
  • Retreading reduces landfills of used casings

Retreading Means Saving

  • Retreading costs less than 30% of a new tyre
  • Retreading gives as good mileage as a new tyre

indag promise

SINCE 1978

INDAG Rubber Ltd. was founded in 1978 by Khemka group in joint venture with M/S Bandag, USA. We went public and got listed on the Bombay Stock exchange in 1984. INDAG revolutionized the Indian retreading industry with the introduction of its cold retreading technology and came to be known as the pioneer of retreading in India.

THE ONLY ALTERNATIVE TO NEW TYRES

INDAG was established with the purpose to provide fleet owners with world-class retreading solutions. INDAG is dedicated to helping fleet owners reduce their expenses on buying new tyres providing lower tyre CPKM (Cost-per-Km) and curtailing carbon footprints making them a sustainable and green fleet.

INDIA’S MOST TRUSTED RETREAD

Over the last 40+ years of excellence we have helped lakhs of fleet owners retreading millions of tyres in association with our vast retreader network across the country.
The retreader community and fleet owners across the country have shown an unwavering trust in our premium retreading solution….Read more
