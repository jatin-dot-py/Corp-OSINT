About Us

Welcome to
Sikko Industries Ltd.

We are a leading Manufacturer and Exporter of Agrochemicals, Bio Agrochemicals,NPK Fertilizers, Seeds etc.

SIKKO GROUP was founded by Mr. Jayantibhai Kumbhani in the year of 1997. Initially he started manufacturing Knapsack Sprayers under the name of “Sikko Sprayers & Export Company” which came into existence as “Sikko Sprayers Pvt Ltd” in the year 1999. Gradually it gained the fame & recognition as “SIKKO INDUSTRIES LIMITED” in the year 2000.

Why are we a leader in the Agriculture Industry

At SIKKO INDUSTRIES LTD, we set the standard in agriculture by combining innovation, sustainability, and expertise to transform how the world produces food. Through cutting-edge technologies like precision farming, AI-driven analytics, and automation, we empower farmers to increase efficiency and yields. Our focus on sustainable practices, such as soil health preservation and water conservation, ensures that we meet today’s demands without compromising the needs of future generations.

“We work for farmers to increase their profitability & ultimately, contribute to the growth of the nation’s economy”

We have spent all our lives discussing, breathing and living with agrochemicals. We will continue doing what we have done all these years- making every farmer’s lives better, easier and happier.

We are manufacturing and supplying a wide range of agrochemicals, pesticides, bio pesticides, organic pesticides, bio larvicides, organic larvicides, bio miticides, organic fertilizers, water soluble fertilizers, chemical fertilizers, and many more products for agri inputs. We are also supplying in the form of bulk packaging to more than 400 companies Worldwide.
