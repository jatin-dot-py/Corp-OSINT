Who We Are

Welcome to Yashhtej – We are a Latur-based company incorporated in the Year 2018, primarily engaged in the production of Soybean Crude Oil and Soybean De-Oiled Cake (DOC), which is widely used as animal feed in the poultry industry. Our crude oil is supplied to refiners for further processing into edible oil.

The Promoter of our Company are Mr. Baswaraj Madhavrao Barge, Mr. Suraj Shivraj Barge and Mr. Shivling Madhavrao Barge
