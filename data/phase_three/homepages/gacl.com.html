OUR COMPANY

Welcome to Gujarat Alkalies and Chemicals Limited (GACL): Elevating Industrial and Economic Growth in India and Enriching “Atmanirbhar Bharat”

GACL, a leader in the Chlor-Alkali sector, was established in 1973 in Vadodara, the cultural city of Gujarat. GACL has emerged as a shining beacon of self-reliance and an indispensable contributor to the industrial and economic growth of Gujarat and India as a whole. Our journey, nurtured under the auspices of the Government of Gujarat, has been marked by an unwavering dedication to cutting-edge technology, pioneering research and development, and strategic diversification.

Sustainability Goals

Shaping a Brighter Future for Generations to Come

GACL stands out in the chemical manufacturing industry, driven by a diverse and dynamic product portfolio. What truly sets us apart is our unwavering commitment to sustainability and eco-friendliness. We take great pride in pioneering eco-friendly technologies, upholding rigorous safety and quality standards, and maintaining our social responsibility. At GACL, we consistently lead the way, making a profound and enduring impact on society to build a better world for future generations.
