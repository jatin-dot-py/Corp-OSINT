Overview

Kretto Syscon Limited is a leading company in India specializing in real estate development and technology solutions. With a commitment to excellence and innovation, we aim to deliver high-quality projects that meet the evolving needs of our clients across various sectors.

Company Activities

The Main Objects Of The Company
Real Estate Development

We engage in the purchase and development of land and immovable properties, both residential and commercial. Our projects include constructing office complexes for sale or rental, and we handle properties across India, including urban and rural areas.

Software and IT Solutions

We specialize in software design, development, and maintenance. Our services include custom software solutions, IT assignments on a subcontracting basis, and comprehensive support for HR, payroll, insurance claims, and more. We also manufacture and maintain computer hardware and related systems.

Application Development

Our team develops and distributes systems and application software for various sectors, including offshore projects and internet services. We focus on emerging technologies and business applications, offering tailored solutions for clients in India and abroad.
