SEBI Directive

As per SEBI directive received on 17th January,2024 which mandates payment of dividend in electronic mode only  w.e.f. April,1,2024  provided the folio is KYC compliant –  
  1. In case of non-updation of PAN or Choice of Nomination or Contact Details or Mobile Number or Bank Account Details or Specimen Signature in respect of physical folios, dividend/interest etc. shall be paid only through electronic mode with effect from April 01, 2024 upon furnishing all the aforesaid details in entirety.
  2. If a security holder updates the PAN, Choice of Nomination, Contact Details including Mobile Number, Bank Account Details and Specimen Signature after April 01, 2024, then the security holder would receive all the dividends/interest etc. declared during that period (from April 01, 2024 till date of updation) pertaining to the securities held after the said updation automatically.

Important Disclosure

Sumedha Fiscal Services Limited (Sumedha Fiscal) is registered with SEBI as a Category I Merchant Banker (Registration No. INM000008753).

Sumedha Fiscal offers services for both SEBI-regulated and Non-SEBI regulated activities.

The Merchant Banking department of Sumedha Fiscal are involved in SEBI regulated activities. However, the Investment Banking department is providing services in non-SEBI regulated activities.

The Non-SEBI Regulated Activities undertaken by the Investment Banking department of Sumedha Fiscal are:

  • M&A Advisory
  • Advisory for Fund Raising
  • Financial Restructuring Advisory
  • Stressed Asset Resolution
  • Debt Syndication Services
  • TEV Study, LIE Report
  • Private Equity Placement

Note: None of the SEBI investor protection mechanisms will be available for any grievances or disputes arising out of or pertaining to the Non-SEBI regulated activities mentioned above.
