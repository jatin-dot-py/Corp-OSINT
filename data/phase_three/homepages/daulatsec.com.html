Welcome to Daulat Securities

Daulat Securities Limited, incorporated on 22nd October 1992, holding CIN:  L67120WB1992PLC056831 and Registration No. 056831 is listed at the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and Calcutta Stock Exchange (CSE), respectively. The company is a member of National Stock Exchange and is a Depository participant at NSDL. The main activity of the company is stock broking, marketing of mutual funds and depository. We provide a comprehensive range of financial services, including equity and derivatives trading, depository services for secure holding and transfer of securities, margin trading, IPO investments and mutual fund distribution. Further, the company facilitates seamless online and offline trading across stock exchanges, offers research and advisory services, and provides risk management solutions. Additionally, we ensure compliance with regulatory norms, promote financial literacy, and integrate advanced technology to deliver secure, efficient, and customer-centric investment solutions.

KYC

KYC is a one time exercise while dealing in securities market - once KYC is done through SEBI registered intermediaries (Broker, DP, Mutual Funds etc.) , you need not undergo the same process again when you approach another intermediary

DP Transactions

For depository transactions - prevent unauthorised transactions in your demat account - update mobile number with DP. You will receive alerts on your registered mobile for all debit and other important transactions in your demat directly through NSDL/CDSL

IPO

No need to issue cheques while subscribing to IPO. Just write the bank account number and sign application form to authorise your bank to make payment in case of allotment. No worries for refund as money remains in investors account.
