IEPF Claim

If the dividends declared by a Company remained unclaimed for seven consecutive years or more, then the dividends as well as the pertaining shares are transferred to IEPF. Share Samadhan specializes in reclaiming these assets, ensuring that they find their way back to you.

Duplicate Issue of Shares

Losing important documents is not uncommon. The papers could get lost, stolen or damaged due to a natural calamity. That's why we offer services for the duplicate issuance of shares, wherein the Company issues duplicate shares in the form of Letter of Confirmation (LOC). We make sure you have necessary docs to recover your investments.

Transmission of Shares

When it comes to transmitting shares from deceased shareholder to legal heir(s), we go beyond the paperwork. Our legal experts navigate the complexities, ensuring a smooth transfer process for inheritors and rightful successors.

Unclaimed Provident Funds

If you are or have been an employee, and you can’t remember your UAN or it’s inactive or don’t have the PF number, or your company is denying to pay the PF amount. Don’t Worry! Share Samadhan is your ally in reclaiming these unclaimed provident funds, providing a hassle-free process.

Unclaimed Insurance

Insurance amount becomes unclaimed due to various reasons like Mis-selling of policy, rejection of death claim, claim short-settled, etc. Our experts navigate the intricacies to ensure that rightful beneficiaries receive their due.

Unclaimed Mutual Funds

Mutual funds play a pivotal role in investment portfolios. If the Mutual Funds have been unclaimed for over 10 years, then they get transferred to Senior Citizen Welfare Fund (SCWF). Share Samadhan specializes in recovering unclaimed mutual funds, bringing back the value to the rightful owners.

In-Operative Bank Deposits

Share Samadhan goes the extra mile to recover funds from dormant or in-operative accounts, or if they have been transferred to Depositor Education Awareness Fund (DEAF) ensuring that no financial asset is left behind.

Unclaimed Debtors
