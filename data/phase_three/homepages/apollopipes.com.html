About Apollo Pipes Ltd

If you’re looking for a top-quality water solutions expert for plumbing, agriculture, sewerage, borewell, bath fittings and water storage solutions then you’re at the right place. With an experience of more than 35 years in this industry, we have built strong distribution chains and reliable retail networks. We have always put new and emerging technologies at the forefront of our service requirements.

We offer the best PVC pipes for water supply for multiple sectors across the country, starting from agriculture and water management to industrial and commercial spaces. Our products include an array of applications ranging from irrigation (Agri and HDPE pipes) and water supply (CPVC, uPVC, and PPR-C) to household and sanitary applications (Column, SWR, and casing pipes), making us an ideal solution for your everyday plumbing needs.

Along with providing durable and robust water solutions, we have kept in mind your need for a stylish bathroom. Hence, our range of bathroom fittings delivers premium quality at affordable prices. As a result, with APL Apollo, you’ll find a 360° service for all things plumbing.

Even though we have one of the largest product portfolios, we don’t believe in resting on our laurels. We are one of the fastest-growing brands in India, and the only way we look is up. We aspire to become a global leader in the world of plastic piping systems.

At APL Apollo Pipes, you will always find a need for creativity and a perpetual zeal for innovation. In our journey toward excellence, we are mindful of the interests of all our stakeholders.

We have always believed in giving back to society, which is why we are working on harnessing the potential of non-renewable energy sources. One of our ultimate dreams is to increase corporate excellence, prioritise customer satisfaction and strengthen every investor’s trust in APL Apollo.

Frequently Asked Questions

How old is APL Apollo Company?

As a trustworthy and reliable brand, APL Apollo success in delivering high-quality plumbing services to its customers for more than 35 years has been a commendable achievement. 
In our expedition of more than 3 decades in exploring the most advanced plumbing solutions, we have achieved feats like being among the market leaders in the piping industry, having the biggest manufacturing unit at a single location under one roof, and more than 1,30,000 MTPA capacity in production.
 

What are the ranges of pipes and fittings available?

At APL Apollo, you will find a solution for your every plumbing woe. We provide cost-effective and robust solutions for a myriad of plumbing services, starting with CPVC plumbing system, UPVC plumbing system , and PPR-C plumbing systems for potable water supply and casing and column pipes for borewell systems.

Our Agriculture and irrigation products include PVC agriculture pipes, HDPE pipes, and sprinkler systems, along with robust and multi-layered water tanks for water storage. Along with this, we also provide our customers with sewerage, adhesives, home solutions, and an amazing yet affordable bath fittings range, starting from faucets to other bathroom accessories.

What is the range of bathroom fittings available?

APL Apollo has got you covered in all the departments. Even for your bathroom, we have designed the perfect combination of style and functionality. Our bathroom fittings range includes faucets, showers, cisterns, seat covers, allied products, and accessories. All of these are designed to keep your bathroom stylish and hygienic. 

What types of water tanks are available?

Our range of robust water tanks is built to keep your stored water safe, cool, and pure. They are multi-layered, UV resistant, and ideal for use in the household as well as industrial purposes. They are available in 500, 700, 1000, 1500, 2000, 3000, and all the way up to 5000 litres. So, whatever the usage, we have got you covered.

Are APL Apollo products ISI certified?

Yes, our products are ISI-certified and comply with ASTM standards as well.

How many APL Apollo manufacturing plants are in India? Where are they located?

APL Apollo has 5 manufacturing plants in India. They are located in Ahmedabad, Raipur, Tumkur (Bangalore), Dadri, and Sikandrabad. There is another upcoming unit in Dadri. We aim to expand this reach even further.

What is the best brand of PVC solvent cement?

APL Apollo PVC solvent cement is the best solvent cement for joining two or more pipes and fittings. Some key benefits of using APL Apollo PVC solvent cement include its strong bonding properties, ease of application, quick drying time, and resistance to water and most chemicals. It provides a reliable and durable joint for PVC pipes and fittings.

Which brand water tank is best for home?

APL Apollo water tanks are among the greatest options for usage at home. They are renowned for their exceptional quality, robustness, and consistent performance. These water tanks are built to resist a variety of weather situations and are constructed from premium materials. According to their needs for water storage, homeowners can choose the APL Apollo water tank from a variety of sizes and capacities. In general, APL Apollo water tanks offer a dependable and durable option for household water storage needs.

Which PVC pipe is best for agriculture?

APL Apollo PVC Agriculture pipes are best for agricultural applications. They are known for their high quality, strength, and longevity, making them suitable for a variety of agricultural applications. APL Apollo PVC pipes are built to survive the harsh circumstances of irrigation systems, water delivery, and drainage in agricultural fields. These pipes are resistant to corrosion, solvents, and UV radiation, ensuring long-lasting performance in outdoor conditions. Furthermore, APL Apollo PVC pipes have smooth inside surfaces, which aid in the effective flow of water and prevent the chance of clogging. Overall, APL Apollo PVC pipes are a dependable and cost-effective alternative for agricultural plumbing.

What are applications of PVC pipes?

PVC stands for polyvinyl chloride, PVC pipes are used for various purposes and in multiple sectors. These pipes are used for various works such as potable water supply or distribution, sewage, and drainage systems, irrigation systems, chemical handling, ventilation ducts, recreation works, and so on.
