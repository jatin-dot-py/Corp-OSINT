Making a Mark Globally

Filatex India Limited is today among the country’s leading manufacturers of Polyester Filament Yarn. With an entry into manufacturing in 1994 with monofilament yarn, today the company fulfils emerging garment needs of millions and adds extra mileage to India’s developing textile industry. We manufacture polyester and polypropylene multifilament yarn and polyester chips at our two production facilities. One at Dadra & Nagar Haveli and the second one at Dahej, equipped with state-of-the-art modern German machines.

The consistent and premium quality of our products has opened a plethora of opportunities for addressing global demand.
