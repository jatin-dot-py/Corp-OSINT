Years of Experience

Shaping the future of entertainment since 2007

Sunshine Pictures has been part of the entertainment world since 2007, creating films, producing web series and strengthening its presence in distribution. Today, the company brings every part of entertainment together – cinema, digital, music and distribution – all under one roof. It’s a space where stories are created, shared and carried to audiences everywhere, with each vertical working in sync to build a complete and modern entertainment ecosystem.

Our Founder

Vipul Amrutlal Shah

"Working with new talent is always exciting."

At the core of Sunshine Pictures is Vipul Amrutlal Shah, a filmmaker, visionary, and creative force behind some of Bollywood’s most enduring films. His journey from theatre to television to mainstream cinema reflects his belief in stories that move audiences both emotionally and socially.

As the founder and guiding spirit of Sunshine Pictures, he has built not just a production company, but a creative ecosystem founded on integrity, innovation, and teamwork. His direction has led the company through changing times, keeping its focus on quality, originality, and purpose-driven entertainment.
