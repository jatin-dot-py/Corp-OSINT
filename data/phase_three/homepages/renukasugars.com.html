our businesses

Sugar Milling

Sugar comes from the vast farmlands in the North, West and South of India. Renuka operates 8 sugar mills in the sugarcane-rich belts of Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra and Karnataka, where sugarcane is crushed to extract the juice, which is refined into pure crystals of sugar.

Know More
our businesses

Sugar Refineries

Renuka is one of the largest exporter of refined sugar from India. Over 1.38 Mn MT of raw sugar is handled at our state-of-art refineries. Upon refining, the pristine white refined sugar is further exported to the international markets like the Middle East, North and Eastern Africa, etc.

KBK Engineering
