About us - ITL Industries Ltd.

ITL (a BSE Listed Company since 1993) came into existence before 30 Years with its own indigenous design & developed India’s first Double Column Bandsaw Machines. ITL offers a wide range of Horizontal & Vertical Bandsaw machines with 52 different models having capacity from 100 MM to 3000 MM featuring Manual, Semi Automatic, Fully Automatic, NC & CNC models.
ITL introduced India’s first Carbide Circular Saw Metal Cutting Machine and offers 7 different NC models from 50 MM to 200 MM dia bar cutting to cater to the needs of Engineering Industries……Read More

Who We Are

We are in to the business of designing, developing and creating cutting edge custom built Engineering Equipments that create value for our customers by helping them redefining productivity & efficiency and in reaching their optimum profitability.
We do this through our team of dedicated professional and experts, who are our strategic assets, using latest & sophisticated designing and manufacturing technology.
Servicing has been a prime consideration for ITL Industries Ltd. We are able to provide servicing in all cities of the country through our Service network. Thus one is always assured of prompt after sales service. We have team of qualified and trained engineers with a through knowledge of all our machines and equipments. ITL holds huge inventory of spares & components for prompt supply and replacement at it’s head office, branches and the USA ware house.
