Welcome to our website

Kay Pulp and Paper Mills Limited was incorporated on the 15th of May, 1991 as a Private Limited company and became public limited company on 9th July 1993 promoted by Mr. Niraj Chandra, a third generation entrepreneur from Chandra Family

The name of company is changed to Kay Power And Paper Limited vide ROC letter No. ROCP/Certificate/2007/61709/1526 dated. 15.02.2008.

The Company engaged in manufacturing of MG Kraft Paper. It has an annual capacity of 21000 tonnes.

The Company also has a Power Plant having the generation capacity of 6 M.W.
