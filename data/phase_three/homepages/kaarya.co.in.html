About Us

Video Intro

Kaarya Facilities & Services Ltd.

KAARYA which means "work" in Sanskrit in its most basic form stands for our pride and dignity of work that we do.

We are a leading integrated Asset and Facilities Management company. We have a rich experience of offering our expertise to blue chip corporates, Government and SMEs. Incorporated in February 2009, we are also one of the fastest growing companies in our domain.

With a strong and professional management team and a culture of innovation, learning, quality and deep service orientation; we have become the partner of choice for organizations who need to focus on their core business and outsource other non-core operations.

From specialized Cleaning and Hygiene services, Housekeeping, Pantry, MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Handyman services - repair & maintenance), Pest Control, Landscaping, Guest House management to Façade cleaning; we have a bouquet of services that meet organizations’ asset and facilities management requirements.

HIGH IMPACT DIFFERENTIATORS

“We run facilities like regulated businesses, not manpower contracts.”

How do we do it

  • Central compliance office (labour, fire, environment, contracts)
  • Digital statutory compliance tracker (site-wise, expiry alerts)
  • Quarterly independent compliance audits
  • Insurance stack: public liability, WC, professional indemnity
  • Audit-ready documentation at all times
“We manage assets, not tickets.”

How do we do it

  • Site-wise asset registers with condition grading
  • Lifecycle costing & replacement forecasts
  • Predictive maintenance (trend-based, not just PPM)
  • Annual capex planning support for owners
  • Asset performance dashboards
“We are accountable for controllable costs.”

How do we do it

  • Energy baseline studies within first 90 days
  • Guaranteed or shared-savings energy programs
  • Chiller plant optimisation & BMS tuning
  • Water reduction and STP efficiency programs
  • Vendor consolidation & bulk procurement savings

Process & Operating Model

Onboarding Phase
  • Site survey & audit
  • Service-level agreement (SLA) definition & KPIs
  • Transition planning (handover from client or incumbent)
Delivery Phase
  • Implementation of hard & soft services
  • Desk & field team structure (national management + regional delivery)
  • Use of technology & dashboards for live monitoring
  • Vendor/sub-contractor management and quality oversight
Review & Governance
  • Monthly service reviews (with client)
  • Quarterly audits (internal & external)
  • Annual performance benchmarking & continuous improvement
  • Risk/Incident management framework
Improvement Loop
  • Data capture → analytics → identify gaps → corrective action → update process & training
  • Innovation roll-out (new technologies, sustainability initiatives, occupant experience enhancements)

SAFEGUARDS, AUDIT & MONITORING MECHANISM

  • Dedicated quality assurance team with defined audit checklists (hard/soft services)
  • Statutory compliance monitoring (fire safety, EHS, electrical, water, waste)
  • Daily/weekly checklists for front-line teams; field inspections by supervisory staff
  • SLA & KPI dashboards accessible to client, preventive maintenance compliance, energy consumption trends
  • Incident reporting & escalation matrix
  • Vendor/sub-contractor score-cards, quarterly performance reviews
  • Client satisfaction surveys, occupant feedback loops
  • Periodic benchmarking and best-practice sharing
  • Risk management: preventive risk assessments (asset failure, safety, regulatory) + mitigation plans
  • Governance forum: monthly client-review meeting
