ABOUT US

Welcome to Bedmutha Industries: a trusted steel wire and wire rope manufacturer in India, delivering excellence in steel wire, wire ropes and copper products since 1988.

Founded in 1988, Bedmutha Industries Ltd stands tall as the flagship company of the Bedmutha Group and one of the leading wire rope manufacturers in India. We manufacture and export an extensive range of high-quality products including SURE Steel Wire Ropes, Galvanized Wires, Spring Steel Wire, Tyre Bead Wire, Wire for Ropes, Galvanized Patented Wire, Phosphate Patented Wire, ACSR Core Wire, Cable Armouring Wire, Earth Wire, Stay Wire, Barbed Wire, Copper Bus Bars, Copper Rods, Copper Foils, Copper Wires, and Copper Cables.

At Bedmutha Industries, we are driven by an unwavering commitment to deliver products of the highest quality.Our commitment to excellence is fortified by our cutting-edge laboratories,

Ensuring precise and rigorous testing of our products. Bedmutha Industries takes pride in its products’ wide acceptance across diverse industries and segments, including Power Corporation of India Ltd., State Electricity Boards, Defense, Railways, Infrastructure Companies, and Automobile Manufacturers, among others. Our influence extends globally, with a robust supply chain presence across 21 countries in South East Asia, Europe, Africa, America, and Australia. The revered brands “SURE” and “COMMANDO” have established themselves as benchmarks of quality and trust within the Indian markets.    We stand tall with certifications from TUV Sud Certified IATF 16949, ISO 9001, 14001 & 45001, and various international and Indian statutory norms, reflecting our dedication to excellence. At Bedmutha, our products showcase cutting-edge technological advancements. We continuously push the boundaries of innovation to deliver solutions that meet the evolving needs of our customers. From advanced materials to precision engineering, our products embody the pinnacle of technical excellence. 

OUR VISION

Bedmutha Industries Limited envisions itself as a global leader in the steel wire and rope industry, driving transformative growth through innovation, sustainable practices, and unwavering commitment to quality. We aspire to be recognized for our excellence in engineering, contributing significantly to the advancement of infrastructure worldwide while fostering sustainable development and creating value for all stakeholders.

OUR MISSION

At Bedmutha Industries, our mission is to innovate and excel in the wire and wire rope industry. We prioritize customer satisfaction by delivering high-quality products that enhance safety and efficiency across various sectors. With a focus on sustainability, we drive product development and operational efficiencies while fostering trust and integrity in our partnerships. Through continuous improvement and technological advancement, we shape the future of wire rope manufacturing while contributing positively to our communities.
