Business & Products

Gravure Inks

Discover unparalleled color brilliance with DIC India's Gravure Inks. A perfect blend of tradition and innovation, our precision-crafted inks can elevate your prints to new heights. Backed by a legacy of reliability and excellence, DIC India is a brand whose inks transform printing into art, ensuring every creation makes a lasting impact. Precision, passion, perfection - experience it all in a stroke with DIC India Gravure Inks.

News Inks

Our News Inks provide dependable solutions for superior print quality and quick drying, meeting the rigorous demands of the news industry. DIC Ink India offers cost-effective, high-mileage inks customized for news printers, enhancing efficiency and reducing waste. Our UV news printing inks cater to the economical printing requirements of Standard and Glazed newsprint, available in slow-speed, high-speed, and high-speed UV options.

Offset Inks

DIC India excels in providing premium Offset Inks tailored for diverse needs. Ideal for packaging, books, magazines, marketing materials, calendars, posters, and playing cards, DIC Inks ensure vibrant, reliable prints. Trust DIC India's expertise for exceptional printing solutions that elevate the visual impact of your projects across various applications.

Flexographic Inks

DIC Ink Company offers a wide range of printing inks including flexographic inks, renowned for their strong adhesion, vibrant colors, and suitability for high-speed printing. At DIC color and comfort, you'll find water-based, solvent-based, and UV Flexo inks, ensuring the perfect match for your printing needs. Trust DIC's commitment to precision and comfort for excellent results on packaging, labels, and flexible substrates.

Lamination Adhesives

DIC India offers an extensive range of lamination adhesives that is ideal for all types of flexible packaging applications. We have an extensive range of high performance solvent-based and solvent-free lamination adhesives that offer excellent chemical and heat resistance with high bonding strength. For applications on lamination, metalized films, thermoformed chocolate packaging, polyethylene films, LDPE based / Transparent films lamination etc.

Speciality Inks

DIC Ink India is a leading printing ink manufacturer, providing an extensive range of specialty inks in vibrant colors and glossy/matte finishes. One of the best printing ink manufacturers in India, DIC offers high-quality options like Screen Ink, Industrial Ink, Metal Deco Ink, Print Varnish, and UV inks for printing on diverse surfaces such as textiles, paper, PVC, acrylic, metal cans, newspapers, and books.

DIC India is the World's Leading Provider Inks

DIC India Limited is a renowned name in printing inks and is one of the leading ink manufacturers in India. With a history spanning several decades, DIC India has established itself as a trusted provider of high-quality printing inks, including liquid and colour inks. DIC India's extensive range of printing inks caters to a broad spectrum of industries, including packaging, publishing, commercial printing, and more. The company's liquid inks offer superior print quality, excellent adhesion, and enhanced durability, making them ideal for various substrates.

DIC Printing Ink: Search by Application

Board Packaging

We provide superior quality inks for offset packaging that offers high-quality imagery with no streaks or spots, excellent print depth, gloss and better picture clarity. Suitable for mono cartons, liner-cartons, food packaging cartons rigid boxes and tobacco packaging.
Lamination Packaging

DIC’s high-quality gravure ink is the ideal ink if you want to print on retort packaging as it allows sterile packaging of a wide variety of food and drink handled by aseptic processing, and is used as an alternative to traditional industrial canning methods.
Blister Packaging

DIC offers inks for printing on blister packaging for the pharmaceutical industry. The inks provide perfect print finish, crystal clear impression and excellent shine.
News Print

DIC India provides news inks that have been carefully crafted to meet the critical aspects of newspaper printing. Choose from our range of slow speed, high speed and high speed UV ink. You can use slow-speed news ink for low to standard quality news-papers and book printing. The high-speed ink is suitable for high-speed news printing, whereas the high-speed UV news ink works wonders for high-speed newspaper printing with high gloss effect.
Printing polyolefin films

DIC India offers a range of inks exclusively formulated with high Mol. wt. non-migrating resins. The inks have a good heat resistance of upto 180 Degrees with good scuff and slip resistance which is ideal for printing of liquid milk pouches or oil resistance products. Choose DIC’s inks for flexo printing suitable for a wide variety of substrates.
Printing on HDPE

HDPE sheets are compatible with screen printing and offset-lithography. Before you attempt to print on HDPE material please contact DIC India to see what types of inks can be used with HDPE sheets.
Self-Adhesive Labels

Ideal for businesses dealing in self-adhesive labels, DIC India provides high-quality inks for printing on labels with a gloss surface. The inks are best-fit for waterproof labels, metallic labels, or fluorescent labels.
Printing on pouches

DIC India offers a full range of inks that are ideal for the manufacturing process of Printed Circuit Boards. The range consists of Etch Resist (alkali soluble acid resist), Two pack Solder Resist) and Two pack Board Marking Inks (black and white).

Frequently Asked Questions

What Are the Environmental Benefits of Using DIC's Eco-Friendly Inks?

DIC's eco-friendly inks offer several environmental benefits, including lower levels of volatile organic compounds (VOCs), reduced emissions, and compatibility with sustainable printing practices such as water-based formulations and UV-curable inks. These eco-friendly options contribute to minimizing environmental impact and promoting sustainable printing practices.

What Innovations Has DIC India Introduced in the Realm of Printing Inks?

DIC India has introduced several innovations in printing inks, including advancements in pigment dispersion technology, the development of eco-friendly formulations, the introduction of specialty inks for unique printing applications, and the integration of digital printing technologies to enhance print quality and efficiency.

How Does DIC Support Clients in Adapting to Changing Market Demands?

DIC India supports clients in adapting to changing market demands by providing technical expertise, customized solutions, and responsive customer support. This includes offering training programs, conducting research and development to address emerging trends, and collaborating with clients to develop innovative printing solutions that meet evolving market needs.

What Types of Support Services Does DIC Offer for Their Products Post-Purchase?

DIC offers various support services for its products after purchase, including technical assistance, training programs, troubleshooting guides, and customer service support. Whether it's providing guidance on ink selection, optimizing printing processes, or addressing specific challenges, DIC is committed to supporting customers throughout their entire product lifecycle to ensure successful outcomes and satisfaction.

How Does DIC's Commitment to Sustainability Influence Its Ink Formulations?

DIC's commitment to sustainability is reflected in its ink formulations that are developed with eco-friendly products to reduce environmental impact. This includes the use of renewable resources, reduction of volatile organic compounds (VOCs), development of low-migration inks for food packaging, and implementation of recycling and waste reduction initiatives throughout the production process.

In What Ways Does DIC Innovate to Meet Evolving Print Quality Demands?

DIC innovates to meet evolving print quality demands by investing in research and development, adopting new technologies, and continuously improving ink formulations. This includes advancements in pigment dispersion, color accuracy, print resolution, and compatibility with various substrates to deliver superior print quality that meets the demands of modern printing applications.

What Types of Printing Can Benefit Most from DIC's Range of Inks?

DIC's range of inks is suitable for a wide variety of printing applications, including packaging, labels, commercial printing, newspapers, magazines, and specialty printing. Whether it's flexographic, gravure, offset, digital, or screen printing,DIC offers high-performance inks tailored to meet the specific requirements of each printing process.

How Does DIC Address Safety and Environmental Concerns with Its Ink Products?

DIC addresses safety and environmental concerns with its ink products by adhering to strict regulatory standards, implementing comprehensive quality control measures, and continuously improving production processes to minimize environmental impact. DIC prioritizes the safety of its employees, customers, and the environment in all aspects of ink manufacturing and distribution.

What Role Does DIC Play in the Development of Eco-Friendly Printing Solutions?

DIC plays a leading role in the development of eco-friendly printing solutions by investing in research and development of sustainable materials, reducing carbon footprint throughout the supply chain, and promoting recycling and waste reduction initiatives. DIC's eco-friendly inks are designed to meet the growing demand for environmentally-responsible printing practices without compromising on performance or quality.

How Does DIC Ensure Consistent Color Quality Across Large Print Runs?

DIC ensures consistent color quality across large print runs through meticulous quality control measures, standardized manufacturing processes, and advanced color management technologies. By maintaining strict adherence to color specifications and using state-of-the-art equipment for color matching and monitoring, DIC ensures that every printed piece meets the desired color accuracy and consistency standards.

How Do DIC Products Drive Innovation in the Printing and Packaging Industries?

DIC products drive innovation in the printing and packaging industries through advanced formulations, cutting-edge technologies, and continuous research and development efforts. By offering high-performance inks, coatings, and materials, DIC enables printers and packaging manufacturers to achieve superior print quality, efficiency, and sustainability, thus driving innovation and pushing the boundaries of what is possible in the industry.

What Are the Flagship Products of DIC That Set Industry Standards?

DIC has several flagship products that set industry standards, including Sun Chemical's SunLit™ sheetfed offset inks, SunVisto® AquaGreen water-based inks, and SunVisto® Advantage Opaque inks. These products are renowned for their exceptional performance, reliability, and environmental sustainability, making them the preferred choice for printers and converters worldwide.

Can DIC Provide Custom Product Solutions Tailored to Specific Industry Needs?

Yes, DIC can provide custom product solutions tailored to specific industry needs through its extensive expertise, technical capabilities, and collaborative approach with customers. Whether it's developing specialized inks, coatings, or materials, DIC works closely with clients to understand their requirements and deliver customized solutions that meet their unique challenges and objectives.

How Do DIC's Products Contribute to More Sustainable Manufacturing Processes?

DIC's products contribute to more sustainable manufacturing processes by offering eco-friendly formulations, reducing energy consumption, minimizing waste generation, and promoting recycling and resource conservation. By developing low-VOC inks, water-based coatings, and bio-based materials, DIC helps printers and converters adopt more environmentally responsible practices without compromising on performance or quality.

What Range of Industries Do DIC Products Serve and What Needs Do They Address?

DIC products serve a wide range of industries, including printing, packaging, automotive, electronics, construction, textiles, and more. They address various needs, such as vibrant color reproduction, durable and long-lasting prints, compliance with regulatory standards, and sustainability requirements across diverse applications and markets.

How Do DIC's Research and Development Efforts Influence Their Product Offerings?

DIC's research and development efforts play a crucial role in influencing its product offerings by driving innovation, improving performance, and addressing emerging market trends and customer demands. Through continuous investment in R&D, DIC develops new technologies, enhances existing products, and introduces breakthrough solutions that set industry standards and push the boundaries of what is possible in printing and packaging.

In What Ways Are DIC Products Pioneering in Environmental Responsibility?

DIC products are pioneering in environmental responsibility by incorporating sustainable materials, reducing carbon footprint, and promoting eco-friendly manufacturing practices. Whether it's developing low-emission inks, recyclable packaging materials, or renewable coatings, DIC is committed to minimizing environmental impact and promoting sustainability throughout the product lifecycle.

What Are Some of the Breakthrough Products Developed by DIC in Recent Years?

Some breakthrough products developed by DIC in recent years include Sun Chemical's Eco-friendly inks, DIC's water-based coatings, and SunVisto® Advantage Opaque inks.
