Welcome to our site...
B & A Limited - The Flagship Company of B&A Group - a listed company in Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) founded by Late Hemendra Prasad Barooah in 1950, was originally incorporated as Barasali Tea Company in 1915 in the state of Assam.

The company is currently engaged in the production of CTC tea in its 8 quality tea estates namely - Barasali, Gatoonga, Kuhum, Mokrung, Salkathoni, Samaguri, Sangsua and Moheema which are located in Golaghat, Jorhat and Sibsagar district of the State of Assam.
