We believe the future of food is blue.

As the world grows, sustainable aquaculture isn’t optional, it is essential.

At Sharat Industries Limited, we’re committed to nourishing future generations with traceable, healthy shrimp. Through complete value-chain stewardship, we work end to end to deliver quality with care.

We operate an end-to-end shrimp supply chain across feed, hatchery, farming processing & exports

Our four divisions work in sync to ensure quality— from feed to processing to exports. While hatchery and farming are now outsourced, our legacy in these areas helps us choose the right partners and maintain full value chain integrity.
