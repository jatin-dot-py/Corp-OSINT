Galaxy Medicare Limited


Galaxy Medicare Limited is one of the top manufacturers of medical devices in Bhubaneswar, India. We have a group of knowledgeable and experienced employees who strive to give our customers complete product pleasure. To ensure the efficient operation of the various business activities, our team closely coordinates and works in their particular areas of competence. Different departments cooperate and help us manage our company successfully and efficiently.


We are an ISO 9001:2015 & ISO 13485:2016 certified Company for the manufacturing of surgical dressings, plaster of paris (POP) bandages, adhesive tapes, compression bandages as well as wound care products. The company is dedicated to produce and deliver healthcare products following legal regulations to satisfy the demands of the medical community and total customer satisfaction.
