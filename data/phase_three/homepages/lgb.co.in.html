Welcome to
L.G.Balakrishnan & Bros Ltd.

A Heritage of 8 decades

LGB founded in 1937 as a Transport Operator is now 84 years young today. LGB has become the first Indian Company to supply Timing Chains to 4 Wheeler OEMs in India and is the largest timing chain exporter from India to the U.S. Today. LGB is the No. 1 OEM supplier of 2 Wheeler Drive Chains, which holds largest market sharing OEM and  Replacement market.

Today LGB Stands proud as premier manufacturer of Automotive Chains, Sprockets and Chain Tensioners under popular brand name ‘ROLON’. LGB also produces world class Fine Blanked Products and Precision Machined Gears and Parts. The company has 20 chain manufacturing plants, all ISO / IATF certified.

All in One Roof! Everything In House!

L.G.B is a leading player in transmission products with its Automotive Timing and Drive Chains, Sprockets, Auto Tensioners, Guides, Fine Blanked Components, Precision Machined Parts, Belts & Rubber Products
