About Duropack Limited

Established in 1986, Duropack Limited is a leading Indian manufacturer specializing in high-quality flexible laminated packaging solutions.

Our Rewari facility features advanced imported machinery, including Roto Gravure printing, pouching, lamination, spout fixing, slitting, and inspection, ensuring precision and excellence in packaging solutions.
