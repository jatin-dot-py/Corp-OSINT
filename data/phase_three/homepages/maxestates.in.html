Max Estates Limited

More About Us

Max Estates is a leading Real Estate developer in Delhi NCR. Established in 2016, Max Estates aspires to build sustainable commercial and residential developments in Delhi NCR, with a focus on well-being. Max Estates in its pursuit of emerging as the most preferred brand in real estate in NCR is driven by our vision to enhance quality of life through the spaces we create.

Skipping Ahead

Philosophy

The well-being of all our stakeholders is at the core of our operating philosophy. Our focus on well-being permeates the entire process, from design to execution, and operations, leading to an unparalleled experience.

Guided by our philosophy of WorkWell and LiveWell, we aspire to create differentiated working and living experiences, in the commercial and residential segments, respectively. We ensure this by moving beyond the conventional separation of work, life, and well-being, and paying attention to the entire spectrum of wellness across physical, emotional, social, and environmental aspects.

Empathetic Hospitality

An intelligent approach to hospitality and service values empathy. We understand the emotional and social needs of our customers, and create spaces where they feel cared for.

Environmental Harmony

We focus on holistic well-being, with immense attention paid to every element. Focus on elements like air, water and biophilia through interventions that aim to provide fresh air, water, and integrate green designs enable us to build developments that prioritise our users.

Generosity

We believe in going beyond the required norm, to build generosity of time, space, and attention to detail into our spaces.

Intentional Design

Our interventions are carefully to promote a healthier lifestyle, with the help of technology and design. We offer comfort with the right ergonomics, and thermal considerations. We integrate aesthetics with functionality, by including carefully curated artwork in our developments and encourage physical movement by building mobility into our spaces.

Inclusivity

Our spaces are designed for the well-being of all in mind, irrespective of age, gender, ability and circumstances.

Peace Of Mind

Our endeavor is to enable well-being for our users, in all ways possible. By carefully choosing well connected locations, opting for highest standards of sanitation and, and ensuring safety measures are in place, we build our users’ peace of mind into our developments.

Belonging

Our developments promote social well-being by integrating amenities and events that promote engagement and interaction, leading to a true sense of community.

Sustainability

Safeguarding the environment through energy and resource efficiency is the core principle of its sustainable development policy.

Food and Nurtition

We care for the health and well-being of our customers by offering easy access to organic vegetables and biotic food sales. We also offer curated F&B options with focus on quality & nutrition.
