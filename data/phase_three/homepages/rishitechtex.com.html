About Our Company

Rishi Techtex Limited is a global standard manufacturer

of PP/HDPE woven bags, technical fabrics, and advanced knitted shade net solutions. With more than 30 years of experience, we are trusted by leading industrial, agricultural, and infrastructure companies across India and worldwide.

Our world class manufacturing facilities in Daman and Vapi operate with a combined capacity of 10000 MT annually, empowering us to deliver precision engineered technical textiles that meet international quality benchmarks.

To manufacture world class technical textiles that provide customers with superior performance, value, and innovation through advanced technology and uncompromising quality standards.

To emerge as a high value, specialty, science-led technical textiles company with a strong domestic and global presence.

We are committed to trust, innovation, integrity, and continuous improvement through R&D driven development and ethical business practices.
