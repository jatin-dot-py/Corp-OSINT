ABOUT GRM

Welcome to the World of 'GRM'

GRM Rice came into existence in 1978, and our passion of providing the world with the finest food products has only increased since then. Our products are trusted in thousands of kitchens around the world.

Over the years, we have challenged conventions, led innovations, and shaped cultures. Combined with persistence, our promise of quality has paved the path for growth and success.

10X BRAND FOR INDIANS

AS THEY DESERVE NOTHING LESS…

The launch of GRM’s flagship Indian Brand—10X marks a pinnacle in the company’s expansion. The vast portfolio of “10X” entails varieties of rice consumed across India, whole and blended spices along with a positive prospect for pulses. Through this venture, GRM intends to provide the Indian consumers with a basket of food staples enriched with goodness.
