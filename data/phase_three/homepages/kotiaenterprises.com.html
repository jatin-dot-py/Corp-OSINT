Kotia Enterprises Limited

Kotia Enterprises Limited was originally incorporated on July 19th, 1980, as "International Pumps And Projects (India) Private Limited" under the provisions of the Companies Act, 1956 with the Registrar of Companies, NCT of Delhi and Haryana. The business of the company was to execute turnkey projects, marketing of pumps, Motors, Switch gears and other allied engineering goods, servicing thereof and deal in their spare parts and to manufacture or get manufactured, process, design, assembled, repair or otherwise deals in all kind of submersible-Pumps and other types of Pumps of any variety or description. Later, in year 1994 it was converted into a public company and the name was changed to “International Pumps And Projects Limited" vide a Fresh Certificate of Incorporation dated December 15, 1994 issued by the Registrar of Companies, NCT of Delhi & Haryana, consequent to change of name on conversion to Public Limited Company. In year 2014, on 05th December, the company got listed on Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) and later on February 01, 2016 got listed on Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). In January 2017, the name of the company was changed to Kotia Enterprises Limited with effect from the date of issuance of certificate of incorporation dated 31st January, 2017.

Our Services & Sites

Civil Construction

We undertake and construct, develop, maintain, operate, own and transfer infrastructure facilities including housing, roads, highways, flyovers, bridges, airports, ports, rail systems, water supply projects, irrigation projects, inland water ways and inland ports, water treatment systems, sea water desalination plants, reverse osmosis systems, under ground drainage systems, solid waste management systems, tertiary treatment plants, sanitation and sewerage systems or any other public facilities of a similar nature; any project for generation and/or distribution of electricity or any other form of power; and any telecommunication services and to deal with the same in any manner whatsoever in India.

Cotton and Fabrics

Cotton plays an important role in the Indian economy as the country's textile industry is predominantly cotton based. The cotton industry is second-largest employer in the country after agriculture. We carry on the business in India of dealers, distributors, stockists, agents, purchasers, sellers, importers, traders of all kinds and description of hosiery goods, readymade garments, wearing apparels, knitwears, cloth, blankets, bad sheets, track suits, T-shirts, shawls, mufflers, socks, under garments, durries and carpets made of all kinds and description of woolen, cotton, Acrylic, silk, artificial silk, jute, angora, nylon, shoddy, mohair, polyester and fabrics etc.

Pulp

We carry on the business of trading in all kinds and classes of paper, board and pulp including writing paper, printing paper, absorbent, paper, newsprint paper, wrapping paper, tissue paper etc.
