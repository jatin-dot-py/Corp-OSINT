Dhunseri Tea & Industries Limited (‘DTIL’) is engaged in manufacture and sale of tea. DTIL has been in the business of manufacture of Tea for the last several decades. It has been expanding its business operations and it presently has 9 Tea Estates and its Tea Factories in Assam. It also has 2 Tea Estates in Malawi where plantation of Tea and Macadamia are being undertaken.
