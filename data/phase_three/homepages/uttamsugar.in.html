Sweetness in a nutshell

Uttam Sugar Mills Ltd. is among the leading producer of Double Refined Sugar in the Country. With extensive Research and Development, Uttam Sugar is now producing the best quality sugar in the country. After entering into packaged sugar, company has now moved from ‘Selling a Commodity’ to ‘Marketing of a Product’. We have ten folded our production in the last ten years with 2,000 TCD in 2001 to 20,000 TCD in 2011. Since our inception, we have striven to achieve total customer satisfaction. It is the policy of all Group Units to strictly follow the tenets of Uttam Quality.
