About Shangar Decor

The Best Event Planner

Shangar Décor is a well established company in Ahmedabad which was incepted way back in 1995. It has years of proven expertise in providing its clients décor related services that range from – Pre-wedding events, Theme wedding, Corporate events, Religious events, Property décor, Lights décor and Catering. All these services are the best in their respective categories.
At Shangar Decor Ltd., we’re driven by the fact that the world’s most successful brands deliver proof on their brand promise at every touch point. Because how brands act is more important than what they say.
With 40+ years in the industry, we believe that happy clients are truly the rubber stamps in the success of any organization and ours is no different which is why we believe that companies aren’t B2C or B2B – they’re B2E (brand to everyone).
