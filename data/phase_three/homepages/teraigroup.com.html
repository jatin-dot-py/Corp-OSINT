 About Us

Terai Tea Company Limited is the flagship Company of Terai Group of Companies. The company was incorporated in the year 1973 and was having only one tea estate namely Bagdogra Tea Estate with an installed capacity of 5 lacs kgs of made tea per annum. At present the group under the able and dynamic leadership of Dr Ajit Kumar Agarwala is the owner of seven tea gardens and five Bought Leaf Factories with a combined annual capacity of 15 Million kgs of made tea. The units of the Group are scattered all over North Bengal around the town of Siliguri.

The company’s shares are listed at Bombay Stock Exchange and also at Calcutta Stock Exchange since 1995. One of the Company’s unit is also a government recognized ‘Export House’. The company had popular brands namely ‘Terai King’ ‘Terai Queen’, ‘Terai Rungneet’ , ‘Terai Monarch’ ‘Terai Maharaja’ ‘Terai Snow view’ all over Russia. The Group is also engaged in export of various merchandise to Bangladesh and other Middle East Countries. Several Real Estate projects are undergoing at present in the Town of Siliguri.

The Company is run by professionals having rich experience in various fields. The Group is one of the most progressive and fastest growing and multi disciplinary conglomerate in North Bengal having an annual turnover over Rs.200 crores. It enjoys leadership position in the business of tea manufacturing and processing . Terai also has a unique spectrum of offering in not only tea plantations and processing units but has also diversified interest in the fields of tea manufacturing , tea export, garment export, food processing, real estate development , Steel industries along with other interest.

The three V’s Vision, Velocity & Values – form the core of every activity at Terai Group. The success of the company stems from the strong philosophy of its top management which believes in building ever lasting relationship with customers. Adding strength to this belief is the support of a team of highly dedicated professionals and technocrats, who lend their expertise to the company.

The group is enjoying banking facilities from Central Bank of India, Bank of India, Yes Bank and UCO Bank as multiple banking arrangement. The relations of the group with their bankers are excellent. Terai Group is in the process of setting up Solar Power Projects at Rajasthan and a jute mill in the out skirts of Kolkata.
