About Xpro India

Legacy

Xpro India Limited forms an integral part of India’s largest and most reputed Industrial House with a global presence in manufacturing and trading activities – the BIRLA Group, a conglomerate comprising many divisions, each consisting of a number of publicly-listed Companies and headed by a member of the Birla Family.

These companies, sharing a common ancestry dating back over 160 years, are engaged in a broad spectrum of business activities and possess an acknowledged track record of achievements in their respective fields, while emphasizing integrity, sound management and a progressive attitude.
