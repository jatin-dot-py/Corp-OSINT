About Jullundur Motor Agency (Delhi) ltd.

JMA Group is a prominent player in the automobile spares and components industry, known for its extensive and diversified operations. With a workforce of approximately 700 employees, the company boasts a vast network of 91 branches and collaborates with around 75,000 dealers across India. This widespread presence ensures coverage across almost all vehicle parts, focusing exclusively on original equipment (OE) and premium quality brands.

The company offers a comprehensive range of products, including engine components, steering and suspension parts, brake equipment and friction materials, clutch and transmission products, bearings, and chassis parts. JMA Group's commitment to quality and extensive distribution network make it a significant entity in the automotive aftermarket sector in India.

WE LET OUR LEGACY SPEAK FOR US

The journey of Jullundur Motor Agency (Delhi) Limited (JMA) exemplifies perseverance, vision, and the transformative impact of leadership. Founded in 1927 by the late Shri Raizada Bindraban Sondhi in Amritsar, the company began as a humble proprietorship under the name Jullundur Motor Agency. Over the years, it evolved into a pioneering force within the Indian automotive industry, making a significant mark on the landscape of automobile dealership and distribution.

Under the stewardship of the late Shri Virat Sondhi as Managing Director, JMA expanded to 80 branches across India, solidifying its position as an industry leader. The company's success reflects the vision, dedication, and effective leadership of its founders and subsequent management, demonstrating the potential for growth through a strong work ethos and culture.
