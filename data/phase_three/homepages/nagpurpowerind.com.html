Nagpur Power & Industries Limited is a part of Khandelwal group of Companies, established in 1946 with business interests in a wide variety of manufacturing and service industries.

The Company mainly operates on Metal Recovery Plant and produces Low Ferro Manganese (Slag) through its profitable slag recovery process. However, there is no more re-usable waste of fine particles remaining in the manganese slag which was dumped at various places at the factory site which can be recovered. The technical consultants are of the same view that the entire stock has been depleted now and hence the company has disposed off the manganese slag recovery plant and closed the metal recovery operations.
The management of the company has foreseen this & thus entered another segment of business couple of years earlier via a majority stake in “The Motwane Manufacturing Company Private Limited”, now a subsidiary company and is looking to develop its business in electrical sector. The company is also looking at other business opportunities including monetizing its assets and redeployment of the same in better business opportunities.

SUBSIDIARY
THE MOTWANE MANUFACTURING COMPANY PRIVATE LIMITED
The MMCPL is an R & D based company and has developed various high technology test and Measurement products which would have applicability in the domestic and international markets. The company’s products are sold primarily to the power sector and energy intensive industries.

DIRECTORS

Mr. GAUTAM KHANDELWAL – Executive Chairman (Key Managerial Personnel)

Mr. Gautam Khandelwal holds a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from the University of Mumbai and has also pursued studies in Economics at the London School of Economics. He brings with him extensive business experience spanning multiple industries. He previously served as a Nominee Director on the Board of Punjab National Bank—India’s second-largest nationalized bank—appointed by the Government of India.

Mr. Gautam Khandelwal currently serves as a director on the board of the Informed Technologies India Limited which is a listed Company. In addition, he holds directorships in several private entities, including Gras Education & Training Services Private Limited, The Motwane Manufacturing Company Private Limited, Motwane Building Systems Private Limited, Motwane Digital Private Limited, Zeppelin Investments Private Limited, Testdata Technologies Private Limited, and Entecres Labs Private Limited.

Mr. VIRAT MEHTA – Independent Director

Mr. Virat Mehta is a graduate of the University of Delhi and holds an MBA in Marketing and International Business from the Fordham Gabelli School of Business, New York. He brings over 30 years of rich experience in the fields of communication and brand marketing. Throughout his career, he has been associated with leading advertising and brand development projects at top-tier agencies such as Ogilvy and JWT.

Mr. Mehta’s most recent role was with Nestlé India, where he served as Vice President – Communications for over a decade. He currently serves as a Director on the Boards of Informed Technologies India Limited and The Motwane Manufacturing Company Private Limited.

Ms. NIDHI SALAMPURIA – Director (Non – Executive)

Ms. Nidhi Salampuria holds a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) degree from the University of Mumbai and is a member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India. She possesses extensive experience in various areas of law, including litigation, contract negotiations, and corporate governance.

Ms. Nidhi Salampuria currently serves as a Director on the Board of Entecres Labs Private Limited. In addition, she holds the position of full-time Company Secretary at John Cockerill India Limited.

MR. SHYAM BAPURAO KANBARGI – Independent Director

Mr. Shyam Kanbargi holds a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree and brings with him over 45 years of extensive experience in the production of ferro alloys. He is currently serving as a Director on the Board of Informed Technologies India Limited.

MR. AJIT RANCHHODDAS KANTELIA – Independent Director
Mr. Ajit Kantelia holds a Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering (B.E. Civil) and has approximately 21 years of experience in the construction industry. His expertise lies in formulating strategic plans and establishing end-to-end operations with a strong focus on profitability. He is well-versed in contemporary construction methodologies and systems, ensuring adherence to industry quality standards.

Miss. Akansha Patel – Company Secretary and Compliance Officer (Key Managerial Personnel)

Akansha Patel Company Secretary and Compliance Officer holds a Bachelor’s degree in Commerce (B.Com) and a Bachelor’s degree in Law (LL.B.). She possesses one year of post-qualification experience in Secretarial and related compliance matters, including company law, corporate governance, and regulatory filings.
