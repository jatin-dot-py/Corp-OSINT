WELCOME TO CFEL

Confidence Futuristic Energetech Limited (CFEL), established in 2017, is a pioneering India-based company at the forefront of high-pressure industrial gas cylinder manufacturing. Listed on BSE, CFEL is renowned for its innovative approach and unwavering commitment to quality.

Adhering to global standards, CFEL produces a wide range of high-pressure seamless steel gas cylinders, including high- quality CNG cylinders and cylinders for industrial gases such as argon, helium, nitrogen, hydrogen, etc. Additionally, CFEL manufactures CNG cascades for mobile and stationary purpose.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing processes ensure top-tier product quality. With a focus on customer satisfaction, innovation, and operational excellence, CFEL is setting new benchmarks in the global market.

OUR MISSION & VISION

Mission:

To lead the industry in manufacturing high-quality, innovative seamless steel and Type 4 cylinders,

promoting safety and environmental sustainability. We are committed to exceeding customer expectations through continuous improvement, advanced technology, and exceptional service, while maintaining the highest standards of quality and integrity.

Vision:

To be the global benchmark in seamless steel and Type 4 cylinder manufacturing, recognized for our commitment to excellence, customer satisfaction, and sustainability. We strive to innovate and expand our capabilities, ensuring safety, reliability, and environmental responsibility in every product we deliver.

MARKET SCOPE

Industrial Gas Sector

Industrial Gas Sector

CFEL is a key player in the industrial gas sector, providing high-pressure seamless gas cylinders for various gases like oxygen, nitrogen, and argon. These gases are essential for numerous industrial applications, including welding, cutting, and medical uses.
Compressed Natural Gas (CNG)

Compressed Natural Gas (CNG)

CFEL manufactures CNG cylinders used in automotive and industrial applications. The demand for CNG as an alternative fuel source is growing, driven by its cost-effectiveness and lower environmental impact compared to conventional fossil fuels.
Green Hydrogen Market

Green Hydrogen Market

With the increasing emphasis on sustainable energy, CFEL is tapping into the green hydrogen market. This involves leveraging Type-4 cylinders for storage and transportation. Green hydrogen is gaining traction in industries aiming to reduce carbon footprints.
Renewable Energy Integration

Renewable Energy Integration

CFEL’s initiatives in solar energy integration reflect its commitment to renewable energy. This market focus aligns with global trends towards sustainable energy solutions and reducing dependence on non-renewable energy sources.
