OUR GLORIOUS PATH TO GREATNESS

Founded in 1991 as M/s. SKP Bearing Industries, our journey at SKP Bearing Industries Limited has been marked by continuous innovation and dedicated effort. Under the leadership of Mr. Shrinand K. Palshikar, our Chairman & Managing Director, we have evolved from a partnership firm to a distinguished public limited entity.
