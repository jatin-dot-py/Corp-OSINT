About Us

Manufacturer & Exporter of Dyes & Chemicals

  • Investment Strategy Development
  • Risk Management

The origin of Mahickra Chemicals Limited goes back to 1997, when Mr. Mitesh Gandhi (Chairman and Managing Director) and Mr. Ashish Gandhi (Whole-time Director) established "Mahak Dyechem Industries" with the main focus to manufacture and specialize in full range of Reactive Dyes.

At our core, we manufacture Dyestuffs, Pigments, and Specialty Chemicals with an unwavering commitment to quality, upheld by a dedicated quality control team. We specialize in a comprehensive range of Reactive Dyes branded as Nikazol and Nikafix, widely used in dyeing textiles. We operate four manufacturing facilities, two of which are dedicated to producing Reactive Dyes.
