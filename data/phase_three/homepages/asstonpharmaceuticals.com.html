ABOUT US

Asston pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd. is leading pharmaceuticals company established in 2019 by the Promoters Dr. Ashish Sakalkar, Mrs. Saili More & Mr. Sachin Badakh have experience of over 25 years in pharma industries for exporting of Healthcare Products to various countries. We Supply pharmaceuticals &Healthcare products in the form of Tablets, Capsules, Sachets & Syrup.

OUR VISION

To be a leading export company in the pharmaceuticals and healthcare industry.

OUR MISSION

  • To become international supplier of healthcare products through the introduction of wide portfolio of branded and generic products.
  • To create and sustained work culture that values people.
  • To be a focused and customer oriented company.

Overview

  • Asston Pharmaceuticals Pvt Ltd. has initiated the export of medicine in the pharmaceuticals market of West African, South African and Asian Countries.

  • We provide our customer with high quality and price affordable products in the form of Tablet Capsules , Syrup , Sachet  and Injection(LVP and SVP)

  • Asston works with WHO GMP approved manufacturing units for manufacturing the entire products range.

  • Asston also have the tie up with WHO , NABL Accredited Laboratory to asses the quality of the products

  • Asston has a set up of Regulatory department for all kind of technical support in terms of preparations of Dossiers as per the guideline , Formulation development.

  • Asston have strong commitment of business ethics and Strong vision to have a smooth approach attaining the desire goal.

  • Quality products and competitive price and commitment are the main area Asston believe in.
