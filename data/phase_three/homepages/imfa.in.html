Indian Metals and Ferro Alloys Ltd (IMFA) is India’s largest producer of value-added ferro chrome, and a trusted partner to stainless steel producers worldwide.

A sure-footed journey since 1961

Established in 1961 and based in the mineral-rich state of Odisha, Indian Metals and Ferro Alloys Ltd (IMFA) is India’s largest producer of value-added ferro chrome. The company produces best-in-class, cost-effective value-added ferro chrome through complete backward integration, encompassing captive chrome ore mining, and captive power generation.
