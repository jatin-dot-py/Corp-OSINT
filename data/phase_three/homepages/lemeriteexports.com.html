who we are

‘Le Merite’ is a renowned Indian Brand, known Globally for its Quality Textile products.

‘Le Merite’ has long standing customer relationship, Exclusive tie-ups and agents in top importing countries of cotton yarn & fabrics.

Bangladesh, China, Turkey, Portugal, Egypt, South Korea, Iran, Italy, Vietnam, Thailand, Peru, UAE, USA… to name a few.

‘Le Merite’ is exporting to almost 40 countries. Our annual export revenue is more than INR 4000 million and we aspire to achieve INR 8000 million within 2 years.
