Vision & Mission

To be a world leader in industrial packaging by providing one- stop solution through continuous innovation and total employee involvement.

BLISSGROUP:

Stanpacks (India) Limited is a unit of BLISS Group of companies. This Chennai based group is a pioneer in the field of packaging having served the industrial packaging industry for more than 5 Decades. The group that was started as a finance company in 1966 has since diversified its activities to the industrial sector from 1970 onwards by reviving the sick packaging unit.
