About Our Nawa Refinery

North India's First Ever Salt Refinery

SSCL's Refinery in Nawa, Rajasthan, holds a historic milestone as North India’s first-ever salt refining plant. Established in 1992 with a vision for purity and excellence, this state-of-the-art facility has been a pioneer in salt processing, setting industry benchmarks for quality and innovation.

Saboo Sodium Chloro Limited was incorporated as a Public Limited Company on 22nd November, 1993, with the explicit goal of establishing North India’s first and largest fully integrated salt refinery, capable of producing one lakh tons per annum, by leveraging the very latest technologies worldwide to refine salt to an exceptionally high degree of purity.
