Welcome to Tarini Group of Companies


V.CHANDRASHEKHAR, a first generation entrepreneur from the family of the Defence background, established “Tarini International” in his start up days in the year 1995. Since the inception of the mother Company, there has been no looking back and with its vast experience and contemplation over the successful years, the “Tarini” nurtured into “Tarini Group” and has emerged as one of the leading Group Companies mainly involved in hydro consultancy , hydro power generators,hydro power turbines, hydro projects in africa, hydro projects in india. Development of small and medium hydro power projects, high voltage electricity transmission and distribution (T&D) works on turn key basis ( including concept, design, engineering, erection and commissioning) and technical consultancy services in related areas.
The Group is in its nascence and is hitherto engaged in prospecting for new projects and securing allotments through competitive bids worldwide in which it has been quite successful. The aggregate value of the domestic hydro projects for which allotments are in place amounts to Rs 300 crores and that of other prospective projects globally in the pipeline would be about USD 250 million. The group has worked out equity tie-ups with financially sound investors in respect of each project. The respective project companies in whose names the projects stand allotted shall be taking up the implementation of these hydro projects in a phased manner.
