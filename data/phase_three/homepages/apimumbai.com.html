Automobile Products of India Limited (API) is a Company incorporated in the year 1949 and has been in the business of manufacturing and trading of the Automobile Products and Automobile parts.

Shares of the Company are listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange Limited. Company has diversified into other businesses over and above trading in the Automobile Products, like trading in commodities, Vehicle Management Services and Human Resource Consultancy.
