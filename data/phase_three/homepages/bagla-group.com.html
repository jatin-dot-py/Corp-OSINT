Bagla Group Legacy

Bagla Group specializes in the manufacturing of Thermoforming Films, POF Shrink Films, Tear Tapes, Adhesive Tapes, and various other packaging products.

In the last 38 years, we have supplied products worth more than 400 Million USD to over 2,000 customers in 50 countries across 6 continents.

With 7 factories across India, Indonesia and Paraguay and 2 warehouses in USA and Europe supported by 16 international sales offices we provide more than 500 SKUs in over 50 product categories.

Along with Six Sigma and over 100 internal quality check points across all processes, our factories are certified by BRC, FDA, ISO and many other international bodies.
