About Us

SVC Industries is a limited company with a net worth of approx. INR 285 crores. It was incorporated in 1989 and listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange in 1993. It has 260 acres of land in Chhata, Mathura with fully developed infrastructure.

The site has a first-mover advantage over the developments around the Western and Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor. Chhata railway rack point, which is around 4 km from our site, further it will be connected to the Western and Eastern Dedicated freight corridor.

SVC Agri-Business division is generating revenues in Warehousing, Agri commodities trading and is looking to set up units of sorting, grading, packaging and processing of wheat, wheat flour, rice, etc. Our project will provide the holistic agri solutions by plugging the gaps in the agri value chain in the district of Mathura, whether it means helping farmers increase their yield or diversifying their crop or helping corporates source high quality agri products at affordable prices, consistently. We plan to create scientific storage on the Agri platform on our land and set up agri processing factories to process the raw material produced by the farmers which helps to reduce the districts post-harvest losses. We supply the product to end users which could be food or non-food agri industries.
