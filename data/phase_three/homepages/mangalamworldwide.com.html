Bright Round Bars

Seamless Pipes

ABOUT

Mangalam Worldwide

Mangalam Worldwide is a trusted name in stainless steel manufacturing, with a legacy spanning over 80 years. Headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, we specialize in producing premium stainless steel products under our brands, Mangalam Saarloh and Mangalam Tubicore. Our state-of-the-art facilities in Halol, Changodar, and Kapadvanj boast a combined annual capacity of 1,80,000 metric tons, offering a diverse portfolio including Austenitic, Ferritic, Martensitic, Duplex, and Super Duplex grades.

We are committed to excellence, sustainability, and customer satisfaction, delivering tailored solutions for critical industries like oil and gas, aerospace, and infrastructure. Guided by values of integrity and innovation, we ensure top-notch quality with complete traceability through Mill Test Certificates.
