About Us

Milestone Global is manufacturer of superior granite monuments and memorials.

Established in 1991, we have been manufacturing beautifully designed granite monuments of the highest quality and exporting everywhere in the world.

Our world class facility is located in Hoskote, near Bangalore, in India. 

We also have offices in the USA and UK.

What Makes Us The Best

The World's Best Granite

We only choose and process superior granite from the best quarries in India and abroad

Excellent Craftsmanship

Our sculptors, cutters, polishers and supervisors have 25+ years of experience

Only Natural Stone

We have never used chemicals or enhancers on our stone - our monuments last forever

World Class Facility

Our plant is fully automated using the best machines and optimised processes
