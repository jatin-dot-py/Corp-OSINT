OEM / ODM

Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEM) sell highly customised products designed to suit a client’s specifications. Meanwhile, Original Design Manufacturers (ODM) produce their own products and essentially lease them out to clients on a private label or white label basis so they don’t have to invest in building their own consumer brand.

Deep Draw Metal Pressing Services

A more practical definition of deep drawing sheet metal is that it’s a metalworking process which pulls metal material into a specific shape and thickness. Deep drawing is usually a compound process that includes stamping – radial tension, tangential compression, and stretch-and-bend along the sides of components.

Plastic Injections Molding

Injection moulding is a manufacturing process that allows for parts to be produced in large volumes. It works by injecting molten materials into a mould (or ‘mold’ in the United States). It is typically used as a mass production process to manufacture thousands of identical items. Injection moulding materials include metals, glasses, elastomers and confections, although it is most commonly used with thermoplastic and thermosetting polymers.

About Us - Who we are

Comrade Appliances Pvt. Ltd.

Comrade Appliances Pvt. Ltd. was established in 2017 with the aim of providing Original Equipment Manufacturing (OEM) at affordable prices. We are a leading manufacturer and supplier of a wide range of Air coolers and Water geysers, Washing machines, and other home appliances which are available in different capacities and specifications. Our products are widely appreciated for their features like energy efficiency, durability, hassle-free operation, and low maintenance cost. With our state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities and a team of expert professionals, we are able to meet the diverse needs of our clients in the best possible manner.
