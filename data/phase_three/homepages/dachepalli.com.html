Knowledge Beyond Horizons

Dachepalli Publishers is one of the largest and most renowned publishing houses in India today. It has long been recognized as an important publishing center for the press. The first book it published was in 1908, and has since expanded its output to include a wide range of educational and academic resources from scholarly works and higher education textbooks, to school courses and digital resources for teaching-learning.
