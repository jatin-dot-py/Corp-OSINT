Euro Leder Fashion Ltd

Euro Leder Fashion LTD is a Public LTD Company involved in the production and Exports of Leather Jackets and finished Leather from Chennai, India. Euro Leder Fashion LTD started its pursuit to be the Leader in Leather Jacket manufacturing and Exporting of quality Leather Jackets and other Leather products in the year 1992 as a small export oriented unit in Chennai, India producing just over few thousand pieces of jackets.
