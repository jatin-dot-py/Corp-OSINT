Precision Engineering & Powering the Future

Who We Are

Founded in 1989, Balu Forge Industries Limited has built 37 years of engineering excellence, evolving into a global precision engineering company with integrated forging and precision machining capabilities. Driven by innovation, advanced manufacturing, and a commitment to quality, we continue to deliver high-performance solutions that meet the evolving needs of industries worldwide.

Today, we serve customers across 80+ countries with a diversified portfolio of precision-forged and machined components across defence, automotive, agriculture, industrial machinery, earthmoving equipment, oil & gas, railways, wind energy, aerospace, marine, and new energy mobility. With a sector-agnostic approach and continuous investments in technology and capacity expansion, we are well positioned to support the next generation of global manufacturing.
