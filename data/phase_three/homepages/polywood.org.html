Dhabriya Polywood Ltd.

Embracing Sustainable Living

The company was incorporated in the year 1992 with the brand name POLYWOOD manufacturing PVC profiles. With continuous growth and innovation, the company extended its product range: uPVC Windows & Doors, PVC Doors, uPVC Furniture Profiles, PVC Wall & Ceiling Panels. Fluted Panels, Charcoal/PS Wallpanels, Soffit Panels, SPC/Laminate Flooring, PVC Marble Sheets & moldings, Garden Fencing & more.

The company has a production capacity of 10,000 metric tons of PVC Profiles annually.

The Company can design and develop dies & tools for UPVC & PVC Profile.
