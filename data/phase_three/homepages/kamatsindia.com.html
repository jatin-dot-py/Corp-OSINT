    Kamats Legacy

    Every kitchen harbours its own secrets, with age-old handwritten recipes and unique spice mixes passed down through generations. Kamats Legacy, envisioned by Dr. Vikram Kamat, encapsulates the essence of these South Indian culinary traditions and brings them to the rest of the country. Kamats Legacy celebrates our roots by preparing food with love and the choicest ingredients, such as white butter, desi ghee, freshly prepared chutneys, podies, and traditional vegetables. Our health-friendly menu, inspired by ancestral kitchens, showcases the finest flavours from all the states of South India. Each element of Kamats Legacy, from the authentic cuisine to the carefully curated ambience and exceptional service, has been designed to offer a dining experience like no other. Guests are greeted and served in traditional South Indian style, ensuring a wholesome and immersive experience.

    KHAS

    KHAS (Kamats Hospitality Academy of Skill) is the education and skill development initiative of THE VITSKAMATS GROUP, dedicated to shaping the next generation of hospitality professionals. Built on over 90 years of hospitality excellence, KHAS combines industry-focused education with hands-on training, empowering students with the practical knowledge, professional values, and real-world experience needed to thrive in the global hospitality industry.

    Designed for aspiring chefs, hotel professionals, and hospitality enthusiasts, KHAS follows a unique learning approach where classroom education is complemented by extensive on-the-job training at VITSKAMATS hotels. With government-recognised programs, experienced industry mentors, and assured placement opportunities, KHAS bridges the gap between education and employment, creating confident, skilled, and career-ready professionals who are prepared to lead the future of hospitality.
