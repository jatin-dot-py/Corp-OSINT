Gokaldas
Milestones

A Tale of Partition

After India’s partition in the year 1947, Jhamandas Hinduja and his two brothers, Naraindas and Devkishendas, moved their Sindh-based silk trading business Gokladas Harbhagwandas Partners to India.

Since 1947

Gokaldas Exports is born

In 1979, the business was split among the three brothers, with Jhamandas Hinduja taking the lead of the major successor, Gokaldas Exports. The Company was subsequently run by his three sons, Madanlal, Rajendra and Dinesh Hinduja, who were involved since its inception.

1979

The Year of the IPO

The Company went public in 2005 and was listed on Indian stock exchanges.

2005

Achieving Milestones and Changing the Game

In March 2007, Gokaldas Exports Ltd crossed the Rs 10 billion sales milestone, a first for an Indian apparel company. Looking at the growing potential of the textile industry, Blackstone, the global private equity giant, acquired a controlling stake of approximately 70% in the Company

2007

Challenging Times for the Indian Apparel Industry

Over the next 10 years, until 2017, under the management and leadership of Blackstone, the Company confronted a changing business environment ushered in the abolition of quotas under the Multi Fiber Agreement and a growing apparel business in Bangladesh.

2007-17

Ushering a New Era

From 2017- 2021 the Gokaldas Exports embarked on a new mission to invest in modernised plant and machinery and expand the factories. The Company performed consistently on all service parameters earned the trust of its customers, building a formidable reputation.

2017

Resurgent Gokaldas

Post covid, the Company set out to deliver strong and consistent revenue growth, invested in new factories, entered new product categories, and expanded to new geographies.
