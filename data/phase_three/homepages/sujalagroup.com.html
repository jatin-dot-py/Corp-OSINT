ABOUT COMPANY

Welcome to Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd

The Company was incorporated as Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd on 18th December, 1981 under the Companies Acts, 1956 in the State of West Bengal. Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd obtained Certificate of Commencement of Business pursuant to section 149(3) of the Companies Act,1956 from the Registrars of companies, West Bengal.

The Companies is registered under Companies Act,1956 with CIN no. Is L51109WB1981PLC034381.

The registered office of our Company is Situated at 1A,Grant Lane,2nd Floor, Room No-202,Kolkata-700 012,West Bengal India Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd was formed with an object to carry on the business in investment in shares & Securities etc and obtained registration with RBI as a Non-Banking Financial Company ("NBFC") vide certificate no.B-05.05724 dated 02.07.2003

The Company primarily engaged in the business activities like loans and advances, acquisition of shares/stock/bonds/debentures/securities issued by government or local authority or other securities of like marketable nature, leasing, hire-purchase, insurance business. Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has classified the company as a 'loan company' in the category of Non-banking Finance Company.
