Welcome to Cryogenic OGS Limited, where engineering excellence meets innovation. We specialize in manufacturing high-quality equipment tailored for diverse industries including oil and gas, petrochemicals, chemicals, and liquor. With a solid reputation, we proudly serve a wide array of clients, including prominent terminal automation companies in India and enterprises listed among the top 500 companies in the Indian economy.

Currently our equipments are installed at 200+ locations in India as well as internationally.

At Cryogenic, we prioritize process safety, reliability, and compliance with both national and international standards, including API, IS, ASME, and ATEX regulations. Our commitment to excellence ensures that our equipment meets the highest industry standards.
