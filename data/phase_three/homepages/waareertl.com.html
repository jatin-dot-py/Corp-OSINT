Clean Power For Vietnam

Waaree successfully commissioned a solar power plant in Khanh Hoa Province in just 100 days. With a 49.5 MW capacity, it generates 78,600 MWh of clean energy annually, showcasing our expertise in delivering premium, cross-border, utility-scale EPC solutions with speed, precision, and reliability.

Floating Solar Solutions

Waaree RTL deployed a 1.1 MWp floating solar array over a man-made reservoir under the RUBAMIN Floating Solar Photovoltaic (FSPV) Project. With 5–15% higher energy yield compared to ground-mounted solutions, it marks a significant leap in solar innovation and sustainability. It also highlights our ability to implement complex solutions addressing challenges like land scarcity.

India’s Largest Solar EPC Project

Waaree RTL is collaborating with Jindal Renewables to pioneer India’s largest single-site solar EPC project of 2GW. The project is set to produce substantial clean energy, representing a significant step toward achieving environmental sustainability and fostering the adoption of clean energy solutions across the nation.
