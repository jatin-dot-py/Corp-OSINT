Welcome to Perfect Octave

At Perfect Octave, we are passionate about preserving, promoting and monetizing the rich musical heritage of India. Our mission is to bring the soulful melodies of traditional Indian music to audiences worldwide, while also supporting and nurturing the incredible talent of our musicians. We are dedicated to bridging the gap between Indian traditional music and Western music, creating a rich tapestry of sound that resonates with audiences around the globe.

Meet Our Visionary Leader :
Dr. Ratish Tagde

Dr. Ratish Tagde is the brain behind Perfect Octave. Dr. Ratish is a renowned Violinist who performed across the world in solo and fusion concerts. He is the President of Musicians Federation of India (affiliated to International Federation of Musicians, in Paris registered under UNESCO). Dr Ratish possesses a triple master’s degree with Gold Medal in Indian Music. Dr Ratish is also the founder of India’s first ever Classical Music Satellite TV channel named “Insync”.  

Dr. Ratish, in addition to being a Law Graduate, is a Fellow Member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India 1990 batch and possessing extensive experience of over 30 years handling most of the functions of corporate world. Dr. ratish is also an Independent Director on the Board of a few listed companies and he handled many listed companies, mergers, acquisitions, compliances, fund raising assignments.

Dr. Ratish Tagde was awarded Doctorate by the prestigious Swiss School of Business Management, Geneva for his remarkable thesis on “Digital Streaming Revenues for Traditional Musicians – Opportunities & Challenges”.
