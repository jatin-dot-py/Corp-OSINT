Who We Are

Avio Smart Market Stack Limited (ASMS) is a listed Indian company with a renewed strategic focus on financial inclusion, agri-tech, and health-tech.

Over the past few years, ASMS has expanded its banking partnerships, strengthened its village-level workforce, and identified high-growth opportunities in agri-tech and healthcare. Today, ASMS stands at the forefront of rural digital transformation in India.

Our Vision

To build the world’s largest agriculture data company and unified rural operating system powering food, climate, healthcare, and rural commerce across India.

Our Mission

To digitize every farmer, enable Smart Agri Stores across rural India, and transform agriculture into a profitable, technology-driven, and globally connected ecosystem.

Building the Most Trusted Impact Ecosystem in Rural India

From financial inclusion to agri and climate innovation. ASMS is redefining rural transformation. Our goal is to empower every farmer and community with access to finance, technology and sustainable opportunities - ensuring progress that reaches the last mile.

HEALTH-TECH DIVISION - Advancing Accessible Diagnostics

In addition to agri-tech, ASMS has strategically expanded into the high-growth health-tech sector through a dedicated subsidiary. The company has partnered with Huwel Lifesciences to address India’s critical molecular diagnostics needs.

Huwel Lifesciences has developed Quantiplus® TB Fast Detection Kit, India’s only low-cost, open-system RT-PCR solution for TB confirmatory testing. The product is ICMR-approved and aligned with the Government of India’s National TB Elimination.

This vertical positions ASMS to participate in India’s rapidly expanding public health diagnostics market.
