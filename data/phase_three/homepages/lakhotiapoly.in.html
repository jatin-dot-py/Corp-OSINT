Welcome

Our group was primary established in 1980 with a company called Lakhotia Textile, which was engaged in manufacturing of textile fabrics under dynamic leadership of shri D.N.LAKHOTIA. Twenty five years of growth, with expertise in textile industry and wide range of products, the group has diversified its product line from manufacturing of Yarns, Packaging Films, Printing Films, Chemicals and many more to come. Also we entered in other segments from our core business of textile to Packaging, Printing and Chemicals Industries..
