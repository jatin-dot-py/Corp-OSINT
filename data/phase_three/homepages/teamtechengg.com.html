Who are we?

SPECIALIST IN FORMWORK

A team of experienced professionals in the formwork industry came together to establish TEAMTECH, driven by a shared goal of transforming the construction industry. TEAMTECH aims to revolutionize the way formwork is used across the segments, promising unmatched quality, speed, and cost-effectiveness in every project. With a commitment to delivering High-standard formwork solutions, TEAMTECH ensures that its clients receive the highest quality products and services tailored to meet the evolving needs of the construction industry.

Headquartered in Hyderabad, with a centrally located state of the art production facilities in Hyderabad, Telangana, TEAMTECH is well-positioned to serve clients across India and globally. This strategic location enables seamless operations and swift delivery of formwork solutions world-wide.

Buy

Our formwork solutions combine modular standard systems for regular, repetitive structures with fully tailored designs for complex and irregular applications. The modular systems are engineered for speed, precision, and high reuse, making them ideal for all vertical structures, while our customized formwork is designed to adapt seamlessly to unique architectural and structural requirements such as curves, varying dimensions, and intricate layouts. Together, they provide the flexibility to handle any project scope efficiently, ensuring optimal performance, superior concrete finish, and cost-effective execution across both standard and challenging construction scenarios.

Rent

Our formwork rental solutions are designed to provide maximum flexibility without the burden of upfront investment. Ideal for short-term and project-based requirements, our rental offerings give you immediate access to high-quality, ready-to-use formwork systems—eliminating capital expenditure while ensuring speed, reliability, and performance on site. With quick mobilization, maintenance support, and scalable availability, we enable you to execute projects efficiently, optimize cash flow, and operate with a truly asset-light model.

refurbishment

The process of restoring used formwork panels by repairing frames and replacing worn surfaces so they can be safely reused across multiple construction cycles; this extends their lifespan significantly (reuse), while end-of-life materials can be recovered through recycling and reducing wastage. It supports sustainability by lowering raw material consumption, minimising environmental impact, and promoting a circular approach to construction practices.
