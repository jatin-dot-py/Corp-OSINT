Valson INDUSTRIES In Textile

BEST Yarn Manufactures INDIA


Valson Industries Limited, established in 1983, is an ISO 9001:2015 certified, leader in yarn manufacturing. We provide a diverse range of high-quality yarns, including Polyester Texturised, Twisted, Nylon Dyed, Cotton Dyed, Spun & Fancy Dyed, and more. Our commitment to quality, using premium raw materials from trusted vendors, ensures products that meet industry standards and can be customised to your needs.

Our yarns are known for colourfastness, durability, and fine texture, ideal for apparel, home furnishings, and industrial fabrics. With advanced infrastructure and a dedicated team, we've earned certifications and trust worldwide.
