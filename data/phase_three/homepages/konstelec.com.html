Our diverse clientele spans across a multitude of industries, encompassing Oil and Gas, Refinery, Steel, Cement, Pharmaceuticals, Textile, Hospital, Health Care, FMCG, and Commercial Complexes, both within India and abroad.

Our Key Projects

Hydrocarbon Oil
& Gas

Oil & Gas are declining finite resources. Their extraction is a complex process, carried out in remote and hazardous conditions. This requires extreme amount of skill, planning, and execution expertise. KEPL offers various Electrical and Instrumentation services for the Oil & Gas industry, including the construction and operation of the power supply system on site, capacity expansion and modernisation of refineries, residue upgradation and more.

Know More

Power

EPC projects for power plants warrant a unique set of skills, capabilities, and expertise. Safety, sustainability, performance, and efficiency are the critical factors defining the success of a power plant. At KEPL, we design and commission power plants and undertakemaintenance. ur scope of work includes supply and installation of parts to the testing and commissioning of equipment. Post-commissioning, a team of KEPL technicians is stationed onsite.

Know More

Steel & Cement

The Steel and Cement sector is the lifeline of a developing nation like India. KEPL holds a vast amount of experience and process maturity for this industry. Our range of offerings includes designing and executing power distribution systems, supplying, erecting, and commissioning electrical and instrumentation equipment, and undertaking electrical, automation and instrumentation projects.

Know More

Substation &
Switchyard

Substation and switchyard installation and maintenance are critical projects for any plant. KEPL offers services like LT and HT installation, substation installation, electrical installation, power protection system design and execution, civil work on turnkey basis, etc. We also liaise with the relevant authorities tosecure all the mandatory regulatory approval including that from the CEIG (Chief Electrical Inspector to Government).

Ports

KEPL's EPC expertise extends to specialized projects and requirements in Ports, Pharmaceuticals, Textiles, and Commercial Complexes as well.
