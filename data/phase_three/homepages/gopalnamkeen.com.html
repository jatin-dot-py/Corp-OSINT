Fake Scheme Alert

🚨 FAKE SCHEME ALERT 🚨

Issued in Public Interest by Gopal Snacks

We wish to inform you that fraudulent investment schemes using the name and logo of Gopal Snacks are being circulated on Telegram and other messaging platforms.

These scams falsely promise to double your money in five days and are unlawfully using our brand name, logo, and even employee images to appear credible.

Gopal Snacks has not created or endorsed any such scheme. This is a clear case of online fraud intended to mislead and exploit unsuspecting individuals.

We have reported the matter to the cybercrime authorities and filed a First Information Report (FIR) to initiate strict legal action against those responsible.

🔒 Please stay alert:

  • Do not click on suspicious links
  • Do not share unverified messages
  • Do not engage with unknown groups claiming association with Gopal Snacks

We urge you to spread awareness and help us protect the trust we’ve built over the years.
