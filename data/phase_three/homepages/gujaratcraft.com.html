WELCOME TO

Gujarat Craft Industries Limited (GCIL)

Gujarat Craft Industries Limited (GCIL), established in 1984 and listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange, is a vertically integrated manufacturer and exporter of PP/HDPE-coated woven products. With ISO 9001:2015 and BRC Certification, we uphold the highest global compliance standards, ensuring consistent quality and reliability across all our offerings.

Our diverse product line includes Flexible Intermediate Bulk Containers (FIBCs/Big Bags), PP/HDPE Woven Fabrics, Woven Sacks, BOPP Bags, PE Tarpaulins, Plastic Sheeting, HDPE Geo-membranes, Vermicomposting Beds, Fumigation Covers, GCIL HDPE Pipe, Lumber Wrap, Roof Underlayment, NFI Bags, and PP Ropes.

OUR MANUFACTURING PROCESS

Let's Experience Exclusive Quality

01
Extrusion & Tape Production
Convert polypropylene granules into high-strength tapes through extrusion and stretching.
02
Weaving Process
Interlace PP tapes on circular looms to create durable woven fabric.
03
Fabric Cutting & Printing
Cut, print, and stitch woven fabric into bags with optional lamination, liners, and other accessories.
04
Finishing & Dispatch
Apply treatments, conduct quality checks, pack, and deliver the finished product.
