OSIYA - Hotel | Resorts

We are a hospitality company and operate in the mid-priced hotel sector, consisting of the upper-midscale, midscale and economy hotel segment. Our properties are located in Rakchham, Kinnaur, Himachal Pradesh & in the state of Goa.

Rakchham village is around 10 km from Chitkul and around 12 km from Sangla and is located between the two. Our first hotel property started operations in FY 19-20. We also operate a café at Rakchham. All our hotels, cafes and restaurants are operated under the brand name “Osiya”. We provide authentic hospitality and give our guests a localized and memorable experience. We seek to cater to family as well as adventure loving guests and deliver differentiated yet superior service offerings, with a value-for-money proposition.

Our the portfolio of properties consist of the following

Osiya Shambhu Lodge at Rakchham – Leased Property

Osiya Shoshla Café – Leased Property

Osiya Rangnoo Rakchham Retreat - Leased Property

Osiya Tashi Dolma Home stay – Marketing & Branding

Osiya Baba Inn Guest Home - Marketing & Branding

Dona Sa Maria - Own Property

Hotel Projects

OSIYA Shambhu Lodge is the oldest hotel in Sangla Valley . It was primarily run as a Bed & Breakfast with 7 rooms in the early years. Having considerable success and in order to keep up with the changing times

the entire hotel underwent a renovation with added room capacity in order to serve all travellers. At present the hotel has 13 rooms which are divided into 3 categories to serve guests with varying range of Budget. Premium Luxury Rooms are with balcony and surrounded by mountains from all the sides with a breath taking view of the Sangla Valley.

OSIYA SHOSHLA CAFÉ was started in the smallest and most modest possible way conceivable as will be evident from its early pictures in the year 2018. Ever since its inception

it has been a focal point of all tourists visiting the Kinnaur Valley. From its humble beginnings the Café has come a long way to now being the most scenic and clicked after property in the entire Kinnaur Valley. Having started out as a small shack the café now boasts of a 2 storey building with a seating capacity of almost 100 people at its peak.

OSIYA RANGNOO RAKCHHAM RETREAT is the jewel in the crown. The construction was started in the year 2018 and has been finally completed in the year 2021 October. The Osiya family now stands complete with the commissioning of this project.

It has 11 wonderfully decorated cottages all made out of the local red stone along with the roof made out of local tiles. The cottages are with a fire place and the furniture again has been done locally from Reckong Peo with wonderfully carved Beds, Chairs and Tables.
