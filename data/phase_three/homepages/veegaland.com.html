Message from Kochouseph Chittilappilly

At Veegaland Homes, we believe a home should be more than a place to live; it should nurture well-being, foster connections, and bring people closer to nature. Guided by this vision, we create thoughtfully designed homes that combine modern comforts, quality craftsmanship, and lasting value. Our biophilic living spaces integrate natural elements into everyday life, promoting sustainable communities. Every Veegaland home reflects our dedication to excellence, innovation, and customer trust. As we continue our journey, our commitment remains unchanged: to create homes and communities that inspire better living for generations to come.

Kochouseph Chittilappilly
Founder, Vice Chairman
and Whole-time Director 

About

Biophilic Urban Homes

01.
Nature Woven Into Everyday Living

Experience homes designed to bring nature closer to daily life. Through thoughtfully integrated greenery, landscaped surroundings, and open spaces, Veegaland creates environments that foster well-being, comfort, and a deeper connection with the natural world.

02.
Spaces That Breathe

Designed to maximise natural light and ventilation, our living spaces encourage healthier, more refreshing lifestyles. Every home is planned to create a seamless flow between indoor comfort and the beauty of the outdoors.

03.
Contemporary Design, Naturally Inspired

Blending modern architecture with elements inspired by nature, Veegaland homes offer a harmonious living experience. Clean lines, functional spaces, and green surroundings come together to create urban residences that feel calm, balanced, and timeless.
