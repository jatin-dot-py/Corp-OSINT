NRB Bearings, India’s leading manufacturer of friction solutions, specialises in cylindrical and needle roller bearings. We pioneered solutions for global automotive giants. Our R&D Centre has driven our success and is expanding into industrial sectors like off-highway, construction, HVAC, robotics, machinery, and agriculture, with plans to enter aerospace and defence.
