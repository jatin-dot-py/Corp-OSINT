The Ascot

amenities

The Ascot Eatery

Enjoy air-conditioned dining with an authentic Gujarati thali for lunch and a buffet-style dinner served poolside. Step out to the open-air café for snacks and ice cream, or unwind at the permit bar with a wide selection of cocktails and beverages.

HEALTH CLUB

Our Health Club features a steam room, sauna, jacuzzi, and massage facilities, available to guests during designated hours. Designed for relaxation and revitalisation, it’s the perfect place to unwind after a long day — whether you choose a soothing therapy or simply a quiet moment in a serene, well-kept space.

INDOOR - OUTDOOR RECREATION

Our recreation facilities cater to all age groups, featuring a game room with table tennis, carrom, and chess, along with outdoor pools, a children’s play area, a badminton court, and a lawn for cricket and other activities.

Celebrate. Connect. Cherish.

Host your celebration in our versatile event spaces — equally suited to parties, gatherings, and milestone occasions. From intimate get-togethers to lively evenings, every detail is thoughtfully arranged for a seamless experience. With elegant setups, curated menus, and attentive service, it’s the ideal setting to celebrate, connect, and make memories that last.

DISCOTHEQUE : “THE RAINFOREST”

Inspired by Matheran’s lush green forests, this themed discotheque features the latest sound equipment, international-standard lighting effects, and a professional DJ spinning tracks of your choice. Step into an immersive, jungle-inspired night out where nature meets the beat.

INDOOR - OUTDOOR RECREATION

Getting Here

From Mumbai / Pune

Take a train to Neral Junction.
Mumbai: 90 km, ~2 hrs.
Pune: 100 km, ~2.5 hrs.

Neral → Dasturi Point

A cab takes you to Dasturi Point, the last motorable entry. Alternatively, board the heritage Toy Train (UNESCO-listed).

Dasturi → Usha Ascot

Choose your mode of transport — all routes wind through Matheran's ancient forest.

From Parking to Resort

Horse Ride

A red-earth forest trot, ~30 min

Toy Train

~20 min on the train, plus a short ~20 min walk

E-Rickshaw

Quick and eco-friendly, ~15 min

Hand Rickshaw

A timeless tradition, ~30 min

On Foot

A picturesque forest walk, ~45 min

Nearest Airport

  1. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport, Mumbai – approx. 100 km from Matheran. Travel time: 2–3 hours by road, depending on traffic.
  2. Pune Airport – approx. 125 km from Matheran. Travel time: 3 hours by road, depending on traffic.
  3. Navi Mumbai International Airport (Panvel) – approx. 50 km from Matheran. Travel time: 1.5 hours by road.
