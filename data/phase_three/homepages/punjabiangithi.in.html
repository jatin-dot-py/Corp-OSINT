Vegorama Punjabi Angithi Limited

Since its launch in July 2014, Vegorama Punjabi Angithi Limited has sparked an era of culinary innovation that celebrates the very essence of vegetarian food. The initial concept of a single shop in Paschim Vihar has grown into a gastronomic enterprise that is thriving and has expanded its Aromic trails to 27 cloud kitchens and 2 dine in restaurants across 5 cities in India, gaining a reputation for exquisite quality and variety.

Growth and Reach :
From humble beginnings, Vegorama Punjabi Angithi has experienced incredible growth. With an unwavering dedication to the highest quality and flavor, our brand has grown from a single store to an impressive chain of 29 stores. This exponential growth rate is testimony to our unwavering commitment to redefining the definition of culinary excellence.

A Journey Rooted in Purity. Crafted Through Hustle. Celebrated in Every Bite.

From One Man’s Vision to a Nation’s Favourite Chaap
Back in 2014, a quiet revolution began in the streets of Paschim Vihar, West Delhi. A young dreamer, Deepak Chadha, driven by the desire for flavourful vegetarian options, decided to change the narrative. Armed with nothing but relentless hustle, an unshakable belief in purity, and an old-school tandoor — Vegorama Punjabi Angithi was born.
