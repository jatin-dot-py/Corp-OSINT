Welcome to INCON
ENGINEERS LIMITED

INCON ENGINEERS LIMITED was established in 1970 as a design and consultancy organization. Over the years, the Company has expanded its activities into manufacturing and execution of turnkey projects.

LOW AND SUB-MICRON SIZE GRINDING

Incon Engineers emerged as a world leader in the area of Ultra-fine Grinding of powders, manufactuiring a wide range of Fluid Energy Mills. The Mills acheived have been recognized for their cost effective grinding solutions through improved design, ensuring consistent product quality, minimal product loss and optimum energy consumption.

INDUSTRIAL AND MEDICAL GASES

Incon Engineers has design and manufacuting capability in the area of Industrial and Medical Gas Manufacturing. Some of the significant acheivements in this area are:

  • Design and manufacture of India's largest Medium Pressure Carbide-to-water Acetylene Generator (125Cu.M/hr).
  • Design and manufacture of Nitrous Oxide Plants for Medical use.
  • Design and manufacture of largest Hot Air Generator having capacity of 40,00,000 K.Cal/hr.
