Welcome to Our Website

Chambal Breweries & Distilleries Limited (CBDL) was set up in the year 1985.


CBDL is engaged in trading of IMFL, Beer and Country Liquor.


Our aim is to deliver best quality liquor to the consumer.


The company is striving hard to improve cost efficiency, widen its reach to new consumer segments and consolidate further on its image. For alcoholic beverages industry, the significant risk factor is the purchasing power of individuals and non existence of brand loyalty. The company is ensuring that its products are available on the shelf at any point of time.
