About Us

Baba Food Processing (India) Limited. is a trusted and renowned name in the agro-products manufacturing industry, particularly across Eastern India. With our roots grounded in the culturally rich land of Bhagwan Birsa Munda, we take immense pride in delivering quality, tradition, and innovation through our products.

As a leading food processor, we cater to both the branded consumer and bulk food segments, offering a wide range of premium wheat flour products tailored to meet the diverse needs of households and businesses alike.

Our state-of-the-art infrastructure is built to global standards, ensuring seamless integration of advanced food processing technologies sourced from around the world. We are committed to quality at every step — from sourcing the finest, most nutritious wheat, to hiring seasoned industry experts who bring vision, experience, and integrity to the table.
