CCAL's focus is on the holistic well-being of the community in which we live,
work and grow. We invest in the environmental, academic, social and
economic well-being of the families in the area, celebrating success
together. Giving Back - Holistically! At CCAL, we understand that collaborations and corporate connections
should extend beyond the office and working hours to build a healthy
working culture, we conduct engagement programs to ensure that we foster
a holistic bonding between all our employees. Working Together - Effectively! Our mantra is, and always will be, growth through innovation and
sustainable practices. In 1985, CCAL Kalapet Plant became the first in India
to successfully implement a new, efficient, environment-friendly technology
for Chloralkali production. Today, this technology is mandatory for industrial
standard! Staying Ahead - Sustainably! CCAL, we believe that there's nothing quite as important as the world we live in and share with native flora and fauna. We strive to innovate technology and techniques to ensure that our footprint is as small as possible while delivering to stakeholders at all levels. Always. Protecting the Environment - Consistently! CCAL works to ensure that CCALmates work in a safe and healthy atmosphere. As a progressive firm in a traditional sector, we focus on diversity and inclusivity in our hiring practices. Growing Together - Exponentially!
