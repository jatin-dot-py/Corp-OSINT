Ashapura
Group of Industries

With a legacy of more than 60 years, Ashapura Group is a leading multi-minerals solution company, involved in processing and exporting of various industrial minerals including Bauxite, Bentonite, Kaolin, Ball clay, Iron ore, & Silica Quartz, etc. Renowned as one of the World’s 3rd largest producers of Bentonite and Bleaching Clay. Ashapura is also an integrated value-added products manufacturer supplying to Metals & Foundries, Oil & Gas, Petrochemicals, Edible oils, Paints, Automobiles, Construction, and related industries.
