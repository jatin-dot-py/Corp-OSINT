DECCAN POLYPACKS Ltd.(DPL)

  • DECCAN POLYPACKS LIMITED (DPL) is a Public Limited Company incorporated in the year 1984 in the State of Andhra Pradesh. The Company is part of the well established Group having diversified interests. having its manufacturing and registered office in Hyderabad ( now Telangana state)India.. DPL a listed company with a shareholder base of more than 7000+ shareholders. Its shares are listed at BSE Mumbai. DPL has a capacity to manufacturing and supply 3.5 million bags per month from its own manufacturing facility.
