Vision For the Future

Operating for over six decades, the group has transformed into a well-diversified family-run conglomerate. The goal for any company associated with Nagreeka has and will always be- providing quality products at an affordable rate. With the new generation of business leaders joining in, Nagreeka will continue to evolve, keep up with technology and enter new consumer-facing businesses to enhance more lives.

History

Nagreeka embarked on its journey in 1953 with modest trading operations under Mr. I. L. Patwari and his family. Following nearly six decades of experience; the company built a reputation in over 40 countries and emerged as a leading manufacturer and exporter of yarns, textiles, cotton and aluminium products.

History

Nagreeka embarked on its journey in 1953 with modest trading operations under Mr. I. L. Patwari and his family.

Following nearly six decades of experience; the company built a reputation in over 40 countries and today; it has emerged as a leading manufacturer and exporter of plastic-coated aluminium and steel tapes and laminates, yarns, textiles, cotton along with aluminium and plastic containers.

CSR

The Group runs multiple CSR activities. Most activities are under either the Nagreeka Foundation or the Patwari Charitable Trust.

The most notable being the all-female college in Rajasthan that has over 1000 students and the free educational classes (yoga, art of parenting, etc.) held in Bombay at the Gita Lounge.
