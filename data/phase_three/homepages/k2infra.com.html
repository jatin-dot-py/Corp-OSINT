About Us

We Build Infrastructure

K2 Infragen Limited (K2IL), the flagship company of the K2 Group, is an engineering and core infrastructure company which aspires to be a leading force working in all the domains of the industry. Established with a mission to realize the dream of a visionary, being built on holistic & ethical values, driven by state-of-art technology, the company is on the way to redefine the parameters of growth and contribute to India’s infrastructure landscape with unending commitment.
