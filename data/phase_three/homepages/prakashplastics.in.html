About Prakash Pipes

The company’s promoter, Shri Ved Prakash Agarwal, has been in the business of manufacturing PVC Pipes and Fittings since 1981. Over the years, Prakash has emerged as a trusted brand for PVC Pipes and Fittings in the market, owing to our utmost focus on quality. We manufacture a wide range of products such as Agri Pipes, Column Pipes, Plumbing Pipes, Casing Pipes, SWR Pipes, Garden Pipes and their related Fittings. Our products find application in irrigation, drainage, housing, and sanitation. Our plant at Kashipur, Uttarakhand, is spread across 60 acres of land area, with more than 50% area being utilized for forestation and plantation.
