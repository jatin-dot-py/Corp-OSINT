Dr. Deepak Kumar Seth

Chairman

Dr. Deepak Kumar Seth is a globally recognised, high-profile businessman engaged in fashion business since 1976. Dr. Seth and his family are majority shareholders of two global conglomerates with respective flagship companies listed in India, generating turnover in excess of $3 Billion, employing more than 42,000 people and serving hundreds of the World’s top retailers. Dr. Seth is the Chairman of the board of both the companies.

Dr. Seth is a pioneer in fashion industry being the first businessman to go outside India to set up marketing offices in countries of the retailers such as UK, US, Germany and sourcing factories/offices in Far East countries such as Bangladesh, China, Hong Kong, Vietnam, Indonesia after quotas were abolished in garment product categories in 1999.

Dr. Seth was also first businessman in fashion industry who recognised the need to set up private educational institutes for technical study and training to develop skill sets specific to fashion industry and set up a very successful fashion education institute in India which has achieved global recognition and top education institute has taken over the running of this institute though Mr Seth is still involved in the business as an advisor.

Dr. Seth’s journey is not just about business success—it’s about building a resilient, ethical, and globally integrated enterprise that continues to inspire the industry. A book by the name of “Resolute by Design” that tracks Dr Seth’s inspirational entrepreneurial journey and life lessons he learned along the way was released by Governor of Maharashtra in 2020 and is available on book stores in India, UK and Amazon.

For his incomparable contribution to the field of apparel manufacturing, fashion and society, Dr. Seth was conferred an honorary Doctor of Philosophy degree by the UPES in 2022.

In December 2023, Dr. Seth was honoured as the “Icon of the Indian Apparel Industry” by the Apparel Export Promotion Council (AEPC), presented by Minister of Textiles Piyush Goyal.

Dr. Seth is an active member of the Apparel Export Promotion Council of India and held the post of Vice Chairman of the Eastern Region of AEPC for 2 years. He is also an executive member of the Apparel Exporters & Manufacturers Association (AEMA) and has been awarded “Udyog Ratna” Award by the Haryana Govt. for his entrepreneurial skills.

Dr. Seth firmly believes in giving back to society and is running various charitable organizations like Meena Seth Clinic – giving free medical aid to the needy; Arpan – providing free tuition in English, Mathematics and Science to the under privileged children (in India and Bangladesh) between age group of 7 to 12 years and Soham for All – another school that provides free education to the children from underprivileged sections of community.

Pallak Seth

Executive Vice Chairman

With over 25 years’ experience in fashion manufacturing, sourcing, and investment ecosystem, Pallak Seth is a serial entrepreneur, who has built a leading entrepreneurial platform in fashion infrastructure. After graduating with a Bachelor of Arts (Economics) from Northwestern University in Chicago, Pallak moved to Hong Kong, at the age of 21, and founded an apparel sourcing business with US $200,000 in initial capital and support from his father. Established in 1999, Norwest Industries made US $1 million in profit in its first year. Today the flagship company, PDS Limited is a US $2.7 billion global enterprise, being one of the largest consumer goods design, product development, sourcing, virtual manufacturing, and distribution organization in Asia. Pallak serves as the Vice Chairman of the PDS Group. PDS operates from a global network of 50+ offices in 22 countries across Europe, North America, Middle East, and Asia.

Instead of remaining as an apparel sourcing company, PDS today is a platform business and has transformed into being one of the most favoured designer-manufacturers for some of the world’s leading brands and retailers with a strong customer base part of FTSE 100, S&P500 and DAX30. Capitalizing on recent market trends, PDS has also developed several in-house proprietary brands, or undertaken collaborations with celebrities and influencers, along with licensing or acquiring its own brands.

Pallak is passionate about building a robust venture ecosystem with a focus on innovation, sustainability, technology and direct to consumer brands / digital brands. He believes in backing young, bright founders and giving them the benefit of the PDS Platform. This led to the formation of PDS Ventures which is a $50M investment fund with $20M deployed across 55+ start-ups and a few institutional funds. Pallak is also an alumnus of Harvard Business School, having completed the Executive Program (Ownership/President Management) from HBS.
