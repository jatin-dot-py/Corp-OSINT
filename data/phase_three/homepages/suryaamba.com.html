Who we are?

Suryaamba Spinning Mills Limited is anchored by Mr Virender Kumar Agarwal and family members, who have been in the textile business for over a period of time prior to incepting Suryaamba. An ISO 9001:2015 certified organization, Suryaamba is a state of art manufacturing unit located strategically at Nayakund, near Ramtek, District: Nagpur (Maharashtra). The unit is well connected by road and is only an hour away from the Nagpur airport. 

Started in 1993, Suryaamba Spinning Mills Ltd. is a leading manufacturer of speciality synthetic spun Yarns. The core competitive strength of the company is its innovative product range, specifically tailor-made for the customers. We produce yearly 14400 mt. of quality Yarns. Surpassing 25 years milestone with the knowledge and experience of the promoters, Suryaamba Spinning Mills Limited has established itself in the domestic and export market with a wide product range from 100% polyester and viscose spun Yarns as well as blended synthetic Yarns, used for weaving.
