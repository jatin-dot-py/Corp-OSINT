Our Solutions

Insecticides

In agriculture, insecticides are precision-engineered chemical solutions deployed to counter insect pests, safeguarding crop yields and quality, thus ensuring agricultural productivity and global food security.

Herbicides

Herbicides are chemicals used to control or kill unwanted plants, commonly referred to as weeds. They are an essential tool in agriculture, forestry, landscaping, and managing invasive plant species.

Fungicides

Fungicides are chemicals used to control or prevent the growth of fungi that cause diseases in plants. They are an essential tool in agriculture for protecting crops from fungal infections that can reduce yields and quality.

Biologicals/PGR

Biological solutions are innovative nature-based technologies that are important in integrated crop management. They can be broadly classified as bio-fertilizers, bio-pesticides and bio-stimulants.

Mr. Shyam Lal Bansal

Independant Director

Mr. Shyam Lal Bansal is a seasoned and accomplished banker with a distinguished career spanning more than four decades during which he handled a wide range of subjects pertaining to commercial banks in various high-level capacities culminating as the Chairman & Managing Director of Oriental Bank of Commerce. Mr. Bansal is an alumnus of Shri Ram College of Commerce, a Masters in Commerce and is a certified associate of the Indian Institute of Bankers. He started his career as a probationary officer in Union Bank of India and went on to have an illustrious professional innings spanning over 39 years in Banking Industry with Union Bank of India and Oriental Bank of Commerce. During his banking carrier with Union Bank, he headed various branches, regions and Zones and promoted to the position of General Manager in 2007. In April 2010 he took over as Executive Director of United Bank of India and in March 2012 he took over as Chairman and Managing Director of Oriental Bank of Commerce. He earlier held Directorships at Canara HSBC Oriental Bank of Commerce Life Insurance Co Ltd and Oriental Insurance Co Ltd. Mr. Bansal joined IIL in February 2024 as a Non-Executive, Independent Director. Besides IIL, he also serves on the Board of Indiabulls Investment Management Limited and ZMV Real Estate Private Limited.
