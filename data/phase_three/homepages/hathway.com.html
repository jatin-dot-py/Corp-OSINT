Looking for a new broadband or WiFi connection? Explore the Hathway plans available in your location and choose a speed based on your household’s activities, number of simultaneous users and connected devices.

Which Hathway Broadband Plan Is Right For Your Home?

The right Hathway internet plan depends on how your household uses the connection, not simply on choosing the highest available speed. Consider the activities you perform, the number of simultaneous users and the number of devices that are actively using the connection.

Internet Need What to Consider Suggested Speed Range
Browsing & social media Everyday internet usage and connected devices 40–100 Mbps
Work from home Video calls, cloud applications, file sharing and simultaneous household usage 75–150 Mbps
Online learning Classes, video calls and other active users at the same time 75–125 Mbps
OTT & 4K streaming Number of screens streaming simultaneously and overall household usage 100–200 Mbps
Online gaming Connection stability, latency and simultaneous usage 100–300 Mbps
Multiple-device homes Number of active phones, laptops, TVs, consoles and smart devices 200–300+ Mbps

These speed ranges are indicative. When comparing Hathway broadband plans, consider the available speed, number of simultaneous users, router requirements, plan price, validity, applicable benefits and service availability at your address.

Ready to find a plan for your household? Check availability in your location, compare the available Hathway internet plans and choose an option that fits your connectivity needs.
