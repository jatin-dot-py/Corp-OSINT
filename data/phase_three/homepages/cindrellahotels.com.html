    The Cindrella Hotel, Siliguri

    Siliguri, a vibrant city nestled in the foothills of the Eastern Himalayas, serves as a gateway to Northeast India. Known for its tea gardens, bustling markets, and diverse culture, it offers a blend of natural beauty and urban charm. The city is a crucial transport hub, linking nearby cities, making it a popular stopover for travellers. 

    Located on Sevoke Road, the Cindrella Hotel is a prime destination for corporate and leisure travellers. This 4-star facility hotel in Siliguri, surrounded by lush greenery, offers easy access to popular tourist attractions, making it an ideal base for exploring the city. Guests can enjoy modern amenities, comfortable accommodations, and exceptional service at arguably one of the best luxury hotels in Siliguri. With its blend of convenience and luxury, The Cindrella Hotel stands out as a premier choice for visitors to Siliguri.

    The Cindrella Hotel, Siliguri, offers 43 well-appointed accommodations in five categories: Deluxe Double/Twin Room, Executive Room, Pool-View Room, Super Deluxe Room, and Suite. Each room features modern amenities, including air-conditioning, complimentary Wi-Fi, and a tea and coffee maker. Most rooms have king-sized beds, ensuring comfort and luxury for all guests. Enjoy a perfect blend of style and convenience during your stay.

    Exquisite Dining at The Cindrella Hotel, Siliguri

    The Cindrella Hotel, Siliguri, features two exceptional dining options: Amrapali Restaurant and Recoil Bar & Patio. Both venues offer fine-dining with a pure-vegetarian menu, ensuring delicious food and drinks. Amrapali excels in gourmet dishes, while Recoil Bar & Patio provides a unique experience with its indoor and al-fresco seating, perfect for enjoying a relaxing meal or drink.

    Elegant Banquet Halls at The Cindrella Hotel, Siliguri

    The Cindrella Hotel, Siliguri, offers seven versatile banquet halls, including two outdoor lawns and five indoor spaces, ranging from 800 sq. ft. to 17,000 sq. ft. These venues are perfect for conferences, meetings, or weddings, providing the ideal setting for any event. Each hall is equipped with modern amenities to ensure a seamless and memorable experience for all guests.
