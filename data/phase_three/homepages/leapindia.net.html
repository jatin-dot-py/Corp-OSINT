LEAP: Scalable Asset Pooling. Smarter Supply Chain Solutions

LEAP is a tech-enabled asset pooling services company transforming how India moves goods. Our model replaces ownership with on-demand access to high-quality assets, helping businesses operate leaner, faster, and more sustainably.

By anchoring our operations in circular economy principles, we enable clients to reduce waste, improve asset utilization, and cut down logistics costs, without compromising performance.

With a network of 29 warehouses/FCs, 7 outsourced pallet manufacturing units, 1,000+ valuable customers, 2,860+ dedicated employees, and support 10,100+ consumer touchpoints, we deliver reliable, future-ready supply chain solutions at every step.
