About us

Ultracab India Ltd. stands as a premier manufacturer and exporter of electric wires and cables, with a strong foothold in the market for over 22 years. Leveraging cutting-edge technology and state-of-the-art machinery, we are committed to delivering superior quality products that meet the highest international standards.

Our journey began with the production of PVC wires and cables, and today we have expanded to serve a diverse range of markets worldwide. Our products are renowned for their reliability, durability, and adherence to rigorous safety standards, earning Ultracab an exceptional reputation across multiple industries. At our advanced manufacturing facility in Shapar Dist. Rajkot (Gujarat), India, we incorporate modern technological solutions and high-precision equipment to ensure optimal production efficiency and quality assurance.
