Saj Story

Saj Hotels was founded in the early 1990s by brothers Rahul and Kartik Timbadia, who began their hospitality journey with a boutique resort in Mahabaleshwar and expanded into nature immersed escapes like Saj by the Lake (Malshej Ghat) and Saj in the forest (Pench).

Grounded in eco luxury and sustainable design, every resort is crafted to offer luxury in harmony with local ecosystems, tribal heritage, and mindful tourism—setting new benchmarks for responsible hospitality in India.

Why Is Saj Resort Unique

SAJ Resorts & Hotels offers immersive eco luxury retreats seamlessly blending sustainability, indigenous design, wildlife immersive adventures, and heartfelt hospitality in biodiverse landscapes.
