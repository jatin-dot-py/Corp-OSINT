    What is Biodiesel?

    Biodiesel is a liquid fuel, technically known as a monoalkyl ester, made from oils and alcohols. Biodiesel is a renewable fuel that can be produced in any climate using already developed agricultural practices. Biodiesel is made from renewable resources such as vegetable oils, or other types of biomass.

     

    B100 is 100% biodiesel. Biodiesel is widely available in both its neat form (B100) and in blends with petroleum diesel (for example B2, B5, B20).

    The Bio-Diesel is a Carbon Neutral Fuel. The Carbon Dioxide (C02) produced by vehicles is reabsorbed by plant used to make the Biodiesel.
