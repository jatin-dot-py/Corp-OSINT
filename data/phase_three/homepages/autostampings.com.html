Automotive Stampings and Assemblies Limited [ASAL]

Headquarter in Pune, India; incorporated as a Public Limited Company on March 13, 1990.

Automotive Stampings and Assemblies Limited (ASAL) is a TATA AutoComp (TACO) group company, TACO holds 75% stake in the Company and balance 25% is held by the Public. The shares of the Company are listed on NSE and BSE.

Engineering Capabilities

Engineering team specializes in process sequence finalization, layout planning, fixture DAP, line installation, and line balancing to optimize and improve manufacturing processess.

The tool engineering vertical focuses on product feasibility, leveraging advanced simulation and thinning analysis. Key activities include product feasibility assessment, method planning, tool design review, home line try out (HLTO) and manufacturing status monitoring to ensure seamless production readiness.

With advanced CAD capabilities, ASAL utilizes tools like Catia V5, UG Nx, Hyperform, Auto CAD, and AutoForm ensuring manufacturing feasiblity and technological excellence.

 

 

Technologies

Battery Trays
Are designed to efficiently house and manage EV battery cells, ensuring performance, safety, and longevity in demanding applications.
Aluminium cooling tubes
Support thermal management in EV battery trays, maintaining optimal battery performance and safety standards
Stamping Technology
High-tensile material processing with different grades on tonnage ranging from 250 T to 1500 T.
Robotic & Manual Welding
Incorporates MIG, TIG, Projection, laser welding, friction stir welding (FSW) with high-strength and reliable assemblies.
Heavy Fabrication & Surface Treatment
Features CNC machining, laser cutting, automated assembly, and corrosion-resistant coatings for durability and performance.
