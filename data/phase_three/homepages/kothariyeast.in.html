    Kothari Fermentation and Biochem Ltd

    Kothari Fermentation and Biochem Ltd. was incorporated on 26th of December 1990. The Company is backed by an experienced Board of Directors and Professionals. It is a Public Limited Company listed on Bombay Stock Exchange.

     

    The Company has its "Yeast & its Derivatives" manufacturing plant based on latest technologies located at Sikandrabad District Bulandshahr (U.P.), which is about 50 kms away from New Delhi (India). The Plant has a capacity to manufacture 25,000 M.T. per annum of Compressed Yeast.
