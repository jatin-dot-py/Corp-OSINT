About Us

Welcome to Jainex Group

Jainex Group, an ISO 9001:2015 certified company established in 1968 is a leading distributor and exporter of high-carbon spring steel wires and strips.  Jainex Group is a diversified enterprise with business units across 4 main segments, – domestic steel, exports, railway engineering, and  precision Hardened and Tempered  steel strips manufacturing.

Having a presence in both domestic and global steel markets  as well as our own manufacturing plant gives us unique insights into the steel industry and enables us to help our clients source quality materials through our network of 5 sales office and service centers across India. 

Through our Railway division we have a depth of experience in government tendering, and  management of engineering projects while dealing with complex regulations.
Whether you require high carbon steel or a partner in your railway engineering projects, Jainex Group is your one stop shop and reliable partner for all your needs.

Our Services
Precision slitting service tailored to meat specific width requirements with our state of the art machines

Slitting

Slitting is a crucial process in the steel manufacturing industry, involving the precise cutting of large coils of steel into narrower strips. This process is essential for creating steel products of various sizes and dimensions, catering to the diverse needs of industries such as automotive, construction, manufacturing, and more.

Cut to length of steel strips

Cut to length of steel strips is a process in metalworking where steel strips or coils are precisely cut into specific lengths according to the requirements of a particular project or application. This process is essential in various industries such as automotive, construction, manufacturing, and engineering, where steel is a primary material used for fabrication.

Bulk Breaking

Bulk breaking is a distribution strategy used in supply chain management where goods, typically bought in large quantities or bulk, are broken down into smaller units for resale or distribution. This process involves dividing large quantities of goods into smaller units to meet the needs of customers or retailers who may not require the bulk quantities.
