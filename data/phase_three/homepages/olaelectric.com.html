    Got questions about Ola Electric vehicles, service, or charging? We’ve got you covered.

    Got questions about Ola Electric vehicles, service, or charging? We’ve got you covered.

    What is the cost of charging an electric scooter?

    The electric scooter charge cost for the Ola S1 2kWh is just ₹14 for a full charge, giving a running cost of only ₹0.17 per km, compared to a petrol scooter which costs about ₹2.5 per km.

    How long do electric scooters take to charge?

    Typically, how long does it take to charge an electric scooter is about 4 hours for the Ola S1 to go from 0–80%. Charging duration may differ depending on the specific variant you choose.

    Does an electric scooter need a license,Regisgration and helmet?

    Yes, does an electric scooter need a license, registration and helmet is a common question, and the rule is clear: a valid driving license, proper registration, and wearing a helmet are mandatory to ride an electric scooter in India.

    Why are riding modes included in electric scooters?

    Electric Scooter Riding Modes provide flexibility to match different riding needs. Eco Mode prioritizes maximum range with efficient battery use. Normal Mode balances daily usability with comfort and range. Sports Mode delivers higher acceleration for a spirited ride, while Hyper Mode unlocks peak performance and top speed. DIY Mode (Do It Yourself) allows riders to customize speed and performance parameters as per preference. Together, these five modes optimize motor torque, battery management, and regenerative braking to offer the ideal mix of efficiency, performance, and control.

    Which riding mode offers maximum speed and rapid acceleration in an electric scooter?

    Electric Scooter Riding Modes give riders the flexibility to choose performance as per need. Eco, Normal, Sports, and DIY modes adjust range and acceleration, while Hyper Mode offers the maximum speed and acceleration with top speed up to 141 km/h and 0–40 km/h in just 2.1 seconds.

    What is the speed of scooter eco mode?

    Electric Scooter Riding Modes are designed to optimize speed and efficiency. In Eco Mode, the scooter offers a top speed of 42 km/h, making it ideal for conserving battery and extending range during daily commutes.

    Will I need to replace the battery in an electric scooter at some point?

    Yes, electric scooter battery replacement may be required over the long term depending on usage and riding conditions. Ola scooters come with a base battery warranty of 3 years or 50,000 km, ensuring reliable performance. For added peace of mind, customers can opt for an extended warranty of up to 8 years or 125,000 km, which greatly reduces the chances of needing a replacement during normal usage.

    Does an electric scooter work in the rain?

    Yes—you can ride your Ola when it’s drizzling or downpouring. Our scooters are IP67-rated, meaning key components (motor, battery pack, wiring) are sealed against splashes and puddle-deep water. The regen-braking maps will stay consistent, and your dashboard stays responsive. So next time monsoon clouds gather, be ready to ride your Ola electric scooter in the rain.

    What is the price of OLA electric scooter?

    The electric scooter price for the Ola S1 range starts at ₹89,999. The exact on-road effective price depends on the variant and state subsidies. Customers can visit book.olaelectric.com
    to check the on-road price for each model.

    What is the warranty on an electric scooter battery?

    The electric scooter battery warranty from Ola covers 3 years or 50,000 km as base warranty. Customers can also extend this coverage up to 8 years or 125,000 km, ensuring long-term protection and peace of mind.

    Are electric scooters eco-friendly?

    Yes, electric scooters are eco friendly. Ola electric scooters emit zero tailpipe emissions, which helps reduce air pollution in cities. They run on electricity, which can be powered through renewable energy sources, making them a sustainable alternative to petrol vehicles. Additionally, Ola scooters feature regenerative braking technology that recovers energy during deceleration, improving efficiency and lowering overall environmental impact.

    What is the average range of an e-scooter in Indian cities?

    The term e-scooter mileage refers to the riding range on a full charge. Ola scooters deliver up to 320 km IDC range, depending on the model and riding mode. Actual range may vary with road conditions, load, and riding style.

    Are electric bikes suitable for long-distance travel?

    Yes. Ola Electric offers a long distance electric bike with an IDC range of up to 501 km on a single charge, making it ideal for extended rides and intercity travel. With Ola Hypercharger, the battery can be charged from 0–80% in just 4 hours, and riders can access 970+ charging points across India, ensuring convenience on long trips.
