A TRUSTED, EVOLVING, EXPANDING NAME IN

CHEMICAL & FEED MANUFACTURING & DISTRIBUTION

Incorporated as “Sun Organosys Private Limited” in 2010, later renamed “Shivam Chemicals Limited” in 2023, our company is a significant player in the manufacturing of Hydrated Lime and distribution of various products. With a robust marketing network, we have distributed over 2.50 Lakh metric tons of products, exceeding INR 7,500 million in cumulative value.

Evolving from animal feed supplement trading, our journey includes establishing a wholly-owned subsidiary, Shivam Chemicals and Minerals Pvt. Ltd, with a 60,000 MT manufacturing capacity Hydrated Lime in Dahej, Gujarat.

We’re a global player – one of the most trusted and reputed names in chemical manufacturing and distribution. We boast of many lasting and rewarding partnerships. Surpassing customer expectations through state-of-the art machinery, world-class integrated processing plants and accreditations by top international regulatory authorities.

Today’ we are one of India’s major distributors of Poultry Feed Supplement (MBM), Di-Calcium Phosphate (Feed Grade), Mono Calcium Phosphate (MCP), Magnesium Oxide, Hydrated Lime, Limestone Powder and Soya De-Oiled Cake.
