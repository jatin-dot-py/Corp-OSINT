About Us

Universal Autofoundry Limited is a renowned global manufacturer and exporter specializing in the production Grey Iron, Ductile Iron, and SG Iron Casting. Our products cater to various industries, including automobiles, earth movers, agriculture, railways, and international markets.

We offer castings in three different conditions: Machined, Semi Machined, and as cast, all of which can be customized with surface treatments to meet our customers’ specific requirements. Our diverse range of components, such as Suspension Brackets, Differential Housings, Hubs, Brake Drums, Flywheels, Adjuster Nuts, Pulleys, Dampers, and more, are widely utilized in the commercial vehicle and engineering sectors.
