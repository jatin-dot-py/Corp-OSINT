Our Milestones

2011- Here onwards we received the gold trophy each year for being the highest exporters of processed yarn in India.
2009- We are also the first to get ISO-18001 certification in the North Zone for Safety Management.
2000- We are the first to get ISO-14001 certification in the North Zone for Environment Management.
1993- We are the second textile unit in India to be awarded the ISO-9002 certification.
1992- Yet again we are the first company in India to offer 100% Cotton melange yarns.
1987- We are the first company in India to offer 100% acrylic (non-bulk) fiber dyed yarns.
