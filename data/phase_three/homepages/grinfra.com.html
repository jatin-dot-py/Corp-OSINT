Who we are

G R Infraprojects Limited is one of the fastest growing organizations in the Indian infrastructure sector, with nearly three decades of legacy.

Our ~10,200 passionate employees, best-in-class assets, latest technology, and 24/7 project execution capability enables us to deliver projects before or on-time with highest standards of quality, safety, financial discipline, and governance.

Our primary business operations are broadly segmented into four categories: -

  • Engineering Procurement and Construction (EPC) and Project Management services for Roads & Highways, Bridges, Airport Runway, Railways & Metro, Hydro & Tunneling, Power Transmission & Distribution, Ropeways, Logistics Infrastructure, Telecom & IT Infrastructure and Energy.
  • Development, operations and maintenance of Infrastructure projects under BOT, HAM, BOOT, and DBFOT models, in Highways, Power Transmission & Distribution, Ropeways and Logistics sectors.
  • In-house manufacturing of Bitumen Emulsions, Construction Chemicals, Fibre Composites, GFRP Rebar, Diesel Exhaust Fluid (GR8DEF), Electrical Panels & Cabinets, Thermoplastic Paint and Road Signages.
  • Fabrication of galvanized Metal Crash Barriers, Noise Barriers, Transmission Line Towers (TLT), OHE Mast, Poles & Solar Mounted Structures.
