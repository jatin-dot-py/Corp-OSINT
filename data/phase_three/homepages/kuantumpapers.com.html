Direct & Indirect Employment

Kuantum for Earth

Our commitment to a sustainable future is underpinned by our focus on social farm forestry initiatives, water conservation and waste management strategies. Our on-site nursery, teeming with clonal species of hardwood plants, not only serves the local farmers with a fast-growing harvest and supplementary income, but also provides us with a sustainable source of raw material. Our operations significantly conserve water through innovative technologies such as advanced water clarifiers, water treatment and rain-water harvesting. Pioneering in India, our HERB recovery boiler with SRS technology optimally processes black liquor, enabling us to recover and recycle chemicals, leading to operations that are both environmentally friendly and energy efficient.
