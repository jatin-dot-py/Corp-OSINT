Concrete & Construction Calculators

Free tools for concrete and construction planning — concrete volume calculator, concrete bag calculator, concrete mix ratio calculator, rebar calculator, block & brick calculator, and slab cost calculator.

Concrete Volume Calculator

Estimate the concrete volume of a slab, footing, or column in cubic feet, cubic yards, and cubic metres from its dimensions, with a 10% waste allowance for ordering

Concrete Bag Calculator

Work out how many 40, 60, or 80 lb bags of pre-mixed concrete you need for a given volume, including a 5% waste allowance and rounded up to whole bags

Concrete Mix Ratio Calculator

Break a concrete volume into cement, sand, and aggregate quantities for any mix ratio, applying a dry-volume factor and converting cement into bags

Rebar Calculator

Estimate the number of reinforcing bars and total bar length for a two-way slab mat from the slab dimensions, bar spacing, and edge clearance

Block & Brick Calculator

Estimate how many concrete blocks or bricks a wall needs from its length and height, using the unit face area including mortar plus a wastage allowance

Slab Cost Calculator

Estimate the cost of a concrete slab from its dimensions and unit prices for ready-mix concrete, labor, and formwork, broken down and totalled

Concrete Curing Time Calculator

Work out when the pour is ready to walk on, to strip forms, and to load, from the cement type and the average curing temperature
