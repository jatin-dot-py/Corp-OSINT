Welcome to Isgec

Heavy Engineering Ltd.

Isgec Heavy Engineering Ltd. is a multi-product, multi-location public company that has been providing engineering solutions to customers across 92 countries for the past 90 years. As a company imbued with this spirit, what we perpetually strive for is excellence. Excellence in all we do, so that our clients may achieve excellence in all that they do.
