About Us

India's 1st BSE listed Entity with the Core Value of EV Manufacturing

Wardwizard Innovations & Mobility Limited is a Public listed company incorporated on 20th October, 1982. In the year 2019, there has been change in the Control & Management of the Company pursuant to successful completion of Open Offer by the Acquirers (Mr. Yatin Gupte & others) on 24th September, 2019.

Shares of the Company got listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange on 27th February, 2015 subsequently the name has since been changed to Wardwizard Innovations & Mobility Limited on 09th June, 2020. Company is engaged in E-Vehicles Manufacturing & consumer durable/electrical items having spacious & modern state of the art facility in Vadodara supported by experienced commercial & technical staff.

Our Prestige

Joy e-bike, a flagship brand of Wardwizard Innovations & Mobility Limited, representing our commitment to advancing sustainable transportation. With a range of zero-emission electric bikes, Joy e-bike combines innovative e-bike designs with environmental responsibility, offering efficient and cost-effective commuting solutions.
