Operations Depth

Supply Chain Logistics

We manage the entire supply chain end-to-end, from sourcing and logistics to delivery, ensuring seamless, reliable, and efficient operations at every stage.

01

USA Coal Supply Chain

International Mine-to-Stockyard Import

Stage 01 • Origination

USA Mine Sourcing

Direct off-take agreements contracted at mines in the Northern Appalachian (NAPP) and Illinois Basin (ILB) coal seams. Raw coal is moved via heavy rail networks to coastal US ports.

Stage 02 • Ocean Transit

Panamax Cargo Charters

Cargo consolidated at load ports (Baltimore/US Gulf) and loaded onto massive Panamax and Supramax bulk carriers, scheduling ocean crossings to secure stable bulk cargo flows.

Stage 03 • Port Discharge

Kandla & Tuna Port Operations

Vessels discharge at our primary hubs in Gujarat. Cargo goes through customs clearance, sizing, screening, and laboratory assays in massive custom-cleared stockyards.

Stage 04 • Last Mile

Inland Railway & Road Despatches

Finished coal cargo is loaded directly into railway rakes or heavy trailers for dispatch to industrial yards and brick kilns across Punjab, Haryana, UP, Uttarakhand, Bihar, Jammu & Kashmir, Rajasthan and Himachal Pradesh.

02

Sponge Iron & Steel Chain

Domestic Sourcing-to-Furnace Siding

Stage 01 • Sourcing

Primary Producer Sourcing

Off-take agreements with integrated DRI kilns and steel manufacturers in Odisha and West Bengal. Strict pre-shipment inspections verify physical size and carbon/Fe parameters.

Stage 02 • Consolidation

Rake Loading Operations

DRI pellets and steel billets are consolidated at major Eastern railheads. We secure rake allotments to move bulk loads (2,700+ MT per booking) cost-effectively.

Stage 03 • National Transit

Indian Railways Rail Transit

Bulk rakes move across the national rail network directly to primary consuming regions, bypassing regional warehouse halts to avoid mechanical degradation of DRI material.

Stage 04 • Delivery

Just-In-Time Furnace Discharge

Rakes are discharged at Mandi Gobindgarh (Punjab), Gujarat, or Rajasthan sidings. Local stockyards manage trailers for JIT delivery directly to furnaces.
