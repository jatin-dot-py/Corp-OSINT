Business Story

The Sprawling unit is located at Malkapur & Khamgaon (Maharashtra) in the industrial belt of Central India. Birla Cotsyn (India) Limited is having Cotton Ring Spun Yarn, Open End Yarn and Synthetic Blended Yarn manufacturing facility with state of Art machineries.

  • Centrally located in India, enabling efficient domestic and export logistics.
  • Advanced machinery infrastructure ensures consistent quality and productivity.
  • Integrated manufacturing process for better control and flexibility across product lines.
