A
Pharma & Life Sciences · Compliance
Auryis Know More →
AI-powered regulatory compliance & safety intelligence ecosystem
Auryis is an AI-powered compliance ecosystem that turns regulatory hurdles into advantages for Pharma and Life Sciences organizations, combining AI with internal expertise to keep documentation audit-ready and turn complex, unstructured data into clear, defensible insights. It includes Auryis Quality Intelligence (AQI), which manages regulated processes like CAPA, deviations, change control, complaints, training, document control, and validation, and Auryis Safety Intelligence (ASI), a pharmacovigilance solution built with Eversana that monitors safety reporting obligations and flags regulatory risks early—together strengthening quality oversight and audit readiness.

ERP · Clubs & Hospitality
ClubAce
End-to-end ERP for clubs worldwide
ClubAce is an integrated, purpose-built ERP platform for sports and lifestyle clubs worldwide that unifies membership, bookings, facilities, finance, events, hospitality and F&B, procurement, HR, and analytics into a single intelligent system. By leveraging real-time data, automation, and Agentic AI, it helps clubs improve operations, reduce revenue leakage, deliver seamless member experiences, and make better decisions—predicting demand, automating workflows, and giving leadership real-time visibility across every function to make club management self-optimising.
