About Us

Welcome to Continental Securities Limited

Established in 1990, Continental Securities Limited (CIN: L67120RJ1990PLC005371) is a Non-Banking Finance licensed by Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and listed in Bombay Stock Exchange.

We believe finance should be supportive and rooted in trust. Since 1990, we've dedicated ourselves to being more than just a financial service provider—we aim to be a reliable partner in your journey toward growth and financial well-being.

Founded by our Managing Director, Mr. Rajesh Khuteta, Continental Securities began with a clear mission: to offer financial solutions that are accessible, transparent, and genuinely helpful for individuals and small businesses alike. Over three decades later, that mission still guides everything we do.
