About Us

Ashapura Group is a leading multi-mineral solutions company, involved in processing and exporting of various industrial minerals including Bauxite, Iron Ore, Bentonite, Kaolin, Silica Sand and Attapulgite.
Ashapura is also an integrated value-added products manufacturer supplying mineral-based products to various industries such as Metals & Foundries, Oil & Gas, Petrochemicals, Edible oils, Paints, Automobiles, Construction and other material-based industries.

Ashapura Innovation & Knowledge Centre

With a campus of over 11 Acres, the Ashapura Innovation & Knowledge Centre is the largest and the most well-equipped Commercial Research & Development Centre for mineral analysis and development in Bhuj, India. Our state-of-the-art analytical and processing facilities are staffed by over 70 Scientists & doctorates from various scientific disciplines. Over the years, we have developed several new applications and process improvements.
