About Our Industry

At Engineering Business, we specialize in the design and manufacture of heavy equipment, machinery, and systems tailored to meet the stringent requirements of various industries. From the HydroCarbon sector to Oil & Gas, Steel Plants, Power Plants, Nuclear Plant Boilers, and Turnkey Projects, our comprehensive portfolio of products and solutions caters to diverse needs and applications.

Our workshop in Murbad is the cornerstone of our operations, boasting accreditation with ISO 9001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, and ASME U stamp certifications. These certifications underscore our unwavering commitment to maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and environmental management across all aspects of our operations.
Furthermore, we adhere to stringent industry standards such as ASME Section 8 Division 1 and ASME Section 3 NB, NC & ND, ensuring compliance and reliability in our engineering solutions. This dedication to excellence has earned us the trust and approval of renowned national and international consultants and EPC companies including Thyssenkrupp, Tecnimont, EIL, LINDE, PDIL, TOYO, Technip Energies, MECON, MN Dastur, Danieli, Primetal Technologies, and SMS Group, among others. Our shops are approved by statutory bodies such as IBR, PESO, etc

Our Approach

Lloyds Engineering Works Ltd –is established in year 1974 at Andheri, Mumbai and subsequently shifted its shop and converted into heavy fabrication, machine building unit at Murbad, Thane Dist. Maharashtra.
Work shop is located at Murbad Dist Thane approx 100 KMS away from Head office in Mumbai. Our workshop is also approved / enlisted by international consultants like Thyssenkrupp, Tecnimont, Aker Kvaerner, Jacobs, EIL, UHDE, PESO, IBR, LINDE, PDIL, TOYO, Technip, MECON, MN Dastur, SMS Group etc.
