An Innovative Pharmaceutical Manufacturer in India

We are a research-driven pharmaceutical company based in India that develops, manufactures, and supplies differentiated generic products in the oral solid dosage forms, both as pre-formulation intermediates as well as finished formulations for certain key therapeutic segments. As a vertically integrated pharmaceutical manufacturer, we offer both pharmaceutical and nutraceutical products.

We have in-house R&D capabilities to offer a range of non-infringing drug delivery solutions based on process technologies that enable patient compliance thereby reducing the cost of treatment.

Know More

We develop complex generic products & novel drug delivery techniques

We provide a comprehensive range of solutions in India and across the globe from a pharmaceutical manufacturer, including product conceptualization, development, BE studies, regulatory support for dossier preparation and submission, product registrations, manufacturing, and supply.

Our offerings encompass differentiated generic products in the form of finished formulations and pre-formulation intermediates, such as granules, pellets (immediate-release, modified-release, delayed-release, etc.), taste-masked powders, dry suspensions, tablets, capsules, and Oral Thin Films (OTF).

Know More

We help our partners gain market-share with our cutting-edge technology

As an experienced pharmaceutical manufacturer,  ZIM Labs uses its proprietary knowledge, technology and manufacturing process expertise to bridge the technology gap in Regulated, Pharmerging and ROW markets while assisting local players in various geographies to launch differentiated /combination generic products to compete in their respective local markets.
