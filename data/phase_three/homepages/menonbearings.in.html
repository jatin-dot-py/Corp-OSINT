Menon Bearings Ltd. Legacy of Vision, Leadership for the Future

Founded in 1991 by Mr. Ram Menon, Menon Bearings Ltd. began with a bold vision to deliver high-precision automotive components that meet the evolving demands of a global industry. His foresight, technical expertise, and commitment to quality laid the foundation for enduring success.

Today, under the dynamic leadership of Mr. Nitin Menon, Executive Chairman, the company has expanded its product range, adopted advanced manufacturing technologies, and strengthened its presence in domestic and international markets. Blending the founder’s legacy with modern innovation, Mr. Nitin Menon continues to drive Menon Bearings Ltd. toward sustainable growth and global leadership in precision automotive components.

His commitment to a “First Time Right” culture has helped MBL achieve the industry’s highest certifications, including IATF 16949, and maintain a proven record of 100% on-time delivery to leading OEMs, earning the reputation of a dependable “Ship-to-Use” partner.

Driving Engineering Innovation Across Bearings, Castings & Brakes​

Menon Bearings Ltd.

Menon Bearings Ltd. specializes in high-performance bimetallic bearings, bushes, thrust washers, and PTFE-coated bushings, catering to the stringent requirements of automotive engines, compressors, and industrial machinery. With fully integrated manufacturing facilities under one roof, the company delivers a diverse range of critical and precision-engineered components.

Menon Alkop Ltd.

.Menon Alkop Ltd. is a trusted expert in the production of critical and fully machined aluminium alloy components, serving the needs of global industry leaders. Utilizing advanced high-pressure and gravity die casting technologies, the company delivers precision-engineered parts that meet the demanding standards of automotive and industrial applications

Read More

Menon Brakes Ltd.

Menon Brakes Ltd. is a fully integrated manufacturing facility offering end-to-end operations under one roof. The company specializes in the production of asbestos-free brake linings, brake pads, and riveted brake shoes, catering to both light and heavy commercial vehicles. With a strong focus on safety, performance, and regulatory compliance, Menon Brakes delivers reliable braking solutions to meet the rigorous demands of the automotive industry.
