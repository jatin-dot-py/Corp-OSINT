Company Overview

Incorporated on August 6, 2007 as Five Star Mercantile Private Limited, a private limited company under the Companies Act, 1956

Our Company was converted into a public limited company on January 3, 2012

Company entered into a Composite Scheme of Arrangement and Amalgamation with the division of Morarjee Textiles Limited called ‘the Integra Division’ and Morarjee Holdings Private Limited & consequently name of the company was changes to ‘Integra Garments & Textile Ltd. in June 2012

Company was acquired by Mr. Vishesh Gupta who is well known entrepreneur holds experience of around 10+years in business administration and prepared the organizational strategy for the group’s diversification in to FMCG and Infra Products. He has led institutions across business development, strategy as well as operations over the period of years.

The name of the Company was changed to “Integra Essentia Limited” on February 16, 2022 & is engaged in business of Life Essentials i.e. Food, Clothing, Infrastructure and Energy

Our Products & Services

FMCG Business: Agro Products

Our Company deals in trading of agro products comprising of certified organic agro products and general agro products such as rice, wheat, flour, grains, pulses, tea, coffee, sugar, dry fruits, spices, vegetables, exotic and general fruits and a variety of other products of the same nature such as juices and nectars, organic herbs, essences, agro nutraceuticals and dairy products.

Textile Business: Clothing

Our Company deals in the clothing and textile segment comprising of clothing and furnishing fabrics, linen material. Our product portfolio in this segment consists of bed linen, table linen for domestic use, hotels and hospitals supplies, upholstery materials, curtains & curtain fabrics, carpets and rugs and apparels for men, women, and children.
