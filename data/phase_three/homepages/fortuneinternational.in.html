About Fortune International Limited

Fortune International Limited is a Govt. of India Recognized Star Trading House, engaged in the business of procuring, trading and exporting a number of product groups including engineering goods and commodities to various parts of the world.

Listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange, the company is committed to complete transparency with its investors and stakeholders through timely disclosures and open access to all corporate documents.

  • Govt. of India Recognized Star Trading House
  • Engineering goods & commodities export business
  • Listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE)
  • 42 Annual General Meetings held since inception
