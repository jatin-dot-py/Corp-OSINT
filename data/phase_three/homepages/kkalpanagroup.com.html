Welcome to the world of

Kkalpanagroup

Kkalpana Industries (India) Ltd. is a group company of Mr. Narrindra Suranna, leading industrialist based in Kolkata.

A true visionary, Mr. Suranna foresaw the importance of plastic recycling and started a recycling unit in Falta Sez, near Kolkata, way back in 2007. Today, Kkalpana stands as a beacon of innovation and commitment in the plastic recycling industry.

Established with the vision to make a significant impact on environmental sustainability, we specialize in processing post - consumer, post-industrial plastic waste into high - quality polyethylene - LDPE, HDPE, PP Granules. Adding value up the chain, we manufacture ready to use compounds for Cable Sheathing / Jacketing, Pipes and Injection Molding. Our products are not just upcycled, they are a testament to the possibilities of circular economic practices.

The unit are ISO and GRS certified and is registered with central and state pollution control boards.

Our Mission

Backed by Continuous R&D and adopting new Technologies we commit ourselves to provide International Standard Upcycled Sustainable Plastic Raw Material. As a Circular Economy Stakeholder, We will contribute towards the Responsibility of mankind to save our Planet with Sensible use of Plastic reducing Carbon Footprint. We will create value for our employees and business partners and ensure that our stake holders receive sustained superior return on their investment.

Our Vision

To establish and maintain KKalpana Industries as the Largest and Trusted Circular Economy contributor across the globe with a continuous focus to value added innovation and deliver the best possible product & services to your Sustainability Target.

Our Future

Keeping with our vision statement, the company has embarked on a mission to enlarge its capacity of High End Mechanical Recycling Facility. Alongside introducing Upcycling in diverse fields by implementing new technologies like AI based Sorting of MSW Plastic taking it further for making High purity Feedstock for Polymer through Advance Recycling Process reducing substantial carbon footprints

KKALPANA INDUSTRIES (INDIA) LTD. made a small beginning in the year 1977 by setting up PVC compounding Plant in Kolkata. Under the leadership of Late Sri D C Surana the chairman of the company, it grew manifold.

The corporate journey of KKALPANA INDUSTRIES (INDIA) LTD begun in 1985 under the stewardship of Mr. Narrindra Suranna, present Chairman and Managing Director, with setting up of green field manufacturing facilities on the eastern and western coasts of India. The Company, having had its successful public issue and listing on the stock markets, emerged on the national and international scene. Thereafter, the Company carried out green and brown field projects to spread its product portfolio.
