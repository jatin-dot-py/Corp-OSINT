About us

Shree Rama began its journey in 1987 as a simple extrusion coating line and has grown into an international scale organization offering variety of Primary Packaging Solutions to a large customer base.

Shree Rama Multi-Tech Ltd. has invested in the most modern plastic processing technologies and have one of the largest fully integrated facility for Laminated and Plastic tubes in the World. Our activities manifest our concern for careful attention to the minutest requirements of our valued customers. We strongly believe that we must live up to the expectations of our customers and provide them superior quality and advanced packaging products, comparable with international standards.

Why SRMTL ?

Advanced Technology

SRMTL employs world class manufacturing practices at plant. Well equipped Research & Development Facilities to design laminate structures for challenging product packaging applications.

Advanced Standards

SRMTL being fully integrated Company understands market related urgencies and is strongly service oriented Company.

Pioneer Player

SRMTL is one of the pioneer players in the Tube Laminate and Lami Tubes Industries having complete in-house backward integration for each process. SRMTL understands technicality of product very well thereby meeting the quality needs of leading Multinational Companies in Oral Care, Cosmetics and Pharma Segment.

Cost effective solutions

Along with maintaining the quality, SRMTL also provides cost effective solutions through its alternate cost effective laminate structures.


Highly Skilled Team

SRMTL has highly skilled and experienced young technical team which can take any challenges.
