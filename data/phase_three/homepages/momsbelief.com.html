About Mom’s Belief

Our Company is a For-Profit Social Enterprise providing intervention plans for children with Neurodevelopmental Disorders (“NDDs”). Such plans are personalised based on each child’s unique needs and condition severity. NDDs include Autism Spectrum Disorder (“ASD”), Attention-Deficit/ Hyperactivity Disorder (“ADHD”), Down Syndrome (“DS”), Cerebral Palsy (“CP”), Intellectual Disability (“ID”), Learning Disabilities (“LD”), and Global Developmental Delays (“GDD”).

Our tailored approach is guided by experts in child psychology, speech, behaviour, occupational therapy and more. Fostering holistic growth and addressing identified developmental needs.

Your Child’s Therapy Roadmap & Key Players

1

Initial Consultation

The journey begins with a consultation session, either in-person or virtually, through our digital services, with qualified clinical professionals. This step serves as the family’s first point of contact with us and is critical for establishing trust and clarity.

Post the consultation, we conduct a comprehensive developmental assessment using standard assessment tools. Assessments are tailored to the child's age, condition, and behaviour.

2

Comprehensive Assessment

3

Enrolment and Goal Planning

Once assessments are complete, we onboard the family into the appropriate therapy program or support model, such as in-centre therapy, digital sessions, or the family support program, which is an e-therapy program provided through the digital mode.

This is the execution phase of the program. We deliver multidisciplinary therapy sessions through our centres or through digital mode, as per the IEP or IGP prepared specifically for the child.

4

Therapy Intervention and Parent Training

5

Goal Attainment, Skill Development, and Inclusion

This phase focuses on evaluating progress and facilitating mainstream integration.
