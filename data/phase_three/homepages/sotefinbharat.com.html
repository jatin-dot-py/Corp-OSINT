Automated Car Parking System

Designing, Manufacturing and Delivering Innovative Parking Solutions

Driving Urban Development and Advancing Automated Parking with Swiss Technology to Make India Smarter

Play

Sotefin Bharat is leading the evolution of automated parking across India. With Swiss engineering at its core, we deliver smart, space-saving and sustainable parking solutions. As cities evolve, the shift from conventional to automated parking is no longer optional — it’s essential. At Sotefin Bharat, we support smart city initiatives with scalable, efficient and sustainable technologies. We closely work with real estate builders and Government bodies to make this transition seamless.

Sotefin stands as a shining example in parking construction technology, having garnered global recognition long before multiple brands of automobiles adorned and brightened the streets of India. Established in 1956, Sotefin secured its inaugural patent in 1959, bringing to light a radically new trolley system facilitating the seamless transfer of parked vehicles. Since its inception, Sotefin has remained at the forefront of innovation, continuously evolving to meet the powerful as well as complicated demands of the contemporary times. Today, we proudly reveal our masterpiece and crowning glory, the pinnacle of mechanized car parking management systems.
