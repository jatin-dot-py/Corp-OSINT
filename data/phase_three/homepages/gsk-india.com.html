It has come to our attention that fraudulent job offers and recruitment messages claiming to represent GSK are being circulated. Applicants are hereby notified that these messages are not authorised and are part of a job scam intended to mislead applicants.

GSK will never ask for any payment(s) from applicants at any point in the recruitment process. All legitimate job opportunities are communicated only through our official channels. All individuals who are offered employment with GSK are always required to go through a formal recruitment process.

If you receive any suspicious communication claiming to offer employment with our organisation, please refrain from engaging with the email sender. DO NOT make any payments under any circumstances and report the email to us, along with relevant details/attachments, by writing to askus@gsk.com

We appreciate your vigilance in helping prevent such fraudulent activities.

Who we are

As a focused biopharma company, we discover, develop and deliver medicines and vaccines to create value for patients and shareholders. We aim to positively impact the health of 2.5 billion people by the end of the decade.

Our R&D focus is on four therapeutic areas: respiratory, immunology and inflammation; oncology; HIV; and infectious diseases.​
