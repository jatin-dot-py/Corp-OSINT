Sustainable Manufacturing

Putting Cause Before Business; we firmly in sustainable manufacturing practices & to embolden our efforts in this endeavor we:

  • 1. Source almost 70% of our energy requirements from Solar Installations enabling us to save 11,103 MT of carbon emission per year.
  • 2. 100% recycling of waste generated during production with 3 in-house waste recycling plants with a combined capacity of 10.5 MT per day
  • 3. Zero liquid discharge facility, we have a sewage Treatment Plant with a total capacity of 150 KLD/day & rainwater harvesting with a capacity of 100,000 litres per day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the top FIBC bag manufacturers in India?

India has several established FIBC bag manufacturers, with Kanpur Plastipack Limited (KPL) being one of the best FIBC bag manufacturers in the industry. KPL has been manufacturing high-quality FIBC bags since 1972 and serves clients across multiple industries worldwide.

Why is KPL considered a leading FIBC bags manufacturer?

KPL has earned its reputation through five decades of consistent quality, advanced manufacturing capabilities, and strict adherence to international standards. The company operates three modern facilities in Kanpur with over 3,000 skilled employees and maintains comprehensive testing protocols for all products.

What makes Kanplas a trusted bulk bags manufacturer?

Kanplas has built trust through reliable product quality, timely delivery, and comprehensive customer support. The company offers customized solutions, maintains international certifications, and has successfully served diverse industries including chemicals, pharmaceuticals, food, and construction materials.

Where can I find reliable jumbo bag manufacturers in India?

Reliable jumbo bag manufacturers can be found through industry associations, trade directories, and direct company research. KPL, headquartered in Kanpur, offers nationwide service and has established itself as a dependable partner for businesses requiring consistent quality jumbo bags.

Does KPL offer customized FIBC solutions for different industries?

Yes, KPL provides customized FIBC solutions tailored to specific industry requirements. The company's engineering team works closely with clients to develop bags with specialized features, dimensions, safety ratings, and performance characteristics that meet unique operational needs.

How is KPL different from other FIBC manufacturers in India?

Can KPL provide bulk orders of FIBC bags for export?
