Hello, We Are
Marine Electricals

We are integrated electrical and automation solutions provider for Marine and Industry / Commercial sector.
We offer wide-ranging solutions like integrated platform management system, power generation and distribution, marine lights, motors and NavCom solutions for shipping segment.
Our industry segment focuses on providing low and medium voltage power distribution solutions for industries like Oil and Gas, data centre, banks, airports, metro rails, hotels and hospitals and so on.
Being in the market for over 45+ years, our list of clients includes many renowned names from different industries. We are proudly associated with 'Make in India' initiative
