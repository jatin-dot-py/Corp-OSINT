Forging a Legacy of Excellence Since 1960

Mr. Baba N. Kalyani
Chairman & Managing Director
Bharat Forge emphasizes collaboration to drive India’s global economic rise. Aligning with Viksit Bharat’s vision, we have transitioned from traditional methods to AI-powered digitalization, revitalizing manufacturing, boosting exports, and expanding globally through R&D and automation.

Bharat Forge and our subsidiaries' strategic focus ensures alignment with market trends, solidifying India’s position as an innovation hub on the world stage.
Mr. Amit B. Kalyani
Vice-Chairman & Joint Managing Director
Bharat Forge leads in quality engineering, innovation, and cost-effectiveness. Upholding our mission statement, we continue to foster sustainable growth, empower industries, and drive technological progress while prioritizing customer satisfaction.

By continuously upgrading technologies, Bharat Forge Ltd. remains a cornerstone in shaping a resilient and inclusive industrial ecosystem at a global level.
60 years of manufacturing excellence

As we celebrate Bharat Forge's 60th anniversary, we honor the people, grit, and pioneering spirit that have shaped our journey from a forging company to a global engineering powerhouse.This milestone, more than a reflection on our legacy, marks the beginning of an exciting new era - as we continue to pursue high-growth opportunities, advance engineering innovation, strengthen our global competitiveness, and contribute to India's industrial and economic progress.
