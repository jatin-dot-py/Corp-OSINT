Frequently Asked Questions

What types of rubber products does Ameenji Rubber Limited manufacture?

Ameenji Rubber manufactures a wide range of industrial rubber products, including bridge bearings, expansion joints, rubber sheets, cow mats, skirt rubber, UIC vestibules, anti-vibration pads, extruded profiles, electric mats, gym tiles, and more catering to infrastructure, rail, industrial, and agricultural sectors.

Does Ameenji offer customized rubber solutions for specific industrial needs?

Absolutely! We specialize in custom engineering, including bespoke designs, sizes, materials (EPDM, Neoprene, Silicone, Natural Rubber, Nitrile), and certifications. From custom extruded profiles to UIC vestibules and expansion joints, our team collaborates with clients to meet precise specifications.

Can I get a quote or technical datasheet for Ameenji’s products?

Yes, simply contact us via phone or email on our Contact page. We’ll provide detailed product datasheets, CAD drawings, and tailored quotations based on your material, size, and application requirements for global supply across India, USA, and Africa.

What industries use Ameenji Rubber products?

Our products serve a wide range of sectors:
Infrastructure (bridges, highways)
Railways & metros (UIC vestibules, rubber pads)
Industrial (machinery pads, extrusion seals, conveyor skirting)
Agriculture (cow mats, chicken-cleaning mats)
Fitness (gym flooring tiles and rolls)
Electrical safety (electric insulation mats)

How does Ameenji ensure product durability and performance?

We use high-grade materials like flame-retardant EPDM, oil-resistant Neoprene, and UV-stable Natural Rubber. All products undergo third-party lab tests in NABL‑accredited facilities. Our manufacturing complies with ISO standards, and we maintain rigorous in-house QA to guarantee durability under heavy-duty and export conditions.
