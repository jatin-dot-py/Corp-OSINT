What We Do

We shape workforce ecosystems at scale, spanning diverse staffing, digital, and global workforce models to bring structure, continuity, and resilience to complex talent environments worldwide.

General Staffing Solutions

We deliver flexible, large-scale staffing solutions aligned to evolving business needs. From workforce planning and onboarding to deployment and compliance, we help maintain operational continuity across locations. Trusted by leading enterprises, we manage end-to-end recruitment with a focus on efficiency and productivity.

Know more

IT Solutions

We enable access to specialized IT talent across project-based and long-term engagements.
Our staffing and managed service models support contract and permanent roles, delivering deployment-ready expertise in data science, cybersecurity, AI/ML, and other emerging technologies across sectors.

Know more

Recruitment Services & RPO Solutions

We manage end-to-end recruitment for organizations building critical, long-term talent through a digital-first RPO model. Designed for high-impact and business-defining roles, our approach improves hiring speed, quality, and predictability, enabling confident talent decisions aligned to enterprise priorities.

Know more

Global Capability Center Solutions

Through Origint, we enable global enterprises to build, operate, and scale Global Capability Centers in India. Our end-to-end model integrates talent, infrastructure, compliance, and managed operations, bringing speed, structure, and operational stability across markets.

Know more

Global Mobility Services

Through Quess International Mobility Services, we help you source and deploy international talent across key markets with our end-to-end mobility services. From recruitment to documentation and deployment, we ensure your cross-border hiring process is compliant, seamless, and reliable, supporting your global talent needs.

Know more

Digital Platforms

The Hamara Suite (Hamara Jobs, Hamara HR, Hamara Academy & Hamara Benefits) is a digital ecosystem that formalizes and empowers the workforce at scale. Integrating staffing, payroll, compliance, upskilling, and financial benefits, it improves visibility and drives productivity, creating structured, inclusive platforms where large workforces are supported and enabled to thrive.

Know more

GIG Workers & Talent Solutions

With Taskmo, we help you quickly deploy gig talent for hyperlocal staffing needs. Serving businesses of all sizes, from startups to Fortune 500 companies, our flexible, digital-first approach ensures fast, reliable, and scalable workforce solutions tailored to meet your on-demand needs.

Know more

Why Choose Quess

Tailored for businesses, trusted by talent, and driven by scalable solutions for sustainable growth.

Our value-creation journey began in 2013 with Fairfax Financial Holdings Limited as a strategic investor, strengthening our governance and growth vision. Thereafter, Quess Corp was listed in 2016, marking a key milestone in our scalable, tech-enabled growth story. We have delivered consistent performance, maintaining a 100% dividend payout over the last three financial years and in the last six years we have paid out a total dividend of ₹850 cr. As of March 31, 2026, shareholding stands at Promoters – 56.83% and Public – 43.17%..

At Quess, work is a pathway to growth and meaningful impact across eight countries. Through our Employee Value Proposition, OCEAN: An Ocean of Opportunities, we enable our people to build expertise, lead with confidence, and explore diverse career pathways.

With 50% women in our core workforce and seven consecutive Great Place to Work recognitions, Quess Corp is ranked #19 among India’s Best Workplaces (2025). Further reinforcing our industry leadership, Staffing Industry Analysts named us India’s No. 1 staffing company (2025) and ranked us 37th globally (2024).
