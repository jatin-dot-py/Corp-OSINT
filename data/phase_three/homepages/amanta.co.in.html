About Us

Taking fluid therapy forward…

Amanta Healthcare, located in Ahmedabad (Gujarat), has emerged as India's most versatile manufacturer and marketer of sterile dosages in Aseptic Blow – Fill – Seal (BFS) and Injection Stretch Blow Moulding (ISBM) space with well-integrated operations – F&D to full-scale manufacturing & capability to develop customized solutions (containers, closures & drug delivery).
