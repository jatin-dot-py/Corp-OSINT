Why Choose Us

Gowra Leasing & Finance Limited

Gowra Leasing & Finance Limited (GLFL) is a trusted Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) registered with the Reserve Bank of India and incorporated on 9th February 1993. Categorized as a Loan Company by the RBI, GLFL has been serving mid-corporate and retail customers with reliable and customer-focused financial solutions for over three decades.

Listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), GLFL has built a strong reputation through its commitment to integrity, professionalism, and quality service. The company offers a wide range of financial products, including Mortgage Loans, Business Loans, Loan Against Rent Receivables, Loan Against Shares & Mutual Funds, Bill Discounting, Personal Loans, and Loans to Corporates and Professionals.

At GLFL, our strength lies in our experienced team, deep industry knowledge, and customer-centric approach. We believe in building long-term relationships by understanding client needs and delivering flexible financial solutions that support growth and stability.
