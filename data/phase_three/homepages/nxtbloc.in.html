About Bigbloc Construction

Bigbloc Construction Limited, incorporated on June 17, 2015, in Gujarat, is a pioneering building material manufacturer in India. As one of the country’s most trusted names in construction material manufacturing, we specialize in delivering eco-friendly and high-performance products tailored for modern infrastructure needs.
We are engaged in the manufacturing and supplying of AAC (Autoclaved Aerated Concrete) Blocks, construction chemicals, and building materials. Our core brands include:
1. NXTBLOC – Premium AAC Blocks made from fly ash and sand-based formulations
2. ZMARTBUILD Wall Panels (by NXTBLOC): Large-format AAC wall panels reinforced with corrosion-protected steel. Engineered for structural integrity and high thermal efficiency, these panels offer a fast-track solution for modern construction.
3. NXTFIX – Advanced block jointing mortar for faster and stronger bonding
4. NXTPLAST – High-coverage ready mix plaster for a smooth and durable finish
As a future-ready construction material supplier, Bigbloc delivers innovative, sustainable, and cost-effective solutions that contribute to greener building practices.
