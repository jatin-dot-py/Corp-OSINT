ABOUT US
Engineering Excellence in
Glass Bottle Manufacturing

With a legacy rooted in decades of glass manufacturing expertise, Haldyn Glass Limited is a trusted producer of high-quality glass containers serving industries such as food, pharmaceuticals, beverages, liquor, and beer.

Our advanced manufacturing capabilities, skilled workforce, and commitment to innovation enable us to create distinctive bottle designs that help brands stand out in competitive markets.

  • 1.5 Million Containers Produced Daily
