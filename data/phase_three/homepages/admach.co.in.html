History of the company

Admach Systems Limited was established in the year 2008 with an aim providing Machine Designing and Machine Building services for the Engineering Industry in India and abroad. Company is the manufacturer, exporter and supplier offering a gamut of customized special purpose machines. Our areas of specialization are SPMs, Automation, Assembly Machines, Packaging Machines, Product Design and Robotic Material Handling Systems. We serve to the various industries including Steel Industry, Automobile Industry, Food Industry, Tooling Industry, other engineering Industries. Offering customized and tailored products as per the customers' unique requirements

Team

Ajay Longani

(Managing Director)

Ajay Longani, the visionary founder of Admach Systems Ltd., brings over 30 years of expertise in the capital goods and equipment industry. A mechanical engineering graduate from Pune University, Ajay began his career as a design engineer and quickly advanced, showcasing exceptional technical and leadership skills. At Admach Systems, he has integrated cutting-edge technologies to deliver innovative, globally compliant engineering solutions. Ajay’s dedication and expertise continue to propel Admach Systems to new heights, establishing it as a trusted partner for sustainable and advanced engineering projects.

Mahesh Longani

(Director)

Mahesh Longani, Director of Admach Systems Ltd., brings 28 years of extensive experience in business and operations. A 1996 graduate in Instrumentation Engineering from Pune University, he is adept at leveraging modern technologies and optimizing supply chain processes to reduce costs. Renowned for fostering high-performing teams and driving growth, Mahesh has played a pivotal role in Admach’s rapid scaling and market success. His visionary leadership and innovative approach are widely respected in the industrial community, solidifying his influence in Admach’s ongoing journey of excellence.
