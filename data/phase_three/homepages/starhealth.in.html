Understand Your Policy

  • Pay a premium to receive coverage for hospitalization, treatments, and care.
  • Coverage includes surgeries and medical expenses as per your plan.
  • Purchase policies via branches, advisors, website, or intermediaries.

Understand Your Premium

  • Premium depends on plan type, coverage amount, age, and health details.
  • Family floater plans consider all members’ age and overall health.
  • Provide accurate details to ensure smooth coverage and full benefits.

Intimate a Claim

  • Choose cashless at network hospitals or reimbursement after paying bills.
  • Submit required documents via app, email, hospital desk, or call centre.
  • Track claim status online; settlement depends on policy terms.

Why should you buy a Health Insurance plan from Star Health Insurance?

 

Health insurance is an essential requirement in today's world. It provides financial security to individuals and families in case of medical emergencies. With the increasing cost of healthcare in India, having a Health Insurance plan is a necessity.
While there are many insurance providers in India,  Star Health Insurance stands out for its unique features and benefits.

 

About Star Health Insurance

 

Star Health Insurance is a leading Health Insurance provider in India. The company was established in 2006 and is headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.
Star Health Insurance offers a wide range of Health Insurance plans that cater to the different needs of individuals and families. The company has a presence across India, with more than 16,000 network hospitals.

 

Why Choose Star Health Insurance?

 

  • Wide range of Health Insurance plans: The company offers plans for individuals, families, senior citizens, and corporate employees. It also offers special policies catering to individuals with diabetes, heart disease, cancer, and children with special needs.

 

  • Cashless Hospitalisation: Cashless hospitalisation is one of the most significant benefits of buying a Health Insurance plan from Star Health Insurance. The company has tie-ups with more than 16,000 network hospitals across India. In a medical emergency, the insured can avail of cashless treatment at any network hospital. As per policy terms the insured does not have to pay any money for the treatment, and the insurance company settles the bill directly with the hospital.

 

  • Coverage for pre-existing diseases: Star Health Insurance offers coverage for pre-existing diseases in its Health Insurance plans. Pre-existing diseases are medical conditions that an individual has before buying a policy. PED are covered after the waiting period as mentioned in your policy terms.

 

  • Maternity benefits: Star Health Insurance offers maternity benefits in its Health Insurance plans. Maternity benefits cover the medical expenses related to hospitalisation during childbirth. The company offers maternity benefits through family floater and individual health insurance plans.

 

  • 24/7 Customer Support: Star Health Insurance offers 24/7 customer support. Customers can contact the company's dedicated customer support team via phone, email, or chat.
