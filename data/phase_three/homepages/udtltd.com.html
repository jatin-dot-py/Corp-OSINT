Manufacturing precision equipment and tools

We are the manufacturer of Large OD multi-start casing connectors and casing pipes, wireline winches, gas lift equipments and downhole tools which matches customer specifications and quality needs. Founded in 1985, UDT relentlessly focuses on pioneering solutions for Exploration and Production sector with R&D clubbing and the use of global technology that enables higher savings for oil production companies. UDT along with its employees serves to offer quality products & services at unmatched prices for our customers. Our aim is to continuously improve and evolve with the changing times.

The manufacturing facilities have five key manufacturing departments, which are as under:
* Machine shop with CNC and Special-Purpose Lathes, Milling Machines, Drilling Machines, Cylindrical Grinders etc.
* Fabrication, Assembly and Testing Shops.
* Design, Research, Development and Engineering Dept.
