Our Products

Sugar

A pioneer sugar company in South India having vast experience in the field of Sugar, Power and Alcohol production.

Alcohol

Two Distilleries with total production capacity of 125 KLPD to produce Rectified Spirit, Extra Neutral Alcohol and Anhydrous Alcohol.

Organic Manure

GreenPLUS is an organic manure originating from the aerobic solid state fermentation of sugar industry press mud using concentrated.

Green Power
