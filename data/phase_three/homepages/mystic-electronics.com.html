Welcome to MYSTIC ELECTRONICS LIMITED:

(Formerly Pearl Electronics Limited)

The Company was incorporated on 30th August, 2011 under the Companies Act, 1956 as PEARL ELECTRONICS LIMITED (PEL).
Mystic Electronics Limited (Formerly Pearl Electronics Limited) is a Public Limited Company registered under the Companies Act, 1956 and got listed on Bombay Stock Exchange in the year 2013. The Registered Office of the Company is situated at Mumbai.
Pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement/Demerger sanctioned by Hon’ble Bombay High Court on 09th October, 2012, the Electronics Division of Nouveau Global Ventures Limited was demerged into Pearl Electronics Limited with effect from appointed date i.e. 1st October, 2011.
In the year 2016, the Company changed its name from “Pearl Electronics Limited” to its current name “Mystic Electronics Limited”
