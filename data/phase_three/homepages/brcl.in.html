Corporate Profile

Textile segment - 

The Baroda Rayon Corporation Limited (BRCL), a public company, was incorporated in 1958 by Gaekwad Chinai and Company Private Limited as Managing Agents. With the abolition of the Managing Agency system, the subscribers of the Memorandum and Articles of Association were no more connected with the Company and Lt. Col. Fatehsinghrao Gaekwad (former Maharaja of Baroda State) had taken over management of the Company with the team of professionals to run the company. The Company is engaged in textile industry, in Surat City of Gujarat, a city basically known worldwide for its Diamond and Textile Business. Plants & Machineries of the Company is conveniently located at the heart of City. Company location offers easy access to Railway Station, Bus Station, Highway and to Surat’s entry – exit points. The Baroda Rayon Corporation Limited, is public limited company listed with prime stock exchange of India, The BSE Limited, Mumbai and having its registered office at P O Fatehnagar, Udhna, Surat – 394220 (Gujarat).

Real Estate Segment - 

The company will develop the existing land at Udhna in Surat City and will construct Industrial Houses in Phase manner.
