Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of copper products do you manufacture?

We manufacture a wide range of copper products, including bare copper strips, paper-covered copper conductors, multi-paper covered connection cables, copper wire rods, copper bus bars, and copper round bars.

2. What industries do you serve?

We serve a diverse range of industries, including electrical, construction, manufacturing, and more. Our copper products are used in electrical wiring, power distribution, grounding systems, construction materials, and various manufacturing applications.

3. How can I place an order with DCG Cables & Wires?

You can easily place an order with us by contacting our sales team via phone, email, or our website. We will work with you to understand your specific needs and provide a customized solution for your copper product requirements.
