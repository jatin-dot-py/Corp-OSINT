Robust Infrastructure, Sustainable Future.


Capital Infra Trust (erstwhile National Infrastructure Trust) is an InvIT which priorities strategic investments in Infrastructure projects mainly national highways. These projects bridge geographical gaps, fostering economic activity and unlocking the potential of underserved regions.

InvIT is committed to responsible development and to build a sustainable Infrastructure platform, delivering superior risk-adjusted returns to unitholders. InvIT aspires to be a leading force for positive change in India's infrastructure landscape.


Philosophy

We are driven by a clear vision to empower India's growth trajectory through a robust framework focusing on Growth, Governance, and Goal.

  • Growth
  • Governance
  • Goal

Our focus lies on making strategic investments in Infrastructure assets mainly National Highways (NHAI) through acquisition from Sponsor and third parties. These projects act as crucial arteries, bridging geographical divides and unlocking the economic potential of underserved regions leading to fostering regional development and stimulating economic activity. Looking beyond immediate gains, our focus is on creating accretive value for unitholders.

We uphold high standard of corporate governance, ensuring complete transparency in financial reporting, project selection, and decision-making. We believe in openness which builds trust with investors, stakeholders, and the public. Further, we foster strong partnership with experienced players across the infrastructure value chain. Our commitment to responsible development ensures a future where economic progress and environmental well-being go hand-in-hand.

Sustainability is our core principle which helps in unlocking long-term value for our investors, stakeholders, and the nation by fostering responsible infrastructure development. We strive to integrate environmental and social considerations throughout the project lifecycle.

Our long-term goal is to efficiently monetize infrastructure projects and maximise the distribution to the unitholders.
