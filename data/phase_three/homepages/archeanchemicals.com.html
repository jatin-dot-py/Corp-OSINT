VISION

We want to be one of the world's most respected specialty chemicals companies. We start with the sea. From its brine, we create materials that power industries across energy, agriculture, pharma, and technology. Our goal is not just to grow bigger. It is to grow better. We are building a company whose products, people, and practices raise the bar for responsible chemical manufacturing in India and across the world.

We combine the power of natural resources, advanced manufacturing, and rigorous science to create consistent, sustainable value. Every day, we work to strengthen the trust of our customers, support the growth of our people, and operate with responsibility towards our communities and the environment.

About Us

Company
Overview

Founded 2009

Archean Chemical Industries Limited is a leading speciality chemicals and advance material company. We are India’s Largest integrated facility producing industrial salt, bromine, and sulphate of potash from natural sea brine. Our main plant is located in Rann of Kutch. We serve 40 domestic and 25 global customers, with 78% of our revenue coming from international markets.

We have expanded into downstream bromine derivatives through Acume Chemicals and Speciality Oilfield Chemicals through Idealis Mudchemie. We are also investing in advanced materials, including silicon carbide semiconductors and zinc-bromide battery technology. From marine chemicals to next-generation materials, we are building a diversified, high-value speciality chemicals platform for the future.

OUR BUSINESS

Marine Chemicals

Archean manufactures speciality marine chemicals from natural seawater brine at Hajipir, Kutch. Core products include Industrial Salt, Bromine, and Sulphate of Potash with combined annual capacity exceeding 6 million metric tons.

Bromine Derivatives

Speciality bromine compounds and downstream derivatives for pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, flame retardants, and industrial applications worldwide.

Oilfield Solutions

Idealis manufactures high-performance drilling fluid additives for volatile downhole environments. Its specialized upstream product portfolio optimizes fluid rheology, hydrostatic pressure, and wellbore stability across global oil and gas exploration projects.

Advanced Materials

Archean is establishing India’s first integrated compound semiconductor fabrication and ATMP platform at Info Valley, Bhubaneswar, Odisha through its subsidiary SiCSem Private Limited. This strategic investment demonstrates Archean’s commitment to advanced materials and semiconductor manufacturing.
