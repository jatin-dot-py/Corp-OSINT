Who We Are

Rashi Peripherals Limited is India’s fastest‑growing value-added distributor of consumer technology, embedded/semiconductors, and enterprise solutions. With our robust distribution network, nationwide presence, comprehensive brand portfolio, and value-added support, we are enabling technology adoption and deployment across the country. We differentiate ourselves by offering pre‑sales support, solution design, techno‑commercial assistance, marketing services, credit solutions, and warranty management services. We play a critical role in India’s ICT ecosystem by enabling adoption and deployment of high-end enterprise technology and digital infrastructure solutions.
