Welcome
DCM Nouvelle Limited under the name of DCM Textiles established in 1991 is in cotton spinning operations. DCM Textiles is based at Hisar, in Haryana State, a cotton belt of North India. Today we are leading manufacturer & exporter of 100% cotton carded and combed yarns in single and two-ply forms of count range Ne 14s to 40s
