Chairman's Message

  • ‘To achieve success one has to keep in mind, four important tenets: Honesty, Hard work, Punctuality and Sincerity.”
    Keeping this in mind I started my journey with minimal capital but a strong desire to succeed. We were one of the first apparel exporters from India and have exported garments to 21 countries till date.
    In the 50’s, Indian exporters suffered from bad repute and Indian made products were considered of inferior quality. We decided to change this perception with our hard work, quality and continual adoption of latest technology. We were successful in our endeavor and have repeat orders and continuous patronage from our buyers to show for it.
    From initial manufacturing facilities of less than 3000 square feet, we have grown today to a total of 300,000 sq. ft. with a capacity to manufacture 600,000 sweaters and 720,000 T-shirts per year.
