Evolution Of Legacy

BRAND LIBERTY WAS BORN

The company's inaugural factory and warehouse commenced operations on April 9th, 1954. Subsequently, it was renamed Liberty Footwear Company, reflecting the founders' core principle: freedom from dependence and subjugation.

...read more

1964

CERTIFICATION ON BOARD

We received our Certificate of Commencement of Business on March 11th, 1988. Solidifying our ability to manufacture and sell wide range of footwear, including both leather and non-leather options, while tapping into shoe uppers and garments.

...read more

1988

BIG STRIDES IN DISTRIBUTION AND SUPPLY CHAIN

We expanded our national presence by opening 40 new showrooms across India, with a particular focus on the South Indian market. To further enhance customer access, the arena became bigger and we established a network of 30 franchise stores and 20 company-owned and company-operated (COCO) stores. These series of initiatives are complemented by investments in strengthened logistics and technology, along with a strategic entry into the e-commerce space.

1954

Pal Boot House

Established in Karnal, Haryana, in 1954 as "Pal Boot House" by Dharam Pal Gupta, Purshotam Das Gupta, and Rajkumar Bansal. The company began with a modest production capacity of just four pairs of shoes per day.

...read more

1975

From India TO the World

Liberty Shoes took a significant step towards becoming a global brand by securing it's first export order. This milestone shipment went all the way to USSR (Union of Soviet Socialist Republics), a major achievement considering the political and economic climate of the time. This event marked the beginning of Liberty Shoes' international journey and their commitment to expanding beyond the domestic market.

...read more

1994

Liberty shoes limited Takes Flight

Incorporated in 1986, Liberty Shoes Limited became a publicly traded company. In order to, cater diverse audience from different landscape, we also launched 10 sub-brands during this period; which includes Fortune, Gliders, Prefect, Senorita, Tiptop, Windsor, Coolers, Footfun, Force 10, and Warrior. This master stroke accelorated the foundation for our future growth and established us as a key player in the footwear industry.

...read more

2022

Growing Yonger

We are rejuvenating brand's image with a youthful approach, strategically launching brands like Leap7X and Healers to capture the attention of new-age customers. This evolution is reflected in our modern product designs and dynamic brand communications, demonstrating our commitment to staying relevant and resonant with the contemporary consumer while maintaining our heritage of quality and innovation.
