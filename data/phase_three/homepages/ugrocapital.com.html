About UGRO Capital

UGRO Capital Limited is a DataTech Lending platform listed on NSE and BSE, focused on addressing India’s small business credit gap through its proprietary DataTech approach and diversified distribution network. The Company has built a scalable MSME lending franchise with a strategic focus through its network of over 300 Emerging Markets branches delivering small ticket loan against property and merchant-led embedded financing to underserved emerging MSMEs.

UGRO’s embedded finance model enables seamless credit access for MSMEs through partnerships with fintech platforms and payment ecosystems, delivering timely financing to merchants at the point of business need. The Company leverages advanced data analytics, AI/ML-driven credit scoring (GRO Score 3.0), and proprietary underwriting model to enable faster, data-driven lending decisions and expand access to formal credit for small businesses.

The Company is backed by marquee institutional investors and guided by a strong governance framework, UGRO continues to advance financial inclusion while supporting the larger vision of “India by MSME.”

⚠️ Important Alert: Beware of Foreclosure Scams

We have identified fraudulent attempts by individuals impersonating UGRO Capital representatives, sharing fake foreclosure letters and payment instructions via calls, WhatsApp, or email.

Key Safety Guidelines:
  • UGRO Capital does NOT collect any payments via WhatsApp.
  • We do NOT ask for loan payments, foreclosure amounts, or charges through calls, WhatsApp, or emails.
  • Do NOT share your loan details, OTPs, or account information with anyone.
  • Do NOT make any payment without verifying through official UGRO channels.
  • You can contact your Relationship Manager (RM) to verify the details before making any payments.
