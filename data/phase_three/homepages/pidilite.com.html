Our Categories, Brands and Businesses

Adhesives, Pigments, Waterproofing and more

We are committed to making a difference  - with superior solutions for adhesives, automotives, textiles, arts and crafts and waterproofing, woodworking and MEP solutions. We believe that a truly innovative solution or a quality sealant can change our customers’ lives for the better.

Categories

Woodworking Adhesives

An industry leader in adhesives that are made strong through water-based, solvent-based and hot melt technologies.

Waterproofing

Innovative construction chemicals that are tailored to meet the requirements of various construction projects.

Tile and Stone fixing

World-class products to suit various tile applications and care - fixing tile-on-tile, new tiles, grouts for tile joints and tile cleaners.

Maintenance and Repair

Trustworthy and suitable products and services, such as instant bond adhesives, leak repair, speciality coatings, , sealants, lubricants and more.

Art, Stationary, Fabric Care

Beloved Craft Adhesives and Art Colors like white glues, glue sticks, acrylic paints, and tempera colours.

Industrial Products

Specialty products that cater to the manufacturing sector such as pigments, adhesives, sealants, industrial and textile resins, construction and paint chemicals, synthetic resins and more.

Decorative Paints

Finely crafted paints for ‘India Inspired’ homes that are distinct and durable.

Wood Finishes

A range of solutions from basic wood polishing to advanced wood coating, guaranteed to make furniture long-lasting and beautiful.
