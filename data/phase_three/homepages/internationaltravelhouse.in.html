International Travel House (ITH)

International Travel House Limited (ITH) is one of India’s leading travel management companies. It started operations in 1981 and is the first publicly listed Indian travel company. ITH offers a diverse product portfolio, including business travel management, car rental services, meetings and events, and leisure travel.

The company is certified under ISO 9001:2015 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and has a strong national footprint across 19 cities. This year, the company has earned the EcoVadis “Bronze Medal” in its Year-2 sustainability assessment. The Bronze Medal serves as an important indicator of the company’s commitment to sustainability and its ongoing efforts in environmental, social, and governance (ESG) practices.

Driven by its philosophy of excellence in customer service and operations, ITH focuses on providing complete travel and mobility solutions to corporates and individuals. The objective is to enable a seamless travel experience and help travellers achieve more from each trip.
