OUR STORY

Concerted Efforts To Build Better.

Ahmedabad Steel Craft Limited is a trusted name in the steel and industrial materials industry, with a legacy spanning over five decades. Incorporated in 1972 and headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, the Company has steadily built its presence through its focus on quality, reliability, and long-term relationships. The Company is primarily engaged in the trading and distribution of steel and engineering products, catering to infrastructure, construction, and industrial sectors. Its offerings include structural steel such as beams, channels, flats, rounds, and plates, along with a diverse range of engineering tools, machinery, hardware, and industrial equipment.
