Cropster Agro Limited

(Previously known as the Planter’s Polysacks Limited)

The Company was incorporated as a Public Limited as on 17th May, 1985 under the Companies Act, 1956 with the Name of Planter's Polysacks Limited at the Bombay. The Name of the Company has been changed to Cropster Agro Limited.
The Company is BSE Main Board Listed Company.
The Main Object of the Company is to carry on the business of preparing, manufacturing, processing, marketing, trading, import, export, improve, sell and deal in all kinds of agro/agri/food products including but not limited to spices, oil seeds, grains, vegetables, herbs, pickles and other items derived from agricultural, farming or relevant activities
