Product Leadership
Like No Other

Product Leader in Automotive Drivetrain Components and
Systems with over 50 years of manufacturing expertise.
Learn More

about usThe Divgi group was established in 1964. Our spirit of innovation has driven breakthrough technologies and cutting-edge solutions in 4-wheel drive systems, automatic and manual transmission catering to an expanding global customer base.

Who We Are
We are a Tier 1 product leader in automotive drivetrain components and solutions with over fifty years of manufacturing expertise.
What We Do
We provide world class torque transfer and transmission solutions to OEMs encompassing utility, passenger, commercial, electric and agriculture vehicles in India and across the globe.
How We Do It
We design & develop, market & sell and supply & support products customized to Indian and international customer requirements.

Milestones

Drag
1964
  • Divgi Metalwares was formed to manufacture gears, fasteners, and drivetrain components.
    Consolidated business with orders from Phillips, Kirloskars, Mahindra and Tata.
1965
  • BorgWarner T90 3-speed transmission components, Dana T18 transfer case and engine timing set for the Kaiser Willys ‘Hurricane’ engine. These Mahindra Jeeps with the Vickers recoilless guns were used in the battle of Assal Uttar in Punjab.
1969
  • First supplier to Mahindra and Tata in India with the full complement of blanking, gear cutting and gear grinding for drivetrain components.
1970's
  • Addition of Eicher, Escorts, Marshall and Sons, Voltas, Siemens and BHEL to customer base.
1980's
  • Divgi group helped Mahindra localize the Peugeot BA10 transmission components.
  • Two of India’s first three modern automobile transmissions were localized with considerable
    contribution by Divgi Metalwares to Hindustan Motors and Mahindra.
1994
  • Divgi Metalwares became India’s first company to manufacture automatic transmission gears for John Deere’s Funk Powertrain Division in Coffeyville, Kansas, USA.
  • Various types of gears & shafts supplied to JCB, Faridabad.
1995
  • Joint Venture agreement signed with BorgWarner Automotive, USA to manufacture a wide range of drivetrain components and solutions for the SAARC region.
1996
  • Developed full set of BA10 transmission components for M&M.
1997
  • First transfer case supplied to Tata Motors.
1998
  • Inauguration of Sirsi plant and introduction of gear teeth honing processes.
  • First transfer case supplied to Mahindra.
1999
  • Automatic Locking Hub manufacturing commenced.
2001
  • Office set up in Muncie, USA for ‘Project Columbia.’
  • Commenced exports to Ford, GM, Hyundai and Ssangyong.
2006
  • Asia’s first torque o demand transfer case project awarded.
  • Export to Mazda, Ford Thailand commenced.
2007
  • Introduction of triple cone steel synchronizers.
2010
  • Developed 2 critical shafts for PTU for front wheel based all-wheel drive systems for M&M W201 project.
2014
  • Volvo Eicher added as a new customer.
2015
  • Won the Confederation of Indian Industry’s National Quality Award for Excellence in Total Quality Management.
2016
  • Divgi TorqTransfer Systems Pvt Ltd was established
  • Develop & Launch of Heavy Duty Dual-offset Transfer-case
2018
  • Develop & Launch of Torque On-demand Transfer-case (TOD)
2019
  • Developed Dual-offset Transfer-case for M&M Roxor for North American market
  • Nextrac Interactive Torque Coupler locally assembled
2020
  • SOP of Mechanical transfer-cases for Mahindra Thar
2021
  • SOP of Nextrac ITM for XUV7OO capable of handling torque upto 800 Nm
  • Developed CAN based Electric-Shift-on-Fly Transfer-case for Mahindra Z101 - Scorpio N
  • Developed Forward-Reverse shuttle - 90mm single cone synchronizer for the M-Star tractor application
2022
  • Develop & Launch of EV Transmission for Tata Motors
  • Development of TOD Transfer-Case for MG Gloster
  • Development of Dog-Hub for Audi AL Program through Borg-Warner, Portugal.
  • SOP of Gear-Planetary-Sun (GPS) for Toyota Hybrid, India
  • We went public! Now we are Divgi TorqTransfer Systems Limited.

Our Valued Customers

Areas of Excellence

Product Engineering

Our product engineering division specialize in design and development of rear wheel and front wheel drive solutions for a wide variety of vehicle architectures. We cater the application of such technologies to suit our customers requirements.

Manufacturing Engineering

With over 20 years of expertise in the manufacturing of automotive drivetrain components & solutions, Divgi TTS has honed its skills in manufacturing engineering to a fine art. We focus on design optimization to deliver superior quality products to our customers.

Manufacturing

To achieve the flawless quality and customer satisfaction, organisation follow robust manufacturing systems, which is recognized as Divgi TTS Production system. It consists of 7 foundational elements and 23 tactical strategies.

Quality Management Systems

Quality Systems Basics (QSB) forms the pillar of Divgi TTS Production Systems. It has, over the years propelled the commitment and drive of the team to achieve manufacturing excellence.

Global Supply Chain

At Divgi TTS we seek to propel our business goals and long-range plans by building and cementing effective long-term relationships with our suppliers. Improving process efficiency, reducing waste and cutting costs with uncompromising quality serves to be the backbone of our global SCM team.
