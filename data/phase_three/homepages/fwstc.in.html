Aviation Courses After 12th: Choose the Right Career Path

If you have completed Class 12 and are interested in an aviation career, the right training program depends on the role you want to pursue. Cabin crew training can prepare you for in-flight service and safety responsibilities, while ground staff programs focus on airport operations and passenger services. Students interested in service-oriented careers can also explore customer service and hospitality training.

Before choosing a course, consider the eligibility requirements, training duration, practical exposure, career pathway and the skills you will develop during the program.
