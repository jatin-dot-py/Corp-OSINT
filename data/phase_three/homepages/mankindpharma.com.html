Formulations
A range of pharmaceutical formulations across acute and chronic therapeutic areas. Multiple brands within the same therapeutic area allow us to cater to the requirements of a wide range of patients.
Consumer
Healthcare
A range of consumer healthcare products to meet the non-prescription segment ranging from antacids to condoms to pregnancy test kits.
Active
Pharmaceutical
Ingredients (APIs)
High-quality Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients to help patients get access to affordable and innovative medicines. We manufacture NCEs, generic APIs, advanced organic intermediates, and agrochemicals
Veterinary Care
Achieving a remarkable 200 crore business within a decade of establishment, we're a leading player in the Indian veterinary sector. Our comprehensive offerings include nutritional supplements, anti-infectives, dewormers, hormonal preparations, and acidifiers.
Pet Care
We support the pet care ecosystem with a diverse lineup of nutritious offerings, including food, medicine, supplements, and grooming products. Our UK-made pet food, sourced from Europe, meets BRCGS standards, ensuring international quality and safety.
Agritech
We meet the agri-input and crop protection demands of growers for safe, productive and sustainable production. Our R&D Centre and collaborations with multinationals bring world-class crop protection solutions to Indian farmers.
