Who We Are

EKI Energy Services Ltd. (EKI), erstwhile EnKing International in the climate sector globally, is a leading Carbon Credit Developer & Supplier across the globe. The Indore headquartered company is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

EKI is 17+ years old and is a global market leader with deep expertise in the realm of climate change, carbon offset solutions, and carbon asset management. The company is today present in 16+ countries and has 3500+ clients across 40+ countries worldwide. As on date, EKI has supplied over 200+ million offsets.
