About delton group

Embarking on our journey close to 76 years ago, Delton Cables has evolved into one of India’s foremost conglomerates in the electrical sector. Spanning diverse business verticals, including Cables & Wires, Networking Solutions, and Switchgear, our commitment to excellence has made us a trusted name in the industry.

Distinguished across residential, commercial, industrial, and infrastructure applications, Delton Cables pioneers futuristic design and engineering. Our range of top-quality products is a testament to our dedication to pushing boundaries and embracing innovation.
