  • BA/BE

  • Clinical Trials

    We are offering a broad range of Clinical Trial Services from Phase II to IV for Pharmaceutical, Biotechnology and Medical Device to bring new drugs and devices to global reach faster. We also extend our services to other areas like Cosmetics and Neutraceuticals.

Who We Are

Jeevan Scientific is an Independent Clinical Contract Research Organization established in the year 1999. We forayed into Clinical Research in 2014 with a motto of providing quality and cost-effective services to Pharma and Biotech companies across the globe.
