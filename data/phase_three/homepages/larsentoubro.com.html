ENGINEERING A SUSTAINABLE FUTURE

Raising the bar to the next level, L&T is undertaking big goals related to Climate Change and Water Security. We aim to achieve Carbon Neutrality by 2040, ahead of the Paris Agreement deadline of 2050, and Water Neutrality by 2035.

Sustainability is embedded in our vision, culture, strategy, and business processes. We are committed to sustainable growth, and integrate Environment, Social and Governance (ESG) principles in all aspects of our business.
