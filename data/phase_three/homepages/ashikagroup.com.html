Alternative Investment Funds (AIF)

Ashika India Alpha Fund and Ashika India Select Fund are two schemes managed by the parent company, Ashika Investment Managers Pvt. Ltd., launched under our Category III Alternative Investment Fund (AIF). These funds aim to maximize risk-adjusted returns over the mid and long-term through a long-only or long-biased strategy. The portfolios primarily consist of equity and equity-related securities of India-domiciled issuers and are sector as well as market cap agnostic.

Ashika Capital Limited, a future-focused investment banking firm acted as the lead advisor to Dehradun-based Windlass Developel from SWAMIH Investment Fund I to the tune of Rs. 171 crores for completion of its Township project - Windlass River Valley.
