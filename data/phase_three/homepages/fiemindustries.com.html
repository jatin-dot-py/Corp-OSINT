Welcome to Fiem Industries Limited

With a history of over 50 years, Fiem Industries Limited has scripted a trailblazing path in its field with farsighted vision, innovation and sheer dynamism. Today, FIEM is a well reputed name in the automotive lighting industry with 9 state of the art manufacturing facilities spread across India, R&D/ Design Centers in India, Japan and Italy and is a preferred supplier to leading OEMs in India and abroad.

Our Company is promoted by Dr. J.K. Jain, Chairman and Managing Director, who is a first generation promoter. He has a rich experience of about 40 years in automotive components industry
