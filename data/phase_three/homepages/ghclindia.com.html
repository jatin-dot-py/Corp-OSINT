About GHCL Limited

Incorporated in 1983, GHCL is India’s largest soda ash manufacturer at a single location. Our portfolio includes Dense Soda Ash, Light Soda Ash, and Sodium Bicarbonate, manufactured with precision and consistent quality standards.

GHCL powers several key Indian industries, including glass, ceramics, detergents, baking powder, solar panels, and lithium-ion batteries supporting the nation’s infrastructure growth and EV ecosystem. Backed by captive raw material resources and our trusted brand ‘LION’, we are committed to strengthening India’s industrial self-reliance while enabling a greener and more sustainable future.

Sustainability

At GHCL, sustainability is embedded in our long-term vision and operational strategy. We are advancing cleaner and more efficient manufacturing through continuous investments in sustainable technologies, carbon reduction initiatives, and process optimization. By integrating innovation with environmental responsibility, we aim to reduce Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 30% by 2030, while contributing towards a more sustainable industrial future for India.
