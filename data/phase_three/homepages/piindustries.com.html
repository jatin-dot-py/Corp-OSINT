Sustainability – Our Way of Life

Water Conservation

With cutting-edge systems, we recycle over 185 million litres of water every year. In FY24 alone, 113 million litres were recycled, an 87% increase from FY23. Supporting our commitment to responsible water use and long-term sustainability.

Waste Management and Circularity

With strategic partnerships, 100% recycled packaging and Eco Scale tracking, we reduce hazardous waste and optimise waste generation per metric tonne, advancing circularity across operations.

Renewable Energy

We harness solar power across key facilities to cut carbon emissions. Renewable sources now contribute 6.60% of total energy use. The shift to cleaner fuels has more than doubled this share, significantly reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
