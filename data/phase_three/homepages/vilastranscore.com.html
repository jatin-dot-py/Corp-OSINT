VILAS TRANSCORE LIMITED

Established in 1996 as Vilas Transcore under Mr. Nilesh Patel's leadership, the company excels in Transformer Lamination, Toroidal Cores, and CRGO Slitted Coils.

With over 30 years of expertise, Vilas Transcore is a market leader known for innovation and reliability. Committed to high standards in products and business practices, the company values long-term relationships, emphasizing customer satisfaction at its core

→ Prosperity → Principle → Responsibility

True prosperity involves benefiting both the company and individuals. Vilas Transcore is dedicated to global social and environmental responsibility in every community it operates.

Business

CRGO

CRGO's superior magnetic properties ensure efficient transformer cores with minimized energy losses. It enhances performance and reduces costs, making it a vital material for transformer manufacturing.

Read More...

NANO CRYSTALLINE

Our nano crystalline materials are engineered for high-performance transformer applications, offering exceptional magnetic properties, low core loss, and high permeability. Ideal for power electronics and renewable energy systems, they ensure efficient energy transfer and reduced energy losses.

Read More...

AMORPHOUS

A revolutionary material for solar inverters, transformers, and high-frequency applications, offering high efficiency, low loss, and exceptional thermal stability across a wide temperature range.

Read More...

RADIATOR

Engineered for efficiency and reliability, our Radiator optimizes heat management, enhances cooling performance, and extends transformer longevity.

Read More...

CTC - CONDUCTOR

Copper, valued for its high conductivity and durability, is crucial in transformer manufacturing, ensuring efficient power transmission with minimal energy loss and environmental impact.
