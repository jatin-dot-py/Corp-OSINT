    About Us

    At Ecoline Exim Limited, sustainability isn’t just what we do – it’s who we are.

    Founded in 2012 by a team of passionate leaders, Ecoline was born from a simple but powerful idea: to replace single-use plastics with eco-friendly, durable, and stylish alternatives that make a real difference.

    What started as a humble initiative in India has grown into a global force, leading the movement for sustainable packaging. Today, we proudly serve clients across 30+ countries, including key markets in North America, Europe, the Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East.
