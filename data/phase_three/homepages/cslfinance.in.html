Why Choose CSL Finance

CSL Finance Limited, incorporated in 1992, is an RBI-registered NBFC listed on both stock exchanges. With a presence in 7 states and 50+ branches, we provide secured loan solutions to SMEs and corporates—bridging the gap between ambition and capital while creating value for employees, customers, and businesses alike.
