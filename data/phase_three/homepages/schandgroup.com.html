Our Business

We are a leading Indian education content company delivering content, solutions, and services across the education lifecycle through our presence in Early Learning, K-12, Higher Education, Test Preparation, and AI-ready data solutions. We have a strong foothold in CBSE, ICSE, and International Curriculum affiliated schools, with a growing presence in state board schools across India.

Bringing People & Knowledge Together

We offer more than 13,000 book titles and sold over 30 million books in the last year. Our pan-India distribution and sales network provide deep market reach, supported by over 4,000 distributors and dealers.

Our in-house sales team of more than 1,500 professionals, operating from over 50 branches and marketing offices, enables us to connect with K-12 and higher education institutions across the country. Through our strong distribution network, we serve all 29 states and 7 union territories in India.

We also export our print content to over 15 countries and deliver digital content across Asia, the Middle East, Africa, Singapore, and other global markets.
