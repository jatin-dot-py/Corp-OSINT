SHIVA GLOBAL AGRO INDUSTRIES LTD.

Shiva Global Agro Industries is a fast-growing, research-led agriculture company. An emerging leader in the agro products domain, we manufacture fertilizers, soil and crop health products, Oil, de-oiled cakes (DOC). Along with its subsidiaries, Shiva Global Agro Industries strives to fulfill its commitment towards India’s food security by way of products that help farmers increase crop yield.

Shiva Global Agro Industries began as a fertilizer manufacturing unit in 1993 and grew over the next 10 years to become a company that provides a holistic range of solutions for the farmer’s agri-based needs and the ability to manufacture our own raw materials has ensured that we are able to supply quality, cost-effective products to the Indian farmer.

Shiva Global Agro Industries’ team ensure that our products adhere to stringent quality norms. Our products are available to the smallest farmer across 3 states in India. Our skilled workforce’s efforts have strengthened our operational excellence, business practices and unique marketing outreach programs.
