Legacy – Landmark

The history of Shalimar Paints is the history of the paint industry in India and South-East Asia. Shalimar Paint Color & Varnish Co. was established in 1902 in Howrah, West Bengal by two Britishers - A N Turner and A N Wright. In the same year, the company set-up a large-scale manufacturing plant in Howrah, the first such plant in South-East Asia.

With rapid expansion and a commitment to quality, Shalimar Paints soon emerged as India's leading paint brand. Our paints have graced the walls of iconic structures like Rashtrapati Bhavan, Howrah Bridge, Vidyasagar Setu, and Salt Lake Stadium, standing the test of time with their enduring beauty. Shalimar Paints has 50+ depots, 3 manufacturing facilities, 1 state of the art R&D Center and the company services 12,000+ dealers across the country.

Welcome to a World of Enchanting Paints

When looking to buy the best emulsion, enamels, varnish, distemper, primer, oxides, or putty for a painting project, your search ends at Shalimar Paints. We offer a range of options across all product categories to cater to every unique need and help you bring out the desired aesthetics. Our high-quality products help create thousands of dream homes every year and we are constantly adding new options to give you an unmatched color palette with exclusive options. Welcome to the home of home wall paints.

What’s in Store?

Shalimar Paints offers you home wall painting solutions for the interior, exterior, metal, wood, and the floor.

Interior

For your interior home wall painting, we offer products such as Emulsions, Distempers, Primers, and Putty.

Exterior

For your exterior home wall painting project, you can choose from a range of Emulsions, Primers, Putty, and Cement Paint.

Metal

When you wish to paint metal fixtures and other items to bring out the desired style during a home wall painting project, Shalimar Paints offers a range of premium quality Primers and Enamels.

Wood

When it comes to painting wood fixtures and other items, we offer some of the best Primers, Enamels, and Varnish.

Floor

We also offer a popular solution for your floor painting requirements in the form of Heart Brand Floor Oxide.
