    Digital MRI

    MRI Scan is a non-invasive imaging technique that uses magnetic waves to capture detailed images of the internal structures of the body. It is primarily used in medical diagnostics as it effectively reveals both pathological and physiological changes in tissues and organs. A full-body MRI scan is often recommended for patients experiencing acute or chronic pain. Specific scans, such as those for the spine or knee, are suggested for individuals facing discomfort or mobility issues. The cost of an MRI scan varies depending on the type of scan performed and the diagnosed condition.

    Know More

    Digital CT Scan

    A CT Scan is a non-invasive diagnostic tool that uses X-rays to create detailed cross-sectional images of the body. It is commonly used to detect bone fractures, joint problems, internal bleeding, or masses caused by trauma or disease. CT scans are essential for identifying conditions like tumors, emphysema, or heart disease, and are often recommended for ongoing monitoring. Specialized scans may be needed for specific areas such as the chest or abdomen. The cost of a CT scan depends on the type of scan and the area being examined.

    Know More

    Digital Mammography

    Mammography is a safe and accurate imaging method that uses low-dose X-rays to examine breast tissue. It plays a crucial role in the early detection of breast cancer and other abnormalities, even before physical symptoms appear. Women are advised to undergo regular screenings, especially after the age of 40, or earlier if there is a family history. Digital mammograms provide clearer images, improving the accuracy of diagnosis. The cost of mammography varies based on location and facility, but early screening can be life-saving.

    Know More

    Ultrasound & Colour Doppler

    Ultrasound with Colour Doppler is a non-invasive imaging method that uses high-frequency sound waves to create real-time images of internal organs and blood vessels. The Colour Doppler function adds visual information about blood flow, helping identify blockages, clots, or abnormal flow patterns. This technique is commonly used in pregnancy scans, abdominal studies, and vascular assessments. It offers quick, safe, and accurate results without radiation. The cost of an ultrasound or Doppler scan depends on the type of study and the area being examined.

    Know More

    Digital X-RAY

    Digital X-ray is a quick and painless imaging technique that uses minimal radiation to capture high-quality images of bones and internal organs. It is widely used for diagnosing fractures, infections, and chest or abdominal conditions. The digital format allows for enhanced clarity and faster processing, leading to quicker diagnoses. Expert radiologists review every scan to ensure accurate results and timely care. The cost of a digital X-ray depends on the body part being examined and the clinical requirement.

    Know More

    DEXA Scan

    DEXA Scan is a non-invasive test that uses low-dose X-rays to measure bone density and assess the risk of osteoporosis. It is commonly recommended for individuals at risk of bone loss, especially postmenopausal women or those with a history of fractures. The scan is quick, safe, and highly precise, helping detect early signs of weakening bones. Accurate results from a DEXA scan allow doctors to create personalized treatment plans. The cost of a DEXA scan may vary depending on the area scanned and the diagnostic need.
