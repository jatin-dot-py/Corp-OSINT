Welcome to Classic Filament Limited

Classic Filaments Ltd was incorporated as a Private Limited Company in the name and style of V.Natwarlal Synthetics Private Limited. The name was changed to Classic Filaments Private Limited on 10.09.1993.

Our Mission

Our Mission is to provide best-in-class products and services to our customers from every walk and stage in life.

Our Vision

Our Vision is to become the strategic player in the market and to create a niche for us.

We Can Ensure a Secure and Comfortable Future

The company was subsequently converted into a Public Limited Company vide the Board Resolution dated 21.01.1995 and fresh certificate was obtained on 15.02.1995
