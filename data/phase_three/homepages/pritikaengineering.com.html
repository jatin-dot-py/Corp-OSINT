ABOUT PRITIKA GROUP

Established Since 1974

Pritika Group of Industries founded in 1974 by Mr. R.S. Nibber, a young technocrat manufacturing small forgings, is today a leading manufacturer and supplier of machined castings designed for applications in tractors, commercial vehicles, material handling equipment and more. Pritika is a steadily growing conglomerate comprising five distinct business entities strategically located across North India. With an annual production capacity of 60,000 metric tons of iron castings encompassing both ductile and cast iron grades with weight ranging from 5 kg to 320 kg, our organization prides itself on its extensive manufacturing and machining capabilities.
