WHO WE ARE?

Apollo Techno

Your Trusted Source for Trenchless Technology and Foundation Technology

Apollo Techno stands as India’s leading manufacturer of horizontal directional drilling rigs and foundation rigs. Born from a vision to revolutionize the industry, we’ve rapidly emerged as a symbol of engineering excellence and innovation.

Our expertise lies in crafting high-quality, new-generation equipment that meets global standards while proudly carrying the “Made in India” badge. We don’t just manufacture drilling machines; we engineer drilling solutions that drive progress in infrastructure development worldwide. Our products, engineered with precision in India, have found their way to global markets, a testament to their world-class quality.

Looking ahead, we’re poised for exciting growth with plans for new product lines and market expansion. At Apollo Techno, we’re not just building machines; we’re shaping the future of foundation drilling technology.
