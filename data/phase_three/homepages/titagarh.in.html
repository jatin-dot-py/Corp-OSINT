    1997

    " Titagarh Rail Systems Ltd began as a freight wagon manufacturer in 1997, producing 15 wagons per month. With a vision to become the leader in the Indian market, Titagarh embarked on a gradual but determined path. Its forward integration from foundry manufacturing enabled it to achieve its goal and become a significant exporter of freight wagons and components from India."

    2007

    "Titagarh commenced the production of its initial EMU/MEMU units for the Indian Railways. Over time, Titagarh has delivered more than 350 transit systems to the Indian Railways, marking the highest number achieved by any private rolling stock manufacturer in India. Titagarh has significantly expanded its foundry capacity to 25,000 metric tons, up from 12,000 metric tons, through the acquisition of a new facility. This expansion is aimed at fulfilling the needs of both the Indian Railways and global markets."

    2013

    Titagarh has come a long way from its initial production capacity of 180 wagons annually. Today, it proudly holds the Research Designs and Standards Organisation (RDSO) - G105 certification and boasts an annual production capacity of 8,400 wagons annually.

    2014

    Titagarh has established a fruitful partnership with the various organizations of the Ministry of Defence, specializing in the supply and installation of specialized heavy engineering equipment such as Mobile Shelters, Integrated Field Shelters, Metallic Canisters and many more for India`'`s Defence sector.

    2015

    The acquisition of Firema SpA in Italy marked a significant milestone for Titagarh, making it the first private Indian company to gain access to cutting-edge technology in modern Passenger Rolling Stock and Propulsion Systems. This strategic move bolstered Titagarh`'`s expertise in the Passenger Division by integrating the extensive 100-year industry experience of the Italian company, Firema. This merger now operates under the brand name Titagarh Firema Spa, showcasing the synergy of knowledge and capabilities from both entities.

    2019

    Titagarh has secured its inaugural order for Metro Coaches within India. The company has proudly unveiled India`'`s pioneering aluminum-bodied metro coaches, to be produced indigenously for the Pune Metro project, in alignment with India`'`s transformative initiatives like Make In India and Atmanirbhar Bharat.

    2022

    "Titagarh has successfully delivered the inaugural warship for the Indian Coast Guard, `'`Kamla Devi,`'` as part of a collaborative effort with GRSE.

    Titagarh has secured the largest-ever order from the Indian Railways, totalling 24,177 wagons. This significant order will be fulfilled over a span of three years. This milestone marks a pivotal moment in the nation`'`s development journey and underscores the Indian Railways`'` crucial role as an essential contributor to the Make in India initiative and its commitment to achieving an annual freight loading target of 3,00,000 tons."

    2023

    "Titagarh & BHEL the truly Atmanirbhar consortium, has successfully secured a groundbreaking contract for manufacturing and maintenance (for 35 years) of 80 Vande Bharat trainsets, along with the modernization of government manufacturing units and the trainset depot.

    Titagarh and Ramkrishna Forgings Consortium has been awarded with a pioneering contract for manufacturing and supply of 1,540,000 forged wheels.

    Titagarh has secured contracts from Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation for designing, manufacturing, supplying, testing, commissioning, and providing training for 72 Standard Gauge Cars, for the initial phase of the Surat Metro Rail project and 30 standard gauge cars for the Ahmedabad Metro Rail Phase-II project."

    2024

    Titagarh achieved a significant milestone by ramping up the production of traction motors to 50 units per month, marking a major step forward in our commitment to strengthening India’s indigenous manufacturing capabilities in rail mobility.

    Further reinforcing its role in shaping the future of urban transport, Titagarh proudly unveiled the design for Phase-II of the Ahmedabad Metro to the Hon’ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi, showcasing its dedication to modern, efficient, and sustainable metro solutions.

    2025

    Titagarh Rail Systems Limited has achieved a series of landmark deliverables, reinforcing its strategic position in India’s rail and mobility ecosystem.

    The company successfully delivered its first indigenously manufactured, fully driverless trainset for BMRCL’s Yellow Line, representing a defining milestone in India’s advanced rail manufacturing capabilities. In parallel, Titagarh has operationalised dedicated production lines for the Vande Bharat trainsets, further strengthening its footprint across high-speed and urban transit segments.

    Titagarh Rail Systems Limited also marked a significant achievement in urban mobility with the rollout of the first driverless trainset for the Ahmedabad Metro. Additionally, the company is privileged to contribute to Bihar’s first-ever metro rail service, with state-of-the-art aluminium-bodied metro coaches—designed and manufactured in India—now deployed for passenger operations in Patna, reflecting Titagarh’s continued commitment to indigenous design, manufacturing excellence, and nation-building.

    Expanding beyond rail into the maritime domain, Titagarh commissioned and launched six 25-ton Bollard Pull tugs for the Indian Navy, underscoring its cross-sector engineering depth and its alignment with the Make in India vision.

    2026

    Titagarh Rail Systems Limited continues to strengthen its commitment to customer-centric and efficient mobility solutions through its Wagon Leasing Company agreement with Indian Railways, enabling faster wagon deployment and optimised freight capacity.
    In parallel, Titagarh has partnered with ABB India for propulsion systems for 25kV metro projects, including the Transfer of Technology (ToT) for TCMS and localisation of key components at its Uttarpara facility. These milestones reflect Titagarh’s commitment to Atmanirbhar Bharat, indigenous manufacturing and a Viksit Bharat, aligned with the vision of Hon’ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi.
    Titagarh Rail Systems also inaugurated its new Design & Operations Centre at Uttarpara, marking another step towards strengthening its design, engineering and operational capabilities. The centre was inaugurated in the presence of Shri Tapas Roy, Hon’ble Minister of Industry & Commerce, Government of West Bengal, and Shri Arjun Singh, Hon’ble Minister of Labour & Transport, Government of West Bengal.

    Ahmedabad Metro

    Forged Wheel Sets

    The consortium of Ramkrishna Forgings Limited and Titagarh Rail Systems Limited (RKFL-TRSL Consortium) has signed the contract, for “Manufacturing and Supply of Forged Wheels under Aatma-Nirbhar Bharat by the Ministry of Railways, Government of India”. The total quantity of Forged Wheels to be supplied will be around 15,40,000 wheels over a period of 20 (twenty) years.

    Mumbai Metro

    Titagarh Rail Systems Limited has been awarded a contract by the Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA) to manufacture 108 standard-gauge metro coaches for Line 6 of the Mumbai Metro Rail Project, in association with NCC Limited. The total project value is Rs. 6,672 crores, with Titagarh’s share amounting to approximately Rs. 2,501 crores. The execution of the contract is stated to commence 62 weeks after signing the contract and is expected to be completed in 154 weeks thereafter.

    Pune Metro

    Titagarh Rail Systems Limited secured a contract from Maha Metro in 2019 for the design, manufacture, and supply of metro rolling stock for the Pune Metro project. As part of the agreement, the company will deliver a total of 102 metro cars (34 trains) out of which 3 trains manufactured at its Italy facility and the remaining 31 trains produced at its Indian plants. The total value of the contract stands at ₹1,125 crore.
