Science That Scales.
Manufacturing That Delivers.

Integrated CRDMO services across discovery, development and commercial manufacturing for small molecules, peptides, lipids, oligos, high-potent APIs and large molecules.

Explore Our Capabilities

Small Molecules

Expert synthesis and development of low-molecular-weight compounds, ranging from medicinal chemistry leads to complex high-potent APIs.

Discovery

Accelerating drug candidates through lead optimization, scaffold design, and comprehensive SAR studies to identify viable clinical molecules.

Clinical & Commercial

Scalable cGMP manufacturing providing end-to-end CMC support, process validation, and late-stage production for global market supply.

Large Molecules

Comprehensive development of biologics, including recombinant proteins, monoclonal antibodies, and enzymes using advanced microbial and mammalian platforms.

Discovery & Pre-clinical

Bridging research and development through cell line engineering, upstream optimization, and rigorous analytical characterization for safety and efficacy.

Clinical & Commercial

Integrated cGMP production with advanced purification, fill-finish capabilities, and regulatory support for large-scale therapeutic biologic manufacturing.
