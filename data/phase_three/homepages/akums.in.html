      Akums Drugs and Pharmaceuticals Ltd.: Leading the Way in Pharma Manufacturing

      ▼

      Akums Drugs & Pharmaceuticals Ltd. stands as India’s largest and most trusted Contract Development and Manufacturing Organization (CDMO), redefining pharmaceutical excellence through innovation, quality, and efficiency. As a preferred partner for leading pharmaceutical and healthcare companies worldwide, Akums is committed to delivering groundbreaking formulations that meet evolving market needs.

      With 15+ state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities, Akums offers unmatched expertise across diverse therapeutic segments and dosage forms, including tablets, capsules, injectables, soft gels, syrups, topicals, nutraceuticals, cosmeceuticals, derma solutions etc. Our robust R&D capabilities drive innovation, ensuring fast, cost-effective product development that aligns with stringent regulatory standards.

      Akums is also a trusted leader in Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) manufacturing, ensuring a seamless and high-quality supply of essential raw materials for pharmaceutical formulations. Our API capabilities are in compliance with international regulatory standards, supporting the development of superior formulations while enhancing supply chain reliability for our partners.

      Quality at Akums is more than a benchmark—it is a culture. We adhere to global regulatory standards, including WHO-GMP, USFDA, EU-GMP, and TGA, ensuring that every product meets the highest safety and efficacy standards. Our advanced Quality Management System (QMS) and compliance-driven approach reinforce our leadership in pharmaceutical manufacturing.

      Akums collaborates with 1500+ pharmaceutical companies, manufacturing over 4100+ commercialized formulations. Our expertise extends beyond manufacturing to include formulation development, technology transfers, regulatory support, and customized solutions, helping brands expand their portfolios and market presence seamlessly.

      While innovation fuels our growth, sustainability shapes our future. Akums integrates eco-friendly initiatives and energy-efficient manufacturing processes, ensuring responsible business operations that contribute to a healthier planet.

      Leading the Make in India initiative, Akums continues to revolutionize global healthcare by providing high-quality, innovative pharmaceutical solutions with a patient-first approach, strengthening its position as a driving force in the industry.
