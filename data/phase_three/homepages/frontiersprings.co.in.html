About

Established in 1981, Frontier Springs Ltd. started with the production of Leaf Springs and Laminated Bearing Springs for Automobiles and Railways. Steadily as the railways modernized, so did we and within a few years had expanded our plant to accommodate the high demand for Coil Springs for wagons, railways and locomotives.
