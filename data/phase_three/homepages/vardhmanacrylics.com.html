Our Profile

Vardhman, a leading textiles group in India ventured into manufacture of Acrylic Fiber & Acrylic Tow in 1999. A modern plant with technology from Japan Exlan Co. Ltd, Japan, a company in forefront of innovation in this field was set-up initially as a joint venture with Japan Exlan Co. and Marubeni Corporation, Japan in the state of Gujarat in western India.
