Who We Are

Delivering Purity and Excellence for Over a Decade

At Solvex Edibles Limited, we are committed to producing refined edible oils that combine purity, nutrition, and trust. Established in 2013, our journey has been built on modern technology, sustainable practices, and a dedication to quality that meets global standards.

With a state-of-the-art facility in Uttar Pradesh and a processing capacity of 200 tonnes per day, we specialize in Mustard Oil, Rice Bran Oil, and De-oiled Cakes that cater to both households and industries. Our products are trusted across 18 states in India, serving families, retailers, and feed industries with uncompromised quality.

We take pride in being an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, delivering not just oils, but a promise of health, reliability, and care. Guided by strong values, ethical practices, and decades of promoter experience, Solvex Edibles stands as a name synonymous with trust and nourishment.

Your Trusted Partner in
Refined Edible Oils & Sustainable By-Products

Pure oils and sustainable by-products for every need.

Refined Rice Bran Oil

Extracted from the nutritious layer of rice, our Refined Rice Bran Oil is rich in antioxidants and Vitamin E. With a high smoke point and neutral flavor, it is ideal for everyday cooking as well as industrial food applications.

Mustard Oil

Cold-pressed and aromatic, our Mustard Oil is valued for its bold flavor and health benefits. Traditionally used in Indian kitchens, it also carries natural antibacterial properties, making it a trusted culinary and wellness choice.

De-oiled Rice Bran (DORB)

A key by-product of rice bran oil extraction, DORB is protein-rich and nutrient-dense. Used widely in cattle, poultry, and fish feed, it supports healthy growth while ensuring better feed efficiency for farmers.

De-oiled Mustard Cakes (DOMC)

Produced after oil extraction, our mustard cakes serve as a sustainable, protein-rich supplement. Nutritious and affordable, they are an excellent choice for cattle and poultry feed, helping improve livestock health.
