About Kilpest Since 1972

Kilpest India Ltd is one of India's leading Agri based companies. Kilpest is an ISO certified company and has representation in India in the field of agriculture business comprising Crop Protection Products and Public Health Products, Bio products, Micro-Nutrients and Mix fertilizers.
Moving with a lustrous record of providing quality products to its customers since past many years, the company's management is now shifting its focus towards 'Biotechnology', keeping in view the hazardous effects of chemical pesticides. Since Biotechnology has been described as a "Sunrise sector" by Government of India, the company is now on its adventurous journey towards exploring potentials of Biotechnology in the field of Organic Agriculture, Public Health, Nutraceuticals, industrial enzymes etc.

Kilpest India Ltd is one of India's leading Agricultural based companies

Kilpest is an ISO certified company and has representation in India in the field of agriculture business comprising Crop Protection Products and Public Health Products, Bio products, Micro-Nutrients and Mix fertilizers. The Company has one of the most advance Research & Development facility at its Bhopal Plant and also has Biological Research Centre. The products are manufactured to high international / BIS specifications with stringent quality control from raw material procurement to finished goods despatch. The Company's manufacturing process is complimented with the technical expertise and it has attained high standard suited to customer needs. The Company has a nation wide distribution network. Kilpest is also doing toll formulations for multinational industries. Products of Kilpest are exported to several countries. Company is Listed with Mumbai stock exchange.
