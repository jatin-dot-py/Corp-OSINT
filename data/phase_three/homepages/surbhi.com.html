Surbhi Group was established in 1976 by Mr. Ratibhai Patel and Mr. Ravjibhai Patel.

On a very small scale but a wider vision to stamp a distinguished mark in textiles business. Passing on the torch of substantial success to the young generation, the active & collective participation of these family members has augmented the scope of the work, perception & technical know-how of this family business.
