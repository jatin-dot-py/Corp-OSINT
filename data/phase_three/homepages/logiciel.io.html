Questions

Straight answers.

What does "owns implementation, governance, and security as one responsibility" actually mean?
A single accountable team for every layer. We don't ship the model and hand off the audit trail to someone else. The same engineering org designs the system, governs it, secures it, and stays accountable until it's running in production.
We already have an internal AI team. Where do you fit?
Most clients have a roadmap and not enough capacity to own its implementation. We integrate with your team, follow your standards, and absorb the build, so your internal team keeps direction and we close the gap to production.
How is the AI Intelligence Layer different from buying a vector DB and an LLM?
It's the connective layer between your data and your products. Governed retrieval, decision and automation APIs, audited outputs, and continuous learning, built once, consumed by every internal team that needs intelligence as a service.
We're regulated. Does your work meet our security and compliance bar?
Yes. Governance and security are designed in from day one, not added afterward. We build systems your security teams approve, your compliance teams audit, and your board can stand behind.
Which industries do you go deep in?
Real Estate, Energy, and Healthcare: production environments where AI must be implemented, governed, and secured at a serious bar. We've built the muscle for all three and continue to specialise here.
How does an engagement start?
One 30-minute conversation. We map your AI roadmap against the gap to production, propose the right scope, and start when you're ready. Whether you're scaling fast or running enterprise complexity, the process is the same.
