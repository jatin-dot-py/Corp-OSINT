Media Matrix worldwide Limited (MMWL)

Media Matrix Worldwide Limited (MMWL) is a B2B focused next-generation technology and services provider, specializing in Value Added Services (VAS) across the mobile and digital ecosystem. Beyond its core VAS offerings, MMWL is also engaged in the distribution of wide range of innovative products in mobility, audio, Consumer Electronics and IT segment across India through one of its subsidiaries.

Media Matrix Enterprises Private Limited (MMEPL)

MMEPL is wholly owned Subsidiary of Media Matrix Worldwide Limited.
MMEPL has been incorporated on 2011 with objectives for making strategic investments in its subsidiary/ associate/ group companies and/ or other companies.

nexG Devices Private Limited (NDPL)
NexG Devices Private Limited is a multi-brand and multi-product -telecom device distribution organization which uniquely bundles white-labelled products from global OEMs with services from telecom operators to offer a wide range of customized solutions to customers.

A strong network of pan-India distributors and warehouses forms the backbone that allows NexG Devices to provide leading brands with reliable countrywide rollout and product availability.
