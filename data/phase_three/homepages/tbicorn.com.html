Introduction

About
The Company

We are a leading manufacturer in the corn grits industry. Established in 1999, our fully automated machinery ensures a production capacity of 150 Tons per day.

We’re an ISO 22000:2018 and HALAL Certified Corn Milling company, catering to the entire domestic market in India as well as the snack & animal feed industries the world over.

Our company sources the highest quality raw maize from Northern Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh, which accounts for 38% of India’s total Maize cultivation.

Shipment

We stand out for delivering on time, even in tricky markets. We know our customers rely on us, so we always make sure to meet our delivery promises without compromise.

Quality

At TBI CORN LTD, we’re all about quality. Our products match the samples we send you…guaranteed. We’re committed to producing and supplying top-notch quality, no matter what.

Warehousing

We store our products in a well-equipped warehouse with proper ventilation and protection against moisture and rain. Our warehouse has ample space and is free of rodents.

Packaging

We use top-notch Poly packs for safe packaging. It keeps products fresh and dry during delivery, guaranteeing quality from our place to your doorstep.
