Operational Assets

Rajasthan

The Rajasthan block, covering 10,000 km², is situated in the Barmer district. The breakthrough discovery of Mangala in 2004 stands as the largest onshore discovery in India to date. This milestone marked the beginning of our journey in Rajasthan, laying a strong foundation for our success in the region.

Gujarat

We operate offshore blocks in the Lakshmi and Gauri fields within the Cambay Basin. In 2024, we submitted our first Field Development Plan (FDP) under the Open Acreage Licensing Policy (OALP) to initiate gas production from the Jaya field. Our commitment to offshore exploration and development in the Cambay Basin remains steadfast as we continue to invest in unlocking its full potential.

Andhra Pradesh

We began our journey in India with the Ravva redevelopment project in 1994, located in the shallow waters of the Krishna Godavari (KG) Basin on the east coast of India. This landmark project not only established our expertise in offshore development but also laid a strong foundation for our growth. As we look ahead, we are focused on increasing production from the KG Basin through strategic investments in deep-sea drilling programs over the coming years.

Assam

Situated in the Arakan Basin of Assam, we hold an extensive acreage of 7,650 km², which includes 12 blocks under the Open Acreage Licensing Policy (OALP) and three Discovered Small Fields (DSF) blocks. Our significant investments in exploration drilling programs are driving progress, and we are currently producing from the Hazarigaon field in Assam.
