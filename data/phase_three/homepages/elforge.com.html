Welcome To EL FORGE

EL FORGE is one of the leading forging producers in South India with over of 6 decades of experience in metal forming. EL FORGE serves major vehicle and automotive component manufacturers and process industries, both domestic and overseas. The range of materials handled include Micro alloy steels, Carbon, Alloy and Stainless Steel, and supplies are made both as forged and machined.

Vision

To serve global customers by building alliances with leading forging majors and acquiring and developing World Class manufacturing technology and practices through a continually developing humane organization to maximize shareholder value.

Our Services

The Best Service For You

EL FORGE's services are supported by fully equipped engineering department. This group besides designing the product related forging tools and process sequence also defines the quality norms to be followed at every stage of forging.

EL FORGE's engineering department for this purpose is equipped with the latest CAM/CAD and simulation facilities to support the customer's product development program.

Our Products

Light Duty Crankshafts

EL FORGE is a premier manufacturer of 'Light Duty Crankshafts' for single and twin-cylinder applications in power and compressor engines. The annual production exceeds 600,000 crankshaft forgings.

Learn More
Rocker Arms

EL FORGE supplies 'Rocker arms' for diesel engines in the weight range of around 2kg.

Learn More
Common Injection Rails

EL FORGE is a premier supplier of 'Common Rails'. HFR is new generation parts which are used in fuel injection systems. These products range from LCVs to MUVs and are manufactured with Micro alloy steel.
