    The MX Series is a multifunction high flexible machine enabling Milling & Turning operation roughing to finishing in one setup part clamping. A structure with mobile column, milling head equipped with integrated torque motor and swivels on 45° plane, tilting from -45 ̊ to 180 ̊. Powerful electro-spindle has two working position vertical and horizontal.
