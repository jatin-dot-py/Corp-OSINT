About NPL

National Peroxide Limited (NPL) is one of India’s leading manufacturers of Hydrogen Peroxide, delivering high-quality chemical solutions to diverse industries across domestic and global markets. With decades of manufacturing excellence, NPL is committed to safety, reliability, and responsible innovation.

The company continuously focuses on improving operational efficiency through advanced technology and sustainable manufacturing practices. NPL strives to maintain the highest standards of quality, environmental responsibility, and customer satisfaction. Through innovation and strong industry partnerships, NPL aims to support the evolving needs of industries while contributing to a more sustainable future.
