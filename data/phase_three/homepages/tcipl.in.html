About Us

About Tejas Cargo

A heavy-asset logistics operator scaling with discipline.

Tejas Cargo India Limited is an tech enabled integrated logistics and supply-chain company headquartered in Faridabad, India, delivering end-to-end logistics solutions across road, ocean, freight forwarding, bulk cargo, and commodity-driven businesses.

Our diversified business portfolio spans Freight Forwarding, Ocean Freight Forwarding, Full Truck Load Logistics, Container Transportation, Car Carrier Logistics, Bulk Cargo, Coal Trading, and Commodity Trading, enabling us to serve businesses across multiple sectors and supply-chain requirements.

Our integrated operating ecosystem is supported by 1,200+ commercial vehicles, 27+ branches, and strategically located maintenance and operational facilities across India, giving us the scale and infrastructure to manage complex logistics requirements.

In H1 FY26 we completed 55,972+ trips and recorded ₹306 Cr total income with EBITDA of ₹47.78 Cr — alongside a five-year EV deployment agreement with Amazon and an enterprise ERP rollout, both reinforcing our technology-led, scalable growth model.
