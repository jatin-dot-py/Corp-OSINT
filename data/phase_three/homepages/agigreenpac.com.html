Our Businesses

AGI Glaspac

AGI Glaspac: is a leading provider of sustainable glass packaging solutions. We specialize in research, design, development, manufacturing, and supply of high-quality glass bottles for various industries. Our commitment to sustainability drives us to create innovative and eco-friendly packaging options. With three state-of-the-art plants in India, we have a daily capacity of over 2000 tonnes, producing bottles from 1.5 ml to 6,000 ml.

Read More
AGI Plastek

AGI Plastek: is a leading supplier of high-quality PET packaging solutions, catering to a wide range of industries, including pharmaceuticals, alcoholic beverages, food and soft drinks, personal care, agrochemicals, and dairy. The company provides customized and innovative PET packaging bottles and products, particularly for the soft drinks, water, edible oils, and personal care sectors.

Read More
AGI Clozures

AGI Clozures: Specializes in producing high-quality, counterfeit-resistant security caps and closures for the alcoholic beverage industry. The company’s team of experienced professionals leverages cutting-edge technology to deliver innovative packaging solutions that protect brands from counterfeiting and safeguard consumers.
