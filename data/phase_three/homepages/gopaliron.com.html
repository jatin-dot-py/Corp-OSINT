Welcome to Gopal Iron & Steels Co. Gujarat Ltd.

Gopal Iron & Steels Co. (Gujarat) Limited (GISCO) is established in the year 1994. We are a leading manufacturer of Structural Steels, Bars and ERW Tube / Pipes in the state of Gujarat. Over the years, GISCO has been continuously upgrading with latest technology and infrastructure. GISCO has earned the status of preferred steel manufacturer for traders, customers, engineering, construction, infrastructure and other primary industry.

Since its inception, as Gopal Rolling Mills Private Limited, the company has never looked back. Every year has brought enormous rewards not only in the form of revenues and records, but also in the form of continuously growing customer base. The company was converted into a Public Limited Company in the year 1995 and since then it is known as Gopal Iron & Steels Co. (Gujarat) Limited (GISCO).

The company continuously pursues new grounds in the area of steel manufacturing and at present engaged in manufacture of Sections like Beams, Channels, Angles and Bars like Flats, Round, Square etc. Recently company has installed fully automatic with State-of-art technology Tube Mill for manufacturing of ERW MS, SS and Galvanized Round, Square and Rectangular Hollow Tubes under the name of “GOPAL” brand and successfully commenced quality production. Quality is our daily practice.

The visionary leader who has promoted Gopal Iron & Steels Co. (Gujarat) Limited is Mr. Bhavesh Patel & his family members has huge experience in this field. The company has rightly and deservingly received ISO 9001:2000 certificates first time in the year 2006 and since then it has been renewed year after year after year and now company holds upgraded ISO 9001:2008 quality certificate. The company is under the process of getting BIS Standards for their products. The logo of the company is registered under the Trademarks Act.

To save the environment, to boost up the concept of Green Gujarat and to save the power cost, Company has set up own power generating “SUZLON” make Wind Turbine Generating Unit in the year 2006 at Lamba, near Porbandar, District – Jamnagar, Gujarat, India and since working successfully.

GISCO is privileged to have earned highest respect and trust from all the stake holders across the spectrum. Integrity, Innovativeness, Striving for Excellence in all pursuits, Loyalty to all stake holders are some of the traits that have earned reverence to Gopal Iron & Steels Company (Gujarat) Limited.

Be it Government, Be it Customers, Be it Stake Holders, they all have offered only kudos to Gopal Iron & Steels Co. (Gujarat) Limited and we constantly strive to keep up this glorious image.
