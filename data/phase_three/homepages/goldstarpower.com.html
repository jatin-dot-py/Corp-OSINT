Welcome to Goldstar Power Limited, India's Reliable Inverter Battery Manufacturer.

Goldstar Power Limited has been providing excellent energy solutions for more than 35 years, and it is pleased to be the top battery manufacturer in India. Since founded by Shri Muljibhai Pansara, also referred to as "The Battery Man," we have established a reputation for excellence, creativity, and reliability. As a leader in the energy industry, our Goldstar brand is widely recognized for its dependability, cutting-edge technology, and sustainability.

Inverter batteries, automobile batteries, VRLA batteries, and E-rickshaw batteries are just a few of the energy solutions that Goldstar offers to satisfy a variety of needs in homes and businesses. We remain the go-to option for energy backup solutions in India because of our dedication to providing unrivaled quality.

Our Mission: Promoting Excellence

At Goldstar Power Limited, our top priorities are to establish new benchmarks in the battery manufacturing sector:

Client Contentment

At GOLDSTAR, we are dedicated to delivering exceptional customer satisfaction by providing high-quality, reliable battery solutions tailored to meet the unique needs of each client. Our goal is to comprehend and surpass the expectations of our customers.

Constant Improvement

At GOLDSTAR, we embrace a culture of continuous improvement, relentlessly pursuing innovation and efficiency in every aspect of our business. We are committed to refining our processes, enhancing product quality, and adopting the latest technologies to deliver superior battery solutions.

Driven by Innovation

At GOLDSTAR, we are driven by innovation, constantly exploring new technologies and solutions to push the boundaries of energy storage. Our commitment to research and development fuels the creation of cutting-edge, sustainable battery products that address the evolving needs of industries worldwide.
