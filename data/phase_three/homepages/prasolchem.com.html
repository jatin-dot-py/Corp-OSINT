Prasol Chemicals Ltd. – Driving Chemistry, Empowering Progress

At Prasol Chemicals Ltd., we are committed to excellence in chemical manufacturing, offering high-quality solvents, additives, and specialty chemicals to industries worldwide. With a strong foundation in innovation, sustainability, and reliability, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for businesses across Home & Personal Care, Performance Chemicals, Agrochemicals, Pharmaceutical and PICA (Paints / Inks / Construction / Adhesives) industries. 

Sustainability is at the heart of our operations. We prioritize eco-friendly processes, responsible sourcing, and waste reduction to minimize our environmental footprint. By continuously innovating and adopting sustainable practices, we aim to contribute to a greener future while meeting the evolving needs of our customers. 

With a commitment to quality, innovation, and sustainability, Prasol Chemicals Ltd. is your trusted partner for high-performance chemical solutions. Whether you’re looking for reliable supply chain support, advanced chemical formulations, or customized solutions, we are here to help you achieve success.
