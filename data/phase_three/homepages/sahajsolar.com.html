A Global Leader in Renewable Energy

Considering global warming, rising crude prices, and economic volatility, the world has turned to a cleaner alternative—Solar Energy. It offers a sustainable solution to the growing energy crisis. Sahaj strongly believes that renewable energy is the next revolution after semiconductors and computers. Among all renewable sources, solar stands out as the most aggressive and promising.
Sahaj plans and develops small to large Photovoltaic Solar pv power plant projects for its clients & investors. This includes carrying out the initial economic feasibility forecasts, finding a suitable location, securing title to the land (including obtaining all necessary permits) and taking care of all feed-in contracts or Power Purchase Agreements (PPA) with energy off-takers. The result is always a high-performance Solar PV power plant with the shortest possible period of amortization and an attractive return on investment.
