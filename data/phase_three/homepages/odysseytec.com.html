Complete Digital Security

xorkee is a digital security framework that enables authentication, digital signing, and verification across systems and applications.

It provides a single, consistent mechanism to identify users, secure actions, and validate digital information using established cryptographic algorithms.

The framework is designed to work with existing platforms and workflows, supporting multiple use cases such as document signing, email signing, and secure authentication without adding operational complexity.

- Effortless digital signing

xorkeesign handles digital signing across documents and data exchanged between users and systems. It allows signatures to be applied wherever documents are created, reviewed, or submitted, without forcing users into isolated signing tools. The service supports individual use as well as organizational workflows, using the same signing model across interfaces.

Document Signing

xorkeesign lets users digitally sign documents such as PDF and XML directly in the browser. No extra software is required, and signing fits naturally into existing workflows.

xorkeesign Mail

xorkeesign Mail allows users to digitally sign emails from their existing email clients. Recipients can easily see that the sender is genuine and the message is unchanged.

xorkeesign India G2C

xorkeesign India G2C enables document signing directly on Indian government websites through a browser extension, without additional installations.

- Authentication tied to identity and user intent

xorkeeAuth lets users securely log in to applications and approve actions without using passwords or one-time codes(OTP). It uses cryptographic keys created and managed by users so that applications can confirm a user’s identity and consent with strong security. The private key stays with the user and authentication is safer and simpler than traditional password- or OTP-based methods.

About Odyssey Technologies

Odyssey Technologies Limited is a 35-year-old Indian technology company headquartered in Chennai, with offices in Mumbai and Delhi. It is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange.

Odyssey has pioneered various technologies across multiple sectors. The company was the first to introduce a CD-ROM-based tax database in India, used by the government, the CA community, and the general public for over a decade.

Odyssey has done groundbreaking work in cryptography and Public Key Infrastructure, introducing robust products ahead of the IT Act 2000. It has served large multinational and Indian banks, the stock broking community, and government institutions.

The company is ISO-certified, reinforcing its commitment to strong governance and information security practices. Odyssey is also a member of leading industry bodies, including CII, NASSCOM, and ASSOCHAM, and actively engages with India’s technology and business ecosystem.
