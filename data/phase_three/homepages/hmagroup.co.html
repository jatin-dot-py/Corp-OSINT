Fresh Gold

Fresh Gold delivers you a range of fresh, buttery, chemical-free and nutritious fish and seafood at your doorstep. Our supply is sourced from the local fishermen to ensure that you get the freshest fish for your special events such as dinner party, family lunch or a special brunch with your extended family members.
