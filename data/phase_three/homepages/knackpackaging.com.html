Our Products

What We Offer

Printed and Laminated Woven PP Bags are now a part of standard packing for many
reputed companies for packaging their bulk produce.

PE/PP Tapes

In the world of woven packaging, the tape is considered as the soul of bags as the quality depends more or less on it.

WPP Woven Fabrics (Coated/Uncoated)

Without fabric, woven packaging would be the next impossible thing unless we get some other viable alternative.

WPP Woven Bags

Woven bags are manufactured with the help of Woven Fabric. These kinds of bags are widely used in the packaging industry.

Printed and Laminated Woven PP Bags (PLWPP)

BOPP’s Biaxial Orientation process results in increased toughness, enhanced clarity and quite improved stiffness.

PLWPP + Pinch Bottom Bags

Undoubtedly it is one of the most innovative packaging material for dry bulk goods.

PLWPP Block Bottom Bags

One of the most prominent packaging solutions in the industry, Block Bottom Bags, truly stands out for its qualities.

PLWPP Bottom Gusset Bags

Ideal for medium to large amounts of content. Provide stability & an aesthetic look.

PLWPP Retail Shopping Bag (Woven/Non-woven Bag)

With an increase in the shopping patterns, we see shopping bags often.

Multi-Filament Yarn

Multifilament yarn is one of the most indispensable parts of the packaging industry. It is manufactured using threads.

Narrow Woven Fabrics

Narrow woven fabrics are highly flexible and manufactured in special looms. NWF have applications in many industries.

Design and Cylinder services

Makes branding more convenient and fruitful. It plays a significant role in designing the eye-catching bags.

Reprocessed Granules

Reprocessed granules are granules which are made by recycling waste materials produced during other processes.
