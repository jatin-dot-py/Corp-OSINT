Methodology

AI EngineeringMethod

A six-phase process where AI is not bolted on at the end — it is the operating system of how we build.

01
Phase 01AI Discovery

Claude analyses stakeholder transcripts, codebases, and business context to generate comprehensive requirements in 48–72 hours.

02
Phase 02Neural Architecture

Three architecture proposals with trade-off matrices and automated ADRs. Senior engineers review and select.

03
Phase 03Intelligent Development

Every engineer works with an AI co-pilot — Windsurf for codebase-aware refactors, Claude for reasoning, Copilot for completions.

04
Phase 04Autonomous Testing

Test suites generated from requirements. Self-healing automation. Predictive QA. 90%+ coverage from sprint one.

05
Phase 05AI-Powered Deployment

Intelligent CI/CD selects deployment strategy, monitors rollout, triggers automated rollback within 90 seconds.

06
Phase 06Continuous Intelligence

L1 incidents self-resolve. L2 get AI-generated dossiers. Predictive monitoring detects degradation 4–6 hours ahead.
