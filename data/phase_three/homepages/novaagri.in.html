Nova Agri Group

For over 19 years, Nova Group has been a trusted name in the Indian agricultural industry, delivering science-backed agri-input solutions that enhance productivity and sustainability. Driven by innovation and supported by strong research & development, Nova Group develops next-generation soil health management, crop nutrition, crop protection, and seed technologies.

With over 900+ product registrations, Advanced manufacturing facilities with capacities more than 120 Kl/ Kgs per day, network with more than 5000 active dealers. Nova Group proudly supports 1.8 Million farmers across India.
Our mission is to empower farmers with reliable solutions that improve crop yields while maintaining environmental responsibility.
