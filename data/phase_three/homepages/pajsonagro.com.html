Who Are We

From Sun-Kissed Fields to Your Kitchen Table: The Pajson Agro Cashew Journey

Pajson Agro stands as a leading name in the Cashew Processing Industry, having embarked on this journey in 2021 with a vision to deliver superior quality cashew products. Based in India, we source Raw Cashew Nuts (RCN) directly from cashew-producing regions including India, Cote d’Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Benin, Togo, and Guinea-Bissau.

This direct procurement approach ensures that we receive only the finest quality produce. Our state-of-the-art processing facility in Visakhapatnam, India, boasts an impressive annual processing capacity of 18,000 MT.

How We Work

At Pajson Agro, we ensure exceptional cashews reach you directly, ready to elevate your culinary creations or satisfy your snacking cravings.

Farm-Fresh Drying

We meticulously dry the Raw Cashews Nuts to perfection, ensuring optimal quality and taste.

Robust Supply Chain

We ensure our product is optimally packed and shipped through global trans-liners.

State of The Art Processing

Our state-of-the-art processing unit in Visakhapatnam carefully processes the RCN into Cashew Kernels which are of highest standards.
