Company Overview

Rukmani Devi Garg Agro Impex Limited

Rukmani Devi Garg Agro Impex Limited [RAIL] (formerly Rukmani Devi Garg Agro Impex Private Limited) has been at the forefront of India’s agricultural commodity sector since 17th April 1998. We have evolved into a trusted leader, specializing in the agricultural commodity aggregation from farmers and supply-chain management. At RAIL, we pride ourselves on our state-of-the-art processing capabilities. This technological leap enabled the launch of our proprietary wheat brands—Sharbati, Happy Family, and Taj Mahal—which have gained widespread recognition across India through our robust dealer and distributor network.
