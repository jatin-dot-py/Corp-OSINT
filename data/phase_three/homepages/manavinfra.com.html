About

Manav Infra Projects

Building Foundations, Strengthening Futures

Manav Infra Projects ltd, originally established in 1995, was founded by Mr. Mahendra Raju, who continues to serve as the Promoter and Managing Director. With over 30 years of expertise in the foundation construction sector, Mr. Raju has played a pivotal role in shaping the industry with advanced engineering solutions.

Specializing in Metro and building piling, Micro Piles, Soil and Rock anchoring, Drilling and grouting, and other cutting-edge techniques, Manav Infra Projects has built a reputation for delivering high-quality, reliable, and efficient infrastructure solutions.
