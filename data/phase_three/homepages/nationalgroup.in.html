National Plastics Group is a leading manufacturer of Plastic injection molded products for over seven decades and has been delivering on the promise of quality products.

We manufacture PET Preforms in FSSC 22000 certified plants and have a nationwide manufacturing footprint to cater to all segments like Packaged Water, Carbonated Soft Drinks, Juice and Dairy.

CSR AND SUSTAINABILITY

Corporate Social Responsibility has been our continuing commitment in contributing to the economic development and improving the quality of life of our workforce and their families as well as of the local community and society at large.

The Group also bases its sustainable development approach on recognized methodologies with the help of external partners, who are experts in their respective fields.
