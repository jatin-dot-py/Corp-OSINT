KDCSOL 100E - Oil Filled

KDCSOL 100E series is Oil-filled closed overhang rewindable motor with Enamelled insulated copper wire. The motor is built using a Permanent magnet rotor, ball bearings and a mechanical seal. The filled oil not only lubricates the bearings but also cools the windings and transfers heat from inside to outside of the motor. The mechanical seal perfectly seals the motor from water entry.(Motor rating – up to 7.5 HP). Main Applications – Solar-powered pump system, Domestic water supply, Groundwater management, General irrigation systems, Spray irrigation systems, Fountains.

KDCSOL 100-WF series is a water filled closed overhang rewindable motor with wrapped polymer insulated winding wire. The water fill not only cools the motor winding, but also lubricates the bearings. SOLOTRAN Controller

The solar pump controller is a variable speed motor drive designed to run AC/DC/PMSM motor. The controller starts the pump slowly and adjust its speed according to pumping load and power available from solar array. Power output from the solar array is optimally matched to the load by Maximum Power Point tracking (MPPT) mechanism throughout all conditions. The controllers are having dry run, over load, open circuit and short circuit protections available. The controllers are available in IP54 & IP65 variants. Main Applications – Solar-powered pump system, Domestic water supply, Groundwater management, General irrigation systems, Spray irrigation systems, Fountains.
