Product Categories Hub

Products that flourish your farm

If the water is life blood of farming, irrigation is the heart! Our products pump life into crops and increase the quality and quantity of yield. All our products are precisely designed for beyond outstanding performance, durability and reliability.

We don’t just manufacture products; we produce systems that fulfil your purpose of water management. We provide solutions for water management that go beyond agriculture, infrastructure, industries, or municipal requirements. If your purpose is water management, Polysil is always there!

Drip Irrigation Systems

Made for Precision irrigation tailored to every crop. Our drip systems deliver water precisely to the plant roots, reducing wastage and ensuring every drop counts. Easy to install, designed to fit farms of all sizes, helping grow more while conserving resources.

Sprinkler Irrigation

Sprinkler systems sprinkle water evenly across fields like rainfall. Perfect for uniform coverage requirements. With reliable performance and easy-to-use field calculators, irrigation becomes simple and accurate. Save time, reduce labour, and ensure consistent growth.

Filtration & Control

Clean water is essential for effective micro irrigation. Our filtration systems remove impurities, protection from clogging. Equipped with valves and automation manages water flow, timing, and pressure with ease. This increases efficiency and ensures crops receive precise irrigation at right time.

HDPE Pipes & Fittings

HDPE pipes are built to last, providing a backbone to convey water/liquid. Engineered for long-term performance, serving as conduits in municipal water supply to industrial and construction projects. Its anti-corrosion formula ensures consistent performance for decades. It adapts seamlessly to all needs, supporting everything from tiny farms to civil infrastructure.
