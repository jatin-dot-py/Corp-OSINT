INDIGINEERING.

The capability came first.

A critical-application engineering practice built up since 1991 — exotic alloys, high-pressure fluid control and precision manufacture of mechanical systems— underwrites every partnership CFF holds.

Read More about our Engineering Core

CFF Fluid Control Limited absorbs world-class technology and manufactures it in India — to the same standards as the country that first developed it. From in-house design and precision manufacturing to installation, commissioning, and lifecycle support, CFF is present at every stage of the platform’s life.
