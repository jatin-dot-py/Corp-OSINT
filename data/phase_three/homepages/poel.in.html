POCL Enterprises Limited

Established in 1988, POCL Enterprises Limited (POEL) is one of India’s leading manufacturers of Metallic
Oxides—including Lead Oxides, Zinc Oxide, PVC Stabilizers, and Lead & Zinc Metals. An ISO 9001:2015, 14001:2015,
and 45001:2018 certified organization, POEL upholds excellence in quality, environment, and safety. The company
is recognized as a Two Star Export House by the DGFT and holds the prestigious AEO Tier 1 certification,
reflecting its strong compliance and secure global supply standards.

Our Business Verticals

Metals

POEL’s Lead Alloying Division produces high-performance lead alloys using advanced technology. Known for its density, corrosion resistance, and durability, lead plays a vital role in batteries, sound insulation, radiation shielding, plumbing and roofing – serving the core needs of modern industrial and energy sectors.

Metallic Oxides

POEL’s Metallic Oxide Division manufactures high-quality Lead Oxides and Zinc Oxide essential for batteries, plastics, ceramics and paints. With decades of expertise, we ensure purity, stability and performance delivering reliable quality to industries worldwide.

Plastic Additives

POEL’s Plastic Additive Division manufactures premium PVC stabilizers for PVC pipes, fittings, profiles, cable insulation, PVC compounds and footwear. These additives enhance durability, stability and performance, supporting the evolving demands of the plastics industry.
