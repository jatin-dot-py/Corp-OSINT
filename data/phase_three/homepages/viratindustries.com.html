About
Virat Industries

We consider it our fundamental concern to understand the needs and requirements of our customers.

Virat Industries Ltd (VIL), is a Public Limited Company, quoted on the Indian Stock Market. VIL manufactures excellent quality socks for export mainly to the European markets. The Company started its operations in 1995 and has successfully completed more than two decade in Socks business. The socks are manufactured on the latest computerized electronic knitting machines by Lonati and Matec, Italy and KTM, Korea supported with balancing equipment from Spain and other European markets.

Virat Industries: Where Every Stitch Ensures Quality Assurance. Step into Comfort, Step into Excellence with Our Premium Socks!

  • RAW MATERIAL INSPECTION

  • COLOUR STANDARD

  • FABRIC INSPECTION

  • SHRINKAGE ASSESSMENT

  • PILOT SAMPLING

Ensuring excellence from the start, our meticulous raw material inspection process guarantees that only the finest fibers and materials are used in our socks, setting a strong foundation for superior quality.

Our rigorous colour standard procedure ensures vibrant, consistent, and long-lasting colors in every pair of socks, providing a perfect match to your expectations and maintaining brand integrity.

Through comprehensive fabric inspection, we meticulously examine each batch for defects and inconsistencies, ensuring that our socks meet the highest standards of durability and comfort.

To maintain size accuracy and fit, our shrinkage assessment process rigorously tests each fabric under various conditions, ensuring minimal shrinkage and optimal performance.

Our pilot sampling procedure involves creating and testing prototype samples to identify and rectify potential issues, guaranteeing that the final product exceeds quality and performance expectations.
