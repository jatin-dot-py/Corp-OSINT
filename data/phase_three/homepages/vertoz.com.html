Who We Work With

Our role is focused and defined: we own paid-media execution while our partners retain strategic control.

Brands

Brands usually come to Vertoz when paid media becomes too complex to run in-house. We step in to run execution across platforms, manage campaigns day to day, and stay responsible for outcomes.

Agencies

Agencies work with Vertoz when execution needs a clear owner. We take care of media execution and delivery, while agencies continue to lead strategy, creative, and client relationships.
