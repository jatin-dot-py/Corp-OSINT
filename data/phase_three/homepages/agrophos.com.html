About Us

Agro Phos India Limited

Incorporated in 2002, our Company M/s. Agro Phos India Limited having CIN:  L24123MP2002PLC015285, is a NSE Listed as well as ISO 9001:2008 certified Company engaged in the manufacturing of fertilisers such as Single Super Phosphate (SSP),  NPK Fertilisers, Zinc Sulphate, Organic manure and Calcium Sulphate. Our Company also undertakes trading of Diammoium Phosphate (DAP), Urea, Ammonium Sulphate and other fertilizers depending upon the demand of the customer. The registered office of our Company is situated at M-87, Trade Centre 18, South Tukoganj, Indore, Madhya Pradesh 452001.
