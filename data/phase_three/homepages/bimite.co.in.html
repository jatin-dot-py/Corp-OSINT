Raw material to fully value added finished products

Bimetal Bearings Limited (BBL) was established in 1961, in collaboration with Clevite Inc., USA, and Repco Limited, Australia. The company boasts fully integrated manufacturing capabilities, encompassing the entire process from raw materials to fully value-added finished products. BBL maintains a robust Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) base that spans the entire automotive spectrum, including Passenger Cars, Multi-Utility Vehicles (MUVs), Light Commercial Vehicles (LCVs), Medium & Heavy Commercial Vehicles (M&HCVs), Tractors, Industrial Engines, and Two-Wheelers. Additionally, BBL serves the demanding needs of defense and railway sectors.

Bimetal Bearings Limited

Manufacturers of High Quality Engine Bearings,
Bushings, Thrust Washers Since 1961.

Bimetal Bearings Limited (BBL) operates as a multi-plant, multi-location entity, boasting state-of-the-art facilities strategically situated in Chennai, Coimbatore, and Hosur. The foundation of BBL's success is its extensive industry experience and the advanced infrastructure developed over many years.


Furthermore, BBL is a proud member of the Amalgamations Group, one of India's premier light engineering conglomerates. The Group spans a diverse range of sectors within Indian industry, including manufacturing, trading, distribution, as well as printing and publishing, serving a broad cross-section of the market.
