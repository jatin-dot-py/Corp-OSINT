Showroom Highlights

Explore Our Top Yamaha Models

Yamaha R15M

The Yamaha R15M is the pinnacle of Yamaha’s R-Series in the 155cc segment, blending racing DNA with futuristic design. With exclusive features like a Traction Control System, Upside Down Front Forks, and Quick Shifter (for select variants), the R15M delivers an advanced riding experience that’s thrilling, safe, and stylish.

Key Highlights

Equipped with cutting-edge tech and premium design elements, the R15M stands out in its class.

  • 155cc Liquid-Cooled, 4V Engine with VVA
  • Traction Control System (TCS)
  • Upside Down Front Forks
  • Quick Shifter (Standard on R15M)
  • Class-D Bi-Functional LED Headlight
  • Bluetooth Enabled Y-Connect App Support
  • Premium Metallic Grey & Blue Color Finish

Yamaha MT-15 V2

The Yamaha MT-15 V2 is a dynamic streetfighter built for performance and agility. With its iconic MT design language, aggressive stance, and a high-performance 155cc engine equipped with VVA (Variable Valve Actuation), the MT-15 V2 is the perfect companion for city streets and spirited rides.

Key Highlights

Engineered with precision and packed with tech, the MT-15 V2 redefines street riding.

  • 155cc Liquid-Cooled, 4V Engine with VVA
  • Upside Down Front Forks
  • Aluminium Swingarm for Better Stability
  • Dual Channel ABS
  • LED Headlight & LED Position Lights
  • Bluetooth Enabled Y-Connect App Support
  • Aggressive Dark Warrior Styling

Yamaha FZ-X

The Yamaha FZ-X brings a bold Neo-Retro look to the streets. Designed to stand out, this bike blends vintage design cues with modern features to create a head-turning ride that’s as functional as it is stylish. Perfect for riders who want a mix of rugged aesthetics and daily practicality.

Key Highlights

With advanced tech and comfort-focused design, the FZ-X is made for urban explorers.

  • 149cc Air-Cooled, Fuel-Injected Engine
  • Neo-Retro Design with Bi-Functional LED Headlight
  • Bluetooth Connectivity via Y-Connect App
  • Comfortable Two-Level Seat
  • USB Charging Port
  • Single Channel ABS with Front Disc Brake
  • Lightweight and Agile for City Riding

Yamaha Aerox 155 Version S

The Yamaha Aerox 155 Version S is India’s first performance scooter with Y-Connect and Smart Key. It delivers a powerful and sporty ride with R15-inspired engineering, perfect for riders who demand thrilling acceleration, bold design, and practical features.

Key Highlights

Designed for performance and convenience, Aerox 155 Version S offers a maxi-scooter experience in a compact, agile body.

  • 155cc Liquid-Cooled SOHC Engine with VVA
  • Yamaha Smart Key System (Version S Exclusive)
  • LED Headlight with Position Lights
  • Bluetooth Enabled Y-Connect App Support
  • Super Wide 140mm Rear Tire
  • Large 24.5L Under-Seat Storage
  • Full Digital LCD Meter Console

Yamaha Fascino 125 Fi

The Yamaha Fascino 125 Fi is a perfect blend of style and innovation. With its stunning curves, chrome accents, and cutting-edge features like Smart Motor Generator (SMG) and Hybrid Power Assist, it delivers both flair and performance for today’s urban riders.

Key Highlights

Lightweight, fuel-efficient, and fashion-forward—Fascino redefines the stylish scooter category.

  • 125cc Blue Core Hybrid Engine
  • Smart Motor Generator (Silent Start)
  • Unified Braking System (UBS)
  • LED Headlight with DRL
  • Lightweight Body (99kg)
  • Fully Digital Instrument Cluster (in select variants)
  • Chrome Accents and Curved Retro Design

Yamaha RayZR 125 Fi Hybrid

The Yamaha RayZR 125 Fi Hybrid is designed with a bold, sporty-retro appeal and powered by a smart hybrid engine. With enhanced mileage, LED headlight, and lightweight frame, it delivers both performance and style in city rides.

Key Highlights

Combining retro flair with modern functionality, the RayZR 125 Fi Hybrid is a perfect urban ride.

  • 125cc Air-Cooled, Fuel-Injected Hybrid Engine
  • Smart Motor Generator (SMG) with Hybrid Power Assist
  • LED Headlight & Fully Digital Instrument Cluster
  • Lightweight Body – Just 99kg (Drum), 98kg (Disc)
  • Side Stand Engine Cut-off Switch
  • Unified Braking System (UBS)
  • Sporty Graphics with Bold Design Lines
