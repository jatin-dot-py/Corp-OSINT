GM Polyplast Limited

GM Polyplast Limited is one of the leading manufacturers of HIPS, ABS, PET, PP, HDPE Sheets and Premium quality Granules which started its operations back in 2003.
The company guarantees that it is up-to-date with the latest technologies in all of its operations. Its primary focus is not only the development of existing products, but also the addition of new designs and colours. It emphasises product Quality & Client satisfaction.
