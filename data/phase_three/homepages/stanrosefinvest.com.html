About our company

Welcome to Stanrose Mafatlal

Stanrose Mafatlal Investments and Finance Limited (formerly Sandeep Holdings Limited) is a distinguished Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) actively engaged in inter-corporate investments, financing, and capital market activities. As a proud member of the esteemed Stanrose Mafatlal Group, the company operates under the visionary leadership of Shri Pradeep Rasesh Mafatlal.

The company holds a strategic controlling interest in the Group’s flagship entity, Standard Industries Limited, and maintains a robust portfolio of equity investments in several prominent blue-chip companies.

Incorporated on April 17, 1980, as a private limited company, Stanrose Mafatlal became a subsidiary of Mafatlal Industries Limited on October 17, 1980. Following a preferential share issue to the shareholders of Mafatlal Industries Limited, the company transitioned into an independent public limited company in 1981–82.

With a legacy of trust, strong corporate governance, and a shareholder base of approximately 22,000, Stanrose Mafatlal has consistently rewarded its investors with healthy dividends and has announced three bonus issues to date. Its equity shares are listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange Limited.

The company’s Board of Directors comprises highly respected professionals with diverse expertise, including accomplished leaders from various fields, contributing to the company’s strategic direction and long-term growth.
