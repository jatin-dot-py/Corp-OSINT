FAQ’S

What is Everest Industries?

Everest Industries is a leading provider of new age building solutions, specialising in roofing, flooring, wall and ceiling, as well as Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEBs). Since its establishment in 1934, Everest Industries Ltd. has been a prominent player in the construction industry, supplying high-quality building solutions, to architects, designers, and engineers. We take pride in delivering customised construction solutions and building lasting relationships with clients. Our extensive portfolio encompasses a diverse range of roofing, ceiling, wall, flooring, and cladding solutions, catering to the industrial, commercial, and residential sectors. As one of the foremost building material manufacturers in India, we have successfully served over 1,00,000 villages, 600 cities and 35 countries, showcasing its widespread impact and influence in the construction market.

What does Everest Industries do?

As one of the leading building material manufacturers in India, we provide roofing, flooring, wall and ceiling solutions for residential, commercial and industrial projects. We also provide PEB solutions across industries.

What type of industry is Everest Industries Limited?

Everest Industries is a building material manufacturer in the construction industry. We provide roofing, flooring, wall and ceiling solutions, as well as PEBs.

These structures are designed to optimise storage space efficiently, providing a cost-effective and durable solution.   3. Commercial and Residential Structures:

    Recognised as a trusted building materials supplier, Everest Industries provides innovative wall solutions and mezzanine flooring solutions ideal for commercial and residential structures. Whether it's roofing for agro markets or PEBs for warehousing, we stand as reliable building construction material suppliers for a wide array of construction solutions.

What types of building materials do we supply?

At Everest Industries as a prominent new age building materials supplier, we offer a comprehensive range of high-quality products designed to meet the highest standards of performance and durability in the construction industry. As leading building construction material suppliers, our diverse portfolio includes:

  1. Roofing Materials:

    As a key player in the market, Everest Industries excels in providing robust fibre cement roofing solutions. Our range includes corrugated fibre cement sheets, colour-coated fibre cement sheets as well as cool roofing sheets. Our range exemplifies our commitment to being top-notch building materials suppliers.

  2. Flooring Solutions:

    Everest Industries takes pride in delivering cutting-edge mezzanine flooring solutions that seamlessly blend functionality and aesthetics. Our commitment is evident in our durable and visually appealing mezzanine flooring options for diverse applications.

  3. Wall and Ceiling Solutions:

    We specialise in offering boards and panels for walls and ceilings that enhance both visual appeal and structural integrity. Our products include textured boards and panels that can imitate various designs, including recreating the look and feel of real wood.

  4. Pre-Engineered Steel Buildings (PEBs):

    A hallmark of our expertise as building construction material suppliers is our pioneering work in pre-engineered steel buildings. These structures, fabricated to precise specifications, exemplify cost-effectiveness, efficiency, and durability in construction projects.

Our commitment as building construction material suppliers is unwavering. We ensure that each product in our portfolio undergoes rigorous quality checks, surpassing industry standards. By choosing Everest Industries you align with a trusted partner dedicated to providing top-notch construction solutions for all your project needs.
