    Who We Are

    Team India Guaranty Limited (TIGL) is a registered Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) that specialises in credit solutions for India’s growing Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs). We combine the strength of a regulated financial institution with the agility of a Tech-driven processes to make business financing faster, easier and more accessible.

    Our Mission

    Our mission is to drive inclusive growth by bridging the credit gap for MSMEs across India. We strive to empower businesses through responsible, transparent and efficient financing that enables them to expand, innovate and lead the next wave of India’s entrepreneurial growth.
