Storing energy.
Powering progress.

Aegis Vopak Terminals Ltd.'s intelligent storage solutions enable the safe and seamless distribution of vital liquid and gas products at scale.

Learn more

AVTL combines deep local insight with global expertise to build and operate India’s most extensive independent storage infrastructure for liquids and gas. We engineer our terminals for safe handling, high throughput, and seamless logistics for LPG, chemicals, edible oils, and emerging fuels.

As a joint venture between Aegis Logistics and Royal Vopak, our facilities, located across major ports and featuring multimodal connections to inland industry, deliver the scale, reliability, and future-ready solutions that power India’s growth.
