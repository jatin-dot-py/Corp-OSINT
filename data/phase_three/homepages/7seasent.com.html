Inside 7Seas Entertainment

India's First ISO 9001:2008 Certified Game Development Studio

7Seas Entertainment Limited is a leading game development and gaming company in India, creating innovative games and digital entertainment across Mobile, PC and Web platforms. Based in Hyderabad, 7Seas develops and publishes games across multiple genres while building original intellectual properties and delivering interactive experiences for players worldwide.

600+ Games Created across genres & platforms
150+ IP Rights Protecting our original creations
100M+ Installs Loved by players worldwide
Featured Portals Expanding our digital reach

Our Ambission

To become a globally recognized game development company by creating innovative and engaging digital experiences. We strive to grow through creativity, technology, dedication, and continuous improvement

Our Purpose

To create quality games across platforms and genres that entertain and connect players worldwide. We combine teamwork, technology, trust, and excellence to deliver experiences that exceed player and customer expectations

Our Principles

Our values are the guiding principles upon which the company was founded and how we strive to conduct our business on a daily basis. Values establish our view of the world as we shape the future
