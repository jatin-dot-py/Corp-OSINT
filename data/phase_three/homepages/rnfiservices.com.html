Our Journey

2015

Started our journey with the incorporation of RNFI Services Private Limited

2017

We became business correspondent of ICICI Bank and Airtel Payments Bank.

2018

Forayed into financial inclusion division by becoming a business correspondent of Bank of India and Paytm Payments Bank.

2019

We entered the tourism industry, emerging as principal agent of IRCTC for train bookings and became business correspondent for Fino Payments Bank.

2020

Established a wholly-owned subsidiary namely, RNFI Money Private Limited.

2021

We acquired a subsidiary namely Paysprint Private Limited and became business correspondent for NSDL Payments Bank

2022

We established a wholly-owned subsidiary namely, Reliassure Insurance Brokers Private Limited, and became designated partner in Reliconnect LLP. Subsequently we also became business correspondent for Union Bank of India and also got associated with Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology for UMANG Project and Centre for Digital Financial Inclusion (CDFI). We also became vendor of BGSS, a wholly owned subsidiary of Bank of Baroda for delinquent loan collections.

2023

We converted into a Public Limited Company from Private Limited Company and subsequently became business correspondent of Indian Overseas Bank and Canara Bank and business facilitator for Kotak Mahindra Bank. Additionally we partnered with Maharashtra State Rural Livelihood Mission (MSRLM) for woman empowerment.

2024

Our Company became business correspondent of two Private sector banks. Also, associated with a Private sector bank for provision of doorstep banking services
