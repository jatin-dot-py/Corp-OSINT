Welcome to Kamron Laboratories Limited.

Kamron was established in the year 1990 by Mr Kamlesh J Laskari who put up a manufacturing facility to produce tablets, capsules and liquid orals at Rakanpur near Ahmedabad. Ahmedabad is the largest town of Gujarat state in India and is well connected by direct international flights to Africa, Europe, USA, Middle East and Far East.

The beginnings were humble and the company started commercial production to supply quality medicines to cater to the domestic market and institutional sales. Looking to the rising demand for injectables in India and the world over, the company added its Small Volume Parenterals Production plant in the year 1993.
