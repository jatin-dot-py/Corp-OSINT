Welcome to Our Company
Rajdarshan Industries Limited (formerly A.R.Enterprises Limited) having its registered office at 59, Moti Magri Scheme, Udaipur (Raj.) was incorporated in the year 1980 with the object of undertaking drilling operations and business of mining, manufacturing and trading of all type of minerals. The Company started activity after drilling business in the field of raising of lime stone and removal of overburden including transportation thereof on hire / contract basis for various cement Plants.
At present the company is engaged in business of Trading of Quartz grit, powder and consumables on commission basis.
