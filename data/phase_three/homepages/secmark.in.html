Our People Make SecMark Unique

Led by industry veterans with deep expertise across India’s financial regulatory ecosystem, combining legal rigor with scalable technology.

Ravi Ramaiya

MD & CEO

Ravi has close to 30 years of experience in financial markets in the areas of Project Management, System Integration, Technology Implementation, Audits and Consulting, Arbitration, Litigation and Representation. He was a senior partner at Shah & Ramaiya and has worked with the National Stock Exchange before taking the position of MD & CEO at SecMark.

Sagar Thanki

CFO & ED

Sagar has over 15 years in financial markets in the areas of Dealing, Operations, Risk Management & Compliance at several leading financial market participants, including Pratham Investments, Tipsons Stock Brokers Private Limited, Relitrade Stock Broking Private Limited and KIFS Trade Capital Limited.

Sunil Bang

AD, CS & Compliance Officer

Sunil has over 25 years of experience in Due Diligence, Compliance, Secretarial, Internal Audit, FEMA, Anti Money Laundering & Insider Trading in the areas of Investment Banking, Asset Management, Broking, NBFC, holding management positions at Avendus Capital, Brics Securities & BSE.

Shardul Shah

Non-Executive Director

Michael D’Souza
