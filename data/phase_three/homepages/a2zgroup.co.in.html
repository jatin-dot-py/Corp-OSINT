About Us
24
YEARS LONG LASTING AND MUTUALLY REWARDING AND RELATIONSHIPS.

 

Incepted in the year 2002, A2Z Group is an infrastructure engineering and services business group providing comprehensive services in various fields such as Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) in Telecom & Power Sectors, Municipal Solid Waste Management, Facility Management Services, Renewable Energy Generation, Waste-to-Energy Generation and Facility Management – Home Services. The Group is driven to excellence by a team of more than 12000 employees. The Group has a Pan-India reach besides international presence as well. The Group’s consolidated turnover stood at more than 336 Crores as at end of financial year 2024-25

Engineering Procurement and Construction (EPC)
