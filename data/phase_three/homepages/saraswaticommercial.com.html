Saraswati Commercial (India) Limited

Saraswati Commercial (India) Limited is a public limited company incorporated on January 24, 1983, under Certificate of Incorporation No. 35720.

The Company was originally registered at 15, Chittaranjan Avenue, 4th Floor, Kolkata – 700072. Over the years, in line with its growth and operational requirements, the Company relocated its registered office to K.K. Chambers, 4th Floor, Sir P. T. Road, Fort, Mumbai – 400001 on October 19, 2006. Subsequently, with effect from August 17, 2009, the registered office was shifted to its present location. This transition reflects the Company’s continued evolution and its commitment to strengthening its presence in key business hubs.

The Equity Shares of company are listed on the BSE Limited, Mumbai and available for Trading on National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) under 'Permitted to Trade' category and the Company has voluntarily delisted from the Calcutta Stock Exchange Limited (“CSE”) with effect from May 11, 2012.

The Company is registered as a Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) (Registration No.: 13.01860) and is primarily engaged in Lending, Investment and Trading in Shares and Securities.
