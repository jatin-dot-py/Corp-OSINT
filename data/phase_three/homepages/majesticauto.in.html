Majestic Auto Limited ventured into commercial real estate in 2015 by providing office space on lease and facility management services.
 
 
 
Facility Management covers two main areas: ‘Space & Infrastructure’ (such as planning, design, workplace, construction, lease, occupancy, maintenance and furniture) and People & Organisation (such as catering, cleaning, ICT, HR, accounting, marketing, hospitality).
 
 
The company is into facility of building named ‘Knowledge Boulevard’ owned by subsidiary company Emirates Technologies Private Limited (ETPL) located at Sector-62, Noida with a built up area of 8,00,000 square feet (approx.) and 11 floors.
 
 
The leasing business is carried by ETPL which involves leasing of its developed and redevelop offices. The utilities and facilities management business supports and complements the lease business.
 
 
The company further ventured into leasing industrial set up in 2019 in Greater Noida.
 
 
We work together to maintain the legacy of providing the best planning, maintenance, construction, custodial and facility operation services to employees and departments so they can conduct public business in a clean, safe and healthy environment.
 
 
We ensure quality services to our clients with the help of continuous monitoring of workspace.
 
 
Majestic Auto Limited was the largest producer of Mopeds in India in the early 80’s. It also manufactured Automobile & Electrical components until 2017.
