Aluminium Flooring Sheets

Manaksia's Aluminum Flooring Sheets with option of 5 Bar & Diamond Pattern are produced in Haldia Plant and has got wide range of application e.g., flooring of Bus, Truck, Rail Coaches & many more. Renders excellent grips and non-slippery surface and can be made available in sizes and thickness to suit customers' requirements.

Aluminum is anti-corrosive, non-slippery and easy to clean. The usage of Aluminum has gone up as flooring option with Govt. policy on preservation of wood & forest.

Aluminium Roofing (or Building) Sheets

Shelter is the basic need of the human being and every shelter needs Roof & Wall Cladding for protection. Manaksia offers an ideal means of providing ‘covers’ to all kind of structures via Aluminum Profiled Roofing Sheet.

Manaksia produces Aluminum Profiled Roofing Sheet with option of Sinusoidal (Circular) & Trapezoidal (Industrial). Having density of 2.71 gms / cm3 lightness is most outstanding feature of Aluminum as building material. Easy transportation, quick erection & dismantling, aesthetically pleasing appearance with high resale value after prolonged use have made Aluminum the preferred material over conventional G.I and Asbestos.
