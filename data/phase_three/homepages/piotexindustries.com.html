About

Piotex Industries Limited is a professional textile industry with its headquarters in Pune(MS), India, and contract manufacturing & exclusive selling ties up with Spinning & Weaving industries situated in Maharashtra. Piotex Industries Limited incorporated in 2019, is the fastest-growing company in the field of manufacturing and trading cotton bales, yarn, and fabrics. The philosophy of the company is a light asset model.
