Welcome To KMEW Group

Introduction

Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited (KMEW) is incorporated in the year 2015 and is engaged in the business of Dredging, Owning, Operating, and Chartering of Port Ancillary Crafts. With a combined promoters experience of 75 plus years in the dredging industry, the Company has developed from a small ship repair unit to major dredging player in 3 countries (India, Myanmar and Bahrain).

KMEW is promoted and founded by 5 young professional (average age 36 years) (Masters in Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering, Marine Mechanical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Company Secretary, Lawyer and Management Graduate and Chartered Accountants).

With the support, guidance and advice of the Non-Executive Directors, Independent Directors, top management, site management and offshore staff (2nd and 3rd tier of the company) we are ready to face any challenge. The team has grown from 5 to 450 plus in last 10 years.
