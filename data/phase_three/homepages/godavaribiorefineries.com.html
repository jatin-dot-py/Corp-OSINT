Godavari Biorefineries Ltd.

We are one of the largest producers of ethanol and a pioneer in manufacturing ethanol-based chemicals in India. Our diversified product portfolio comprises bio-based chemicals, sugar, rectified spirits, ethanol, other grades of alcohol and power. We are the only company in India to have such a vast portfolio of bio-based products and are also the largest manufacturer of MPO worldwide, one of only two manufacturers of natural 1,3 butylene glycol globally, the fourth largest manufacturer of ethyl acetate in India, and the only company in India to produce bio ethyl acetate.
