Plastiblends: Pioneering 'Colourful Possibilities' in the Plastics Industry

Since 1991, Plastiblends has been at the forefront of the plastics industry in India, proudly holding the title of the country's leading producer and exporter of colour, white, black, additive and filler masterbatches. Our innovative products help to continuously shape advancements in plastics and textile industries.

Headquartered in Mumbai, with key product development and application units in Daman, Roorkee and Palsana, our extensive manufacturing infrastructure supports an impressive annual production capacity of over 1,30,000 MT.

Our commitment to quality and innovation has earned us global recognition. We proudly export to over 60 countries, reflecting our brand’s reputation for trust and cutting-edge solutions.

At Plastiblends, our mission is clear: to explore and implement creative, innovative approaches to meet and exceed the evolving needs of the plastics industry.

Kolsite Group

With over 60 years of industry excellence, the Kolsite Group embodies a legacy of vision and innovation. Our collective expertise shines through each of our distinguished companies, including:

  1. Plastiblends India Limited known for Masterbatches & Engineering Plastics Compounds
  2. Kabra Extrusiontechnik, renowned for its specialization in Blown Film Lines and Drip Irrigation Pipe Extrusion Lines
  3. Maharashtra Plastics & Industries known as leading manufacturer and exporter of quality Polypropylene (PP) Box Strapping and Polyester (PET) strapping products
  4. Kolsite Corporation LLP – Agency Division (KCLLP)
