FOUNDER'S VISION & LEGACY

Late Shri Abhey Kumar Oswal

Founder & Chairman Oswal Greentech Limited

“If you do something for humanity the whole world will salute you this is what I want in my life, when you die, die with respect....”

Shri Abhey Kumar Oswal was the Founder Chairman of Oswal Greentech Limited, formerly Oswal Chemicals & Fertilizers Limited, and of Oswal Agro Mills Limited. He was a builder of enterprises and, in equal measure, a builder of institutions that asked nothing in return.

Born into a distinguished Ludhiana business family as a son of the late Lala Vidya Sagar Oswal, he was a keen learner, an innovator and a leader by instinct. It was his appetite for calculated risk, and his conviction that technologies should be integrated rather than merely adopted, that led him to step beyond the family’s traditional textile business and into grassroots agro-industry. He travelled widely and studied what a modernising India would need before India itself had asked for it — and then he built it.

OUR LEGACY

Our Legacy: Four Decades of Enterprise, Courage and Nation-Building

The Company's journey began with a vision to identify emerging opportunities and create sustainable businesses. Founded under the leadership of Shri Abhey Kumar Oswal, the Company moved beyond traditional business activities and explored opportunities across diverse industrial sectors, laying the foundation for future growth.

Early Years
Beyond the family trade

The Group’s story begins in Ludhiana, in a distinguished business family led by Lala Vidya Sagar Oswal. His son, Shri Abhey Kumar Oswal, was a keen learner and an innovator with an appetite for calculated risk. It was that appetite, and his conviction that technologies must be integrated rather than merely adopted, that led him beyond the family’s traditional textile business and into grassroots agro-industry.

1981
The Foundation of an Enterprise

The Company was incorporated as a Private Limited Company on 17 November 1981 and became a Public Limited Company on 29 January 1982. In September 1995, it changed its name to Oswal Chemicals & Fertilizers Limited. Subsequently, on 23 November 2011, the Company was renamed Oswal Greentech Limited to reflect the change in its principal business activities.

1983
Forward integration

The Group extended into soaps, detergents, fatty acids, glycerine and stearic acid, marketing premium bath soaps under the OASIS, KALPA and 24 CARRATS brands, alongside edible-grade cooking oils and margarine produced at Mandideep, Bhopal.

1984
A hospital in a mother’s name

Together with his brothers, the founder established the Mata Mohandai Oswal Cancer Hospital at Ludhiana in memory of their mother. Through his efforts it grew into a multi-speciality institution serving the poor and the needy.

1986
Into paddy processing

A paddy processing complex was established at Dhuri, Punjab.

1987
An integrated agro base

The Group acquired a sugar complex at Phagwara, creating a uniquely integrated agro-based industry.

1989
Petrochemicals, and a home for the forgotten

The Group diversified into petrochemicals, acquiring plants from multinationals including Union Carbide in Mumbai and ICI in Kolkata. In the same year the founder built a housing complex at Ludhiana for the elderly and for widows in need — free accommodation with facilities for education, healthcare, safe drinking water, sanitation and recreation.

1995
Entering the Chemicals and Fertilizer Sector

The Company expanded into the chemicals and fertilizer industry, reflecting its ambition to participate in India's agricultural growth story. During this period, the Company changed its name to Oswal Chemicals & Fertilizers Limited, representing its strategic focus on fertilizer and chemical operations. The Company subsequently developed large-scale fertilizer manufacturing capabilities, including operations at Paradip, Odisha.

1999
Paradip: the defining test

A devastating cyclone struck coastal Odisha, including Paradip, where the Company’s DAP plant stood. Thousands of lives were lost and many thousands more left without homes. The founder, together with Smt. Aruna Oswal, personally led teams of volunteers from Paradip, providing food, clothing and shelter for the rehabilitation of the affected population. The Company did not withdraw from Odisha; it rebuilt there.

2000
The Paradip plant

Grassroots, indigenous Di-Ammonium Phosphate fertiliser plant was built at Paradip.

2005
A landmark divestment

The Paradip plant was sold to IFFCO for ₹2,180 crore, concluding the Company’s fertiliser chapter and freeing capital for its next.

2006 onwards
Transition Towards Real Estate

The founder turned to research-led ventures and to real estate, conceiving Centra Greens, a residential project of approximately fifteen lakh square feet at Ludhiana, Punjab. The foundations of the Company’s present business were laid in this period.

2016
A legacy passes on

Shri Abhey Kumar Oswal passed away in March 2016. The enterprise he built continued under the stewardship of his family, guided by the standards he had set.

Today
A listed company, patiently deployed

Oswal Greentech Limited is listed on the BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited, with its registered office at Ludhiana and its corporate office at Antriksh Bhawan, New Delhi. Its principal business is the trading and development of real estate projects; its surplus funds are deployed as interest-bearing inter-corporate deposits, keeping capital productively engaged while opportunities are evaluated with care.
