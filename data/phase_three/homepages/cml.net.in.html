WE HAVE A WAREHOUSE OF 2000 Sq.M. CLOSE TO MUMBAI & WHICH IS EASILY ACCESSIBLE FROM INDIA's BUSIEST WESTERN EXPRESS HIGHWAY. THE LOCATION OF THE WAREHOUSE IS AT THE DOOR OF THE NEW INDUSTRIAL GATEWAY 'GUJARAT' AND NEIGHBOURING MAHARASHTRA.

JR GROUP HAS BUILT A NAME FOR QUALITY MACHINES & EQUIPMENTS. TRUST BUILT IS REPOSED BY CUSTOMERS MAKING REPEAT PURCHASES.

OUR CLIENTELE IS FROM THE AUTOMOBILE, PHARMA, HOME APPLIANCES, TEXTILE, METALWORKING, FORGING, FABRICATION, MOLD MAKERS, STEEL, BEARING, GLASS, PLASTIC,POWER GENERATION,AVIATION, GEAR MAKING, CRANKSHAFT, MINING & CONSTRUCTION AND VARIOUS OTHER UNITS.

JR GROUP IS A LEADER IN SUPPLYING AFFORDABLE MACHINES BOTH NC & CNC.
