Redefining the

At Glenmark, we are shaping the future of global health in a brave new world.

A new world that requires us to formulate bold new ways by relentlessly pursuing a dual mission: pioneering transformative, innovative, breakthrough therapies that redefine treatment paradigms, and expanding access to high-quality, essential medicines for underserved patients around the globe.

We unite scientific excellence, responsible execution and commercial agility to effectively address unmet patient needs of today and proactively anticipate the emerging health needs of tomorrow.

Innovation Fuels Breakthroughs Breakthroughs Unlock Possibilities Possibilities Enhance Lives

Innovation at Glenmark is driven by a clear purpose: to advance differentiated, science-led therapies that address health conditions and deliver meaningful benefit to patients. Our innovative portfolio is concentrated across Respiratory, Dermatology, and Oncology – the therapeutic areas where medical need is significant, science is evolving rapidly, and Glenmark has built deep clinical and development expertise. 

Our approach to innovation is defined by therapeutic focus, scientific rigor, and global partnerships, accelerating access to next-generation therapies. By advancing novel and specialty therapies with clear clinical relevance, we aim to improve patient outcomes while strengthening Glenmark’s position as a global, innovation-led pharmaceutical company. 

Accessibility is embedded into how we develop, manufacture, and deliver medicines. Through scalable manufacturing, fair pricing models, and differentiated delivery formats, we work to make high-quality therapies across diverse healthcare segments, especially in markets where access remains limited.

By combining our deep scientific expertise with agile commercial strategies, we are uniquely positioned to deliver our products reliably, responsibly, and sustainably supporting patients, healthcare professionals, and  healthcare segments worldwide.

CAUTION NOTICE REGARDING FRAUDULENT RECRUITMENT

This is to notify to the public that certain unscrupulous persons, purporting to be part of and/or being authorised by the human resources department of Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Limited (“Glenmark”) are fraudulently offering jobs via certain websites/or through telephone calls, SMS or fake letters and asking the prospective job aspirants to appear for a face-to-face interview for a job opening with Glenmark and/or to pay a fee for securing a job with Glenmark. We wish to caution the public that Glenmark does not collect any monies or any other valuables as a pre or post-employment requirement.
