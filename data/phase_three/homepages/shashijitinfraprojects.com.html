Welcome to Shashijit Infra Projects Ltd.

Shashijit Infraprojects Ltd., a leading name in industrial construction and infrastructure development. With a legacy of excellence, we specialize in delivering turnkey solutions for industrial buildings, factories, warehouses, utility structures, and civil construction projects. From concept to commissioning, we provide end-to-end services tailored to meet the specific needs of manufacturing and process industries.

Our commitment to quality, timely execution, and innovative engineering has earned us the trust of renowned clients across various sectors. Our consistent performance and client satisfaction have resulted in repeated orders and long-term partnerships with many leading companies. Backed by a skilled team, advanced machinery, and a strong safety-first approach, we continue to build structures that drive industrial progress and support India’s growing infrastructure landscape.

Why Us?

At Shashijit Infraprojects Ltd., we combine decades of experience, technical expertise, and a client-first approach to deliver exceptional infrastructure solutions. Here’s what sets us apart:

  • Proven Track Record: Over 30 years of experience in delivering complex industrial and infrastructure projects across sectors.
  • Turnkey Expertise: From concept to completion, we offer end-to-end services including EPC, PMC, and factory construction.
  • Skilled Manpower & Modern Machinery: Backed by a strong in-house team and advanced equipment fleet, ensuring quality, speed, and cost control.
  • Client-Centric Approach: We prioritize client satisfaction with transparent communication, timely delivery, and value-driven solutions.
  • Commitment to Quality & Safety: Adherence to the highest standards of construction, safety, and environmental responsibility.
  • Long Term Associations: Trusted by clients for consistent quality, leading to repeated orders and long-term associations.
