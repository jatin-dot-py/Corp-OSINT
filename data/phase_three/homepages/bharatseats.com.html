Joint Venture

  • Bharat Seats Ltd is a joint Venture of Suzuki Motor Corporation Japan, Maruti Suzuki India Ltd, Rohit Relan and Associates with the aim to manufacture complete seating systems and auto components.

Customer Centricity

  • At Bharat Seats ‘Customer’ is always first and at the core of business. By being customer centric Bharat Seats anticipate customers’ needs and delight them with world class quality products and services. We create products, processes, policies and culture that is designed to support customers with great experience as they are working towards their goals.

Certifications

  • All facilities of Bharat Seats Limited are individually IATF 16949 certified & owing to its initiatives towards preserving environment & safe manufacturing practices, it has also been certified for ISO 14001 & OHSAS 18001.
