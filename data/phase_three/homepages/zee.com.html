'Z' is rooted in a legacy of creating purpose-driven content. From launching India’s first private satellite TV channel in 1992 to reaching 1.4 billion viewers, 'Z' continues to revolutionise entertainment across platforms worldwide.

OUR PURPOSE

To enrich the lives of people around the world, by creating extraordinary moments, which celebrate the power of optimism and togetherness.

OUR PLATFORMS

BROADCAST

We bring extraordinary tales to life, showcasing extraordinary journeys, touching the souls of our viewers through a million screens. Entertainment, every day.

DIGITAL

Powered by technology, we empower our consumers with more choice, more freedom and more access. Anytime, Anywhere.

MOVIES

With powerful scripts, unforgettable characters and unique storytelling, we set new standards in cinematic experiences. Stories that resonate with the soul.

MUSIC

From original compositions to chart-topping tracks, we create melodies that shape the landscape of Indian music. One song at a time.
