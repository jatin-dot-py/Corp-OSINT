About Gennex Laboratories Limited

Gennex Laboratories Limited is an Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient manufacturing company, setup in 1995 with its unit located in IDA Bollaram. Gennex is among the leading ‘Vertically Integrated’ pharmaceutical companies in India and has a robust product portfolio spread over major product segments encompassing Expectorants, Muscle Relaxants, Analgesic and Anti Fungal. The Mission at Gennex is highly Quality driven with the industry’s best practices in place.
Gennex has a prominent Global presence, own modern state of the art infrastructure, strategic alliances, subsidiaries and joint ventures. Gennex Laboratories is into manufacturing, marketing and export of APIs.

Our Vision

To emerge as one of the leading Pharmaceuticals Manufacturing Companies with a global Footprint in all emerging and developed markets of the world.

Our Mission

To achieve its vision of a being globally prominent Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Company while following its core principles of efficiency, transparency and innovation while maximizing shareholder and stakeholder value.

Product Range

We take great pride in offering a wide and diverse product range that caters to the needs of patients and healthcare professionals worldwide with a strong focus on quality, safety, and innovation, we strive to develop and manufacture products that improve the health and well-being of individuals.

At our pharmaceutical manufacturing company, we are committed to continuously expanding and improving our product range to meet the evolving needs of the healthcare industry & professionals. We adhere to strict quality standards and regulatory requirements to ensure the safety and efficacy of our products. Through our dedication to innovation and patient-centricity, we aim to make a positive impact on global healthcare.

Our primary focus over 2 decades of manufacturing has been to deliver quality products at competitive prices to ensure highest customer satisfaction.

At Gennex, our knowledge driven team places highest emphasis on product, quality and systems development while prioritizing efficient and good manufacturing mechanisms.
