Welcome to CRESTCHEM

Crestchem Limited was incorporated in the year 1991, as a body corporate, having its securities listed on premier Stock Exchanges in India.

The Company is in the business of manufacturing, marketing and exporting the products derived from the newly developed field of Nutraceutical Ingredients, emerging from the Conventional Chemical, Food and Pharmaceutical Fields.

Latest News

Company has developed two new products namely Capric / Caprylic Triglycerides and Lactose Free products, by indigenous technology efforts in the nutritional chemical field.

Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has granted permission to commence Trading of Shares of CRESTCHEM LIMITED WITH effect from 01st October, 2012, on BSE Platform.
