About Manglam Infra & Engineering Limited

Manglam Infra & Engineering Limited (MIEL) “(the company)” an ISO certified company (ISO 9001 2015 Certified) is an engineering consulting organization was established in 2010 as a Partnership Firm Manglam Associates that delivers design, planning, engineering, consulting and construction management solutions for Highways, Roads, Bridges, Tunnels, Buildings. We also provide services & solution for rural & urban development, capacity building and environmental engineering and undertake construction material testing and geotechnical investigations.

Bridges

Our dedication to innovation and meticulous attention to detail shines through in our work on bridges, ranging from pedestrian bridges to multi-million-dollar river crossings. Our in-house capabilities enable us to provide comprehensive services for bridge projects, including feasibility studies, environmental assessments, earthquake analysis, constructability reviews, retrofits, Life cycle costs, Concrete slab , Concrete box girder, pre-stressed concrete girder, post-tensioned concrete box girder (span to span or segmental), Composite steel girder, Steel plate girder, Steel box girder, Steel truss Tied arch , Cable stayed , Vertical Lift.

Urban Development

Manglam, a prominent firm in the infrastructure development sector, is dedicated to fostering both rural and urban progress. In rural areas, they focus on building essential amenities like roads and sanitation, improving livelihoods. In urban regions, Manglam contributes to constructing sustainable, modern infrastructure, enhancing the quality of life and urban development We are committed to translating rural & urban visions into reality through comprehensive planning policies and smart zoning codes.
