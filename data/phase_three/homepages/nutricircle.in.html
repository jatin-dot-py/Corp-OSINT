    Harnessing the Power of
    Indian Agriculture to Solve
    India’s Protein Challenge.

    Nutricircle Ltd is pioneering and disrupting the plant-protein landscape with a consumer health–first approach, redefining how India meets its nutrition and protein needs.

     

    We believe in harnessing the power of agriculture by going back to the roots of our home-grown millets and pulses, building an integrated model of 

    Soil → Seeds → Science → Sustainability → Solutions.

    ​​

    ​Nutricircle Ltd is proud to be the first company in the world to isolate protein from millets - a breakthrough that unlocks new possibilities for clean, functional, and climate-resilient nutrition. 

    ​

    Nutricircle Ltd is unlocking value from crops such as foxtail millet, amaranth, pearl millet, horse gram, chickpea, orange lentils, kidney beans, and mung beans - transforming under-utilised crops into high-value nutrition ingredients rich in essential amino acids. 

     

    Our work extends further through collaborations with leading global and national institutes to develop high-protein varieties of Chickpea and Pearl Millet, creating sustainable value chains that benefit farmers, food manufacturers, and consumers.
