Our Journey

A Journey of Growth, Gratitude, and New Beginnings

Over the past eight years, our journey has been nothing short of extraordinary. What began as M/s Ganesh International, an ambitious Infrastructure EPC firm, has evolved into a dynamic and innovative public limited company—M/s Ganesh Infraworld Limited. This transformation reflects more than just a change in name; it symbolizes our unwavering dedication, relentless pursuit of excellence, and the trust placed in us by our clients, partners, and stakeholders.

We are deeply grateful for the continued support that has propelled us forward. Every project, every challenge, and every achievement has been a stepping stone, in building our legacy in the infrastructure landscape. As we embrace this new chapter as Ganesh Infraworld Limited, we are not just looking ahead with ambition; we are also reflecting on the invaluable experiences and relationships that have shaped us.

This transformation is a testament to our growth—an evolution driven by our commitment to quality, innovation, and integrity. With enhanced capabilities, greater financial strength, and a renewed sense of purpose, we are poised to seize new opportunities and make a meaningful impact on the infrastructure sector.

We remain committed to the principles that have defined us: delivering excellence, fostering strategic partnerships, and maintaining a client-first approach. As we look to the future, our gratitude runs deep for the trust and collaboration of those who have been part of our journey. Together, we are not just building infrastructure but shaping the future.

Ganesh Infraworld Limited stands ready to continue this growth journey, guided by our values and our vision of creating a lasting legacy in the world of Infrastructure EPC. With gratitude and a bold outlook, we move forward, ready to achieve new heights and redefine possibilities.
