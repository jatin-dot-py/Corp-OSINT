Aeron Composite

... providing long lasting & new age “GREEN” solution to ageing Infrastructure.

Metal, a traditional material, is used for various infrastructure application such as sewage pipelines, water pipelines, crude pipelines, gas pipelines, industrial as well as residential walkways, light poles, power distribution transformers, scaffoldings, storage tanks, etc which are generally exposed to extremities of environment which leads metal to corrode and spoil the functionality as well as aesthetics. Soil composition, presence of acidic contents, humidity, salt, direct & long water contact, etc are main factor which damage metal the most.

FRP/GRP Composite is a New Age “GREEN” solution to points discussed above and at the same time meeting the strength requirement.

Salient features of FRP/GRP products are:

  • Corrosion Resistance
  • Impact Resistance
  • Light Weight
  • Better Ergonomics
  • Easy Installation
  • low maintenance
  • long lasting
