Welcome To Diamines and Chemicals Ltd.

Quality-Focused with a Passion for Innovation

Diamines and Chemicals Ltd., established in 1976 and commencing production in 1982, stood as the sole manufacturer of Ethyleneamines in the Indian subcontinent for over two decades.

The Promoters – S Amit & Company Is a leading full-service Chemistry Solutions provider in India delivering products and services in the areas of Speciality chemicals,intermediates and performance chemicals for diverse applications including pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, polymers & plastics, and paints and coating. Focuses onoffering a full range of supply chain services including – representation of manufacturers, global and local sourcing, distribution, virtual manufacturing,and identifying problems and recommending innovative chemical solutions.

With proprietary technology and extensive R&D, Diamines has expanded its Product range. By including Piperazine Anhydrous and Piperazine solution DACL is becoming key supplier to the pharmaceutical and gas sweetening industries. Our commitment to customer satisfaction, integrity, and professionalism has fostered long-lasting relationships and value creation for our clients.

Accredited with ISO 9001: 2015, ISO 14001: 2015 & ISO 45001: 2018 and aiming for further certifications OHSAS 18001 and Responsible Care. We invest in green technology, contributing to environmental sustainability through renewable energy sources. We are now equipped with differentiating technological solutions in the wide field of chemistry offering greener and sustainable solutions to your processes.
