Who We Are

We are a global recyclable exchange company, specializing in paper and passionate about innovating. We serve the leading Indian paper mills, sourcing and exporting grades of recycled paper from suppliers all over the world. We have a robust global presence to establish strong supplier relationships, maintain steady inventory supply, and to comply with local laws and sensitivities. We are passionate about transforming the recycling  industry and pioneer cutting edge technology to help our ecosystem of partners make better decisions on things that matter most to them.

Introducing

ERIS or Exim Routes Intelligence System is at the heart of innovation and our relentless pursuit to reimagine the trade value chain. ERIS is the world’s first AI-enabled closed B2B marketplace that enhances decision making, and reduces risk for our trade partners. ERIS smartly matches buyers and suppliers for the best deals, provides intelligent market insights, and helps manage trades on the go.
