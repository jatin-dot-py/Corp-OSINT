About the Panorama studios

Panorama Studios International, with its subsidiaries and associates, is a diversified film studio with various business segments such as Production, Distribution, Music Label, Talent Management, Advertisement, Syndication, Equipment rental and Line production. Panorama Studios International attracts the best talent in media and entertainment to create, package, and deliver high-quality cinema worldwide. With our films, we have tried to delight our audiences worldwide.

KUMAR MANGAT PATHAK

Mr. Kumar Mangat Pathak stands out as a prominent figure in the Bollywood film industry, boasting a remarkable 40-year career. In addition to his role as a seasoned producer, he has adeptly steered the trajectory of superstar Ajay Devgn’s career for over 25 years. With an up-close understanding of the industry’s inner workings, Mr. Pathak possesses the managerial acumen and production prowess to support projects of any magnitude.

Renowned for his exceptional production skills, Mr. Pathak’s portfolio includes groundbreaking films such as ‘Omkara.’ This independent production, an adaptation of Shakespeare’s “Othello,” defied Bollywood norms and garnered national and international acclaim, earning prestigious awards.

Abhishek Pathak

Abhishek Pathak is a young, visionary filmmaker, primarily known for producing cults like Pyaar Ka Punchnama, Drishyam, Section 375 and Raid.

Abhishek is formally trained as a filmmaker from New York Film Academy to pursue his passion for cinema. Abhishek made his directorial debut with his short fiction film “Boond” which received several awards including the prestigious 57th National Film Award.

Drishyam 2 helmed by Abhishek starring Ajay Devgn, Tabu and Akshaye Khanna, released theatrically worldwide on 18th November 2022 and is a huge blockbuster.

He was recently felicitated as one of 2023’s as Most Influential Young Indians by GQ India.
