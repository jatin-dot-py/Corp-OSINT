Welcome To Tilak Ventures Limited

The Company was originally incorporated on 18th August,1980 as “Tilak Finance Limited” with the object of Finance and Investment related activities. Name of the Company has been changed from ” Tilak Finance Limited” to Out Of City Travel Solutions Limited on 28th December 2011 with the object of Travel agents and tour operators for travel in India or elsewhere and provide all the services related to Tours and Travels.

On 28th March, 2014 the name of the company has been changed from “M/s. Out of City Travel Solutions Limited to M/s Tilak Finance Limited”  with the object of providing loans and advances to any person, firm or or body corporate and to receive money on deposit or loan to carry on business as financiers factors and to undertake and carry on business as financiers factors and to undertake and carry on execute all kinds of financial operations but the company shall not carry on banking business as defined in the Banking Regulation Act, 1947. Now the Name of the Company has changed to Tilak Ventures Limited w.e.f 13.10.2015.
