R R Crusher Plant | Rajasthan

Taurian has completely revolutionized our operations. The 2 stage fully supported plant - 250TPH (Jaw & Cone Crusher) along with the feeders, hoppers, conveyors and screens has made a huge impact on our production process. Taurian's efficient team really understands our requirements and consistently exceeds our expectations.

Suraj Bhosale

Yashomala Stone Crusher | Maharashtra

Taurian’s 250 TPH wheeled 3-stage crusher plant resolved our need for both mobility and high output. Their team customized the jaw, cone, and VSI setup to handle materials like recycled concrete, basalt, and hard rocks, significantly improving our crushing efficiency. The plant has been running successfully for the past 7 years, with fast relocation and reduced downtime increasing our production by 20%, meeting project demands on time.

What truly sets them apart is their dedication to customer service – the support team is always available and ensures that our plant operates efficiently.

Kolkata, West Bengal | 23rd to 26th Oct, 2024 – Taurian MPS, a leading provider of crushing, screening, and washing solutions, participated in IMME 2024 (International Mining & Machinery Exhibition) held in Kolkata, India. Customer Success Story

Enhancing Operational Efficiency and Mobility with Taurian’s 250 TPH Wheeled 3-Stage Crusher Plant.

Yashomala Stone Crusher, a key aggregates supplier for infrastructure projects in Kolhapur, Maharashtra, played a pivotal role in the 2016–17 expansion of NH-166 from 2-lane to 4-lane, covering 32.5 km between Kolhapur and Ratnagiri. Spearheaded by NHAI and Maharashtra PWD, the project demanded 8,50,000 + tons of aggregates (GSB & PQC road) in a span of a year. To meet this scale efficiently, owner Mr. Suraj Bhosale turned to Taurian MPS for a high-capacity, flexible crushing solution, ensuring seamless supply for this critical highway upgrade.
