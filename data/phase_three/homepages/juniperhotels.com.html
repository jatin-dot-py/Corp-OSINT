ABOUT US

Juniper Hotels stands at the forefront of the hospitality industry as a leading luxury hotel development and ownership company. The company’s portfolio comprises seven hotels with 1836 keys (including 245 serviced apartments) across strategic locations in India. With a focus on active asset management, Juniper Hotels has emerged as a market leader by operating its assets efficiently while providing quality guest experience.

Juniper Hotels is a strategic partnership between Saraf Hotels, a hotel developer with a strong and well-established track record of over 40 years, and Hyatt, a premier international hospitality brand. This unique partnership signifies not only a commitment to excellence but also a dedication to owning and managing remarkable assets.

ART AT JUNIPER HOTELS

Art forms an integral part of our business and our social responsibility. It moves us. It completes us. Art touches the soul and creates an intangible experience for our guests.

Grand Hyatt Mumbai houses one of the finest collections of commissioned art in a public space in the region. Inspired by the city of Mumbai and the great archaeological sites of Elephanta near Mumbai, the works at the hotel showcase both established Indian artists like Jitesh Kallat, Krishnamachari Bose, Sudarshan Shetty, Atul Dodiya, Anju Dodiya, Laxman Shreshtha, Nalini Malani, Tanuja Rane among others, as well as local artisans and craftspeople.

The hotel’s public areas, lobbies, gardens and restaurants are unique galleries with special commissioned installations that give artists a platform to present their works to a larger audience while nurturing indigenous craft forms and giving them contemporary relevance.
