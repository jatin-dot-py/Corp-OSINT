Welcome to Autoline Industries limited

A trailblazing story! That is how we could describe our journey in this highly competitive, fast paced and ever changing auto industry. Autoline Industries Ltd (AIL) (incorporated on December 16, 1996, as Autoline Stampings Private Ltd.) was initially set up in January 1995 as a partnership firm known as “Autoline Pressings” under Indian Partnership Act 1932.

We introduce our self as a largest automotive sheet metal components manufacturer in India supplying to major OEM like, Tata Motors Volkswagen, Ford, GM, Renault-Nissan, Daimler India, Cummins USA, Ashok Leyland, AMW, FIAT, etc.

We are serving our customers through 10 different manufacturing facilities located at Pune, Dharwad & Uttarakhand. We also have capability to design the product backed up with in-house tool room.

We are specialized in supplying, Heavy Sheet Metal Components & Assemblies, Exhaust System, Pedal System, Door Assemblies, Load body, Door hinges & Skin panels.

We are ISO/TS 16949 Company listed on Bombay Stock Exchange as well as on National Stock Exchange.

Today, more than 3000 products from Autoline fits into a different passenger cars, SUVs, LCVs, HCVs & Gensets.
