Bhaskar Agrochemicals Ltd

Est. 1959 · Hyderabad, India

Bhaskar Agrochemicals has been nurturing harvests for over six decades, supporting farmers with high-quality and innovative crop protection solutions. Built on trust, quality, and innovation, we offer advanced herbicides, fungicides, insecticides, and plant growth promoters that enhance crop health, protect yields, and improve overall farm productivity.

We follow strict quality standards at every stage, from research and development to manufacturing and delivery, ensuring safe, effective, and sustainable solutions for modern agriculture. Certified to ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, and ISO 45001:2018, BACL combines trusted quality with a strong field network – helping farmers across South India grow more, season after season.

With a vision for continuous growth, we build strong, long-term relationships with farmers and partners while expanding our reach. At Bhaskar Agrochemicals, we deliver reliable performance and lasting value, helping farmers achieve better yields and a more sustainable future.

Our Products and Services

Fungicides

Fungicides are chemical or biological substances used to prevent, control, or eliminate fungal diseases affecting crops, plants, and soil health.

Explore Our Products

Insecticides

Insecticides are chemical used to kill, repel, or control insects that damage crops, plants, or stored products.

Explore Our Products

Herbicide

Herbicides are advanced crop protection solutions used to control unwanted weeds that compete with crops for nutrients, water, and sunlight, ensuring healthier crop growth and improved yield performance.

Explore Our Products

PGP

Plant Growth Promoters (PGP) are innovative agricultural solutions that enhance plant growth, flowering, nutrient absorption, and stress tolerance, supporting stronger crops and higher farm productivity.
