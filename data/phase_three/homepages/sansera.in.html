Innovating the Future with Commitment and Expertise

overview

An engineering-led integrated manufacturer of complex and critical precision engineered components across automotive and non-automotive sectors.

Since the inception, we have taken long and successful strides throughout our journey driven by in-house expertise combined with a wealth of experience.
We are a technology-driven company with a strong focus on design, engineering, machine building and automation capabilities.

These capabilities enable us to roll out new products in a timely manner and develop higher strength components required for high-end performance and graduate from manufacturing individual parts to the designing and manufacturing of sub-assemblies, thereby moving us up the value chain.

our strengths

It’s not about ideas. It’s about making ideas happen.

Integrating innovation with our engineering and product manufacturing processes is our forte. Strong capabilities in design and engineering, machine building and automation resulting in continuous new product development and improved productivity, with fungibility of equipment, machinery and production lines across product families and sectors.

Our unique capabilities enable us to deliver complex and high-quality precision forged and machined components in fast-growing markets and stay ahead of the competition.

Our core strengths lie in building indigenous CNC-SPMs and focusing on operational efficiency by implementing multiple automation projects for higher productivity and cost control. We harness these strengths to allow our customers to benefit from the dual advantage of cost-effectiveness and enhanced machining capabilities.

Over 34 years of
Engineering Experience

We possess engineering capabilities from product conceptualization to production, which encompass product, process, machine, fixture, and cutting tool design to supply finished assembled products.

Extensive Manufacturing
Experience

We manufacture and supply a wide range of precision forged and machined components for different segments. Our capabilities include manufacturing finished components whose weights range from 1.75 grams (an aerospace component) to 8,940 grams (an automotive component).

Proposition

We take pride in our healthy & ever-lasting relationships with well-known Indian as well as global OEMs. Our diversified business model and proven financial performances are driven by generating value across our product segments and stimulating customer engagement.
