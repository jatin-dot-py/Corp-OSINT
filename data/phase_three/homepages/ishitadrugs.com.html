About Ishita Drugs & Industries Limited

Ishita Drugs & Industries Limited is the flagship entity of the “Ishita Group,” a business house that has earned a strong reputation in the Indian industry for over three decades. The Group’s operations span the manufacturing of a wide range of bulk active ingredients, their derivatives, and drug intermediates.

Why Choose Us

ISHITA has been founded and promoted by Mr. Jagdish Agrawal, an M.S. (Chemical Engineering) from the University of Florida, USA. He has more than 45 years' experience in the chemical and pharmaceutical industry and is widely acknowledged as an expert in this field.
