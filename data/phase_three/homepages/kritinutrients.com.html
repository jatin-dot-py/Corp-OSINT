Our business model

Balancing the regional and the international

We see ourselves as a geographically balanced company. We address the needs of the regional customer through our edible oils brand; we address the needs of the global customer through our value-added products.

Kriti Nutrients

Kriti Nutrients is principally driven by a commitment to ethic and values

Our operating philosophy is driven by two words:Do Right.This two-word philosophy encapsulates all that we are, how we intend to conduct ourselves and all that we intend to be. We believe that even as Do right; was always relevant,its importance has deepened.

Our business model

Kriti Nutrients is committed to superior standards that enhance respect and growth

We are engaged in a responsible business at a time of accelerated change. As India graduates from the consumption of loose and unbranded edible oils to organized and branded alternatives, there is a premium on the need to manufacture and deliver the highest quality standard.

Our business model

Kriti Nutrients is an innovative,future-ready and technology-led company

Our business model

Kriti Nutrients is driven a by a responsible commitment to ESG

A robust environment-social-governance (ESG) commitment represents the heart of our business.

We believe that ESG is important and critical in our business where the products we make need to be used in food preparation, which is integral to human health.
