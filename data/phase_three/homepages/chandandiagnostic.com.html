Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Centre Details & Locations

We run many labs all over Lucknow. Open the website or type "Chandan Diagnostic Centre near me" in any search bar to see the one that sits closest to you.

Chandan has set up labs in many towns of Uttar Pradesh so that more people reach a test centre without a long journey.

Go to our website or open Google Maps. Type Chandan Diagnostic Centre - the map shows the branch that sits nearest to you.

Every lab holds NABL besides ISO badges - the same set of rules for machines, staff and reports applies in each building.

Many branches send a worker to your house to take blood or urine. Book the visit on the website or call the desk number and pick a time.

Health Checkup & Packages

We sell multiple check up plans - a head-to-toe exam, a panel for women, a panel for people over sixty-five and any mix of tests that suit your personal needs.

A full check up once every year suits most people - your doctor will tell you if you need the tests more or less often because of your age, habits or illnesses.

A qualified pathologist checks every blood result plus a qualified radiologist checks every image. We follow a written quality control routine for every step.

Open our website choose the plan you want and place the order. The booking ends in a few clicks.

For most plans we send a collector to your home to take blood, urine or swabs.

Home Sample Collection

Call us or click "book collector." A trained collector arrives at your door, checks your ID but also takes the samples.

If the date no longer suits you, contact the helpline or open the booking page and pick a new slot.

Reports

Type your mobile number or booking ID into the login screen - your reports appear on the screen as well as you may save or print them.

We do not release your name, phone number or test results to anyone except you or the doctor you name in writing.

Tests Information & Instructions

Some tests need you to stop eating for eight to twelve hours - the collector will remind you when you book.

Drink plain water, skip alcohol and do not eat a heavy meal the night before the test. Follow the fasting rule if you receive one.

Before a scan you might need to stop eating or stop certain drugs. The radiology desk prints the exact rule for the scan you booked.

For an abdomen scan, you may need to arrive with an empty stomach or with a full bladder - the technician phones you the day before or tells you which rule applies.

Take off watches chains, rings, hairpins and any other metal. Tell the staff if you have a pacemaker, valve, plate or other implant. Follow the fasting rule also the contrast drink rule if the scan needs them.
