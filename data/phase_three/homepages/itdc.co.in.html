2. What Are Cookies?


Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, mobile, or tablet) when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, improve user experience, and collect analytical information.


3. How We Use Cookies


ITDC may use cookies for the following purposes:


Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly, including:


  • Website navigation
  • Security features
  • Session management
  • Form submissions

Without these cookies, certain services may not work correctly.


Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand:


  • How visitors use the website
  • Which pages are visited most often
  • Website performance and errors

This information helps improve website functionality and user experience.


Preference Cookies

These cookies remember:


  • Language preferences
  • Region settings
  • User preferences

Third-Party Cookies

Some pages may include services from third parties such as:


  • Analytics providers
  • Social media platforms
  • Embedded videos or maps

These third parties may place cookies on your device according to their own privacy policies.


4. Information Collected


Cookies may collect limited technical information such as:

5. Managing Cookies
