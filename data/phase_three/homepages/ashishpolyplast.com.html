About Us

Ashish Polyplast Limited was established in February 1994. Over the years, the company has rapidly evolved, setting benchmarks in the production of flexible industrial and agricultural hoses under the brand name “REALON”.

RealOne hoses are engineered for applications that demand superior strength and durability. They are manufactured using fully automatic, state-of-the-art machinery. With years of experience and advanced technology, the company ensures strict quality control at every stage of production, providing end-users with maximum quality assurance.

Crafted using high-quality raw materials and advanced compounding and blending techniques, every RealOn hose is produced under a rigorous quality control system to meet all required specifications.
