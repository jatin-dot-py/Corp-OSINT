About Us

Super Tannery Ltd, is the world’s leading Buffalo leather tannery and specializes in the manufacturing of high quality leather for Automotive & Furniture Upholstery Bags, Belts, Sporting Goods and Equestrian equipment.

We Offer

Leather

We are the world leaders of best buffalo leather since 1953 offering a wide range of tannages for embossed leather, lifestyle/ milled leather and leather for belts.

Accessories

We are the leading manufacturers of premium leather fashion bags, belts and other accessories crafted to meet the styling needs of all foreign markets.

Equestrian

We are a manufacturer and supplier of equestrian goods to leading brands across the globe and stand as their top-choice for their requirements. We have a wide range of leather & non-leather product lines ranging from saddles, bridles, reins etc.

Our Brand

Pioneered in 1953, under the competent vision of Mr. Eshan Kareem’s modest production and a constant innovative vision encompassed in generation has made Super Tannery Limited an International leather empire. 

Years of supplying premium leather to the horseback riding industry, Super Tannery Limited has now moved a step ahead in manufacturing of Equestrian products with the launch of its self trademark “Equestria”.

Whether it’s a question of rider’s comfort or horse body shape, Equestria is a one stop solution for all your Equestrian problems. Extra pliant seats crafted with excellent quality leather provides riders a sheer comfort and well-molded horse saddlery makes Equestria earn the promised commitments.
