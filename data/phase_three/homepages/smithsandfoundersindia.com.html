Smiths & Founders (India) Limited

Smiths & Founders (India) Limited was originally promoted as a small Enterprise by the members of the Shastry family of Shimoga in the year 1982. It was reconstituted into a Company during 1990. The Company issued public shares and listed them on the Bombay Stock Exchange in 1993 and became a widely held public limited Company.


The Business:

Smiths & Founders (India) Limited is engaged in manufacturing Forgings and Machined Components used in Automobile, Agriculture, Mining, Material Handling, Power Transmission, Hydraulic and General Engineering Industries. The Company has long-standing experience of over three decades in engineering small, precision, intricate Steel Forgings


Location:

The Registered Office of the Company is located in Bengaluru and the manufacturing units are situated at Shimoga, in the state of Karnataka, INDIA. The Company has developed hundreds of varieties of Forgings. The Company is catering to both Domestic and Export Markets.


Quality Policy

Smiths & Founders (India) Limited

Smiths & Founders (India) Limited was originally promoted as a small Enterprise by the members of the Shastry family of Shimoga in the year 1982. It was reconstituted into a Company during 1990. The Company issued public shares and listed them on the Bombay Stock Exchange in 1993 and became a widely held public limited Company.


The Business:

Smiths & Founders (India) Limited is engaged in manufacturing Forgings and Machined Components used in Automobile, Agriculture, Mining, Material Handling, Power Transmission, Hydraulic and General Engineering Industries. The Company has long-standing experience of over three decades in engineering small, precision, intricate Steel Forgings.


Location:

The Registered Office of the Company is located in Bengaluru and the manufacturing units are situated at Shimoga, in the state of Karnataka, INDIA. The Company has developed more than 500 varieties of Forgings & Castings and more than 1000 models of Cylinder Liners. The Company is catering to both Domestic and Export Markets.

FORGINGS

We manufacture wide range of forgings for our clients. The product range includes:

  • Automobile Components- Rocker Arms, Connecting Rods, Tie Rod Ends Yokes, Spiders, Clutch Hubs, Clutch Release Levers, Clevis, Clutch Plates, Pressure Plates, etc.
  • General Engineering - Gear Blanks, Wheel Dressers, Insulator Pins, Insulator Hooks, Tool Holders,Shackles, Rotating Eyes, Chain Links and similar products.
  • Hydraulic Forgings - Valve Bodies, Rod Eye Ends, Elbows, Ports, etc.
  • Mining Tools - Coal Augurs, Rotary Drill Bits, Mining Picks, Bit Holders, Pick Holders, etc.
  • Agricultural Implements - Rotary Tines (Wet & Dry) , Rotovator Shovels, Cultivator Blades, etc.
