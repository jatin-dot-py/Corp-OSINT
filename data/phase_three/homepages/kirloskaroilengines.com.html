    Fluid Dynamics
    • Residential Pumps

      Designed to manage water supply from wells, municipal lines and storage tanks, these pumps ensure consistent water pressure for everyday household use.

    • Agriculture Pumps

      Reliable and energy-efficient pumping solutions for irrigation, water transfer and agricultural water management. Built for dependable performance in demanding farming environments.

    • Industrial Pumps

      High-performance pumping solutions engineered for industrial fluid handling, wastewater and infrastructure applications, delivering durable and efficient operation.

    • Solar Pumps

      Solar-powered pumping systems that use photovoltaic panels to operate without grid dependency. Ideal for remote locations, they offer sustainable performance with minimal operating costs.

    • Diesel Engine

      High-compression engines known for durability, efficiency and high torque, widely used in generators, tractors and heavy-duty equipment.

    • Diesel Engine Pumps

      Heavy-duty engine-driven pumps built for reliable water transfer in areas without electricity, delivering high torque and efficient long-duration performance.

    • Petrol Engine Pumps

      Lightweight and portable pumps designed for residential and light commercial water transfer applications, offering easy operation and low maintenance.

    • Control Panel

      Advanced control systems designed for efficient monitoring, operation and management of pumping and fluid-handling applications.

    • International Business - Agriculture

      Integrated agricultural power solutions including diesel gensets, gas-powered gensets, battery energy storage systems (BESS) and intelligent power monitoring systems.

    • International Business - Industrial

      Engineered fluid-handling solutions for industrial and commercial applications, supporting water, oil and chemical transport across infrastructure and energy sectors.
