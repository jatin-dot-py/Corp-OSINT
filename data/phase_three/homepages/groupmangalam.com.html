About Us

Group Mangalam

Group Mangalam is a multinational conglomerate, with a rich legacy of 8 decades spanning from 1942 when it was founded in Beawar, Rajasthan. Led by our dynamic chairman Mr. Vipin Prakash Mangal, Group Mangalam has strategic interests in various crucial industries like Agriculture (Mangalam Agri), Stainless Steel (Mangalam Saarloh), E-waste Management (Mangalam ECS), Specialty Chemicals (Mangalam Dura) and Structured Finance.

Our Reach

Group Mangalam's proud business verticals

Mangalam Agri

India's leading processor of Castor, Mustard and Soya bean with exports to various countries. Also having Cotton Ginning operations and Rice and Wheat Mill.

Mangalam Saarloh

State of the art plant for Stainless Steel Melting and Rolling. The various engineering grades are used in many critical machinery and high performance applications.

Mangalam ECS

An environment-friendly e-Waste management company committed to end-to-end management of electrical and electronic waste.

Mangalam Dura

The leading supplier of environment friendly colours for various substrates with a comprehensive range of colours. Many Global OEMs prefer to use our colours to maintain quality and consistency of their finished products.

Mangalam Finserv

This division provides customised logistics and finance solutions for companies depending on their needs. Our group facilitates services like bill discounting, bonded warehousing facilities and collateral management.
