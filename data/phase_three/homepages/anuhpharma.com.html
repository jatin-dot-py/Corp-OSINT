Who We Are

A dependable partner for your API supply.

Anuh Pharma pairs six decades of process expertise with world-class quality systems to supply Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients to formulation companies worldwide — with the consistency, documentation and regulatory reach global customers expect.

Backed by the resources of the INR 8.5 bn SK Group, we manufacture at scale while holding every batch to the same exacting standards.

  • USFDA — Zero 483 (2026)
  • EDQM & EU-GMP (AEMPS)
  • WHO-PQ Prequalified APIs
  • Multiple CEPs (EDQM)
