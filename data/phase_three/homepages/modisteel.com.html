Welcome to

Modi Steel

Well known in the Steel Industry with experience of over three decades in Rolling/Re-Rolling. Being an ISO 9001:2008 certified company, we are constantly making every possible effort to upgrade our technology.

We have production capacity of 1,20,000 TPA and we roll almost all kinds of alloy, special & also non-ferrous steel, which find wide applications in various industries.

Strength and Durability

Steel is renowned for its strength and durability, making it a preferred choice for structural applications.

Sustainability

Steel is an eco-friendly material as it is 100% recyclable, making it a sustainable choice for construction. It can be reused without compromising its quality, reducing the environmental impact associated with building materials.

Design Flexibility

Steel’s malleability and strength allow architects and engineers to push the boundaries of design.

Energy Efficiency

Steel buildings can be designed to optimize energy efficiency through insulation, efficient HVAC systems, and sustainable design principles.
