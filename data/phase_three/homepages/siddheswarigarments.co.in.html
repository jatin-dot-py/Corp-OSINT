Welcome to Siddheswari Garments Ltd.

Welcome to **SIDDHESWARI GARMENTS LIMITED**, a symbol of quality and trust in the garment manufacturing industry since 1994. As a public limited company, we are proudly listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and the Calcutta Stock Exchange Ltd. Our journey of three decades has been defined by a relentless pursuit of excellence and a steadfast commitment to our clients and stakeholders.

We specialize in producing a diverse range of high-quality apparel, blending traditional craftsmanship with modern technology. Our operations are guided by a strong corporate governance framework, ensuring transparency and accountability in all our endeavors. We are dedicated to delivering sustainable value to our investors, fostering a positive work environment for our employees, and contributing to the economic growth of our community.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a global leader in the garments industry, setting the standard for quality, innovation, and sustainability. We aim to create exceptional value for our customers, employees, and stakeholders by continuously improving our processes and expanding our reach into new markets.

Our Mission

Our mission is to produce high-quality garments that meet the diverse needs of our clients. We are committed to ethical business practices, fostering a safe and rewarding work environment, and contributing positively to the communities we serve. Through dedicated research and development, we strive to stay at the forefront of textile technology and design.

Our Products & Services

Garment Trading

We offer end-to-end garment trading solutions, from design to production and delivery. Our facilities are equipped with state-of-the-art machinery.

Private Label Services

Our private label services allow brands to create and market their own apparel lines with our expertise in design and production.

Sustainable Solutions

We are committed to eco-friendly practices, including the use of sustainable materials and ethical production methods to minimize our environmental impact.
