Emami Paper, the paper manufacturing arm of the diversified Emami Group, is a leading name in India’s paper industry. Backed by the Group’s strong legacy across diverse industries like personal care & healthcare, edible oils & foods, bio-diesel, pharma retail, real estate, bookstores, contemporary art and sports, Emami Paper combines innovation with scale to deliver high-quality paper solutions.

Emami Paper specializes in the production of newsprint, writing and printing paper, and multilayer coated board. It proudly holds the distinction of being India’s largest newsprint manufacturer and the only producer in Eastern India. Additionally, it is a prominent player in the multilayer coated board segment, catering to a broad range of packaging and printing needs.
