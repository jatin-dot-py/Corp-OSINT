Welcome to
Rushil Decor Limited

Rushil Décor Ltd. is one of the leading company in Laminate and MDF panel boards industry in India with a global foot print in and around 57+ countries. Founded in 1993, the company is today the partner of choice for our discerning customers around the world. We are defined by our commitment to excellence and our passion to bring to life designs that meld expertise with art.

We aim to create a strong emotional connection in the hearts of our customers, through design & experiences. We are always conscious of who we are designing for and the total experience we intend to create.

We revel in our ability to inspire and excite the world, challenge and break paradigms, influence & form the future.
