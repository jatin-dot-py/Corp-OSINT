More About Us

Who we are

Lincoln is proud of its manufacturing capabilities and we are really distinct and powerful when it comes to the production capacity.

There are global standards and norms which a manufacturing lab should scan through and we ensure that our processes are in compliance with WHO-cGMP norms.

Our lab has been given ISO 9001:2015 certifications; thoroughly highlighting Lincoln’s dedication in direction of high quality and safety.
