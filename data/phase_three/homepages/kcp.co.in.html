Celebrating 85 years of technology
innovation and inclusive growth.

KCP started as a small cooperative sugar plant in 1941 by its founder Sri. V. Ramakrishna. It has over the years expanded rapidly enthused by the leadership of its founder and inspired on the lines of the technological foresight. Today KCP has 9 manufacturing locations over various geographies in India and Vietnam with over 2000 in employee strength.

Our Businesses

Technology provides the cutting edge to our services, state of the art facilities and latest process trends across all our verticals.

Cement

KCP’s Cement Business is over 60+ years with manufacturing plants in Macherla & Mukthyala - Andhra Pradesh, producing a combined annual capacity of 2.2 million tons of the finest quality premium grade cement in India.

+KNOW MORE

Heavy Engineering

KCP Heavy Engineering is over 60+ years offering a world class Build to Order heavy manufacturing facility, with a fully integrated Steel Foundry, strategically located in very close proximity (9 km) to the Chennai port for sea transport.

+KNOW MORE

Power

KCP's foray into the Power sector via Hydel, Wind, Waste Heat Recovery, Solar and Thermal based plants at its various locations was primarily for internal consumption.

+KNOW MORE

Sugar & Power EPC

FIVES CAIL – KCP Limited is a Joint Venture (JV) with French engineering pioneer Fives Group to build turnkey Sugar Plant and Power plants.

+KNOW MORE

Sugar

KCP Vietnam Limited is a KCP subsidiary based in Vietnam. It has 2 sugar plants located at Son Hoa & Dong Xuan with a combined capacity of 8000 TCD.

+KNOW MORE

Hospitality

Mercure Hyderabad KCP, a 4 star hotel in partnership with ACCOR Group of France, the world's largest hotel group is located in the heart of Hyderabad's Banjara Hills and a walk away from City Centre.
