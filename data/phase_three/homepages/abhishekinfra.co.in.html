Welcome to Abhishek Infraventures Limited

M/s. Abhishek Infraventures Limited, a listed company, was incorporated on 27.07.1984 in the name and style as Executive Leasing Limited. Subsequently the name of the company was changed to Executive Stock Broking Services Limited on 03.02.1998, and then finally changed to Abhishek Infraventures Limited on 02.09.2013.

At present, the company is listed at BSE Limited and Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited with the Paid up Capital of Rs. 5,04,90,000. The company’s registered office is situated at Block No 6C/2, 6th Floor, Melange Towers, Madhapur, Hyderabad, Telangana 500081
