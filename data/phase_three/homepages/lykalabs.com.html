Advancing Complex Therapeutics Through Science and Technology

Lyka Labs combines pharmaceutical research, manufacturing expertise, and regulatory excellence to develop specialized formulations across critical therapeutic segments. Our integrated capabilities support hospitals, fertility specialists, healthcare institutions, and veterinary professionals through reliable, science-backed solutions.

Critical Care

A leader in sterile injectable formulations with expertise in lyophilized injections, anti-infectives, antifungals, oncology support therapies, and hospital care solutions.

Fertinova

Animal Health

Our Animal Healthcare Division delivers research-driven pharmaceutical and nutritional solutions for livestock, poultry, equine, and companion animals, supporting healthier and more sustainable veterinary ecosystems.
