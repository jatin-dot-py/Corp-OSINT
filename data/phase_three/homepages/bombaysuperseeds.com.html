About Bombay Super Seeds

Four Decades of Seed Excellence

Founded by a farming family with deep agricultural roots, Bombay Super Seeds has grown from a regional seed producer into a recognized seed company serving farmers across India. Our focus remains simple: provide superior quality seeds that help growers maximize yield, profitability, and sustainability.

Agriculture Sowing Seeds Company

Welcome to Bombay Super Hybrid Seeds Limited!

Bombay Super Seeds, a leading agriculture Sowing Seeds Company in India, supplies a wide range of high-quality seeds to farmers. We supply high-yielding seeds of grains and vegetables. We have extensive experience in offering premium seeds to farmers all across the country. We empower farmers to gain maximum profits from our seeds. Our seeds are not only resistant to many diseases, but they are also very easy to sow. We are committed to offering seeds that help farmers in getting sustainable high yields.

A Trusted Agriculture Seeds Company India

We are a reliable agriculture seeds company because we intend to serve the farmers. We sincerely value the hard work of the farmers, and therefore, we leave no stone unturned to provide them with the best quality seeds so that they get maximum benefits. With our seeds, most farmers enjoy high profit margins. We supply seeds that also reduce the operating costs on the farms. A renowned agriculture seeds company in India, Bombay Super Seeds is a favorite of clients all around the country.

Why Are We Considered Among The Top 10 Seeds Company In India

As one of the topmost agriculture seeds exporters in India, we focus primarily on the quality of our seeds. Our seeds are uniform in size. Also, the seeds are free from insect damage. Our seeds are free from any seed-borne diseases. Our genetically pure seeds turn out to be very profitable for the farmers. Farmers can grow nutrient-rich crops from our seeds. And these all reasons make us one of the prominent Agriculture listed companies in India.
