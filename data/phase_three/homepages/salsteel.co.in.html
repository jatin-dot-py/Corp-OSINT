Who We Are

Excellence in Steel Manufacturing

SAL Steel Ltd. is one of India's leading producers of Sponge Iron and Ferro Alloys. Located in the Gandhidham district of Gujarat, our strategically located plant leverages advanced technology to deliver superior quality steel inputs essential for infrastructure development.

Driven by a vision of growth and integrity, we continue to expand our capacity while maintaining strict compliance with safety and environmental standards.

Frequently Asked Questions

What products does SAL Steel manufacture?

We primarily manufacture high-grade Ferro Chrome, including Low Silicon Ferro Chrome and High Carbon Ferro Chrome and Sponge Iron using rotary kiln technology.

Where is your plant located?

Our manufacturing facility is located in Gandhidham, Kutch district, Gujarat, providing excellent logistical connectivity.

Are you ISO certified?

Yes, SAL Steel Ltd is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, ensuring international quality standards.
