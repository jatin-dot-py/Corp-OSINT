Moldtek delivers precision-driven civil engineering, BIM, steel detailing, and construction management solutions. Our expertise ensures efficiency, sustainability, and structural integrity, optimizing projects from urban infrastructure to industrial developments. As a trusted partner across the world, we engineer resilient, high-performance designs that set industry benchmarks.

Key Highlights

Steel Detailing

● A background in commercial, industrial, infrastructural, residential, and specialized steel structure design.
● Model accuracy and efficiency with Tekla, AutoCAD, Advance Steel, Revit, Navisworks, and SDS/2.
● AISC, CISC, BS, AS/NZS, IS, and OSHA safety regulations compliance.

BIM

Engineering Precision in Plants,
Empowering Industrial Growth

Moldtek offers comprehensive plant engineering solutions that integrate piping, process, mechanical, electrical, structural, and instrumentation services. Our approach ensures functional synergy, safety, and execution-ready designs tailored for industrial environments. From greenfield setups to complex retrofits, we deliver coordinated engineering that enhances efficiency, reduces rework, and supports long-term operational excellence. As a trusted partner to global manufacturers, we help build future-ready plants engineered for performance and reliability.

  • Process engineering
  • Piping
  • Civil & Structural engineering
  • Mechanical engineering
  • Electrical engineering
  • Instrumentation engineering

Key Highlights

Piping Engineering

● 3D piping layouts with isometric drawings and CAESAR II-based stress analysis.
● Expertise in utility and process piping across oil & gas, pharma, and energy sectors.
● Seamless integration with structural and equipment models for clash-free execution.

Learn More

Process Engineering

● Simulation-driven process flow design optimizing efficiency and resource utilization.
● End-to-end engineering from concept to commissioning across industrial plants.
● Custom process layouts ensuring compliance with ISO, ASME, and API standards.

Learn More

Mechanical Engineering

● 3D modeling and detailing of equipment, skids, and material handling systems.
● Engineering for manufacturability—reducing rework and enhancing plant performance.
● Integrated mechanical layouts aligned with electrical, structural, and process teams.

Learn More

Electrical Engineering

● Complete power system design including MCCs, cable trays, and lighting layouts.
● ETAP-based load flow, short circuit, and protection coordination analysis.
● Compliant with NEC, IEC, and OSHA standards for safe and efficient power distribution.

Learn More

Civil & Structural Engineering

● Precision design of pipe racks, platforms, and equipment foundations using STAAD & Tekla.
● Proven expertise in structural steel detailing and concrete design for industrial projects.
● BIM-integrated workflows ensuring safety, accuracy, and cost-effective construction.

Learn More

Instrumentation Engineering

● Field device layout, loop diagrams, and hook-up drawings for full plant automation.
● SmartPlant and AVEVA-based instrumentation documentation and tag management.
● Integration with PLC, SCADA, and DCS systems for accurate control and monitoring.
