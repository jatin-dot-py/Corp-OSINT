Who We Are

Incorporated in 2015, Purple Style Labs (PSL) is an omnichannel platform, with a online and offline presence, PSL is providing carefully curated selections in luxury fashion through its channels, both nationally and globally.

Our Vision

Tapping into Indian design’s large potential, we aim to expand the reach of India based brands to the biggest luxury markets of the world. Through a continuous expansion of our global footprint, increase and optimization of our product categories, we are en route to creating a carefully curated selections in luxury fashion in the country, providing global access to Indian design.
