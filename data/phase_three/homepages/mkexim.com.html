Welcome to MK Exim India Limited

For over 30 years, we at MK Exim India Limited have worked hard to deliver to you, our valued customer, the best in professional salon products and supplies. We have curated the finest brands that the professional beauty industry has to offer and paired them with our world-class services and education. MK Exim India Limited is one of the largest suppliers of professional hair, skin, nail care, and beauty equipment products. We are proud to represent some of the world’s best brands to over 10,000 salons across India.

Mission Statement

To provide salons and consumers with superior professional products and accessories to maximise salon profits and consumer satisfaction.
