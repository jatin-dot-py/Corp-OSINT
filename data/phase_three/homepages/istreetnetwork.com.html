iStreet Network is a sovereign AI infrastructure and solutions provider, enabling enterprises operationalise and scale artificial intelligence (AI) through unified, AI-native platforms. With a primary focus on security, governance and compliance, iStreet is specifically engineered to meet the rigorous demands of the world’s most highly regulated sectors.

iStreet AI Centre of Excellence (CoE)

The iStreet AI Centre of Excellence (CoE) serves as the strategic core of the organization, designed to architect unified AI ecosystem that transitions artificial intelligence (AI) from experimental pilots to large-scale, enterprise-grade production. It acts as a specialized engine that integrates proprietary technology, industry-specific expertise, and rigorous governance to ensure AI is a foundational element of modern infrastructure rather than just an additive tool.
