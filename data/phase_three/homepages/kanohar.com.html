About Us

KANOHAR provides end-to-end service offerings across the product lifecycle, including site installation support, supervision of testing and commissioning of transformers. Our portfolio includes Power Transformers up to 500MVA 400kV , Shunt Reactors up to 125MVAR 400kV , Scott-connected Transformers up to 100 MVA 220/2x55 kV , Traction Transformers up to 30.24MVA 220kV , Gas Insulated Switchgear, and AIS/GIS Substations and Transmission Lines up to 400kV class on an EPC basis.
