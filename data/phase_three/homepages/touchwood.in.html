Who We Are

Touchwood Entertainment Ltd.
With over 2 decades of experience and making a strong place in the industry, Touchwood Entertainment Ltd is India’s first NSE Listed event management company.

We are a Specialized Wedding & Event Management Company for Bespoke & Luxury Weddings and Celebrations.
We curate and execute bespoke event experiences for any Destinations within India and Internationally
We are a team of high octane individuals who are always committed to the job to deliver the best experiences to our patrons. Our team is highly passionate and driven when it comes to servicing our prestigious clients.

Management

  • Vijay Arora
    Whole-Time Director

Vijay Arora

Whole-Time Director

Vijay Arora journey in event management forayed its seeds with the family owned business of facility providers. He grew in an atmosphere where ‘events’ was a day to day word and ‘challenges’ were a child’s play. His exposure to set designing and execution for numerous events led him to learn things from scratch.
He heads the business verticals that engage in BTL Activations, Corporate Events, Artist Management, Large scale weddings, Public sector events. Carrying over 19 Years of wealth of experience in managing large format, people & process oriented deliveries, he has effectively led his company to the next level. His extensive knowledge of his product, know how and technique has made his company apt for handling projects of any scale in any part of the world.

Managing Director
