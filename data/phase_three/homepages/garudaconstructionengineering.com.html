What differenciates us

Expertise in EPC Solutions

Garuda Construction and Engineering Pvt. Ltd. is more than just a construction company; we are your trusted EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) specialist. With a wealth of experience and a proven track record, we have honed our skills to deliver turnkey solutions that exceed expectations.

What we do

A Legacy of Excellence

Today, Garuda Construction and Engineering stands as a multifaceted powerhouse in the realm of civil construction and engineering. Our portfolio boasts some of the most exceptional engineering talent in India. Among our proud achievements is the iconic New Delhi Police Headquarters, a testament to our unwavering dedication to quality and precision.

Based in the business capital of India, Mumbai; Garuda Construction and Engineering is not just a civil engineering company; we are creators of art in concrete and steel. Our vision reaches for the skies while staying firmly rooted in the ground, constructing structures that are not just buildings but statements of craftsmanship and durability.

What we do

Prioritizing Safety, Comfort, and Choice

At Garuda, we understand that a building is more than just bricks and mortar. It’s a space where lives unfold, where dreams take shape, and where memories are made. This is why every project we undertake is driven by a commitment to consumer safety, unparalleled comfort, and a deep understanding of individual preferences.

What are we known for

From Residential Bliss to Commercial Marvels

Our expertise spans a diverse range of construction domains, from crafting residential spaces that exude comfort and warmth, to erecting commercial marvels that define skylines. Whether it’s your dream home or a cutting-edge commercial project, Garuda brings the same level of passion, precision, and professionalism to every endeavor.
