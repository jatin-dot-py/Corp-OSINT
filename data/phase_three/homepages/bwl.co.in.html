about us

Company

Blue Water Logistics Limited (BWL) is a trusted logistics partner established in 2010, evolving from a Customs House Agency and freight forwarding specialist into a multimodal solutions provider across sea, air, and land. With over 15 years of hands-on industry expertise, we ensure safe, compliant, and efficient cargo movement for clients across sectors. Our public listing in June 2025 marked a defining step in BWL’s growth, strengthening our scale, governance, and ability to serve global trade demands. As owners and operators of ISO tank containers, we lead in chemical and bulk liquid logistics, supported by a fleet of 75 dedicated trucks that ensures dependable nationwide transport.

Backed by global reach with local intelligence and the leadership of L. N. Mishra, Lalit Panda, Madhusmita Mohanty, and Supriya Mishra, BWL is committed to building a future-ready, client-centric logistics ecosystem.

We are the logistics partner of choice for 400,000 worldwide customers in a diverse range of industries.
