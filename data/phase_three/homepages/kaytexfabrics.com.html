Our Story

Since our inception in 1996, Kaytex Fabrics Limited has evolved into a key player in the fashion industry, delivering much more than just textiles. We provide the foundation upon which fashion brands build their success. From leveraging disruptive digital printing technology to offering a fully integrated production process, we’ve moved beyond tradition to become a driving force in today’s fast-paced fashion market.

Our innovation-focused approach allows us to serve the organized fashion industry while having deep-rooted connections to the unbranded retail markets, ensuring that we are accelerating growth at every level of the market.
