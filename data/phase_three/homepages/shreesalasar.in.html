Wagering requirement example

The wagering requirement is one of the most important numbers in any casino bonus.

Suppose a promotion offers:

  • Deposit: ₹100
  • Bonus: ₹100
  • Wagering: 30x bonus
  • Minimum deposit: ₹10
  • Expiry: 30 days

If wagering applies to the ₹100 bonus only:

₹100 × 30 = ₹3,000

The player would therefore need ₹3,000 in qualifying wagers to complete the wagering requirement.

If the terms instead specify 30x deposit + bonus, the calculation would be:

(₹100 + ₹100) × 30 = ₹6,000

This difference is significant, which is why players should always confirm exactly what amount the wagering multiplier applies to.
