EXPO ENGINEERING AND PROJECTS LIMITED

(Formerly known as Expo Gas Containers Limited)

Established in 1982, under the visionary leadership of the late Shri. Shaukatali
Mewawala, a passionate engineer and IIT Bombay alumnus.

An established fully integrated Engineering company, which has evolved into a
prominent heavy engineering company specializing in the engineering and manufacturing of diverse process plant equipment.

With a legacy of excellence and innovation, we are dedicated to delivering superior
engineering solutions that exceed industry standards and drive sustainable growth
for our clients and communities.
