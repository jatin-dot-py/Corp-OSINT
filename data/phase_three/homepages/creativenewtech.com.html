Overview

Who We Are

Since 1992, Creative Newtech has been navigating global brands across Indian markets with market intelligence, reach and execution depth.
With expertise in market entry specialization, brand licensing, contract manufacturing and enterprise solutions, Creative Newtech Limited has shaped strong journeys for iconic brands such as Honeywell, GoPro, ViewSonic, Samsung and many more.

With deep expertise in distribution, we serve as a trusted enterprise solutions provider, revolutionizing industries through forward-thinking strategies and strong collaborations.  

Our north star has always guided us toward larger horizons, wider routes and stronger momentum!
