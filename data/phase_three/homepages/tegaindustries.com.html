      Who Are We

      Partnerships in
      Practice

      We are a global leader in designing and manufacturing of critical consumables for the mineral processing, mining and material handling industries. We are a multi-national company with presence in 92+ countries and are headquartered in Kolkata, India. Our manufacturing hubs, located across India, Australia, Chile and South Africa help us deliver quality and trust. Since our inception in 1976, we have significantly expanded our product portfolio and global footprint. Our robust research and development, quality control and customer engagement have established us as the preferred partner for 700+ customers.
