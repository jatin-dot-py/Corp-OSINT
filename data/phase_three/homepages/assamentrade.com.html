About Assam Entrade Limited



Assam Entrade Limited is a trusted Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) operating since 1985. Registered with RBI and listed on BSE, we specialize in loans, investments, and financial solutions for individuals and corporates. With decades of industry experience, we have built a strong reputation for reliability, transparency, and customer-focused services. Our expertise spans across providing loans and advances, dealing in shares, stocks, bonds, and other financial instruments, as well as supporting businesses with tailored financial solutions.
