Who We Are

About Us

 Established in 1987 and headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra, Damodar Industries Limited is a well-established name and trendsetter in the Indian textile sector. Listed on both the BSE and NSE, the company has grown into a leading manufacturer and exporter, recognized for its diverse capabilities spanning spinning, advanced post-spinning processes, and weaving. 

 

At Damodar Industries, our state-of-the-art greige yarn manufacturing infrastructure combines high-volume capacity with specialized craftsmanship. Driven by an impressive production capacity of 1,500 tons per month, our advanced facilities are engineered to deliver consistent quality, strength, and uniformity at scale.

Powered by a robust industrial setup comprising 80,000 spindles, 2,200 rotors, and 30 TFOs, alongside specialized fancy, slub, and bouclé machines, we produce a diverse portfolio of standard and novelty greige yarns. This cutting-edge machinery allows us to seamlessly execute complex textures and creative variations directly from the core, providing global apparel manufacturers and fabric weavers with a reliable foundation of premium raw materials.

Infrastructure & Innovation

 Driven by a strong emphasis on Research & Development (R&D), Damodar operates advanced manufacturing infrastructure—anchored by its key production facility at the MIDC Textile Park in Amravati, Maharashtra. The company integrates modern technologies (such as high-speed spinning and specialized Italian machinery) alongside post-spinning innovations like TFO, fancy doubling to deliver unique visual textures and superior yarn performance. 

Core Business & Product Portfolio

 

  • Yarn Manufacturing & Export: Damodar Industries specializes in a wide spectrum of cotton, synthetic, and blended yarns. Beyond standard commodities, the company has carved a niche in value-added and fancy yarns—including melange, slub, neps, multi-blend, linen-like, silk-like .
  • Fashion Fabrics: Moving beyond the spinning stage, the company utilizes integrated operations to produce high-quality fashion, apparel, and specialized seasonal fabrics. Its product lines cater directly to B2B segments spanning global fashion houses, apparel brands, and home textile markets.
