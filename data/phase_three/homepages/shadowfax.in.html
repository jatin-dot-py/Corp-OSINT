Our Services

Designed to meet your business's evolving logistics needs.

Ecommerce & D2C
We provide end-to-end delivery services, from standard shipping to same-day delivery (SDD) and next-day delivery (NDD), designed for D2C and e-commerce brands. Powered by intelligent technology and a reliable logistics network, we help businesses deliver faster, operate more efficiently, and create exceptional customer experiences.
Hyperlocal & Quick Commerce
Deliver within hours or offer customers flexible same-day and next-day delivery slots. From groceries and medicines to food and other daily essentials, our technology-driven network enables fast, reliable deliveries with real-time tracking.
SME & Personal Courier
The Shadowfax Courier App powers on-demand pick-up and drop-off services within the city for individuals and small businesses. Affordable, reliable, and backed by one of India's fastest-growing last-mile delivery networks.

Our Key Solutions

Built for ecommerce, D2C brands, large marketplaces, small businesses, and more.

Express Parcel
Reliable parcel delivery built for businesses of every size. From first-mile pick-up to last-mile delivery, we ensure efficient shipping, consistent service, and a seamless customer experience.
Reverse Parcel
Simplify returns with our efficient reverse logistics network. We offer doorstep quality checks, instant refund enablement, and hassle-free reverse parcel management for D2C brands and enterprises.
Prime
Deliver faster with Shadowfax Prime. Our high-speed delivery network enables same-day and next-day deliveries across multiple platforms, helping businesses improve customer satisfaction with reliable, on-time fulfilment.
Prime Large
Built for shipments above 15 kg, Prime Large enables fast, dependable same-day and next-day delivery for bulky and heavy goods. Powered by scalable infrastructure and technology-first execution, it ensures smooth logistics for high-volume, heavy-duty shipments.
Critical Logistics
We manage the secure, end-to-end movement of high-value, time-sensitive, and critical goods from manufacturing plants to warehouses, depots, and distribution centres. Every shipment is handled with speed, precision, and strict compliance with operational protocols.
Hyperlocal
Our hyperlocal delivery solution offers affordable point-to-point courier services for groceries, pharmaceuticals, food, retail, and more. With real-time tracking, extensive coverage, and a trained delivery network, businesses can fulfil orders in as little as 10 minutes or within a few hours.
Quick Commerce
Built for the speed of modern commerce, our quick commerce solution supports both dark stores and retail outlets with express and scheduled doorstep deliveries, helping businesses meet rising customer expectations.
Electric Vehicle Fleet
Our electric vehicle fleet enables sustainable, efficient last-mile deliveries for courier, e-commerce, and retail businesses. Reduce emissions while maintaining reliable, cost-effective delivery performance across cities.
