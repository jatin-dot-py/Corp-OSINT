125 years of pushing performance forward

We are DISA. Built on a legacy of innovation and mastery, we’ve shaped the foundry industry for over a century. Our expertise runs deep - proven through every machine, every casting, and every partnership that stands the test of time.

In partnership with you and our Norican colleagues, we strive to keep your foundry one step ahead in our everchanging industry and world

We develop and manufacture a complete range of metal casting production solutions for the green sand foundry industry.

Headquartered in Taastrup, Denmark, DISA has the industry’s broadest moulding product portfolio, comprising vertical (DISAMATIC®), matchplate (DISA MATCH) and horizontal (DISA FLEX) green sand moulding systems.

We provide complete, integrated foundry lines for a wide variety of customers

Lines which, in addition to our moulding technology platforms, include complete sand plants, sand mixers, conveyor systems and cooling drums, as well as an unparalleled services and spare parts network.

We also offer the class-leading Monitizer® digital solutions, developed in partnership with Norican Digital Lab and AI partner DataProphet, that support the whole digital journey from data collection to AI-driven casting quality optimisation. Monitizer works in all types of foundries and processes, and with any vendor equipment, to deliver serious process improvements – fast.

Your single source for green sand excellence

From moulding and sand preparation to digital optimisation, automation, and aftermarket support, DISA delivers the industry’s most complete green sand foundry solution - engineered to work seamlessly together for maximum uptime, efficiency, and the lowest cost per casting.
