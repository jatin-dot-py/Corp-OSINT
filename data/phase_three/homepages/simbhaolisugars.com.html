About Simbhaoli Sugar

Starting in 1933 as a sugar mill in Simbhaoli, UP, Established by the honourable founder of the Group Sh. Sardar Raghbir Singh Sandhanwalia, CIE (1946), OBE (1918), the Simbhaoli Group is now a diversified farm-to-consumer Agri-Business and FMCG company with leading consumer brands in categories such as staples, food, beverages, home and personal care, and agri-inputs. Armed with a rich legacy from the pre-Independence era, grounded in its strong ties to lakhs of farmers and rural families, the Simbhaoli Group is committed to continue to build world-class consumer products from the inputs provided by the farmers of India.
