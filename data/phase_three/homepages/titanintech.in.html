About Us

We Do Display Technology Differently

At Titan Intech, we believe digital displays are not just screens — they are powerful communication tools.

Founded in 1984, Titan Intech Limited is a publicly listed company with 40+ years of trust, driving India’s transition from technology consumption to creation. Aligned with Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat, we deliver advanced display and integrated Audio-Visual (AV) solutions for varied business needs including those for mission-critical applications.

At the core is UltraLED Displays— our brand of new range of products, indigenously designed, locally assembled, and quality-controlled in India—backed by a ₹250 crore manufacturing investment in India and strengthened by global technology partnerships. Titan Intech serves railways, aviation, smart cities, metros, and public infrastructure, while building a comprehensive AV and LED technology ecosystem with India at its heart.
