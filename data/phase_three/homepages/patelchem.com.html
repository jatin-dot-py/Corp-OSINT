Welcome to Patel Chem Specialities Limited

Patel Chem Specialities Limited in 2008 in India. The Company is GMP & ISO Certified and FDA Approved Manufacturer – Exporter Promoting Quality Products in Pharmaceutical Excipients like Carboxymethyl Cellulose Sodium IP, BP, EP, USP-NF, Croscarmellose Sodium IP, BP, EP, USP-NF, Sodium Starch Glycolate IP, USP-NF , Sodium Starch Glycolate BP, EP and Calcium CMC BP, EP, JP, USP-NF and speciality chemicals like Sodium Monochloro Acetate, Cellulose Powder and Carboxy Methyl Starch.

Our PRODUCTS

Rheollose® - Carboxymethyl Cellulose Sodium

CARBOXYMETHYL CELLULOSE SODIUM is a Cellulose Gum (Cellulose Ether), commercially known as CMC or CMC Sodium or Sodium CMC. CARBOXYMETHYL CELLULOSE is an anionic water-soluble polymer available in number of grades and viscosity types with wide application. SODIUM CARBOXYMETHYL CELLULOSE is odourless, tasteless, non-toxic powder..

view details

Disolwell® - Croscarmellose Sodium

Croscarmellose Sodium is produced by cross linking of Sodium Carboxymethyl Cellulose. It is a white, fibrous, free-flowing powder.Croscarmellose Sodiumis mainly used as superdisintegrant in oral pharmaceutical and nutraceutical formulations like swallowable tablets, orally dispersible tablets, hard capsules, blends, granules and pellets premix..

view details

BlowTab® - Sodium Starch Glycolate

Being the sodium salt of carboxymethyl ether, Sodium Starch Glycolate is a white to off-white, odourless, tasteless, free-flowing powder. It is practically insoluble in water and also in most organic solvents. This product is used as a pharmaceutical-grade dissolution agent and super disintegrant which is produced from corn or potato starch.

view details

Swellcal® - Calcium CMC

Calcium CMC is calcium salt of Carboxy Methyl Cellulose. It is also popularly known as Carmellose Calcium and Croscarmellose Calcium. Calcium CMC ( Carmellose Calcium ) is highly pure white powder used for tablet disintegration. Carboxymethylcellulose Calcium is insoluble in water but it swells in water and Carmellose Calcium used as super disintegrant in pharmaceutical.

view details

Hindcel® - Microcrystalline Cellulose

Microcrystalline Cellulose is a versatile product and used in pharmasutical formulations as Excipient.Mcc is also used in food industries as an anti-caking agent and bulking agent.Microcrystalline Cellulose is derived from wood pulpand the most common from is used in vitamin supplements or tablets.

view details

PharLub™ - Magnesium Stearate

Magnesium Stearate is the most commonly used lubricant for tablets. Magnesium Stearate is also useful because it has lubricating properties, preventing ingredients from sticking to manufacturing equipment during the compression of chemical powders into solid tablets. Magnesium Stearate is often used as an anti-adherent in the manufacture of medical tablets, capsules and powder.

view details

AmyloTab™ C - Pregelatinized Starch

Pregelatinized starch is Physically modified starch, it is partially soluble in Cold water. Pregelatinized starch is widely used in pharmaceutical because it is bland, odourless and capable of digestion. Pregelatinized Starch swell in cold water and therefore reduce time/cost compared with traditional starch paste preparation. Pregelatinized starch is widely used as a pharmaceutical aid, especially as a filler-binder.

view details

Sodium chloro Acetate

It is derived from Monochloro Acetic Acid by neutralizing with Alkali White Powder Highly Soluble in Water Pungent Odor Highly Hygroscopic in Nature Odorless Hazardous Product Under UN No.2659 – Packing Group III, IMCO Class 6.1. Used in manufacturing of Sodium Carboxy Methyl Cellulose, Carboxy Methyl Starch, Guar Gums, Dyes etc
