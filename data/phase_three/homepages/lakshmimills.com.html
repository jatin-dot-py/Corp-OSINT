Founded in 1910, Lakshmi Mills is one of India’s oldest yarn and fabric manufacturing company. The company is a publicly listed entity based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu having a workforce of over 2000 people. The production processes at Lakshmi Mills are on par with global standards. Research and development initiatives have resulted in excellent business practices earning the company numerous certifications and accreditations in the textile industry. At Lakshmi Mills, corporate social responsibility is a way of doing business and the company supports several community initiatives.
