Wings of Variman

Under the umbrella of Variman Global Enterprises Ltd, two key subsidiaries operate, each playing a pivotal role in our diverse business portfolio.

For the past five years, Straton Business Solutions Pvt Ltd has been a dynamic presence in Hyderabad's IT sector. Specializing in the distribution of an extensive array of IT hardware, our offerings include HP's diverse range of laptops and desktops, Acer laptops, and projectors. Additionally, we provide All-in-One (AIO) solutions, peripherals, accessories, and LG commercial TVs. Our comprehensive product lineup caters to the entire state of Telangana, ensuring a one-stop solution for all IT hardware needs.

Verteex Vending & Enterprise Solutions Pvt Ltd has been a distinguished distributor in Hyderabad for the past 15 years, specializing in Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL) products for Out-of-Home (OOH) consumption and institutional bulk sales. We also proudly handle the distribution of Kwality Walls Ice Cream Division, catering to both corporate and retail sectors. Our expertise and commitment have made us a trusted partner in delivering quality solutions across the city.
