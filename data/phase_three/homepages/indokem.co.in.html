Who Are We?

Indokem Ltd, a member of the Khatau group was established in 1946. It was one of the first companies in India to recognize a growing textile industry and has since been a leader in the market of textile dyes and chemicals. We have always been a customer driven and highly service oriented group consistently providing value added products. As an experienced team, we are always on our toes to serve Domestic as well as International market with excellently developed products and round-the-clock customer service.

Indokem Welcomes Refnol:
Expanding Our Reach in the Chemical Sector

Following a successful merger finalized in September 2023, Refnol Resins & Chemicals Ltd. has amalgamated with Indokem Ltd., creating a stronger combined entity in the chemicals sector. This amalgamation brings together Refnol's expertise in resins and chemicals with Indokem's established presence in dyes, sizing chemicals, and electrical capacitors, offering a broader product portfolio and enhanced capabilities for their customers.
