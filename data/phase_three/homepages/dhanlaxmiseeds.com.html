Company Overview

Dhanlaxmi is incorporated in 2005

Our Company was originally incorporated as a Private Limited Company in the name of “Dhanlaxmi Agrichem Private Limited” on November 28, 2005.

                       
                           

Started in 1994 as a rural agriculture shop

                           

In 2005 registered as a company

                           

In 2006 started marketing for cotton seed as a co-marketer of Nuziveedu seeds

                           

In 2015 became ICRISAT member and ISO certified company with govt recognized R&D and Bio-Tech lab

                           

In 2015 Company became direct sub-licensee of Monsanto for BG-II Cotton
