WELCOME TO DHARMAJ CROP GUARD LIMITED Agro Chemicals Manufacturer

Established in 2015, Dharmaj Crop Guard Limited is engaged in the business of manufacturing, distributing, and marketing of a wide range of Agro chemical formulations such as insecticides, fungicides, herbicides, plant growth regulator, micro fertilizers and antibiotic to the B2C and B2B customers. We also engage in the marketing and distribution of agrochemical products under brands in-licensed by us, owned by us and through generic brands, to Indian farmers through our distribution network. We provide crop protection solutions to the farmer to assist them to maximize productivity and profitability. We export our products to more than 38 countries in Latin America, East African Countries, Middle East and Far East Asia. We sell our agrochemical products in granules, powder and liquid forms our customers. Additionally, we manufacture and sell general insect and pest control chemicals for Public Health and Animal Health protection. With an aim to offer a wide product portfolio across the Agri-value chain, we continue to expand our product portfolio by introducing new products. We manufacture and sell various formulations of insecticides, fungicide and herbicides, plant growth regulators, micro fertilizers and antibiotics.

Full-scale Agrochemical Products

We classify our product portfolio under the following categories

Insecticides

We manufacture a wide range of insecticides to suit the requirement of farmers in preventing and curating different crops from being damaged by insects. We develop effective, safe, and sustainable range of insecticides to help farmers and prevent the crops from damage. Certain of our top branded insecticides products are Padgham, Lubrio, Nilaayan, Dahaad, Prudhar and Remora amongst others.

Fungicides

We manufacture a wide range of fungicides that protect the crop from the diseases caused by pathogenic organisms. Fungicide is a specific type of pesticide that is responsible for controlling fungal diseases by inhibiting the fungal growth and killing the fungus. Certain of our top branded fungicides products are Gagandip, Sajaag, Lokraj, Rishmat and Kaviraj amongst others.

Herbicides

Herbicides effectively eliminate weeds and thus reduce mechanical and manual weeding. It also prevents soil erosion and the wastage of resources, ensuring optimum consumption of the resources by the desired plants. Certain of our top branded herbicides products are Dharozar, Aatmaj, Rodular, Dharolik, Kohha, Kawayat Super and Sadavirum amongst others.

Plant growth regulator

We also manufacture plant growth regulator which are also known as plant hormones and helps in increasing the crop yield and improving its quality. Certain of our top branded plant growth regulator products are Rujuta, Greenoka, and Stabilizer amongst others.

Micro Fertilizers

In order to increase the production of crops and to make the soil more fertile, we manufacture micro fertilizers such as sulphur, zinc, boron and ferrous. Micro fertilizers are required for crops to reap the maximum yield from crops. Under this category we sell branded products such as Aakuko, Thandaj and Zusta amongst others.

Antibiotic

Antibiotic is a major tool to treat the bacterial issues of plants and tree fruits. Antibiotic boost and develop immune system of plants. Under this category we sell brand product namely Retardo.

Production Facilities

Dharmaj operates two state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities in Gujarat, India, to support its growing product demand. The first facility is located at Plot No. 408 to 411, Kerala GIDC Estate, Off NH-8, Kerala, Taluka Bavla, Ahmedabad – 382220. The second facility is situated at Plot No. DP – 154 & 153/2, GIDC Chemical Zone, Saykha-II, Taluka Vagra, District Bharuch – 392140.

Dharmaj’s manufacturing operations are supported by two advanced facilities in Gujarat, India. Our formulations plant in Kerala GIDC, Ahmedabad, has an installed production capacity of 25,500 TPA, while our multi-purpose facility for active ingredients and intermediates at Saykha GIDC, Bharuch, offers a capacity of 8,000 TPA.

Both facilities are equipped with modern infrastructure and machinery designed to produce high-quality agrochemical products. We manufacture a wide range of agrochemical formulations including insecticides, herbicides, fungicides, micro fertilizers, and plant growth regulators. Our commitment to quality is demonstrated by our certifications, which include ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, and ISO 45001:2018.

We also maintain an in-house Research & Development (R&D) center and a quality control laboratory at our manufacturing units. The quality control lab is responsible for monitoring raw materials and finished products, and is accredited by the National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories (NABL) in accordance with ISO/IEC 17025:2017 standards.

Global Presence

Dharmaj has established a strong international footprint by exporting agrochemical formulations to institutional customers across the globe. Our products are currently exported to approximately 38 countries, including regions in Latin America, East Africa, the Middle East, and Far East Asia.

We have secured a total of 607 product registrations from the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIB&RC). Of these, 236 registrations are exclusively for export, and 75 are for Technical products intended for international markets. We remain committed to expanding our global reach by investing in new product registrations, enabling us to offer a broader portfolio across emerging and existing markets.
