Welcome Jackson Investments Ltd.

Jackson Investments Limited was incorporated as a Public Limited Company under the provisions of the Companies Act, 1956 on 25th August 1982 in the City of Kolkata, West Bengal.

At present the Company is carrying the business of Investments in Capital Market via investing money in Listed and Unlisted Securities as well as other commercial papers. Apart from the Capital Market activities, the Company is also doing financing activities and providing finance to both Corporate and HNI category of Clients.
