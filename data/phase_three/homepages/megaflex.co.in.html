Introduction

Welcome to Mega Flex Plastics Ltd

We are pioneers and one of the leading manufacturers of Leno Bags in Eastern India. We have state of art facility for manufacturing of Leno Bags in PolyPark, Howrah. In addition to Leno Bags, which are used for packing of horticultural produces, we also manufacture Woven Fabric, Technical Textile Material and Sutli (twins). We are part of a group whose other business interests include manufacturing of rigid plastic packaging products and paper packaging products, warehousing etc.

We embarked on the journey in the year 2007 under visionary and dynamic leadership of Late Anup Chand Sethia, Bsc. Engg. With 4 decades of rich experience in plastic processing, oil milling, paper packaging and other industries, he visualized tremendous opportunity available in the field of packaging of horticultural produce, specially potato, onion and other vegetables. His vision became reality in the form of Mega Flex Plastics Ltd which started operation with 27 looms.
