ELEVATING HOSPITALITY ACROSS BORDERS

Ventive Hospitality offers a broad and distinctive portfolio of business, luxury hotels and resorts that spans vibrant cityscapes and serene seashores. From India’s bustling business hubs to idyllic Maldives atolls, and soon in sublime sunny Sri Lanka, Ventive Hospitality creates experiences tailored to both business and luxury travellers. Also within our fold are prime office parks and retail spaces, strategically located to serve the needs of modern businesses. This unique combination enables us to deliver value consistently.

Ventive Hospitality currently offers 14 hotels with 2199 rooms across business and luxury segments, mixed-use commercial and retail spaces of approx. 3.4 million sq. ft. Additionally, we are expanding with 7 new hospitality projects in India and Sri Lanka. Of these, 3 hotels are being developed by Ventive Hospitality and its subsidiaries, while the remaining 4 are being developed by the Promoter Group companies on a Right of First Offer (ROFO) or alternative structure basis for Ventive Hospitality, and will subsequently be transferred to the Company under a suitable arrangement.
