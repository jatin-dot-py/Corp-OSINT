About Us

Jayant Infratech Ltd. was incorporated in the year 2003 & is a technology driven company with excellence in Railway Electrification & Infrastructure Projects. Our major work encompasses electrification of new & existing Railway tracks helping nation to reduce dependability of fossil fuels thereby reducing the carbon print foot. Our services include concept to commissioning of Railway Infrastructure right from design to energization. We have completed thousands of KMS of electrification & erected several traction substation in a span of 20 years.
