Our Company

In the year 1968, under the stewardship of Mr. Pravinchandra B Shah, Vipul Group of companies began its Napthol, Fast color bases and dye intermediates business. What began as a small venture, expanded at a rapid pace and set up its manufacturing facility at Thane, outskirts of Mumbai.

Vipul Dye Chem was established in 1972, which was later renamed Vipul Organics Limited, to reflect the product portfolio in the year 2017. Vipul Organics Limited is celebrating its Golden Jubilee and is "50 years young" in the Color Industry.

In 1989, the second generation of the promoters, Mr. Vipul P Shah, joined the company, after completing his Chemical Engineering. He was the one who really gave wings to the company's growth story. Vipul Organics now also ventured into the pigments segment, making it a holistic Dyes and Pigments company that has one-stop products and solutions for anything to do with the category.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Dyes are soluble colouring substances that chemically bond to the material they are applied to, whereas pigments are insoluble particles that sit on the surface of a substrate held by a binder. Dyes are widely used in textiles, while pigments are preferred in paints, inks, plastics, and coatings where lightfastness and opacity are critical.
Specialty dyes and organic pigments are used across a wide range of industries including textiles, paints and coatings, printing inks, plastics and masterbatches, rubber and latex, agriculture (seed colouring), leather, paper and packaging, food and beverages, cosmetics, and wood coatings.
Backward-forward integration means a manufacturer controls the entire production chain — from making raw dye intermediates, through to producing pigment powders, and finally manufacturing finished pigment dispersions. This eliminates dependency on external suppliers, ensures consistent quality, and results in more competitive pricing for buyers.
When sourcing dyes and pigments, look for ISO certifications for quality management, REACH compliance for European markets, APEO/NPEO-free and heavy metal-free certifications for eco-friendly products, FDA approval for food and cosmetic colours, and compliance with Oeko-Tex or ZDHC standards for textile applications.
India is home to several established dyes and pigments manufacturers and suppliers, with Mumbai and Gujarat being the key hubs. Vipul Organics Ltd., founded in 1968 and operating with 6 manufacturing units near Mumbai, is one of the most trusted names — offering a complete range of organic pigments, reactive dyes, acid dyes, vat dyes, naphthols, fast color bases & salts, food colors, and dye intermediates, and exporting to over 45 countries worldwide.
