Shanmuga Hospital is one of the top multi speciality hospital in Salem with a strong legacy. Established in 1981 as Shanmuga Cancer institute, the only cancer hospital in Salem and its 8 surrounding districts, our journey to reach the “best hospital in Salem” status has been enormous. Today, we have over 20+ specialities right from general medicine to cancer treatment. We are filled with top-notch facilities to handle advanced equipment for accurate results. We have Doctors of multiple specialisations to handle all major fields of medicine. The care and support given by our experienced medical staffs makes our relationship with our patients much more special. In other words, we give you “a legacy of caring”

Get consulted from the comfort of your home

For those of you who are unable to visit us in person for consultation due to time constraints, distance or various other reasons, we offer tele consultation.

At Shanmuga hospital, we have enabled online consultation that helps you access healthcare benefits from us, through video calls.

You can talk to our doctors by booking an appointment at your convenience.
