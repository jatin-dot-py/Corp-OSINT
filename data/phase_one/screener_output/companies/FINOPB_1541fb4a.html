About

Fino Payments bank commenced banking operations in 2017 as a specialised payments bank. It provides deposit, payment and also transaction banking services to underserved
and underbanked customers, including low-income households, gig workers and rural communities. Operations follow a phygital
model, combining merchant-led assisted banking with digital channels viz FinoPay.
Core offerings include CASA accounts,
cash management services, domestic
money transfer, AePS/Micro ATM, UPI
and mobile banking; loans, fixed deposits
and insurance distributed through partner arrangements[1][2]

Key Points

Representative of Banks:[1]
Company acts as Business Correspondent
for multiple banks. As of Q1 FY27, network comprises ~20 lakh merchants including own and open banking merchants, along with 126 branches & CSPs[2]; merchant network covers ~98% of India’s pin codes. BC agents provide banking services on behalf of partner banks, including account opening, remittances, AePS, bill payments and recharges
