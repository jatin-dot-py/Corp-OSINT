About

Kilburn Engineering Ltd. is engaged in designing, manufacturing and commissioning customized equipment / systems for critical applications in several industrial sectors like Chemical, Steel, Nuclear Power, Petrochemical and Food Processing etc.[1]

Key Points

Business Overview[1]
The company primarily designs and manufactures drying systems for diverse applications including rotary dryers, coolers, kilns, heat exchangers, solvent/vapor recovery systems, and calciners, primarily for the chemical and petrochemical sectors.
It is a market leader in solid, liquid, and gas drying systems, providing solutions for the industries such as tea, fertilizer, carbon black, soda ash, pharmaceuticals, dyes, pigments,
and specialty chemicals.
