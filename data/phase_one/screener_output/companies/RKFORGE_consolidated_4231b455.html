About

Ramkrishna Forgings Ltd is primarily engaged in manufacturing and sale of forged components of automobiles, railway wagons & coaches and engineering parts. [1][2] It is the 2nd largest forging player in India.[3]

Key Points

Market Leadership:[1]
The company leads India’s forging sector.
It holds the distinction of being the largest forging company in Eastern India and ranks amongst the top nationwide, supplying high-quality forged components to both domestic and international clients.
