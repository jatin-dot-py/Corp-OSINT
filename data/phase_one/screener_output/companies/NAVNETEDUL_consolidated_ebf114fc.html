About

Navneet Education Limited is primarily into manufacturing and trading of education books, reference books, technical & professional books in paper form and e-learning form, and also paper and non-paper based stationery products. [1]

Key Points

Business Segments:
[1]
A) Stationery (~57% in FY26 vs ~59% in FY25)[2]
The company offers a range of stationery products for both academic and non-academic purposes, consisting of paper and non-paper-based stationery under the brands Navneet and YOUVA. The stationery business is split into:
a) Stationery Exports (~62% of Stationery revenue in FY26):
The Co. exports to 40+ countries with 1,500+ SKUs developed for international markets. The exports revenue declined 10% due to the US tariff challenges.
b) Stationery Domestic (38%):[3]
The company has 1,250+ SKUs for the domestic market. It's key clients include Walmart, Target, Staples, etc.
