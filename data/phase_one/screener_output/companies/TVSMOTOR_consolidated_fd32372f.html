About

TVS Motor Company Ltd (TVSM) is engaged in manufacturing two-wheelers and its accessories; it currently manufactures a wide range of two-wheelers and three-wheelers. [1]

Key Points

Overview[1]
The company is in the 2-wheeler industry and is the only company to have a presence in all 3 categories, i.e., motorcycles, scooters, and mopeds, catering to both domestic and international markets. It is also the only company to have a presence in the moped segment for the domestic market.
