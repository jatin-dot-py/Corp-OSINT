Aditya Infotech Limited (AIL) manufactures and provides video security and surveillance products, solutions, and services under the brand name 'CP Plus'.[1]

Key Points

Business Overview[1]
Aditya Infotech Limited is an Indian video security and surveillance products, solutions and services company operating primarily under the CP PLUS brand. Its portfolio covers analogue and IP cameras, DVRs/NVRs, AI-enabled cameras and analytics, access-control and biometric products, networking and power accessories, cloud storage, software platforms and field services for residential, commercial, industrial and government customers.
