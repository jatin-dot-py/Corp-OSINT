About

Incorporated in 2017, Infibeam Avenues Ltd is in the business of software development services, maintenance, web development, payment gateway services, e-commerce and other ancillary services[1]

Key Points

Market Leadership [1]
The company is a payment infrastructure provider offering omni-channel, full-stack B2B digital payment solutions, enterprise e-commerce software platforms, and lending solutions. It ranks among the top 3 B2B online payment gateways in India. The company entered the Middle East in Jun 2018 and became the 2nd-largest non-bank private payment company in the UAE within 18 months. It also operates India’s first retail payment gateway and first enterprise e-commerce marketplace software platform, with an estimated 8% share of India’s digital payments market.
