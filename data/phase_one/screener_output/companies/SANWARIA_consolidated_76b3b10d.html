About

Sanwaria Consumer Ltd (formerly
Sanwaria Agro Oils Ltd; ) is the listed
parent of a historically integrated soy,
edible-oil, rice and staples group. Under
CIRP since May 29, 2020, normal manufacturing /trading has largely ceased; only limited warehousing, leasing and job work continue while a liquidation application is pending. [1]

Key Points

Current status: Insolvency:[1]
NCLT admitted CP(IB) 7/2019 on May 29, 2020; the Board’s powers remain suspended and vest with RP Gautam Mittal. Five eligible EOIs were received, but no resolution plan won CoC approval; the CoC voted to liquidate and the RP filed IA/90(MP)2021. The January 16, 2026 NCLT cause list still showed the liquidation application for hearing; Q1 FY27 accounts continue to call it pending. Liquidation is therefore proposed, not yet ordered.[2]
