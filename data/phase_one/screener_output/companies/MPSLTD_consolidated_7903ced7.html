About

MPS is a B2B learning and platform solutions company powering education, and research for corporates. MPS has unlocked a new growth trajectory due to the combined effect of lower attention spans, rapid growth in digital consumption, and the recent advances in AI/ML.

Key Points

Business Segments:[1]
a) Research Solutions (60% in FY26 vs 63% in FY25):[2]
The company’s DigiCorePro platform
provides large-scale peer review, research integrity, and production workflow solutions.
Its Research Integrity Check platform is recognized as a Top Innovator at STM AI Innovation Day 2025. It uses AI-driven tools
to detect research misconduct, including paper mills, identity fraud, and image manipulation. The AJE Digital and Rubriq platform has processed over 1 Mn manuscripts and demonstrated a 52% acceptance rate in controlled trials compared with the 32% STM industry benchmark.
b) Education Solutions (27% in FY26 vs 21% in FY25):[2]
The Unbound Intelligence Assist provides AI-powered clinical decision support integrated
into flagship education platforms like uCentral and Nursing Central. The segment also focuses on accessibility and media asset development, significantly reducing production costs by automating the creation of over 30 Million
accessible media assets annually. Additionally,
its app-based assessment solutions enable
data-driven insights into learner performance
and outcomes.
c) Corporate Learning (13% in FY26 vs 16% in FY25): [2]
This segment offers AI-driven solutions,
including real-time analytics and dash
boards to track learner engagement, performance, and business impact.
Its AI roleplays and simulations
provide adaptive practice environments
that enhance real-world skills through
interactive conversations and feedback. Additionally, BridgeAI enables scalable multilingual translation and localization
of learning content with faster, accurate,
and context-aware capabilities.
