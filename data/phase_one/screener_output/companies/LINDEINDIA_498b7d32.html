About

Linde India Limited, a subsidiary of BOC Group,UK (owns 75% stake in the co), is primarily engaged in manufacture of industrial and medical gases and construction of cryogenic and non cryogenic air separation plants.[1]

Key Points

Business Segments:[1]
a) Gases, Related Products & Services (78% in Q1 FY27 vs 87% in Q1 FY26):[2]
The company supplies industrial and specialty gases such as oxygen, nitrogen, and argon through onsite pipeline systems, bulk liquid distribution networks, and packaged gas solutions. Its healthcare portfolio caters to hospitals and healthcare providers by offering medical gases and associated solutions.
b) Project Engineering Division (22% in Q1 FY26 vs 13% in Q1 FY26):[3]
The company offers turnkey engineering solutions for cryogenic gases, including the design, supply, installation, and commissioning of medium- to large-sized Air Separation Units (ASUs), nitrogen plants, and gas distribution networks. The division also manufactures cryogenic equipment such as distillation columns, cold boxes, cryogenic vessels,
and vaporizers for both internal use and
external customers.
