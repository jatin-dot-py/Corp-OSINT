About

Cera Sanitaryware Ltd is engaged in the business of manufacturing, selling and trading of various kinds of building products. It also has non-conventional wind & solar power for captive use in the state of Gujarat.[1]

Key Points

Product Offerings
The company offers a full spectrum of products, including sanitaryware, faucetware, tiles, wellness products (bathtubs, shower panels & enclosures), kitchen sinks, and mirrors under three brands, Senator, CERA Luxe, and CERA, catering to evolving consumer aspirations. [1]
