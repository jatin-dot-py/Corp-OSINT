About

Power Finance Corporation Limited is a Systemically Important Non-Deposit taking NBFC registered with the RBI as an Infrastructure Finance Company. It is engaged in extending financial assistance to the Indian power sector. [1]

Key Points

Business Overview [1] [2]
The company is a Maharatna CPSE under the Ministry of Power and India’s largest government-owned NBFC, established in 1986 to finance the country’s power sector. Its lending portfolio covers power generation, transmission and distribution, along with renewable energy and select infrastructure segments such as e-mobility, roads, ports and metro rail. It provides term loans, short-term finance, lease financing and other funding solutions to utilities, government entities, equipment manufacturers and infrastructure developers. It also serves as the nodal agency for key government programmes including RDSS, UMPP and IPDS and has supported nearly 50% of India’s installed power capacity. As of Jun 2026, the GoI holds a 56% stake in the company.
