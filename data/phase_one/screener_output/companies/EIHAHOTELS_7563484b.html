About

EIH Associated Hotels Ltd is primarily engaged in owning premium luxury hotels under the luxury 'Oberoi' and five-star 'Trident' brands.[1]

It is a Joint-Venture between the Oberoi Family and the Rajan Raheja Group.[2]

Key Points

History[1]
The company was established in 1983 as Pleasant Hotels Ltd. Its name was changed
in 1989 to Oberoi Associated Hotels Limited. Under further change, the company was
named EIH Associated Hotels Ltd
