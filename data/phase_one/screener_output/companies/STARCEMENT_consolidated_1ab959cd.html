About

Star Cements Ltd is engaged in manufacturing and selling of Cement Clinker & Cement. It sells its products across north-eastern and eastern states in India.[1] It is the largest cement manufacturer in North-east India.[2]

Key Points

Product Offerings[1]
Company offers a diverse product mix that comprises Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC 43-Grade and OPC 53-Grade), Portland Pozzolana Cement (PPC), and value-added premium products (Anti-Rust and Weather Shield Cement).
