About

MSTC (Metal Scrap Trade Corporation) Ltd is a Mini-Ratna Category-I PSU of GoI and was set up in September 1964 for regulating export of ferrous scrap from India.
Currently, the company is under the Ministry of Steel, GoI and is engaged in trading items and e-commerce services, e-auctions and e-procurement services.[1]

Key Points

Company Overview[1]
MSTC was established in 1964 to regulate exports of ferrous scrap from India. Since 1991, it has been directly engaged in marketing melting scrap, coke/coal, iron ore, and other products, with gradual diversification into multiple products and services over time.
MSTC is a Mini Ratna Category-I PSU of the Government of India, headquartered in Kolkata. Post its IPO in March 2019, the Government reduced its stake to 64.75%.
