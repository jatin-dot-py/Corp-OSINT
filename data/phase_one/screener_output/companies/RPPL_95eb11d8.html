About

Incorporated in 2011, Rajshree Polypack Ltd
deals in manufacturing and trading of Plastic Packaging Product[1]

Key Points

Product Portfolio[1]
a) Barrier Packaging: Packaging locks in freshness by blocking moisture, air, and contaminants.
b) Rigid Packaging: Durable, structured packaging protects products and maintains shape during handling and storage.
c) Plastic Rigid Sheets: Sturdy, versatile plastic sheets used in form-fill-seal processes and other durable packaging applications.
d) Injection Moulding: A process to manufacture precise, high-quality plastic parts by injecting molten material into a mould.
