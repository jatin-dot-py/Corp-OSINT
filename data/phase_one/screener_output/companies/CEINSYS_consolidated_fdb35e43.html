About

Incorporated in 1998, Ceinsys Tech Ltd provides Enterprise Geospatial & Engineering Services and sale of software and electricity[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1] [2][3]
The company is a part of the Nagpur-based Meghe Group. It is an ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and CMMI DEV Level 5-certified organization. It is a pure-play geospatial and technology solutions company offering end-to-end GIS data services, enterprise Web GIS/DSS, and full-vehicle product & industrial automation engineering.
