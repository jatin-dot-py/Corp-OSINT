Avanti Feeds Ltd. manufactures and sells shrimp feed, and exports processed shrimp.[1]

Key Points

Business Segments [1]

The company is a shrimp feed manufacturer, with an estimated 51-53% market share in the domestic shrimp feed market. Its product portfolio includes Manamei, Profeed, Prostar, Titan, and High Boost, etc catering to different shrimp species and farming requirements. The company has an installed shrimp feed manufacturing capacity of 7.75 lakh MTPA, spread across 5 ISO- and BAP-certified plants.
