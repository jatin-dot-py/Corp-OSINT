About

Motherson Sumi Wiring India, a JV between Sumitomo Wiring System and Motherson Group, is a market leader in the Indian wiring harness industry with a market share of over 40%.[1]

Key Points

Business Profile:[1]
The company was established in April 2021 as a result of the reorganisation of Samvardhana Motherson International Limited, in which the automotive wiring harness business for Indian OEMs was demerged from the parent company and established as MSWIL. It is a JV with Sumitomo Wiring Systems, Ltd., a global leader in manufacturing wiring harnesses, harness components, and electrical wiring.
