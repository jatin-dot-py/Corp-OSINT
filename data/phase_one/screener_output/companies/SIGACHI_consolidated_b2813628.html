About

Incorporated in 1989, Sigachi Industries Ltd is a manufacturing and selling of MCC.[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1][2] [3]
The company is a manufacturer of pre-formulation and cellulose-based excipients and ranks among India's largest producers of Microcrystalline Cellulose (MCC), with a portfolio of over 60 MCC grades ranging from 15 to 250 microns. It offers 100+ products, including pharmaceutical excipients, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), film coatings, polymer blends, food and nutrition ingredients, and operations and management solutions.
