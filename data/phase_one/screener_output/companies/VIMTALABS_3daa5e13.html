Incorporated in 1990, Vimta Labs Ltd is a contract research and testing services provider[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1]
Vimta Labs Limited is a leading Indian contract research and testing company serving pharmaceuticals, biopharmaceuticals, food, medical devices, agrochemicals, consumer products, electronics, infrastructure, power, cement, oil & gas and mining industries. Its services span preclinical research, clinical research, analytical testing, biologics and peptide development, food testing, environmental monitoring, and EMI/EMC testing. The company also operates a National Reference Food Laboratory in Hyderabad and manages the National Food Laboratory at Navi Mumbai under a 25-year PPP contract with FSSAI.
