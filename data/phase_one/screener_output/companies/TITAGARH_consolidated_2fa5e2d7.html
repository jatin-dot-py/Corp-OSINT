About

Titagarh Rail Systems Ltd (Formerly Titagarh Wagons Limited), incorporated in 1997, is mainly engaged in the manufacturing and selling of Freight Wagons, Passenger Coaches, Metro Trains, Train Electricals, Steel Castings, Specialised Equipments & Bridges, Ships, etc. The company caters to both domestic and export markets.[1]

Key Points

Market Position [1] [2]
The company is one of India’s largest wagon manufacturers and the only private-sector player with integrated capabilities across freight and passenger rail systems, including freight wagons, metro and Vande Bharat coaches, propulsion systems, Train Control and Management Systems, steel castings, and other railway components.
