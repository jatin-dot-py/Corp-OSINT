Business Overview:[1][2]
POMPL is operating a satellite television channel “Insync” which is showcasing Indian Classical Music and other non-film music genres. It is currently distributed on Tata Sky, Incable, Siti cable, Aadhar, Jio TV (mobile platform), JPR, and several other small cable operators in different parts of the country. It is estimated that the channel now reaches almost 35 million households and is popular as a family channel amongst all age profiles.
