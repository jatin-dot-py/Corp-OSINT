Incorporated in 1921, Ludlow Jute & Specialities Ltd manufactures and sale of Jute Goods[1]

Key Points

Business Overview
Products include jute mesh used in roofing, agriculture, and horticulture; webbing for furniture; rubber-bonded jute cloth for landscaping; special fabrics for furnishing and clothing; soil-saving fabrics; and carpet-backing cloth. It also makes hessian cloth, sacking, shopping bags, lifestyle products, home décor, and other finished jute products.[1] [2]
