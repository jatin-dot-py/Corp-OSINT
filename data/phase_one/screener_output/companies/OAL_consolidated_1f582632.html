About

Incorporated in 1973, Oriental Aromatics Ltd is a manufacturer of specialty-based aroma chemicals, camphor, and flavour and fragrance products[1]

Key Points

History:[1]
In August 2008, Oriental Aromatics acquired a 57.6% controlling stake in Camphor & Allied Products Ltd., a leading manufacturer of camphor and specialty aroma chemicals. In April 2017, Oriental Aromatics was amalgamated with CAPL, following which the merged entity was renamed Oriental Aromatics Limited (OAL).
