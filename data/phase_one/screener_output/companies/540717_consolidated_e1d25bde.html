About

Polo Queen Industrial and Fintech Ltd is a Mumbai-based trading-led group spanning textiles, branded FMCG, chemicals/minerals
and a small non-deposit-taking NBFC. Its Poloqueen /Doan Rajkamal portfolio serves civilian and defence channels, while specialty cleaning additives are produced through third-party job work. It also owns Mahad and Dombivli property assets and proposes agro-processing and data-centre projects.[1]

Key Points

Product Range:[1]
The core business trades fabrics /textiles,
FMCG, chemicals and minerals. The FMCG comprises personal care products, mosquito coils, household cleaners, air fresheners, dishwash, detergents, fabric soaps and whiteners, sold under Poloqueen through
Doan Rajkamal to civilian and defence channels.
Management states that internally developed, biodegradable specialty cleaning additives are produced through third-party job work and positioned as import substitutes for conventional surfactants.[2]
