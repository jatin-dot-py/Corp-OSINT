About

Godrej Agrovet Limited is a diversified, Research & Development focused agri-business company, dedicated to improving the productivity of Indian farmers by innovating products and services that sustainably increase crop and livestock yields. It holds leading market positions in the different businesses in which it operates - Animal Feed, Crop Protection, Oil Palm, Dairy and Poultry and Processed Foods. [1]

Key Points

Business Segments[1] [2]
a) Animal Nutrition (46% in FY26 vs 48% in FY25): [3] [4] [5]
The company is among India’s largest organised animal feed manufacturers, offering a diversified portfolio of cattle, layer, broiler, shrimp, fish and other animal feed products. It also has a presence in Bangladesh through its JV, ACI Godrej Agrovet Pvt Ltd. During FY26, the company introduced Dhanalaxmi G, a premium cattle feed targeted at high-yielding cattle, and Bypro Plus, an advanced high-protein formulation. The company’s sales volume in this segment increased from 14,75,144 tonnes in FY25 to 16,46,545 tonnes in FY26.
