About

Established in 1929, South Indian Bank was the first ‘scheduled bank’ amongst the private banks in Kerala. It has strong presence in south India and particularly in Kerala. South Indian Bank provides retail and corporate banking, para banking activities such as debit card, third party financial product distribution, in addition to Treasury and Foreign Exchange Business.[1]
