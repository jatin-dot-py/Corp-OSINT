About

Incorporated in 1987, Hester Biosciences Ltd is one of India’s leading animal healthcare companies and a major manufacturer of poultry and animal vaccines, including a leading position in PPR and Goat Pox vaccines.[1]

Key Points

Business Overview[1]
Hester operates an integrated animal healthcare platform across Poultry Healthcare and Animal Healthcare, offering vaccines, therapeutics, feed supplements, diagnostics, and petcare products. It's portfolio serves poultry farmers, livestock producers, veterinarians, and pet owners through a combination of R&D, manufacturing, quality systems, technical services, and field support.
