About

Incorporated in 1961, Bimetal Bearings Ltd manufactures Engine Bearings, Bushings, Thrust Washers, Alloy Powder and Bimetallic Strips.[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1]
Bimetal Bearings Limited is a manufacturer of engine bearings, bushings, thrust washers, alloy powders, and bimetallic strips, operating three integrated plants across Chennai, Coimbatore, and Hosur. Backed by the Amalgamations Group, the Company serves leading automotive OEMs, defence, railway, and export markets.
