About

Founded in 2002, Ceigall India Limited is an infrastructure construction company with expertise in specialized structural projects, including elevated roads, flyovers, bridges, railway overpasses, tunnels, highways, expressways, and runways.[1]

Key Points

Business Profile[1]
Ceigall India Ltd is a leading EPC company with over 20 years of experience in infrastructure projects, specializing in highways, expressways, flyovers, tunnels, elevated roads, and metro rail. It follows an asset-light model, operating primarily with leased equipment.
