About

Heidelberg Cement India is the Indian subsidiary of HeidelbergCement Group, Germany. The Company has its operations in Central India at Damoh (Madhya Pradesh), Jhansi (Uttar Pradesh), and in Southern India at Ammasandra (Karnataka). [1]

The company sells under the MyCem and MyCem Power brand. [2]

Key Points

Entity & evolution
Heidelberg Materials entered India in 2006 through Mysore Cements and an Indorama Cement JV; Indorama was fully acquired by 2008, merged in 2009, and the entity was renamed HCIL. Zuari Cement and Gulbarga Cement are group companies and their business is not consolidated into HCIL. [1] [2] [3]
