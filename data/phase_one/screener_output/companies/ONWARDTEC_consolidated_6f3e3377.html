About

Incorporated in 1991, Onward Technologies is a software outsourcing company specializing in Digital and ER&D services.[1]

Key Points

Business Overview[1]
Onward Technologies is a software outsourcing company specialising in Digital and Engineering Research & Development (ER&D) services. The Company serves the Transportation & Mobility, Industrial Equipment & Heavy Machinery, and Healthcare & Life Sciences sectors through offshore delivery centres in India and operations across 6 countries. With a workforce of 2,800+ employees, it provides capabilities in mechanical engineering, digital & data engineering, electronics & embedded engineering, and digital factory solutions.
