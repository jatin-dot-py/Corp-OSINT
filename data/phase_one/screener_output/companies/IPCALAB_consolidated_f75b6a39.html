About

Ipca Laboratories is an integrated pharmaceutical company manufacturing branded and generic formulations, APIs and intermediates. Indian branded formulations are its core profit engine, complemented by formulation exports across Europe, Africa, the Americas, Asia, CIS and Australasia. The consolidated business includes Unichem Laboratories and its US, Brazil, Europe and South Africa operations.[1]

Key Points

Integrated nature of operations
Ipca Laboratories is a fully integrated pharmaceutical company that manufactures over 350 formulations and 80 APIs for various therapeutic segments. [1]
IPCA is among India’s top 20 pharmaceutical companies. [2]

Business segments FY26:
API Business[3]
Ipca produces over 80 APIs; nearly 79% of the APIs and Intermediates business is from exports, making them one of India’s top exporters of APIs, serving over 100 countries around the globe.
