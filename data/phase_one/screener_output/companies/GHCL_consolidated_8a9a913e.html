About

Established 1983, GHCL Limited is engaged in the business of manufacturing and trading of Inorganic Chemicals (Soda Ash).[1][2]

Key Points

Company Overview[1]
GHCL is a leading Indian soda ash and raw salt manufacturer with an integrated soda ash value chain. The company focuses on manufacturing excellence, capacity expansion, process optimisation and sustainability-led operations. It serves 272 customers and is increasingly expanding into value-added products, circularity and sustainable chemistry to drive long-term growth.
