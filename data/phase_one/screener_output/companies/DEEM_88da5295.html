About

Incorporated in 2003, Deem Roll-Tech Ltd is in business of roll manufacturing[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1][2]
a) DRTL manufactures steel rolls, essential components for the iron and steel rolling mill industry.
b) It produces steel and alloy rolls using materials like steel scrap, roll scrap, pig iron, nickel, ferro molybdenum, other ferro alloys, and resin-coated sand.
c) The company makes custom and various types of rolls for long product mills, flat product mills, and seamless tube rolling mills.
d) DRTL sells its rolls directly to rolling mill manufacturers and the replacement market, including iron and steel rolling mills, through a network of dealers, distributors, and agents. The rolls serve as consumables for these mills.
