About

Incorporated in 1989, Elango Industries Ltd performs Operation & Maintenance and other service activities[1]

Key Points

Current vs legacy business:[1]
The original steel-ingot business at Karaikal /Pondicherry was divested. The company had ~7 MW gas plant (2005) and 63 MW coal-based captive plant (2012).
Its current offerings are power-plant O&M, marketing /sale of clients power, power-related spares and rooftop-solar marketing. However, in FY26, no O&M was undertaken, and other report sections describe no active business; ownership /status /utilisation of the legacy plants is not disclosed and they should not be treated as current owned capacity.[2]
