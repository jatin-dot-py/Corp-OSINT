About

CIE Automotive Limited is a subsidiary of CIE Automotive S.A, Spain. Mahindra Vehicle Manufacturing Ltd. was a significant shareholder in the company. The company is engaged in the business of production and sale of automotive components to original equipment manufacturers and other customers in India and overseas. [1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1]
The company is an automotive components manufacturer supplying products to automotive OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers across multiple markets. It is part of the CIE Automotive Group of Spain and benefits from the Group’s extensive experience partnering with customers and co-developing products for the automotive industry.
