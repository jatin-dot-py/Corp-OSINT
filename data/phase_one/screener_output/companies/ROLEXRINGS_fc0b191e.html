About

Rolex Rings is a leading manufacturer of forged and machined components in India and is one of the top 5 forging companies in India. The company is a Global supplier of hot rolled forged, machined bearing rings and automotive components for various segments of vehicles, Industrial Machinery, Wind Turbines and Railways

Key Points

Business Structure:[1]
Rolex Rings manufactures and sells forged
and machined rings, along with automotive components. It reports this as a single segment, Diversified Auto Components, split into two product families: Bearing Rings and Automotive Components. The company supplies bearing manufacturers, automotive OEM and Tier suppliers, and customers across industrial machinery, railways, renewable energy, and mobility applications.
