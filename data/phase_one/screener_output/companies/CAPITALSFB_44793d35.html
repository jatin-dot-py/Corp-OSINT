Key Points

Business Overview:[1]
CSFBL, originally a local area bank, is now a full-fledged Small Finance Bank focused on middle-income customers. It offers secured lending products, including agriculture loans (Kisan Credit Cards, term loans), MSME & trading loans, mortgage/housing loans, corporate loans, and consumption loans. The company avoids direct exposure to Microfinance Institutions (MFIs).
