About

Manappuram Finance is a Non-Banking Finance Company (NBFC), which provides a wide range of fund based and fee based services including gold loans, money exchange facilities, etc. The Company is a Systemically Important Non-Deposit taking NBFC(NBFC-ND).(Source : 202003 Annual Report Page No:120)

Key Points

Joint Promoter[1] [2] [3]
In Mar 2025, the company entered into a definitive agreement with Bain Capital for a strategic investment of Rs. 4,385 Cr through a preferential allotment of equity shares and warrants, enabling Bain Capital to acquire an 18% stake on a fully diluted basis, with a mandatory open offer for an additional 26% stake. It received the requisite approvals from the Reserve Bank of India in Mar 2026. It has received Rs. 2,740 Cr in equity infusion from Bain Capital in Mar 2026, while the remaining Rs. 1,644 Cr towards share warrants is expected over the next 4-18 months. Upon completion, Bain Capital became a joint promoter alongside the existing promoters, with board representation and joint control of the company.
