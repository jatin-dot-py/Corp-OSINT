About

Glenmark Life Sciences, a subsidiary of Nirma Limited, a diversified conglomerate with interest in consumer products, cement, chemicals and pharmaceuticals. It is a leading developer and manufacturer of select, high-value, non-commoditized, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) in chronic therapeutic areas. The company further operates in Contract Development and manufacturing operations to offer services to specialty Pharmaceutical companies.

Key Points

History
Before 2019 API business was an integral part of Glenmark Pharma (GPL). It was separated from GPL into GLS through a Business Transfer Agreement in Jan 2019, and GLS was subsequently listed on NSE & BSE in FY22. [1]
