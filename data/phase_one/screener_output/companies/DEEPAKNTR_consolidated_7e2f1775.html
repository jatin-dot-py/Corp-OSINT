About

Incorporated in 1970, Deepak Nitrite Ltd manufactures Basic Intermediates, Fine
& Speciality Chemicals, Performance
Products, and Phenolics[1]

Key Points

Business Segments:[1]
The company is one of the fastest growing chemical intermediates companies in India with a portfolio of 36+ products and 56+ applications, divided into 2 segments:
a) Phenolics (68% in FY26 vs 70% in FY25): [2][3][4]
The company produces Cumene, Phenol, Acetone, Isopropyl Alcohol, Alpha Methyl Styrene (AMS), etc., catering to industries like laminates & plywood, automotive, construction, pharmaceuticals, and adhesives. It is the largest producer of Phenol and Acetone in India with a 50% market share.
b) Advanced Intermediates (32% in FY26 vs 30% in FY25):[5] [6]
The company manufactures Nitrites, Nitro Toluidines, Xylidines, Oxyimes, Cumidines, Optical Brightening Agents (OBA), DASDA, etc., serving industries like colorants, dyes, rubber, paper, agrochemicals, pharmaceuticals, water treatment, etc. It is India's largest producer of Sodium Nitrite and ranks among the top 3 global manufacturers of Xylidines and Oximes.
