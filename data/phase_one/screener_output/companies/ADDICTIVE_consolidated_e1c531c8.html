About

Incorporated in 2017, Addictive Learning Technology Ltd is in the business of providing consultancy education services[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1]
a) ALTL is an edtech platform focused on upskilling and career services, primarily for senior and mid-career professionals, with some offerings for young professionals.
b) Through its brands LawSikho, Skill Arbitrage, and Dataisgood, it provides a broad range of courses, including Law, Finance, Accounting, Corporate Governance, Compliance, HR, Business Consulting, AI, Content Writing, Video Editing, and Data Science.
c) ALTL also offers programs for college students and recent graduates seeking advanced skills and employment.
d) Its courses open up remote career opportunities in the international market, with popular subjects like US IP Law, Tax Law, Accounting, Corporate Compliance, Contract Drafting, and Business Law.
e) It provides international bar exam preparation for Canada, the UK, and California, USA.
