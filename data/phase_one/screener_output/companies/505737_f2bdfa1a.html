Key Points

Business Overview:[1]
The Company is an engineering equipment manufacturer serving core industries such as steel, cement, fertiliser, chemicals, mining and infrastructure. Its product portfolio includes grinding mills, screening and feeding equipment, conveyors, bulk material handling equipment, crushers, dryers, sizers, screen decks, flip flow screens, industrial gearboxes and geared motors. Its Building Material Division in Ajmer, Rajasthan manufactures building material products for the construction industry.
