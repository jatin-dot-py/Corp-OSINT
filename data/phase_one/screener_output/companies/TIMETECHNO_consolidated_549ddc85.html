About

Time Technoplast is a multinational conglomerate involved in the manufacturing of technology and innovation driven polymer & composite products.(Source : 202003-01 Annual Report Page No:74)

Key Points

Market Leadership[1]
The company is a leading global industrial packaging player with a 55% market share in India's industrial packaging segment and is the world's largest manufacturer of large-size plastic drums. It is India's first manufacturer of Type-IV composite cylinders for LPG, CNG, and hydrogen, the world's 2nd-largest composite cylinder producer, the 2nd-largest MOX film manufacturer in India, a major HDPE pipe manufacturer, and the 3rd-largest IBC manufacturer globally. The company also holds a market-leading position in 9 of the 11 countries where it operates.
