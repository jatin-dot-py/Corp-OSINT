About

NTPC (National Thermal Power Corporation) Ltd along with its subsidiaries/ associates & JVs is primarily involved in generation and sale of bulk power to State power utilities. Other business of the group includes providing consultancy, project management & supervision, energy trading, oil & gas exploration and coal mining.[1]

Key Points

Business Segments - FY26:[1]
a) Generation (94):[2][3][4]
The company is the largest power-generating company in India with an aggregate installed capacity of 89,108 MW, constituting 17% of India’s total installed capacity. It owns 53 power stations across India (27 coal-based, 7 gas-based, 1 Hydro, 1 Wind, 17 Solar, and 1 Small hydro plant). It is a major player in coal-based thermal generation in India.
