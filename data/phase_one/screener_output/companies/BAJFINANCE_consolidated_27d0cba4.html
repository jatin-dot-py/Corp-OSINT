About

Bajaj Finance is mainly engaged in the business of lending. BFL has a diversified lending portfolio across retail, SME and commercial customers with a significant presence in urban and rural India. It also accepts public and corporate deposits and offers variety of financial services products to its customers.(Source : 202003 Annual Report Page No:124)

Key Points

Business Overview:[1]
The company is India's largest private-sector Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC), catering to both individual and business customers through a portfolio of financial products and services. Its offerings span loans, fixed deposits, payments, insurance, and mutual funds through its mobile app, website, and social media, and through its branch network.
