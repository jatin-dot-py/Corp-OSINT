About

Goodluck India Ltd is an engineering conglomerate engaged in the business of manufacturing and selling engineering products such as sheets, pipes, engineering structures, fabricated structures, forgings, and automobile tubes. [1]

Key Points

Business Segments[1]
a) CR Coils, Pipes & Tubes (34% in FY26 vs 36% in FY25): [2]
The company manufactures cold-rolled coils and sheets, corrugated sheets, hollow sections (square, rectangular, and round), GI pipes, and support structures catering to end-user industries such as road bridges, railways, and infrastructure development. Its products are supplied to public-sector OEMs, central government entities, private-sector OEMs, and state governments.
