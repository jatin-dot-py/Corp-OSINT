About

EID Parry is engaged in Sugar, Nutraceuticals and ethanol production. It also has a significant presence in the Farm Inputs business including Bio pesticides through its subsidiary, Coromandel International Limited. [1]

Key Points

Part of the Murugappa Group[1]
The company is the flagship sugar business of the Rs. 871 Bn Murugappa Group, one of India's leading diversified conglomerates. The Group comprises 10 listed companies on the NSE and BSE and has a presence across businesses including sugar, fertilisers, engineering, financial services, general insurance, and agro-inputs, with manufacturing operations spanning 11 countries across 5 continents. Other major Group companies include Tube Investments of India Ltd, Cholamandalam MS General Insurance Company Ltd, Coromandel International Ltd, and NACL Industries Ltd.
