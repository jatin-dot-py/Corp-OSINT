About

Founded in 2005, Laurus Labs is a research-driven pharmaceutical and biotechnology company having a global leadership
position in select Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) including anti-retroviral, oncology drugs (including High Potent APIs), Cardiovascular, and Gastro therapeutics. They also offer integrated CMO and CDMO services to Global Innovators from Clinical phase drug development to commercial manufacturing.
Laurus employs 6,500+ people, including around 1,050+ scientists, at more than 11 facilities approved by global agencies USFDA, WHO-Geneva, Japan-PDMA, UK-MHRA, EMA, TGA etc. [1]

Key Points

Business Segments:
a) Generic API: [1]
The company has one of the largest
HiPotent API manufacturing capacities
in India and is the world's leading third-party supplier of antiretroviral APIs. Its portfolio includes antiretrovirals, oncology, steroids, hormones, and cardiovascular APIs, serving global generic pharmaceutical companies.
b) Generics FDF:[2]
The company offers oral solid formulations, across therapeutic areas like ARVs, Anti-diabetic, Cardiovascular, and Proton-Pump Inhibitors (PPIs).
c) CDMO Synthesis:[1]
The custom synthesis division, Laurus Synthesis, offers drug development and manufacturing services like contract manufacturing, clinical supplies, and analytical research to global pharma, crop science, animal health, specialty ingredients, and biotech firms. Key markets include the US, EU, and Japan.
The company has a CDMO pipeline
of 70+ active projects, including break
through designated molecules, 10 of
which are commercial (APIs and intermediates).
It is working on 20+ active projects in Animal Health and Crop Protection chemicals, with ongoing commercial validation supplies. Both projects are expected to reach peak potential by FY27-28.[3]
d) Biotechnology:
The company’s subsidiary Laurus Bio**
offers end-to-end services from clone
and strain engineering to large-scale
production, supporting clients in the
microbial precision fermentation. Its
offerings include nutraceuticals, dietary supplements, cosmeceuticals, and
alternative food proteins, catering
to regenerative medicine, vaccines,
cultured meat, and more.[2][4] Laurus Bio secured an equity investment
of Rs. 120 Cr from Eight Roads Ventures
and F-Prime Capital in Dec 2024, with an additional Rs 40 Cr co-investment from
the company. The partnership will fund
a 400 KL microbial fermentation facility
in Vizag, expected to be completed by
the end of 2026.[5]
