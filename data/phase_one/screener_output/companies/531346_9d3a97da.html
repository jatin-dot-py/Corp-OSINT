Incorporated in 1993, Eastern Treads Ltd
is in the business of manufacture and sale

Key Points

Business Overview:[1]
ETL primarily supplies tyre treads for trucks and buses, and has also developed specialised compounds for LCVs, cars, and two-wheelers. It now supports tyre makers without in-house retreading by handling first-cycle retreading for them. The company additionally offers a pay-per-kilometre fleet management service to selected customers.
