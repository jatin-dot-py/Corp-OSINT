About

Incorporated in 1984, Bliss GVS Pharma Limited is engaged in manufacturing, marketing, trading and export of pharmaceutical formulations in the form of suppositories, pessaries, capsules, tablets, and syrups.

Key Points

Change of Promoter[1] [2]
In May 2026, the company disclosed that certain promoter and non-promoter shareholders had entered into a Share Purchase Agreement with Anupam Rasayan India Ltd for the proposed acquisition of approximately 43.28% of its paid-up equity share capital for Rs. 1,369 Cr, subject to regulatory approvals and other closing conditions. The transaction also triggered a mandatory open offer for up to an additional 26% stake from public shareholders.
