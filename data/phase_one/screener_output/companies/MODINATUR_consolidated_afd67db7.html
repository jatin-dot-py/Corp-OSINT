About

Modi Naturals Ltd operates across branded consumer foods and edible oils, bulk rice
-bran products and grain-based ethanol.
The company's edible-oil operations trace
back to rice-bran processing. The consumer portfolio now covers premium oils and packaged foods, while ethanol is now manufactured through Modi Biotech
Pvt Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary.[1][2][3]
