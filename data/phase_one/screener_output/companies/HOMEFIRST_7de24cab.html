About

Home First Finance Company India (HFFC) is a Housing Finance Company which offers home loan with 48 hours sanctions and hassle-free process. [1]

Key Points

Offerings[1]
The company is a technology-driven affordable housing finance company with a pan-India presence, focused on first-time homebuyers from families earning below Rs. 50,000 per month. Housing loans account for 83% of AUM with an average ticket size of ~Rs. 12 lakh. The company has a diversified lender base of 31 banks/FIs, positive ALM, no commercial paper exposure, and utilizes data-driven underwriting and proprietary credit scoring models. Its distribution network covers ~80% of India's affordable housing market.
