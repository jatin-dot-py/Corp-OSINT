About

Incorporated in 2015, IRM Energy Ltd is in the business of selling and distributing natural gas.[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1]
IRMEL is engaged in storing, supplying, and distributing natural gas, and laying, operating, and maintaining CGD networks. It has a five-year district marketing exclusivity for Banaskantha and FS and an eight-year exclusivity for Diu & Gir Somnath and NT, and a 25-year infrastructure exclusivity for all GAs. IRMs have been held 50% by Cadila Pharmaceuticals Limited and its related parties and 23% by other strategic partners.
