About

Phoenix Mills Ltd is engaged in the operation & management of malls, construction of commercial & residential property and hotel business in India. Its core businesses include Retail, Office, Hotel and Asset Management.[1]

Key Points

Business Segments:
A) Property & Related Services (79% in 9M FY25 vs 72% in FY23):[1] [2]
a) Retail:
The company is India's largest retail mall operator and offers retail malls and office spaces on a license basis. It manages 12 retail properties spanning 11.5 Mn sq ft across 8 major cities in India. [3]It is further developing 4 new malls across Kolkata, Gujarat, Thane, and Coimbatore with a gross leasable area of 4.3 Mn sq ft. The Phoenix Grand Victoria mall in Alipore, Kolkata, and a retail mall in Gujarat, totaling 2 Mn sq ft of leasable area, are set to be operational by 2027.[4]
