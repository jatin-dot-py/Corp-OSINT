About

Incorporated in 1979, Sukra Jewellery Ltd manufactures diamond studded gold jewelry and trades cut, polished diamonds. It also deals in Real estate and Construction work[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1][2]
SJL, originally a silver retailing company, holds ISO 9001 accreditation, a BIS Silver Hallmarking license, and a Certificate of Excellence from the Indo American Business Promotion Council, USA. Its silver jewellery collection features contemporary designs, temple jewellery, gift articles, silverware, pooja items, designer jewels, heirloom and antique pieces, and the Kaavyam collection.

Currently, SJL has diversified into the Real Estate and Gems & Jewellery businesses. The company has launched two real estate projects: Shanti Shukra in Ahmedabad and Shubh Shukra in Visnagar, both of which are now operational.
