About

Incorporated in 1996, Manika Plastech
Limited is engaged in the manufacturing
of rigid polymer packaging products,
including battery casings, pails, and
thin-wall containers.[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1]
Manika Plastech is a design-led manufacturer
of precision-engineered rigid polymer packaging products, serving industries such as energy storage, automotive, paints, lubricants, food, dairy, chemicals, agrochemicals and the construction chemicals.
Its core products include battery casings,
pails and thin wall containers, along with
painting services for automotive components. The company provides an integrated offering covering product design, mould development, manufacturing, labelling, quality testing and delivery.
