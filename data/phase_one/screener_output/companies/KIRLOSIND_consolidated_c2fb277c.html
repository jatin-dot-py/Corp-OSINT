About

Kirloskar Industries Limited is the listed core investment company of the Kirloskar Group.
Its consolidated operations are dominated
by 46%-owned subsidiary Kirloskar Ferrous Industries Limited, an integrated ferrous manufacturer spanning iron ore, pig iron, castings, alloy steel and seamless tubes.
KIL also owns Avante Spaces, which is developing Grade-A commercial real
estate in Pune, and holds strategic
listed stakes in Kirloskar Brothers,
Kirloskar Oil Engines and Kirloskar
Pneumatic. The parent’s standalone
income is mainly dividends, interest
and investment returns; consolidated
analysis is necessary to capture KFIL
and the real-estate investment cycle.
