About

Rain Industries Limited (RAIN) is a leading vertically integrated producer of carbon, cement and Advanced materials products. Headquartered in India, RAIN has manufacturing facilities in eight countries across three continents. [1]

Key Points

Business Segments Q1 CY26[1]
1) Carbon (75%):[2] [3]
The carbon segment converts byproducts from oil refining and steel production into high-value, carbon-based products like Calcined Petroleum Coke (CPC), Coal Tar Pitch (CTP), and Other Carbon Products (OCP), which are then used to manufacture aluminium, graphite electrodes, carbon black and titanium dioxide. It is the world's largest producer of CTP and the second-largest manufacturer of CPC.
