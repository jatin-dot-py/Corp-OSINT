About

Apar, founded by Mr. Dharmsinh D. Desai in 1958, is a market leader in India with a global presence. Contributing to India’s process of electrification it started from manufacturing power transmission cables to having three broad business segments, which are Conductors, Transformer and specialty oils (TSO), and Power/telecom Cables. [1] [2]

Key Points

Business Segments:[1] [2]
a) Conductors (51% in FY26 vs 48% in FY25):[3][4]
The company is the world’s largest aluminium conductor manufacturer and the largest exporter of conductors from India, serving leading utilities and EPC players. Its portfolio includes conventional conductors, HTLS conductors, OPGW, OPPC, railway conductors, busbars, ACS wires, speciality alloy rods, etc. It has a technology tie-up with CTC Global (USA) for ACCC conductors, and was among the pioneers in India to test 765kV, 800kV, and 1200kV conductors. It further expanded its product portfolio in FY25 by launching coated conductors, high-fibre-count OPGW, Cu-Mg catenary wires, Cu-Ag contact wires, and lead connection cables. The company is also a market leader in reconductoring, having completed over 272 projects covering 7,075 ckm+ of transmission lines.
