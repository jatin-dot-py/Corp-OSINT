About

Incorporated in 1976, Sunil Healthcare Ltd is a certified manufacturer of Empty Hard Gelatin capsules shell and HPMC Capsules shell.[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1][2]
a) SHL was earlier engaged in trading (including merchant trading) agro based commodities, under brand Sunloc foods However, this was discontinued in FY20
b) Currently, it is mainly engaged in the manufacturing empty hard gelatin capsule (EHGC) shells and hydroxypropyl methyl cellulose (HPMC) and the choice in 9 sizes available are 00, 0SEL, 0EL, 0,1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
c) The company is a Hard-shell capsule manufacturer in India and pioneer in double lock and triple lock technology for capsules in India.
d) The company is WHO-GMP certified company and has also been recognized as Star Export House by GOI.
