About

Greenply Industries Ltd is an interior-infrastructure company with products
spanning plywood and blockboards,
MDF and pre-laminated MDF, decorative veneers, flush and PVC/WPC doors, specialty plywood, HDF flooring and furniture fittings.
The group sells predominantly in India through a pan-India trade network.[1][2]

Key Points

Business Model:[1]
Greenply has evolved from a plywood-focused company into a broader wood-panels and interior-solutions business.[2]
The plywood model combines owned manufacturing with outsourced and traded products. Owned plants are used for quality- and margin-sensitive products, while outsourcing extends product availability and market coverage without equivalent capital investment. In Q1 FY27, own manufacturing contributed 68% of plywood volume and 73% of plywood value.[3]
